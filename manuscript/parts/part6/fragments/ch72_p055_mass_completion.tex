\begin{proposition}
\label{prop:p055-boundary-mass-completion}
Let \(j:X\hookrightarrow\overline X\) be an open embedding of locally compact
Hausdorff spaces.  Let \(\overline Y\) be a locally compact Hausdorff space,
let \(\overline h:\overline Y\to\overline X\) be a proper continuous map, and
put
\[
 U:=\overline h^{-1}(X),\qquad h:=\overline h|_U:U\longrightarrow X.
\]
Let \(\mu_{\mathrm{NA}}\) be a finite positive Radon measure on
\(\overline Y\), and let \(\mu_{\mathrm{poly}}\) be a finite positive Radon
measure on \(X\).  Suppose that
\begin{equation}
 h_*\bigl(\mu_{\mathrm{NA}}|_U\bigr)=\mu_{\mathrm{poly}}
 \label{eq:p055-local-pushforward}
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
 \mu_{\mathrm{NA}}(\overline Y)
 =\mu_{\mathrm{poly}}(X)<\infty.
 \label{eq:p055-equal-mass}
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}
 \mu_{\mathrm{NA}}(\overline Y\setminus U)=0
 \label{eq:p055-zero-source-boundary}
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
 \overline h_*\mu_{\mathrm{NA}}=j_*\mu_{\mathrm{poly}}
 \label{eq:p055-global-pushforward}
\end{equation}
as finite positive Radon measures on \(\overline X\), where
\(j_*\mu_{\mathrm{poly}}\) denotes extension by zero across
\(\overline X\setminus X\).

Consequently, in a polarized realization for P05.5, assume that
\[
 \mu_{\mathrm{NA}}=\MA_\theta(u),\qquad
 \mu_{\mathrm{poly}}=\MA_{\mathrm{poly}}(a),
\]
that \eqref{eq:p055-local-pushforward} holds, and that established mass
formulae give
\[
 \mu_{\mathrm{NA}}(\overline Y)=(L^d)
 =\deg(\gamma)=\mu_{\mathrm{poly}}(X).
\]
Then the global comparison is \eqref{eq:p055-global-pushforward}.  This
consequence asserts none of the required realization, polarization,
plurisubharmonicity, local-comparison, or mass-formula hypotheses.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
We first record the measure-theoretic fact used below.  If
\(q:Z\to W\) is a proper continuous map between locally compact Hausdorff
spaces and \(\lambda\) is a finite positive Radon measure on \(Z\), then
\(q_*\lambda\) is Radon.  Indeed, such a map is closed: if \(F\subseteq Z\)
is closed and \(w\notin q(F)\), choose a compact neighbourhood \(K\) of
\(w\); then
\[
 \operatorname{int}(K)\setminus q\bigl(F\cap q^{-1}(K)\bigr)
\]
is an open neighbourhood of \(w\) disjoint from \(q(F)\).  For a Borel set
\(B\subseteq W\), inner regularity of \(\lambda\) supplies, for each
\(\varepsilon>0\), a compact set \(C\subseteq q^{-1}(B)\) such that
\(\lambda(C)>\lambda(q^{-1}(B))-\varepsilon\).  The compact set
\(q(C)\subseteq B\) then proves inner regularity of \(q_*\lambda\).  For
outer regularity, choose an open set \(V\supseteq q^{-1}(B)\) with
\(\lambda(V)<\lambda(q^{-1}(B))+\varepsilon\).  Since \(q\) is closed,
\[
 G:=W\setminus q(Z\setminus V)
\]
is open, contains \(B\), and satisfies \(q^{-1}(G)\subseteq V\).  Hence
\((q_*\lambda)(G)<(q_*\lambda)(B)+\varepsilon\).  Finiteness gives local
finiteness, completing the proof of the fact.

The restriction \(h:U\to X\) is proper.  Indeed, if \(K\subseteq X\) is
compact, then \(K\) is compact in \(\overline X\), so
\(\overline h^{-1}(K)\) is compact in \(\overline Y\); it is contained in
\(U\) and equals \(h^{-1}(K)\).  The restriction of a Radon measure to an
open subspace is Radon, so the preceding fact shows that the pushforward in
\eqref{eq:p055-local-pushforward} is Radon.

Evaluating \eqref{eq:p055-local-pushforward} on the whole space \(X\) gives
\[
 \mu_{\mathrm{NA}}(U)
 =h_*\bigl(\mu_{\mathrm{NA}}|_U\bigr)(X)
 =\mu_{\mathrm{poly}}(X).
\]
Together with \eqref{eq:p055-equal-mass}, positivity and finite additivity
give
\[
 0\leq\mu_{\mathrm{NA}}(\overline Y\setminus U)
 =\mu_{\mathrm{NA}}(\overline Y)-\mu_{\mathrm{NA}}(U)=0,
\]
which proves \eqref{eq:p055-zero-source-boundary}.

Define first a finite positive Borel measure \(\nu\) on \(\overline X\) by
\[
 \nu(B):=\mu_{\mathrm{poly}}(B\cap X)
 \qquad(B\subseteq\overline X\text{ Borel}).
\]
Thus \(\nu\) is the measure-theoretic extension by zero; we do not yet need
to invoke its regularity.  For every Borel set \(B\subseteq\overline X\),
the boundary nullity, the equality \(U=\overline h^{-1}(X)\), and
\eqref{eq:p055-local-pushforward} give
\begin{align*}
 (\overline h_*\mu_{\mathrm{NA}})(B)
 &=\mu_{\mathrm{NA}}\bigl(\overline h^{-1}(B)\bigr)\\
 &=\mu_{\mathrm{NA}}\bigl(\overline h^{-1}(B)\cap U\bigr)\\
 &=\mu_{\mathrm{NA}}|_U\bigl(h^{-1}(B\cap X)\bigr)\\
 &=\mu_{\mathrm{poly}}(B\cap X)=\nu(B).
\end{align*}
Since \(\overline h\) is proper, the fact proved at the start shows that its
pushforward of \(\mu_{\mathrm{NA}}\) is Radon.  The equality just proved shows at
once that \(\nu\) is Radon and is precisely the measure
\(j_*\mu_{\mathrm{poly}}\) appearing in the statement.  This proves
\eqref{eq:p055-global-pushforward}.  The final assertion follows by
substitution of its expressly assumed local comparison and mass identities.
\end{proof}
