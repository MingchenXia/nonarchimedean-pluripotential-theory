\begin{proof}[\Cref{thm:p031-lfs-envelope-pullback}]
Pullback preserves the unit-logarithm presentations in
\cite[\S7.9]{GJR26}. Every base minorant therefore pulls back to a cover
minorant, giving
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:p031-lfs-envelope-easy-inequality}
 s_{\mathrm{LFS},U}\circ\pi\leq s_{\mathrm{LFS},E_U}.
\end{equation}
Conversely, let $u$ be a cover minorant and fix $e\in U_i$. The individual
sheet function $b_i:=u|_{U_i}\circ\sigma_i$ is LFS on $U$, lies below
$f$, and satisfies $b_i(\pi(e))=u(e)$. Hence
$u(e)\leq s_{\mathrm{LFS},U}(\pi(e))$. Taking the supremum over cover
minorants proves the reverse inequality. A base minorant pulls back,
and any cover minorant gives a base minorant by restriction to one sheet;
thus emptiness also agrees. No maximum of the sheet functions is needed.
\end{proof}
