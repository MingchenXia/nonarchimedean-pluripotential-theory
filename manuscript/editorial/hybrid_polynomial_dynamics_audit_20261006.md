## Mathematical scope

The family is meromorphic at zero, nonlinear, regular, and has fixed
forward/inverse degrees at least two; the induced C((t)) map has the
same regularity and degrees. Classical dimension/degree balance is an
imported geometric input. Combined homogeneous tuples must be nowhere
zero; they supply semipositivity of degree-normalized metrics on O(1).
The local max escape potential differs from the global relative weight
by the reference potential. Both curvature terms are retained.

The parameter-independent approximation index and the positive hybrid
factor turn the displayed logarithmic uniformity estimate into a
continuous hybrid metric. Projective Monge–Ampère continuity then gives
the projective probability limit. A common **closed** hybrid support
and extension by zero of a compactly supported cutoff give the affine
weak limit. The affine projection itself is not called proper.

The complex Guedj–Sibony maximum/product identity is attributed to its
read author layout. Non-Archimedean class membership, escape ranks and
maximum/product identification remain explicit inputs. The new
Irokawa v1 uniformity proof is not certified: its map-coefficient-only
covering parameters fail the displayed regular-family test. Neither
that failure nor the rank-coordinate diagnostic refutes its main
measure convergence theorem or constitutes a complete repaired proof.

For Hénon maps the corrected total-exponent leading term is the direct
Jacobian determinant identity with denominator **-log r**. The earlier
2023 v2 formula fails at a(t)=t; the 2025 published full text was not
obtained, and no diagnostic is extended to that edition. One-step
eigenvalues do not replace the derivative cocycle; no first-exponent
asymptotic enters the book. Canonical problem statuses and the general
