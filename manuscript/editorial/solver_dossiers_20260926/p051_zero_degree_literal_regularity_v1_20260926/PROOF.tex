% P05.1 zero-degree literal regularity proof, 26 September 2026.

\begin{proof}
Refine the finite structure so that $\gamma$ is affine on every edge, and
insert a rational vertex in any component having no vertex.  The
one-dimensional polyhedral Monge--Amp\`ere formula gives
\[
 \deg(\gamma)
 =\sum_v\sum_{e\ni v}[\mathbf X](e)s_{e/v}(\gamma).
\]
The two outgoing-slope contributions of every bounded edge cancel.  Hence
\begin{equation}
 \deg(\gamma)
 =\sum_{e\ \mathrm{unbounded}}
   [\mathbf X](e)s_e^\infty(\gamma).
 \label{eq:frontier-polyhedral-zero-degree-ends}
\end{equation}
Every summand is strictly positive because $\gamma$ is positive at
infinity.  Since the structure is finite,
\eqref{eq:frontier-polyhedral-zero-degree-ends} shows that
$\deg(\gamma)=0$ exactly when there is no unbounded edge, which is equivalent
to compactness of $\mathbf X$.

Assume these conditions.  Let $f\in\mathrm{PC}(\mathbf X,\gamma)$ and fix a
connected component $C$.  Compactness gives a maximum value $M$ of $f$ on
$C$.  Ordinary convexity on an edge shows that a maximum occurring in its
relative interior is also attained at an endpoint, so choose a vertex $v$
with $f(v)=M$.

Let $u_{e/v}$ and $w_e=[\mathbf X](e)>0$ be the primitive outgoing direction
and balancing weight of each edge germ at $v$.  For every sufficiently small
$t>0$, put $x_e(t)=v+tu_{e/v}$.  The balancing identity
$\sum_e w_eu_{e/v}=0$ makes $v$ the barycentre of the $x_e(t)$ with
coefficients $w_e/\sum_jw_j$.  Polyhedral convexity and maximality give
\[
 M=f(v)
 \leq\frac{1}{\sum_jw_j}\sum_e w_ef(x_e(t))
 \leq M.
\]
All weights are positive, so $f(x_e(t))=M$ for every incident germ and every
sufficiently small $t$.  A convex function on an interval which is constant
on an initial segment and bounded above by the same constant is constant on
the whole interval.  Thus $f=M$ on every edge incident to $v$.  Propagation
across the connected finite graph proves that $f$ is constant on $C$.

Apply this conclusion first to $f=\gamma$.  Its constant value on each
component is rational because $\gamma$ is a literal piecewise-affine
function.  Now fix $u\in\mathrm{PA}_{\mathbf Z,b}(\mathbf X)$ and set
\[
                 m_C:=\min_{x\in C}(\gamma(x)+u(x)).
\]
Every admissible envelope competitor is constant on $C$ and is at most
$m_C$, while the componentwise constant function with value $m_C$ is itself
an admissible competitor.  This proves
\eqref{eq:frontier-polyhedral-zero-degree-envelope}.

Refine once more so that $\gamma+u$ is affine on every edge.  Its minimum
occurs at a rational vertex and is therefore rational.  Hence the
componentwise constants $m_C$ belong to
\[
                 \mathrm{PAPC}_{\mathbf Z}(\mathbf X,\gamma).
\]
Every literal obstacle therefore has a literal polyhedrally convex envelope.
The regularity criterion in
\cite[Theorem~6.4 and Definition~6.5]{BMPS26} completes the proof.
\end{proof}
