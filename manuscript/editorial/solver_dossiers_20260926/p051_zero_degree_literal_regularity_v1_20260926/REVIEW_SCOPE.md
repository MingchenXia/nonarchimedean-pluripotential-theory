# Review scope

Review the following claims.

1. Bounded-edge contributions cancel in the degree formula, leaving the
   positive weighted sum of slopes on unbounded ends.
2. A finite structure has no unbounded edge exactly when the underlying
   space is compact.
3. On a compact connected balanced graph, the balancing barycentre at a
   maximum vertex forces the function to equal its maximum on every incident
   germ.
4. One-variable convexity propagates that equality along each entire edge,
   and graph connectivity propagates it through the component.
5. A literal piecewise-affine function on a compact finite rational
   subdivision has a rational minimum.
6. The componentwise minimum is the exact envelope and belongs to the
   literal polyhedrally convex class.

Do not read the theorem as a positive-degree classification.  The remaining
one-dimensional case has higher-dimensional local relation cones and places
the reference slope vector on a proper dual face at every positive-mass
vertex.
