# P05.1 noncompact literal nonregularity: sources

## Primary source

Botero--Mazzon--Pille-Schneider, *Monge--Ampere measures on balanced
polyhedral spaces*, arXiv:2603.08664v1.

- Definitions 2.1--2.4 place every rational polyhedral space inside a fixed
  lattice vector space and require a finite rational polyhedral structure.
- Lemma 2.9 supplies finite rational simplicial refinements.
- Definition 2.28 requires integral linear parts and rational constants in
  the literal piecewise-affine class.
- Definitions 3.14 and 3.23 define the bounded-growth convex and
  regularizable classes.
- Definition 4.15 says that positivity at infinity is positivity of the
  recession slope in every nonzero recession direction; the following
  sentence identifies this with proper growth to plus infinity.
- Theorem 6.4 and Definition 6.5 identify regularity with the requirement
  that every bounded literal piecewise-affine obstacle have a regularizable
  envelope.

## Internal comparison

- `thm:frontier-polyhedral-one-dimensional-literal-classification` gives the
  earlier one-dimensional classification by edgewise roofs.
- `thm:frontier-polyhedral-affine-orthogonality` proves complete-affine
  regularity only in the book's explicit real scalar extension.  The present
  theorem explains why no literal integral-slope version is possible.

## Scope

The proof uses only the finite embedded rational polyhedral structure, not
balancing or pure dimensionality.  It settles the literal noncompact part of
P05.1 in every dimension.  It makes no claim about real-PA regularity,
compact higher-dimensional spaces, or orthogonality.
