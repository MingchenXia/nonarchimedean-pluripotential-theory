# P05.1 noncompact literal nonregularity: review scope

Date: 26 September 2026

The review covers the theorem and proof in `STATEMENT.tex` and `PROOF.tex`,
their source boundary in `SOURCES.md`, and their live placement in Chapter
72.  It checks the construction of the vertex-cone obstacle, exactness of
the envelope, the integral-slope separation, exceptional initial structures
with lineality, disconnected spaces, the finite inventory, compilation, and
rendered output.

The review does not claim a model-verifier result, a theorem about compact
higher-dimensional literal regularity, a higher-dimensional real-PA
classification, or any new orthogonality result.
