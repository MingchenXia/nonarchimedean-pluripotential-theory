# Review scope

Review the following claims.

1. Strict positivity on the normalized local relation cone permits one
   common integer `N` for all relations.
2. The star obstacle is continuous at the central and boundary vertices and
   has only integral slopes.
3. The candidate envelope satisfies every local relation inequality at the
   central vertex and every ordinary edge-convexity inequality at the
   boundary of the star.
4. Endpoint chords on all incident segments, together with equality to the
   obstacle off the star, prove maximality of the candidate.
5. The integral left derivative at `t=R/N` yields the stated uniform
   separation in two exhaustive cases.
6. Positive total degree on a local-rank-one graph produces a vertex where
   strictness holds.

Do not read the corollary without its positive-degree hypothesis.  Do not
read the packet as a classification of higher-rank local cones or of
higher-dimensional balanced spaces.
