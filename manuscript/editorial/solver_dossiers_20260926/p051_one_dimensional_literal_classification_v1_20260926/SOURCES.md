# P05.1 one-dimensional literal regularity classification: sources

## Primary source

Botero--Mazzon--Pille-Schneider, *Monge--Ampère measures on balanced
polyhedral spaces*, arXiv:2603.08664v1.

- Definition 2.28 fixes integral linear parts and rational constants in the
  literal piecewise-affine class.
- Definitions 3.14 and 3.23 define the bounded-growth convex and
  regularizable classes.
- Theorem 6.4 and Definition 6.5 identify regularity with the requirement
  that every bounded literal piecewise-affine obstacle have a regularizable
  envelope.

## Internal dependencies

- `thm:frontier-polyhedral-one-dimensional-real-regularity` supplies the
  repaired real-scalar comparison but is not used to infer the literal
  result.
- `thm:frontier-polyhedral-zero-degree-integral-regularity` proves that
  degree zero is equivalent to compactness and gives literal regularity in
  that case.

## Scope

The theorem completely classifies the literal regularity property for
one-dimensional balanced rational polyhedral spaces with finite structure.
It makes no higher-dimensional regularity claim and does not repair the
coefficient mismatch in the source as printed.
