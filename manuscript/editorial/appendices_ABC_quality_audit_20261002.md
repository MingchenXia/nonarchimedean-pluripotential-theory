Appendix A now excludes infinite values on nonzero field elements in the
definition of a Krull valuation. Its integral-closure criterion has exact
Huneke--Swanson locators. The monomial example proves both inclusions
without an uncited monomial theorem. Rees algebras are distinguished from
finite-type test configurations, and a common denominator is required for
integral regrading. Normalized blowups require a nonzero ideal, while the
degree-zero normalization is identified correctly on a nonnormal base.
The injectivity condition for a characteristic-zero universal
homeomorphism is stated on the ring map. The line-bundle norm includes
the determinant of the finite algebra in its denominator. Roberts--Singh
exponents now satisfy N >= p, so no negative power of zero is formed.

Appendix B replaces a potentially misleading graded-pushforward shortcut
by a chart and valuation-ring proof of the Rees-divisor criterion.
Blowup divisors require a nonzero ideal. The alteration example requires
projectivity before claiming that the Stein birational arrow is projective.
The domination, flattening, finite-free and Stein inputs retain their exact
Stacks hypotheses. De Jong's regular source and the perfect-field smooth
source are distinguished, and projectivity is deduced using the graph in
the projective compactification.

Appendix C matches analytic coherence to the book's |f|^2 exp(-phi)
weight, gives the one-pole radial calculation, and identifies the
degree-zero birational transformation rule separately from local
vanishing. Algebraic subadditivity uses DEL's ideal Variant 2.5; extension
from rational to real exponents preserves the finitely many floors on a
joint resolution. The normalized graded-ideal limit now assumes full
support and includes its nonzero-index Fekete proof. A parity example
shows why supported semivaluations need the stated divisibility-net
convention. Asymptotic test ideals require the displayed noetherian
hypothesis. Frobenius roots and subadditivity work over regular F-finite
rings; the geometric discrepancy comparison is restricted to the smooth,
algebraically closed setting verified by Canton. The real-exponent Skoda
identity has a complete Frobenius-root calculation. HY Theorem 2.1 is
identified as a related containment, rather than the exact identity.
Fixed-twist statements specify integral tensor powers. Characteristic-zero
multiplier ideals, positive-characteristic test ideals and comparison
after spreading remain separate constructions.
