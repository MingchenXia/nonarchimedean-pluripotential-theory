## Reader path and mathematical limits

- **D.8.3--D.8.4:** homogeneous Grothendieck/Lascoux/atom packets,
  minimal homogenization and layers, ordinary operator conventions,
  finite-box divided-power complements, exact all-field volume
  realizations, and support consequences. A toric quartic has
  Lorentzian volume realization and Hard Lefschetz in its smaller
  apolar algebra but fails the middle Hodge--Riemann sign.
- **F.9.4--F.9.5:** literal quotient-flag sequences and creation states,
  generated whole rows/co-rows, nonnegative graph divergence with a
  common total-Chern parameter, balanced packet ranks, integral
  incidence extraction, and the multiplication-annihilator construction
  of a full Chern moment algebra. Positive inverse certificates require
  the full Hodge package; a monic relation alone is insufficient.
- **G.9.9--G.9.10:** the minimum-circuit tropical convention, generating
  witnesses on rays, padded finite matroid and equivariant Chern
  certificates, full tropical moment algebras, and generated-Chern
  nonvanishing through joint rank functions and all-subset inequalities.
  Geometric Chow classes and nonrepresentable fan classes keep distinct
  scopes.
- **L.80:** exact source versions, return-stamped dependencies, sign and
  field conventions, the Kaveh--Manon flag-layer discrepancy, and
  classical boundary-descent/combinatorial Hodge foundations.

All-field incidence realization does not assert an integral generic
section zero scheme, smooth realization in positive characteristic, or
universal volume preservation by each individual divided difference.
The scalar/apolar specialization does not automatically inherit the
full moment algebra's Hodge package. Smaller direction sets, nonflat
base change, raw substitution and normalization need separate checks.
The Cattani--Kaplan--Schmid descent foundation is cited, not independently
reproved. No arbitrary exact category of tropical bundles or determination
of all historical tropical-cohomology questions is asserted.
