# Dependency audit

Date: 13 September 2026

## Mathematical dependencies

1. The finite ambient-star × convex-polyhedral comparison theorem
   `thm:p071-finite-star-polyhedral-collapse`, including its
   junction-convex envelope on a compact core and its quantitative one-step
   smoothing estimate on a prescribed smaller core.
2. The Stone × convex-polyhedral comparison theorem
   `thm:p071-stone-polyhedral-collapse` for the away-from-node reduction.
3. Elementary compact-Hausdorff quotient descent, countable Boolean
   algebras, compact metrizable zero-dimensional spaces, and finite clopen
   partitions.
4. The standard compact-metric upper regularization of an upper
   semicontinuous extended-real function.

## New deductions proved here

- A countable family of local Stone factorizations admits one common compact
  metrizable zero-dimensional quotient after a compact clopen shrinking.
- Upper semicontinuity descends through that quotient, including at
  minus-infinity values.
- The star–polytope junction-convex envelope is nonexpanding in the uniform
  norm and therefore preserves continuity in the Stone parameter.
- Fibrewise maxima over the atoms of a finite clopen partition preserve both
  branchwise joint convexity and the product junction inequality.
- Quantitative finite-family smoothing plus a summable tail correction gives
  a single decreasing sequence on one fixed smaller product neighbourhood.

## Firewalls

- The affine structure on the Stone factor is locally constant.
- The star has finitely many branches and is induced by one ambient vector
  space; no nonambient calibration is asserted.
- The polyhedral factor lies in one convex chamber, though it may be
  unbounded, lower-dimensional, or zero-dimensional and may include faces
  and corners.
- Convexity is joint in every star/polyhedral branch chamber, not merely
  separate.
- The proof imports the quantitative one-step smoothing estimate, not merely
  the final sheaf equality of the dependency theorem.
- The result narrows but does not retire P07.1.
