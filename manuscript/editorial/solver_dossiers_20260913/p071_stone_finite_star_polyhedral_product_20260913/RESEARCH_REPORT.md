# P07.1 Stone–finite-star–polyhedral product collapse

Date: 13 September 2026

## Result

The theorem treats the simultaneous product

    X = Z × S × P,

where Z is an arbitrary locally compact Hausdorff clopen-base space with
locally constant affine structure, S is a finite ambient real-affine star,
and P is one convex polyhedron.  For generators that are locally independent
of Z and jointly convex in the star and polyhedral variables, the first
decreasing-limit stage, the full generated psh sheaf, and the explicit
Stone-factorized fibrewise comparison sheaf coincide.  Every germ admits a
decreasing smooth-generator approximation on one fixed smaller product
neighbourhood.

## Mechanism

All members of a decreasing sequence factor through one compact metrizable
zero-dimensional quotient of the Stone parameter.  Compact-metric upper
regularization gives continuous decreasing majorants.  Fibrewise application
of the finite-star–polyhedral junction-convex envelope preserves their order
and varies continuously because that envelope is nonexpanding in the uniform
norm.  Nested finite clopen partitions then replace the Stone parameter by
finitely many comparison functions.  The existing quantitative one-step
finite-star–polyhedral smoothing applies to this finite family on a common
core, and summable tail constants restore exact monotonicity.
