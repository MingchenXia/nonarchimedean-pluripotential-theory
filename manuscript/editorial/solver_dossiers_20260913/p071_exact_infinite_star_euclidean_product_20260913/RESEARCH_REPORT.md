# Research report

Date: 13 September 2026

## Target

The candidate treats the next unresolved P07.1 product model:

- the exact cofinite-branch CLD star `X_B`, with arbitrary nonempty branch
  set;
- one active open convex Euclidean factor;
- jointly convex branch--Euclidean generators, including mixed Hessian
  terms;
- the exact finite-activity and node-balance conditions stated in the
  theorem.

The comparison sheaf uses branchwise extended-real joint convexity, exact
trace at every included `-infinity` endpoint, one common countable activity
set near the node, and a reciprocal-distance junction Jensen inequality
with a Euclidean barycentre constraint.

## Mechanism

The proof first takes the lower partial Legendre transform in the Euclidean
variable on a compact convex core.  Each fixed dual covector then gives a
scalar profile in the already verified exact-star comparison sheaf.  The
exact-star slope budget permits truncation to finitely many active branches.
Local biconjugacy reconstructs the explicit decreasing majorants

`max(u_b(t,y), c(y)-n)`

on retained branches and `c(y)` elsewhere.  Compactness reduces the dual
supremum to finitely many covectors at each stage.  Exact-star harmonic
smoothing, lifted affine Euclidean terms, a regularized maximum, and a
summable tail correction produce a genuinely decreasing sequence of exact
product generators on one fixed neighbourhood.

The terminal endpoint uses the same transform with a finite interior slice
as the truncation floor.  The node value `-infinity` and ordinary interior
points are treated separately.

## Scope

This is a book-local theorem for the explicitly defined exact product
generator sheaf.  It does not claim a result for polyhedral factors with
faces or corners, a simultaneous Stone parameter, separately convex
generators, non-CLD infinite stars, or nonambient branch structures.  It
therefore narrows P07.1 without retiring it or changing the active-atom
count.
