## Mathematical changes

- G.9.11 fixes finite rational pure balanced cycles with positive integer facet weights, positive divisors tested against all positive curves, and the raw top-intersection convention with coefficients `d! a_n/n!`. The attributed positivity theorem requires one facet satisfying every block-subset inequality. The global polymatroid criterion uses both projection-purity and facet selection; translation-admissibility is stronger.
- The first worked example has positive support but fails translation-admissibility, and its volume Hessian has two positive eigenvalues. The second preserves a translation-admissible irreducible tropicalization's support under reweighting, but the new weighted cycle has polynomial `5s²+8st+5t²`, again non-Lorentzian. Neither example refutes the volume theorem for an irreducible algebraic cycle.
- G.9.12 defines the unit-weight augmented fan of an arbitrary caged integral polymatroid, including its strict cone-compatibility inequality and multisymmetric lift. The attributed theorem controls support and Lorentzian coefficients for all positive divisors; zero-or-one multidegrees are asserted only for standard tropical hyperplanes. Rank-zero and degree-one conventions are explicit.
- The Hodge argument retains compatible projective refinement, nonempty strictly convex cones, a positive fundamental weight and hereditary star conditions. The rank-two example demonstrates why factorial normalization is necessary. The full multiplication annihilator uses complementary degree `r-k`; a wonderful compactification is asserted separately for representable arrangements.
- L.81 distinguishes recession replacement of divisors from replacement of cycle facets, generated big nef classes from ample classes, the deficient algebraic-image case from the positive generic-degree case, and the controlling FS preprint numbering from the published edition. It records the EL complementary-degree correction and two ADH proof/formula issues without rejecting the correctly formulated Hodge results.
