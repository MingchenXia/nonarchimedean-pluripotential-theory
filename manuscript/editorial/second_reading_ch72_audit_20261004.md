  locally Fubini–Study and weakly smooth conclusions separate. The toric
  invariant positive cases do not solve arbitrary higher-dimensional germs.
- Envelope descent retains its competitor, coefficient, scalar-extension
  and normal-branch hypotheses. Rational LFS closure is not asserted for
  arbitrary real coefficients. A topological defect is not an analytic
  counterexample without a realized psh competitor family.
- The Gauss comparison uses swept boundary mass, algebraizable common log
  models and the defined synchronized bounded completion. Its finite-map
  reference factor is not probability normalization. Full monotone polar
  mass remains distinct from nonpluripolar truncation.
- Polyhedral regularity distinguishes literal integral slopes and their
  real scalar extension. The one-dimensional graph classification,
  complete-affine theorem and separable product theorem do not establish
  general higher-dimensional regularity. Ordinary-current positivity does
  not replace generated-psh membership in the projection recursion.
- Global polyhedral mass completion assumes an actual proper realization,
  local measure equality, semipositive lift and the exact mass calibration.
  Balancing alone constructs none of these inputs. The source's APW
  bibliography entries are in preparation, not available comparison theorems.
- Singular pluriharmonic descent and the residual reverse implication have
  different domains. Scalar ascent of a zero current and positive-current
  regularization remain separate gates. Fixed-carrier and product-chart
  results are not extended to arbitrary analytic fibres or germs.
- Retired hierarchy and metric questions retain their dated counterexamples
  and source domains. Cardinal stabilization is not a uniform countable-stage
  bound; ordinary smooth metrics do not imply rational nef approximation.
- Normalized singular links and ideal-order finite-map calculus are not a
  geometric Monge–Ampère realization. Reeb comparisons retain their fixed
  polarization and invariance; the two finite-energy residuals remain open.
- The full Kähler MP–DXZ comparison keeps its Kähler hypothesis. I-realizable
  zero-mass reconstruction does not produce a general raw representative or
  raw mass. Strict-interior filtration maximality does not dispose of the
  terminal boundary. The Chebyshev benchmark retains positive ideal volume
  and its auxiliary Kähler-current potential.
- Arbitrary-field toric skeletal existence is not general finite-energy
  existence. Proper with ample polarization is already projective. A
  nonproper affine-cone construction and equidistribution do not settle the
  nonprojective residual equation. Recent stability and hybrid claims keep
  their distinct published/preprint, static/degenerating domains.
- The Bedford–Taylor residual distinguishes component-diagonal, smooth,
  specified-cover and weak-approximation results from arbitrary positive
  currents in dimension at least two. Strong order-zero current operations
  do not supply geometric descent or missing approximation.
