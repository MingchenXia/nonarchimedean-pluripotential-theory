% Source audit for the Chapter 18 delta-metric degree correction.

\paragraph{Disposition.}
This packet is a source-faithful correction and synthesis of
\cite[\S\S9.7--9.13]{GK17}; it makes no novelty or priority claim.  It
separates four objects that the previous live wording conflated: the global
piecewise smooth Chern form, the residual codimension-one generalized
delta-form, the conditional ambient codimension-zero Hessian lift, and the
ambient current obtained after intersecting with the tropical cycle.

\paragraph{Exact source map.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \cite[\S9.7, especially (9.7.1)]{GK17}: global piecewise smooth part
 and current decomposition.
\item \cite[Proposition~9.8]{GK17}: the residual part is a generalized
 delta-form of codimension one, uniquely characterized by its residual
 current identity after every algebraic pullback.
\item \cite[Definition~9.9]{GK17}: an ordinary delta-metric requires the
 facewise Hessian to be the restriction of an ambient bidegree-\((1,1)\)
 superform with piecewise smooth coefficients.
\item \cite[Remark~9.10]{GK17}: the ambient superform is precisely a
 generalized delta-preform of codimension zero; the piecewise smooth
 condition alone does not supply it.
\item \cite[Proposition~9.11, Definition~9.12, and \S9.13]{GK17}: the
 codimension-zero and residual pieces sum to the unique first Chern
 delta-form.
\item \cite[Proposition~1.8, Proposition~1.14, Corollary~1.15, and
 Corollary~3.19]{GK17}: ambient extension, stable-intersection restriction
 of corner loci, and the facewise-Hessian plus corner-locus current formula.
\item \cite[Definition~2.9 and Example~2.10]{GK17}: ambient bidegree equals
 coefficient bidegree plus support codimension.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{No-jump audit.}
Frame independence is asserted only after a common very-affine refinement,
where the transition function is an integral affine tropical coordinate.
Residual uniqueness is asserted only with the universal pullback property
of \cite[Proposition~9.8]{GK17}, not from the current on \(W\) alone.
The proof never treats a facewise Hessian on a positive-codimension tropical
cycle as an ambient codimension-zero preform.  It never infers delta-form
compatibility merely from equality of CLD currents.  Finally, the statement
distinguishes the codimension-zero coefficient \(\gamma\) from
\(\gamma\wedge\delta_C\), whose ambient support codimension is \(c\), and
the ambient representative is asserted only on
\(C\subseteq\operatorname{trop}_U(V)\), where the Hessian lift is defined.
