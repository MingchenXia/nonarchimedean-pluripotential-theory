## Dyadic domain and measure route

Li's Sections 4.2–4.3 were freshly reread, lines 1480–1739. The printed
annulus \(R_i\leq|z_i|\leq2R_i\) and proposed "shrunken" annulus (16),
\(2R_i/3\leq|z_i|\leq3R_i/2\), do not satisfy containment. This is a
literal auxiliary-domain issue, not a counterexample to the principal
CY convergence claim.

The [parent-cover derivation](research_dossiers/li_dyadic_parent_cover_repair_20261004.md)
uses full cores \([R_i,2R_i]\) and larger parents \((R_i/2,4R_i)\).
Cores cover the selected interior; parents have bounded overlap and
remain in a larger interior for large \(T\). Global energy \(O(T)\),
a threshold proportional to \(T^{1-m}\), and local probability mass
\(O(T^{-m})\) control bad-core mass. Angular/transverse Poincaré,
convexity of the averaged local psh function, and actual interior
polydiscs give the upper error \(C\sqrt T\). Measurable core assignment
makes the average and Chebyshev deletion precise; geometric and wall
deletions share one mass budget.

Section integration uses the full core. An arbitrarily thin intersection
with a selected compact set would lose the radial Gram bound. Distinct
leading exponent classes give orthogonal angular Fourier modes; the
transverse Gram matrix is positive definite within each class. A uniform
analytic remainder bound absorbs the error in \(L^2\), for arbitrary
coefficient tuples. The weighted error is \(C_{\varepsilon,l}\sqrt T\).
Degree and discarded-mass dependence is permitted at this step and must
be handled by the later order of limits.

The route retains separate probability/raw-volume assumptions, reduced
SNC coordinates, bounded local background potentials, generic coverage
and analytic remainders. It does not infer these from a formal Taylor
series. Zero transverse dimension and zero parent energy are included.
Logarithmic dimension zero is outside this dossier.
