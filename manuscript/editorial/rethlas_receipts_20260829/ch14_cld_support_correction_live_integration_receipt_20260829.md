## Mathematical and bibliographical scope

For a positive closed current of bidimension ((p,p)) over a nontrivially
valued field, the CLD support theorem uses tropical (H^p)-negligibility.  A
product of (q) curvature currents on an (n)-dimensional trivially valued
space has bidimension ((n-q,n-q)), so the scalar-extension argument forces
the exponent (H^{n-q}).  The exponent (H^q) printed in CLD Corollary 16.8.6
is therefore an index error.  The integrated trivially valued
one-dimensional Gauss-point kink is a counterexample to the printed exponent.
The packet does not extend the trivially valued statement from curvature
products to arbitrary positive closed currents.
