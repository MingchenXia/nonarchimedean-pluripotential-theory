the original arbitrary-dimensional P06.2.

polynomial monic division is bounded, extends by completeness, and the
strict tail is solved by contraction. The triangular coordinate change has
a bounded inverse and makes the highest reduced coefficient a nonzero
constant. Normalization is a finite **injected** Tate subalgebra, so applying
it to a field forces its dimension to be zero. No Nullstellensatz statement
is used as an axiom. Analytic point finiteness uses exactly the separately
stated standard affinoid-spectrum foundations.

hypothesis. Spectral-radius decay is established before the tail estimate.
Finite coefficients converge with bounds in the base algebra, so the
finite-module topology is justified as well as algebraic generation. The
source application shrinks using the image of a compact bad locus; it does
not assert that every small source affinoid is proper over an arbitrary
target. The relative-interior characterization and affine tensor calculus
are the stated standard analytic background, not newly proved foundations.

not nilpotent integration weights in a current calculation. It constructs
a reduced closed subspace only to obtain legitimate curve probes. The
current recognition on that subspace is not inferred. Surjectivity follows
locally from a dense complement of finitely many rigid images and a closed
finite image. This is the missing dimensional correction, not a proof of
Thuillier comparison or harmonic descent. The no-isolated-point check uses
strict neighbourhoods and spectral surjectivity to the prime spectrum;
it does not incorrectly give every singleton carrier K-dimension zero.
