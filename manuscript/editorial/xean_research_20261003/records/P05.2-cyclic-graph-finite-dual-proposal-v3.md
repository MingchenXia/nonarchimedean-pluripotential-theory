## A fixed rational graph subdivision and finite partial infima

Refine reference and obstacle spatial complexes together. Positivity
at infinity of gamma excludes spatial lineality of their cells, and
bounded u has zero derivative on each recession direction. Spatial
cells have finite rational point/ray generators with real height costs.
Project vertices to the graph edge parameter and subdivide at their
finitely many rational positions. On a bounded edge interval containing
no projected vertex, fibre vertices of each spatial cell should move
affinely in the parameter, with rational spatial coefficients. For a
dual p, the fibre infimum

    F_p(t)=inf_z(f(t,z)-p(z))

should therefore be a minimum of finitely many expressions affine in t,
whose t coefficients are affine in p with rational p gradients and real
constants. Its restriction to that interval is concave. Verify empty
cell fibres, all vertical recession constraints and bounded-end values.

Beyond the last projected vertex on an unbounded end, the same finite
description should apply using nonvertical recession rays. Its asymptotic
slope s_e(p) is a finite minimum of affine p functions, with rational
gradients (the ratios of the rational spatial ray coordinates). For
every t>=0 in that tail,

    F_p(t)>=F_p(0)+t*s_e(p).

Every convex scalar minorant k<=F_p has all tail secants <=s_e(p).
Consequently replacing it by k(0)+t*s_e(p) raises it and remains below
F_p. On bounded intervals its endpoint chord similarly raises k while
remaining below the concave F_p. Both replacements increase the outgoing
derivatives at their endpoints. These claims require proof for the actual
rational-endpoint PC class, not presumed raw continuity.

Let D be the common domain of finiteness of partial infima. Since
gamma-C<=f<=gamma+C, it should equal the domain for gamma. The propagation

Thus D has rational normal cones, although its vertices need not be
rational. Establish the same D at every graph point and include all
boundary p values. Treat separate graph components separately.

    c_vw=w_vw/l_vw,

    a_v<=F_p(v),                 (L a)_v<=B_v(p).

All incidence coefficients are rational. The right sides are finite

## Proposed full contact annihilation from harmonic dual values

The following is the main new proposed contact step, still unproved.
At a potential atom x=(v,z_0), put P=H(v,z_0) and let

    C={p in D: a_v(p)+p(z_0)=P}.

It is a convex polytope, and a_v=P-p(z_0) is affine on C. All active
dual planes have p in C. If dim(C)<m, subtract one active affine plane:
the active p differences annihilate a nonzero rational E direction
(rationality needs the spatial-domain proof). This is shared carrier
and function lineality, so the entire local top recursive divisor
vanishes, not just its top-face-interior part.

Suppose dim(C)=m and x is NONCONTACT. At v the fibre infimum F_p(v)
is a minimum of finitely many affine p functions f(v,y_j)-p(y_j),
with rational spatial vertices y_j. If equality a_v(p)=F_p(v) holds
on an open part of C, equality of affine gradients forces y_j=z_0
and equality of constants forces P=f(v,z_0), contradicting noncontact.
Thus a_v<F_p(v) on a dense subset of C; exceptional equality hyperplanes
have empty interior. Prove this also at every degenerate boundary case.

At any such p with positive own bounded-neighbor degree, optimality of

    kappa_v(p)=B_v(p)-(L a(p))_v=sum_e w_e*m_e(p)=0.

On C, every m_e(p) is concave: for a bounded neighbor it is

    m_e(p)=alpha_e+beta_e(p),
    sum_e w_e*alpha_e=0,          sum_e w_e*beta_e=0.
