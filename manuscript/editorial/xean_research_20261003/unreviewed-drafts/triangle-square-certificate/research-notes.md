# Research notes and failed routes

The single-graph cylinder proof was examined through its finite partial-infimum
description, graph incidence polyhedron, exact Legendre reconstruction,
rational spatial domains and exposed-dual quotient contact. It is not used as
a theorem here. With a second cyclic graph the complete affine support domain
is absent, so retaining just its local cones would lose global cyclic support
conditions. No load-bearing extension was identified.

Investigated a finite vertex-value construction with cellwise convex roofs.
Its one-dimensional analogue uses endpoint chords and a greatest feasible
incidence assignment. Rectangles have no jointly convex bilinear interpolation:
the explicit st diagnostic fails a rational-endpoint PCC in the actual product.
A lower convex roof of corner values is a plausible replacement, but there is
no supplied proof of simultaneous feasibility at both sets of graph junctions,
all oblique spatial obstacle faces, remote endpoints and cycles. This route
was not promoted to a theorem or encoded as a misleading finite LP.

Ordinary nested infima over fixed Cartesian sets do commute; that does not
establish commutation or admissibility of separately reconstructed envelopes.
Raw product PC is not replaced by separate scalar convexity. Arbitrary real
affine maxima also do not establish rational spatial domain regularization.

The retained exact upper comparison avoids these gaps by requiring obstacle
contact or tight actual PCC witnesses at a supplied candidate's known cell
vertices. A recession-endpoint PCC argument forces all raw competitors below
the candidate without their continuity. Full recursive coefficients at any
noncontact witnessed vertices must then be computed separately. This yields
a rigorous certificate, but not arbitrary data.

For the concrete two-cycle example, flatten a positive-growth reference near
the product junction via c max(g1+g2,r); raise the central flat region using
eta (r-g1-g2)_+, and add a nonconvex rational tent to the
bounded BC times BC rectangle. The envelope is nonseparable, as is the obstacle.
All first and second divisor coefficients are computed with actual plane
indices. Choosing c=1,r=1/2,eta=epsilon=1 gives fourteen positive contact atoms,
total 128, and exactly zero full integral. The noncontact junction is bounded
by a tight actual PCC of three rational cut endpoints, with coefficients
1/4,1/4,1/2. That junction and the noncontact rectangle centre have zero mass.
This is positive partial evidence and supplies no negative obstruction.

No source cache or older artifact was modified. No additional model calls,
delegation, network, installation, external messages or heavy computation
were used. No supervised heavy-compute service was exposed in tool metadata;
the small fraction-arithmetic and document tasks did not require it.
