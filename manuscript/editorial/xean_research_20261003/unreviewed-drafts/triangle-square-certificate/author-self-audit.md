# Author audit of the partial working note

This is an author audit of an unverified draft, not an independent review.
The requested universal two-graph theorem has not been proved. The minimum
triangle-square case for every permitted reference/obstacle has not been
settled. The reported certificate imposes finite vertex contact on a supplied
candidate or tight actual PCC witnesses for its vertex bounds. Its explicit
example is positive contact evidence, not a negative
obstruction or an arbitrary-pair classification.

The proof was checked at the following load-bearing points.

1. Raw upper comparison uses same-cell PCCs with rational vertices and
   rational recession endpoints at integer distances. Their barycentric
   coefficients and centres are allowed to be real. The remainder constant
   is suppressed by coefficients of size 1/R. Raw continuity is never used.
2. The candidate is a global competitor. In the general certificate actual
   PC is a hypothesis. In the concrete example ambient convexity proves all
   PCC inequalities on every permitted structure directly by Jensen.
3. Every cell is pointed, and its vertex-plus-recession representation is
   proved by a homogenized pointed cone and a compact section. The vertex
   contacts are imposed on the known candidate structure, not assumed to
   exist for arbitrary obstacles or unknown envelopes. A tight PCC from the
   three cut endpoints bounds every raw competitor at (A,A) despite the
   positive obstacle gap there; coefficients are 1/4,1/4,1/2.
4. Real PC PA is shown PAPC against all real local cycles in all positive
   dimensions. A rational incident balancing cone is generated by rational
   weights; corrected rational endpoints prove each generating inequality.
   There is no borrowed PC locality or gluing theorem.
5. The full divisor is reconstructed with saturated tangent lattices.
   Refinement, balancing, commutativity, affine annihilation, and product
   normalization are proved. The real extension is explicit and is not a
   literal integral-slope conclusion.
6. In the example all primitive graph directions have coprime coordinates,
   each star has kernel dimension one, and determinant-one pairs prove the
   correct actual saturated span. Both bounded triangles remain in the
   product. No lift or cycle cutting is used.
7. All spatial cuts of H are rational because the equation is g1+g2=r
   with rational r. Scaling by arbitrary real c does not change that wall.
   The rational tent is added, not formed by an arbitrary real affine max.
8. The obstacle is actual, continuous across the bounded rectangle boundary,
   bounded, nonseparable, and non-PC. The candidate also is nonseparable and
   differs from both the reference and obstacle.
9. Candidate vertices are exactly nine original pairs plus six cut endpoints.
   The obstacle refinement adds only the bounded-rectangle centre. Full
   recursion supports the first fifteen vertices; refinement gives zero at
   the new centre and any artificial points. Every product edge cell, end,
   horizontal/vertical junction fibre and cut-line interior is accounted for.
10. At each cut the actual indices are b/gcd(a,b), a/gcd(a,b), and one.
    Index-two cones really occur. Formula (5) checks every local first ray,
    including zero artificial rays and both signs of the line direction.
    First-cycle balancing and the second coefficient are explicit.
11. At all eight original nonflat pairs the two first-divisor families give
    2 c^2 kappa_V kappa_W. This includes negative edge slopes at B,C;
    using an interior convex-volume formula alone would miss these junctions.
12. Fourteen positive atoms have total mass 128 c^2. Each has zero gap. The
    noncontact product junction (A,A) and the additional noncontact refinement
    vertex both have mass zero, as do their open lift regions. Complete integral
    zero is proved from support,
    separately from total-mass consistency.
13. H+1/j is a decreasing uniform PAPC regularization in the reference growth
    class, and all its measures are identical. No compactification or
    boundary mass theorem is imported.

Source bodies and boundaries were checked by newline-only indexing. A first
excerpt-range draft had overly broad or misaligned boundaries; these were
corrected against the actual identifiers and complete proof endings before
the retained excerpts were generated. Lemma 2.35's source proof invokes
external intersection theory; that external result is not used, since the
quotient-two-fan computation is supplied in full. Source Lemma 3.4 and
Proposition 6.13 are not imported as higher-dimensional or raw-continuity
theorems. The actual round-10 committed run was inspected, and no result from
it is a premise here. Its differing local result-notes file was not used.

The decisive remaining gap is global construction/regularization for arbitrary
two-graph obstacles. The certificate does not repair it. The mathematical
claims above still require independent review; exact execution, hashes and
compilation only document reproducibility.
