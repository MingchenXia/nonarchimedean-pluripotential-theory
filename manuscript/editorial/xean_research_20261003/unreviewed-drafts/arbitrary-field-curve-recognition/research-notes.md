transport to the completed algebraic closure, intrinsic recognition there, then
descent of an already recognized function. The separation is necessary: a
top-measure projection identity alone does not pull back a continuous current.

turns mixed orbit polynomials into traces of powers of product-unit logarithms;
Newton hull sums are C1, and their mollification controls tangent Hessians on
every calibrated one-dimensional segment. The estimate is made on the carrier

At the completed stage, the decisive observation is exactness after small Banach
perturbations. Rational conjunctions and their strict loci remain literally the

domain. Thus each compact ordinary smooth scalar test and its derivative is an
exact pullback from a finite stage. No differentiated completion limit is needed.
