# Research record and failed routes

The source files, earlier tree draft, and finite-dual proposal were treated
as fallible data. The controlling book's one-dimensional graph result was
read to avoid presenting its cyclic case as new.

The tree lift cannot be used on the triangle. With independent formal
directions a,b,c and leaves c-a,a-b,b-c, the walk has period a+b+c.
Closing the actual graph imposes that extra relation. This is a failure of
that construction only, not a counterexample to contact.

The ambient epigraph-hull route was not adopted: graph-PC functions on a
cycle do not automatically possess one ambient convex extension. No
extension was presumed, and failure to find one was not used negatively.

An initially considered exact fibre-integral formula for a junction's
Monge-Ampere coefficient would require additional recursive intersection
work. It is unnecessary in the final argument. Two full-recursion
vanishing lemmas instead annihilate every noncontact coefficient:
lineality for smaller active dual faces, and rational quotient dimension
for full-dimensional ones.

The finite dual proposal suggested a useful direction but proved no
reduction. The argument reconstructs the fibre vertex traces and endpoint
limits, exact tail slopes, common finite domain, graph feasibility and
greatest assignments, rational-gradient elimination, actual raw equality,
and rational spatial generators. Contact uses local exposed dual faces and
coordinate maximality; it does not require independent remote branches.

During document preparation JavaScript string escapes removed some inline
math delimiters. These were repaired before rendering the standalone TeX.
The mathematical display equations were preserved. Build checks record
actual compilation outcomes.

After the general proof was written, an actual nonseparable obstacle was
constructed on the triangular cylinder. Its complete six-node incidence
system, common dual vertices, and envelope formula are stated in the
authoritative argument. The exact full recursive divisor program gives
four positive contact atoms of total 16/5 and zero on every noncontact
candidate vertex. This adds concrete evidence; the general proof is not
inferred from the finite output.

No heavy-compute service was exposed in tool discovery. None was needed or
launched. The complete result remains an unverified proof draft. Original
P05.2 is partial, not solved; P06.2 is not pursued.
