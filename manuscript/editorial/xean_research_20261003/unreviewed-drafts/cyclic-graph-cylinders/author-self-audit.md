# Author self-audit

This is an author check of an unverified draft, not an independent review.
No prior theorem or review is imported. The proof is entirely supplied in
P05.2-argument.md, including elementary convex facts and divisor algebra.

The claimed new scope is every finite balanced rational graph whose local
nonnegative relation cone is the strictly positive weight ray, with all
bounded edges and cycles retained, times every complete affine dimension.
The coefficients are real, while all spatial affine domains are finite and
rational. All permitted references and bounded obstacles are included.
Original P05.2 remains open outside this subclass.

The following critical points were checked against the argument.

1. Actual PCCs: endpoint rationality is used to complete rational slices;
   real coefficients and all real centres are retained for the final
   competitors. Rational shrinking simplices compare raw competitors with
   the continuous constructed envelope. No raw continuity theorem is used.
2. Arbitrary structures: projection of a convex cell cannot pass a branching
   vertex because rank-one positive relations exclude opposite rays there.
   Bivalent straight chains are handled by scalar convexity. Thus remote
   endpoints and cells crossing artificial subdivisions are covered.
3. Finite obstacle data: sliced pointed-cell vertices lie on spatial
   one-faces. Changes occur at projected spatial vertices. Endpoint
   continuity of partial infima is proved separately using finite limits
   and the fixed-z upper estimate. Neither empty fibres nor recession
   constraints are silently removed.
4. Dual domain: every vertical recession ray is constrained; boundedness of
   the obstacle perturbation gives the same constraints for the reference.
   Positivity supplies an interior point, and a fibre linear-growth bound
   supplies compactness. No rationality of dual vertex coordinates is used.
5. Cycles: all bounded-edge incidences enter one finite system. Chord
   replacement increases outgoing endpoint slopes on both sides. Tail
   replacement uses the exact minimum recession slope. Feasibility,
   componentwise maximum closure, attainment, and greatest assignments are
   proved directly.
6. Parametric finiteness: elimination is on a rational-coefficient system
   with real constant terms. The one-coordinate sections are downward
   rays by uniform lowering. Thus node values are finite minima of affine
   functions with rational dual gradients.
7. Raw equality and growth: every raw competitor is bounded by the
   reconstructed candidate. The candidate is PC for every actual PCC,
   lies below the obstacle, and is bounded below by gamma minus its bounded
   obstacle norm. Both directions of equality and two-sided growth hold.
8. Rational walls: normal-cone and supergradient descriptions give spatial
   generators with rational coordinates. Real dual vertices are allowed.
   The domains agree on every vertex fibre. Their vertices occur only over
   the chosen graph nodes, so edge-interior atoms are excluded by the whole
   recursive complex, rather than a maximal-face comparison.
9. All PAPC tests: an incident balancing cone is rational polyhedral.
   Testing rational generators via corrected rational inward endpoints
   proves nonnegativity for every real local cycle of every dimension.
10. Primitive normals: quotient plane indices are retained in the
    balancing and commutativity calculation. Direct product lattices are
    actual, with no replacement of nonsaturated ray-generated lattices.
    The d! normalization is retained.
11. Junction contact: a lower-dimensional active dual face gives common
    fibre lineality. A full-dimensional active dual face at noncontact
    makes the obstacle cap strictly inactive by coercive exposed contact.
    Greatest scalar assignments force the Laplacian to vanish there.
    Positive-weight concavity makes each branch slope affine, with rational
    gradient. The entire germ then factors through a rational quotient of
    dimension m<d.
12. Full recursion: both vanishing lemmas concern every intermediate
    cycle, including all lower-dimensional faces. They use proven
    refinement, balancing, locality, and affine annihilation. No volume
    formula, top-face-only measure, isolated negative atom, or
    regularity-failure dichotomy replaces contact.
13. Ends and operator domain: the explicit PAPC sequence P+1/j decreases
    uniformly and has identical finite measures. The integrand is bounded.
    All tails were included in the construction; the PA operator introduces
    no new boundary measure.
