report. No prior acceptance, running review, hash or production identifier is a
theorem premise. The scope is R16 and H16/E16 on pure curves. Original P06.2 is
not solved; P05.2 is not addressed. All operative proof bodies occur directly

The exact standard foundations admitted as external theorems are: strict
affinoid noetherian algebra/closed ideals and Nullstellensatz; analytic
domain/scalar construction and dimension; smooth/quasi-smooth Jacobian and
boundary composition; finite normalization; the smooth proper curve
semistable-reduction theorem; and curve Serre duality/Riemann--Roch. Their
statements and citations are explicit at each application; primary proof
bodies of the invoked CLD, GR, Thuillier, Wanner and van der Put results are
embedded. This does not pretend to rederive all standard foundations or
every transitive bibliography from axioms.

The main proof is intended to be complete for R16/H16/E16, relative to these
precisely stated external foundations, but that is an author judgment.
Independent review may uncover a gap. Source matching, hashes, finite tree
diagnostics and TeX compilation do not certify mathematical correctness.
