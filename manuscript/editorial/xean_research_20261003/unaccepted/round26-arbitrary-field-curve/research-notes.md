# Research record and rejected routes

The new contribution is the integration, not a repetition of either
supporting gate. The exact chain is: complete valued algebraic closure;
n701 ordinary current transport plus actual pure-curve geometry; n700
intrinsic recognition; published GR harmonicity detection; classical
curve-test equivalence; optional GR/GJR regularity consequence.

The field completion's algebraic closure is proved directly by successive
roots of approximating polynomials, avoiding a characteristic-zero
Krasner assumption. An actual joint extension with any test field is
constructed from its valued algebraic closure. This construction appears
only in the function-level source application check; it grants no
current-ascent step over a transcendental extension of C.

These excluded approaches were assessed logically, not reported as numerical
experiments or analytic counterexamples:

* Invert a current direct image to get the upstairs zero equation. Gap:
  a direct image can vanish without its source current vanishing.
  The exact n701 all-test theorem, rather than inversion, is used.
* Replace scalar transport by a finite same-carrier degree calculation.
  Gap: CLD 14.4.3 is a different operation with a real [E:K] factor;
  CLD 15.3.1 has no such factor. Completion has no finite degree.
* Normalize a curve over the original imperfect K and invoke smooth
  comparison. Gap: normality need not imply geometric smoothness there.
  Recognition occurs only after the checked algebraic-closure extension.
* Apply Wanner directly to arbitrary-field analytic curves. Gap:
  algebraic closure and an actual smooth proper algebraic curve remain
  essential hypotheses of that comparison. n700 supplies the exact
  intrinsic algebraically closed theorem; its scope is not enlarged.
* Pull the original continuous current to every curve or every analytic
  extension. Gap: that blanket functoriality is not supplied. All universal
  curve quantifiers here are imposed on already recognized functions.
* Treat ordinary smooth decreasing psh approximation as a condition on
  recognition. Gap: it is a stronger separate construction. No such
  approximants are produced by this integration.
* Transfer old inconclusive all-K recognition or round23 nullproof.
  Gap: neither is a theorem premise. Only n700/n701 at their exact bindings
  and published standard function-level foundations are used.
* Use connectedness as irreducibility in a finite-map descent proof.
  Gap: that implication is false. The finite-map converse of GR 4.2(6)
  is not used by the new chain.

No theorem outside the strict boundaryless pure curve scope is claimed.
General higher-dimensional recognition, mixed-dimensional current
conventions, non-strict invisible-boundary repairs, and the stronger
ordinary smoothing campaign remain separate. No counterexample satisfying
the original strict ambient hypotheses is produced.

Work uses bounded local source processing and two TeX compilation passes.
No resource-heavy computation is useful or launched, and no supervised
heavy-compute service was supplied. No additional model/subagent, network,
external message/install, credential/environment changes, database work,
or old-artifact write occurred. All new artifacts are in this workspace.
