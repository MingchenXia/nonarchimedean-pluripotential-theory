  We shall also need the notion of psh functions on Berkovich spaces. The following
definitions and facts are taken from [CLD12].
Definition 2.10. A continuous function u on Z is plurisubharmonic (psh) if d′ d′′ u ≥ 0,
i.e., hu, d′ d′′ αi ≥ 0 for all compactly supported smooth (n − 1, n − 1)-forms α on Z.
   We say u is psh-approachable if any point in Z has an open neighborhood U on which
u is the uniform limit of smooth psh functions.
Example 2.11. Let u be a function on Z. Assume that for any z ∈ Z, there is an open
neighborhood U of z and a moment map f on U such that u|U = f ∗ v for some convex
function v on some polyhedron ftrop (U ). Then u is psh-approachable.
