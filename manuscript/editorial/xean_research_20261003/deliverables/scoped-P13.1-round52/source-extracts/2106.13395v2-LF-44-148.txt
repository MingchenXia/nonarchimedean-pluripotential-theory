   The main strategy is to first prove the case when ξ is rational. In this case, write
X = C(V, L) as the cone of a polarized pair (V, L). The test configuration for (X, ξ)
corresponds to the cone of an ample test configuration (V, L) for (V, L). This gives us
a Fubini-Study metric φ on Lan . We show that E NA (ϕ) = E NA (φ). The general result
then follows from approximating ξ by rational ones. We anticipate that this energy be
extended to a bigger class of functions, e.g. the finite energy class, and other related
non-Archimedean functionals be defined in our future work.
