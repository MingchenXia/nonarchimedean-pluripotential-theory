# Authentic cached source evidence

The six copied source files come from the original read-only CLD v2 cache
and the shared primary Ducros and Berkovich caches, as recorded in
sources/manifest.json. Their edition headers and relevant statement/proof
bodies were checked. Content hashes are integrity data, not correctness
certificates. The argument imports primary analytic foundations and
reproves its algebraic, Banach, support, tensor, and completion steps.

embed_sources.py extracts by unique anchored **primary headings**, with
each ending at the next primary heading after the entire selected proof.
It does not cut to an output budget. It records each ending, next heading,
newline range, byte length, and hash in sources/primary-units.json and
embeds all 33 units in the authoritative P06.2-argument.md. The individual
unit files retain the exact source bytes. Form-feed page delimiters are
displayed as newlines in the embedded rendering, and the PDF rendering
alone explicitly normalizes unsupported extraction glyphs. All selected
proof endings are included, including CLD 1.9.4, 3.4.4, 14.1.12,
14.2.2, 14.3.6, 15.1.1, 15.3.1, and Ducros Theorem 3.2.

Applicability is checked in Section 2 of the current proof: ordinary
descriptions use an ordinary open cover, compact cutoffs use only the
compact-set assertion, the ambient-extension lemma is used only in its
closed-subset scope, Stokes uses its arbitrary (n−1,n) form version,
and polyhedrality uses Ducros's dimension **upper bound**. The false
broad dimension equality in CLD 12.2.4 and overbroad fixed-cover-index
compact-support reading of 15.2.9 are excluded.

The dimension proof uses the full CLD 11.2.3 domain/local-dimension
statement and complete scalar total-dimension invariance, with a new
separate rational-descent argument for purity. It does not import flat
completed tensoring or a simultaneous normalization theorem. All moment
calibration degrees use actual finite Artinian algebras; the elementary
dimension/length sum is proved in Equation (22). The extracted CLD
13.3.3 contains a missing zero-dimensional condition in one OCR line;
the proof explicitly supplies the finite Artinian argument rather than
relying on that defective sentence. No source-internal catalogue claim
is used as an independently established recognition theorem.

The extraction/build programs adapt ordinary mechanical tooling from
the read-only finite-Galois workspace. This reuse supplies no mathematical
premise; primary ranges are re-extracted from original caches and checked
against those caches. The current proof and new all-bidegree and purity
arguments are authoritative here. Source-internal theorem references
remain normal primary references; the embedded selected bodies are not
a purported reproof of the entire Berkovich or CLD theory.
