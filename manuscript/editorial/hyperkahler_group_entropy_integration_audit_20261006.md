## Mathematical scope

The exact eight-page Denisi–Oguiso–Onorati–Rizzo–Viktorova
arXiv:2610.05173v1 reports, in Theorem 1.1, dense existence over a
small disc for a non-isotrivial polarized smooth family of irreducible
holomorphic symplectic manifolds with second Betti number at least six.
Each parameter in the dense set admits some birational map with first
dynamical degree greater than one. Its Corollary 1.3 reports the
projective-fibre analogue over an irreducible complex analytic base.
The account retains those hypotheses and explicitly attributes the
statement; it does not certify the entire proof chain.

The entropy convention concerns the spectral radius on H2, equivalently
the first dynamical degree in the stated setting. Full topological entropy
in every higher dimension is not identified with that number. The common
subgroup theorem retains its finite exceptional parameters and finite
index in the very general group. Passing to a power puts a specified
very general element in that subgroup without choosing a morphism of
the whole family. Strong intersection density along period discs is
distinguished from ambient density. Fixed deformation type and the
negative-square bound remain in the lattice argument.

For K3 surfaces the maps are regular, so the fixed-fibre currents already
discussed in Chapter 66 apply to each chosen positive-entropy map. Both
the map and its degree may vary with the fibre. The dense-existence
statement supplies neither one relative automorphism, a common Picard
torsor, uniform normalized potentials nor mixed-product continuity.
Consequently the existing family limit remains conditional on its own
analytic and geometric inputs.

Appendix L.66 retains the distinction between monodromy groups and their
images, the infinite-group qualifier in Oguiso's zero-entropy result,
similarity versus actual-lattice finiteness, and the positive Picard
lattice versus its orthogonal negative part. It records the odd-lattice
isotropic-partner diagnostic and the need for a noncritical local period
parametrization. The complete 2016 Amerik–Verbitsky author copy remains
separate from the changed-title 2020 publication. The controlling published
homogeneous-dynamics proof and its measure-theoretic chain remain external
inputs. No literal defect is asserted to refute the new main theorem.
