# Regular sections and regular zero schemes

Date: 9 October 2026. This addendum clarifies a label in the frozen
diagnostic records and the corrected book integration. It adds no primary
reading credit and changes none of the explicit algebraic computations.

A **regular section** has a zero ideal locally generated by a regular
sequence. Its zero scheme is a regular embedding (a local complete
intersection); the zero scheme need not be a regular scheme or smooth.
These meanings must not be interchanged.

The example used in
[the primary audit](Dubocs_Green_currents_source_review_20261009.md)
is the section $(X^2,Y^2)$ of $\mathcal O(2)\oplus\mathcal O(2)$ on
$\mathbf P^2_{\mathbf Q((t))}$. Its local ideal $(x^2,y^2)$ is a
regular sequence, and its length-four zero scheme is **nonreduced and
not regular**. Its generic blowup has the singular chart
$\mathbf Q((t))[x,y,u]/(y^2-ux^2)$, as already checked.

The first family's name in the frozen exact-diagnostic JSON, “Nonreduced
regular zero scheme with singular generic blowup,” is therefore read as
“Nonreduced zero scheme of a regular section, with singular generic
blowup.” The frozen archive/record bytes remain unchanged; its recorded
ideal, section, basis, length, equation and dimension tests are correct.

The rendered v2 book review caught the same imprecise phrase in a
paragraph heading after sixteen actual pages/eight matching views.
Fresh v3 changes that heading to “A regular section can have a singular
generic blowup.” It does not change the example or proof-boundary
conclusion. All final v3 selected pages receive their own genuine reading
gate, with no partial-v2 reuse credit.

This distinction does not assert a singular blowup of a smooth centre
in a smooth variety over a characteristic-zero field. The separate
$(tX,Y)$ example concerns a singular **raw total model**, even though
its generic centre and generic blowup are smooth.
No general Green identity, CLD comparison, Siu/envelope bridge or LPWZ
density status is changed by this addendum.
