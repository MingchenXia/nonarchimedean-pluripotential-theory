## Complete question statements and current knowledge

**BS.Q6.21:** in the normal integral projective number-field category,
for a continuous quasi-algebraic adelic real Cartier divisor with big
geometry, does all-direction Gâteaux differentiability of the essential
minimum force the defect/inradius liminf criterion? The stronger question
asks whether all-place equidistribution already forces that criterion.
The source states the sufficient direction, the exact nef-approximation
radius, and equivalence under a special semipositive upper bound with
both minima equal to the original essential minimum. Semipositive toric
special cases are retained. The general necessity is not claimed solved.

**BS.R7.16:** can logarithmic equidistribution be obtained from the
defect/inradius approximation and vanishing effective-cycle height gap
without eventual constancy of every approximant measure? The full source
condition requires one common threshold for all places. The supplement
keeps the unnormalized cycle height, dimension factor, approximant mass,
Green convention and the separate derivative/Green-integral equality.
Weak convergence and logarithmic integrability do not by themselves give
uniform control of logarithmic tails. A simple probability example only
calibrates that missing step; it is not an adelic counterexample.

The saved source provides full definitions, exact hypotheses, published

the remaining scope and what would count as a resolution. A targeted
9 October search found no checked general resolution; no exhaustive
