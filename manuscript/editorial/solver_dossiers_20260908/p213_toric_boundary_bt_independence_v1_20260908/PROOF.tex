\begin{proof}
By the invariant-function correspondence (5.5) of \cite{BGJKK25}, there
are unique finite continuous functions $\varphi,\psi$ on $O$ such that
\[
 u=\varphi\circ\operatorname{trop},\qquad
 v=\psi\circ\operatorname{trop}.
\]
The test-form calculation in the proof of Proposition~5.3.10(iv) of
\cite{BGJKK25} uses only this factorization and continuity.  It gives
\[
 [\varphi]=\operatorname{trop}_*[u],\qquad
 [\psi]=\operatorname{trop}_*[v],
\]
and the pushforward commutes with $\dprimeddprime$.  Hence, for
$h:=\varphi-\psi$,
\[
 \dprimeddprime[h]=0\quad\hbox{on }O.
\tag{*}\label{eq:p213-toric-boundary-zero-hessian}
\]

We prove that $h$ is locally smooth with zero Hessian on the tropical toric
partial compactification.  Fix $x\in O$, let $\sigma\in\Sigma$ be the
unique cone with $x\in N(\sigma)$, and choose a basic neighborhood
$W=U(\sigma,\Omega,x_0)\Subset O$, where $\Omega\subset N_{\mathbb R}$
is open, bounded, and convex.  Its dense part $W\cap N_{\mathbb R}$ is the
open convex set $\Omega+\sigma$.  Restricting (*) to this dense part shows,
by the distributional calculation for all second partial derivatives, that
\[
 h(y)=\ell(y)+c\qquad(y\in\Omega+\sigma)
\]
for an affine functional $\ell+c$.

For $y\in W\cap N_{\mathbb R}$ and $w\in\sigma$, the entire compactified
ray $[y,y+\infty w]$ lies in $W$.  Continuity of $h$ at its finite endpoint
therefore makes $\ell(y+tw)+c$ converge as $t\to\infty$.  Thus
$\ell(w)=0$ for every $w\in\sigma$.  The affine function descends through
$N_{\mathbb R}\to N(\sigma)$, and continuity plus density show that on $W$
the function $h$ is exactly that descended affine function.  Equivalently,
$h$ is constant towards every boundary face of $W$ and is smooth for the
standard Lagerberg structure.  Its $\dprimeddprime$ is zero as a smooth
form.

Proposition~2.3.4 of \cite{BGJKK25} pulls this smooth tropical function
back to a smooth CLD function $h\circ\operatorname{trop}$ on
$\operatorname{trop}^{-1}(W)$ and commutes with $\dprimeddprime$.  Since
$u-v=h\circ\operatorname{trop}$, it is locally CLD-smooth with zero
Hessian.  Write $f=u-v$.  Directly from the graded Leibniz rule and the
$\dprime$- and $\ddprime$-closedness of $T$,
\[
 \dprimeddprime(fT)
 =\dprime\bigl(\ddprime f\wedge T\bigr)
 =\dprimeddprime f\wedge T
 =0.
\]
Linearity of multiplication by $T$ proves the assertion.
\end{proof}
