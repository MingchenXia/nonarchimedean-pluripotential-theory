\begin{proposition}[Invariant split-toric zero-current regularity, including boundary]
\label{prop:p062-invariant-split-toric-boundary-zero-current-candidate}
Let $K$ be a complete non-Archimedean field with nontrivial valuation, let
$N$ be a lattice, let $\Sigma$ be a rational fan in $N_{\mathbb R}$, and
let $X_\Sigma$ be the associated split toric $K$-variety.  Write
\[
 \operatorname{trop}:X_\Sigma^{\An}\longrightarrow N_\Sigma
\]
for its extended tropicalization.  Let $O\subseteq N_\Sigma$ be open, put
$V=\operatorname{trop}^{-1}(O)$, and let $S$ be the maximal compact torus.
Assume that $V$ is good and strictly $K$-analytic.
If $h:V\to\mathbb R$ is finite, continuous, and $S$-invariant, and if its
Chambert--Loir--Ducros current satisfies
\[
                         \dprimeddprime[h]=0,
\]
then $h$ is locally a smooth CLD function with zero Hessian.  In particular,
$h$ is locally $\mathbb R$-PL, harmonic in the sense of
Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff, and classically pluriharmonic.
\end{proposition}
