\section*{Scope and load-bearing inputs}

The target is a restricted continuous-pair P21.3 assertion on an open inverse
image $V=\operatorname{trop}^{-1}(O)$ in a split dense torus.  It does not
make a claim for noninvariant potentials, toric boundary strata, nonsplit
tori, arbitrary toroidal charts, or the full P21.3 ambient class.

The proof uses the following precisely delimited inputs.
\begin{enumerate}
\item For the maximal compact torus $S$ and any open $O$ in the tropical
 dense stratum, BGJKK25 equation~(5.5) identifies continuous functions on
 $O$ with $S$-invariant continuous functions on
 $\operatorname{trop}^{-1}(O)$.
\item The test-form computation in the proof of BGJKK25 Proposition~5.3.10(iv)
 shows that the current of such a descended continuous function is the
 pushforward of its invariant analytic function-current.  Its ingredients are
 exactly the factorization in (5.5), continuity, and support of pulled-back
 compact top-degree forms on the canonical skeleton.  The candidate does not
 invoke the proposition's psh positivity conclusion outside its stated scope.
\item Proper pushforward commutes with $\dprime$ and $\ddprime$; this is also
 the book's \cref{prop:current-pushforward-formal}.  The tropicalization of a
 toric variety is proper, and its restriction over an open target is proper.
\item On an ordinary Euclidean ball, a zero Lagerberg $\dprimeddprime$ current
 has all distributional second partial derivatives zero, hence its continuous
 representative is affine.  The identical calculation is recorded in
 \cref{prop:ch16-cell-rigidity}.
\item An affine function of the torus moment coordinates pulls back to a
 smooth zero-Hessian CLD function (\cref{lem:ch19-toric-chart}).  Finally,
 for a positive closed current, a smooth zero-Hessian difference gives equal
 products (\cref{cor:ch16-smooth-h}).
\end{enumerate}

Please reject the candidate if any one of the following occurs: the
continuous-current pushforward calculation has a hidden psh or support
requirement; properness fails after restriction to $O$; zero tropical Hessian
does not imply the stated local affine conclusion; or the cited smooth-factor
corollary fails in the P21.3 bidegrees $0\leq p\leq n-1$.
