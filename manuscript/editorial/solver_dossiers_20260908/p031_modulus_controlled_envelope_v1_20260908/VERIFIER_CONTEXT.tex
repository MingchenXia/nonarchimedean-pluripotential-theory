\section*{Source-scoped verification context}

The candidate concerns a nonempty family $\mathcal F$ of finite-valued
classically psh functions.  It assumes directly that the pointwise supremum
is finite and that the family has the displayed common local modulus.  These
assumptions give continuity of the raw supremum by two elementary inequalities.

The only imported facts are from Gubler--Rabinoff, arXiv:2506.13548v2:
Proposition 4.2(3) says finite maxima of classically psh functions are
classically psh, and Theorem 6.5 says a pointwise limit of a net of
classically psh functions is classically psh when the limit is upper
semicontinuous.  Direct the finite nonempty subsets of $\mathcal F$ by
inclusion; their finite maxima converge pointwise to the raw supremum.

For the stated local-Fubini--Study/semipositive-real-PL application, the same
source gives Theorem 4.3 for semipositive real PL functions and Proposition
4.2(1)--(3) for the local Fubini--Study operations.  Verify that the
candidate neither removes its explicit finiteness/common-modulus hypotheses
nor claims CLD pshness or a full P03 classification.
