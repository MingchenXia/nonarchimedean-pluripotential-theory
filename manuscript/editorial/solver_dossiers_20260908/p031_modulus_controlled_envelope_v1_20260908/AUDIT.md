# Scope audit — P03 modulus-controlled envelope criterion

This candidate supplies a sufficient condition for one fixed competitor
family.  It does **not** classify spaces on which every obstacle envelope has
the condition, and it does not change the active count of 48 questions.

The common-modulus hypothesis is used twice and only twice: it makes the raw
pointwise supremum continuous, and that upper semicontinuity permits the
source's arbitrary-net theorem.  No sequence is extracted from the family.
No assertion is made that the full local-Fubini--Study/PL minorant family is
automatically finite-valued or equicontinuous; those are explicit hypotheses.

The conclusion is classical psh and continuity only.  It makes no CLD-v2
claim and does not use a curvewise equality
$(\sup h\circ\gamma)^*=(\sup h)^*\circ\gamma$, which remains the genuine
higher-dimensional P03 obstruction.
