# P03 modulus-controlled envelope criterion — source ledger

Primary source: Walter Gubler and Joseph Rabinoff, *Classically psh and
pluriharmonic functions on Berkovich spaces*, arXiv:2506.13548v2, cited in the
book as `GR25Classical`.  The frozen local PDF is
`../rethlas_packets_20260830/p031_perfected_unibranch_curve_envelope_20260830/sources/Gubler_Rabinoff_2506.13548v2.pdf`.

The proof uses only the following source results.

- Proposition 4.2(3), pp. 21--22: finite maxima of classically psh functions
  are classically psh.
- Theorem 6.5, p. 34: a pointwise-convergent net of classically psh functions
  has a classically psh limit when that limit is upper semicontinuous.
- Theorem 4.3, p. 22: on a good strict analytic space over a nontrivially
  valued field, a real PL function is semipositive exactly when it is
  classically psh.
- Proposition 4.2(1)--(3), pp. 21--22: the sheaf, logarithm, positive-scaling,
  and finite-maximum operations used to identify locally Fubini--Study
  functions as classically psh.

The continuity argument is elementary and does not invoke an unsupported
interchange of upper-semicontinuous regularization with curve pullback.
