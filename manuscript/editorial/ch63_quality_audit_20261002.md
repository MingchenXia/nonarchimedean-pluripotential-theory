The principal repairs and checks are:

- The model domain is proper, reduced and pure-dimensional, with relative
  normality. Completion first takes the integral extension bundles and
  only then divides their product by the representing exponents. Relative
  nefness supplies positivity; the arbitrary formal product can be signed.
- The original BFJ signature now includes connectedness, matching the
  source. The projective, tropical-spectrum and complex domains retain
  their distinct geometric and positivity hypotheses.
- Additive analytic valuations have x_E = lambda ord_E/b_E, with
  lambda = -log|pi|. The frequently used expression ord_E/b_E is explicitly
  measured in lambda units. Model values and their intersection pairing
  include lambda, while the atomic masses do not acquire that factor.
  Test-configuration and hybrid parameter scales remain separate.
- The first master-diagram equivalence fixes reference model metrics on
  fixed generic line bundles. Vertical rational twists describe differences
  from those references, modulo common domination. Its measure equality
  uses completed formal models, the central multiplicities and rational
  exponents.
- The trivially valued row is the full rational PL correspondence of
  BJ Theorem 2.31(i). Semiample test configurations and rational FS metrics
  form the separate positive subdomain of part (ii); the ample integrally
  closed uniqueness assertion is Corollary 2.32. PL and FS are not conflated.
- The continuous common locus chooses a rapidly convergent model sequence
  with errors e_j = lambda 2^{-j}, then adds 3e_j. The consecutive-error
  inequality proves simultaneous decrease, and 3e_j is a rational
  special-fibre shift in lambda units. Curvature is unchanged. Uniform
  approximation and bounded monotone continuity identify the measures;
  no sequential cofinality of all models is asserted.
- The local differential branch uses a boundaryless strict algebraic
  analytification on its common locus. The GK input retains the complete
  algebraically closed nontrivially valued ground field and local PL
  hypotheses. Discrete models are pulled to the completed algebraic
  closure before that interface is used. An unexplained arrow from smooth
  forms to formal metrics was removed; nefness precedes the positivity
  comparison.
- The BFJ skeletal image is given by both defining clauses: reconstructed
  usc membership, retraction inequality and uniform model-closure on every
  determining complex. Compatible facewise-convex data alone do not give
  the converse. The published skeletal reconstruction supplies injectivity.
- The DXZ inverse-system realization retains positive initial mass in each
  perturbed big class and the projective pseudo-effective rational domain.
  It preserves only the orders, limits and operations proved at the cited
  comparison. Zero-mass levels use the stated separate convention.
- Ideal values are minima in the local ring at the center. Changing
  generators gives both inequalities with nonnegative local coefficient
  values. Support-prime values may be infinite, the zero ideal has value
  infinity and the unit ideal has value zero.
- The projective DXZ--MP--BJ square now has functions as its bottom objects.
  The carrier homeomorphism induces a pullback of functions; it does not
  send a metric to a bare point or identify psh membership by topology.
- The local Gauss-shell comparison retains its ideal normalization, shell
  and swept reference factor. The Reeb branch is a one-way quotient
  recovery on the stated rational quasi-regular locus, with the invariant
  embedding and orbifold data. It is not presented as an unrestricted
  equivalence of the global and affine-cone theories.
- Raw/probability conversion keeps the geometric class, potential and
  positive reference volume fixed. Raw dual energy is V times probability
  dual energy on nu/V. Rescaling the closed mass simplex proves both
  Legendre formulas, the raw contact gradient and the inward derivative.
  Zero masses are allowed; inactive threshold slack cannot replace the
  normalized solution in a mass derivative.
- The model/ray conversion keeps f_D >= 1, reduced discrepancy, the
  independently verified Chapter 62 scalar correction and the lower
  Mabuchi slope bound. It does not infer entropy-slope equality.
- The filtration/ray comparison fixes one smooth strictly positive initial
  weight, its inner products, norm convention and parameter. Its proof is
  explicitly headed as a cited comparison and uses RWN Theorem 9.2 and
  Section 9. The invalid BHJ invariant-theory assertion is not used.
- A blown-up P1 model with bundle q*O(3)-E1 has degrees 2 and 1.
  The divisor E1/2 has raw pairing lambda/2, intersection 1/2 and
  probability pairing lambda/6. At lambda = 2 the first pairing is 1.
  This separates valuation units, degree and mass normalization.
- The product-action example is fully computed for O(d). Degree-m weights
  j/m have raw empirical mass d+1/m, limiting Lebesgue mass d and
  probability density 1/d on [0,d]. Their finite first moments are
  d^2/2+d/(2m) and d/2. Endpoint maximization gives the FS weight;
  its normalized curve profile has raw measure d delta_ord0 and energies
  -d^2/2 and d^2/2 before and after the constant shift. The latter is the
  raw DH first moment. Degree two detects the raw/probability difference.
