## Scaling, homogeneous functions and ideal algebra

Normalization commutes exactly with scaling because the supremum scales.
Dropping the outer factor can change a fixed line-bundle class, without
leaving every FS class. A real-weight homogeneous GF presentation can be
replaced by its zero-weight terms by taking the large-scale limit.

Convexity and the finite upper bound on a positive scaling half-line prove
monotonicity; arbitrary approximants need not be nonpositive. The orbit is
either finite everywhere on that half-line or identically minus infinity.

monomial exponents. Elimination sums omit zero coefficients at poles.

The conductor argument for a coherent fractional ideal is one-way. A
correct counterexample to the converse is -log|m_q| on P2: all prime-divisor
values on the original model vanish, while the exceptional blowup value is

whose logarithm is nonpositive. An initial monomial-convexity justification

nonlinear minimum valuations. The real homogeneous PL span is explicit.

## Cartier, Weil and numerical classes

the coefficientwise subspace topology and a converging net need not have
