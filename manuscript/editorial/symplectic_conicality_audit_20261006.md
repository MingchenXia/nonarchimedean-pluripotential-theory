## Mathematical admission and boundaries

1. For two affine cones with good torus actions over the complex numbers,
   conjugacy of maximal tori in the formal automorphism group and positive
   weight spaces recover an algebraic isomorphism from the existence of
   a formal isomorphism. The given tori can differ in dimension and can
   be extended. The specified original formal map is not asserted to be
   algebraic or convergent.
2. Reflexive form jets require sufficiently deeper ring jets. The
   Artin–Rees and double-dual input is retained. The symplectic-dilating
   group preserves a line; positive form weight permits correcting a
   scalar over the complex numbers. The resulting algebraic isomorphism
   preserves the specified symplectic forms. Kollár Lemma 55's printed
   `Spec` when recovering the projective base is not copied: that step
   uses `Proj`. The uniqueness-of-grading classification is not imported.
3. The known Theorem 6.2 supplies a minimizing Reeb direction inside a
   dilating torus on the symplectic stable cone. It gives existence of
   a K-semistable Reeb choice, rather than minimality of every positive
   form-dilating grading. The earlier weighted four-dimensional example
   remains consistent. No general K-polystability, rational ray,
   divisorial minimizer or hyperKähler metric is inferred.
4. Artin Corollary 2.6 provides a common pointed étale neighborhood of
   formally isomorphic finite-type complex algebraic germs. Its maps
   give local analytic isomorphisms. This application is to spaces and
   does not preserve a prescribed symplectic form or Poisson bracket.
   The pulled-back cone form can differ from the original form, as
   Namikawa–Odaka Theorem 6.3 explicitly says. An analytically transported
   action dilates that pulled-back form. No convergence of the original
   formal map or unrestricted global algebraic cone action is asserted.
5. Metric Theorem 6.3 retains polarized projective smoothability or the
   equivalent projective symplectic resolution in its setting. Theorem
   6.6 assumes the singular hyperKähler metric, parallel symplectic form
   and the source's Donaldson–Sun Conjecture 1.3. Quasi-regularity in
   Theorem 6.16 retains its additional hypotheses; Theorem 6.19 isolates
   completeness of hyperKähler rotations. The weight-two scaling may
   have a finite kernel, distinct from a primitive faithful grading.
6. Nilpotent-orbit and hypertoric automorphism applications remain a
   separate direction. No arbitrary hyperKähler quotient claim is imported.
   General Siu/closure, BJ22's recorded proof gap and A2–A4 are unaffected.
