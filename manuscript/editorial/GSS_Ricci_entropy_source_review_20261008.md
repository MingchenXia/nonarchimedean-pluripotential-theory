## Scope, calculations and defects

1. Keep D=i partial bar-partial=2 pi times the book ddc, geometric volume omega^n/n!, nonpositive div-grad Laplacian 2 tr_omega D, Ric=-D log omega^n, and [omega]=2 pi c1(L). A measure rescaling preserves RCD, while noncollapse uses the Riemannian Hausdorff measure. The completion starts from the regular-locus intrinsic length space; identification with the complex space is a separate assertion.
2. The GSS Proposition 5.1 criterion includes smooth resolution approximants pi*theta+epsilon theta_Y+D u_j, uniformly bounded potentials, a common smooth-reference L^r density with r>1, local smooth convergence, the geometric Sobolev/heat background and form-domain/capacity inputs. The curvature entropy is integral rho log(1+rho) dV_g for the negative Ricci eigenvalue relative to the current metric, not Nash entropy of a fixed-reference volume density.
3. The J-invariant real Hessian part has closed-Kähler energy integral |B|^2=1/2 integral (Delta f)^2. Its gain in the Bochner calculation yields W1,2 control of log(1+|grad f|^2). Spectral recovery needs two min-max passages, exceptional-volume control and no L2 mass loss; the limiting eigenspace must be complete. The bounded gradient truncation Phi_R=e^h/(1+e^h/R) has Phi_R^2/(e^h Phi_R')=1. Remove the zero-capacity cutoff before R tends to infinity. Moser iteration and Honda's full criterion then supply eigenfunction Lipschitz bounds and conditional RCD.
4. GPSS's minimal Laplacian is symmetric, not thereby self-adjoint; use the closed form and Friedrichs realization. Common lower density away from the controlled exceptional set, a subcritical Sobolev estimate, local Rellich compactness and small-volume tails are retained. Honda's glued zero-capacity examples prevent replacing all these conditions with classical regular-locus Ricci or a BE inequality. CCHSTT's earlier negative-Ricci power threshold p>(2n-1)/n is distinguished from the volume-density exponent.
5. Fixed smooth twist regularization yields nonnegative exceptional forms P_i,epsilon, logarithmic weights, and an entropy error bounded by |log epsilon| times the exceptional intersection. Codimension of the exceptional image at least two makes this O(epsilon |log epsilon|). Prescribed divisorial boundary images require a fresh calculation.
6. GSS Section 7.2 needs more than a conversion of Ric>=-omega_j-C theta into Ric>=-A omega_j: adding a multiple of the nonsmooth current does not make the exact twist smooth. The draft's regularized max(q_j,q+delta+delta^2 rho)/(1+delta^2) is an explicitly conditional formal calculation, with the pole correction, exact-class rescaling, global psh-extension and positive background hypotheses. The positive-exponential PDE, uniform reference-density domination and local elliptic convergence still require verification. No full general approximation theorem is promoted here. FGS remains in its projective threefold or separately assumed resolution category; Vâjâitu supplies a distinct local pluriharmonic extension input.
7. On the crepant A1 chart pi(t,z)=(t,t z^2,t z), the ambient pullback determinant is |t|^2(1+4|z|^2+|z|^4), while the quotient-flat adapted determinant is 1/4. The density is L^p against the ambient volume for p<2, but not L^p against the adapted smooth upstairs volume for any p>=1. This diagnoses the unweighted exp(pF) transfer on CCHSTT physical page 7 and the wrong reference measure in GKSS Lemma 5 on page 24. It does not refute a global theorem or an estimate retaining compensating Jacobian factors. If dnu=b dmu, b is L^(1+eta)(mu), and a is L^p(nu), then ab is L^q(mu) for 1<q<p(1+eta)/(p+eta). The smaller exponent and reference measure are essential.
8. The EGZ erratum Section 2.2 withdraws the old global comparison step used for EGZ13 Corollary 2.5. Cho–Choi v2 supplies a newer capacity/envelope route for the exact resolution pullback background. Its global smooth-obstacle GLZ input and the EGZ Lemma 3.2 small negative Jacobian power convert bounded upstairs density in the needed direction. Connected fibres allow descent and Richberg applies once continuity is known. This does not certify approximation for every arbitrary big semipositive form. The GLZ local Remark 5.6 envelope value is false: max(-1,log(sqrt(2)|z1|)-1) is admissible and exceeds -1 at (3/4,0). That defect does not affect the global smooth-obstacle inequality used by Cho–Choi.
9. The GSS geometric implication assumes an ambient Hölder potential. Its finite projection yields a trace potential, short base paths and a finite monodromy chain joining prescribed sheets; one root lift is insufficient. Ambient distance conversion uses a separate subanalytic bridge. The analytic companion used in its Theorem 7 was in preparation and was not acquired. Ordinary Cho–Choi continuity does not replace quantitative Hölder regularity.
10. GKSS v1's singular theorems assume a polarized projective Q-Gorenstein smoothing, and its general-density statement additionally assumes logarithmic positivity. Printed bridges remain unverified: real section coefficients must be complex; tangential gradient control gives intrinsic regularity without the asserted uniform ambient comparison; removing upstairs loops does not remove projected backtracking; fibre transfer needs prescribed endpoints and semipositive metric error control. The direct injective exceptional arc (0,s,5s-12s^2+8s^3) on A1 times a line projects to length 1+2 sqrt(6)/9 rather than one. This refutes that construction's bound, not existence of another good lift or the main theorem.
11. GKSS's bounded-normalized-potential to bounded-SL-matrix inference fails alone: on the smooth nondegenerate conic z0 z1=z2^2, diag(a,a,a^-2) is unbounded with determinant one while the sup-normalized relative potential stays between log(2/3) and zero, and the tensor ratio at [1:0:0] is a^-6. Additional Bergman Gram constraints need a separate argument; the example is not a disproof of a theorem with those constraints verified.
12. The smooth fixed-base stability argument needs Q+sqrt(VQ), not Q+sqrt(Q), unless mass is one. Only the reference Ricci bound is needed for one-sided comparison; both are needed for two-sided identity-marked distance convergence. The cone on P1 and its P1xP1 product have d_omega(0,r)=sqrt(2/a) atan(r^a) and d_theta(0,r)=sqrt(2) atan(r), whose ratio diverges for 0<a<1. Exact smooth Ricci-nonnegative, common-Lp approximations identify that actual distance limit. The forward Lipschitz identity in both GSS Theorem 1.2 statements is therefore false; stability equation (6.13) has the correct sqrt(c)d_theta<=d_omega<=C d_theta^beta direction. This does not refute canonical RCD existence or stability. The smooth-base flow application is one-sided and must repair its changing class before invoking stability.
