## Exact proposed scope

The main generic theorem uses a number-field semiabelian variety with
split toric part, a normal proper toric compactification, big T-effective
geometry, a semipositive monocritical toric metric, and **arithmetic
T-effectivity**. The last condition is attainment of the global roof
maximum at the origin by the distinguished zero-character section; it
is not automatic for a big semipositive metric. The abelian factor is
symmetric ample with its canonical metric. The quasi-canonical case
displays a normalized **mixed** local measure, whose mass is the mixed
intersection rather than the unadjusted full top self-intersection.

The Bogomolov statement additionally requires ample geometry. It explicitly
distinguishes the standard product-of-P1 compactification from a general
fan: the latter requires an independently established curvature–Haar slice
formula, lattice-index normalization and absence of extra boundary mass
for all subvarieties used by the difference-morphism argument. Its
special-point hypothesis is also retained for the converse. Those source
conditions cannot be dropped because the abstract-page summary is shorter.

## Findings from the actually read pages

- Definition 3.3, p. 7: integral support slopes require a stated real
  extension when treating real divisors.
- Proposition 3.6, p. 7: a section over the full tropical space cannot
  have its entire image in the valuation-zero compact unit torus; the
  full toric skeleton section is a different object.
- Definition 3.20, p. 11: Archimedean semipositivity tests the Chern current
  itself. The printed additional ddc of that closed current is not this
  test.
- Definition 3.21, p. 11: a normalized variety-height quotient requires
  nonzero geometric degree before division.
- Theorem 4.1, pp. 13–16: the unit-norm period-transition assertion rests
  on a verbal theta/descent cancellation without the required period
  absolute-value calculation. Changing local sections with fixed lifts
  is different from changing period-lattice lifts. This is a flagged
  input to the induced-metric gluing in Proposition 4.17.
- Proposition 4.18, p. 17: the nef limit needs a variable ample base
  coefficient; choosing an ample toric divisor on a merely proper fan
  needs projectivity or a modified-model argument.
- Corollary 4.19, p. 17: the full top intersection versus its leading
  mixed term omits the binomial coefficient. On P1 times an elliptic
  curve, (D+N)^2=2 D*N. Positivity survives this factor, but probability
  normalizations must keep it.
- Definition 4.21, p. 19: the proposed skeleton-point norm must use a
  multiplicative canonical/unique-Shilov base norm. An arbitrary affinoid
  supremum norm is not enough; the annulus calculation is in the notes.
- Proposition 4.24, pp. 19–20: the displayed transformation law with
  lambda=0 still permits a nonzero additive c_trop period term, so it
  does not imply a constant tropical correction. Rigidification fixes
  an additive constant, not all period slopes. The raw-theta/canonical-
  norm identification and principal-divisor passage remain unverified.
