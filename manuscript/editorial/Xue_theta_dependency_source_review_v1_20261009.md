## Correction of an earlier conditional diagnostic

Remark 3.3, Remark 3.18 and §4.1 use formal Raynaud affine-open splittings,
not arbitrary affinoids. **Lemma 4.2, p. 15** identifies the coefficient
supremum norm with evaluation at the unique analytic point reducing to
the generic point of the good-reduction abelian special fiber. This norm
is multiplicative. Hence the weighted Gauss construction is legitimate
in this intended scope; the earlier arbitrary-affinoid example does not
refute it. This is an addendum to the frozen Xue 20/83-page checkpoint,
not a rewrite of that checkpoint or a main-theorem clearance.

## Remaining distinction: theta representatives and descended metrics

Theorem 3.10 and Proposition 3.19 retain both c(γ) and e_{λγ} in descent.
Example 3.16 gives Picard-zero data with λ=0 and c(γ)=x'^γ in the totally
degenerate case: c(0)=1 does not force all period norms to be one.
Definition 4.9 uses the pullback of the model metric on the good-reduction
base B. The proof of **Theorem 4.10, p. 17** explicitly retains the norms
of the descent factors; its polarized transformation law includes
c_trop(γ). Proposition 3.25 removes an additive period term using a
translation of a **principally polarized** bundle, not rigidification
alone. That normalization cannot simply be transferred to λ=0.

Consequently the inference in Xue Proposition 4.24 from λ=0 to a constant
tropical correction remains unverified. Theorem 4.1's unit-norm period
transition claim remains a separate input to metric gluing. These source
checks do not establish a counterexample to the intended equidistribution
or Bogomolov theorem.

The arXiv version's general rational-lifting proof (Theorem 5.2, p. 19)
forms a meromorphic quotient, then uses a global Fourier expansion and a
nonzero zero coefficient without justifying those steps in the displayed
argument. It is flagged separately; no claim is made about a correction
in the journal version. The explicit translate-product construction of
Proposition 5.6 uses exactly matching tensor descent data and is tracked
under those stronger hypotheses.
