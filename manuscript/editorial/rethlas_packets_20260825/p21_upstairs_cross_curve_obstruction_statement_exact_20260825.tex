\begin{proposition}
Let $K$ be a complete algebraically closed nontrivially valued
non-Archimedean field.  Choose $a,b,c,d\in K^\circ$ with pairwise distinct
residue classes, let $q$ be the standard type--$2$ Gauss point, and put
\[
 X=\bigl(\mathbf P^1_K\setminus\{a,b,c,d\}\bigr)^{\mathrm{an}}.
\]
The analytic units
\[
 f=\frac{z-a}{z-b},\qquad g=\frac{z-c}{z-d}
\]
define a proper tropicalization
\[
 \tau=(s,t)=(-\log|f|,-\log|g|):X\longrightarrow\mathbf R^2
\]
whose image is the full cross.  Its four primitive rays, in the directions
toward $a,b,c,d$, are respectively
\[
 e_1,-e_1,e_2,-e_2,
\]
and the tropicalization of the fundamental cycle has weight one on every
ray.  The functions
\[
 U=2|s|\circ\tau,\qquad V=(|s|+|t|)\circ\tau
\]
are continuous psh and satisfy, as Chambert--Loir--Ducros currents and as
delta-currents,
\[
                 \mathrm d'\mathrm d''[U]
                 =\mathrm d'\mathrm d''[V]=4\delta_q.
\]

Nevertheless there is no positive closed order-zero Chambert--Loir--Ducros
current $T$ of bidegree $(0,0)$ on $X$ whose proper tropical pushforward has
horizontal ray weights $2$ and vertical ray weights $1$.  More generally,
if the four ray weights of $\tau_*T$, ordered by $a,b,c,d$, are
$w_a,w_b,w_c,w_d$, then
\[
                         w_a=w_b=w_c=w_d.
\]
The same obstruction applies to a positive closed delta-current whenever
its restriction to smooth test forms has the displayed ray weights.
Consequently the four-punctured smooth-curve construction cannot solve
\textup{\texttt{prob:frontier-cross-fan-upstairs}}.
\end{proposition}
