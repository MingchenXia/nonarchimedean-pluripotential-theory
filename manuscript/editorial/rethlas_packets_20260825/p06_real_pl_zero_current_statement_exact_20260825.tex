\begin{proposition}
Let $K$ be a complete nontrivially valued non-Archimedean field, and let
$X$ be a good, separated, boundaryless, strictly $K$-analytic space that is
pure of dimension $n\geq1$.  If $h:X\to\mathbf R$ is an $\mathbf R$-PL function and
its Chambert--Loir--Ducros current satisfies
\[
                         \mathrm d'\mathrm d''[h]=0,
\]
then $h$ is harmonic in the sense of Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff.  Equivalently,
the residue class $L_h(x)\in\operatorname{Pic}(\operatorname{red}(X,x))_
{\mathbf R}$ is numerically trivial for every $x\in X$.  Consequently $h$
is classically pluriharmonic in the sense of Gubler--Rabinoff.
\end{proposition}

Verify this proposition and the accompanying proof source-awarely and
hostilely.  In particular, check that the proof does not use a nonexistent
pullback operation for arbitrary CLD currents, and check separately every
use of localization, compact support, boundary avoidance, common refinement
of tropicalizations, tropical Poincare--Lelong, integer divisor weights,
$\mathbf Q$-linear independence of the real coefficients, and the passage
from vanishing of each integral curvature current to numerical triviality.
The fixed-chart cancellation problem is central: a tropicalization may
identify different analytic fibres, so it is not enough to prove a divisor
identity in one coarse chart.  The proof must really refine by the moment
map tropicalizing each arbitrary test form before separating coefficients.
Also check the hypotheses and directions of Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff
Proposition~5.8 and Lemma~5.10, Chambert--Loir--Ducros
Theorem~18.8.4 with $d=1$, Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff
Definition~7.2/Proposition~7.1, and Gubler--Rabinoff Theorem~4.3.  Do not
infer the assertion for arbitrary continuous functions; no novelty or
priority claim is intended.
