Let
\[
 \Sigma=(\mathbf R\times\{0\})\cup(\{0\}\times\mathbf R)
 \subset\mathbf R^2.
\]
Assume that, in the definition under consideration, a harmonic
tropicalization is a continuous map
\[
                  g:W\longrightarrow\mathbf R^2
\]
whose source \(W\) is a compact strict analytic domain.  Then no harmonic
tropicalization has image equal to \(\Sigma\).
