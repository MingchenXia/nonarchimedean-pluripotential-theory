\begin{proof}
Write $\mathcal J_{\mathrm{eucl}}$ and
$\mathcal G_{\mathrm{eucl},0}$ for the exact comparison and generator
sheaves of
\Cref{thm:p071-infinite-star-euclidean-collapse} with one Euclidean
coordinate.

\medskip\noindent
\emph{Locality, monotonicity, and decreasing closure.}
The finite-stratum comparison conditions are local by the contraction
argument in the proof of the cited star--Euclidean theorem.  The two exact
trace conditions are local on their endpoint components.  At
$(\eta,-\infty)$, compactness of a closed terminal interval in $S_-$ and
the cofinite-branch topology give one product chart and one countable
activity set after shrinking.  Thus the displayed conditions define a
sheaf.

For every fixed $x$, the function $s\mapsto u(x,s)$ is nondecreasing on
the finite stratum.  Indeed, it is convex there, and a negative secant
slope would force its values to tend to $+\infty$ as $s\to-\infty$,
contrary to \eqref{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-trace}.  This is the
monotonicity argument in
\Cref{thm:p071-cld-infinite-collar-endpoint}; it also covers an
identically $-\infty$ fibre.

Let $u_j\downarrow u$ in $\mathcal J_-$.  Joint convexity and the finite
product junction inequality pass to the limit as in the
star--Euclidean theorem.  The proof of the one-dimensional infinite
endpoint theorem shows, fibre by fibre, that the exact $S_-$-trace is
preserved: monotonicity gives one inequality, and one sufficiently
advanced row gives the reverse inequality, also when the limiting value
is $-\infty$.  The exact full-star endpoint trace passes by the same
argument in the other variable.  On a fixed smaller compactified product
chart, the union of the countable activity sets of all rows is countable.
Finally, the endpoint slice is the decreasing limit of the scalar
exact-star slices $u_j(\,\cdot\,,-\infty)$, so it belongs to the scalar
comparison sheaf.  Hence $\mathcal J_-$ is closed under pointwise
decreasing sequential limits.

Every generator belongs to $\mathcal J_-$.  Its two endpoint traces are
immediate from the two exact collars.  On the finite product stratum, the
derivative-balance calculation in the star--Euclidean theorem gives
\eqref{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-jensen}.  On the endpoint slice, the
same calculation is constant in the $S_-$-coordinate.  Minimality gives
\begin{equation}
                         \mathcal P_-\subseteq\mathcal J_-.
\label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-minimality}
\end{equation}

\medskip\noindent
\emph{The moving terminal clamp.}
Fix a nonnegative even
$\theta\in C^\infty_c((-1,1))$ of integral one.  For a fixed
$0<\delta<1/4$, put $\theta_\delta(q)=\delta^{-1}\theta(q/\delta)$ and
\begin{equation}
 M_\delta(a,b)=\int_{\mathbf R^2}
   \max\{a+q_1,b+q_2\}\theta_\delta(q_1)
       \theta_\delta(q_2)\,\mathrm dq_1\,\mathrm dq_2.
\label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-regularized-maximum}
\end{equation}
This is smooth, convex, and nondecreasing in both variables; it equals
$a$ when $a>b+2\delta$, equals $b$ when $b>a+2\delta$, and lies between
$\max\{a,b\}$ and $\max\{a,b\}+\delta$.  Define
\begin{equation}
 \kappa_n(s)=M_\delta(s,-n)\quad(s\in\mathbf R),
 \qquad \kappa_n(-\infty)=-n.
\label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-clamp}
\end{equation}
Then $\kappa_n$ is smooth, convex, nondecreasing, and exactly constant on
a terminal tail.  Moreover
\begin{equation}
 \kappa_{n+1}\leq\kappa_n,
 \qquad
 \kappa_n(s)=s\quad\text{for every fixed finite $s$ and all large $n$}.
\label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-clamp-order}
\end{equation}
The first assertion follows from monotonicity of $M_\delta$ in its second
argument; the second follows from its exact regions.

We shall use the following already proved refinement of the
star--Euclidean theorem.  If a finite continuous member of
$\mathcal J_{\mathrm{eucl}}$ is nondecreasing in its Euclidean coordinate,
then on a prescribed compact product core it can be approximated from
above, with arbitrarily small uniform error, by a member of
$\mathcal G_{\mathrm{eucl},0}$ whose Euclidean derivative is nonnegative.
Indeed, the monotone-coordinate refinement in the proof of
\Cref{thm:p071-infinite-star-finite-collar-product-collapse} chooses only
nonnegative Euclidean dual slopes.  The finite dual maximum and exact-star
smoothing preserve those slopes.  The decreasing approximants converge
uniformly to a finite continuous target on a compact core by Dini's
theorem.

\medskip\noindent
\emph{The node over $-\infty$.}
Let $u\in\mathcal J_-$ near $(\eta,-\infty)$, and shrink to
$U\times[-\infty,r)$ as in the statement.  Shrink $r$ once and choose
$r'<r-3\delta$.  Let $D\subset U$ be a compact closed standard star core
whose interior contains a smaller fixed standard neighbourhood $U'$ of
$\eta$.

If $c(s_0)=-\infty$ at one finite $s_0$, convexity makes $c$ identically
$-\infty$ on the connected finite tail.  The scalar exact-star theorem,
compactness of $\{\eta\}\times[-\infty,r']$, and one common shrinking then
make $u$ identically $-\infty$ on $U'\times[-\infty,r')$; the constants
$-n$ give the required approximation.  We may therefore suppose that
$c(s)$ is finite for every finite $s<r$.

Let $K$ be the countable activity set of the product chart, and choose
nested finite sets $K_n\subset K$ with union $K$.  Choose an integer
$N\geq1$ so large that $-N<r'$.  In the remainder of the construction
$n\geq N$; the resulting sequence is reindexed by the positive integers
at the end.  On
$D\times I_n$, where
\[
                         I_n=[-n,r'+2\delta],
\]
define a finite-stratum star comparison function $V_n$ by
\begin{align}
 (V_n)_a(t,q)&=\max\{u_a(t,q),c(q)-n\},
                                      &&a\in A,\notag\\
 (V_n)_b(t,q)&=\max\{u_b(t,q),c(q)-n\},
                                      &&b\in K_n,\label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-star-truncation}\\
 (V_n)_b(t,q)&=c(q),
                                      &&b\in B\setminus(A\cup K_n).
\notag
\end{align}
The partial-Legendre truncation argument in the proof of the
star--Euclidean theorem shows that $V_n$ belongs to
$\mathcal J_{\mathrm{eucl}}$.  It is finite, continuous on
$D\times I_n$, finite-active, and nondecreasing in $q$.  Finiteness and
continuity at every full star endpoint follow exactly as in that proof:
the floor $c(q)-n$ removes the value $-\infty$, and Dini's theorem makes
the terminal convergence uniform on the compact $q$-interval.

Define on the fixed compactified tail
\begin{equation}
                        T_n(x,s)=V_n(x,\kappa_n(s)).
\label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-comparison-rows}
\end{equation}
The image of $[-\infty,r')$ under $\kappa_n$ lies in $I_n$.  Indeed,
$\kappa_n\geq-n$ and
$\kappa_n(s)\leq\max\{s,-n\}+\delta<r'+2\delta<r$; our choice of $N$
also makes $I_n$ a nonempty subinterval of the original finite tail.  We
claim
that
\begin{equation}
                         T_{n+1}\leq T_n,
 \qquad T_n\longrightarrow u
 \quad\text{pointwise on }D\times[-\infty,r').
\label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-row-limit}
\end{equation}
On an already retained branch, the first inequality follows from
\eqref{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-clamp-order}, monotonicity in $q$,
and the decreasing floor $c(q)-n$.  On a newly retained full branch, the
scalar exact-star comparison gives $u_b(t,q)\leq c(q)$, so its new maximum
is at most the preceding inactive value.  The inactive branches are
immediate.  For a finite $s$, the clamp is eventually the identity, every
active branch is eventually retained, and the floor tends to $-\infty$.
At $s=-\infty$, the exact trace gives
$u(x,-n)\to u(x,-\infty)$, while
$c(-n)-n\to-\infty$.  These observations prove
\eqref{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-row-limit}, including at every full
star endpoint.

Choose positive numbers $e_n$ with $\sum_ne_n<\infty$.  Apply the
monotone-coordinate refinement above to $V_n$ on $D\times I_n$.  Its
constructive proof permits the same preassigned $U'$ for every $n$ and
gives a generator $W_n$ on $U'$ times an open interval containing $I_n$
such that
\begin{equation}
 V_n\leq W_n\leq V_n+e_n,
 \qquad \partial_qW_n\geq0
 \quad\text{on }U'\times I_n.
\label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-uniform-smoothing}
\end{equation}
Put
\begin{equation}
 d_n=\sum_{k>n}e_k,
 \qquad
 G_n(x,s)=W_n(x,\kappa_n(s))+d_n.
\label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-final-approximants}
\end{equation}
Convex monotone composition shows that $G_n$ is jointly convex on every
finite branch product.  It is exactly constant in $s$ on a terminal tail.
Its finite activity is that of $W_n$, and
\[
 \sum_b\partial_tG_{n,b}(0,s)
   =\sum_b\partial_tW_{n,b}(0,\kappa_n(s))=0.
\]
The full-star terminal collars are unchanged.  Hence
$G_n\in\mathcal G_{-,0}$ on the fixed neighbourhood
$U'\times[-\infty,r')$.

Equations \eqref{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-row-limit} and
\eqref{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-uniform-smoothing} give
\[
 G_{n+1}\leq T_{n+1}+e_{n+1}+d_{n+1}
       \leq T_n+d_n\leq G_n.
\]
They also give $G_n\to u$ pointwise.  This proves the fixed-neighbourhood
first-stage approximation at $(\eta,-\infty)$.

\medskip\noindent
\emph{The remaining strata over $-\infty$.}
At a full star endpoint, work on a compact terminal branch interval
$D=[-\infty,t_1]$ and choose a finite branch point $t_0<t_1$.  On
$D\times I_n$ replace \eqref{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-star-truncation}
by
\begin{equation}
             V_n(t,q)=\max\{u(t,q),u(t_0,q)-n\}.
\label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-corner-truncation}
\end{equation}
Before using this formula, apply the extended-real convexity dichotomy on
the connected finite product stratum.  If $u$ is identically $-\infty$
there, the two exact traces give the same value on both included endpoint
faces and the constants $-n$ give the required approximation.  Otherwise
$u$ is finite throughout a smaller connected finite product chart, and
\eqref{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-corner-truncation} applies.
The full-endpoint part of the star--Euclidean proof shows that $V_n$ is
finite, continuous, jointly convex, and nondecreasing in $q$.  After
composition with $\kappa_n$, the rows decrease to $u$, including at the
double endpoint: the first term converges by the $S_-$-trace and the
second tends to $-\infty$.  Uniform monotone-coordinate regularization,
composition, and the same tail correction therefore give the required
decreasing generators on one fixed double-terminal neighbourhood.

At an ordinary star-branch point, a locally extended-real jointly convex
function on the finite product stratum is either identically $-\infty$ or
finite.  The first case uses constants.  In the second case, restrict the
branch coordinate to a fixed compact interval.  On each $I_n$ the target
is finite and continuous, so ordinary convex smoothing, chosen with
nonnegative $q$-slopes, gives the same uniform approximation; composition
with $\kappa_n$ and the tail correction finish the argument.

At a point with finite $S_-$-coordinate, the assertion is exactly
\Cref{thm:p071-infinite-star-euclidean-collapse}.  We have therefore proved
$\mathcal J_-\subseteq\mathcal P_{-,1}$ everywhere.  Together with
\eqref{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-minimality} and the tautological
inclusion $\mathcal P_{-,1}\subseteq\mathcal P_-$, this proves
\eqref{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-collapse} and the fixed-neighbourhood
assertion.
\end{proof}
