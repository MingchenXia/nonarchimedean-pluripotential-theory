\begin{theorem}
\label{thm:p071-infinite-star-infinite-collar-product-collapse}
Let $B\ne\varnothing$, let $X_B$ be the exact
Chambert--Loir--Ducros star of
\Cref{thm:p071-infinite-star-collapse-candidate}, and put
\[
 S_-=[-\infty,R_0)\subset\mathbf R\cup\{-\infty\},
 \qquad R_0\in\mathbf R\cup\{+\infty\}.
\]
Give $S_-$ the constant-terminal-collar smooth structure of
\Cref{thm:p071-cld-infinite-collar-endpoint}, and put
$X=X_B\times S_-$.

Define the exact product generator sheaf $\mathcal G_{-,0}$ as follows.
On $X_B\times(S_-\cap\mathbf R)$ it is the one-dimensional Euclidean
product generator sheaf of
\Cref{thm:p071-infinite-star-euclidean-collapse}.  On a product
neighbourhood meeting $X_B\times\{-\infty\}$, require in addition that a
generator be independent of the $S_-$-coordinate on some terminal tail,
locally uniformly in the $X_B$-variable.  The finite-activity and balance
conditions at the star node, and the terminal-collar condition at every
full star branch endpoint, are exactly those of the cited theorem.  Let
$\mathcal P_{-,1}$ be the sheaf of local pointwise decreasing limits of
sequences in $\mathcal G_{-,0}$, and let $\mathcal P_-$ be the smallest
subsheaf of $\mathbf R\cup\{-\infty\}$-valued functions which contains
$\mathcal G_{-,0}$ and is closed under such limits.

Define a comparison sheaf $\mathcal J_-$ by the following local
conditions.  On an ordinary branch interval times a finite interval in
$S_-$, every branch function, with its star-node value inserted, is
extended-real jointly convex.  At a full star branch endpoint, require the
exact terminal trace of
\Cref{thm:p071-infinite-star-euclidean-collapse}.  In the $S_-$-direction
require the fibrewise exact trace
\begin{equation}
 u(x,-\infty)=\lim_{s\to-\infty}u(x,s)
       \quad\text{in }\mathbf R\cup\{-\infty\}
 \label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-trace}
\end{equation}
for every $x$ in the displayed product neighbourhood.  In particular,
the finite-side limit is not allowed to be $+\infty$.

Near $(\eta,-\infty)$, choose a standard star neighbourhood $U$, with
finite exceptional set $A\subset B$ and exceptional collars
$(-R_a,0]$, and a terminal interval $[-\infty,r)\subset S_-$.  Write
\[
 u_b(t,s)=u(p_b(t),s),\qquad c(s)=u(\eta,s).
\]
Require one countable set $K\subset B\setminus A$ such that
\begin{equation}
 u_b(t,s)=c(s)
 \quad
 \bigl(b\in B\setminus(A\cup K),\ t\in[-\infty,0],\
       s\in[-\infty,r)\bigr).
\label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-countable-activity}
\end{equation}
For a finite set $F\subset B\setminus A$, numbers $r_b>0$ for $b\in F$
and $0<\rho_a<R_a$ for $a\in A$, put
\[
 \Lambda=\sum_{b\in F}\frac1{r_b}
              +\sum_{a\in A}\frac1{\rho_a}.
\]
For finite $s_0,s_b,s_a<r$ satisfying
\begin{equation}
 \Lambda s_0=
   \sum_{b\in F}\frac{s_b}{r_b}
      +\sum_{a\in A}\frac{s_a}{\rho_a},
\label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-barycentre}
\end{equation}
require, whenever $\Lambda>0$ and $c(s_0)\in\mathbf R$,
\begin{equation}
 \Lambda c(s_0)\leq
   \sum_{b\in F}\frac{u_b(-r_b,s_b)}{r_b}
      +\sum_{a\in A}\frac{u_a(-\rho_a,s_a)}{\rho_a}.
\label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-jensen}
\end{equation}
At the endpoint slice $s=-\infty$, require the scalar exact-star
comparison conditions of
\Cref{thm:p071-infinite-star-collapse-candidate}; equivalently, when
$c(-\infty)$ is finite, require the same inequality with every displayed
$s$-coordinate replaced by $-\infty$.  Away from the $S_-$-endpoint or
the star node, omit the corresponding endpoint or node conditions.

Then $\mathcal J_-$ is a sheaf and
\begin{equation}
                    \mathcal P_{-,1}=\mathcal P_-=\mathcal J_-.
\label{eq:p071-star-infinite-collar-collapse}
\end{equation}
Every germ in these sheaves has a pointwise decreasing approximation by
members of $\mathcal G_{-,0}$ on one fixed smaller product neighbourhood.
Each approximant is exactly constant in the $S_-$-coordinate on a
terminal tail, although the tail threshold may tend to $-\infty$.

Thus an arbitrary exact CLD branch set and the nonambient endpoint
$-\infty$ of \Cref{thm:p071-cld-infinite-collar-endpoint} may occur
simultaneously, with mixed branch--interval Hessian terms on the finite
stratum.  The theorem does not cover products of two branched stars,
non-CLD infinite stars, nonambient branch structures, or nonconstant
affine directions on an additional Stone factor.  Consequently it
narrows but does not retire P07.1.
\end{theorem}
