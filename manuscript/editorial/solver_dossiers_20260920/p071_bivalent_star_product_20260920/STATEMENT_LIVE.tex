\begin{theorem}
\label{thm:p071-bivalent-star-product-collapse}
Let $B\ne\varnothing$, let $C=\{c_-,c_+\}$, and let $X_B$ and $X_C$ be
the exact Chambert--Loir--Ducros stars of
\Cref{thm:p071-infinite-star-collapse-candidate}.  Put
$X=X_B\times X_C$.  Unfold the bivalent factor by the homeomorphism
\begin{equation}
 \chi:X_C\longrightarrow[-\infty,+\infty],\qquad
 \chi(p_{c_-}(s))=s,\quad
 \chi(p_{c_+}(s))=-s,
 \label{eq:p071-bivalent-unfolding}
\end{equation}
where $s\in[-\infty,0]$ and $\chi(\eta_C)=0$.

Define the exact two-star product generator sheaf
$\mathcal G_{B,C,0}$ by the following local conditions.  A generator is
real-valued.  On every finite branch rectangle its branch function is
smooth and jointly convex.  At a node of the first factor, use a standard
neighbourhood with finite exceptional set $A\subset B$ and require one
finite active set $K\subset B\setminus A$, locally uniform in the second
factor, together with
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{a\in A}\partial_tg_a(0,y)
   +\sum_{b\in K}\partial_tg_b(0,y)=0
 \qquad(y\in X_C\text{ in the product chart}).
 \label{eq:p071-bivalent-first-balance}
\end{equation}
At the bivalent node require
\begin{equation}
 \partial_sg(x,p_{c_-}(0))
   +\partial_sg(x,p_{c_+}(0))=0
 \qquad(x\in X_B\text{ in the product chart}).
 \label{eq:p071-bivalent-second-balance}
\end{equation}
The branch functions have their common node-slice values at $t=0$ or
$s=0$.  At every included full endpoint in either factor, require exact
constancy on a terminal collar, locally uniformly in the other factor.
Let $\mathcal P_{B,C,1}$ be the sheaf of local pointwise decreasing
limits of sequences in $\mathcal G_{B,C,0}$, and let
$\mathcal P_{B,C}$ be the smallest subsheaf of
$\mathbf R\cup\{-\infty\}$-valued functions which contains
$\mathcal G_{B,C,0}$ and is closed under such limits.

Define a comparison sheaf $\mathcal J_{B,C}$ through the unfolding
\eqref{eq:p071-bivalent-unfolding}.  Near a point whose unfolded second
coordinate is finite, require the pullback to belong to the exact
star--Euclidean comparison sheaf of
\Cref{thm:p071-infinite-star-euclidean-collapse} with $d=1$.  Near the
endpoint $-\infty$, require it to belong to the exact star--infinite-collar
comparison sheaf of
\Cref{thm:p071-infinite-star-infinite-collar-product-collapse}.  Near
$+\infty$, impose the same condition after the reflection $q\mapsto-q$.
Thus the comparison conditions include joint branch--line convexity, the
exact endpoint traces in both factors, one countable active set in the
$B$-factor, and the reciprocal-weight product junction inequalities with
the unfolded coordinate allowed to vary subject to its barycentre
identity.

Then $\mathcal J_{B,C}$ is a sheaf and
\begin{equation}
             \mathcal P_{B,C,1}
             =\mathcal P_{B,C}
             =\mathcal J_{B,C}.
 \label{eq:p071-bivalent-product-collapse}
\end{equation}
Every germ in these sheaves has a pointwise decreasing approximation by
members of $\mathcal G_{B,C,0}$ on one fixed smaller product
neighbourhood.

By symmetry, the same conclusion holds whenever either factor in a
product of two exact CLD stars is bivalent.  The theorem permits the other
factor to have an arbitrary nonempty branch set.  It does not cover a
product in which both node valences are at least three, a non-CLD star, or
a nonambient branch structure.  Consequently it narrows but does not
retire P07.1.
\end{theorem}
