\begin{proof}
The whole affine space $\mathbf R^d$ is a nonempty convex polyhedron in its
own affine span: it is the intersection of the empty finite family of closed
affine half-spaces.  Apply
\Cref{thm:p071-stone-infinite-star-polyhedral-collapse} with
$P=\mathbf R^d$.  Its conclusion is an identity of sheaves on
$Z\times X_B\times\mathbf R^d$, so restriction to the ordinary open subset
$Z\times X_B\times\Omega$ gives
\eqref{eq:p071-stone-infinite-euclidean-collapse}.

For any $y_0\in\Omega$, choose an open convex neighbourhood $V'$ of $y_0$
with compact closure in $\Omega$.  The fixed-neighbourhood assertion and
the common countable activity set are then exactly the corresponding local
assertions of the cited theorem.  If $\Omega$ is open and convex, the
restricted ambient smooth jointly convex generators and the local
branch--Euclidean comparison conditions are precisely the specialization of
the polyhedral definitions to $P=\mathbf R^d$; both descriptions are
germwise on convex product neighbourhoods.  This proves the final
identification and the stated scope.
\end{proof}
