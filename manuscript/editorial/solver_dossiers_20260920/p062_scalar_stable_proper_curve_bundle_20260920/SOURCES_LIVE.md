# Sources and dependency boundary

- Wanner, Theorem 4.7 and Corollary 4.8, together with Thuillier,
  Proposition 3.1.11: over a completed algebraic closure, both signs of the
  scalar-stable fibre restriction are subharmonic, and the proper connected
  maximum principle makes the function constant.
- Berkovich, *Spectral Theory and Analytic Geometry over Non-Archimedean
  Fields*, Section 1.3 and Proposition 2.1.15: scalar extension and density
  of rigid points.
- CLD, Theorem 16.5.4 and Corollary 16.5.5: smooth projection formula; the
  proof supplies the compact-support approximation that extends it to the
  displayed continuous multiplier.

No source used here states that a downstairs continuous Hessian-current
identity automatically ascends to a completed algebraic closure.  That
property remains an explicit hypothesis.
