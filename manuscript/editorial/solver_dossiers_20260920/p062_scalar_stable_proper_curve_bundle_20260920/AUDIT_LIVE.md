# Local mathematical audit

Date: 20 September 2026

## Scope

The proposition removes the algebraic-closure hypothesis from proper
smooth curve-bundle descent only under an explicit fibrewise scalar-stability
condition at rigid base points.  It does not claim automatic pullback of a
continuous Hessian current to a completed algebraic closure.

## Proof obligations

1. The added hypothesis permits Wanner's theorem over a completed algebraic
   closure.  Proper connectedness and the maximum principle force each rigid
   fibre restriction to be constant.
2. An arbitrary closed point of the curve supplies a finite faithfully flat
   multisection.  Equalizer descent, rigid-point density, and the quotient
   topology replace the unavailable rational section; no separability is
   asserted or needed.
3. A normalized compactly supported fibre top form descends the Hessian and
   first-current equations; no current is pulled back along the multisection.
4. The continuous-multiplier projection formula is expanded in the proof,
   and the fixed fibre-form normalization is explicitly kept over $K$.

The standard surjectivity of analytic scalar extension is used to descend
constancy from the completed algebraic closure to the original
residue-field fibre.
