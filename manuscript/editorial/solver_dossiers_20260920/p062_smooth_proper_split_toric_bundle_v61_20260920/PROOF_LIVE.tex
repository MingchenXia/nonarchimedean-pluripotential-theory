\begin{proof}
Choose $\varpi\in K$ with $0<|\varpi|<1$, and put
$R_n=|\varpi|^{-n}$ for $n\geq1$.  Fix a displayed trivializing open
$U$.  Since $X_\Sigma$ is proper, its fan is complete.  Thus the affine
toric opens $X_\sigma$, with $\sigma\in\Sigma(q)$, cover $X_\Sigma$.
Smoothness says that the primitive ray generators of every such $\sigma$
form a basis of the cocharacter lattice.  The dual characters therefore
give an equivariant isomorphism
\[
                         X_\sigma\simeq\mathbf A_K^q;
\]
this is the regular affine-chart description of a smooth toric variety
\cite[Example~1.2.21 and Theorems~3.1.19 and~3.4.1]{CLS11}.  Write
$z_{\sigma,1},\ldots,z_{\sigma,q}$ for the
resulting affine coordinates and set
\[
 P_{\sigma,n}:=
 \bigl\{x\in X_\sigma^{\mathrm{an}}:
       |z_{\sigma,r}(x)|<R_n\ \text{for }1\leq r\leq q\bigr\}.
\]
Then $P_{\sigma,n}\simeq(D^-_{R_n})^q$.  The opens
$P_{\sigma,n}$, as $\sigma\in\Sigma(q)$ and $n\geq1$ vary, cover
$X_\Sigma^{\mathrm{an}}$: the affine toric charts cover, and the finitely
many coordinate norms of a point in one chart are bounded by some $R_n$.

Restrict the zero-current identity to
$U\times_KP_{\sigma,n}$.  Successive application of
\Cref{prop:p062-open-disc-descent} to the $q$ strict open-disc factors
gives a continuous zero-current function $b_{U,\sigma,n}$ on $U$ with
\[
 h|_{U\times_KP_{\sigma,n}}
      =\operatorname{pr}_U^*b_{U,\sigma,n}.
\]
At every step the new base remains positive-dimensional, good,
topologically separated, pure, and strict, as required by the cited
proposition.  Its strengthened conclusion also gives
$\mathrm d'[b_{U,\sigma,n}]=0$ whenever $\mathrm d'[h]=0$.

Let $e\in T(K)\subset X_\Sigma(K)$ be the identity of the dense split
torus.  It belongs to every affine toric chart, and every displayed affine
coordinate has value $1$ at $e$.  Since $R_n>1$, the point $e$
lies in every $P_{\sigma,n}$.  Pulling the preceding identity back along the
constant section $s_{U,e}:U\to U\times_KX_\Sigma^{\mathrm{an}}$ gives
\[
                  b_{U,\sigma,n}=h\circ s_{U,e}
\]
for all $\sigma,n$.  Denote this common function by $b_U$.  The covering
property of the $P_{\sigma,n}$ now gives
\[
                   h|_{\pi^{-1}(U)}=\operatorname{pr}_U^*b_U,
                   \qquad
                   \mathrm d'\mathrm d''[b_U]=0.
\]

On overlaps of trivializing opens, the two pullback identities agree, and
the nonempty fibres force the corresponding base functions to agree.  Hence
the $b_U$ glue to a unique continuous $b$ on $B$ with
$h=\pi^*b$.  The local zero-current identities glue by
\Cref{prop:ch14-currents-sheaf}; the same argument applies to the local
first-current identities.  This proves every assertion.
\end{proof}
