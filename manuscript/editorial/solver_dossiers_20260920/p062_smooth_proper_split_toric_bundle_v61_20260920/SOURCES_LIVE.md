# P06.2 smooth proper split-toric bundle descent: source ledger

- `prop:p062-open-disc-descent`: exact descent of a continuous zero-current
  function through one standard strict open-disc factor, including the
  strengthened first-current conclusion.
- `prop:ch14-currents-sheaf`: analytic-open gluing of the descended current
  identities on the base.
- Cox--Little--Schenck, *Toric Varieties* (`CLS11`), Example 1.2.21 and
  Theorems 3.1.19 and 3.4.1: a regular full-dimensional cone gives an affine
  toric chart isomorphic to affine space, smoothness is equivalent to
  regularity of the fan, and properness is equivalent to completeness.
  Thus the maximal-cone affine spaces form a finite cover.
- Direct toric calculation: the identity of the dense split torus belongs
  to every affine toric chart and has all regular-cone coordinates equal to
  one.  It is therefore a common point of every strict-polydisc exhaustion
  used in the proof.

No invariant-function hypothesis is used.  The argument does not invoke a
general pullback theorem for continuous Hessian currents and does not claim
anything for singular, nonsplit, nonproper, or non-locally-trivial toric
fibres.
