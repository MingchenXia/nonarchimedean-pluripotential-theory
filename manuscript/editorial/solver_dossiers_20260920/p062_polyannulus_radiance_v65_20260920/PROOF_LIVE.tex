\begin{proof}
The affine cocycle identity in
\eqref{eq:p062-annular-affine-cocycle} reads
\[
 c_{\alpha\gamma}
 =c_{\beta\gamma}+M_{\beta\gamma}c_{\alpha\beta}.
\]
This is exactly the Cech one-cocycle law for
$\mathscr P_W(\mathbb V_{\pi,\mathbf R})$, with
$c_{\alpha\beta}$ written in the $\beta$-frame.  The sheaf is well defined
because its transition matrices are locally constant: multiplying a vector
of zero-current functions by such a matrix again gives a vector of
zero-current functions.  The standing reverse-transition identity gives
$c_{\beta\alpha}=-M_{\beta\alpha}c_{\alpha\beta}$, while the identity chart
has zero translation, so this cocycle has the required alternating
normalization.

We first check independence.  On a common refinement, let
$\widehat L_\alpha$ be the logarithmic vector associated with a second set
of annular coordinates.  The standing change-of-atlas hypothesis gives
\[
 \widehat L_\alpha=\pi^*e_\alpha+(N_\alpha\circ\pi)L_\alpha,
 \qquad
 N_\alpha\in\operatorname{GL}_s(\mathbf Z),
 \qquad
 e_\alpha\in\mathscr P_W(U_\alpha)^s,
\]
with $N_\alpha$ locally constant.  Substitution in the two overlap formulas
gives
\[
 \widehat M_{\alpha\beta}
 =N_\beta M_{\alpha\beta}N_\alpha^{-1},
 \qquad
 \widehat c_{\alpha\beta}
 =N_\beta c_{\alpha\beta}
   +e_\beta-\widehat M_{\alpha\beta}e_\alpha.
\]
The first identity is the canonical change of frame for the local system;
under it, the second changes the translated cocycle by the Cech coboundary
of $(e_\alpha)$.  Thus the class is unchanged.  Passing to the union of two
atlases and then to a common refinement proves independence under
replacement of the atlas as well.

For a zero-cochain $d=(d_\alpha)$, formula
\eqref{eq:p062-annular-coordinate-transition} gives
\[
 \widetilde L_\beta
 =\pi^*\bigl(c_{\alpha\beta}+d_\beta
                 -M_{\alpha\beta}d_\alpha\bigr)
  +(M_{\alpha\beta}\circ\pi)\widetilde L_\alpha.
\]
Hence \eqref{eq:p062-radiance-linear-transition} holds precisely when
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:p062-radiance-coboundary}
 c_{\alpha\beta}+d_\beta-M_{\alpha\beta}d_\alpha=0.
\end{equation}
By the direct-limit definition of Cech cohomology, such a zero-cochain
exists after a refinement precisely when $\rho_0(\pi)=0$.  The purely
linear transition law then glues the local continuous vectors
$\widetilde L_\alpha$ to the asserted section $\Lambda_\pi$ of
$\mathcal E_\pi$.  They are not claimed to be locally constant along the
fibres.

Let $h$ have zero current.  In the notation of
\eqref{eq:p062-annular-local-normal-form}, put
\[
 \widetilde b_\alpha=b_\alpha-a_\alpha^{\mathsf T}d_\alpha.
\]
The function $\widetilde b_\alpha$ has zero current because $a_\alpha$ is
locally constant.  On an overlap, use
\eqref{eq:p062-annular-coefficient-transition} and
\eqref{eq:p062-radiance-coboundary} to obtain
\begin{align*}
 \widetilde b_\alpha
 &=b_\beta+a_\beta^{\mathsf T}c_{\alpha\beta}
   -a_\beta^{\mathsf T}M_{\alpha\beta}d_\alpha\\
 &=b_\beta-a_\beta^{\mathsf T}d_\beta
 =\widetilde b_\beta.
\end{align*}
Thus the $\widetilde b_\alpha$ glue to
$b\in\mathscr P_W(W)$.  Since
$a_\alpha=M_{\alpha\beta}^{\mathsf T}a_\beta$ and
$\widetilde L_\beta=M_{\alpha\beta}\widetilde L_\alpha$, their pairing is
independent of the chart.  Rewriting the original local formula as
\[
 h=\pi^*\widetilde b_\alpha
   +(a_\alpha\circ\pi)^{\mathsf T}\widetilde L_\alpha
\]
therefore gives \eqref{eq:p062-radiance-global-coefficient-formula}.
Uniqueness follows from the standing uniqueness of the chartwise base
coefficient.  The cohomological
vanishing assertion is immediate from
\eqref{eq:p062-zero-current-radiance-class}, and the final qualification
records that the correcting zero-cochain was chosen in $\mathscr P_W$, not
in the sheaf of logarithms of analytic units.
\end{proof}
