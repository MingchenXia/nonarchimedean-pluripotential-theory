## Conservative theorem scope

- `K` is algebraically closed, complete, nontrivially valued.
- `C` is a smooth proper connected **algebraic** `K`-curve.
- `L` is an algebraic line bundle and the metric is continuous.
- Every local weight is CLD-psh.

No assertion is made here for a nonalgebraized proper strict analytic
curve, a non-algebraically-closed ground field, or a singular curve.

## Hostile checks

1. **Metric-order sign:** PASS. Thuillier's norms increase pointwise to the
   target norm, hence `log(||.||/||.||_alpha)` is nonnegative and decreases
   to zero. The finite-subcover/directedness argument proves uniformity.

2. **Global transition functions:** PASS. The CLD-to-Thuillier step uses
   harmonic frame changes. Thuillier's approximants are global metrics on
   `L`. The graph correction is a global relative function, so it cannot
   alter the cocycle.

3. **Type-III cuts:** PASS only with the graph-movement step retained.
   Deleting it and citing Thuillier 3.2.5 directly would be invalid under
   the strict GJR/GR R-PL convention, by GJR Example 5.3.

4. **Field mixing:** PASS. Wanner/type-II density are used over the
   algebraically closed field. BFJ/Zhang is a remark in a distinct field
   scope. No field is assumed both algebraically closed and discretely
   valued.

5. **Degree zero:** PASS. Positivity plus total mass zero forces zero
   curvature. The target is harmonic/lisse. Rational nef approximants also
   have positive measure of total mass zero, hence are componentwise
   numerically trivial. Thuillier supplies independent existence.

6. **R-to-Q rationalization:** PASS. It occurs on one fixed model inside a
   finite rational polyhedron; no uncontrolled descent, breakpoint, or
   varying-model argument is hidden.

7. **Analytic branch:** REMOVED. GR Thm. 5.6 does not by itself supply the
   claimed global real-model bridge for an arbitrary nonalgebraized proper
   strict analytic curve. That remains a P08.2 bridge unless an independent
   algebraization/gluing theorem is inserted.

8. **Ordinary CLD-smooth approximation:** NOT CLAIMED globally in degree
   zero. A nef residual degree-zero line bundle need not be semiample, so
   the FS/ordinary-smooth arrow requires an additional hypothesis or a new
   argument.
