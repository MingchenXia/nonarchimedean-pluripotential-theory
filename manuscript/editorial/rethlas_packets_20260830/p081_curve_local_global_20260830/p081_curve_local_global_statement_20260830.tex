\begin{theorem}
\label{thm:p081-curve-local-global}
Let \(K\) be an algebraically closed complete non-Archimedean field with
nontrivial absolute value, let \(C\) be a smooth proper connected
algebraic \(K\)-curve, and let \(L\) be a line bundle on \(C\). We write
the same symbols for their analytifications. Let
\(\lVert\cdot\rVert\) be a continuous metric on \(L\) whose weight
\(-\log\lVert s\rVert\) is CLD-psh for every local invertible section \(s\).
Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(\deg L\geq 0\);
\item\label{itm:p081:model}
      \(\lVert\cdot\rVert\) is a uniform limit of semipositive
      \(\mathbf Q\)-formal metrics on \(L\);
\item the metrics in \cref{itm:p081:model} are semipositive algebraic
      model metrics,
      so \(\lVert\cdot\rVert\) is semipositive in Zhang's sense;
\item if \(\deg L>0\), the approximants
      can be chosen to come from relatively ample \(\mathbf Q\)-models.
      The metric then also belongs to the uniform Fubini--Study closure.
\end{enumerate}
If \(\deg L=0\), the curvature of \(\lVert\cdot\rVert\) is zero and every
semipositive model approximant may be chosen numerically trivial on the
special fibre. Independently of the given metric, every degree-zero line
bundle on \(C\) admits at least one semipositive \(\mathbf Q\)-formal
metric.
\end{theorem}
