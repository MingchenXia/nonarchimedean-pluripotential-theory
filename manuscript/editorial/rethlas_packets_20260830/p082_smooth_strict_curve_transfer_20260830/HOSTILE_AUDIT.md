   paracompact range of the strict Berkovich--rigid equivalence.

   and dimension one pass to the associated rigid space.

   connectedness required in Lütkebohmert, Theorem 1.8.1(a).

   Mitsui, Theorem 8.12, independently supplies projectivity for arbitrary
   proper one-dimensional rigid spaces.

   analytic line bundle. Full faithfulness applied to evaluation and its
   inverse is needed to prove that the algebraized coherent sheaf is
   invertible.
6. CLD-psh weights are invariant under analytic isomorphism, so the already
   audited P08.1 theorem applies without changing the metric.
7. The statement keeps the original algebraically closed, complete,
   nontrivially valued, smooth, proper, connected, and one-dimensional
   hypotheses. It does not algebraize a chosen formal model and makes no
   higher-dimensional claim.
