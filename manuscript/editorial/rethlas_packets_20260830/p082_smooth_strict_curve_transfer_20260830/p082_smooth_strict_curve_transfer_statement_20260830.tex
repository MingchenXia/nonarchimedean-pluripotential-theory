\begin{corollary}
\label{cor:p082-smooth-strict-curve-transfer}
Let \(K\) be an algebraically closed complete non-Archimedean field with
nontrivial absolute value. Let \(X\) be a smooth proper connected strict
\(K\)-analytic curve, let \(L\) be an analytic line bundle on \(X\), and let
\(\lVert\cdot\rVert\) be a continuous metric on \(L\) whose weight
\(-\log\lVert s\rVert\) is CLD-psh for every local invertible section \(s\).
Then there exist a smooth projective connected algebraic \(K\)-curve \(C\),
an algebraic line bundle \(M\) on \(C\), and an isomorphism
\[
                    (X,L)\simeq(C^{\An},M^{\An}).
\]
Define \(\deg L:=\deg M\), and interpret algebraic model and
Fubini--Study metrics through this identification. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(\deg L\geq0\);
\item \(\lVert\cdot\rVert\) is a uniform limit of semipositive
      \(\mathbf Q\)-formal metrics, equivalently of semipositive algebraic
      model metrics;
\item if \(\deg L>0\), the approximants may be chosen relatively ample and
      the metric lies in the uniform Fubini--Study closure;
\item if \(\deg L=0\), its curvature is zero and the model approximants may
      be chosen numerically trivial on the special fibre.
\end{enumerate}
Independently of the given metric, every analytic line bundle of degree zero
on \(X\) admits at least one semipositive \(\mathbf Q\)-formal metric. The
assumption that \(X\) admit a formal model with projective special fibre is
therefore unnecessary in this smooth curve scope.
\end{corollary}
