\begin{proposition}
\label{prop:p083-curve-parlinear-global-dpsh}
Let \(K\) be an algebraically closed complete non-Archimedean field with
nontrivial absolute value.  Let \(X\) be a smooth proper connected strict
\(K\)-analytic curve, and let \(L\) be an analytic line bundle on \(X\)
endowed with a parlinear metric \(\lVert\cdot\rVert\).  Then the metrized
line bundle \((L,\lVert\cdot\rVert)\) is globally dpsh in the literal sense
of \cite[17.8.5]{CLD25}: there are analytic line bundles \(L_1,L_2\) on
\(X\), globally psh metrics \(\lVert\cdot\rVert_i\) on them, and an
isometry
\[
 (L,\lVert\cdot\rVert)\simeq
 (L_1,\lVert\cdot\rVert_1)\otimes
 (L_2,\lVert\cdot\rVert_2)^{\vee}.
\]
The line bundles \(L_1,L_2\) may be chosen algebraic after choosing the
algebraization of \(X\).
\end{proposition}
