Let \(X\) be an affine \(G\)-tropical space in the sense of
Chambert--Loir--Ducros.  Write \(\operatorname{Psh}_{X,0}\) for the sheaf of
smooth real-valued functions \(v\) such that
\(\mathrm d'\mathrm d''v\geq0\), and write
\(\operatorname{Psh}_{X,1}\) for the sheaf of functions that are locally
pointwise decreasing limits of sections of
\(\operatorname{Psh}_{X,0}\).  Thus
\(\operatorname{Psh}_{X,1}\) is the sheaf of locally approachable psh
functions of Chambert--Loir--Ducros, Definition~8.1.13.

Let \(U\subseteq X\) be open, let
\[
 u_1\geq u_2\geq\cdots,\qquad
 u_n:U\longrightarrow\mathbf R\cup\{-\infty\},\qquad
 u=\inf_n u_n,
\]
and fix \(x\in U\).  Suppose that there is a compact neighbourhood
\(K\subseteq U\) of \(x\), and, for every \(n\geq1\), an open set
\(\Omega_n\subseteq U\) containing \(K\) and a sequence
\[
 v_{n,1}\geq v_{n,2}\geq\cdots
       \quad\hbox{in }\operatorname{Psh}_{X,0}(\Omega_n)
\]
such that \(v_{n,k}\downarrow u_n|_{\Omega_n}\) pointwise.

Then there are smooth psh functions \(g_n\) on the one fixed open
neighbourhood \(K^\circ\) of \(x\) such that
\[
                     g_{n+1}\leq g_n,\qquad g_n\downarrow u|_{K^\circ}.
                     \tag{1}
\]
In particular, \(u\) is locally approachable at \(x\).

Consequently, assume that \(X\) has the following property: for every open
\(U\subseteq X\), every decreasing sequence
\((u_n)\subseteq\operatorname{Psh}_{X,1}(U)\), and every \(x\in U\), the
preceding compact \(K\), open sets \(\Omega_n\), and row approximations
\((v_{n,k})_k\) can be chosen.  Then
\[
                    \operatorname{Psh}_{X,1}=\operatorname{Psh}_X,
                    \tag{2}
\]
where \(\operatorname{Psh}_X\) is the smallest subsheaf containing the
smooth psh functions and stable under pointwise decreasing sequential
limits, as in Chambert--Loir--Ducros, Definition~8.1.1.
