Work with the standing parlinear parameters
\(K=\mathbf Q\) and \(G=\mathbf R\) used in the body of
Chambert--Loir--Ducros.  Let \(Z\) be a locally compact Hausdorff space
with a basis of clopen subsets.  Give
\(Z\) the following affine \(G\)-tropical structure: its domains are the
locally closed subsets, and its parlinear and affine functions are the
locally constant real-valued functions.  Let
\(\Omega\subseteq\mathbf R^d\), \(d\geq0\), be a nonempty open convex set
with its
standard affine structure, and give
\[
                         X=Z\times\Omega
\]
the product affine \(G\)-tropical structure of
\cite[\S\S9.1.1--9.1.4]{CLD25}.  Thus a function on \(X\) is smooth precisely when,
locally on \(Z\times\Omega\), it is independent of the \(Z\)-variable and
smooth in the Euclidean variable.  Such a smooth function is psh precisely
when its Euclidean Hessian is positive semidefinite.

Write \(\operatorname{Psh}_{X,0}\),
\(\operatorname{Psh}_{X,1}\), and \(\operatorname{Psh}_X\) for the smooth
psh generator sheaf, its first local decreasing-limit stage, and the full
generated CLD psh sheaf, respectively.  Then
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:p071-stone-collapse}
                    \operatorname{Psh}_{X,1}
                    =\operatorname{Psh}_X.
\end{equation}
More precisely, for every \(u\in\operatorname{Psh}_X(U)\) and every
\((z_0,x_0)\in U\), there are a compact clopen neighbourhood \(C\) of
\(z_0\) and an open Euclidean ball \(B_0\) about \(x_0\), with
\(C\times\overline{B_0}\subseteq U\), such that the following fixed-
neighbourhood re-selection holds.  For every ball \(B\) about \(x_0\) with
\(\overline B\subseteq B_0\), there are smooth psh functions
\(g_k\in\operatorname{Psh}_{X,0}(C\times B)\) satisfying
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:p071-stone-fixed-neighbourhood}
                          g_{k+1}\leq g_k,
             \qquad      g_k\downarrow u|_{C\times B}.
\end{equation}

The local comparison class used in the proof has an intrinsic
factorization description.  After shrinking \(C\times B\), every section
of \(\operatorname{Psh}_X\) can be written
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:p071-stone-factorization}
             u(z,x)=\bar u(q(z),x),
\end{equation}
where \(q:C\twoheadrightarrow Y\) is a continuous surjection onto a compact
metrizable zero-dimensional space, \(\bar u\) is upper semicontinuous on
\(Y\times B\), and, for every \(y\in Y\), the function
\(x\mapsto\bar u(y,x)\) is either finite-valued convex or identically
\(-\infty\).  Conversely, every function locally admitting
\eqref{eq:p071-stone-factorization} with these properties belongs to
\(\operatorname{Psh}_{X,1}\).

In particular, the conclusion applies for arbitrary \(d\) and to the
nonmetrizable, nonambient examples
\[
                    Z=\{0,1\}^{\Lambda}
\]
for every index set \(\Lambda\), including uncountable \(\Lambda\), with
the locally constant affine structure just specified.  The result concerns
this exact product structure; it makes no assertion for a different affine
structure on the same underlying space, for general polyhedral face
gluings, or for arbitrary affine \(G\)-tropical spaces.
