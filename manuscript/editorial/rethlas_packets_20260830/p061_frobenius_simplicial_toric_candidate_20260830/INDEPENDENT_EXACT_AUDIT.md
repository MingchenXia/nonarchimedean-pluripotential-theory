## Candidate A

### Analytic points and the radicial map

After restriction to a strict affinoid target, the finite source is strict
affinoid.  A point of the target consists of a support prime together with a
rank-one absolute value on its residue field.  Finiteness and surjectivity
supply a prime upstairs; radiciality makes it unique and makes the residue
extension purely inseparable.  Such an absolute value has exactly one
extension, since the value of an element whose `p^r`-th power lies below is
forced by the `p^r`-th-root formula used in the proof.  Thus the induced map
on Berkovich points is bijective.  Compactness of the affinoid source and the
Hausdorff property of the affinoid target make it a homeomorphism.  Notice
also that the stronger displayed `q`-power hypothesis itself forces
uniqueness of a lifted seminorm, so no hidden separability assertion is used.

### From an arbitrary G-cover to finite rational charts

Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff Definition 5.1 is stated for a good strictly analytic
space over a nontrivially valued field and uses the G-topology generated by
strict affinoid domains.  Therefore the PL presentation may first be refined
to strict affinoid charts.  G-quasicompactness of the affinoid source yields a
finite subcover.  Gerritzen--Grauert then gives a finite rational refinement
of each affinoid domain in the ambient affinoid.  Restriction preserves every
analytic unit.  This justifies exactly the finite rational G-cover used in
the proof; it does not replace an arbitrary G-neighbourhood by an ordinary
neighbourhood.

### Descent of rational domains

For a rational presentation by `b_0,...,b_s`, the elements generate the unit
ideal.  Raising a Bezout identity to the `q`-th power is legitimate because
`q` is a power of the field characteristic: all cross terms vanish.  The
uniform hypothesis puts both `b_i^q` and the `q`-th powers of the Bezout
coefficients in the injective copy of the target algebra.  Hence the
descended `a_i=b_i^q` generate the unit ideal downstairs and define a genuine
rational domain.  At the unique lifted Berkovich point, the downstairs
inequality is precisely the `q`-th power of the upstairs inequality.  The
two pullback rational-domain structures consequently coincide by the
universal property of rational localization.  Their finite set-theoretic
cover is a G-cover.

### Descent of units

For an upstairs unit `t`, the proof descends both `t^q` and `t^{-q}`.  The
restricted target algebra is reduced because it is an analytic domain in a
reduced analytic space.  The restricted finite map is surjective, so the
kernel of its affinoid algebra homomorphism is nilpotent; reducedness makes
the homomorphism injective.  The equality `(t^q)(t^{-q})=1` therefore already
holds downstairs.  This proves actual analytic invertibility, rather than
incorrectly inferring it merely from pointwise nonvanishing.  Dividing the
real coefficients by `q` is allowed, and no integral- or rational-PL claim is
made.

These checks prove Candidate A exactly as stated.

## Candidate B

### The non-full-dimensional lattice calculation

The lattice
`N_sigma=N intersection span_R(sigma)` is saturated: if a nonzero integral
multiple of a lattice vector lies in that real vector subspace, then the
vector does as well.  A saturated subgroup of a finite free abelian group is
a direct summand.  The primitive ray generators of a simplicial cone are
linearly independent and span `N_sigma` over the reals, hence generate a
finite-index sublattice.  Adjoining a lattice complement gives the displayed
finite-index `N'` and, in the dual coordinates, the semigroup
`N^r direct-sum Z^(n-r)`.  Thus the smooth source really is
`A^r times G_m^(n-r)` even when the cone is not full dimensional.

### The finite diagonalizable quotient

For `M subset M'`, every monomial of the finitely generated semigroup algebra
upstairs is integral over the downstairs algebra after taking a suitable
positive power.  Hence the lattice morphism is finite; lying over gives
surjectivity.  The `H=M'/M` grading has degree-zero algebra exactly
`K[sigma^vee intersection M]`, so the target is the affine quotient by
`D(H)`.  No module-flatness conclusion follows or is used; the standard
quadratic-cone example in `HOSTILE_AUDIT.md` correctly excludes it.

### Tame and wild factors

In characteristic zero the entire finite diagonalizable kernel is etale.  In
characteristic `p>0`, the canonical decomposition
`H=H_p direct-sum H_{p'}` separates the genuinely wild part.  A finite
extension containing the roots of unity of exponent of `H_{p'}` splits only
the prime-to-`p` factor.  The polynomial producing those roots is separable
for exactly the reason stated in the proof.  The `p`-primary factor is never
called constant.

If `q=p^e` kills `H_p`, then `qM_1 subset M`.  Frobenius gives `B^q subset A`
term by term, including coefficients in a possibly imperfect field.  Prime
uniqueness follows from `b in q_1` implying `b^q` lies in the common base
prime and hence `b in q_2`; the residue extensions are purely inseparable by
the same `q`-power calculation.  Finiteness plus lying over supplies
surjectivity.  This verifies the wild map is finite, surjective, and
radicial.

### Affinoid base change and completion

For an arbitrary target strict affinoid domain with algebra `C`, the inverse
algebra is the completed tensor product with the finite algebra `B`.  Since
`B` is finite over `A`, the ordinary tensor product is a finite `C`-module,
is complete in its canonical affinoid topology, and equals the completed
tensor product.  Hence every element is an actual finite sum
`sum c_i tensor b_i`; there is no infinite-series issue.  Frobenius sends it
to `sum c_i^q tensor b_i^q`, which lies in the image of `C`.  The same
argument works after every affinoid base change (and in particular every
rational localization), providing exactly the uniform hypothesis of
Candidate A.  The target affinoid is reduced because the split toric variety
is geometrically integral and analytic domains preserve reducedness;
surjectivity then makes `C -> D` injective.

### The split constant quotient and Reynolds invariants

After the chosen root-of-unity extension, the prime-to-`p` diagonalizable
group is a finite constant group of nonzero order in the field.  For a target
affinoid algebra `C_1` and its finite inverse algebra `E`, the algebraic
Reynolds projector base-changes to the bounded `C_1`-linear idempotent
`|G|^{-1} sum_gamma gamma` on `E`.  It is bounded even if `|G|` is not a unit
of the valuation ring, since its inverse is still a field scalar.  Its image
is the base change of the degree-zero summand, namely `C_1`; conversely every
invariant element equals its Reynolds average.  Thus `E^G=C_1`, and the
restriction is the analytic categorical quotient required by the already
production-verified constant-group descent proposition.  No exactness of
invariants over the valuation ring and no finite-flat argument is used.

### Pluriharmonicity, scalar descent, and harmonicity

The inverse affinoid in the smooth toric source is a good strict
quasi-smooth analytic domain, so Dependency D1 applies without a
boundarylessness, separatedness, or global quasicompactness hypothesis.
Gubler--Rabinoff Proposition 7.2(6) gives the scalar-extension equivalence
for pluriharmonicity, and Proposition 7.2(7), forward direction, gives
arbitrary analytic pullback.  Constant-group descent followed, in positive
characteristic, by Candidate A therefore proves real-PL regularity on each
target affinoid over the splitting field.

Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff Corollary 5.12 is stated for a good strictly analytic
space, the completion of an algebraic extension, and a divisible coefficient
group.  Here the extension is finite and complete and the coefficient group
is `R`, so it descends real-PL regularity to `K`.  Finally,
Gubler--Rabinoff Theorem 4.3 applies over a nontrivially valued field to an
R-PL function on a good strict analytic space and identifies classical psh
with GJR semipositivity.  Applying it to both signs of the classically
pluriharmonic `h` gives precisely harmonicity under GJR Definition 7.2.

These checks prove Candidate B exactly as stated.
