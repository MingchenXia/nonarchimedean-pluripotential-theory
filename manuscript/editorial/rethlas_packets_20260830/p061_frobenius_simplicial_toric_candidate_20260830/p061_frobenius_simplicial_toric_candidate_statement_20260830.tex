\begin{proposition}[Candidate A]
Let \(K\) be a complete non-Archimedean field with nontrivial absolute
value and characteristic \(p>0\).  Let
\[
                         f:Y\longrightarrow X
\]
be a finite, surjective, radicial morphism of good strict \(K\)-analytic
spaces, and assume that \(X\) is reduced.  Suppose that there is a power
\(q=p^e\), with \(e\geq 0\), such that the following holds for every strict
affinoid domain \(U=\mathcal M(C)\) in \(X\).  If
\(f^{-1}(U)=\mathcal M(D)\), then the induced homomorphism
\(C\hookrightarrow D\) is injective and
\[
                              D^q\subseteq C.
\]
Here the inclusion means that \(d^q\) belongs to the image of \(C\) for
every \(d\in D\).

Let \(g:X\to\mathbf R\) be continuous.  If \(f^*g\) is
\(\mathbf R\)-PL, then \(g\) is \(\mathbf R\)-PL.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Candidate B]
Let \(K\) be a complete non-Archimedean field with nontrivial absolute
value, let \(N\) be a lattice, and let
\(\sigma\subset N_{\mathbf R}\) be a strongly convex simplicial rational
polyhedral cone, not necessarily full dimensional.  Let
\(X_{\sigma,N}\) be the associated split affine toric \(K\)-variety, and
let \(U\) be a good strict analytic domain in
\(X_{\sigma,N}^{\mathrm{an}}\).  Then every classically pluriharmonic
function
\[
                              h:U\longrightarrow\mathbf R
\]
is \(\mathbf R\)-PL.  Moreover, \(h\) is harmonic in the sense of
Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff.
\end{theorem}
