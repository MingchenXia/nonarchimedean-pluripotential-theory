Let \(B\) be a nonempty set.  Write \(X_B\) for the set obtained from
\(B\times[-\infty,0]\) by identifying all the points \((b,0)\) to one
point \(\eta\), and write
\[
                    p_b(t)=[(b,t)]\in X_B .
\]
We give \(X_B\) the topology of Chambert--Loir--Ducros,
Section~6.7.3: a subset \(V\subseteq X_B\) is open if and only if every
\[
 V_b=\{t\in[-\infty,0]:p_b(t)\in V\}
\]
is open in the order topology and, when \(\eta\in V\), the set of
\(b\in B\) for which \(V_b\ne[-\infty,0]\) is finite.  Thus this is not
the quotient topology when \(B\) is infinite.

A standard open neighbourhood of \(\eta\) is specified by a finite set
\(A\subseteq B\) and numbers \(R_a>0\), \(a\in A\), and has branches
\begin{equation}
 U_b=[-\infty,0]\quad(b\notin A),
 \qquad
 U_a=(-R_a,0]\quad(a\in A).
 \label{eq:p071-star-standard-neighbourhood}
\end{equation}
Every neighbourhood of \(\eta\) contains a standard one.

We use the exact smooth structure of that example.  A real-valued
function \(f\) on a neighbourhood of \(\eta\) is smooth precisely when
each \(f_b=f\circ p_b\) is ordinarily smooth on the finite stratum,
is constant on a neighbourhood of \(-\infty\) whenever that endpoint is
in its domain, only finitely many branches fail to be both full and
constant, and
\begin{equation}
                         \sum_{b\in B} f_b'(0)=0 .
 \label{eq:p071-star-smooth-balance}
\end{equation}
The sum in \eqref{eq:p071-star-smooth-balance} is finite.  For such a
smooth function, positivity of
\(\mathrm d'\mathrm d''f\) is equivalent to \(f_b''\geq0\) on every
finite branch stratum.  Let
\(\operatorname{Psh}_{X_B,0}\) be this sheaf of smooth psh functions,
let \(\operatorname{Psh}_{X_B}\) be the smallest subsheaf of the sheaf
of \(\mathbf R\cup\{-\infty\}\)-valued functions which contains
\(\operatorname{Psh}_{X_B,0}\) and is stable under pointwise decreasing
sequential limits, and let \(\operatorname{Psh}_{X_B,1}\) be the sheaf
of functions which are locally pointwise decreasing limits of sections
of \(\operatorname{Psh}_{X_B,0}\).

On a standard neighbourhood \(U\) as in
\eqref{eq:p071-star-standard-neighbourhood}, define
\(\mathscr J_B(U)\) as follows.  A function
\(u:U\to\mathbf R\cup\{-\infty\}\) belongs to \(\mathscr J_B(U)\) if:
\begin{enumerate}
\item every branch function \(u_b=u\circ p_b\) is extended-real convex
  on finite subintervals of its domain, and, for each full branch,
  \begin{equation}
   u_b(-\infty)=\lim_{t\to-\infty}u_b(t)
             \quad\text{in }\mathbf R\cup\{-\infty\};
   \label{eq:p071-star-endpoint-trace}
  \end{equation}
\item if \(c=u(\eta)\in\mathbf R\), then for every finite
  \(F\subseteq B\setminus A\), every \(r_b>0\), \(b\in F\), and every
  \(0<\rho_a<R_a\), \(a\in A\), one has
  \begin{equation}
   \sum_{b\in F}\frac{c-u_b(-r_b)}{r_b}
       \leq
   \sum_{a\in A}\frac{u_a(-\rho_a)-c}{\rho_a}.
   \label{eq:p071-star-finite-point-balance}
  \end{equation}
  The empty set \(F\) is allowed.
\end{enumerate}
On an arbitrary open subset of \(X_B\), define \(\mathscr J_B\) by these
conditions locally, using ordinary finite intervals away from \(\eta\),
the trace condition at an included endpoint \(-\infty\), and standard
neighbourhoods at \(\eta\).

\begin{theorem}
\label{thm:p071-infinite-star-collapse-candidate}
For the preceding CLD tropical star, one has an equality of sheaves
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{Psh}_{X_B,1}
   =\operatorname{Psh}_{X_B}
   =\mathscr J_B.
 \label{eq:p071-star-first-stage-collapse}
\end{equation}
More precisely, let \(u\in\operatorname{Psh}_{X_B}(V)\) and let
\(x\in V\).

If \(x=\eta\) and \(u(\eta)=-\infty\), there is one fixed open
neighbourhood \(W\subseteq V\) of \(\eta\) on which \(u\equiv-\infty\).

If \(x=\eta\) and \(c=u(\eta)\in\mathbf R\), there is one fixed standard
neighbourhood \(W\subseteq V\), with exceptional set \(A\), such that
all finite-stratum values of \(u|_W\) are real.  The endpoint derivatives
\begin{equation}
 d_i=D_-u_i(0)
     :=\lim_{r\downarrow0}\frac{c-u_i(-r)}{r}
 \label{eq:p071-star-endpoint-derivatives}
\end{equation}
are real, \(d_b\geq0\) for \(b\notin A\), and
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{b\in B\setminus A}d_b+\sum_{a\in A}d_a\leq0.
 \label{eq:p071-star-slope-balance}
\end{equation}
The first sum is the supremum of its finite subsums.  In particular it
is finite, and \(u_b\not\equiv c\) on at most countably many full
branches.  There are smooth psh functions \(g_n\) on that one fixed
neighbourhood \(W\) such that
\begin{equation}
                  g_{n+1}\leq g_n,
                  \qquad
                  g_n\longrightarrow u|_W
                  \quad\text{pointwise}.
 \label{eq:p071-star-fixed-neighbourhood-approximation}
\end{equation}
At a point different from \(\eta\), the same fixed-neighbourhood
conclusion holds on an ordinary interval or on a constant-collar
neighbourhood of \(-\infty\).
\end{theorem}
