## Scope of the retired atom

The theorem applies to continuous functions on open subsets of the
analytification of a smooth proper algebraic curve over an algebraically
closed, complete, nontrivially valued field.  In this scope it identifies:

1. positivity of the ordinary CLD current;
2. Thuillier subharmonicity; and
3. local membership in the first decreasing-limit stage generated by
   ordinary CLD-smooth psh functions.

It does not assert ordinary-smooth *harmonic* approximation, does not treat
upper-semicontinuous functions with value `-infinity`, and does not prove
first-stage collapse in arbitrary higher dimension.  Those boundaries are
preserved in the book.
