\begin{warning}{The continuous zero-current problem}
\Cref{prop:ch18-real-pl-zero-current-harmonic} settles the implication for
$\mathbf R$-PL functions under its hypotheses, while
\Cref{prop:p062-proper-full-rank-moment} reaches continuous functions already
factored through one proper full-rank moment with full open calibrated image.
The latter proposition does not construct such a factorization.  For an
arbitrary continuous function $u$ in higher dimension, it is still not known
in this generality whether $\dprimeddprime[u]=0$ forces harmonicity or
classical pluriharmonicity: there is no residual line bundle or integral
skeletal calibration from which to recover the face-balancing law, no proper
full-rank moment factor is supplied, and arbitrary CLD currents cannot simply
be pulled back to all curve probes.  CLD further note in
\cite[Remark~7.8.3]{CLD25} that it is not even known whether local
integrability plus $\dprimeddprime[u]=0$ forces continuity.  The published
continuous result on smooth proper algebraic curves is recorded in
\Cref{cor:ch19-continuous-zero-current-harmonic}; see also
\Cref{def:ch16-harmonic-function,sec:ch16-harmonic-ambiguity}.
\end{warning}

The proper full-rank moment-factor result
\Cref{prop:p062-proper-full-rank-moment} is a separate book-level deduction.
Its essential bridge is the proper calibrated-current identity
$F_*[h]=m[\varphi]$ from \cite[Proposition~5.10.1 and \S7.7.8]{CLD25},
followed by the Euclidean distributional-Hessian description in
\cite[\S1.6.4 and Proposition~8.3.2]{CLD25}.  Its terminal harmonic and
classical identifications use \cite[Definition~7.2 and
Remark~7.3(5),(6)]{GJR26} and
\cite[Proposition~7.2(2),(3)]{GR25Classical}.  The exact frozen statement and
proof passed production Rethlas run
\nolinkurl{20260829T031126Z_75e9cb498ce4}, item
\nolinkurl{pi_f96fe9f8904943602779f274}, final \texttt{correct}, with zero
critical errors and zero gaps.  No novelty or priority claim is made, and the
unrestricted continuous problem is not settled.

For an $\mathbf R$-PL function on a separated boundaryless pure-dimensional
space, \Cref{prop:ch18-real-pl-zero-current-harmonic} removes the weakly
smooth hypothesis when the equality is interpreted as an equality of CLD
currents.  The separate
\Cref{prop:p062-proper-full-rank-moment} reaches continuous functions of the
form $\varphi\circ F$ under its proper full-rank, full-image, and calibration
hypotheses.  Neither result identifies the weakly smooth and CLD calculi, and
neither extends the conclusion to an arbitrary continuous function outside
those factorized hypotheses.
