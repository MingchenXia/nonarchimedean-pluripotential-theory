This chapter gives a source-dated list of the main problems left by the
theories developed in this book.  Its literature cutoff is 24 August 2026,
with the book's verification record updated through 29 August 2026.  The list
is selective rather than encyclopedic: a problem is included when solving it
would remove a structural boundary between two parts of the subject, extend a
core existence theorem to its natural domain, or settle a repeatedly used
regularization or compactness gap.

\Cref{prop:p062-proper-full-rank-moment} supplies a further
higher-dimensional continuous scope.  If $h=\varphi\circ F$ factors through
one pleasant full-rank moment whose image is the nonempty connected open set
$F(X)=F(\Sigma_F^{(n)})$, if $F$ is proper in the compact-inverse-image
sense, and if the pushed-forward canonical calibration is the constant
positive multiplicity $m|e_1\wedge\cdots\wedge e_n|$, then
$\dprimeddprime[h]=0$ forces $\varphi$ to be affine.  Hence $h$ is ordinary
CLD-smooth, $\mathbf R$-PL, GJR harmonic, and classically pluriharmonic.  In
the boundaryless setting of \Cref{prob:frontier-pluriharmonic},
\Cref{cor:p062-proper-full-rank-moment-automatic-calibration} derives the
constant calibration from harmony and gives the equivalence between current
vanishing and affinity of $\varphi$.

The proof uses the exact proper-pushforward identity
$F_*[h]=m[\varphi]$ and Euclidean distributional-Hessian recognition; it does
not pull an arbitrary CLD current back to curves.  The exact frozen statement
and source-aware proof passed production Rethlas run
\nolinkurl{20260829T031126Z_75e9cb498ce4}, item
\nolinkurl{pi_f96fe9f8904943602779f274}, using
\texttt{gpt-5.6-sol} at \texttt{xhigh}, final \texttt{correct}, with zero
critical errors and zero gaps.

This does not close P06.2.  Outside the proper full-rank factor scope,
current vanishing for an arbitrary continuous function supplies neither the
required factor map nor a legitimate pullback to all curve probes, and the
higher-dimensional problem remains open.  In the published curve scope---an
open subset of the analytification of a smooth proper algebraic curve over an
algebraically closed complete nontrivially valued field---Wanner's comparison
does settle it; see
\Cref{cor:ch19-continuous-zero-current-harmonic}.
