\begin{lemma}
\label{lem:p213-arbitrary-valuation-source-localization}
Let $K$ be a complete non-Archimedean field, possibly trivially valued,
let $Y$ be a good separated normal $K$-analytic space that is purely
one-dimensional, let $y\in Y$ be nonrigid, and let $W$ be an open
neighborhood of $y$.  Let $\alpha$ be an ordinary CLD-smooth form near
$y$, and let $G$ be a finite analytic moment block near $y$.  If a smooth
source cutoff $\chi$ is to be retained, also regard $\chi$ as part of the
data.

After shrinking around $y$, there are a compact analytic neighborhood
$V\subset W$ of $y$, analytic moments $F_{\mathrm{bd}}$,
$F_{\mathrm{form}}$, and, when $\chi$ is present, $H$, and a smooth
superform $\eta$ such that, for
\[
 F=(F_{\mathrm{bd}},F_{\mathrm{form}},G,H):V\longrightarrow
       \mathbf G_{m,K}^{N},
\]
with the last block omitted when there is no $\chi$, one has
\begin{align}
 \alpha|_V&=F^*(\pi_{\mathrm{form}}^*\eta),
 \label{eq:p213-arbitrary-source-local-form}\\
 F_{\mathrm{trop}}(y)&\notin
       F_{\mathrm{trop}}(\partial(V/W)),
 \label{eq:p213-arbitrary-source-local-boundary}
\end{align}
and, when $\chi$ is present, there is a smooth function $\Phi$ on a
neighborhood of $H_{\mathrm{trop}}(V)$ such that
\begin{equation}
 \chi|_V=\Phi\circ H_{\mathrm{trop}}.
 \label{eq:p213-arbitrary-source-cutoff-factorization}
\end{equation}
Here $\pi_{\mathrm{form}}$ denotes projection to the
$F_{\mathrm{form}}$-coordinates.  In particular,
\begin{equation}
 F_{\mathrm{trop}}(z)=F_{\mathrm{trop}}(z')
       \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \chi(z)=\chi(z')
       \qquad(z,z'\in V).
 \label{eq:p213-arbitrary-source-cutoff-fibre-constancy}
\end{equation}

Put
\begin{equation}
 \Omega=\mathbf R^N\setminus
       F_{\mathrm{trop}}(\partial(V/W)),
 \qquad U=F_{\mathrm{trop}}^{-1}(\Omega)\subset V.
 \label{eq:p213-arbitrary-source-local-omega}
\end{equation}
Then $U$ is an open neighborhood of $y$ in $W$, and
$F_{\mathrm{trop}}|_U:U\to\Omega$ is proper.  If $\lambda$ is a smooth
function compactly supported in $\Omega$, with support in the domain of
$\pi_{\mathrm{form}}^*\eta$, then
\[
 F^*(\lambda\,\pi_{\mathrm{form}}^*\eta)
\]
has compact support in $U$ and extends by zero to an ordinary smooth form
on $W$.  It agrees with $\alpha$ wherever $\lambda=1$ on the tropical
image.

Consequently, let a compact test support consist of nonrigid points and fix
a finite cover of it by open source neighborhoods.  At each point the
construction can be
performed inside a prescribed member containing that point; compactness
then gives a finite subcover by the resulting neighborhoods.  Smooth
partitions of unity may be used to pass between the resulting local
comparisons, but a partition function is never asserted to be an analytic
tropical coordinate: if it must occur in a local moment presentation,
\eqref{eq:p213-arbitrary-source-cutoff-factorization} is used and the
analytic block $H$, not $\chi$, is appended.
\end{lemma}

\begin{remark}
\label{rem:p213-arbitrary-source-localization-scope}
The nonrigidity assumption is essential over a trivially valued field:
the boundary-avoiding moment statement fails at the rigid origin of the
open unit disc by \cite[Remark~12.3.7]{CLD25}.  The lemma makes no
strictness or rig-smoothness assumption.  It concerns ordinary CLD-smooth
forms; it does not assert the corresponding factorization for weakly
smooth forms presented only by harmonic tropicalizations.
\end{remark}
