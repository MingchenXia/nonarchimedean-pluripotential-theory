Let (u,v:(\mathbf C^n,0)\to\mathbf R\cup\{-\infty\}) be
plurisubharmonic germs, where (n\geq1), and normalize the centered
valuation space by \(\nu(\mathfrak m)=1\).  If
\[
              \nu_\varphi(u)=\nu_\varphi(v)
\]
for every tame plurisubharmonic weight \(\varphi\), then their
Boucksom--Favre--Jonsson valuative transforms agree pointwise:
\[
                         \widehat u=\widehat v
              \quad\hbox{on }\mathcal V.
\]
It is enough to impose the displayed equality for the analytic weights
\(\varphi_{\mathfrak a}=\log\max_j|f_j|\), where
\(\mathfrak a=(f_1,\ldots,f_N)\) ranges over the
\(\mathfrak m\)-primary ideals.
