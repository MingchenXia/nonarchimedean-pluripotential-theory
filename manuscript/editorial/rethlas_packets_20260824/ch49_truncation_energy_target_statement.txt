Let k be algebraically closed and trivially valued, let X be an integral unibranch projective k-variety of dimension n at least one, and let omega be the ample class of an ample line bundle, with volume V.  Use probability Monge--Ampere normalization, E_omega(0)=0, and E_omega(phi+t)=E_omega(phi)+t.  Let psi be omega-plurisubharmonic, let its finite supremum c equal psi at the trivial valuation, and suppose c-a <= psi <= c for some real a >= 0.  Prove that psi has finite energy and that c-n*a/(n+1) <= E_omega(psi) <= c.  More precisely, for the probability mixed measures mu_j=V^{-1}(omega+dd^c psi)^j wedge omega^{n-j}, prove mu_0 is the Dirac mass at the trivial valuation and (n+1)(E_omega(psi)-c)=sum_{j=1}^n integral(psi-c) d mu_j.  Deduce the oscillation and supremum-zero forms of the estimate.  The proof must expose the exact monotonicity, fixed-class approximation, decreasing-pairing continuity, mixed-measure duality and mass-one inputs, and must derive the bounded mixed-energy identity from the PL telescoping identity.  Also verify the complete adjacent sharpness remark by calculation: constant functions attain the upper bound; for every n the stated toric Fubini--Study function on projective n-space has supremum zero, lies between -1 and 0, and has energy -n/(n+1), so the lower coefficient is sharp; the maximal-ideal truncation on projective two-space has energy -1/3 rather than -2/3 by the displayed blowup intersection numbers; and the displayed deformation-to-the-normal-cone computation for a smooth conic supplies a non-toric equality case in dimension two.
