Under the standing Boucksom--Favre--Jonsson hypotheses, fix one SNC model on which the closed model form theta is determined, and let Delta be its finite dual complex.  Let F be a nonempty family of theta-plurisubharmonic functions on the Berkovich analytification, uniformly bounded above on the whole analytification.  Define u on Delta as the pointwise supremum of the restrictions to Delta of the members of F.  Prove that u is finite and continuous, and that there is an increasing sequence u_j such that every u_j is the restriction to Delta of a finite maximum of members of F and u_j converges uniformly to u on Delta.  The claim is strictly local to this one fixed dual complex: it makes no assertion that one sequence works simultaneously on all skeleta.
