## Mathematical scope

Let (K) be complete and nontrivially valued.  If

\[
C_1\times_K\cdots\times_K C_m\times_KY
\]

is good, the (C_i) are arbitrary good strict analytic curves, and (Y) is
good and strict with quasi-smooth reduction, then every classically
pluriharmonic function on the product is real-PL and harmonic in the sense of
Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff.  The same conclusion holds on the stated local open
analytic product models.  No reducedness or geometric-reducedness hypothesis
is imposed on the curves.  The case (m=0) includes nilpotent thickenings of
quasi-smooth spaces.

The exact firewall remains part of the theorem: reduced singular factors of
analytic dimension at least two, arbitrary nonproduct singular germs,
infinite products, nongood or nonstrict spaces, trivially valued fields, and
merely continuous zero-current functions are not covered.  P06.1 is narrowed
but not retired.
