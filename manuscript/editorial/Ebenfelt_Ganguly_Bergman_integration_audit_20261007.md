## Mathematical interface and qualifications

1. The kernel is defined with Euclidean volume. The source coefficient
   metric is partial-barpartial log K, its complex-trace Einstein scalar
   is -n and its determinant ratio is C_n=(n+1)^n pi^n/n!. The book's
   dd^c convention rescales the form by 1/(2pi); determinants and Ricci
   conventions are translated explicitly.
2. The positive-inside MA determinant and scalar defect give
   B=(n+1)^n J(K^(-1/(n+1))) and Scal+n=-Delta u. MA variation has
   leading coefficient m(n+2-m), while the complex harmonic indicial
   polynomial is lambda(lambda-n)/(n+1). The critical orders n+2 and n
   are not interchanged. An exact ball identity fixes signs and factors.
3. The dimension-two logarithmic kernel term cancels the r^3 MA term.
   The text defines c_0, C, eta_1 and polyhomogeneous notation and gives
   the checked Graham equation (4.11). The resulting r^4 defect is beyond
   harmonic root two. Higher dimensions use a separate curvature-invariant
   sphericity step and stronger scalar-defect information. MA obstruction
   flatness alone does not imply sphericity; Graham's examples are retained.
4. Full polyhomogeneous expansions with differentiated all-order remainders
   are attributed inputs. Finite Hölder boundary regularity alone is not
   substituted. The local MA and kernel inputs retain their distinct
   source scopes and hypotheses.
5. The extremal scalar-gradient field vanishes by transverse boundary
   uniqueness and the identity theorem, giving cscK and harmonic u.
   Flatness of u is then followed by a distinct unique-continuation step.
6. The real metric is g_R/[2(n+1)], with x=sqrt(r), a separately named
   geodesic defining function, volume growth e^(2ns) and Laplacian
   4(n+1) Delta_complex. The bent-end weight controls scaled lateral
   derivatives. Hörmander Theorem 28.2.3 assumes its full-symbol
   inequality (28.2.6); the local theorem alone does not supply the
   noncompact uniform estimate. EG supplies chart uniformity, commutator
   absorption and globalization. Those steps are attributed, not replaced
   by characteristic-set positivity alone.
7. At fixed weight gamma, all-order flatness permits M>n+gamma and
   integrable cutoff tails. Lateral and radial cutoffs are removed before
   gamma increases. The denominator-cleared real-analytic identity
   det(K K_{alpha barbeta}-K_alpha K_barbeta)=C_n K^(2n+1) propagates
   across possible kernel zeros; Gram positivity then gives nondegeneracy
   on K>0. Kernel positivity everywhere on an unbounded domain is not
   asserted.
8. Chapter 68 connects the fixed Bergman metric with its existing boundary
   and cscK comparisons. Arbitrary compact cscK metrics are not declared
   Einstein. The unpublished finite-type extension and unread original
   external proof inputs retain their source-audit gates. No result here
