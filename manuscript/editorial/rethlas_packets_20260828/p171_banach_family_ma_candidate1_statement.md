# Continuity of mixed Monge--Ampère measures over an integral Banach base

Resolve Pille--Schneider, *Global pluripotential theory on hybrid spaces*,
Question 4.9, in its literal stated scope. In the notation of Definition 4.8,
let \(A\) be an integral Banach ring, let \(X/A\) be an \(n\)-dimensional
projective scheme, and let \(L_1,\ldots,L_n\) be semiample line bundles on
\(X\). Assume in addition, as in Question 4.9, that \(X\) is flat over
\(A\), and let \(\phi_i\in\operatorname{CPSH}(L_i)\). For every
\(\psi\in C^0(X^{\mathrm{an}})\), determine whether
\[
 I_\psi(x):=
 \int_{X_{\mathcal H(x)}^{\mathrm{an}}}
 \psi|_{X_{\mathcal H(x)}^{\mathrm{an}}}\,
 \operatorname{MA}\bigl((\phi_1)_x,\ldots,(\phi_n)_x\bigr)
\]
is continuous on the Berkovich spectrum \(\mathcal M(A)\).

A complete proof must cover precisely these hypotheses, including arbitrary
integral Banach rings, real tropical weights, semiample bundles, nonreduced
and nonpure fibres in the source's conventions, and Archimedean-to-
non-Archimedean transitions. It must not assume the desired continuity of a
relative Deligne, Chow, resultant, Mahler, or Gauss metric. The user's
Deligne-pairing paper is known to contain an error and may not be used.
