or at most q negative Hessian eigenvalues in the smooth case. It differs
from p-plane trace positivity. A q-Bremermann function has the dual
(n-q-1)-super condition. Determinant zero is a smooth observation;
general continuous non-psh members are not assigned a Bedford--Taylor
positive measure. The diagonal (-2,0,1) example keeps that distinction
concrete. Addition increases q-indices; adding an ordinary psh witness
