\begin{corollary}
\label{cor:p061-v3-zariski-etale-finite-split-toric-charts}
Let \(K\) be complete and nontrivially valued.  Let \(\mathcal X\) be a
separated finite-type \(K\)-scheme admitting, Zariski locally on the
source, ordinary etale charts to products
\[
                         \mathcal Z\times_K\mathcal Y,
\]
where \(\mathcal Y\) is a smooth \(K\)-variety and, for every chart, there
is a finite analytic field extension \(L/K\) for which
\(\mathcal Z_L\) is a split toric \(L\)-variety.  Then every classically
pluriharmonic function on every ordinary open analytic subspace of
\(\mathcal X^{\mathrm{an}}\) is \(\mathbf R\)-PL and
Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic.
\end{corollary}
