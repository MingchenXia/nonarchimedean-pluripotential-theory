\begin{proof}
The invariant correspondence in \cite[Theorem~5.3.11]{BGJKK25}, for the
trivial toric line bundle and zero reference Green function, writes
\(u=\varphi\circ\operatorname{trop}\) for a finite convex function
\(\varphi:O\to\mathbf R\).  Choose a radial mollifier and a decreasing
sequence \(\varepsilon_j\downarrow0\) small enough that \(\overline B\subset
O_{\varepsilon_j}\) for every \(j\).  By \Cref{prop:ch09-radial-mollification}, the
convolutions
\[
                         \varphi_j:=\varphi*\rho_{\varepsilon_j}
\]
are smooth convex functions on \(B\), decrease pointwise and locally
uniformly to \(\varphi|_B\), and have positive
\(\mathrm d'\mathrm d''\)-Hessian.

Put \(u_j:=\varphi_j\circ\operatorname{trop}\).  The pullback construction
of \cite[Proposition~2.3.4 and Remark~5.3.8]{BGJKK25} makes \(u_j\) a
CLD-smooth function with positive Hessian current; equivalently, it is psh.
It is \(S\)-invariant by construction.  Pullback preserves the displayed
monotonicity and local uniform convergence, proving the claim.
\end{proof}
