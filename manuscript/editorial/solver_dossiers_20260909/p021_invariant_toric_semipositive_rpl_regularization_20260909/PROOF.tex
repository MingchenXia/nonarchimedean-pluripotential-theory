\begin{proof}
By \cite[Theorem~5.3.11]{BGJKK25}, write
\(u=\varphi\circ\operatorname{trop}\) for a finite convex function
\(\varphi:O\to\mathbf R\).  Choose an open Euclidean ball \(B'\) with
\(\overline B\subset B'\) and \(\overline {B'}\subset O\).  Radial
mollification gives smooth convex functions \(\varphi_j\) on \(B'\) which
decrease locally uniformly to \(\varphi\).  Put
\[
                         g_j:=\varphi_j+2^{-j}.
\]
Then \(g_j-g_{j+1}\geq2^{-(j+1)}\) on \(B\), and \(g_j\) still decreases
locally uniformly to \(\varphi\).

Put \(\delta_j:=2^{-(j+2)}\).  For each \(j\), compactness of \(\overline B\)
and continuity of the differential of the smooth convex function \(g_j\)
give finitely many tangent affine functions
\(\ell_{j,1},\ldots,\ell_{j,m_j}\) such that
\[
 g_j\leq q_j:=\max_{1\leq r\leq m_j}\ell_{j,r}+\delta_j
       \leq g_j+\delta_j
 \qquad\text{on }B.
\]
Indeed, every tangent affine function lies below \(g_j\); a finite subcover
of the neighbourhoods on which one tangent plane is within \(\delta_j\) of
\(g_j\) yields the displayed estimate.  Thus \(q_j\) is finite, convex, and
real piecewise affine on \(B\).  Moreover,
\[
 q_{j+1}\leq g_{j+1}+\delta_{j+1}
 \leq g_j-2^{-(j+1)}+2^{-(j+3)}
 \leq g_j\leq q_j.
\]
The two-sided estimate and the convergence of \(g_j\) show that
\(q_j\downarrow\varphi\) locally uniformly on \(B\).

Set \(v_j:=q_j\circ\operatorname{trop}\).  Each \(v_j\) is finite,
\(S\)-invariant, and real PL.  Since \(q_j\) is convex, the same toric
correspondence makes \(v_j\) psh, so its CLD Hessian current is positive.
The real-PL current comparison
\Cref{thm:p01-real-pl-positive-current-candidate} applies on \(W\) and makes
\(v_j\) semipositive.  Pullback preserves the displayed monotonicity and
local uniform convergence, proving the claim.
\end{proof}
