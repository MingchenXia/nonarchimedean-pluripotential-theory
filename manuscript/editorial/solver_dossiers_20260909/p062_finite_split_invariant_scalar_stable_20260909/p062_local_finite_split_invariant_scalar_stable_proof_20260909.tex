% Direct local-isomorphism proof for the finite-split invariant corollary,
% 9 September 2026.

\begin{proof}
Fix \(w\in W\), and choose the displayed data.  The transported function
\(\widetilde h\) on \(V\) satisfies every hypothesis of
\Cref{cor:p062-finite-split-invariant-scalar-stable-zero-current}.  Hence it
is locally \(\mathbf R\)-PL, Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic, and
classically pluriharmonic on \(V\).  Transfer these three local properties
back through \(\psi\).  Since \(w\) was arbitrary, they hold locally on
\(W\).
\end{proof}
