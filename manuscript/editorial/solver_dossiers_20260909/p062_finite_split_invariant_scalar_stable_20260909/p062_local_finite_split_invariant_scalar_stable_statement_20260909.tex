\begin{corollary}[Analytic-local finite-split scalar-stable invariant zero-current regularity]
\label{cor:p062-local-finite-split-invariant-scalar-stable-zero-current}
Let \(K\) be a complete non-Archimedean field with nontrivial valuation,
let \(W\) be a good strict \(K\)-analytic space, and let
\(h:W\to\mathbf R\) be finite and continuous.  Suppose that every point
\(w\in W\) has a good strict open neighbourhood \(U\), a \(K\)-variety
\(X\), a finite analytic field extension \(L/K\), a rational fan
\(\Sigma\), an \(L\)-isomorphism
\[
                         \iota:X_L\xrightarrow{\sim}X_\Sigma,
\]
an open \(O\subseteq N_\Sigma\), a good strict open subspace
\(V\subseteq X^{\mathrm{an}}\), and a \(K\)-analytic isomorphism
\(\psi:U\xrightarrow{\sim}V\), such that
\(\iota^{\mathrm{an}}\) identifies \(V_L\) with
\(\operatorname{trop}^{-1}(O)\).  Assume that the latter open subspace is
good and strict.  Write \(S\) for its maximal compact torus, and put
\(\widetilde h:=h|_U\circ\psi^{-1}\).  If \(\widetilde h_L\) is
\(S\)-invariant and
\[
                         \dprimeddprime[\widetilde h_L]=0,
\]
then \(h\) is locally \(\mathbf R\)-PL, Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic,
and classically pluriharmonic on \(W\).
\end{corollary}
