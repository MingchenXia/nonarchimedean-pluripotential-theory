\begin{corollary}[Analytic-local finite-split scalar-stable invariant zero-current regularity]
\label{cor:p062-local-finite-split-invariant-scalar-stable-zero-current}
Let \(K\) be a complete non-Archimedean field with nontrivial valuation,
let \(W\) be a good strict \(K\)-analytic space, and let
\(h:W\to\mathbf R\) be finite and continuous.  Suppose that every point
\(w\in W\) has a good strict open neighbourhood \(U\), a finite analytic
field extension \(L/K\), a rational fan \(\Sigma\), an open
\(O\subseteq N_\Sigma\), and an \(L\)-analytic isomorphism
\[
            \iota_U:U_L\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{trop}^{-1}(O).
\]
Assume that the latter open subspace is good and strict.  Via \(\iota_U\),
write \(S\) for its maximal compact torus.  If the scalar pullback of
\(h|_U\), transported through \(\iota_U\), is \(S\)-invariant and
\[
                     \dprimeddprime[(h|_U)_L]=0,
\]
then \(h\) is locally \(\mathbf R\)-PL, Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic,
and classically pluriharmonic on \(W\).
\end{corollary}
