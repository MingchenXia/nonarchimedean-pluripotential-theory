\begin{corollary}[Finite-split scalar-stable invariant zero-current regularity]
\label{cor:p062-finite-split-invariant-scalar-stable-zero-current}
Let \(K\) be a complete non-Archimedean field with nontrivial valuation, and
let \(X\) be a \(K\)-variety.  Suppose that a finite analytic field extension
\(L/K\), a rational fan \(\Sigma\), and an \(L\)-isomorphism
\[
                           \iota:X_L\xrightarrow{\sim}X_\Sigma
\]
are given, where \(X_\Sigma\) is the split toric \(L\)-variety associated to
\(\Sigma\).  Let \(O\subseteq N_\Sigma\) be open, and let \(V\) be a good
strict \(K\)-analytic open subspace of \(X^{\mathrm{an}}\) for which
\(\iota^{\mathrm{an}}\) identifies \(V_L\) with
\(\operatorname{trop}^{-1}(O)\).  Assume that this latter open subspace is
good and strict.  Write \(S\) for its maximal compact torus.  If
\(h:V\to\mathbf R\) is finite and continuous, its scalar pullback \(h_L\)
is \(S\)-invariant, and
\[
                         \dprimeddprime[h_L]=0,
\]
then \(h\) is locally \(\mathbf R\)-PL, Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic,
and classically pluriharmonic.
\end{corollary}
