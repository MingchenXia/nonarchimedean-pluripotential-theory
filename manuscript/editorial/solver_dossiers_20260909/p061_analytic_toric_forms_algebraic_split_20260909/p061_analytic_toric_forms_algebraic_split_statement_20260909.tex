\begin{corollary}[Algebraic-split analytic toric forms]
\label{cor:p061-algebraic-split-analytic-toric-forms}
Let \(K\) be complete and nontrivially valued, and let \(X\) be a good
strict \(K\)-analytic space.  Suppose that every point of \(X\) has an
ordinary open neighbourhood \(U\) for which there are a complete analytic
field extension \(L/K\), which is the completion of an algebraic field
extension of \(K\), a split toric \(L\)-variety \(\mathcal X\), and an
\(L\)-analytic isomorphism from \(U_L\) onto an ordinary open analytic
subspace of \(\mathcal X^{\mathrm{an}}\).  Then every classically
pluriharmonic function on every ordinary open analytic subspace of \(X\) is
\(\mathbf R\)-PL and Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic.

More generally, if \(Y\) is a good strict quasi-smooth \(K\)-analytic
space, the same conclusion holds on every ordinary open analytic subspace of
\(X\times_KY\), and on every good strict analytic space analytically
locally isomorphic to such an open subspace.
\end{corollary}
