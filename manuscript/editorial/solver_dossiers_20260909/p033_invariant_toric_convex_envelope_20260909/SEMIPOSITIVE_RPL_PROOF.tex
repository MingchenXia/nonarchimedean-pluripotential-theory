\begin{proof}
By \cite[Definition~7.8 and~(7.9)]{GJR26}, a locally Fubini--Study
function is real PL and semipositive at every point.  Hence every member of
\(\mathcal E^S_f(V)\) belongs to \(\mathcal S^S_f(V)\).  The reverse
inclusion is immediate from the second alternative in the definition of
\(\mathcal E^S_f(V)\), so the two displayed competitor classes agree.

Every affine function $\ell$ on $O$ pulls back to a real PL function
\(\ell\circ\operatorname{trop}\) on $V$.  Since $\ell$ is convex, the
toric correspondence in \cite[Theorem~5.3.11]{BGJKK25} makes this pullback
psh, hence its CLD Hessian current is positive.  The real-PL comparison
\Cref{thm:p01-real-pl-positive-current-candidate} then makes
\(\ell\circ\operatorname{trop}\) semipositive.

For every $x\in O$, the proof of
\Cref{prop:p033-invariant-toric-real-pl-envelope} supplies a supporting
affine function \(\ell_x\leq p\leq\varphi\).  The preceding paragraph gives
\(\ell_x\circ\operatorname{trop}\in\mathcal S^S_f(V)\), so
\[
 \sup_{u\in\mathcal S^S_f(V)}u\geq p\circ\operatorname{trop}.
\]
Conversely, every member of \(\mathcal S^S_f(V)\) has positive CLD Hessian
current by the same real-PL comparison, and hence belongs to
\(\mathcal P^S_f(V)\).  Its value is therefore at most
\(P^S_Vf=p\circ\operatorname{trop}\).  This proves the claim.
\end{proof}
