% Direct proof of the algebraic-split scalar-stable corollary, 9 September 2026.

\begin{proof}
Transport \(h_L\) through \(\iota\).  It is a finite continuous
\(S\)-invariant function on the good strict open inverse image
\(\operatorname{trop}^{-1}(O)\), and its CLD Hessian vanishes by hypothesis.
Thus the preceding invariant split-toric proposition makes \(h_L\) locally
\(\mathbf R\)-PL and classically pluriharmonic.

Because \(L\) is the completion of an algebraic extension of \(K\),
ground-field descent for \(\mathbf R\)-PL functions
\cite[Corollary~5.12]{GJR26} makes \(h\) locally \(\mathbf R\)-PL on
\(V\).  The converse scalar-extension implication in
\cite[Proposition~7.2(6)]{GR25Classical} makes \(h\) classically
pluriharmonic.  In particular, both \(h\) and \(-h\) are classically psh.
Since \(h\) is locally \(\mathbf R\)-PL on the good strict space \(V\),
\cite[Theorem~4.3]{GR25Classical} makes both signs semipositive.  By
\cite[Definition~7.2]{GJR26}, this is Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonicity.
\end{proof}
