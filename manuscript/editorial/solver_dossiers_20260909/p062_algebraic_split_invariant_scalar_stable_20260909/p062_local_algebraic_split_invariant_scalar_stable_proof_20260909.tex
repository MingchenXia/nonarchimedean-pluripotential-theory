\begin{proof}
Fix \(w\in W\), and choose the displayed data.  The scalar pullback
\((h|_U)_L\), transported through \(\iota_U\), satisfies every hypothesis of
\Cref{cor:p062-algebraic-split-invariant-scalar-stable-zero-current}.  Hence

they hold locally on \(W\).
