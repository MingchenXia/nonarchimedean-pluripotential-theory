\begin{corollary}[Algebraic-split analytic standard-etale toric charts]
\label{cor:p061-analytic-standard-etale-algebraic-split-forms}
Let \(K\) be complete and nontrivially valued, and let \(X\) be a good
strict \(K\)-analytic space.  Suppose that every point of \(X\) has an
ordinary open neighbourhood analytically isomorphic to an ordinary open
subspace of a strict affinoid space \(\mathcal M(C)\) with the following
presentation.  There are a \(K\)-variety \(\mathcal X\), a complete analytic
field extension \(L/K\) which is the completion of an algebraic field
extension of \(K\) and for which \(\mathcal X_L\) is a split toric
\(L\)-variety, a good strict quasi-smooth \(K\)-analytic space \(Y\), a
strict affinoid domain \(W=\mathcal M(A_0)\) in
\(\mathcal X^{\mathrm{an}}\times_KY\) which is an ordinary neighbourhood
of the image \(w\) of the chosen source point, and
\[
 C=A_0\langle T,U\rangle/
      \bigl(g(T),h(T)U-1\bigr),
\]
where \(g,h\in A_0[T]\), \(g\) is monic, and \(g'(T)\) is invertible in
\(C\).  Then every classically pluriharmonic function on every ordinary
open analytic subspace of \(X\) is \(\mathbf R\)-PL and
Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic.
\end{corollary}
