% Direct proof of the algebraic-split analytic standard-etale corollary.

\begin{proof}[Proof of
\Cref{cor:p061-analytic-standard-etale-algebraic-split-forms}]
Let \(\Omega\) be an ordinary open subspace of \(X\), let
\(h\in\operatorname{PH}(\Omega)\), and write
\[
                     \Pi:X_L=X\times_KL\longrightarrow X
\]
for scalar extension.  Fix an arbitrary point \(\widetilde x\) of
\(\Pi^{-1}(\Omega)\), and choose at \(x=\Pi(\widetilde x)\) one of the
displayed source charts.  The scalar extension of its presentation is
\[
 C_L=A_{0,L}\langle T,U\rangle/
       \bigl(g_L(T),h_L(T)U-1\bigr),
 \qquad A_{0,L}=A_0\widehat\otimes_KL.
\]
The scalar extension \(W_L\) remains a strict affinoid domain and is an
ordinary neighbourhood of the image of \(\widetilde x\): pull back an
ordinary open neighbourhood contained in \(W\).  Moreover,
\(\mathcal X_L\) is split toric, \(Y_L\) is good, strict, and
quasi-smooth, and monicity and derivative invertibility survive base change.
Classical pluriharmonicity is preserved by scalar extension
\cite[Proposition~7.2(6)]{GR25Classical}.  Thus the pointwise proof of
\Cref{cor:p061-analytic-standard-etale-all-orbits} applies to the lifted
chart.  It makes \(\Pi^*h\) locally \(\mathbf R\)-PL at
\(\widetilde x\).

The choice of \(\widetilde x\) was arbitrary, so \(\Pi^*h\) is locally
\(\mathbf R\)-PL on \(\Pi^{-1}(\Omega)\).  Since \(L\) is the completion
of an algebraic extension of \(K\), ground-field descent for
\(\mathbf R\)-PL functions \cite[Corollary~5.12]{GJR26} gives that \(h\)
is locally \(\mathbf R\)-PL on \(\Omega\).  Finally both \(h\) and \(-h\)
are classically psh.  Applying \cite[Theorem~4.3]{GR25Classical} to these
two \(\mathbf R\)-PL functions makes both signs semipositive, which is
Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonicity.
\end{proof}
