\begin{corollary}[Algebraic-split ordinary-etale toric charts]
\label{cor:p061-zariski-etale-algebraic-split-toric-charts}
Let \(K\) be complete and nontrivially valued.  Let \(\mathcal X\) be a
separated finite-type \(K\)-scheme admitting, Zariski locally on the
source, ordinary etale charts to products
\[
                         \mathcal Z\times_K\mathcal Y,
\]
where \(\mathcal Y\) is a smooth \(K\)-variety and, for every chart, there
is a complete analytic extension \(L/K\) which is the completion of an
algebraic field extension of \(K\) and for which \(\mathcal Z_L\) is a
split toric \(L\)-variety.  Then every classically pluriharmonic function
on every ordinary open analytic subspace of \(\mathcal X^{\mathrm{an}}\)
is \(\mathbf R\)-PL and Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic.
\end{corollary}
