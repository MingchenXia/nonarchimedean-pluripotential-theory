% Direct proof of the algebraic-split ordinary-etale corollary, 9 September 2026.

\begin{proof}[Proof of
\Cref{cor:p061-zariski-etale-algebraic-split-toric-charts}]
Let \(\Omega\subset\mathcal X^{\mathrm{an}}\) be ordinary open and let
\(h\) be classically pluriharmonic on \(\Omega\).  Fix a point of
\(\Omega\), choose a source-Zariski chart containing it, and replace
\(\Omega\) by its intersection with that chart.  Write the resulting chart
as an ordinary etale map
\[
              q:\mathcal U\longrightarrow\mathcal Z\times_K\mathcal Y
\]
and choose the displayed complete algebraic splitting extension \(L/K\).
After base change, \(q_L\) is a source-Zariski-local ordinary etale chart to
the product of the split toric \(L\)-variety \(\mathcal Z_L\) and the smooth
\(L\)-variety \(\mathcal Y_L\).

By \cite[Proposition~7.2(6),(7)]{GR25Classical}, the scalar pullback
\(h_L\) is classically pluriharmonic on the ordinary open subspace
\(\Omega_L\).  Applying
\Cref{cor:p061-v3-zariski-etale-toric-charts} over \(L\) shows that
\(h_L\) is \(\mathbf R\)-PL on \(\Omega_L\).  Since \(L\) is the
completion of an algebraic extension of \(K\),
\cite[Corollary~5.12]{GJR26} descends \(\mathbf R\)-piecewise linearity to
\(h\) on \(\Omega\).

Both \(h\) and \(-h\) are classically psh.  Since \(h\) is now locally
\(\mathbf R\)-PL, \cite[Theorem~4.3]{GR25Classical} makes both signs
semipositive.  By \cite[Definition~7.2]{GJR26}, this is precisely
Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonicity.  The source-Zariski chart domains cover
\(\mathcal X^{\mathrm{an}}\), proving the assertion.
\end{proof}
