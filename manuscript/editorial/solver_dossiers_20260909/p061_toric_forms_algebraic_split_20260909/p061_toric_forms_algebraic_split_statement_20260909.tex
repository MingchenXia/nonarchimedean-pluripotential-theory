\begin{corollary}[Algebraic-split toric forms]
\label{cor:p061-algebraic-split-toric-forms}
Let \(K\) be complete and nontrivially valued, and let \(X\) be a
\(K\)-variety.  Suppose that there is a complete analytic field extension
\(L/K\), which is the completion of an algebraic field extension of \(K\),
such that \(X_L\) is a split toric \(L\)-variety.  Then every classically
pluriharmonic function on every ordinary open analytic subspace of
\(X^{\mathrm{an}}\) is \(\mathbf R\)-PL and
Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic.  More generally, if \(Y\) is a good
strict quasi-smooth \(K\)-analytic space, the same conclusion holds on every
ordinary open analytic subspace of
\[
 X^{\mathrm{an}}\times_KY,
\]
and on every good strict analytic space analytically locally isomorphic to
such an open subspace.
\end{corollary}
