## Conditional estimates composed

The [FS composition](research_dossiers/li_uniform_fs_composition_20261004.md)
combines the fixed-matrix replacement, pointwise Bergman construction,
generic weighted Fourier lower bound and comparison truncation. Its
signature explicitly includes connected smooth projective fibres,
positive smooth raw volume, normalized fibrewise psh potentials and
uniform Skoda constants. The Bergman upper bound and generic lower bound
use the same raw volume; probability mass is measured separately.

Zero-mass chamber parts are removed, and only their active coefficient
rows are used in the minor selection. A surviving chamber contributes
a full basis, so the active matrix retains full rank. All possible active
row sets belong to a finite collection. Their replacement sections come
from one fixed inverse-minor family, and their common constants remain
independent of parameter, point and chosen generic region at fixed degree.
No infinite row weight is introduced.

The max FS potential lies below the original on the generic region. The
replacement bounds the upper difference by a degree error plus an error
vanishing after scaling. The max-truncation/strict-sublevel argument
bounds the lower difference with constants independent of degree. First
choose the discarded mass at that degree, then shrink that degree's
radius. This gives a uniform error \(C_*/l\); a radius uniform over all
degrees is neither asserted nor needed.

Skoda bounds the section suprema on the generic region from below,
including permitted section zeros. Polynomial model bounds then bound
the finite coefficient vector at each degree. The hybrid FS weights in
the degree-\(l\) convention are \(-lc_a\), not \(-c_a\).
Compactness for diagonal extraction takes place in finite real coefficient
spaces, not in the NA fibre.

## Topological passage written separately

The [hybrid-limit derivation](research_dossiers/li_hybrid_limit_passage_20261004.md)
adds the explicit projective hybrid, continuous-psh MA, volume-convergence
and NA uniqueness imports. It requires punctured-total-family continuity
of the original potential: fibrewise smoothness alone does not supply it.
The generic fibre is geometrically integral for the uniqueness step.

The log-map description reduces a central neighborhood to finitely many
model constraints on one common SNC model. At a point of the stratum
for the minimal face of a target log vector, choose local coordinates
with \(|z_i|=|t|^{w_i}\) and phases satisfying the exact monomial equation
\(\prod z_i^{b_i}=t\). This constructs a point of that neighborhood
on every sufficiently small complex fibre. Vertices and repeated central
components are included. The construction does not use the
top-dimensional-face restriction in the source's Lemma 5.12, and does
not replace ordinary density by an unjustified assertion about sequence
tails.

Real-weight FS continuity and the max's coefficient Lipschitz estimate
give joint continuity on the hybrid space times each coefficient box.
Diagonal coefficient limits yield uniformly Cauchy central FS metrics.
On the restricted sequence base, the extended potential is continuous
at zero, and its supremum normalization passes using a convergent subnet
of maximum points. A CLN/model-test comparison writes out the MA passage
on that restricted base; the full-disc Theorem 7.11 is not misquoted as
a theorem about arbitrary base subsets. Independent weak convergence of
the CY volume and NA uniqueness identify every extracted central limit.

For the final passage, each coefficient cluster vector gives a central
FS approximation within the degree error. Compare the moving FS family
to a fixed cluster-vector family. Compactness gives a convergent subnet
of alleged bad hybrid points, proving the uniform small-fibre bound.
This fixed continuous comparison supplies a neighborhood proof of full
hybrid continuity. Scalar-sequence uniqueness is not used as a sequential
continuity criterion on a nonmetrizable NA space. No psh condition in the
parameter is added, and no converse from weak MA convergence to C0
convergence is assumed.

## Remaining application and admission gates

The full chain now has a conditional FS approximation and limit passage.
Application to an unspecified degeneration still needs its faithful
geometric Laurent expansions, convergent section-module/base change,
projective/SNC model and meromorphic reference/volume inputs, generic-mass
and leading-remainder estimates, and uniform Skoda hypotheses. The
topological argument allows nonreduced SNC components, while the earlier
dyadic analytic proof has its own reduced-SNC restriction; they are not
silently conflated.

Singular or geometrically disconnected generic fibres, nonprojective
Kähler degenerations, mixed characteristic, zero volume and noncontinuous
NA psh metrics are outside this signature. Sequential compactness is used
only for the scalar parameter and finite dimensional coefficient boxes;
hybrid compactness uses subnets. New formal theorem promotion would also
need the repository's exact-current independent verification protocol.
None is performed or claimed here.
