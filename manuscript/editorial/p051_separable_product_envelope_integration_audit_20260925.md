# P05.1 separable product envelopes: integration audit — 25 September 2026

## Result integrated

For a finite product of rational polyhedral spaces and a separable obstacle
`u=sum_i p_i^*u_i`, the book now proves the exact identity

```text
P_{sum_i p_i^*gamma_i}(u)=sum_i p_i^*P_{gamma_i}(u_i)
```

whenever every factor envelope is real piecewise affine.  A product
polyhedral convex combination projects to one in every factor, while a slice
at rational fixed coordinates is a valid factor competitor.  Iterating
factorwise maximality gives the reverse inequality on rational product points;
continuity and density extend it to the whole product.

Combining the theorem with the already proved one-dimensional graph and
complete-affine regularity results gives real-PA envelopes for all separable
bounded obstacles on products of finite balanced graphs and complete affine
spaces.  The range includes higher-dimensional products with branched graph
factors.
