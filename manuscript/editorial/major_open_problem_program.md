## A. Local positivity and CLD--Gubler--Rabinoff theory

### OP-01. Pullback of positive PL currents to curves

- **Kind:** primary-source boundary, sharpened by the book.
- **Source:** Gubler--Rabinoff, arXiv:2503.05513, Remark 1.8 and Theorem 5.3.
- **Question:** does positivity of \(\mathrm d'\mathrm d''[h]\) for a real PL
  function imply residue-nef semipositivity?
- **Status:** the exact conditional curve-restriction implication is
  verified; a coarse tropical restriction principle is disproved; the
  analytic positive-current converse remains open.
- **Packet:** cld_current_curve_pullback.md (run completed; use only the
  dispositions recorded in the attempt ledger).

### OP-02. Regularization of arbitrary classical psh functions

- **Kind:** book synthesis of open boundaries in GR, GJR, Wanner, and CLD.
- **Question:** is every classically psh function locally a decreasing limit
  of local Fubini--Study, semipositive PL, weakly smooth psh, or CLD-smooth
  psh functions?
- **Status:** open beyond the real-PL and curve loci; one run produced no
  verified blueprint.
- **Packet:** classical_psh_regularization.md.

### OP-03. Upper-semicontinuous regularization of local envelopes

- **Kind:** book-formulated local analogue of global envelope theory.
- **Question:** when is the usc regularization of the local FS/semipositive-PL
  envelope classically psh, CLD psh, or continuous?
- **Status:** open; the raw supremum is psh only when already usc, and the
  missing step is compatibility of usc regularization with all analytic
  curve pullbacks.
- **Packet:** local_fs_envelope.md.

### OP-04. A common singular local Monge--Ampère product

- **Kind:** book-formulated comparison problem.
- **Verified core:** ideal/model data and rational semipositive data on
  algebraizable common log models; the explicitly defined joint bounded
  closure; finite-map projection; exact BFJ--CLD normalization.
- **Disproved overextensions:** probability normalization under literal
  restriction, and uniqueness of an unrestricted unbounded continuation.
- **Open remainder:** arbitrary nonalgebraizable analytic models, arbitrary
  bounded classical psh functions outside the joint closure, finite-energy
  polar-mass rules, and singular/nonnormal germs.
- **Packet:** local_singular_ma_comparison.md (verified receipt only in the
  stated restricted framework).

### OP-05. Balanced-polyhedral regularity and orthogonality

- **Kind:** three BMPS source directions plus two stronger book questions.
- **Source questions:** higher-dimensional polyhedral smoothness implying
  regularity and orthogonality; characterization of Calabi--Yau
  tropicalizations with those properties; matroids whose Bergman fans have
  orthogonality.
- **Book questions:** an intrinsic singular-fan contact theorem and recursive
  calibrated comparison with CLD/delta/non-Archimedean measures.
- **Verified core:** complete affine space, including contact and
  orthogonality; exact factorial normalization; strict cross-fan
  counterexample; conditional recursive analytic comparison.
- **Status:** the general higher-dimensional and analytic-realization
  questions remain open.
- **Packets:** balanced_polyhedral_regularity.md (run and verified in its
  exact core) and bmps_exact_source_questions.md (prepared, not run).

### OP-06. Pluriharmonic regularity

- **Kind:** source questions from GR/GJR/Wanner.
- **Question:** remove the extra hypotheses in GR Proposition 7.8; recover a
  harmonic representative from distributional current vanishing without
  weak smoothness; obtain CLD-smooth monotone regularization on every smooth
  Berkovich curve.
- **Status:** weakly smooth current-zero functions are harmonic PL; all three
  unrestricted upgrades remain open.
- **Packet:** pluriharmonic_regularity.md.

### OP-07. Early collapse of the CLD psh hierarchy

- **Kind:** corrected book formulation prompted by CLD.
- **Source correction:** CLD Lemma 8.1.4 proves eventual cardinal
  stabilization, while Remark 8.1.6 literally says stationarity is unknown.
  The meaningful analytic question is collapse at stage one, or at a
  uniformly controlled early ordinal.
- **Status:** open; no example of genuine second-stage rank is known at the
  cutoff.
- **Packet:** cld_psh_early_collapse.md (prepared, not run).

### OP-08. Local psh metrics versus global psh metrics

- **Kind:** two primary-source questions.
- **Source:** CLD Remark 17.8.10.
- **Questions:** does a continuous locally CLD-psh metric on a projective
  variety admit global smooth-psh approximation; and does CLD Corollary

  projective special fibre?
- **Status:** open outside the common semiample/formal/model loci.
- **Packet:** cld_local_global_psh_metrics.md (prepared, not run).

### OP-09. Two remaining Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff questions

## B. Valuative singularities and the trivially valued frontier

### Resolved history: generalized Lelong numbers

- **Former question:** does equality against every tame generalized Lelong
  weight force equality of valuative transforms?
- **Answer:** yes.  The proof in
  `thm:local-generalized-lelong-separation` shows that primary-ideal weights
  already suffice.  Product ideals give exact polarization to all Cartier
  \(b\)-divisor inputs, and a negative-definite relative Hodge-index form
  separates the trace on every model.
- **Verification:** the exact frozen statement and source-aware proof passed
  production Rethlas run
  `20260824T140127Z_ef8316d19c12`, `gpt-5.6-sol`/`xhigh`, final `correct`,
  zero critical errors and zero gaps.  An independent hostile audit found no
  defect before integration; the run is not claimed as a post-integration
  byte-identity gate.
- **Provenance:** a dated book-level derivation, without a priority claim.
- **Verification record:**
  `editorial/rethlas_receipts_20260824/ch70_lelong_separation_exact_current_receipt.md`;
  despite the historical filename, it controls the frozen submitted packet.

### OP-12. Functorial local theory on singular germs

- **Kind:** book-formulated extension programme.
- **Question:** build normalization, conductor/branch descent, envelopes,

- **Status:** open.

### Resolved history: the former trace item

- **Source boundary:** the complete trace theorem in `DX24`, as
  reproduced in the supplied book chapter, proves that trace depends only on

### OP-13. Finite-energy equations on polarized affine cones

- **Kind:** source-stated comparison and Monge--Ampère image problem.
- **Source boundary:** Wu's fixed-Reeb operator is defined on
  \(\mathcal E^1(\xi)\), is continuous along decreasing nets, and is
  bi-Lipschitz onto its image. Proposition 3.3 compares only proportional
  rescalings of one Reeb ray.
- **Question:** compare \(\PSH(\xi)\) for nonproportional Reeb fields well
  enough to obtain uniform differentiability; then characterize the image in
  \(\mathcal M^1(\xi)\) and solve the local finite-energy Monge--Ampère
  equation exactly on that image.
- **Status:** open. Rational Reeb approximation does not provide the required
  uniform cross-polarization estimates, and the source expressly does not
  claim surjectivity.
- **Packet:** `affine_cone_finite_energy_equation.md` (prepared, not run).

Three items from that pass bear on this programme because they change how the
problems should be *stated*, not what is proved:

- **OP-07.** The source diagnosis is now exact. Eventual stationarity of the CLD
  hierarchy is proved in CLD Lemme 8.1.4 by a cardinality argument; the literal
  sentence of Remarque 8.1.6 denying it is superseded by the authors' own lemma;
  and CLD Définition 8.1.13 already names the live property, *localement
  approchable*. A further claim made in the first draft of that pass — that
  collapse at any finite stage is equivalent to collapse at stage one — was
  **withdrawn as unproved** after an independent audit in the same session; the
  reduction lemma at stage two does not exclude an intermediate finite regime.
- **OP-14, DXZ subquestion.** In the zero-mass \(\tau\)-range the filtration
  maximality conjecture subsumes the book's already-open zero-mass
  \(\mathcal I\)-model problems. It should be stated for the positive-mass range,
  with the zero-mass range explicitly deferred, rather than as one
  undifferentiated conjecture.
- **OP-05, BMPS matroid direction.** The Bergman fan of a rank-\(r\) matroid has
  dimension \(r-1\), so every rank-two matroid falls under the source's own
  one-dimensional theorem. The matroid question concerns rank \(\ge3\) only, and
  the \(U_{3,4}\) obstruction is a failure of the growth hypotheses on
  \(\gamma\), not of the fan.
