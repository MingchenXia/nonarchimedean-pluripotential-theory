\begin{proof}
Let \((u_j)_j\) be the sequence supplied by
\Cref{cor:p024-invariant-toric-cld-smooth-regularization}.  Each \(u_j\) is
an ordinary CLD-smooth psh function.  The canonical inclusion of ordinary
CLD-smooth forms into weakly smooth forms in
\cite[Proposition~10.14]{GJR26} therefore makes every \(u_j\) weakly smooth.
Here ``psh'' is unchanged: in the terminology of \cite[\S7.13]{GJR26}, it
means positivity of the CLD current
\(\mathrm d'\mathrm d''[u_j]\), which is precisely the positivity already
proved in the cited corollary.  Thus each \(u_j\) is weakly smooth psh.
The pointwise decrease, local uniform convergence, finiteness, and
\(S\)-invariance are the corresponding conclusions of that corollary.
\end{proof}
