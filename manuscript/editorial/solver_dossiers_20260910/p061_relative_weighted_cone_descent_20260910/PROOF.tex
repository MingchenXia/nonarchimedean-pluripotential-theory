\begin{proof}
Choose a strict affinoid ordinary neighbourhood \(V\) of \(y\) contained
in \(s^{-1}(\Omega)\), and fix \(\varpi\in K\) with
\(0<|\varpi|<1\).  For \(n\geq1\), let \(U_n(V)\) be the displayed
weighted domain.  It is strict affinoid: after rescaling the coordinates by
the radii \(|\varpi|^{nw_i}\), it is the corresponding quotient of a strict
Tate algebra over the strict affinoid algebra of \(V\).  The domains are
nested compact subsets of \(U_1(V)\), and
\[
 \bigcap_{n\geq1}U_n(V)=s(V).
 \tag{\(\clubsuit\)}
\]
Indeed, every \(T_i\) vanishes at a point of the intersection, so the
defining evaluation factors through the zero section.  Since
\(s(V)\subset\Omega\), compactness applied to the nested closed subsets
\(U_n(V)\setminus\Omega\) of \(U_1(V)\) gives \(U_n(V)\subset\Omega\)
for some \(n\).  The choice of \(V\) makes \(y\) an ordinary interior
point of \(V\), and all the displayed coordinate inequalities are strict at
the zero section.  Thus \(s(y)\) lies in the ordinary interior of this
\(U_n(V)\).  This is an ordinary-topology argument; it does not identify an
analytic fibre product with a Cartesian topological product.

Fix \(x\in U_n(V)\), including a nonrigid point, put
\(H:=\mathscr H(x)\), and write \(a_i:=T_i(x)\in H\).  The map from the
affinoid closed unit \(H\)-disc \(D:=\mathcal M(H\langle\tau\rangle)\)
defined by
\[
 b\longmapsto b(x)\quad(b\in\mathcal O(V)),
 \qquad T_i\longmapsto a_i\tau^{w_i}
 \tag{\(\spadesuit\)}
\]
is bounded by the defining inequalities for \(U_n(V)\).  If \(F_j\) has
weighted degree \(d_j>0\), its image is
\[
 \tau^{d_j}F_j\bigl(a_1,\ldots,a_m\bigr)=0.
\]
Hence \((\spadesuit)\) defines an \(H\)-analytic morphism
\[
q_x:D\longrightarrow (U_n(V))_H.
\]
At \(\tau=1\) it is the canonical \(H\)-rational lift of \(x\), while at
\(\tau=0\) its projection to \(X\) is \(s(p(x))\).

Classical pluriharmonicity is stable under analytic scalar extension and
analytic pullback \cite[Proposition~7.2(6),(7)]{GR25Classical}.  Thus the
pullback of \(h\) by \(q_x\) is classically pluriharmonic on \(D\).  The
affinoid maximum--minimum principle on the closed disc
\cite[Proposition~7.3]{GR25Classical}, whose Shilov boundary is a singleton,
makes this pullback constant.  Evaluation at \(0\) and \(1\), followed by
forgetting the scalar extension, gives
\[
 h(x)=h\bigl(s(p(x))\bigr).
\]
As this holds for every point of \(U_n(V)\), it proves
\eqref{eq:p061-relative-weighted-cone-factorization}.  The same analytic
pullback assertion shows that \(g=s^*h|_V\) is classically
pluriharmonic.

If \(Y\) is quasi-smooth, \(g\) is locally \(\mathbf R\)-PL by
\Cref{thm:p061-quasismooth-ph-rpl}.  Pullback stability of
\(\mathbf R\)-PL functions \cite[Lemma~5.4(1)]{GJR26} and
\eqref{eq:p061-relative-weighted-cone-factorization} make \(h\) locally
\(\mathbf R\)-PL at \(s(y)\).  Finally, the two signs of \(h\) are
classically psh, hence semipositive by \cite[Theorem~4.3]{GR25Classical};
this is Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonicity
\cite[Definition~7.2]{GJR26}.
\end{proof}
