\begin{theorem}[Pluriharmonic constancy at a positive-weighted cone vertex]
\label{thm:p061-positive-weighted-cone-vertex-constancy}
Let \(K\) be a complete non-Archimedean field with nontrivial absolute
value, let \(w_1,\ldots,w_m\) be positive integers, and let
\(I\subset K[T_1,\ldots,T_m]\) be a proper ideal generated by
polynomials homogeneous for the weights \(w_i\).  Put
\[
 X:=\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(K[T_1,\ldots,T_m]/I\bigr),
 \qquad o:=(T_1=\cdots=T_m=0)\in X(K).
\]
For every ordinary open neighbourhood \(\Omega\) of \(o\) in
\(X^{\mathrm{an}}\), every classically pluriharmonic function
\(h:\Omega\to\mathbf R\) is constant on an analytic neighbourhood of
\(o\).  In particular, its germ at \(o\) is \(\mathbf R\)-PL and
Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic.
\end{theorem}
