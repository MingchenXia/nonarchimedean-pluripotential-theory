\begin{proof}
Choose \(\varpi\in K\) with \(0<|\varpi|<1\).  For \(n\geq1\), let
\[
 U_n:=\left\{x\in X^{\mathrm{an}}:
 |T_i(x)|\leq |\varpi|^{nw_i}\ \text{for }1\leq i\leq m\right\}.
 \tag{\(\heartsuit\)}
\]
Each \(U_n\) is a strict affinoid domain: it is the inverse image under the
closed immersion \(X^{\mathrm{an}}\hookrightarrow\mathbf A_K^{m,\mathrm{an}}\)
of a closed polydisc whose polyradii lie in \(|K^\times|\).  The point \(o\)
is in its ordinary interior, the domains are nested, and
\[
                    \bigcap_{n\geq1}U_n=\{o\}.
 \tag{\(\heartsuit_1\)}
\]
Indeed, a point in the intersection annihilates every coordinate \(T_i\),
and hence is the evaluation point at the vertex.  The \(U_n\) are cofinal
among ordinary neighbourhoods of \(o\).  For otherwise, if an ordinary
neighbourhood \(V\) contained no \(U_n\), the nonempty closed sets
\(U_n\setminus V\) would be nested in the compact space \(U_1\); their
intersection would be nonempty, contradicting \((\heartsuit_1)\).

Choose \(n\) with \(U_n\subset\Omega\).  It suffices to show that
\(h|_{U_n}\) is constant.  Fix \(x\in U_n\), write
\(H:=\mathscr H(x)\), and set \(a_i:=T_i(x)\in H\).  On the closed unit
\(H\)-disc \(D:=\mathcal M(H\langle\tau\rangle)\), the assignments
\[
                        T_i\longmapsto a_i\tau^{w_i}
 \tag{\(\heartsuit_2\)}
\]
define an analytic morphism \(q_x:D\to (U_n)_H\).  The inequalities in
\((\heartsuit)\) prove boundedness.  If \(F\) is a weighted-homogeneous
generator of \(I\), of weighted degree \(d\), then its image under
\((\heartsuit_2)\) is \(\tau^dF(a_1,\ldots,a_m)=0\); thus all the
relations are respected.  At \(\tau=1\), the morphism gives the canonical
\(H\)-rational lift of \(x\), while at \(\tau=0\) it gives the lift of
the vertex \(o\).

Classical pluriharmonicity survives analytic scalar extension and analytic
pullback \cite[Proposition~7.2(6),(7)]{GR25Classical}.  Thus the pullback of
\(h\) to \(D\) through \(q_x\) is classically pluriharmonic.  The closed
unit disc has singleton Shilov boundary, so its affinoid
maximum--minimum principle \cite[Proposition~7.3]{GR25Classical} forces this
pullback to be constant.  Evaluating at \(0\) and \(1\), then forgetting
the scalar extension, gives
\[
                              h(x)=h(o).
\]
As \(x\) was arbitrary, \(h\) is constant on \(U_n\).

The ordinary interior of \(U_n\) is therefore a neighbourhood of \(o\) on
which \(h\) is constant.  A constant is \(\mathbf R\)-PL, for example as a
real multiple of \(\log|a|\) for a constant \(a\in K^\times\) with
\(|a|\ne1\) \cite[Definition~5.1]{GJR26}.  Both signs remain classically psh, hence are
semipositive by \cite[Theorem~4.3]{GR25Classical}; this is exactly
Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonicity \cite[Definition~7.2]{GJR26}.
\end{proof}
