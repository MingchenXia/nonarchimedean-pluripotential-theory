\begin{proposition}[Zero-current descent through locally trivial projective bundles]
\label{prop:p062-projective-bundle-descent}
Let \(K\) be a complete non-Archimedean field with nontrivial absolute
value, let \(m,q\geq1\), and let \(B\) be a good, topologically
separated, locally holomorphically separated, paracompact, pure
\(m\)-dimensional strict \(K\)-analytic space.  Let \(\pi:Y\to B\) be a
morphism, where \(Y\) is a good, topologically separated, pure
\((m+q)\)-dimensional strict \(K\)-analytic space.  Suppose that \(B\) has
an analytic open cover by \(U\)'s for which there is an isomorphism over
\(U\)
\[
       \pi^{-1}(U)\simeq U\times_K(\mathbf P^q_K)^{\mathrm{an}}.
\]
If \(h\in C^0(Y,\mathbf R)\) satisfies
\(\mathrm d'\mathrm d''[h]=0\), then there is a unique
\(b\in C^0(B,\mathbf R)\) such that
\[
                    h=\pi^*b,
                    \qquad
                    \mathrm d'\mathrm d''[b]=0.
\]
Moreover, \(\mathrm d'[h]=0\) implies \(\mathrm d'[b]=0\).
\end{proposition}
