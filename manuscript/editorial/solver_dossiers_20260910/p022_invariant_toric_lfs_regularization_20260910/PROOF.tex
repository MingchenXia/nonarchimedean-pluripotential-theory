\begin{proof}
By \cite[Theorem~5.3.11]{BGJKK25}, write
\(u=\varphi\circ\operatorname{trop}\) for a finite convex function
\(\varphi:O\to\mathbf R\).  Since \(O\) is open and convex, the compact
convex hull of \(\overline B\) has an open convex neighbourhood \(B'\)
with \(\overline B\subset B'\Subset O\).  The radial regularization of
\Cref{prop:ch09-radial-mollification} supplies smooth convex functions
\(\varphi_j\) on \(B'\), decreasing locally uniformly to \(\varphi\).
Put
\[
               g_j:=\varphi_j+2^{-j}.
\]
Then
\[
                        g_{j+1}\leq g_j-2^{-(j+1)}
\tag{1}\label{eq:p022-strict-smooth-gap}
\]
on \(\overline B\).

Set \(\delta_j=\eta_j:=2^{-(j+4)}\).  Compactness of \(\overline B\)
and the supporting-plane formula for the smooth convex function \(g_j\)
give finitely many affine functions \(\lambda_{ij}\) such that
\[
 g_j-\delta_j\leq
 p_j:=\max_i\lambda_{ij}\leq g_j
\quad\hbox{on }\overline B.
\tag{2}\label{eq:p022-finite-support}
\]
Put \(q_j:=p_j+\delta_j\), so
\[
                         g_j\leq q_j\leq g_j+\delta_j.
\tag{3}\label{eq:p022-q-bound}
\]

Let \(M=N^\vee\) be the character lattice of \(T\).
We use the following elementary approximation of the finitely many affine
summands of \(q_j\).  If
\(\lambda(x)=\langle a,x\rangle+b\) is affine on \(\overline B\) and
\(\eta>0\), there are an integer \(r\geq1\), an
\(m\in M\simeq\mathbf Z^n\), and a
\(c\in-\log|K^\times|\) for which
\[
 0\leq r^{-1}\bigl(\langle m,x\rangle+c\bigr)-\lambda(x)\leq\eta
\quad\hbox{on }\overline B.
\tag{4}\label{eq:p022-lattice-affine}
\]
Indeed, choose a nonzero \(\gamma\in-\log|K^\times|\).  For sufficiently
large \(r\), round \(ra\) to \(m\), and then choose
\(c\in\gamma\mathbf Z\) just above the scalar needed to compensate for
the minimum of
\(\langle r^{-1}m-a,x\rangle\) on \(\overline B\).  The rounding error
and the mesh \(|\gamma|/r\) can both be made smaller than any prescribed
fraction of \(\eta\).  This proves \eqref{eq:p022-lattice-affine}; one
common sufficiently large \(r\) works for every member of a finite family.

Apply \eqref{eq:p022-lattice-affine}, with error \(\eta_j\), to every
affine summand \(\lambda_{ij}+\delta_j\) in \(q_j\), using a common
integer \(r_j\).  Let \(L_{ij}\) be the resulting affine functions and put
\[
                          Q_j:=\max_i L_{ij}.
\]
Equations \eqref{eq:p022-finite-support}--\eqref{eq:p022-lattice-affine}
give
\[
                    q_j\leq Q_j\leq q_j+\eta_j
\quad\hbox{on }\overline B.
\tag{5}\label{eq:p022-lattice-max-bound}
\]
Write
\(L_{ij}=r_j^{-1}(\langle m_{ij},\cdot\rangle+c_{ij})\) and choose
\(a_{ij}\in K^\times\) with
\(-\log|a_{ij}|=c_{ij}\).  The torus convention
\(-\log|\chi^m|=\langle m,\operatorname{trop}\rangle\) gives
\[
 Q_j\circ\operatorname{trop}
 =\frac1{r_j}\max_i\{-\log|a_{ij}\chi^{m_{ij}}|\}.
\tag{6}\label{eq:p022-lfs-normal-form}
\]
Consequently
\[
                         v_j:=Q_j\circ\operatorname{trop}
\]
is locally Fubini--Study by \cite[\S7.9]{GJR26}; the formula is valid on
the common domain \(\operatorname{trop}^{-1}(B)\), not merely on
neighbourhoods selected separately for different \(j\).

Finally, \eqref{eq:p022-strict-smooth-gap},
\eqref{eq:p022-q-bound}, and \eqref{eq:p022-lattice-max-bound} show
\[
\begin{aligned}
 Q_{j+1}
 &\leq g_{j+1}+\delta_{j+1}+\eta_{j+1}\\
 &\leq g_j-2^{-(j+1)}+\delta_{j+1}+\eta_{j+1}
 \leq g_j\leq q_j\leq Q_j.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus \(v_{j+1}\leq v_j\).  The same bounds give
\[
                 g_j\leq Q_j\leq g_j+\delta_j+\eta_j,
\]
so \(Q_j\) decreases locally uniformly to \(\varphi|_B\).  Pullback by
\(\operatorname{trop}\) preserves this convergence and the
\(S\)-invariance, which proves the result.
\end{proof}
