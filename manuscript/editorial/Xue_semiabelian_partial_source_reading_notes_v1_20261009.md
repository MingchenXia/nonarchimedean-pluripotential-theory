a homomorphism is determined by its toric and abelian maps with the

toric convention. The smooth-fan Picard decomposition is recalled through

geometric degree, although the general introductory Definition 3.21

and measure definitions explicitly require positive degree. These
normalizations must not be applied to a zero-degree restricted cycle.
Quasi-canonical means normalized variety height equals essential minimum,
with positive geometric degree. Convergence of all generic small orbit
