## Scope firewall

The theorem proves the natural positive-cone analogue of the source question
whenever the residue model has dimension at most one.  It includes all good
strict analytic curves, allows boundary points, and assumes no relation
between the coefficient group and the value group.  It does not solve the
unrestricted higher-dimensional question: there the nef cone need not be a
single rational half-space.  No novelty or priority claim is made without a
separate literature comparison.
