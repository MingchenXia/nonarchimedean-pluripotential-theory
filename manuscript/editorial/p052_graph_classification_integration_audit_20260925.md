## Result integrated

be the cone of nonnegative balancing relations among the primitive outgoing
directions at a vertex `v`.  For every real-piecewise-affine polyhedrally
convex reference positive at infinity, real-polyhedral orthogonality holds if
and only if `dim K_v=1` at every vertex.

raised above the reference near any selected vertex and cut off by a maximum.

Monge--Ampere mass.  At a higher-rank vertex, projection of the local slope
vector to the boundary of the dual relation cone supplies a relation of zero

envelope at a deliberately noncontact vertex and forces a strictly negative
orthogonality integral.

condition in the repaired real-PA category.
