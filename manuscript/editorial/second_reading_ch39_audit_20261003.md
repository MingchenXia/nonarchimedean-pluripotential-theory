## Reflection, Cartier scope and proper support

The interpolation proof distinguishes exact local drift cancellation from
general moving-class closure. Veronese scaling multiplies both section
degree and weights. The rational fixed pole is enlarged by an integer;
ample openness supplies the required margin, and pullbacks are only claimed
nef. The actual reflection trace D(v) is finite at every divisorial point,
although the abstract interpolation lemma permits an infinite limit.

General homogeneous psh limits are not claimed to have finite-support Weil
traces on every model. An explicit P1 example uses infinitely many distinct
points and coefficients 2^-j; its partial sums decrease in PSH_hom(O(1)),
and the limit has no single real Cartier determination. This proves that
Cartier realization is not generally onto, rather than claiming it fails
for every space and class. It is a scope repair, not a research solution.

is computed at every point through the value of the point ideal, including
higher-model divisors and infinite values. Finite nonzero constants in the
upper-semicontinuous extension family are correctly identified as
inhomogeneous; the members zero and minus infinity already show failure of
trace determination without psh. Spatial upper semicontinuity bounds the

empty and zero-dimensional cases are separately handled. Order reflection
comes from analytic surjectivity, and carrier use includes both the tail
estimate and the reflected truncation term. No subtraction of common
