## Primary arithmetic normalization

Theorem 2.5 and its complete proof are on article pages 798–799
(physical 11–12). Let A have dimension g>=1 over a number field, L be
ample symmetric with canonical rigidification, and Q=phi_L(q) in Picard
zero with its canonical rigidification. The mixed arithmetic product is
Qbar squared times Lbar to g-1 = -(2/g)(L^g) hhat_L(q), and all other
mixed terms of total degree g+1 vanish. The **full power** (Lbar+Qbar)
to g+1 therefore includes binomial(g+1,2) and equals
-(g+1)(L^g) hhat_L(q). The normalized height is -hhat_L(q).

the full power. This exact primary-source addendum resolves the earlier

is inferred.

Theorem 4.2 and its full proof (article 804–807, physical 17–20) keep
raw arithmetic intersection, geometric degree and normalized variety
