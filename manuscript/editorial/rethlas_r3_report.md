## 1. Executive disposition

1. There is no literal equality of the five classes requested in the broad
   formulation.  Smooth CLD-psh functions include non-PL convex functions,
   while semipositive PL functions include corners and therefore need not be
   CLD-smooth.  The elementary functions \(x^2\) and \(|x|\) already disprove
   class equality on a tropical interval.
2. Within the three local PL notions, the broadest locus on which every
   comparison arrow now passes Rethlas verification is the
   semipositive/model PL core.  Over a complete nontrivially valued
   field, on a good strict boundaryless analytic space, a real-valued
   \(\mathbf R\)-PL function is model semipositive exactly when it is
   classically psh and exactly when it belongs to the CLD-v2 psh sheaf.  The
   three-way equivalence holds on the relative interior of a general good
   strict space.  On a separated space these conditions imply positive
   current on the relative interior.  The converse from the old current
   condition \(d'd''[h]\geq0\) to semipositivity remains open.  This is not a
   BFJ--CLD comparison theorem: identifying the local CLD operator with the
   global BFJ/BJ construction, even on shared model data, remains open.
3. Every CLD-psh function is classically psh.  Hence the CLD maximum-principle
   question is resolved by Gubler--Rabinoff.  The old ledger entry calling it
   open is obsolete.
4. Global BFJ/BJ psh metrics give local classically psh weights: apply the
   curve-tested calculus to their Fubini--Study/model approximants and then
   the Gubler--Rabinoff upper-semicontinuous net-closure theorem.  The reverse
   local-to-global arrow is not known in higher dimension and cannot hold
   without polarization and gluing data.
5. A useful local envelope statement survives: the raw supremum of a
   nonempty pointwise-bounded-above family of classically psh minorants is
   classically psh whenever that raw supremum is already upper
   semicontinuous.  The later exact-live nodal construction
   `prop:ch15-nodal-local-envelope` shows that the unrestricted conclusion
   after usc regularization is false: for a continuous real-valued obstacle
   on a reduced strict nodal affinoid curve the regularized envelope is
   discontinuous and is neither classically psh nor CLD-v2 psh.  This does
   not give a positive classification for normal or analytically unibranch
   domains.
6. The requested local BJ theory is not a blank slate.  Boucksom--Favre--
   Jonsson already construct formal psh functions and a mixed local
   Monge--Amp\`ere operator on the normalized valuation space of a smooth
   complex germ.  R3-26 now realizes that link as the shell
   `u_m=-1` in a `C((t))`-analytic polydisc and proves term-by-term agreement
   between the swept CLD boundary product and the BFJ measure for ideal
   functions and algebraizable rational semipositive model functions.  It
   also proves the comparison on
   an explicitly defined joint bounded monotone closure and the exact
   finite-map/reference-change formula.  Arbitrary curve-test psh functions,
   nonalgebraizable analytic models, unbounded finite energy, and singular or
   nonnormal germs remain open.
7. The finite-energy MP--DXZ comparison is valid for a K\"ahler class and
   \(\mathcal I\)-maximal finite-energy test curves, by composing their two
   bijections with maximal finite-energy rays.  It is an
   \(\mathcal I\)-comparison, not a \(P\)-comparison.
8. MP's unchanged definitions do not extend to a non-K\"ahler big class: a
   relatively K\"ahler model restricts to a K\"ahler generic-fibre class.
   At zero volume, MP's probability Monge--Amp\`ere and normalized energy
   formulas divide by zero.  DXZ's inverse system remains defined for every
   pseudoeffective class, but its finite-energy space is a big-class object.
9. Bingener's comparison rigorously supplies analytification, proper direct
   images, coherent algebraization near excellent Stein compacta, and
   relative Riemann existence.  It does not supply positivity, envelopes,
   valuation-space comparison, or Monge--Amp\`ere theory.
10. All six Chapter 69 R2 outcomes are mathematically supportable in their
    displayed scopes.  The live chapter now includes the explicit smooth
    convex approximants required by the cross-fan proof, restricts the
    fixed-dual-complex theorem to smooth projective \(X\) in characteristic
    zero, and gives the toric result its exact Guenancia/Cox--Little--Schenck
    source interface.  Its Cao section-growth term is correctly written as a
    limsup first and as an actual limit only after the toric lattice argument.
11. The new Chapter 69 three-way PL theorem is verified in its exact domain.
    The missing CLD-v2 arrow is supplied by decreasing log-sum-exp
    regularization of locally Fubini--Study functions followed by a summable
    constant-shift selection.  This is not an equivalence with CLD smoothness
    and it gives only a one-way implication to the old current predicate.

There are two independent failures of an overbroad “equivalence.”  First,
regularity differs: a smooth strictly convex function need not be PL, and a
semipositive PL corner need not be smooth.  Second, global BFJ/BJ psh is a
polarized cone of metrics, whereas classical psh is a local sheaf of
functions.

  DXZ compatibility must extend toward arbitrarily small K\"ahler
  perturbations; a tail of large shifts is final, rather than initial, for
  the variance of this limit.  Whether the specific DXZ envelope maps impose

* Keeping MP's formulas at \(V=0\) is impossible: both the energy and the

  theory with a new signature.

## 5. Local theory blueprint

The broadest defensible programme has four layers.

1. **Local psh sheaf.**  Use classically psh functions as the functorial
   ambient sheaf.  Inside it, retain semipositive PL/LFS functions as the
   model core.  For an obstacle with values in
   \(\mathbf R\cup\{-\infty\}\), define the raw local FS envelope
   \[
     P_U^{\mathrm{raw}}f=
       \sup\{h:h\text{ is local FS or semipositive PL on }U, h\leq f\}.
   \]
   It is classical psh when the raw supremum is already usc.  The unrestricted
   assertion for its usc regularization, including continuity and
   classical/CLD-v2 plurisubharmonicity, is false by the continuous-obstacle
   nodal counterexample `prop:ch15-nodal-local-envelope`.  Any positive
   replacement must impose branch compatibility or an analytically
   unibranch hypothesis; no such classification is asserted here.
2. **Valuative singularities.**  For a smooth closed germ, import the
   normalized valuation space and formal psh functions of BFJ-local.  For a
   general excellent local ring one must first choose a normalization of
   valuations (an ideal, maximal ideal, or boundary divisor); homogeneity
   without such a choice leaves a scaling ambiguity.
3. **Products and energy.**  On continuous CLD psh functions use the CLD
   Bedford--Taylor recursion.  For bounded formal psh functions on a smooth
   local germ use BFJ-local mixed Monge--Amp\`ere.  A comparison of these two
   products is open beyond model/polyhedral cases.  For unbounded functions,
   neither theory supplies the requested general common product.
4. **Gluing.**  Classical psh functions glue because they form a sheaf.
   Curvature currents glue when local weights differ by pluriharmonic
   transition functions.  Probability mass and energy do not form sheaves:
   they require polarization, a reference current, or boundary conditions.

The local probability-valued sheaf signature is actually impossible; see
R3-08.  This does not obstruct local positive measures or relative
Dirichlet energies.

## 9. Release rule

No new result from this pass may be given a conventional parenthetical
title.  A promotable statement must have a number only; any descriptive
phrase belongs in the surrounding prose.  Established names such as the
Fenchel--Moreau theorem may be retained only with a primary-source anchor.
This rule applies in addition to, not instead of, the full Rethlas receipt.

The 15 August binding release rule also forbids exercises anywhere in the
book.  Every theorem, lemma, proposition, and corollary must carry a complete
proof, unless it is visibly marked as an imported deep result and precisely
anchored to its primary source.  A citation alone must not disguise an omitted
proof.  Chapter 72's `problem` environments are exempt only in the literal
sense that they record research-frontier status and do not purport to be
proved statements or reader assignments.  A fresh global scan after the
central conversions finds no remaining `exercise` environment anywhere in
the manuscript.
