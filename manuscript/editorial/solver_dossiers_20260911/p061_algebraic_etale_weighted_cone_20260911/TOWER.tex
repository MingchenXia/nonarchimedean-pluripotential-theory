\begin{theorem}[Algebraic-etale stability along finite weighted-cone towers]
\label{thm:p061-algebraic-etale-weighted-cone-towers}
Let \(K\) be a complete non-Archimedean field with nontrivial absolute
value.  Fix \(\ell\geq0\) and a tower of finite-type \(K\)-schemes
\[
 \mathcal Z_\ell\xrightarrow{\pi_\ell}\mathcal Z_{\ell-1}
 \longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow
 \mathcal Z_1\xrightarrow{\pi_1}\mathcal Z_0=\mathcal Y,
\]
where \(\mathcal Y\) is smooth and every \(\pi_i\) has a section
\(s_i:\mathcal Z_{i-1}\to\mathcal Z_i\).  Fix
\(z_0\in\mathcal Y^{\mathrm{an}}\), and recursively put
\[
                         z_i=s_i^{\mathrm{an}}(z_{i-1}).
\]
For every \(1\leq i\leq\ell\), assume that there is an affine open
neighbourhood \(\mathcal V_{i-1}=\operatorname{Spec}R_{i-1}\) of the
scheme-theoretic support of \(z_{i-1}\) and an isomorphism
\[
 \mathcal Z_i\times_{\mathcal Z_{i-1}}\mathcal V_{i-1}
 \simeq
 \operatorname{Spec}
 \frac{R_{i-1}[Z_{i,1},\ldots,Z_{i,m_i}]}
      {(F_{i,1},\ldots,F_{i,r_i})}                                  \tag{\(*_i\)}
\]
carrying \(s_i\) to the zero section.  Here \(m_i\geq1\), the new
variables have positive integral weights, the coefficient ring has weight
zero, and every \(F_{i,j}\) is homogeneous of strictly positive weighted
degree.  No common grading among different stages is required.

Let \(q:\mathcal X\to\mathcal Z_\ell\) be a separated finite-type etale
morphism of \(K\)-schemes, and let \(x\in\mathcal X^{\mathrm{an}}\) satisfy
\[
                              q^{\mathrm{an}}(x)=z_\ell.
\]
Then every classically pluriharmonic germ at \(x\) on
\(\mathcal X^{\mathrm{an}}\) is locally \(\mathbf R\)-PL and
Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
We argue by induction on \(\ell\), strengthening the induction statement to
all separated finite-type algebraic etale sources over the displayed tower.
If \(\ell=0\), etaleness over the smooth scheme \(\mathcal Y\) makes an
ordinary analytic neighbourhood of \(x\) good, strict, and quasi-smooth.
The assertion is then \Cref{thm:p061-quasismooth-ph-rpl}.

Suppose \(\ell\geq1\), and assume the strengthened assertion for towers of
length \(\ell-1\).  Apply the construction in the proof of
\Cref{thm:p061-algebraic-etale-weighted-cone-zero-section} to the top chart
\((*_\ell)\), but do not use smoothness of \(\mathcal Z_{\ell-1}\).  That
construction needs only a strict affinoid neighbourhood
\(V=\mathcal M(B)\) of \(z_{\ell-1}\) in
\(\mathcal V_{\ell-1}^{\mathrm{an}}\).  It gives a relative
positive-weighted cone
\[
 A=B\langle Z_{\ell,1},\ldots,Z_{\ell,m_\ell}\rangle/
       (F_{\ell,1},\ldots,F_{\ell,r_\ell})
\]
and an actual strict source neighbourhood of \(x\) with presentation
\[
 C=A\langle T,U\rangle/\bigl(g(T),h(T)U-1\bigr),                  \tag{\(\dagger\)}
\]
where \(g\) is monic and \(g'\) is invertible in \(C\).  The principal
source open, target shrink, and strict bounds \(|T(x)|<1\), \(|U(x)|<1\)
are all retained.

Let \(\mathcal U\to\mathcal Z_\ell\) be the actual algebraic
standard-etale principal-open source neighbourhood used to construct
\((\dagger)\), and put
\[
 \mathcal E=\mathcal U\times_{\mathcal Z_\ell}
                         \mathcal Z_{\ell-1}.
\]
Then \(\mathcal E\to\mathcal Z_{\ell-1}\) is a separated finite-type
algebraic etale morphism.  Put
\[
                           D=C\widehat\otimes_A B
\]
for the central-fibre algebra.  Then \(\mathcal M(D)\) is a strict affinoid
domain in \(\mathcal E^{\mathrm{an}}\).  More is true: it is an ordinary
neighbourhood of \(x\) there.  Indeed, \(V\) is an
affinoid neighbourhood of \(z_{\ell-1}\), and after imposing the top zero
section the same strict inequalities \(|T(x)|<1\) and \(|U(x)|<1\) put
\(x\) in the ordinary interior of \(\mathcal M(D)\).

Consequently a classically pluriharmonic germ at \(x\) on
\(\mathcal M(D)\) restricts to an ordinary neighbourhood of \(x\) in
\(\mathcal E^{\mathrm{an}}\), and hence represents a germ there.  The
induction hypothesis, applied to
\(\mathcal E\to\mathcal Z_{\ell-1}\) and the first \(\ell-1\) stages,
makes it locally \(\mathbf R\)-PL.  Thus the central-fibre hypothesis of
\Cref{thm:p061-standard-etale-weighted-cone-tube} holds for
\((\dagger)\).  That theorem gives local \(\mathbf R\)-PL and
Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic regularity at \(x\), closing the induction.
\end{proof}
