\begin{proof}
Let \(\xi\) be the scheme-theoretic support of \(x\), and replace
\(\mathcal Z\) by the affine target in \((*)\).  Since \(q\) is etale at
\(\xi\), Stacks Project, Tag~025C, gives an affine open source neighbourhood
of \(\xi\) and an open immersion from it into
\[
 \operatorname{Spec}
 \bigl(R_Z[\Theta,G'(\Theta)^{-1}]/(G(\Theta))\bigr),               \tag{\(\dagger\)}
\]
over \(\operatorname{Spec}R_Z=\mathcal Z\), where \(G\in R_Z[\Theta]\)
is monic.  The image is open.  As principal opens form a basis on the
affine scheme \((\dagger)\), choose one which contains the image of
\(\xi\) and is contained in that open image.  Its defining element can be
written as \(P/(G')^N\), with \(P\in R_Z[\Theta]\).  Since \(G'\) is
already a unit on \((\dagger)\), the numerator \(P\) defines the same
principal open.  Put
\[
                              H=G'P.
\]
Restriction of the open immersion identifies an actual source
neighbourhood of \(\xi\) with
\[
 \operatorname{Spec}
 \bigl(R_Z[\Theta,H(\Theta)^{-1}]/(G(\Theta))\bigr).                \tag{\(\ddagger\)}
\]
Thus \((\ddagger)\) retains both the source principal-open shrink and the
inverse of the derivative; it is not merely a presentation of a local ring.

Choose a strict affinoid neighbourhood \(V=\mathcal M(B)\) of \(y\) in
\(\mathcal Y_0^{\mathrm{an}}\).  Since \(\mathcal Y\) is smooth, \(V\) is
quasi-smooth.  Restrict the coefficients of the \(F_j\) to \(B\), and put
\[
 A=B\langle Z_1,\ldots,Z_m\rangle/(F_1,\ldots,F_r).
\]
The affinoid space \(\mathcal M(A)\) is the part of the algebraic relative
cone above \(V\) cut out by \(|Z_i|\leq1\).  It is an affinoid
neighbourhood of \(s^{\mathrm{an}}(y)\): the base point lies in the ordinary
interior of \(V\), and every \(Z_i\) vanishes at the zero-section point, so
the strict loci \(|Z_i|<1\) give an ordinary open neighbourhood there.
This is the required target shrink; no assertion is being made about a
general target neighbourhood away from the zero section.

The point \(x\) belongs to the analytification of \((\ddagger)\), so
\(H(\Theta(x))\ne0\).  Choose \(c,d\in K^\times\) such that
\[
                 |\Theta(x)|<|c|,
        \qquad |H(\Theta(x))|^{-1}<|d|.                             \tag{\(\clubsuit\)}
\]
Such choices exist because the valuation is nontrivial.  If
\(e=\deg G\), define
\[
 T=c^{-1}\Theta,
 \qquad g(T)=c^{-e}G(cT),
 \qquad h(T)=dH(cT),
\]
and form the strict affinoid algebra
\[
 C=A\langle T,U\rangle/\bigl(g(T),h(T)U-1\bigr).                   \tag{\(\spadesuit\)}
\]
Indeed, first impose \(|\Theta|\leq|c|\) in the closed subspace
\(G(\Theta)=0\) above \(\mathcal M(A)\), and then take the rational domain
\(|H(\Theta)|^{-1}\leq|d|\).  This is exactly \((\spadesuit)\), and it
maps into \((\ddagger)^{\mathrm{an}}\), which we have already identified with
an actual source open in \(\mathcal X^{\mathrm{an}}\).  The polynomial \(g\)
is monic.
Moreover,
\(h(T)=dG'(cT)P(cT)\) is invertible in \(C\), hence so is
\(g'(T)=c^{1-e}G'(cT)\).

The inequalities \((\clubsuit)\) say \(|T(x)|<1\) and \(|U(x)|<1\).
Together with the target-interior condition, they show that the inverse
image of the target interior, intersected with these two strict loci, is an
ordinary open neighbourhood of \(x\) contained in \(\mathcal M(C)\).
Thus the source principal open, the target shrink, and the bounded analytic
variables have all been retained explicitly.

Finally the zero-section map \(A\to B\) puts \(x\) on the central fibre
\[
                         D=C\widehat\otimes_A B.
\]
The presentation \((\spadesuit)\) satisfies all hypotheses of
\Cref{thm:p061-standard-etale-weighted-cone-tube}.  Since
\(\mathcal M(B)=V\) is quasi-smooth, the final clause of that theorem
applies.  Restricting any classically pluriharmonic germ on
\(\mathcal X^{\mathrm{an}}\) to the ordinary source neighbourhood just constructed
therefore makes it locally \(\mathbf R\)-PL and Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff
harmonic at \(x\), as required.
\end{proof}
