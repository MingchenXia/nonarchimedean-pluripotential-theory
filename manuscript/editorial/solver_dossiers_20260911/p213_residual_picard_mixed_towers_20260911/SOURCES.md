# Source map

Date: 11 September 2026.

- Amaury Thuillier, *Théorie du potentiel sur les courbes en géométrie
  analytique non archimédienne*, Theorem 2.3.21 and Lemma 2.3.22,
  <https://theses.hal.science/tel-00010990v1>.  These identify the quotient
  of harmonic germs by the real span of logarithms of analytic units, show
  that it is supported at type-2 points, and identify its type-2 stalk with
  `Pic^0(C_x) tensor R`.  They also supply the finite-residue-field and
  Mumford-curve vanishing cases.
- Walter Gubler, Philipp Jell, and Joseph Rabinoff, *Forms on Berkovich
  spaces based on harmonic tropicalizations*, Proposition 15.7 and Remark
  15.10, <https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.05741>.  Proposition 15.7 compares
  their harmonic functions with Thuillier's on rig-smooth curves.  Remark
  15.10 identifies the real span of logarithms of local analytic units with
  the ordinary CLD-smooth harmonic sheaf, namely the kernel of the ordinary
  smooth `d'd''` map.
- Veronika Wanner, *Comparison of two notions of subharmonicity on
  non-archimedean curves*, Theorem 4.7 and Corollary 4.8,
  <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00209-018-2205-z>.  The existing book theorem
  `thm:wanner-curve-comparison` imports the equivalence between continuous
  current-psh and Thuillier-subharmonic functions; its two-sign corollary
  turns a continuous zero-current function into a Thuillier harmonic one.

The mixed-tower statements use only the already integrated exact descent
propositions `prop:p062-open-polydisc-bundle-descent`,
`prop:p062-projective-bundle-descent`, and
`prop:p062-proper-smooth-curve-bundle-descent`, followed by ordinary CLD
form pullback and the smooth Bedford--Taylor multiplier criterion.
