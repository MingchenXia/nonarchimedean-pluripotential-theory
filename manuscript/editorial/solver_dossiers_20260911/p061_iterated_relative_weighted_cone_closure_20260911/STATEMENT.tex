\begin{corollary}[Iterated relative weighted-cone closure]
\label{cor:p061-iterated-relative-weighted-cone-closure}
Let \(K\) be a complete non-Archimedean field with nontrivial absolute
value.  Say that a point \(z\) of a good strict \(K\)-analytic space has
property \({\rm PH}_{\mathbf R{\rm PL}}\) if, for every ordinary open set
\(z\in\Omega\) and every classically pluriharmonic
\(h:\Omega\to\mathbf R\), there is an ordinary open set
\(z\in W\subset\Omega\) on which \(h\) is \(\mathbf R\)-PL.

In the setting of
\Cref{prop:p061-relative-positive-weighted-cone-descent}, if \(y\in Y\)
has property \({\rm PH}_{\mathbf R{\rm PL}}\), then the zero-section point
\(s(y)\in X\) has the same property.

Consequently, let \(X_0=\mathcal M(B_0)\) be strict affinoid and let
\(x_0\in X_0\) have property \({\rm PH}_{\mathbf R{\rm PL}}\).  Recursively,
for \(1\leq j\leq \ell\), choose positive integral weights
\(w_{j,1},\ldots,w_{j,m_j}\), let
\[
 F_{j,1},\ldots,F_{j,r_j}
 \in B_{j-1}[T_{j,1},\ldots,T_{j,m_j}]
\]
be homogeneous of strictly positive weighted degree, and put
\[
 B_j:=B_{j-1}\langle T_{j,1},\ldots,T_{j,m_j}\rangle/
          (F_{j,1},\ldots,F_{j,r_j}),
 \qquad X_j:=\mathcal M(B_j).
\]
Write \(p_j:X_j\to X_{j-1}\) and \(s_j:X_{j-1}\to X_j\) for the projection
and zero section, and set \(x_j:=s_j(x_{j-1})\).  Then \(x_\ell\) has
property \({\rm PH}_{\mathbf R{\rm PL}}\).  Every classically
pluriharmonic germ at \(x_\ell\) is moreover Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff
harmonic after this shrinking.  In particular, the conclusion holds when
\(X_0\) is quasi-smooth on an ordinary neighbourhood of \(x_0\).
\end{corollary}
