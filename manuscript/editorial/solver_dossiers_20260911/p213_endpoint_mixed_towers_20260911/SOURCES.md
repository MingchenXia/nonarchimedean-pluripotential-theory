# Dependency map

Date: 11 September 2026.

This packet is a book-local synthesis; it introduces no new external
analytic input.

- `thm:p062-endpoint-polycylinder-v4-research` classifies every continuous
  zero-current function on a standard strict endpoint polycylinder as a
  unique logarithmic-affine function of its annular coordinates, including
  closed and half-closed endpoints.
- `prop:p062-open-polydisc-bundle-descent`,
  `prop:p062-projective-bundle-descent`, and
  `prop:p062-proper-smooth-curve-bundle-descent` give exact descent through
  the three permitted locally trivial fibre types.
- `prop:cld-form-pullback` preserves ordinary CLD smoothness and the zero
  smooth Hessian under the composite analytic pullback.
- `cor:ch16-smooth-h` gives the Bedford--Taylor identity for an arbitrary
  positive closed ordinary CLD current once the difference of the two
  continuous potentials is ordinary smooth with zero Hessian.

The endpoint theorem's production source and proof archive remain the
controlling provenance for the explicit base classification.  This packet
only composes that theorem with already integrated exact descent results.
