\begin{proof}
Choose \(\varpi\in K\) with \(0<|\varpi|<1\).  For \(n\geq1\), put
\[
 A_n:=B\langle S_1,\ldots,S_m\rangle/
             (F_1(S),\ldots,F_r(S))
\]
and define \(A\to A_n\) by
\(Z_i\mapsto\varpi^{nw_i}S_i\).  If \(d_j>0\) is the weighted degree of
\(F_j\), then
\[
 F_j(\varpi^{nw_1}S_1,\ldots,\varpi^{nw_m}S_m)
       =\varpi^{nd_j}F_j(S_1,\ldots,S_m),
\]
so this map is well defined.  On analytic spectra, \(\mathcal M(A_n)\)
is precisely the weighted affinoid tube
\[
 \{z\in\mathcal M(A): |Z_i(z)|\leq|\varpi|^{nw_i}\ (1\leq i\leq m)\}.
 \tag{\(\clubsuit\)}\label{eq:p061-standard-etale-weighted-tube}
\]

Give \(A\) and every \(A_n\) the quotient Gauss norms from the displayed
presentations.  The maps
\[
                  B\longrightarrow A\longrightarrow A_n\longrightarrow B
\]
are contractions, and both composites from \(B\) to \(B\) are the
identity.  Write \(J=\ker(\varepsilon)\).  Every \(a\in J\) has a
representation
\[
                            a=\sum_{i=1}^m Z_i a_i,
                 \qquad a_i\in A.
 \tag{\(\spadesuit\)}\label{eq:p061-standard-etale-kernel-generation}
\]
Indeed, lift \(a\) to a convergent Tate series, subtract its constant
coefficient, and group every remaining monomial by one variable dividing
it before passing to the quotient.  Consequently
\[
 \|a\|_{A_n}
 \leq\max_i |\varpi|^{nw_i}\|a_i\|_A
 \longrightarrow0.
 \tag{\(\diamondsuit\)}\label{eq:p061-standard-etale-kernel-decay}
\]
There are only finitely many coefficients of \(g\) and \(q\).  Applying
\eqref{eq:p061-standard-etale-kernel-decay} to the difference between each
coefficient and the constant lift of its image in \(B\) verifies the
coefficient-contraction hypothesis of
\Cref{lem:p061-v3-contractive-standard-etale-tube}.

Set \(C_n=C\widehat\otimes_AA_n\).  The cited lemma gives, for all
sufficiently large \(n\), an \(A_n\)-algebra isomorphism
\[
 C_n\simeq A_n\widehat\otimes_BD
      \simeq D\langle S_1,\ldots,S_m\rangle/
                  (F_1^D(S),\ldots,F_r^D(S)),
 \tag{\(*\)}\label{eq:p061-standard-etale-cone-tube-isomorphism}
\]
whose restriction to the central fibre is the identity of \(D\).  The
right side is a relative positive-weighted cone over \(\mathcal M(D)\).
Notice that no reducedness, normality, irreducibility, or flatness of this
cone was used.

Let \(\phi\) be classically pluriharmonic on an ordinary open
neighbourhood \(\Omega\) of \(x\) in \(\mathcal M(C)\).  Since every
\(Z_i\) vanishes at \(x\), the point \(x\) lies in the ordinary interior
of the affinoid domain \(\mathcal M(C_n)\) for every \(n\).  Thus
\[
             \Omega\cap\operatorname{Int}_{\mathcal M(C)}\mathcal M(C_n)
\]
is an ordinary neighbourhood of \(x\).  Transport its restriction of
\(\phi\) through \eqref{eq:p061-standard-etale-cone-tube-isomorphism}.
The central-fibre compatibility identifies \(x\) with the zero-section
point over the same point of \(\mathcal M(D)\).  The one-step assertion of
\Cref{cor:p061-iterated-relative-weighted-cone-closure}, applied with the
hypothesis on pluriharmonic germs at \(x\) in \(\mathcal M(D)\), now makes
\(\phi\) locally \(\mathbf R\)-PL.

Both \(\phi\) and \(-\phi\) are classically psh.  They are semipositive by
\cite[Theorem~4.3]{GR25Classical}; together with the local
\(\mathbf R\)-PL conclusion, this is Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonicity by
\cite[Definition~7.2]{GJR26}.

Finally, write \(g_0,q_0\in B[T]\) for the coefficientwise images.  Then
\[
 D=B\langle t,u\rangle/\bigl(g_0(t),q_0(t)u-1\bigr),
\]
and both \(g_0'(t)\) and \(q_0(t)\) are units in \(D\).  The affinoid
Jacobian matrix for the two displayed relations has determinant
\[
 \det\frac{\partial(g_0,q_0u-1)}{\partial(t,u)}
                         =g_0'(t)q_0(t)\in D^\times.
\]
Thus the presentation is a Jacobian presentation and
\(\mathcal M(D)\to\mathcal M(B)\) is quasi-smooth
\cite[Definitions~5.2.2 and~5.2.4]{Ducros18Families}; this assertion does
not discard the possible relative boundary of the displayed Tate domain.
If \(\mathcal M(B)\) is quasi-smooth, composition of quasi-smooth maps
\cite[\S5.2.13]{Ducros18Families} makes \(\mathcal M(D)\) quasi-smooth.
In that case
\Cref{thm:p061-quasismooth-ph-rpl} supplies the required central-fibre
hypothesis.
\end{proof}
