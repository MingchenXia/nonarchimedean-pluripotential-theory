## Remaining interfaces

The model and whole-tube hypotheses are indispensable to this proof as written. Given only an arbitrary ordinary open W and a point on a tropical wall, this packet has not proved that a whole proper residual curve tube or the needed compact strips lie in W. No construction by deformation or proper modification is asserted to solve that capture problem automatically.

A second unproved case concerns an SNC PAIR with selected horizontal components. Their multiplicities in div(pi) would be zero, and their logarithmic radii can be infinite on the generic divisor. The local finite-projection statement allows zero exponents, but the global proof above uses positive selected exponents to obtain finite continuous radii, upper bounds and the eta-only exclusion of zeros/poles. A separate upper radial cutoff and extension check are required for the horizontal case.

The different-moment comparison still needs actual common source forms and source isolation, not agreement of tropical values or an ambient polycylinder basis. The new functional identity is an advance inside the stated model geometry, not a global P21.3 solution.
