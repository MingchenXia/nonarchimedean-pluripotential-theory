Let \(k\) be algebraically closed with the trivial absolute value, in
arbitrary characteristic. Write
\[
 F_0=k(t),\qquad R_0=k[t]_{(t)},\qquad R=k[[t]],\qquad
 K=k((t)),\quad |t|=\mathrm e^{-1}.
\]
Let \(S/k\) be normal, integral and projective, and let
\(\pi:S\to\mathbf P^1_k\) be dominant. Assume its generic fibre \(Y/F_0\)
is smooth and geometrically integral. Let \(A\) be an actual ample,
globally generated line bundle on \(S\), and put \(E_m=\pi_*A^{\otimes m}\).
Assume
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p081-curvebase-generation}
 E_1\text{ is globally generated},\qquad
 \operatorname{Sym}^m E_1\longrightarrow E_m
       \text{ is surjective for every }m\geq1.
\end{equation}
Choose finitely many \(q_\ell\in H^0(S,A)=H^0(\mathbf P^1,E_1)\)
which generate both \(E_1\) on the base and \(A\) on \(S\).
Use base coordinates \([T:U]\), with \(t=T/U\), and set
\[
 \mathscr S=S\times_{\mathbf P^1}\operatorname{Spec}R,\qquad
 \mathscr A=A|_{\mathscr S},\qquad
 X=Y_K,\qquad L=A|_X.
\]
The full analytic fibre over \(\eta=\operatorname{ord}_{t=0}\) is
\(\mathcal F=(\pi^{\mathrm{an}})^{-1}(\eta)=X^{\mathrm{an}}\).
This is not a Gauss-section image.

On \(L\) use the model metric of \((\mathscr S,\mathscr A)\), denoted
\(|\cdot|_0\). On line bundles over \(S/k\) use the trivial metric.
An FS potential on \(L\), relative to this reference, is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p081-curvebase-fs}
 f=\frac1m\max_j\{\log|s_j|_0+\lambda_j\},\qquad
 s_j\in H^0(X,L^{\otimes m}),\quad \lambda_j\in\mathbf R,
\end{equation}
where \(m\geq1\) and the finite section system has no common zero.
The corresponding metric has norm \(|\cdot|_0\exp(-f)\).
This is \cite[Definition~5.2]{BE2021}; over \(k\) write \(H_{\mathbf R}\)
and \(H_{\mathbf Q}\) as in \cite[Definition~2.18]{BJ22}.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:p081-curvebase-exact-extension}
Under these hypotheses, fix \(M\geq0\) and an integer
\(C\geq\max\{1,M\}\). Every FS potential \(f\) with \(-M\leq f\leq0\)
extends exactly to
\[
 \Phi\in H_{\mathbf R}(B_C),\qquad
 B_C=A\otimes\pi^*\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(C),
\]
such that \(\Phi|_{\mathcal F}=f\), \(-C\leq\Phi\leq1\) everywhere
on \(S^{\mathrm{an}}\), and every normalized weight in a section
representation of \(\Phi\) belongs to \([-C,1)\).
The bundle \(B_C\) is ample. Rational-weighted inputs have
rational-weighted extensions. The same \(C\) works independently of
denominator, number of sections, section coefficients and accuracy;
the restriction error is zero.
\end{proposition}

Define \(\operatorname{PSH}_{\mathrm{FS}}(L)\) to be the relative
potentials which are not identically \(-\infty\) and are pointwise
decreasing-net limits of FS potentials on this fixed \(L\).
This is precisely the nontrivial part of the metric class in
\cite[Definition~7.2]{BE2021}, not an implicit comparison with another
definition of non-Archimedean psh.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:p081-curvebase-ep-transfer-candidate}
Assume, in addition, the BJ envelope property on \(S\) in each class
\(c_1(B_C)\), \(C\in\mathbf Z_{\geq1}\): the usc regularized supremum
of a nonempty globally bounded-above family in that psh class is psh.
Then any nonempty family \((u_i)\subset
\operatorname{PSH}_{\mathrm{FS}}(L)\) bounded above by a common real
constant satisfies
\[
                (\sup_i u_i)^{*\mathcal F}
                        \in\operatorname{PSH}_{\mathrm{FS}}(L).
\]
For FS inputs \(f_i\in[-M,0]\), choose the extensions from
\cref{prop:p081-curvebase-exact-extension} with one common \(C\).
They satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p081-curvebase-usc}
       (\sup_i\Phi_i)^{*S}|_{\mathcal F}
                       =(\sup_i f_i)^{*\mathcal F}.
\end{equation}
The stars refer to the specified ambient and fibre topologies.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:p081-curvebase-arrange}
Let \(Y/F_0\) be any smooth geometrically integral projective variety,
and let \(L_0\) be an ample line bundle on \(Y\).
There are a normal integral projective \(S/k\), a dominant
\(\pi:S\to\mathbf P^1_k\) with generic fibre \(Y\), an integer \(N>0\),
and an ample globally generated \(A\) satisfying
\eqref{eq:p081-curvebase-generation}, with \(A|_Y\simeq L_0^{\otimes N}\).
Consequently, assuming the stated trivial-field EP on the resulting
normal projective \(S\), the conclusion of
\cref{thm:p081-curvebase-ep-transfer-candidate} holds for \(L_{0,K}\)
itself, in the decreasing-FS metric class.
\end{proposition}

The supplied unibranch-envelope theorem asserts the required ambient EP
because \(S\) is normal. Its entire proof is an explicit external input,
not re-proved here. This new bridge remains candidate-only. It does not
claim that an arbitrary polarized variety over \(k((t))\) descends to
\(k(t)\), nor a result over arbitrary complete fields, in arbitrary
numerical classes, or for CLD/BFJ comparison. Arbitrary Laurent-series
coefficients of sections on the fixed family are allowed; equations of
an arbitrary \(K\)-variety are not thereby algebraized.
Continuous-obstacle continuity is neither assumed nor concluded.
