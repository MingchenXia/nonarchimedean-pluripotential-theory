# Nonconstant curve-base fibre extension: isolated research candidate

Date: 2026-08-31.
Authoring agent: /root/p082_independent_reaudit.
Status: author-checked candidate; not an independent audit, not production
verified, not live-integrated, and not an inventory retirement.

The packet proves an exact uniformly controlled weighted-FS extension
under explicit normal-model and relative-generation hypotheses. It then
gives a conditional envelope-transfer argument from the supplied
trivial-field unibranch EP. Finally, a graph embedding, relative Serre
vanishing, and an explicit grouping of monomials arrange the hypotheses
after replacing an ample polarization by a power.

The resulting scope is smooth geometrically integral projective varieties
with ample polarization defined over k(t), then completed to
K=k((t)), where k is algebraically closed and trivial, in any characteristic.
The target is the decreasing-FS metric class of BE Definition 7.2.
No arbitrary complete-field theorem or algebraization of arbitrary
k((t))-varieties is asserted. Arbitrary Laurent-series coefficients of
sections on the fixed algebraic family are permitted and handled exactly.
Continuous-obstacle continuity and comparison with CLD/BFJ psh are
separate questions, not inputs or conclusions.
