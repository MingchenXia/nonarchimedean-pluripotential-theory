\section*{Bidegree-zero scalar rigidity: exact open target}

Let \(K\) be a complete nontrivially valued non-Archimedean field.
Let \(X\) be a connected, good, separated, strict, boundaryless,
quasi-smooth \(K\)-analytic space, pure of dimension \(n\geq2\).
Is every positive ordinary Chambert--Loir--Ducros current \(T\) of
bidegree \((0,0)\), with
\(\mathrm d'T=\mathrm d''T=0\), of the form
\[
                         T=c\,\delta_X,\qquad c\geq0?
\]

Conditional on the previously isolated source-skeletal representation
input, the same target asks whether the descriptions of \(T\) by
nonnegative source calibrations satisfy
\[
       \kappa_F=c\,\mu_F
\]
for one scalar \(c\), for every tropical chart \(F\) on every ordinary
open subset of \(X\). Here \(\mu_F\) is the canonical analytic
calibration. All ordinary restrictions and all common analytic
refinements are required, not only a chosen finite set of moments.
Reconstruction from a specified compatible family is not uniqueness
among such families.

This target remains open in this research packet. No noncanonical
positive closed analytic current is constructed. The note records
precise obstructions to proof shortcuts, not a counterexample to
scalar rigidity.

Even an affirmative answer would settle only a bidegree-zero,
nontrivially valued, quasi-smooth part of the current Chapter~72
problem. It would not settle positive bidegrees, arbitrary singular
or reducible spaces, or the trivially valued scope of P21.3.
No ordinary-polycylinder-basis assertion is assumed.
