\section{Exact inputs and conditional conclusion}

This is an isolated conditional research proof, not an accepted theorem of
the book. Its two new topological inputs are being checked in separate
packets; this proof and all dependencies require independent and production
review before integration.

Let \(X\) be a connected compact K\"ahler manifold, let \(\omega\) be a
K\"ahler form, and write \(\alpha=\{\omega\}\) and
\(V=\int_X\omega^n>0\). We use the MP ideal spectrum \(X^\beth\).
Let \(D\) consist of the rational divisorial valuations together with the
zero valuation \(o\). For semivaluations put
\[
 q\leq v\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 q(\mathfrak a)\leq v(\mathfrak a)
 \text{ for every coherent ideal }\mathfrak a.
\]
For a DXZ inverse-system object \(\Gamma\), let \(c_\Gamma\in\mathbf R\)
be its endpoint and use its raw transform
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p143k-raw}
 F_\Gamma(v)=\sup_{\tau<c_\Gamma}
 \left\{\tau-\sup_{m\geq1}\frac1m
 v\bigl(\mathcal I(m\Gamma^{\omega+\eta}_\tau)\bigr)\right\}.
\end{equation}
Here \(\eta\) is any K\"ahler perturbation; equality of multiplier ideals
under transition makes the expression independent of \(\eta\).
In particular \(F_\Gamma(o)=c_\Gamma\) and \(F_\Gamma\leq c_\Gamma\).

The two additional inputs are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item\label{hyp:p143k-usc} Every raw transform in \eqref{eq:p143k-raw}
is upper semicontinuous on \(X^\beth\).
\item\label{hyp:p143k-density} For every \(v\in X^\beth\), the point
\(v\) belongs to the closure of \(\{q\in D:q\leq v\}\).
\end{enumerate}
The second assertion is stronger than ordinary divisorial density. It
does not follow just by replacing a net with a sequence, and we do not
make that replacement.

We use the existing finite-energy DXZ--MP correspondence, obtained from
\cite[Theorem~3.17]{DXZ23} and
\cite[Theorems~5.1.4, 5.1.7 and Section~5.2]{MP24}. It is a bijection
between the finite-energy spaces, agrees with \(F_\Gamma\) on \(D\),
and preserves energy. The zero-valuation value is checked explicitly
below. We also use the DXZ operations, endpoint bounds and divisorial
order criterion of \cite[Section~4.1--4.4]{Xia23}, and the MP comparison
theorem \cite[Theorem~4.3.8]{MP24}. None of these inputs assumes the
full-cone comparison asserted next.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:p143k-full-comparison}
Assume \cref{hyp:p143k-usc,hyp:p143k-density}. Then
\[
 \PSH^{\NA}_{\mathrm{DXZ}}(X,\omega)
 \longrightarrow\PSH_{\mathrm{MP}}(\alpha),\qquad
 \Gamma\longmapsto F_\Gamma
\]
is an order isomorphism. Equality here is equality of functions on every
semivaluation, including those with proper support. No finite-energy,
projectivity, or rationality assumption on \(\alpha\) is imposed.
On finite-energy objects it is the existing correspondence, and the
extended energy functionals agree, with value \(-\infty\) off their
finite-energy domains.
\end{theorem}

This theorem concerns K\"ahler classes. It does not assert an unchanged
relatively nef model realization for a big non-nef class, nor a general
zero-volume measure or energy theory.
