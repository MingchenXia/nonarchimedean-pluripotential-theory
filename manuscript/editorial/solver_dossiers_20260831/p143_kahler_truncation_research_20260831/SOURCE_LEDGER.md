## New proof steps, not attributed as verbatim source theorems

The compactness/directedness lemma is proved in the packet. In particular it is not justified by calling a minimum of model psh functions psh. A continuous minimum occurs only as a strict upper comparison function; a single model approximant below it is selected by compactness.

The full-cone comparison is a deduction from the stated topological hypotheses and the source interfaces. The dominated-divisorial hypothesis is not a consequence of mere topological density; its separate proof is under independent audit. The raw-USC hypothesis is bound to the separate P14.2 proof. A mathematical PASS for this conditional packet alone does not validate either dependency.

For the zero valuation, normalized test-curve levels have supremum zero, so the inverse Legendre ray has supremum exactly the endpoint times logarithmic time. The existing ray slope identity then identifies the endpoint with the MP value. The ordinary- versus squared-norm conversion changes both potential and logarithmic time by the same factor; it does not add a factor to their quotient.

No general theorem for non-nef or zero-volume classes, no all-semivaluation interpretation of an arbitrary finite minimum, no metrizability, and no sequence extracted from the model category is assumed. The only new sequence used in the full comparison is the explicitly indexed bounded truncation sequence. Its closure is proved using a net.
