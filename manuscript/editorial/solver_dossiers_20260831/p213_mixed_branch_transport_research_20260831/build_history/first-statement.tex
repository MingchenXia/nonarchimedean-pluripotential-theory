\begin{proposition}
\label{prop:p213-mixed-branch-transport}
Let \(F\) be a complete nontrivially valued non-Archimedean
field, let \(L/F\) be a finite extension (possibly \(L=F\)),
and put \(v=-\log|\cdot|\). Fix \(\delta>0\).
Regard the following ordinary open domain over \(L\) as an
\(F\)-analytic space:
\[
 D=\{(t,w)\in(\mathbf G_{m,L}^2)^{\mathrm{an}}:
             0<v(t)<\infty,\ 0<v(w)<\delta\}.
\]
Let \(T\) be a positive ordinary CLD current of bidegree
\((0,0)\) on \(D\), with \(\mathrm d'T=0\).
All current operations in this statement are over \(F\).

Set \(r=v(t)\), \(s=v(w)\), \(q=w-t\), and define the
ordinary open subdomain
\[
 E=\{x\in D:q(x)\ne0,\ 0<r(x)<\delta,\ v(q(x))>r(x)\}.
\]
On \(E\) write \(h=v(q)\). The two moments and their ranges are
\[
 \tau_D=(r,s),\quad
 Q_D=(0,\infty)\times(0,\delta),\qquad
 \tau_E=(r,h),\quad
 Q_E=\{(x,y)\in\mathbf R^2:0<x<\delta,\ y>x\}.
\]
For ordered target coordinates \(x,y\), let
\[
 \Omega_{xy}=\mathrm d'x\wedge\mathrm d''x\wedge
             \mathrm d'y\wedge\mathrm d''y .
\]
There exist unique finite \(B_T,C_T\geq0\) such that
\begin{align}
 \langle T,\tau_D^*(H\Omega_{xy})\rangle
   &=B_T\int_{Q_D}H(x,y)\,\mathrm dx\,\mathrm dy,
       &&H\in C_c^\infty(Q_D),\label{eq:p213-mixed-old}\\
 \langle T,\tau_E^*(H\Omega_{xy})\rangle
   &=C_T\int_{Q_E}H(x,y)\,\mathrm dx\,\mathrm dy,
       &&H\in C_c^\infty(Q_E).\label{eq:p213-mixed-new}
\end{align}
In the second line \(T\) is restricted to \(E\); both test
forms have compact analytic support and are extended by
zero in \(D\) when necessary. Then
\begin{equation}
 \label{eq:p213-mixed-equality}
                         C_T=B_T .
\end{equation}
For the canonical integration current over \(F\),
\[
                    B_{\delta_D}=C_{\delta_D}=[L:F].
\]
The assertion compares these two moment coefficients only.
It does not assert \(T=(B_T/[L:F])\delta_D\), separation of
all smooth test forms by these moments, or access to all
ordinary moment cells.
\end{proposition}
