# Raw DXZ transform: upper semicontinuity

Author research packet, 31 August 2026. Addresses the complete active
P14.2 signature in Chapter72, not just the projective or divisorial case.
The textbook and open-problem inventory have not been changed.

The mathematical text is statement.tex followed by proof.tex and example.tex. It proves a
topological lemma for any monotone nonnegative lower-semicontinuous family,
then applies it to coherent multiplier-ideal evaluations. A finite mesh
controls the parameter supremum uniformly after a moving finite floor.
The stronger explicit construction gives a decreasing sequence of bounded
continuous finite-ideal expressions, with no metrizability assumption.

The natural elementary theorem only needs a complex manifold and a
decreasing family of quasi-psh functions with a fixed finite upper parameter.
Concavity, maximality, positivity of the background class, and compactness
are unnecessary for this topological assertion. The DXZ corollary retains
the entire original compact-Kaehler pseudoeffective inverse-system domain.

Do not confuse continuous finite-ideal approximation with semipositive
model approximation. The construction takes finite minima and supplies no
MP positivity. P14.3--P14.8, the image problem P14.1, and the general
nontrivially valued envelope property are not solved by this argument.
