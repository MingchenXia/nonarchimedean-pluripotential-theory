# Real and rational model functions: isolated foundational correction

31 August 2026. Author: root. Not integrated and not production verified.

The live real-model maximum assertion is false. This packet keeps real
coefficients in numerical forms and convex combinations, proves maximum
closure for rational model functions, and proves approximation at the exact
fixed real reference form when its generic numerical class is ample.
It does not replace the general BFJ theory by a rational-reference theory.

The real-model obstacle family is not generally upward directed, even when
the reference class is ample. Its supremum nevertheless equals that of the
max-directed rational subfamily under the ample-class hypothesis. The other
elementary real-model envelope properties retain their original semipositive
reference hypotheses, without adding ampleness to them.

Proposition 3.12 and Corollary 3.13 with their proofs; local source
