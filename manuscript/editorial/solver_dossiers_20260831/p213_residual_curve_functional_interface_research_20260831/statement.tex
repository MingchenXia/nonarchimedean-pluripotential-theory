\begin{proposition}
\label{prop:p213-product-residual-functional}
Let \(K\) be a complete discretely valued non-Archimedean field,
let \(R=K^\circ\), let \(\pi\) be a uniformizer, and suppose that
the residue field \(k\) is perfect. Put
\(v=-\log|\cdot|\) and \(\ell=v(\pi)>0\).
Let \(\mathcal C\to\operatorname{Spec}R\) be a smooth proper curve
with geometrically connected fibres. Write \(C=\mathcal C_K\),
\(C_0=\mathcal C_k\), and
\[
 A=\{|\pi|<|z|<1\}\subset\mathbf G_{m,K}^{\mathrm{an}},
 \qquad X=A\times_K C^{\mathrm{an}}.
\]
Let \(T\) be a positive ordinary CLD current of bidegree \((0,0)\)
on \(X\), with \(\mathrm d'T=\mathrm d''T=0\).

For every closed point \(p\in C_0\), let \(R_p/R\) be the finite
unramified extension with residue field \(\kappa(p)\), and let
\(K_p=\operatorname{Frac}R_p\). The tube of \(p\) is an open unit
disc over \(K_p\), viewed as one \(K\)-analytic domain. Choose a
formal parameter \(w_p\) using
\(\widehat{\mathcal O}_{\mathcal C,p}\simeq R_p[[w_p]]\).
On a sufficiently small outer collar put
\[
 u=v(z),\quad s_p=v(w_p),\quad
 Q_p=(0,\ell)\times(0,\delta_p),\quad
 \Omega_p=
 \mathrm d'u\wedge\mathrm d''u\wedge
 \mathrm d's_p\wedge\mathrm d''s_p.
\]
There is a unique finite number \(b_T(p)\geq0\) such that
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:p213-residual-branch-coefficient}
 \langle T,h(u,s_p)\Omega_p\rangle
   =b_T(p)\int_{Q_p}h(u,s)\,\mathrm du\,\mathrm ds
 \qquad\bigl(h\in C_c^\infty(Q_p)\bigr).
\end{equation}
Here the test form is extended by zero from the collar; its
analytic support is compact. The number is independent of the
formal parameter, the sufficiently small collar, and the
test function used to evaluate it.

The finite-valued positive homomorphism
\[
 \lambda_T:\operatorname{Div}(C_0)\longrightarrow\mathbf R,
 \qquad
 \lambda_T\Bigl(\sum_p n_p p\Bigr)=\sum_p n_pb_T(p)
\]
annihilates every principal divisor:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:p213-residual-principal-relation}
 \sum_{p\in C_0}
   \operatorname{ord}_p(f)\,b_T(p)=0
 \qquad\bigl(f\in k(C_0)^\times\bigr).
\end{equation}
In particular, \(b_T(p)=c[\kappa(p):k]\) for a single \(c\geq0\).

For the canonical integration current,
\(b_{\delta_X}(p)=[\kappa(p):k]\).
If, additionally, the source-calibration representation in the
separate representation packet is assumed for \(T\), then on
the single source Gauss plane of each displayed collar the
calibration ratio is
\[
 \frac{\kappa_T}{\mu_X}
   =\frac{b_T(p)}{[\kappa(p):k]}=c .
\]
This last interpretation is conditional on that representation;
the construction of \(\lambda_T\) and its principal-divisor
relation do not assume it. No assertion is made about other
source cells, general semistable neighbourhoods, or scalar
rigidity of \(T\) on \(X\).
\end{proposition}
