\section*{Manuscript inputs, stated only for the standalone check}
These two inputs are accepted results in the parent manuscript.  Their
exact source files and hashes are recorded in the accompanying manifest.
They are not reproved or submitted for verification by this packet.

\begin{theorem}
\label{thm:p063-current-psh-ordinary-regularization}
Over an algebraically closed complete nontrivially valued field, on an
open subset of a smooth proper algebraic curve, every continuous
Thuillier-subharmonic function is locally a decreasing uniform limit
of ordinary CLD-smooth psh functions.  In particular it is CLD-psh.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}
\label{thm:p081-corrected-curve-comparison}
On a smooth proper geometrically connected algebraic curve over a
complete nontrivially valued field, a continuous metric on a line bundle
of positive degree with locally CLD-psh weights is a uniform limit of
semipositive rational formal metrics on that fixed line bundle.
\end{theorem}

Only the algebraically closed case of this second input is needed
in Step~3.  Step~4 independently spells out the descent of the resulting
approximation.  Neither input uses the present skeletal-measure solution.
