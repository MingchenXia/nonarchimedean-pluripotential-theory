\begin{proposition}
\label{prop:p213-torsor-residual-functional}
Let \(K\) be complete and discretely valued, let \(R=K^\circ\),
let \(\pi\) be a uniformizer, and suppose that the residue field
\(k\) is perfect. Set \(v=-\log|\cdot|\) and \(\ell=v(\pi)>0\).
Let \(\mathcal C/R\) be a smooth proper curve with geometrically
connected fibres, put \(C=\mathcal C_K\) and \(C_0=\mathcal C_k\),
and let \(\mathcal L\) be any line bundle on \(\mathcal C\).
Write \(L=\mathcal L_K\), equipped with its formal metric.
For the complement \(L^\times\) of the zero section of the
analytic total space, set
\[
 X=\{\xi\in L^\times:|\pi|<\|\xi\|_{\mathcal L}<1\},
 \qquad \rho:X\longrightarrow C^{\mathrm{an}},
 \qquad u(\xi)=-\log\|\xi\|_{\mathcal L}.
\]
No degree, nefness, or triviality condition is imposed on
\(\mathcal L\). The function \(u\) is used as a continuous
radius, not assumed to be an ordinary CLD smooth function.

Let \(T\) be a positive ordinary CLD current of bidegree
\((0,0)\) on \(X\), with \(\mathrm d'T=\mathrm d''T=0\).
For a closed \(p\in C_0\), put \(d_p=[\kappa(p):k]\).
Let \(R_p/R\) be the finite unramified extension with this
residue field, and choose a formal parameter and frame
\[
 \widehat{\mathcal O}_{\mathcal C,p}\simeq R_p[[w_p]],
 \qquad e_p\text{ a basis of }\widehat{\mathcal L}_p.
\]
On the inverse image of the tube of \(p\), write \(\xi=x_pe_p\).
This is an actual product over \(K_p=\operatorname{Frac}(R_p)\),
viewed as one \(K\)-analytic domain. On each sufficiently small
outer collar,
\[
 q_p=(u,s_p)=(v(x_p),v(w_p)),\qquad
 Q_p=(0,\ell)\times(0,\delta_p),\qquad
 \Omega_p=\mathrm d'u\wedge\mathrm d''u\wedge
                \mathrm d's_p\wedge\mathrm d''s_p.
\]
The occurrences of \(\mathrm d'u,\mathrm d''u\) here are confined
to this actual unit-coordinate collar.

There is a unique finite \(b_T(p)\geq0\) such that
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:p213-torsor-collar}
 \langle T,q_p^*(h\Omega_p)\rangle
    =b_T(p)\int_{Q_p}h(u,s)\,\mathrm du\,\mathrm ds,
 \qquad h\in C_c^\infty(Q_p).
\end{equation}
The test form has compact analytic support and is extended by
zero. The coefficient is independent of the chosen formal
frame, parameter, and sufficiently small outer collar. These
coefficients define a finite-valued positive homomorphism
\[
 \lambda_T:\operatorname{Div}(C_0)\longrightarrow\mathbf R,
 \qquad \lambda_T\Bigl(\sum_p a_pp\Bigr)=\sum_p a_pb_T(p),
\]
which annihilates all principal divisors:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:p213-torsor-principal}
 \sum_p \operatorname{ord}_p(f)b_T(p)=0
       \qquad(f\in k(C_0)^\times).
\end{equation}
Consequently \(b_T(p)=c\,d_p\) for one \(c\geq0\).

For the canonical integration current \(b_{\delta_X}(p)=d_p\).
If the separate chartwise source-calibration representation
is assumed, it identifies the calibration ratio on each
displayed source Gauss plane with \(b_T(p)/d_p=c\).
Only this final interpretation assumes that representation.
No assertion is made about all source cells, scalar rigidity
on all of \(X\), or a general semistable neighbourhood not
given by this annular-torsor construction.
\end{proposition}
