\begin{theorem}\label{thm:p143-dominated-divisorial}
Let \(X\) be a connected compact K\"ahler manifold. Write \(X^\beth\) for
the space of semivaluations of coherent ideal sheaves, with the topology
of pointwise convergence into \([0,+\infty]\). Put
\[
 q\leq v\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 q(\mathfrak a)\leq v(\mathfrak a)
 \quad\hbox{for every coherent ideal }\mathfrak a\subseteq\mathcal O_X .
\]
Let \(X^{\mathrm{div}}\) consist of the nonnegative rational multiples
of orders along prime divisors on proper bimeromorphic models of \(X\),
including the trivial valuation, as in \cite[Definition~2.3.1]{MP24}.
Then, for every \(v\in X^\beth\),
\begin{equation}
 v\in\overline{\{q\in X^{\mathrm{div}}:q\leq v\}}.
 \label{eq:p143-dominated-closure}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, there is a net \(q_\lambda\in X^{\mathrm{div}}\) converging
to \(v\), each member of which satisfies \(q_\lambda\leq v\).
The net is not asserted to be increasing. Semivaluations with nonzero
support, and infinite values on nonzero ideals, are included.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:p143-usc-antitone-comparison}
Let \(X\) be a connected compact K\"ahler manifold, and let \(\alpha\)
be a K\"ahler class. Suppose that \(F:X^\beth\to[-\infty,c]\), for some
\(c\in\mathbf R\), is upper semicontinuous and reverses the ideal order:
\(q\leq v\) implies \(F(q)\geq F(v)\).
If \(\phi\in\operatorname{PSH}_{\mathrm{MP}}(\alpha)\) satisfies
\(F=\phi\) on \(X^{\mathrm{div}}\), then
\begin{equation}
                         F=\phi\quad\hbox{on }X^\beth.
 \label{eq:p143-all-point-comparison}
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:p143-finite-energy-realization}
Assume the upper-semicontinuity conclusion of the frozen P14.2 candidate
specified in the dependency ledger. Let \(X\) be a connected compact
K\"ahler manifold, let \(\omega\) be a K\"ahler form, and let \(\Gamma\)
be a finite-energy DXZ non-Archimedean metric in \([\omega]\).
Then its raw ideal-valuative transform is the MP finite-energy potential
corresponding to \(\Gamma\), at every point of \(X^\beth\), not merely
at rational divisorial points.

Explicitly, for \(c=\Gamma_{\max}\), using either its finite-energy test
curve or one of its compatible perturbation components,
\begin{equation}
 F_\Gamma(v)=
 \sup_{\tau<c}\left\{\tau-
     \sup_{m\geq1}\frac1m v\bigl(\mathcal I(m\Gamma_\tau)\bigr)\right\}.
 \label{eq:p143-raw-transform}
\end{equation}
In particular the established finite-energy DXZ--MP bijection is
literally \(\Gamma\mapsto F_\Gamma\) as a map of functions on \(X^\beth\).
No arbitrary pseudoeffective MP cone, continuous-envelope theorem,
or unrestricted K\"ahler bijection is asserted in this first packet.
\end{corollary}
