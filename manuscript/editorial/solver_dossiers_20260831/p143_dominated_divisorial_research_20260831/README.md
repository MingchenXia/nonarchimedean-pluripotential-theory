# P14.3: dominated divisorial approximation

Status: isolated author research candidate; independent and production review required. No live manuscript changes. No production calls or permission workaround.

The packet proves a proposed order-controlled strengthening of ordinary divisorial density, on every semivaluation of coherent ideals of a connected compact Kähler manifold. It then gives an abstract all-point comparison and a finite-energy Kähler DXZ–MP consequence conditional on the independently audited, production-pending P14.2 USC candidate.

The new argument is not ordinary density in disguise: ordinary density supplies a lift through a proper modification; weighted monomial ideals plus a coherent annihilator supply domination on all ideals. Rational weights are rounded on log resolutions of X, not on normalized test-configuration simplices.

No priority claim is made. The proof is a detailed synthesis of standard analytic principalization and MP valuation constructions; its strengthened order conclusion has not been located as an explicit theorem in the MP source read.
