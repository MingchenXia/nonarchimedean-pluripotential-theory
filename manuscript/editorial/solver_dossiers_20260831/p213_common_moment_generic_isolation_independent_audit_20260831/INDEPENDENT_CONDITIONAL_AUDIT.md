## 3. Source inclusion and the full-rank/rank-drop dichotomy

For every compact analytic neighborhood W of a point, the affine projection identity implies dim J(W)≤dim H(W). Taking the infimum defining local tropical dimension gives dim_trop J≤dim_trop H. Each is at most n, so S_F∪S_G⊂S_H. This is a source-set conclusion, obtained without target properness.

For a compact H-top cell C, H|C is an affine homeomorphism onto an n-cell. If p_J has rank n on aff H(C), then J|C is also an affine homeomorphism onto an n-cell. Every neighborhood of an interior point contains a relatively open n-dimensional piece of C, so its J-image has dimension n. Thus C°⊂S_J. Closedness of S_J and density of C° in C give the stronger C⊂S_J, including the boundary. The independent strong-support check in the note is sound: a pulled-back positive coordinate top form is nonzero on C°, whereas Lemma5.3.4 would make it vanish as a germ at any point outside S_J.

Conversely, suppose p_J has rank <n on aff H(C). A top-cell interior is open in S_H by Lemma2.2.8. Around any point of C° there is therefore an ordinary source neighborhood W whose S_H-trace lies in C°. If W∩S_J were nonempty, it would be an open PL subset of the pure n-dimensional space S_J. Since J|S_J is a piecewise immersion, that open subset contains a compact n-dimensional source J-cell with n-dimensional J-image. Source inclusion puts it inside C, contradicting dim J(C)<n.

This proves the exact dichotomy (4). The second assertion deliberately concerns C°, not all of C: a boundary point may be incident to a different full-rank H-cell. The proof does not discard rank-drop cells as lower-dimensional. They are n-dimensional in S_H even though their projection has smaller dimension.

## 4. Finite PL exceptional set

When p_J has full rank on C, the already established containment C⊂S_J allows comparison within the same canonical source PL structure. Let B_J be the union of lower-dimensional cells of the chosen J-decomposition.

Local finiteness and compactness of C imply only finitely many J-cells meet C. Each relevant intersection C∩D is a genuine compact source PL subset, by Lemma3.2.8 and the compatibility of the induced structures. Its dimension is at most dim D. Since the lower-dimensional D have dimension ≤n−1, both Z_{C,J}=∂C∪(C∩B_J) and the two-projection Z_C have dimension ≤n−1. They are closed and compact.

This “finite” exception means a finite union of compact PL pieces, not a finite set of points in dimensions n>1. A finite PL subset of dimension <n has empty interior in the n-cell C, so its complement is nonempty, relatively open, and dense.

There is no substitution of intersecting target images for intersecting source cells. There is also no claim here that an arbitrary unrelated coefficient measure cannot charge an arbitrary analytic set. The exception is tied to these fixed cell presentations.

## 5. Ordinary-open equality, not only equality of target germs

For x outside Z_C, the cell interiors partition property gives unique J-top cells C_J containing x in their interiors. Each of C°, C_F°, C_G° is open in its own source skeleton. Since each skeleton is closed in V, each complement S_J\C_J° is closed in V. Thus the finite complement (7) is an actual ordinary open neighborhood U of x.

The equalities (8) follow by two inclusions, without any identification of source points:

- U∩S_H⊂C°, and the full-rank assertion gives C⊂S_F∩S_G. Hence U∩S_H⊂U∩S_J.
- S_J⊂S_H gives the reverse inclusion.

By the definition of U, U∩S_J=U∩C_J°; similarly U∩S_H=U∩C°. Therefore all traces in (8) are exactly the same set P. The proof correctly does not assert that C° is itself ordinary open in V.

This remains valid after shrinking U around x. The one-projection version is the same proof with the unused G-complement omitted.

For restricted moments, Proposition5.2.5(b) says the maximal-variation set is the n-dimensional part of the original source trace. Because an ordinary open subset of the pure n-dimensional S_J remains pure n-dimensional, that part is the whole trace. Thus no new source skeleton is inadvertently introduced by ordinary restriction.

## 7. Every-dimensional determinant and source-cutoff calculation

Now assume (R) for the moments involved. The common patch P is relatively open in the three top source cells. Therefore J(P) is an open n-dimensional subset both of p_J(aff H(C)) and of aff J(C_J), forcing those affine spans to coincide. The coordinate change in (9) is thus an affine isomorphism of n-planes, with j_J=|det A_J|>0.

The source cutoff exists by Corollary15.2.5 because U is an ordinary open neighborhood and V is locally holomorphically separated. Squaring a cutoff preserves its value 1 near x and makes it nonnegative. It may be regarded as a globally smooth function on V with compact support contained in U; alternatively, zero-extension of the compactly supported function from U gives the same global test.

The quantity I is finite: rho is bounded, H(C) is compact, and its coordinate support is contained in the image of the compact set supp(rho)∩C. It is strictly positive because rho=1 on a source neighborhood of x, whose intersection with P maps homeomorphically to a nonempty relatively open n-dimensional patch. No target cutoff with compact source inverse image is used.

The equality rho J*alpha_J=rho H*(p_J*alpha_J) holds on all of V because J=p_JH holds on all of V. It is not deduced from equality of restrictions to P.

There are two different Jacobian effects:

1. In the J-description, changing Lebesgue coordinates y=A_Jx+c_J gives dy=j_J dx. Its coefficient is therefore b_J j_J I.
2. In the H-description, the pulled-back ordered (n,n)-form contributes det(A_J) in each differential block, hence det(A_J)^2=j_J^2. Its coefficient is b_H j_J^2 I.

Reordering interleaved differentials into the two blocks introduces the same constant sign before and after pullback, so that sign cancels in every n. In particular, orientation-reversing coordinates do not introduce a minus sign into either positive integral. There is no omitted integer-index or lattice factor because the coordinates are arbitrary chosen affine coordinates and the canonical coefficients are evaluated in those same coordinates.

The equal test evaluations give b_J=j_J b_H. Applying the identical calculation to the canonical integration current gives a_J=j_J a_H. Dividing yields kappa_J=kappa_H. The argument does not divide by b_J or b_H, so zero current coefficients cause no exception.

The only uses of (R) here are exactly those stated: arbitrary ordinary compact source multipliers, coefficient-constant cell formulas, and no omitted lower-cell coefficient term. ## 8. Adjoining units and the remaining obstruction

The one-projection result applies when h is obtained by adjoining any finite list of actual analytic units to f on the same ordinary V. On a noncontracted H-top cell, each full-dimensional common-refinement piece has the same normalized coefficient as the old F-cell meeting it. Since the H-coefficient is constant on the original H-cell, this comparison is consistent across all its pieces. In particular, a common old ratio c transfers to that H-cell.

On a rank-drop H-top cell, C° is disjoint from S_F and all old F-top forms restrict to zero on C. That says why their tests cannot determine b_{H,C}; it does not determine that coefficient, say it vanishes, or allow the cell to be removed from an n-dimensional representation. Additional closedness identities such as particular branch calculations may help, but they are not consequences of this note.

Thus generic S1 is derived on the noncontracted locus at full stated scope. All-chart accessibility and the coefficients on genuinely new rank-drop families remain separate research questions.
