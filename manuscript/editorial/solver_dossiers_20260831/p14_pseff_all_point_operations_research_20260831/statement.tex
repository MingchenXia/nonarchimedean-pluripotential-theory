\section{Conditional realization and operations}

Let \(X\) be a connected compact K\"ahler manifold. Write
\(\mathcal K_X\) for its K\"ahler cone and
\(N(\alpha)=\PSH^{\NA}_{\mathrm{DXZ}}(X,\alpha)\), for a real
pseudoeffective class \(\alpha\). Let \(F_\Gamma\) be the raw
ideal-valuative function of \(\Gamma\), with finite endpoint
\(c_\Gamma\), on the MP spectrum \(X^\beth\). Let \(D\) be its
rational divisorial locus, including the zero valuation \(o\).

We assume the frozen P14.2 raw upper-semicontinuity theorem, the frozen
P14.3 dominated-divisorial comparison, and the conditional full K\"ahler
DXZ--MP order isomorphism. These are independently audited research inputs,
not production-accepted theorems. In particular, all conclusions below
remain conditional and outside the live book.

Define a set of actual functions, with pointwise order, by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p14op-mp-qpsh}
 \QPSH_{\mathrm{MP}}(X)
   =\bigcup_{\kappa\in\mathcal K_X}\PSH_{\mathrm{MP}}(\kappa).
\end{equation}
On the DXZ side use the ordered direct limit of the class inclusions
\(N(\alpha)\hookrightarrow N(\alpha+\eta)\), where \(\eta\) is
K\"ahler; this is \(\QPSH^{\NA}_{\mathrm{DXZ}}(X)\) in
\cite[Definition~3.5 and (3.5)]{Xia23}.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:p14op-global-realization}
Under the stated candidate inputs, the map \(\Gamma\mapsto F_\Gamma\)
induces an order isomorphism
\[
 \QPSH^{\NA}_{\mathrm{DXZ}}(X)
       \longrightarrow\QPSH_{\mathrm{MP}}(X).
\]
For every pseudoeffective \(\alpha\), its restriction to \(N(\alpha)\)
is an injective order-reflecting realization by actual functions on every
semivaluation. Each such function is upper semicontinuous, bounded above
by \(c_\Gamma=F_\Gamma(o)\), and reverses the coherent-ideal order.
It belongs to \(\PSH_{\mathrm{MP}}(\kappa)\) whenever both
\(\kappa\) and \(\kappa-\alpha\) are K\"ahler.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:p14op-all-point-operations}
Let \(\Gamma\in N(\alpha)\), \(\Gamma'\in N(\beta)\),
\(C\in\mathbf R\), and \(a>0\). The DXZ operations satisfy
the following equalities on all of \(X^\beth\):
\begin{align}
 F_{\Gamma+C}&=F_\Gamma+C,\label{eq:p14op-translation}\\
 F_{a\Gamma}&=aF_\Gamma,\label{eq:p14op-scaling}\\
 F_{\Gamma+\Gamma'}&=F_\Gamma+F_{\Gamma'},
       \label{eq:p14op-addition}\\
 F_{\Gamma\vee\Gamma'}&=\max\{F_\Gamma,F_{\Gamma'}\}
       \quad\text{if }\alpha=\beta.
       \label{eq:p14op-maximum}
\end{align}
If \((\Gamma_i)_{i\in I}\) is a decreasing net in \(N(\alpha)\)
and \(\inf_i c_{\Gamma_i}>-\infty\), its DXZ infimum satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p14op-infimum}
 F_{\inf_i\Gamma_i}(v)=\inf_i F_{\Gamma_i}(v)
       \qquad(v\in X^\beth).
\end{equation}
All extended-real operations use only finite constants and values in
\([-\infty,+\infty)\); no difference of two infinite values occurs.
\end{theorem}

For a non-nef \(\alpha\), \cref{thm:p14op-global-realization} does
\emph{not} identify its image intrinsically inside
\eqref{eq:p14op-mp-qpsh}, make it the unchanged relatively nef MP
model cone in class \(\alpha\), or provide a measure or energy theory.
Membership after increasing the background class is a different assertion.
