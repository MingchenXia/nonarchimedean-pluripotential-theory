## Boucksom--Chen: ample subseries and divisibility

Sébastien Boucksom and Huayi Chen, *Okounkov bodies of filtered linear series*, [arXiv:0911.2923v1](https://arxiv.org/html/0911.2923v1), 15 November 2009; published as Compositio Mathematica 147 (2011), 1205--1229, DOI 10.1112/S0010437X11005355.

Independently read Definition 1.1, the filtration boundedness setup, and the full proof of Lemma 1.6 in version-pinned primary HTML. 'Contains an ample series' includes a rational Kodaira decomposition with a complete ample subseries in every sufficiently large divisible degree. Applied to the complete section ring of big L, Lemma 1.6 supplies this for each tau below the maximal normalized weight. Multiplication by the fixed effective factor does not change the birational map of that ample subsystem. A larger system containing it is also birational: its field of ratios already contains the function field generated by the very ample subsystem.

Consequently one fixed m0 can be chosen for the given tau so that W_{m0 q} is birational for every sufficiently large integer q. It is legitimate to set q=2^j. No requirement that m0 divide a power of two is imposed. The attempted author-hosted published PDF open returned an internal retrieval error. This review therefore verifies the cited interface in v1 and in DXZ's explicit published-source use; it does not claim an independent visual check of the 2011 PDF.
