## Mathematical scope actually retained

For each fixed e >= 0, GVF_e requires ht(2) <= e, not equality. The
standalone formulation gives all tuple-height axioms, globality, the
projective height convention, field operations and height predicates.
Ordinary height is -infinity on the zero tuple; the continuous-language
encoding assigns -1 there. The gauges and height-preserving nature of
embeddings belong to the problem.

The full question asks for a model complete theory mutually embeddable
with GVF_e. Its equivalent elementary-class question concerns all
existentially closed models. The precise existential condition permits
epsilon slack both in the height bound and in the quantifier-free
formula value. Inductivity and existence of existentially closed
extensions are known logical inputs; these do not establish their
axiomatizability.

The height/place dictionary is established in the cited version. Ordinary
proper adelic representation retains the countable-field hypothesis;
the place space is not required to be countable or discrete. Arbitrary
fields instead use the stated pseudo-valuation/local-measure framework.
None of these statements settles the model-companion conjecture.

The classification discussion following Conjecture 12.7 is conditional
and up to scaling. Algebraic-closure bars are retained. Its earlier
existential-closure inputs are attributed and are not recursively
reconstructed here. Lemma 10.2 sets ht_Q[r](2) = r log(2), so preserving
a prescribed H requires r = H/log(2); the literal r = H assignment of
Corollary 12.8 is excluded.

The source leaves the fixed-e conjecture OPEN. No exhaustive later
literature search is claimed. A successful positive result must address
the exact elementary-class/companion question for each claimed parameter;
a negative result must give a failure in that exact language and scope.
No envelope, homogeneous Siu, Fujita or equidistribution claim follows
from the question alone. BJ22 homogeneous Siu remains OPEN.
