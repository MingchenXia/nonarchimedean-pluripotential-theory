\begin{proof}
For $U\in\mathcal U$, locality of currents gives
\[
 \mathrm d'\mathrm d''[h|_{B_{\ell,U}}]=0.
\]
Apply \Cref{thm:p062-interleaved-polyannulus-mixed-bundle-towers} to the
base-changed tower over $U$.  The restriction of $h$ is classically
pluriharmonic, locally $\mathbf R$-PL, and Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic
on $B_{\ell,U}$.  These sets cover $B_\ell$, and all three conclusions are
local for the analytic topology.  They therefore hold on $B_\ell$.
\end{proof}
