# P06.2 curve-local annular mixed towers — dependency audit

Date: 19 September 2026

## Claim boundary

One analytic open cover of the original published curve must coordinate
every annular stage after base change.  On each member of that cover, the
entire restricted tower, including every exact stage and its base
hypotheses, must satisfy
`thm:p062-interleaved-polyannulus-mixed-bundle-towers`.

The annular radii, coordinates, and product trivializations may vary across
the curve cover, and no compatibility on overlaps is assumed.  The
corollary consequently asserts only classical pluriharmonicity, local
real-PL regularity, and GJR harmonicity.  It does not assert a global
logarithmic coefficient formula.

## Proof ledger

1. Restricting the zero-current equation to each inverse image of a curve
   cover member preserves the equation.
2. The interleaved-tower theorem applies verbatim to that restricted tower
   and gives all three regularity conclusions there.
3. The inverse images cover the tower top, and the three conclusions are
   local for the analytic topology.

The result does not treat annular product charts available only over
arbitrary opens of an intermediate tower level.  Such opens need not retain
the earlier tower structure or the paracompactness hypotheses of its exact
stages.  P06.2 remains open, and the inventory count is unchanged.
