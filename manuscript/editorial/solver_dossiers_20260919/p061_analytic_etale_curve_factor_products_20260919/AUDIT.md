# P06.1 analytic standard-etale charts with arbitrary curve factors — audit

Date: 19 September 2026

## Claim boundary

The corollary treats actual strict affinoid monic standard-etale
presentations over an ordinary target neighbourhood in a toric variety
times a quasi-smooth parameter.  The toric variety must become split after
a complete extension obtained by completing an algebraic extension.  The
result simultaneously permits arbitrary finite good strict analytic curve
factors and one final factor with quasi-smooth reduction.

The result does not assert the existence of such a standard-etale chart for
an arbitrary analytic etale morphism, treat toric factors outside the stated
splitting class, or cover locally twisted nonproduct germs.

## Proof ledger

1. Scalar extension reaches a split toric standard-etale presentation.
2. Orbit localization absorbs the split orbit torus into the quasi-smooth
   target parameter, and the established analytic Newton contraction
   manufactures the pointed weighted-toric product in the source.
3. Product shrinking stays inside the function domain, and
   singleton-Shilov factorization removes the weighted toric factor.
4. The quasi-smooth fixed-fibre factor can be grouped with the final factor:
   the reduction of their product is quasi-smooth.  The arbitrary-curve
   theorem applies to the retained parameter.
5. Pullback gives the upstairs conclusion; completed-algebraic-extension
   descent and the two-sign comparison return it to the ground field.

This is a further verified positive range, not a closure of P06.1.  The
problem and active-atom inventory count remain unchanged.
