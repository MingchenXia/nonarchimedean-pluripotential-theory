\begin{proof}
Let $\Omega$ be an ordinary open analytic subspace of the displayed
product, let $h\in\operatorname{PH}(\Omega)$, and fix a point of $\Omega$.
Choose at its $X$-coordinate one of the displayed charts and its complete
algebraic splitting extension $L/K$.  The scalar-extension principle used
in the proof of
\Cref{cor:p061-analytic-standard-etale-algebraic-split-forms} makes $h_L$
classically pluriharmonic.  We work at an arbitrary point above the chosen
one.

The pointwise construction in the proof of
\Cref{cor:p061-analytic-standard-etale-all-orbits} restricts the split toric
target to its principal orbit chart, separates the pointed
full-dimensional toric factor from the split orbit torus, and absorbs the
torus into $(Y_0)_L$.  It then restricts the target to a product of a
weighted pointed strict toric affinoid and a quasi-smooth strict affinoid
parameter, while preserving the monic standard-etale presentation.  Applied
to that restricted presentation,
\Cref{prop:p061-analytic-standard-etale-toric-product} gives a strict
affinoid source neighbourhood isomorphic to
\[
                              U\times_LD,
\]
where $U$ is a weighted pointed toric affinoid, $D$ is quasi-smooth, and
every analytic fibre of the projection to $D$ has singleton Shilov
boundary.

Choose strict affinoid neighbourhoods of the remaining coordinates.  The
product-neighbourhood lemma \Cref{lem:p061-product-open-tube}, followed by
a deeper weighted toric domain, gives inside $\Omega_L$ a product
\[
                              U'\times_LV
\]
around the chosen point, where $V$ is a strict affinoid neighbourhood in
\[
 D\times_L(C_1)_L\times_L\cdots\times_L(C_m)_L\times_LY_L.
\]
The projection to $V$ has a fixed section and singleton-Shilov analytic
fibres.  Hence \Cref{prop:p061-v3-single-shilov-factorization} gives
\[
                     h_L|_{U'\times_LV}=p_V^*q,
                     \qquad q\in\operatorname{PH}(V).
\]

The reduction of $D\times_LY_L$ is quasi-smooth.  Indeed,
$(Y_{\mathrm{red}})_L$ is quasi-smooth and reduced, and its nilpotent
closed immersion into $Y_L$ identifies it with $(Y_L)_{\mathrm{red}}$.
On strict affinoid charts,
\Cref{lem:p061-product-factor-reduction} gives a nilpotent closed immersion
\[
 D\times_L(Y_L)_{\mathrm{red}}\longrightarrow D\times_LY_L.
\]
The source is quasi-smooth and reduced, so it is the reduction of the
target.  On an ordinary open neighbourhood of the parameter point contained
in $V$, \Cref{thm:p061-arbitrary-curve-factors-ph-rpl} therefore makes $q$
locally $\mathbf R$-PL and Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic.  Pullback
stability \cite[Lemma~5.4(1)]{GJR26} and
\Cref{prop:ch18-harmonic-functorial} give the two conclusions for $h_L$
near the chosen point.

Since the point was arbitrary, sheafiness makes $h_L$ $\mathbf R$-PL on
$\Omega_L$.  Ground-field descent \cite[Corollary~5.12]{GJR26} makes $h$
$\mathbf R$-PL on $\Omega$.  The final two-sign comparison in the proof of
\Cref{cor:p061-analytic-standard-etale-algebraic-split-forms} gives
harmonicity over $K$.  The chartwise conclusion is local in the ordinary
topology, and transfer through analytic local isomorphisms proves the last
assertion.
\end{proof}
