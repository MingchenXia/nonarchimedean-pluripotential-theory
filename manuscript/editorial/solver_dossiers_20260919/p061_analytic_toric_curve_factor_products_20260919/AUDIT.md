# P06.1 analytic toric charts with arbitrary curve factors — audit

Date: 19 September 2026

## Claim boundary

The corollary permits the toric factor itself to be analytic and only
pointwise locally identifiable, after a complete algebraic splitting
extension, with an ordinary open subspace of a split toric variety.  The
parameter may simultaneously contain an arbitrary finite family of good
strict analytic curves and a final factor with quasi-smooth reduction.

The splitting chart remains an explicit hypothesis.  The result neither
constructs such charts nor treats nonalgebraic splitting extensions,
locally twisted nonproduct gluings, or arbitrary singular germs.

## Proof ledger

1. Restrict to one displayed ordinary analytic splitting neighbourhood.
2. Scalar extension preserves classical pluriharmonicity and identifies the
   restricted product with an ordinary open subspace of a split toric
   product.
3. Base-changed curve factors remain good strict curves, and quasi-smooth
   reduction of the final factor is preserved.
4. `cor:p061-toric-arbitrary-curve-factor-products` gives real-PL
   regularity and harmonicity after scalar extension.
5. `GJR26`, Corollary 5.12 descends real-PL regularity; the established
   two-sign comparison gives harmonicity over the ground field.

This is a further verified positive range, not a closure of P06.1.  The
problem and the active-atom inventory count remain unchanged.
