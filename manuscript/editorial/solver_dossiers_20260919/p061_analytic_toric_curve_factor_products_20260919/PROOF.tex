\begin{proof}
Let $\Omega$ be an ordinary open analytic subspace of the displayed
product, let $h\in\operatorname{PH}(\Omega)$, and fix a point of $\Omega$.
Choose a neighbourhood $U$, an extension $L/K$, and a split toric
$L$-variety $\mathcal X$ as in the hypothesis.  After replacing $\Omega$
by its intersection with
\[
 U\times_K C_1\times_K\cdots\times_K C_m\times_KY,
\]
scalar extension identifies $\Omega_L$ with an ordinary open analytic
subspace of
\[
 \mathcal X^{\mathrm{an}}\times_L(C_1)_L\times_L\cdots
       \times_L(C_m)_L\times_LY_L.
\]

The scalar-extension assertion used in the proof of
\Cref{cor:p061-algebraic-split-analytic-toric-forms} makes $h_L$
classically pluriharmonic.  Each $(C_i)_L$ is again a good strict analytic
curve, and the reduction of $Y_L$ is quasi-smooth.  The split case of
\Cref{cor:p061-toric-arbitrary-curve-factor-products} therefore makes
$h_L$ locally $\mathbf R$-PL and Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic.

The space $\Omega$ is good and strict, and $L$ is the completion of an
algebraic extension.  Ground-field descent
\cite[Corollary~5.12]{GJR26} makes $h$ locally $\mathbf R$-PL.  Since
$\mathbf R$-PL functions form a sheaf, $h$ is $\mathbf R$-PL on $\Omega$.
The final two-sign comparison in the proof of
\Cref{cor:p061-algebraic-split-analytic-toric-forms} gives harmonicity over
$K$.  As the chosen point was arbitrary, this proves the product assertion.
Transfer through an analytic local isomorphism proves the final assertion.
\end{proof}
