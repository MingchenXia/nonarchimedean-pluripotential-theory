\begin{proof}
We first record a consequence of the stagewise geometry.  For every
$0\leq r\leq\ell$, each locally constant function on $B_r$ is the pullback
of a unique locally constant function on $W$.  Indeed, every fibre at every
stage is connected.  This is immediate for a finite product of open annuli
or open discs, for analytic projective space, for a smooth proper split
toric variety, and for the analytification of a geometrically connected
proper curve.  Hence a locally constant function is constant on every
fibre.  The local product charts provide local sections: use the origin for
an open polydisc, a coordinate point for projective space, the identity of
the dense split torus for a split-toric fibre, and a $K$-point for a
nonempty proper curve.  At a globally
coordinated annular stage, algebraic closedness of $K$ makes $|K^\times|$
divisible, so each interval $(r_{j,k},R_{j,k})$ contains the norm of an
element of $K^\times$; their product gives a global section.  Pullback along
these sections produces locally constant functions on the preceding base,
and fibrewise constancy makes the local functions agree on overlaps.
Iteration gives the assertion; uniqueness follows from surjectivity.

We now proceed by induction on the length of the tower.  Simultaneously, we
prove the displayed coefficient formula and the following two properties of
$B_r$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item every continuous zero-current function is classically pluriharmonic
and $\mathbf R$-harmonic;
\item every $u\in C^0(B_r,\mathbf R)$ with
$\mathrm d'\mathrm d''[u]=0$ and $\mathrm d'[u]=0$ is locally constant.
\end{enumerate}
For $r=0$, the coefficient formula has no annular term.  The first property
is \Cref{cor:ch19-continuous-zero-current-harmonic}, and the second is
\Cref{lem:p062-curve-first-current-rigidity}.

Suppose the assertions hold through level $r-1$.  First assume that
$\pi_r$ is one of the four exact bundle types.  The applicable descent
proposition gives a unique continuous zero-current function $g$ on
$B_{r-1}$ with $h=\pi_r^*g$.  Pulling back the induction formula for $g$
gives the formula at level $r$, with the same coefficients on $W$ and with
each earlier $L_{j,k}$ pulled back along $\pi_r$.  This also proves
uniqueness.  The first two transfer assertions of
\Cref{prop:p062-mixed-exact-bundle-tower-transfer} give classical
pluriharmonicity and $\mathbf R$-harmonicity at level $r$.  Its
first-current-rigidity assertion gives the second induction property.

Now assume that $\pi_r$ is a globally coordinated polyannulus stage.  The
induction hypotheses verify the two base assumptions of
\Cref{prop:p062-open-polyannulus-rigid-base}.  Applying that proposition
with base $B_{r-1}$ gives unique continuous functions
$c,\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_{s_r}$ on $B_{r-1}$ such that
\[
 h=\pi_r^*c+
   \sum_{k=1}^{s_r}(\alpha_k\circ\pi_r)q_{r,k}^*\log|T_{r,k}|,
 \qquad \mathrm d'\mathrm d''[c]=0,
\]
and every $\alpha_k$ is locally constant.  By the preliminary observation,
there is a unique locally constant $a_{r,k}:W\to\mathbf R$ with
$\alpha_k=a_{r,k}\circ(\pi_1\circ\cdots\circ\pi_{r-1})$.  Applying the
induction formula to $c$ gives the displayed formula at level $r$.
Uniqueness follows successively from the rigid-base proposition, the
preliminary observation, and induction.

The rigid-base proposition makes $h$ classically pluriharmonic.  The
induction hypothesis makes $c$ $\mathbf R$-harmonic.  Each $\alpha_k$ is
locally constant, while $\log|T_{r,k}|$ is $\mathbf R$-harmonic by
\Cref{prop:ch18-harmonic-basic}\,\cref{itm:ch18-hb:units}.  Harmonic
pullback and the sheaf and vector-space properties therefore make $h$
$\mathbf R$-harmonic.  Finally, the last assertion of the rigid-base
proposition gives first-current rigidity on $B_r$.  This completes the
induction.  At $r=\ell$, $\mathbf R$-harmonicity gives local
$\mathbf R$-PL regularity and Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonicity.
\end{proof}
