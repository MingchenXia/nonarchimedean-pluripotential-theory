\begin{proof}
We first verify the two base hypotheses of
\Cref{prop:p062-open-polyannulus-rigid-base} for $B_\ell$.  The first is
\Cref{thm:p062-mixed-bundle-towers-over-curves}.  For the second, let
$u\in C^0(B_\ell,\mathbf R)$ satisfy
\[
             \mathrm d'\mathrm d''[u]=0,
             \qquad \mathrm d'[u]=0.
\]
Starting at the top of the tower, apply the strengthened first-current
conclusion in the applicable one of
\Cref{prop:p062-open-polydisc-bundle-descent,prop:p062-projective-bundle-descent,prop:p062-smooth-proper-split-toric-bundle-descent,prop:p062-proper-smooth-curve-bundle-descent}.
This gives a continuous function $v$ on $W$ with
$u=\Pi^*v$, $\mathrm d'\mathrm d''[v]=0$, and $\mathrm d'[v]=0$.
By \Cref{lem:p062-curve-first-current-rigidity}, the function $v$, and
hence $u$, is locally constant.

The rigid-base proposition now gives unique continuous functions
$\widetilde b,\widetilde a_1,\ldots,\widetilde a_s$ on $B_\ell$ such that
\[
 h=p^*\widetilde b+
   \sum_{i=1}^s(\widetilde a_i\circ p)q_i^*\log|T_i|,
 \qquad \mathrm d'\mathrm d''[\widetilde b]=0,
\]
and every $\widetilde a_i$ is locally constant.  Applying
\Cref{thm:p062-mixed-bundle-towers-over-curves} to $\widetilde b$ gives a
unique continuous zero-current function $b$ on $W$ with
$\widetilde b=\Pi^*b$.

Every fibre occurring in the tower is connected.  Indeed, it is a standard
strict open polydisc, an analytic projective space, a smooth proper split
toric variety, or the analytification of a geometrically connected proper
smooth curve.  A locally constant function on the total space of any
displayed locally trivial bundle is therefore constant on every fibre.
Each displayed fibre also has a $K$-rational point: use the origin for an
open polydisc, a coordinate point for projective space, the identity of the
dense split torus for a split-toric fibre, and algebraic closedness of $K$
for the nonempty proper curve.  On every trivializing open set that point
gives an analytic section, along which the fibrewise-constant function pulls
back to a locally constant function on the base.  These local functions
agree on overlaps and give the unique descent.  Applying this observation
successively to each $\widetilde a_i$ gives a unique locally constant
function $a_i$ on $W$ with $\widetilde a_i=\Pi^*a_i$.  Substitution gives
the displayed formula.
Its uniqueness follows from the uniqueness in the rigid-base proposition
and the surjectivity of $\Pi$.

The rigid-base proposition already makes $h$ classically pluriharmonic.
By \Cref{cor:ch19-continuous-zero-current-harmonic}, the function $b$ is
$\mathbf R$-harmonic.  Its pullback is $\mathbf R$-harmonic by
\Cref{prop:ch18-harmonic-functorial}.  Locally every $a_i$ is constant,
while $\log|T_i|$ is harmonic by
\Cref{prop:ch18-harmonic-basic}\,\cref{itm:ch18-hb:units}; the same
functoriality and the sheaf property therefore make the displayed sum
$\mathbf R$-harmonic.  Thus $h$ is Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic and
locally $\mathbf R$-PL.
\end{proof}
