# Source and dependency ledger

Date: 19 September 2026; updated 20 September 2026 for the split-toric stage

## Integrated dependencies

- `prop:p062-open-polydisc-bundle-descent`: unique zero-current descent and
  strengthened first-current descent for locally trivial standard strict
  open-polydisc bundles.
- `prop:p062-projective-bundle-descent`: the same two descents for locally
  trivial analytic projective-space bundles over locally holomorphically
  separated paracompact bases.
- `prop:p062-smooth-proper-split-toric-bundle-descent`: the same two
  descents for locally trivial smooth proper split-toric bundles over
  locally holomorphically separated paracompact bases.
- `prop:p062-proper-smooth-curve-bundle-descent`: the same two descents for
  locally trivial proper smooth connected curve bundles over algebraically
  closed fields and the stated locally holomorphically separated paracompact
  bases.
- `prop:ch18-harmonic-functorial`: analytic pullback preserves real-harmonic
  functions.
- `GR25Classical`, Proposition 4.2(6): analytic pullback preserves classical
  psh functions.

## Dependency closure

The proposition only composes the four already integrated exact
descent statements.  It introduces no scalar extension of a current, no
section pullback of a current, no new fibre-slicing assertion, and no
external regularity theorem beyond the two already used pullback facts.
