\begin{proof}
Starting with $\pi_\ell$ and proceeding down the tower, apply respectively
\Cref{prop:p062-open-polydisc-bundle-descent,prop:p062-projective-bundle-descent,prop:p062-smooth-proper-split-toric-bundle-descent,prop:p062-proper-smooth-curve-bundle-descent}
according to the specified type of each stage.  The hypotheses above retain
all geometric assumptions of the applicable proposition.  At every step
one obtains a unique continuous zero-current function on the preceding
base.  The pullback identities compose, and uniqueness propagates because
every displayed bundle is surjective with nonempty fibres.  This gives the
unique function $b$ on $B_0$ and the displayed identities.

Assume the first additional hypothesis.  Both $b$ and $-b$ are classically
psh.  Analytic pullback preserves classical psh functions by
\cite[Proposition~4.2(6)]{GR25Classical}; hence both $h=\Pi^*b$ and $-h$
are classically psh, proving the first transfer assertion.

Under the second additional hypothesis, $b$ is $\mathbf R$-harmonic.
The pullback assertion of \Cref{prop:ch18-harmonic-functorial} makes
$h=\Pi^*b$ $\mathbf R$-harmonic.  Such a function is locally
$\mathbf R$-PL by definition and is Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic.

Finally, suppose that $h$ also satisfies $\mathrm d'[h]=0$.  At every
stage, the strengthened first-current conclusion of the applicable descent
proposition makes the uniquely descended function satisfy the same
first-current equation.  Thus $\mathrm d'[b]=0$.  The third additional
hypothesis makes $b$ locally constant, and so its pullback $h$ is locally
constant.  This proves the last transfer assertion.
\end{proof}
