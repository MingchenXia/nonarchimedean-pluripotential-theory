\begin{proof}
Starting with $\pi_\ell$ and proceeding down the tower, apply respectively
\Cref{prop:p062-open-polydisc-bundle-descent,prop:p062-projective-bundle-descent,prop:p062-proper-smooth-curve-bundle-descent}
according to the specified type of each stage.  The hypotheses above retain
the full geometric assumptions of the applicable descent proposition.  At
every step one obtains a unique continuous zero-current function on the
preceding base.  The pullback identities compose and uniqueness propagates,
giving the displayed function $b$ on $W$.

By \Cref{cor:ch19-continuous-zero-current-harmonic}, the function $b$ is
classically pluriharmonic and Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic.  Analytic
pullback preserves classical psh functions by
\cite[Proposition~4.2(6)]{GR25Classical}; applying this to both $b$ and
$-b$ makes $h=\Pi^*b$ classically pluriharmonic.  Finally, the pullback
assertion of \Cref{prop:ch18-harmonic-functorial} makes $h$
$\mathbf R$-harmonic.  Hence it is Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic and,
in particular, locally $\mathbf R$-PL.
\end{proof}
