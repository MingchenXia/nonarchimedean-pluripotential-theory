# P06.2 annular bases under mixed exact towers — dependency audit

Date: 19 September 2026

## Claim boundary

The results allow annular geometry at the bottom of a finite mixed exact
tower.  For a globally coordinated curve-polyannulus product, the unique
logarithmic coefficient formula survives after pulling it through the tower.
For locally trivial curve-polyannulus and mixed curve-polycylinder bundles,
only classical pluriharmonicity, local real-PL regularity, and GJR
harmonicity are claimed.

Every later stage must be an admissible open-polydisc, projective-space, or
proper smooth connected curve bundle.  Annular stages are not interleaved at
arbitrary later levels, and annular fibrations without the displayed local
charts remain outside the statements.

## Proof ledger

1. The mixed exact-tower transfer gives a unique continuous zero-current
   function on the chosen annular bottom space.
2. In the globally coordinated case, the curve-polyannulus product theorem
   gives the unique coefficient formula; composing with the tower projection
   preserves uniqueness.
3. In all three cases, the bottom theorem gives classical
   pluriharmonicity and real-harmonicity, which the transfer proposition
   carries to the top.

No global coefficient formula is inferred from locally twisted annular
coordinates.  P06.2 remains open, and the inventory count is unchanged.
