\begin{proof}
Let $\Omega$ be an ordinary open analytic subspace of the displayed
product, let $h\in\operatorname{PH}(\Omega)$, and restrict near an arbitrary
point to one displayed source chart.  Choose its complete algebraic
splitting extension $L/K$.  The scalar-extension step in the proof of
\Cref{cor:p061-zariski-etale-algebraic-split-toric-charts} makes $h_L$
classically pluriharmonic.  We show that it is locally
$\mathbf R$-PL and harmonic at every point of $\Omega_L$.

The base-changed chart is ordinary etale over the product of the split toric
variety $\mathcal Z_L$ and the smooth variety $(\mathcal Y_0)_L$.  At the
chosen toric orbit, the principal-open decomposition
\eqref{eq:p061-orbit-product-chart} separates a pointed full-dimensional
toric factor from a split orbit torus; absorb that torus into
$(\mathcal Y_0)_L$.  The construction in
\Cref{prop:p061-v3-etale-toric-product} then gives a strict affinoid source
neighbourhood isomorphic to
\[
                         U\times_LD,
\]
where $U$ is a weighted pointed toric affinoid neighbourhood of its fixed
point, $D$ is quasi-smooth, and every analytic fibre of the projection to
$D$ has singleton Shilov boundary.

Choose strict affinoid neighbourhoods of the remaining coordinates.  The
product-neighbourhood lemma \Cref{lem:p061-product-open-tube}, followed by
a deeper weighted toric domain, gives inside $\Omega_L$ a product
\[
                          U'\times_LV
\]
around the chosen point, where $V$ is a strict affinoid neighbourhood in
\[
 D\times_L(C_1)_L\times_L\cdots\times_L(C_m)_L\times_LY_L.
\]
The projection to $V$ has a fixed section and singleton-Shilov analytic
fibres.  Hence \Cref{prop:p061-v3-single-shilov-factorization} factors
\[
                  h_L|_{U'\times_LV}=p_V^*g,
                  \qquad g\in\operatorname{PH}(V).
\]

The reduction of $D\times_LY_L$ is quasi-smooth.  Indeed,
$(Y_{\mathrm{red}})_L$ is quasi-smooth and reduced, and the nilpotent
closed immersion $(Y_{\mathrm{red}})_L\to Y_L$ therefore identifies it
with $(Y_L)_{\mathrm{red}}$.  On strict affinoid charts,
\Cref{lem:p061-product-factor-reduction} then gives a nilpotent closed
immersion
\[
 D\times_L(Y_L)_{\mathrm{red}}
       \longrightarrow D\times_LY_L.
\]
Its source is quasi-smooth and reduced, so it is the reduction of the
target.  Thus, on an ordinary open neighbourhood of the chosen parameter
point contained in $V$, the function $g$ lies in the local-model scope of
\Cref{thm:p061-arbitrary-curve-factors-ph-rpl}.  It is locally
$\mathbf R$-PL and Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff harmonic there.  Pullback stability
\cite[Lemma~5.4(1)]{GJR26} and
\Cref{prop:ch18-harmonic-functorial} give the same two conclusions for
$h_L$ near the chosen point.

It follows at every point, and sheafiness makes $h_L$ $\mathbf R$-PL on
$\Omega_L$.  Ground-field descent \cite[Corollary~5.12]{GJR26} makes $h$
$\mathbf R$-PL on $\Omega$.  The final two-sign comparison in the proof of
\Cref{cor:p061-zariski-etale-algebraic-split-toric-charts} gives harmonicity
over $K$.  The source charts cover $\mathcal X$, and transfer through an
analytic local isomorphism proves the last assertion.
\end{proof}
