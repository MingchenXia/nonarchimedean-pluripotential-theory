# P06.1 ordinary-etale toric charts with arbitrary curve factors — audit

Date: 19 September 2026

## Claim boundary

The corollary treats source-Zariski-local separated finite-type
ordinary-etale charts to a toric variety times a smooth variety, where the
toric factor is split after a complete extension obtained by completing an
algebraic extension.  It permits, simultaneously, arbitrary finite good
strict analytic curve factors and one final factor with quasi-smooth
reduction.

The result does not cover genuinely log-etale charts not represented by the
displayed ordinary-etale map, toric factors outside the stated splitting
class, or locally twisted nonproduct singular germs.

## Proof ledger

1. Scalar extension reaches a split ordinary-etale toric chart.
2. Orbit localization absorbs the split orbit torus into the smooth target
   factor, and the established Newton-tube construction manufactures an
   actual product of a pointed weighted toric affinoid and a quasi-smooth
   fixed-fibre factor.
3. Product shrinking stays inside the function's ordinary open domain.
   Singleton-Shilov factorization then makes the function the pullback of a
   classically pluriharmonic parameter function.
4. The quasi-smooth reduction of the final factor stays quasi-smooth and
   reduced after scalar extension.  The product of the quasi-smooth
   fixed-fibre factor with that reduction is therefore the quasi-smooth
   reduction of the enlarged final factor.  The arbitrary-curve-factor
   theorem gives real-PL regularity and harmonicity on the parameter.
5. Pullback gives the upstairs result; completed-algebraic-extension descent
   and the two-sign comparison give the ground-field result.

This is a further verified positive range, not a closure of P06.1.  The
problem and the active-atom inventory count remain unchanged.
