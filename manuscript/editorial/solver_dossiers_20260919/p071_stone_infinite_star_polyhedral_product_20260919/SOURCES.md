Stone family and one compact metrizable zero-dimensional quotient.  On the
resulting compact star--polytope reduction, the nonexpanding exact envelope
turns uniform Stone discretization into a finite family of comparison
functions.  The already proved uniform one-step smoothing estimate and a
summable tail then produce one decreasing generator sequence on a fixed
smaller product neighbourhood.
