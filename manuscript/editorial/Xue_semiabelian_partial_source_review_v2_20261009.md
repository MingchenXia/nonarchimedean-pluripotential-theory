## A concrete failure of the printed compression estimates

Pages 34 and 38–39 define L(m,n)=Gn(D_m)+pi_n^*N and compare source and
target with the same horizontal index m. For a translated quasi-canonical
toric metric, the metrized n-pullback instead has horizontal index mn.
Remark 57 explicitly records (1/n)phi_n^*D_m=D_mn, but still claims that
the geometric degree/local-intersection comparison bypasses this change.

The simplest source-permitted case tests that claim exactly. Set K=Q,
A=0, G=Gm, X=P1 and D=[0]+[infinity]. Translate its canonical boundary
metric by a=2. The critical vectors val_v(2) have weighted sum zero;
the metric is semipositive, quasi-canonical and arithmetically T-effective,
with global roof zero. D_m has complex probability measure on the circle
of radius 2^(1/m), Gn=G and phi_n(z)=z^n. For the smooth projective test
f(z)=|z|²/(1+|z|²), the printed source integral of f(z^(mn)) is
4^n/(1+4^n), while its target integral is 4/5. The difference tends to
**1/5**, rather than O(1/n). Unnormalized mass-two measures give 2/5.
The discrepancy is independent of positive m, including m<=n.

This disproves the literal auxiliary measure comparison in Lemma 54,
Proposition 55 and Lemma 56. It **does not refute the intended main
equidistribution theorem**: translated toric equidistribution is a known
case. Changing the family index would alter the rest of the proof, which
has not been repaired or completely read. No new book theorem or
independent verifier receipt is claimed.

## Other proof obligations and a direct scaling check

Genuine group multiplication also acts on the abelian quotient: its total
degree is m^(t+2g), and the symmetric canonical abelian factor scales by
m². The separately asserted m exponent in Lemma 34 omits that effect.
A compatible joint n,m projection calculation gives a factor
(nm)^(r-1)*(m/n)^(g-j), which can retain the desired upper bound for
j<=g and m<=n. This conditional formal calculation does not fix the
noncanonical metric mismatch just exhibited.
