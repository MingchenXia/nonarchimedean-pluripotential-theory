Let X be a connected smooth projective complex variety of dimension n. For
1 <= i <= n, let L_i be a big line bundle and let h_i be a singular
plurisubharmonic metric on L_i with positive Cao volume. For every surjective
valuation nu: C(X)^times -> Z^n_lex that is trivial on C^times, let
Delta_nu(L_i,h_i) be the partial Okounkov body defined using the multiplier-
ideal graded series. Normalize mixed Euclidean volume by
vol(K,...,K)=vol_R^n(K), and define

  vol(dd^c h_1,...,dd^c h_n)

as the mixed non-pluripolar product of the corresponding I-model envelopes,
equivalently the mixed volume in Cao's sense. Then

  vol(dd^c h_1,...,dd^c h_n)
  = n! sup_nu vol(Delta_nu(L_1,h_1),...,Delta_nu(L_n,h_n)),

where the supremum ranges over all such valuations nu.
