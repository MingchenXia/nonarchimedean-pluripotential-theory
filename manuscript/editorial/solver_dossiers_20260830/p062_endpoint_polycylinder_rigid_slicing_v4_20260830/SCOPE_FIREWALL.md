# Scope firewall

- This dossier is a sibling of v3 and changes no v3 or live file.
- The strict-open annulus proposition applies only to `r<|T|<R`.
- The endpoint proposition applies only to open--closed, closed--open, and
  closed--closed annuli.
- The strict-open polycylinder theorem applies only to coordinatewise strict
  open factors.
- The endpoint theorem assumes the current equation on the absolute analytic
  interior and uses finite residue extensions at rigid base points.
- No claim is made for non-strict radii, arbitrary analytic germs, or arbitrary
  complete scalar extensions in the endpoint-interior identity.
- Historical production receipts certify only their recorded exact inputs;
  they do not certify the new v4 strict-open exact bytes.
