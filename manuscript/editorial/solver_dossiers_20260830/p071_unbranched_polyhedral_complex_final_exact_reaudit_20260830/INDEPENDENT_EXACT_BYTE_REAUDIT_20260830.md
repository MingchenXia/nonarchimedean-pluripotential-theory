## Mathematical audit

### Typed local model

The hypothesis is stronger than combinatorial or topological
unbranching: every complete germ, together with its specified stage-zero
generator sheaf, is identified with a relatively open convex subset of one
polyhedron carrying the exact one-chamber generator.  The visible cells are
only a subdivision.  This is precisely the hypothesis needed for chart
transport and does not silently infer cross-face positivity from cellwise
positivity.

The overlap condition is sufficient (and, given the fixed source sheaf,
partly redundant) to make the product generator sheaf well defined.

### Transport of stages

A generator-preserving homeomorphism transports a first-stage decreasing
sequence term by term.  For the full generated sheaf, pulling back the
target full sheaf gives a decreasing-sequentially closed sheaf containing
the source generators; minimality gives one inclusion, and the inverse
homeomorphism gives the other.  The argument has the correct direction and
uses no hidden global development.

### Restriction to the open chart target

The added restriction argument is valid.  If `S` is open, the sheaf
generated by `G_0|S` is contained in `G|S`.  Conversely, the subsheaf of
`G` whose germs over `S` lie in the generated sheaf on `S` contains
`G_0` and is stable under local decreasing limits.  For a decreasing
sequence of its sections, all restrictions to the common open set
`U intersect S` are sections of the same generated sheaf on `S`; hence
there is no illicit countable intersection of shrinking neighbourhoods.
Minimality proves the reverse inclusion.

Since the chart image `V` is relatively open in `P`, `Z times V` is an
open subset of the model `Z times P`, so this lemma applies at exactly the
place where it is used.

### Chart independence of the comparison sheaf

In one chart, the factorized fibrewise-convex condition equals the model
first stage by the pinned theorem.  A transition preserving stage zero
preserves stage one.  Applying the pinned equality in the second chart,
after a convex shrinking, recovers exactly the same type of Stone quotient
and fibrewise-convex factorization.  Reversing the charts proves
equivalence.  The new open-set definition of `C_X` then makes it an
ordinary subsheaf, not an untyped collection of germs.

### Equality and fixed-domain approximation

The inclusions

```text

```

follow directly from the model equality and transport.  For the stronger
local statement, one model sequence is constructed on one fixed
`C times W` and the whole sequence is pulled back.  The domain crosses all
faces represented in the complete chart; no chamberwise gluing is used.

### Monodromy and quantifiers

The theorem is an equality of sheaves and gives one sequence only on a
fixed chart neighbourhood of a given germ.  It never continues a chart
around a loop and never asserts a global approximating sequence.  Affine
monodromy therefore does not enter the proof.  This matches the local
quantifiers in CLD Definition 8.1.1 and Lemma 8.1.4.

### Obstruction firewall

The perpendicular two-ray calculation is correct for every positive
cellwise-affine rescaling: the ambient affine generator `-x-y` unfolds with
positive left slope and negative right slope, hence is nonconvex.  It proves
that a PL/topological unfolding need not preserve generators; it is not
misrepresented as a counterexample to hierarchy collapse.

The companion straight-edge and balanced-tripod tests correctly derive the
necessary star-Jensen inequality from a common ambient tangent covector.
They show that chamberwise or branchwise convexity is too weak.  The ledger
states only this necessary obstruction and explicitly leaves unrestricted
higher-dimensional chamber gluing open.

### Boundary cases

Dimension zero, lower-dimensional affine spans, relative boundary and
corner neighbourhoods, and unbounded polyhedra are inherited at exactly the
strength of the model theorem.  Empty complexes are vacuous.  The theorem
does not turn codimension-one incidence into control of singular lower
links; branched and singular-link germs remain excluded.
