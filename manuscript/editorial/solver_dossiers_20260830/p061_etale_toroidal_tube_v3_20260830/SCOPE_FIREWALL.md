# Scope firewall

The principal theorem is local near one point and assumes an **actual
ordinary algebraic etale chart** to a split pointed toric factor times a
smooth factor.  The following conclusions are not asserted.

- A general etale morphism is an analytic local isomorphism at a nonrigid
  Berkovich point.
- The fixed-fibre cover (D\to V) is trivial.  It is retained as a factor.
- A formal-completion or completed-local-ring product converges to an
  analytic product.
- A bare log-etale, Kummer-etale, or logarithmically smooth morphism is
  ordinary etale on underlying spaces along the boundary.
- A chart that exists only after an arbitrary etale source cover descends to
  the original source.  The present global corollary assumes the ordinary
  toric-times-smooth chart on a Zariski source neighbourhood.
- Independently chosen standard-etale trivializations glue along a whole
  stratum.
- The toric tube or real-PL conclusion holds over a trivially valued field.
- The good/strict hypotheses in the GJR real-PL pullback step can be removed.
- The finite coefficient-contraction criterion is logically necessary among
  all possible analytic trivialization criteria.  It is the weakest
  presentation-level sufficient hypothesis used by this proof.

The general singleton-Shilov proposition deliberately stops at
factorization.  Its separate real-PL corollary adds every hypothesis required
by GJR Lemma 5.4(1).

The pointed toric factor is allowed to have rank zero.  That case is the
one-point factor `Spec K` and is proved separately; in the all-orbits
corollary it is precisely the transverse factor at the dense torus orbit.
This inclusion does not weaken any ordinary-etale, nontrivial-valuation,
goodness, or strictness hypothesis above.
