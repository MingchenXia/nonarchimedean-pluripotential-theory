\begin{proposition}\label{prop:p213-hessian-regular-basis-candidate}
Let \(X\) be a pure \(n\)-dimensional good \(K\)-analytic space.  Assume that
there is a basis \(\mathscr B\) for its ordinary topology with the following
property: if \(Q\in\mathscr B\) and \(h\in C^0(Q,\mathbf R)\) satisfies
\[
                 \mathrm d'\mathrm d''[h]=0,
\]
then \(h\) is ordinary Chambert--Loir--Ducros smooth on \(Q\) and
\(\mathrm d'\mathrm d''h=0\) as a smooth form.

Then every positive \(\mathrm d'\)- and \(\mathrm d''\)-closed
Chambert--Loir--Ducros current \(T\) of bidegree \((\ell,\ell)\), where
\(0\leq\ell\leq n-1\), on \(X\) has continuous-pair
Bedford--Taylor independence: for every open \(W\subseteq X\) and every
\(u,v\in C^0(W,\mathbf R)\) such that
\[
                 \mathrm d'\mathrm d''[u]
                 =\mathrm d'\mathrm d''[v],
\]
one has
\[
 \mathrm d'\mathrm d''\bigl(uT|_W\bigr)
 =\mathrm d'\mathrm d''\bigl(vT|_W\bigr).
\]
In particular, the conclusion holds for continuous psh pairs \(u,v\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:p213-polycylinder-basis-candidate}
Let \(K\) be a complete non-Archimedean field with nontrivial absolute
value, and let \(X\) be a pure \(n\)-dimensional good \(K\)-analytic space that
has a basis of ordinary open subsets analytically isomorphic to standard
strict open polycylinders
\[
 \prod_{i=1}^{a}\{|T_i|<R_i\}
 \times_K
 \prod_{j=1}^{b}\{r_j<|S_j|<R'_j\},
\]
where
\[
 a+b=n,\qquad
 R_i,r_j,R'_j\in|K^\times|,
 \qquad 0<r_j<R'_j.
\]
Then every positive closed Chambert--Loir--Ducros current of bidegree
\((\ell,\ell)\), \(0\leq\ell\leq n-1\), on \(X\) has continuous-pair
Bedford--Taylor independence.

More locally, the same conclusion holds on an open set \(W\subseteq X\)
whenever every point of \(W\) has a neighbourhood in \(W\) analytically
isomorphic to one of the displayed polycylinders.  In particular, if \(P\)
itself is a standard strict open polycylinder and
\(u,v\in C^0(P,\mathbf R)\) have equal curvature currents, then the
Bedford--Taylor identity holds on \(P\) for every positive closed current
on \(P\).
\end{corollary}
