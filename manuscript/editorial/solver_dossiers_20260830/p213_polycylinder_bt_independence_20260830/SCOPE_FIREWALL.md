# Scope firewall

This candidate is a bridge from zero-Hessian regularity to the
continuous-pair Bedford--Taylor predicate.  It does **not** retire P21.3.

- The ground-field conclusion uses a nontrivial absolute value, exactly as
  in the verified P06.2 strict-open-polycylinder theorem.
- The geometric hypothesis is a *basis* of ordinary open sets analytically
  isomorphic to standard strict open polycylinders.  A smooth, quasi-smooth,
  toroidal, or log-etale analytic space is not asserted to have such a
  basis.
- An arbitrary ordinary open subset of a standard polycylinder need not be
  a standard polycylinder.  The final sentence proves the identity when the
  domain on which the pair is given is itself a standard polycylinder; it
  does not prove the full Bedford--Taylor predicate for every open subset of
  that polycylinder unless the stated basis hypothesis holds there.
- Closed and half-closed polycylinders are absent.  Their endpoint versions
  were explicitly removed from the accepted P06.2 v3 theorem.
- The potentials are continuous.  Nothing is claimed about the stronger
  literal predicate for discontinuous CLD-psh functions.
- Positivity is used to ensure that multiplication by a continuous function
  is defined for the order-zero current and to obtain symmetry.  No product
  with a general higher-order distribution is asserted.
- The result covers all bidegrees for which multiplication and two current
  differentials are defined; it makes no support or skeletal assumption on
  the current.
