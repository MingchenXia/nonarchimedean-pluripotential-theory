# Exact residual after the counterexample

## Closed negatively

The following proposed assertion is false:

> For every arbitrary-radius standard endpoint polycylinder `P`, every
> continuous `h` with `d'd''[h]=0` on `Int(P)` is affine in the logarithms
> of the annulus coordinates.

The reciprocal-radius bidisc in the principal statement disproves it.
Thus this broad assertion must be removed from the positive P06.2 queue,
not left as an unresolved conjecture.

## Closed positively under the exact additional hypothesis

If the pullback equation holds on the full absolute interior after one
complete strictifying extension, the strict v4 endpoint-polycylinder
theorem applies upstairs and surjectivity descends the affine formula.
This is the scalar-stable proposition in the dossier.

## What genuinely remains

The remaining research question is to characterize the arbitrary-radius
domains or radius tuples for which the base-field equation on `Int(P)`
forces the scalar-stable equation.  Berkovich Proposition 1.5.5(iv) gives
only

```text
p_L^{-1}(Int(P)) subset Int(P_L),
```

and the inclusion can be strict.  The counterexample realizes the missing
part as an open boundary-resonance tube after strictification.

A corrected future problem may therefore be posed in either of two exact
forms:

1. classify the radius tuples for which no relatively open
   boundary-resonance stratum occurs and prove (or disprove) current ascent
   for those tuples; or
2. adopt scalar-stable vanishing as the definition in the non-strict
   category, in which case the affine classification is already reduced to
   the strict v4 theorem.

No claim is made here that density of `Int(P)` alone suffices for current
ascent, and no one-dimensional arbitrary-radius theorem is imported.
