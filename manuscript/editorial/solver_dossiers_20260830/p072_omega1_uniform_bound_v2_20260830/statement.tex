Let \(X\) be an affine \(G\)-tropical space in the sense of
Chambert--Loir--Ducros.  Denote by
\(\operatorname{Psh}_{X,\alpha}\) the transfinite filtration generated from
the smooth functions \(v\) satisfying
\(\mathrm d'\mathrm d''v\geq0\), as in
\cite[Lemma~8.1.4]{CLD25}, and let \(\omega _1\) be the first uncountable
ordinal.  Then
\begin{equation}
 \label{eq:p072-omega-one-local-union}
 \operatorname{Psh}_{X,\omega _1}
 =
 \underset{\alpha<\omega _1}{\bigcup\nolimits^{\mathrm{loc}}}
 \operatorname{Psh}_{X,\alpha}.
\end{equation}
Here the right-hand side is the sheaf-theoretic local union: a section
belongs to it if and only if every point has a neighbourhood on which the
section belongs to \(\operatorname{Psh}_{X,\alpha}\) for some
\(\alpha<\omega _1\), allowed to depend on the neighbourhood.  Equivalently,
the stalk at every point is the ordinary union of the corresponding stalks.
Moreover,
\begin{equation}
 \label{eq:p072-omega-one-psh}
             \operatorname{Psh}_{X,\omega _1}
             =\operatorname{Psh}_X.
\end{equation}
The same conclusion holds for the continuous hierarchy:
\begin{equation}
 \label{eq:p072-omega-one-cpsh}
             \operatorname{CPsh}_{X,\omega _1}
             =\operatorname{CPsh}_X.
\end{equation}

More precisely, let \(U\subseteq X\) be open, let
\((u_n)_{n\geq1}\) be a decreasing sequence in
\(\operatorname{Psh}_{X,\omega _1}(U)\), with pointwise limit
\(u:U\to\mathbf R\cup\{-\infty\}\).
For every \(x\in U\), there exist an open neighbourhood \(V\) of \(x\) and
a countable ordinal \(\alpha<\omega _1\) such that
\begin{equation}
 \label{eq:p072-common-countable-rank}
       u_n|_V\in\operatorname{Psh}_{X,\alpha}(V)\quad(n\geq1),
       \qquad
       u|_V\in\operatorname{Psh}_{X,\alpha+1}(V).
\end{equation}
Consequently, \(\operatorname{Psh}_{X,\omega _1}\) is stable under the
decreasing-limit operation that generates \(\operatorname{Psh}_X\).

These assertions concern the CLD hierarchies just specified.  They do not
assert a finite or fixed countable rank bound, and they do not assert
\(\operatorname{Psh}_{X,1}=\operatorname{Psh}_X\).
