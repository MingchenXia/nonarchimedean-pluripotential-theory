1.5.5. Proposition. -- (i) If Y is an analytic domain in X, then Int(Y/X)
coincides with the topological interior of Y in X.

(ii) For a sequence of morphisms Z -> Y -> X, one has
Int(Z/Y) intersect psi^{-1}(Int(Y/X)) contained in Int(Z/X).
If psi is locally separated and good, then equality holds.

3.7.4. Corollary. -- A smooth morphism is an open map.
