\begin{proof}[Proof of
\cref{prop:p213-hessian-regular-basis-v2-candidate}]
Fix \(W,u,v\) as in the statement and put \(h=u-v\). A positive CLD
current is symmetric and of order zero in the stated analytic framework.
Thus multiplication of \(T|_W\) by the continuous functions \(u,v,h\) is
defined and linear, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p213-linear-reduction-v2}
 \mathrm d'\mathrm d''\bigl(u(T|_W)\bigr)
 -\mathrm d'\mathrm d''\bigl(v(T|_W)\bigr)
 =\mathrm d'\mathrm d''\bigl(h(T|_W)\bigr).
\end{equation}
The curvature hypothesis says that
\(\mathrm d'\mathrm d''[h]=0\) on \(W\).

Let \(x\in W\). Since \(\mathscr B\) is a basis, not merely an open cover,
there exists \(Q\in\mathscr B\) such that

\[
                           x\in Q\subseteq W.
\]
After restriction to \(Q\), \eqref{eq:p213-basis-zero-current-v2} holds.
The defining property of \(\mathscr B\) therefore makes \(h|_Q\) ordinary
CLD-smooth with zero smooth Hessian. For a smooth function and a symmetric
current closed under both differentials, the smooth-current Leibniz formula
gives

\[
 \mathrm d'\mathrm d''\bigl(h(T|_Q)\bigr)
 =\mathrm d'\mathrm d''h\wedge T|_Q=0.
\]
Every point of \(W\) has such a neighbourhood \(Q\). Since currents and
their differentials form sheaves, the right-hand side of
\eqref{eq:p213-linear-reduction-v2} vanishes on \(W\). This proves
\eqref{eq:p213-basis-bt-v2}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of
\cref{cor:p213-polycylinder-basis-v2-candidate}]
Let \(Q\) be one member of the asserted basis, and let
\(h\in C^0(Q,\mathbf R)\) satisfy
\(\mathrm d'\mathrm d''[h]=0\). Transporting \(h\) and its current equation
through the analytic isomorphism reduces the question to the standard
strict open polycylinder \eqref{eq:p213-standard-polycylinder-v2}. The
arbitrary-field strict-open-polycylinder result
\cref{thm:p062-arbitrary-field-polycylinder-candidate} gives unique real
constants \(c,\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_b\) such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p213-p062-form-v2}
                       h=c+\sum_{j=1}^{b}\lambda_j\log|S_j|.
\end{equation}
Each \(S_j\) is an analytic unit. Hence \(-\log|S_j|\) is an ordinary
CLD-smooth moment coordinate, and \eqref{eq:p213-p062-form-v2} is affine in
those coordinates. It is therefore ordinary CLD-smooth and has zero smooth
Hessian. The displayed basis satisfies the hypothesis of
\cref{prop:p213-hessian-regular-basis-v2-candidate}, which proves the
corollary for every open \(W\subseteq X\).
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of
\cref{prop:p213-fixed-polycylinder-v2-candidate}]
Put \(h=u-v\). The P06.2 theorem used above applies directly on the whole
domain \(P\), and \eqref{eq:p213-p062-form-v2} makes \(h\) ordinary
CLD-smooth with \(\mathrm d'\mathrm d''h=0\). Order-zero multiplication,
linearity, and the smooth-current Leibniz formula therefore give

\[
 \mathrm d'\mathrm d''(uT)-\mathrm d'\mathrm d''(vT)
 =\mathrm d'\mathrm d''(hT)
 =\mathrm d'\mathrm d''h\wedge T=0.
\]
This proof uses \(P\) itself as the P06.2 domain; it makes no refinement
claim for an arbitrary ordinary open subset of \(P\).
\end{proof}
