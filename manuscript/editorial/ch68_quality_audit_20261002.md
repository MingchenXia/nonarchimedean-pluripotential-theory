The repairs and checks include the following:

- Analytic entropy is half the ordinary relative entropy, as in BHJ19
  equation (1.10). The former full-entropy definition was incompatible
  with the chapter's Mabuchi variation and slope theorem. Ricci curvature,
  scalar curvature and mixed Ricci energy now have explicit formulas.
  Their constant shifts cancel the energy shift in Chen--Tian's formula.
  Ordinary entropy in Chapter 66 is explicitly twice this H. E extends
  by decreasing truncation, while J is defined by its formula and is not
  claimed monotone. Both remain continuous in the finite-energy topology.
- BDL's exact convexity interface is transferred through u=phi/(2pi) and
  K_BDL=2M_book. These positive changes preserve finite-energy geodesics,
  lower semicontinuity and convexity. The smooth first-variation argument
  is stated separately from this deep imported theorem.
- The normalized DF invariant is unchanged by simultaneous polarization
  powers. It is not homogeneous under normalized ramified base change.
  The new correction proposition writes C=sum(1-b_E)(E.L^n)/V, proves its
  nonpositive sign, and derives DF_d=dDF+dC-C_d from homogeneous M. The
  sufficiently divisible reduced-fibre assertion is precisely BHJ17
  Proposition 7.16. The valuation-function action is the Chapter 67
  dilation D_d phi(v)=d phi(v/d), not pointwise multiplication.
- Smooth positive-ray slopes and canonical weak-ray energy slopes retain
  their separate hypotheses. The weak Mabuchi liminf statement assumes
  finite real energy and Ricci-energy slopes, allowing infinite entropy
  without subtracting infinities. The exact published erratum preserves
  Sections 1--4; the orbit-closure/coercivity results affected by its gap
  remain quarantined.
- Divisorial measures are probabilities and use reduced entropy, excluding
  the total-space discrepancy shift. The class derivative fixes the
  measure. The Chapter 62 normalized model criterion is kept distinct from
  the stronger all-big-model raw inequality. Finite weighted sums of
  divisorial Dirac masses, rather than only individual divisors, are tested.
- The minimizer proof uses its stated compactness, lower semicontinuity
  and regularity assumptions, then applies smooth first variation. It no
  longer invokes an unstated weak Euler--Lagrange theorem. The Fano Ding
  converse now records BBJ's model approximants and energy inequality,
  rather than assuming equality between an arbitrary ray's energy and its
  non-Archimedean energy. The contradiction c>=(1-epsilon)c for c<0 is
  written out. Deep compactness, regularization and uniqueness remain
  exact source imports with finite-automorphism scope.
- The Fano P1 divisor example computes beta=0 and truncated j=2. Extending
  j past its threshold adds an unbounded tail. The product P1 example
  computes finite monomial weights, J=2s coth(2s)-1, half-entropy=J,
  Ricci energy=-J and M=0. This directly exposes the old entropy error.
  E/J use denominator 2s, whereas entropy, Ricci energy and M use s.
- The normal quotient of P1 times A1 by diagonal mu_m supplies an actual
  nonreduced test configuration. Line-bundle descent, generic fibre,
  central multiplicity and component degree are checked. Its corrected
  M=0, ordinary J=1/2 and DF=(m-1)/m exhibit the failure of DF homogeneity.
  Normalized degree-m base change removes the multiplicity and has DF=0.
  A reduced component remains reduced under normalized ramified base change.
- The model-stability existence chain keeps all-big-model testing and its
  exact finite-energy completion interface. The two 2026 preprints remain
  dated orientation, with no full dependency verification claimed. Trusiani
  Corollary A is identified as entropy regularization; the following source
  discussion derives entropy-slope equality, and Corollary B records the
  stated stability equivalences. No unrestricted compact-Kaehler YTD
  theorem or canonical termwise approximation identity is inferred.
