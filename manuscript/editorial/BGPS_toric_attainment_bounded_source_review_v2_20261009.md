## Separate-place versus simultaneous attainment

Proposition 5.13's literal condition uses **ground-field** T(K), with
u_v in val_v(T(K)) tensor Q separately at every place. Its proof claims
commensurability of a product of independently selected valuation groups
with the image of one simultaneous S-unit valuation map. This is a
substantive unresolved input in the source audit, not cleared by a
reference to Dirichlet's unit theorem.

A complete **adversarial research candidate** tests that converse over
K=Q(sqrt(2)), with P1 and the degree-one translated canonical metric.
Set u_+=-log 2, u_-=log 2, and finite coordinates zero, with real weights
1/2. The roof is zero and its interior sup-differentials meet the exact
monocritical criterion. The separate-place condition has witnesses 2
and 1/2. The direct height formula is nonnegative and would force a
height-zero torus point alpha to have modulus 2 at every conjugate over
the first real place and 1/2 over the second. Its field norm q in K would
then have moduli 2^d and 2^-d. The first forces q=±2^d in K, contradicting
the second. Roots of powers of the unit 1+sqrt(2) have heights tending
to zero, so the candidate separates essential minimum zero from torus
point attainment. Boundary point zero is excluded from the target
special-point notion and is not claimed to have positive height.
