## Primary source

Walter Gubler and Joseph Rabinoff, *Classically psh and pluriharmonic
functions on Berkovich spaces*, arXiv:2506.13548v2.

- Proposition 4.2(3) states that finite maxima of classically psh
  functions are classically psh.
- Theorem 6.5 states that a net of classically psh functions converging
  pointwise to an upper-semicontinuous function has a classically psh
  limit.  The statement explicitly uses nets.
