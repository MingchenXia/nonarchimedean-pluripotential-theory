## Claim separation

Only forward pullback is taken from the cited source.  The source is not
cited for descent, reflection, openness, existence of analytic sections,
extension of local competitors, or local-to-global cofinality.  The reverse
local inequality is instead proved directly by restriction along the given
analytic section.
