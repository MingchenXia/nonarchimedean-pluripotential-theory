## Gubler--Rabinoff

Walter Gubler and Joseph Rabinoff, *Classically psh and pluriharmonic
functions on Berkovich spaces*, arXiv:2506.13548v2.

- Section 2.9 states that analytic scalar projection is continuous,
  topologically closed, and surjective.
- The repository-pinned v2 PDF has SHA-256
  `83ca9a19394f44bb2e4e1aa316de95938f311acd53a00fe250fa0f605184b726`.

The scalar-fiber identification with
`M(H(x) completed-tensor K')` and compactness of Berkovich spectra are
proved in Chapter 4 of this book and are cited by their live labels.

## Poineau--Turchetti

Jérôme Poineau and Daniele Turchetti, *Berkovich Curves and Schottky
Uniformization. I: The Berkovich Affine Line*, Lecture Notes in Mathematics
2275 (2021).

- Proposition 5.6.1 gives continuity, properness, and surjectivity of
  scalar projection on the analytic affine line.
- Lemma 5.6.2 identifies the scalar fiber of the stated type-4 point with a
  positive-radius closed disc.
- Remark 5.6.4 records the resulting failure of openness.

These are Proposition I.5.1, Lemma I.5.2, and Remark I.5.4 in the combined
arXiv preprint 2010.09043.  The inspected preprint PDF has SHA-256
`bd0cb1701b829b531d0b90cab8da661ca5f2cba8d1499a1252f477174d0d34f0`.

## Self-contained steps

The compact-fiber maximum formula, the open-map characterization, the
fiberwise-constancy criterion, and the bounded binary defect construction
are proved directly in the packet.  No novelty or priority claim is made.
