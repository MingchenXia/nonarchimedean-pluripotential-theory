## Claim separation

Only the forward pullback statements are used for the new theorem.  The
source is not cited for descent through a finite morphism, existence of
analytic sections, dominance by a repaired competitor away from one fiber,
or regularity of the raw envelope.  The nonclosure example is inherited from
the already integrated v40 result.
