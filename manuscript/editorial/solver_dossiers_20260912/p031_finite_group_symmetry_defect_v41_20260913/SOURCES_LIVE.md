## Claim separation

The source supplies the real-PL cone property, PL reflection,
semipositivity reflection, and the inclusion of LFS functions in the
semipositive real-PL class.  It is not cited for dominance by averaging,
vanishing or regularity of the symmetry defect, a scalar-extension orbit
presentation, or LFS descent.
