## Exact support used

- Definition 7.8 defines locally Fubini--Study (LFS) functions.
- Section 7.9 characterizes the local Fubini--Study condition by
  nonnegative real linear combinations of finite maxima of negative
  logarithms of invertible analytic functions.
- The discussion on the following printed page explicitly applies Section
  7.9 to conclude that the maximum of two real-PL LFS functions is again
  real-PL LFS.

These passages justify both operations used in the proof: transport of the
local presentation through an analytic isomorphism, and closure of the full
real-coefficient LFS class under finite maxima.  No quarantined v35 or v38
result is used in the live proof.
