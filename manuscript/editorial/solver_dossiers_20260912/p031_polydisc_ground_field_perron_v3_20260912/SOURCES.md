## Gubler--Rabinoff

Walter Gubler and Joseph Rabinoff, *Classically psh and pluriharmonic
functions on Berkovich spaces*, arXiv:2506.13548v2.

- Definition 4.1 and Section 4.1.3 give the scalar-stable curve-test
  definition and the reduction to smooth connected strict curves.
- Proposition 3.5(3) extends subharmonicity from the relative interior of an
  analytic curve across its relative boundary.
- Section 3.1(3) identifies the relative boundary of an affinoid analytic
  curve with its finite Shilov boundary.
- Proposition 4.2 gives the logarithm, cone, maximum, scalar-extension, and
  pullback calculus for classically psh functions.
- Theorem 4.3 identifies semipositive real-PL functions with classically psh
  functions on good strict spaces over a nontrivially valued field.
- Theorem 6.4 is the global affinoid maximum principle.
- Proposition 7.2(7) preserves pluriharmonicity under analytic pullback.

## Ducros

Antoine Ducros, *Families of Berkovich Spaces*, Astérisque 400 (2018),
Corollary 6.2.5: every quasi-smooth boundaryless morphism of Berkovich
analytic spaces is open.  The proof uses this only on an ordinary open
neighbourhood of an interior zero-section point.  The inspected arXiv v6
PDF has SHA-256
`edaa82af3b6d4ebc150de804908a4d1c78ae44f983055d87bbab0834c73e4049`.

## Poineau--Turchetti

Jérôme Poineau and Daniele Turchetti, *Berkovich Curves and Schottky
Uniformization. I: The Berkovich Affine Line*, Lecture Notes in Mathematics
2275 (2021), Lemma 5.6.2 and Remark 5.6.4.  Over the stated algebraically
closed non-spherically-complete base and maximally complete extension, the
fiber above a type-4 point is a positive-radius closed disc; consequently
the scalar projection is not open in general.  These are Lemma I.5.2 and
Remark I.5.4 in the combined arXiv preprint 2010.09043.  The inspected
preprint PDF has SHA-256
`bd0cb1701b829b531d0b90cab8da661ca5f2cba8d1499a1252f477174d0d34f0`.

## Self-contained steps

The open-pullback lemma for usc regularization, uniform graph-tube
existence, the relative-Gauss boundary calculation, and convergence of the
relative Gauss points to the zero section are proved directly in the
packet.  No novelty or priority claim is made.
