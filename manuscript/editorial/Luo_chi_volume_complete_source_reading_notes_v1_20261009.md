## View 1

Read the complete selected text and displayed matching rendered image for physical pages 1–2. The title and arXiv margin pin Wenbin Luo, 2303.01604v1, 2 March 2023, rather than the later 2025 journal edition. The scope announced is a Hilbert–Samuel formula for semiample semipositive adelic line bundles, birational invariance of arithmetic chi-volume, and continuous extension on the semiample cone. The base is a proper adelic curve; section-space norms use geometrically reduced projective schemes and require either a discrete sigma-algebra or one countable subfield dense in every completion. Dominance, measurability and the adelic vector-bundle assertion are imported from Chen–Moriwaki; they are not recursively proved by reading this introduction.
