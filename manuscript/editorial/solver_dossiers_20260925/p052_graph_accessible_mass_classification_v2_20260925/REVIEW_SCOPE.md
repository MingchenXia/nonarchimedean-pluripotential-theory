# P05.2 graph-accessibility review scope

The principal target is the exact statement and proof in this directory.
Its dependency closure consists of the real-polyhedral definitions,
one-vertex slope lemma and classification, and the local rank-one graph
theorem in the two preceding P05.2 dossiers.

Review must check:

1. the slow ambient rational-polyhedral norm makes every vertex accessible
   without changing the reference growth;
2. the boundary projection in the local dual cone preserves every
   nonnegative-cycle inequality and retains strictly positive distinguished
   mass;
3. the balanced barycentre built from the supporting relation pins every
   envelope competitor at the chosen vertex;
4. equality of the envelope and the constructed competitor at that vertex
   gives the required one-sided slope comparison and positive envelope mass;
5. every higher-rank vertex therefore yields a counterexample, while the
   existing local rank-one perturbation proves the converse direction.

The result completely classifies the repaired real-piecewise-affine
one-dimensional graph problem.  It does not repair the literal
integral-slope BMPS convention and makes no higher-dimensional claim.
Model-backed review is not formal verification, peer review, or a claim of
literature priority.
