Review the exact statement and proof in `STATEMENT_LIVE.tex` and
`PROOF_LIVE.tex`.

The intended claim is a conditional reduction for P06.2, not a solution of
the remaining atom.  Check especially:

1. whether zero Hessian for `h_L` gives nonnegative Hessian currents for
   both signs;
2. whether membership of both signs in the CLD-psh hierarchy implies
   classical pluriharmonicity;
3. whether Proposition 7.2(6) of `GR25Classical` gives descent from `L` to
   `K` for an arbitrary analytic extension;
4. whether the contrapositive corollary states an exhaustive alternative;
5. whether local real-PL regularity follows from the supplied P06.1 theorem,
   and whether the separate real-PL positive-current comparison gives GJR
   harmonicity under the added pure-dimensional hypothesis.

Do not infer automatic scalar pullback of currents or a general
positive-current regularization theorem.
