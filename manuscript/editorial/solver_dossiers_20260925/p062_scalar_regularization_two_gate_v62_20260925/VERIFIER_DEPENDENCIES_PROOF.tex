\begin{proof}[\Cref{thm:cld-to-classical-psh-verifier-extract}]
CLD-psh functions are upper semicontinuous and stable under analytic
pullback and scalar extension.  On a smooth analytic curve, a smooth
CLD-psh function is subharmonic.  Subharmonic functions are stable under
decreasing limits, so transfinite induction through the CLD definition
gives the all-extensions curve test for classical psh functions.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Scalar pluriharmonic descent extract]
Classical pluriharmonicity means that both signs are classically psh.
Classical psh is defined by testing every analytic scalar extension and
every analytic curve over that extension.  Hence the property is unchanged
after replacing the ground field by one analytic extension; this is the
content of Proposition~7.2(6) of the cited source.
\end{proof}
