# P03.1 isolated scalar-defect nonremovability: audit

Date: 25 September 2026.

## Mathematical scope

The packet retains the notation of
`thm:p031-curvewise-scalar-defect-localization`.  It proves that an isolated
point of the strict canonical-retraction defect locus is a strict local
maximum of the retracted curve function, and therefore an actual failure of
subharmonicity.  Combining this inclusion with the earlier localization
`F_g \subseteq closure(B_g)` gives equality when the defect locus is closed
and discrete; local finiteness has the same consequence because the test
curve is Hausdorff.

The proof uses only upper semicontinuity of the subharmonic comparison
function, equality of the two functions off the strict-defect locus, and the
one-dimensional maximum principle.  The cases `h_g(c)=-infinity` and finite
`q_g(c)` are handled at the choice of the separating real number.  The
corollary then applies the all-extension curve-test definition of classical
plurisubharmonicity.

## Boundary of the conclusion

This is a nonremovability theorem.  It neither produces an isolated defect
nor proves that every defect locus is locally finite.  A nonempty defect
locus without isolated points is not excluded.  Consequently P03.1--P03.3
remain open and the active inventory is unchanged.

No model-verifier result is claimed for this packet.
