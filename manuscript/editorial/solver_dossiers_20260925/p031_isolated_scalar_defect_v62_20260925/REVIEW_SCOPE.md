# Review scope: P03.1 isolated scalar-defect nonremovability

Review the exact files `STATEMENT_LIVE.tex` and `PROOF_LIVE.tex` together
with the immediately preceding live theorem
`thm:p031-curvewise-scalar-defect-localization`.

The critical checks are:

1. the extended-real choice of `alpha` when `h_g(c)=-infinity`;
2. the use of upper semicontinuity only for `h_g`, not for a difference of
   upper-semicontinuous functions;
3. equality `q_g=h_g` on the punctured neighborhood from
   `h_g <= q_g` and the definition of `B_g`;
4. applicability of Thuillier's local maximum principle;
5. the two inclusions giving `F_g=B_g` only when `B_g` is closed and
   discrete;
6. the final classical-psh contrapositive through the all-extension curve
   test.

Do not infer existence, discreteness, polarity, or vanishing of the scalar
defect locus, and do not retire a P03 atom.
