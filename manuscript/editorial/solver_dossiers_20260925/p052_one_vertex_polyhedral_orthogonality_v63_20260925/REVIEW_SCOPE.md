# Review scope

The principal claim is the exact one-vertex classification in the real scalar
extension of the piecewise-affine class.  The original supplied draft
contained the raywise-affine argument, but its replacement withdraws the
positive theorem.  The live theorem is therefore book-local.  Verification
exposed the same convention conflict: BMPS Definition 2.28 literally
requires integral slopes, while Proposition 6.13 and the intervening
convex-vector-space arguments use real affine functions.  The live statement
uses `PA_R`, makes no claim to settle the literal formulation, and adds the
displayed bounded-reference-shift corollary.

The review must check:

1. the integral/real PA convention against BMPS Definition 2.28 and
   Proposition 6.13;
2. the local polyhedral-convexity criterion against BMPS Definitions 3.2--3.3;
3. the one-dimensional real-linear Laplacian formula against BMPS Definition 6.15 and
   Proposition 6.17;
4. both envelope perturbations in the rank-one case, including every slope
   inequality at the cut points;
5. the proper-support extreme relation and the exact envelope computation in
   the higher-rank counterexample;
6. the support and sign of the final Laplacian integral;
7. the bounded-reference-shift deduction and the claim that no literal
   integral-slope, general-graph or higher-dimensional P05.2 classification
   follows.

Repair of the source's integral-slope category, the higher-dimensional
classification, graphs with bounded edges, and any claim of authorship or
priority are outside the review scope.
