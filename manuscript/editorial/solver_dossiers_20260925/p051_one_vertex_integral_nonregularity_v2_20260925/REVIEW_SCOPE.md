# Review scope

Review the following claims.

1. The displayed obstacle is bounded, continuous, and literal
   integral-slope piecewise affine.
2. The candidate envelope is polyhedrally convex at the unique vertex and
   raywise convex at `t=N`.
3. The endpoint-chord argument bounds every admissible competitor.
4. The integral one-sided derivative at `t=1` gives the stated uniform
   separation in both exhaustive cases.
5. Bounded reference shift applies to the eventual raywise-affine reference
   without changing coefficient categories.
6. The conclusion about literal orthogonality uses only the explicit
   regularity hypothesis in BMPS Definition 6.9.

Do not read this packet as a theorem about the real-scalar category or about
higher-dimensional balanced polyhedral spaces.
