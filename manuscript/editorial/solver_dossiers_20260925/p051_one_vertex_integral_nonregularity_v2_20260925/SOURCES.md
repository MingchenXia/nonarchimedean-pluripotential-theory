# P05.1 one-vertex integral-slope nonregularity: sources

## Primary source

Botero--Mazzon--Pille-Schneider, *Monge--Ampère measures on balanced
polyhedral spaces*, arXiv:2603.08664v1.

- Definition 2.28: literal piecewise-affine functions have integral linear
  parts and rational constants.
- Theorem 6.4 and Definition 6.5: equivalent regularity conditions and the
  definition of regularity.
- Definition 6.9: orthogonality is defined only after assuming regularity.

The exact source text was checked in the locally preserved PDF and text
extraction.  The fractional-envelope formula and quantitative separation
estimate are proved directly in this packet.

## Internal dependencies

- `prop:frontier-polyhedral-bounded-reference-shift` transfers regularity
  between references differing by a bounded literal piecewise-affine
  function.
- `prop:frontier-polyhedral-integral-regularity-counterexample` is the
  special case of the present construction on the balanced affine line with
  `N=2`.

## Scope

The packet proves literal nonregularity for every positive-at-infinity
integral-slope reference on a one-vertex balanced rational fan.  It does not
alter the real-scalar regularity or orthogonality theorems and makes no
higher-dimensional assertion.
