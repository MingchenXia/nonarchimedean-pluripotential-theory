# P05.1 one-dimensional regularity repair: sources

## Primary source

Botero--Mazzon--Pille-Schneider, *Monge--Ampère measures on balanced
polyhedral spaces*, arXiv:2603.08664v1.

- Definition 2.28: literal piecewise-affine functions have integral linear
  parts and rational constants.
- Definition 2.29: the global literal PA class is obtained by rational
  polyhedral refinement.
- Proposition 6.3: elementary envelope properties used to start the proof.
- Theorem 6.4 and Definition 6.5: equivalent regularity conditions and the
  definition of the regularity property.
- Proposition 6.13: the one-dimensional assertion whose proof uses affine
  replacement; the present theorem records its valid real-slope form.

The real-line counterexample and the `1/4` separation estimate are proved
directly in the packet.  No external result is used for that estimate.

## Supplied withdrawal

The replacement author source *Withdrawn draft: Orthogonality on
one-dimensional balanced fans*, SHA-256
`0ad51074108eb2202d4b624637c035610d275c67fd8456dab59282d543d5f5fb`,
withdraws its positive theorem for exactly the integral/real coefficient
conflict above.  It is preserved as provenance and is not cited as support
for the real-slope theorem proved in this packet.

## Boundary

The packet does not alter the source PDF or claim that the source has adopted
the convention repair.  It records the incompatibility between the literal
Definition 2.28 and Proposition 6.13, then states and proves the real-slope
repair needed by the book's one-dimensional orthogonality results.
