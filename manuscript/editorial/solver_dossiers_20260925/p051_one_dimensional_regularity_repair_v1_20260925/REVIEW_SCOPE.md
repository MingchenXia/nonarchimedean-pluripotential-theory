# P05.1 one-dimensional regularity repair: review scope

The principal target is the exact statement and proof in this directory.
Review must distinguish the literal integral-slope class of BMPS Definition
2.28 from its real scalar extension.

The review must check:

1. the edgewise affine majorant lies below the affine obstacle on every
   bounded edge;
2. bounded convexity of `P-gamma` on an unbounded edge makes it nonincreasing,
   so the parallel affine replacement lies above the envelope and below the
   obstacle;
3. replacing every edge simultaneously preserves all polyhedral Jensen
   inequalities, including those centred at a vertex;
4. envelope maximality forces equality with the edgewise replacement;
5. the real-line formula is the exact envelope;
6. the integral left slope at `1` gives a uniform-distance lower bound of
   `1/4` from every literal integral-slope convex PA function;
7. this lower bound excludes membership in the literal regularizable class,
   not merely membership in the literal PA class.

The result proves the complete one-dimensional real-slope regularity range
and gives a literal source-convention counterexample.  It makes no
higher-dimensional classification claim.  Model-backed review is not formal
verification, peer review, or a novelty certificate.
