## Mathematical boundary

The packet separates two conclusions.  In the real scalar extension, every
one-dimensional finite balanced rational polyhedral space has regularity for
every real-piecewise-affine reference positive at infinity.  In the literal
integral-slope category, the affine line with reference `|x|` already fails
regularity: one envelope has slope `1/2` and remains at uniform distance at
least `1/4` from every integral-slope convex PA function.

The proof repairs the edgewise affine replacement in the source by treating
bounded and unbounded edges separately and by checking vertex-centred Jensen
inequalities after simultaneous replacement.  It does not address
higher-dimensional regularity.  P05.1 therefore remains active.
