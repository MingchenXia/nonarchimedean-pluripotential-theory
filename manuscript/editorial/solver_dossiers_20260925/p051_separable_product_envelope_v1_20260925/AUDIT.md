# P05.1 separable product envelope: audit

## Mathematical boundary

The packet proves exact factorization of the envelope for a finite sum of
factorwise obstacles.  It converts the one-dimensional finite-graph and
complete-affine real-PA regularity theorems into a higher-dimensional family
that permits branched graph factors.  It does not approximate or dominate an
arbitrary nonseparable obstacle by separable ones and therefore does not
settle the full product regularity problem or retire P05.1.
