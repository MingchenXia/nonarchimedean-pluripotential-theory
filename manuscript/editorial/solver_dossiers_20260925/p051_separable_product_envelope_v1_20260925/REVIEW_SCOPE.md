# P05.1 separable product envelope: review scope

The principal target is the exact statement and proof in this directory.
The result concerns only separable obstacles on finite products.

Review must check:

1. a product polyhedral convex combination projects to one in every factor;
2. pullback and finite sums therefore preserve polyhedral convexity;
3. a slice at rational fixed coordinates is polyhedrally convex in the
   remaining factor;
4. after subtracting the other factor values, the slice has the precise
   obstacle and upper-growth bounds required by the factor envelope;
5. the iterative replacement of each obstacle term by its factor envelope
   proves the upper bound;
6. density of rational points and continuity extend that upper bound from
   rational product points to the whole product;
7. the corollary invokes only the proved one-dimensional and complete-affine
   real-PA theorems;
8. no claim is made for nonseparable obstacles or for the full regularity
   property of a product pair.

No model-verifier result is claimed for this packet.  Any later review must
remain confined to the exact bytes it checks.
