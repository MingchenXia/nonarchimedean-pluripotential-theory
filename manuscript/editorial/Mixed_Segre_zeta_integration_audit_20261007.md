## Mathematical change and controlling sources

**C.9.2** defines the pushed stabilized mixed Segre series using the joint
blowup of a product of nonzero proper homogeneous ideals. Its positive-degree
generator lists, formal Cartier intersections and stabilization assumptions
are explicit. The ordinary zeta function of the product requires multinomial
weights; the raw mixed diagonal is different. The unnamed imported theorem
combines rationality, integral-closure invariance and denormalized Lorentzian
positivity of the chosen modified numerator `Q=D−P`, with algebraic closure
required for the last assertion. Denominator factors are candidate poles
that can cancel; invariance is not a converse for arbitrary mixed sequences.
The tautological quotient sign is `O_B(−e_i)=O_B(E_i)`. Source Example 7.1
and Example 7.6 are worked explicitly, retaining their disjoint-variable
complete-intersection scope. Neither is a general formula for pairs of ideals.

**D.8** introduces multi-index factorial normalization, the simultaneous
exchange definition of M-convex support, the quadratic-derivative signature
characterization and denormalized Lorentzian polynomials. Low degree and
zero conventions are explicit. The imported nef-volume statement uses the
published Brändén–Huh Theorem **4.6**, not the literal main-preprint citation
4.16. It requires an irreducible projective variety over an algebraically
closed field. The latter published number is a Corollary about M-matrix
support. No explanation based on an uninspected earlier edition is invented,
and the locator defect is not called a disproof of the mixed theorem.
The termwise homogenization identity includes the `t_0` factorial. It does
not assert that normalization commutes with all nonnegative coordinate
changes. The raw quadratic toy example and Aluffi's existing covolume
example distinguish direct normalization from exponent reversal and from
volume-polynomial coefficients.

**F.9.1** gives the equal-domain/target-dimension specialization of the
source's globally generated Chern-cycle result. Positive kernel rank,
irreducible incidence variety, regular zero section, proper cycle pushforward
and the exact derivative factor `e/d!` are retained. If the image has smaller
dimension, the pushforward and coefficient polynomial are zero; a nonzero
degree-`d` volume is not assigned to that smaller image. This is ordinary
algebraic intersection theory, with no extension to individual curvature
forms, arbitrary Weil b-divisor products or the missing big-nef numerical
converse. The existing ample boundary is unchanged.

**L.78** records the exact editions, actual primary-page selection,
attribution, locator correction and uncleared dependency boundaries. The
controlling main source is the 29-page `2507.06424v2`, submitted 10 July
2026 at 20:56:10 UTC and internally dated 14 July. The 71-page published
Brändén–Huh article is used for its checked locators. Aluffi's controlling
preprints are 29-page `2304.02043v4` of 17 December 2023 and 19-page
`1606.03098v2` of 27 July 2016. Their unread journal editions are not
authenticated by preprint numbering.

Aluffi's conjecture concerns one multihomogeneous ideal on a product of
projective spaces; the mixed theorem concerns several homogeneous ideals
on one projective space. The source-era conjecture is not declared solved
by the latter theorem or certified as currently open. The local
componentwise Segre/generic-Lê finite-pullback frontier stays separate.
