## Repeated components and the descent reduction

The [nonreduced/descent derivation](research_dossiers/li_nonreduced_obstruction_and_ramified_descent_20261004.md)
distinguishes the whole angular fibre's character lattice
\(\mathbb Zb\) from a single component's \(\mathbb Z(b/\gcd b)\).
The whole-fibre Poincaré inequality fails on piecewise constants.
Per-component Poincaré cannot supply the full Fourier estimate without
an additional potential comparison across components. This rules out
extending the reduced analytic proof merely by changing constants.
The two-sign leading-mode example is local/formal, not a globally
realized independent section basis in a geometrically integral CY family.

For \(K=\mathbb C((t))\subset K'=\mathbb C((\tau))\),
\(t=\tau^q\), the trace proof works with the extended absolute value
\(|\tau|=e^{-1/q}\). Average an FS metric over the cyclic group using
all tensor products, a finite family permuted with its weights.
The coefficients
\(q^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}(\tau^{-j}P)\), \(0\leq j<q\), are
sections defined over \(K\). The coefficient decomposition and the
ultrametric trace inequality give both directions of an exact maximum
identity after multiplying the coefficients by \(|\tau|^j\).
Global generation descends from that identity and faithful field base
change. Uniform approximation then gives continuous psh descent.
All log weights, tensor degrees and the unit \(|q|=1\) are explicit.

This supplies the positivity step for the conditional ramified route
without assuming arbitrary model-function descent. The book's Chapter
30 retains its general conditional model-descent statements; the new
reduction is limited to continuous psh metrics on an ample line bundle
in this cyclic characteristic-zero extension. It is not an unbounded,
mixed-characteristic or wild descent claim.

On a supplied regular reduced SNC cover, the earlier conditional
analytic/FS route gives the continuous hybrid limit. The source scaling
is \(1/T_\tau\), with \(T_t=qT_\tau\); the original potential is
the child potential divided by \(q\). The central valuation map
also divides the restriction by \(q\). Model/FS tests prove
continuity of the hybrid map, and compact Hausdorff quotient topology
descends the invariant full-family limit. Invariance follows from
continuity and density of the punctured part, without a first-countable
or NA sequential-compactness assumption. Both the reference and the
potential are renormalized before applying trace descent.

BJ Theorem 6.7 gives \(q^m\) for the unnormalized skeletal volume
pushforward; the factor cancels in probability measures. Its
Lemma 6.8 keeps the squared residue Jacobian. The independent volume
limit and the prior MA passage identify the descended equation.
No extra \(q^n\) is inserted in its probability normalization.
