\chapter*{Mathematical revision log}

This log is not included in ordinary printings.  It records material changes
made during mathematical audit.

\begin{enumerate}
\item The CLD part was reorganized from concrete examples to invariant
  constructions; the source's order was not followed.
\item The envelope conjecture in the earlier complex manuscript was replaced
  by the theorem proved in the supplied unibranch manuscript.
\item The trace provenance was re-audited against the supplied full trace
  chapter and \cite{DX24}.  On its natural domain, trace depends only on the
  input \(\mathcal I\)-class, but every output is \(\mathcal I\)-good and
  two choices are \(P\)-equivalent.  Thus the sourced map is
  \[
   D_Y/{\sim_{\mathcal I}}
   \longrightarrow
   \QPSH(\widetilde Y)/{\sim_P},
  \]
  and the earlier claim that the target was only an
  \(\mathcal I\)-class was removed.  The formerly listed open
  ``\(P\)-valued refinement'' is already solved by the source theorem.  In
  the split-product construction one has
  \(\operatorname{Tr}(T)\sim_P P_\alpha[S]_{\mathcal I}
  \sim_{\mathcal I}S\); if \(S\) is \(\mathcal I\)-good, then also
  \(\operatorname{Tr}(T)\sim_P S\).
\item The unrestricted Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff question whether the canonical
  injection
  \(\Lambda^{p,q}M_{\mathbf R}\to H^{p,q}(A)\) is always an isomorphism was
  removed from the open-problem list.  For the constant good-reduction
  elliptic curve over \(\widehat{\overline{\mathbf C((t))}}\), the Raynaud
  torus is trivial, hence the source in bidegree \((1,1)\) is zero, whereas
  GJR Theorem~1.14 gives a one-dimensional target.  The complete displayed
  proof passed exact-current production run
  \texttt{20260824T123617Z\_fcddc3abc33d} with final \texttt{correct}, zero
  critical errors, and zero gaps.  The totally degenerate restriction is not
  inferred.
\item The natural positive-cone version of the GJR semipositive
  integral-decomposition question is proved whenever the residual dimension
  is at most one.  On one strict analytic neighbourhood, a semipositive
  \(\Lambda\)-PL germ is a finite \(\Lambda_{\geq0}\)-linear combination of
  integral semipositive PL functions, all but at most one harmonic, for
  \(\Lambda=\mathbf Z\) or a divisible additive subgroup of \(\mathbf R\).
  Boundary points are allowed, and no algebraic-closedness or value-group
  compatibility is assumed.  The proof uses the common residue model and an
  integral splitting of the single degree functional.  The exact current
  theorem and proof passed production run
  \texttt{20260824T135721Z\_81346238f7dd} with final \texttt{correct}, zero
  critical errors, and zero gaps.  Arbitrary residual dimension remains open,
  and no novelty or priority claim is made.

  the relative Hodge-index form separates the trace on every model; hence
  equality against all tame weights forces pointwise equality of valuative
  transforms.  The exact frozen statement and source-aware proof passed
  production run \texttt{20260824T140127Z\_ef8316d19c12} with final
  \texttt{correct}, zero critical errors, and zero gaps.  An independent
  hostile audit rechecked the source locators, one-Weil-input domain,
  polarization, compactification, intersection sign, and extension to all
  normalized valuations before integration in Chapter~70.  This was not a
  post-integration byte-identity gate, and no novelty or priority claim is
  made.  Singular and nonnormal germs remain outside the theorem.

  dimension statement was rejected because it did not identify the book's
  mixed mass with Cao's cohomological definition.  The replacement proof uses
  the asymptotics of \(\vol(K+\varepsilon Q)\) in Cao's criterion.  A

  Proposition~4.4, on physical page~19 of the archived arXiv PDF.  A contrary

  integral or rational big toric classes; no irrational-real extension or
