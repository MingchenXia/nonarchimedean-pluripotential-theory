## Conditional geometric consequence

The input is an integral flat projective scheme/germ model over
C{t}, a relatively ample A0 with fixed meromorphic generic
identification L^k0, a finite cover t=tau^q, finite normalization,
and finitely many normalized blowups of nonzero coherent vertical
ideals. The final smooth model and its reduced SNC fibre are supplied.
The analytic realization, finite normalization and coherent/projective
comparison are explicit imports.

Finite morphisms are affine and hence quasi-affine. The actual
Stacks Lemma 29.38.7(2), Tag 0892, preserves ampleness under their
pullback; restriction to affine base opens gives relative ampleness.
The same argument applies to the pulled-back original polarization
and to the tautological ample ideal of each blowup. The ideal becomes
an invertible vertical ideal on the normalized blowup. Its divisor
need not be exceptional: an invertible vertical ideal has identity
blowup and can still have a nonzero Cartier divisor. The zero ideal
is excluded.

Stacks Lemma 29.38.8, Tag 0C4K, composes the relative ample bundles
with sufficiently large integer powers on quasi-compact bases. The
resulting vertical bundle, twisted by a power of A0, is relatively
ample on the supplied final model and restricts to L'^{ak0} with
the fixed generic identification. Taking a very ample power and its
root FS metric gives a rational positive reference. The pair of
projective embedding and parameter map is an immersion, so adding
the base form to half its curvature is Kähler on the total space,
including at critical points of the parameter map.

No extra blowup is required after the final model to obtain this
polarization; its supplied reduced central fibre is retained. Powers
depend on the finite presentation. The meromorphic comparison with
the supplied reference has a vertical divisor and O(T_tau) potential,
with the full continuous hybrid model limit. A bare O(T) bound would
not justify hybrid reference change. The previously written analytic
route applies conditionally in either essential dimension zero or
positive dimension. Ramified descent still rescales the entire log
metric by q, rather than only the potential.
