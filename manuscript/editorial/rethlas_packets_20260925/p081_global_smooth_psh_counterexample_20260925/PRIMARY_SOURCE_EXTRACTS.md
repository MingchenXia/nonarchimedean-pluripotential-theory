# Primary-source locators and exact dependency claims

The verifier should use the following narrowly stated source inputs. They
were checked directly in Antoine Chambert-Loir and Antoine Ducros,
*Formes différentielles réelles et courants sur les espaces de Berkovich*,
revision dated 25 July 2025.

- Definition 8.1.7 defines continuous psh functions through local uniform
  approximation by smooth psh functions; Propositions 8.1.9 and 8.1.11 give
  the uniform-closure/locality statements used in the final step. Lemma
  8.1.10 permits a decreasing subsequence after adding constants tending to
  zero, although the proof packet needs only uniform closure.
- Proposition 8.3.1(a) gives the direction used here: a psh function has a
  positive Hessian current.
- Proposition 15.1.1(b) and Corollary 15.1.2 give the local moment-map
  presentation of ordinary smooth functions. Theorem 15.7.1 supplies the
  analytic/tropical current formalism underlying the one-dimensional
  integration on a residual disc.
- Definitions 17.8.1 and 17.8.5 give local psh metrics and global psh
  metrics; Remark 17.8.10 asks the literal local-to-global question answered
  by the packet.

No claim depends on Stacks Project Tag 0DSV. The supplied PDF cited that
tag for the degree-one Picard identification, but 0DSV is unrelated. The
packet instead uses the standard Riemann--Roch and chord-and-tangent
identification \(E(\mathbf C)\simeq\operatorname{Pic}^0(E)\).
