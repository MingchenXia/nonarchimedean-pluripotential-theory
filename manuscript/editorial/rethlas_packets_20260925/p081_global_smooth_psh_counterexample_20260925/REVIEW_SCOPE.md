# Verification scope

Audit the exact theorem and dependency-closed proof in this directory.
Check especially:

1. the sign in the outgoing-slope inequality \(a_R\leq d_R\);
2. the passage from equality with a real span of principal divisors to a
   torsion divisor class;
3. the elliptic divisor identity for \(R_n=(n+1)P\);
4. smoothness and positivity of the convex cutoff on \(B_{R_n}\);
5. the fixed-neighbourhood uniform error estimate;
6. the distinction between locally ordinary CLD-psh and global uniform
   approximation by ordinary smooth psh metrics.

The primary-source locator file is supplied as an exact proof dependency.

author's original PDF.
