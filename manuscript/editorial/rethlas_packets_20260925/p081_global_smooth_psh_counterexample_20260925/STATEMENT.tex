Let \(K=\mathbf C((t))\), with \(0<|t|<1\), and let
\(E/\mathbf C\) be the elliptic curve \(y^2=x^3-x\), with origin \(O\).
Choose a transcendental \(\alpha\in\mathbf C\), a square root
\(\beta^2=\alpha^3-\alpha\), and put \(P=(\alpha,\beta)\).  Write
\[
  X=(E_K)^{\mathrm{an}},
  \qquad L=\mathcal O_{E_K}(P-O)^{\mathrm{an}}.
\]

Prove the following assertions.

1. The integral model metric \(h_0\) on \(L\) induced by
   \(\mathcal O_E(P-O)\) on the constant smooth model
   \(E\times_{\mathbf C}\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf C[[t]]\) is continuous
   and nef, and every local weight of \(h_0\) is plurisubharmonic in the
   ordinary Chambert--Loir--Ducros sense.  Near the good-reduction point,
   its weight is a uniform limit, on one fixed neighbourhood, of ordinary
   smooth psh functions.

2. The line bundle \(L\) admits no globally defined ordinary smooth psh
   metric.

Consequently a continuous metric with locally ordinary CLD-psh weights on
a projective non-Archimedean variety need not be globally psh in the sense
of uniform approximation by globally defined ordinary smooth psh metrics.
