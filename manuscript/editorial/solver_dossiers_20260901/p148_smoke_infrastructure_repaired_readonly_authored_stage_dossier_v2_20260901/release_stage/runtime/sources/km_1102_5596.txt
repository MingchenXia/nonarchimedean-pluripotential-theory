                                                            ON ZERO SETS IN THE DIRICHLET SPACE

                                                                      KARIM KELLAY AND JAVAD MASHREGHI




arXiv:1102.5596v1 [math.CA] 28 Feb 2011
                                                 Abstract. We study the zeros sets of functions in the Dirichlet space. Using Carleson
                                                 formula for Dirichlet integral, we obtain some new families of zero sets. We also show that
                                                 any closed subset of E ⊂ T with logarithmic capacity zero is the accumulation points of
                                                 the zeros of a function in the Dirichlet space. The zeros satisfy a growth restriction which
                                                 depends on E.
