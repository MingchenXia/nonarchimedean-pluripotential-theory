                                                OKOUNKOV BODIES OF FILTERED LINEAR SERIES




arXiv:0911.2923v1 [math.AG] 15 Nov 2009

                                                 Abstract. We associate to certain filtrations of a graded linear series of a
                                                 big line bundle a concave function on the Okounkov body whose law with
                                                 respect to Lebesgue’s measure describes the asymptotic distribution of the
                                                 jumps of the filtration. As a consequence we obtain a Fujita-type approxima-
                                                 tion theorem in this general filtered setting. We then specialize these results to
                                                 the filtrations by minima in the usual context of Arakelov geometry, thereby
                                                 obtaining in a simple way a natural construction of an arithmetic Okounkov
                                                 body, the existence of the arithmetic volume as a limit and the arithmetic
                                                 Fujita approximation theorem for adelically normed graded linear series.

0.4. Relations to other works. We have already mentioned above that The-
orems A and B yield in particular simpler proofs of the main results of [Yua08,
Che08, Mor09b], and it is therefore conversely clear that these works have had
a strong influence on the present article. The work of Witt-Nyström [WN09]
was also influential as far as the idea of constructing a concave function on the
Okounkov body is concerned, even though the final outcome of our construction
is not a Chebyshev-type function.
   While the present work was being written X.Yuan has introduced in [Yua09]
another kind of concave function c[L] on the Okounkov body of a line bundle
endowed with Arakelov-geometric data. His construction is closer in spirit to
that of [WN09], since it consists in summing up the analogues of Witt-Nyström’s
Chebyshev functions at all places of K. It is also closely related to Rumely-
Lau-Varley’s study of the sectional capacity [RLV00], and indeed Yuan’s goal
is to show that the mean-value of c[L] coincides with the quantity he denotes
by volχ (L) and which is equal by definition to − log of the sectional capacity
in [RLV00].
   We shall discuss in more details the relation between [Yua09] and the present
work in Section 4. We will show in particular on an example that the two con-
structions do not coincide in general.

0.5. Organization of the paper. Let us briefly describe the structure of our
article.
      • Section 1.1 contains the necessary definitions and results on Okounkov
         bodies extracted from [LM08], whereas Section 1.2 introduces some ter-
         minology related to filtrations.

     • In Section 1.3 we define the concave function attached to a filtered graded
       linear series and prove our main results in this general setting. Theorem
       A corresponds to Theorem 1.9 whereas Theorem B is Theorem 1.12.
     • We then show in Section 2 how to relate the Arakelov-geometric setting
       (and more generally the adelic setting) to the filtered case using filtrations
       by minima.

     • The final Section 4 discusses the relation between the present work and [RLV00,
       Yua09].
