new mathematical candidate. No TeX, BibTeX, PDF build, or production call
was made. A syntax or hash check is not a mathematical verdict.

- bidegree-(0,0) rigidity from the full higher-bidegree P21.3 statement;
- the ordinary locally convex test topology from algebraic cohomology;
- a canonical positive G-form evaluation, a Hahn–Banach linear extension,
