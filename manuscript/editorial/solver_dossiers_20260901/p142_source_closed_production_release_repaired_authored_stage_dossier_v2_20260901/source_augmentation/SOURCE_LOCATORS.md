## Scope firewall

left-mesh upper-semicontinuity argument and its explicit decreasing
continuous approximation are the candidate's new proof.  None of the
sources is to be cited as already proving that general compact-Kahler
conclusion, and no source here supplies MP positivity of the finite
approximants.
