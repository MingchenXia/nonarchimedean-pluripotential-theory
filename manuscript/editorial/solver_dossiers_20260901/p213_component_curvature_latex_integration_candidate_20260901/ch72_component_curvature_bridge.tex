A separate comparison now applies in every dimension $n\geq1$ and over
every complete non-Archimedean field:
\Cref{prop:ch14-component-curvature} proves that equal canonical
curvatures of two locally smoothly uniformly approachable continuous
potentials are equal against every reduced analytic component current.
Generic nonreduced multiplicities are retained, and divisorial
component intersections are allowed.  More generally, the same conclusion
holds for a continuous $\operatorname{CPsh}$ pair whose common curvature
has zero coefficient-measure mass on the crossing locus.  One approachable
member, a local approachable potential for that common curvature, or
zero common curvature suffices; see
\cref{itm:ch14-component-curvature:common}.
This is a consequence of the first-stage analytic-nullity input, not a
classification of arbitrary positive closed currents.  It therefore does
not by itself prove the displayed every-current predicate, remove the
crossing-nullity hypothesis for arbitrary $\operatorname{CPsh}$ pairs,
or settle positive bidegrees.
