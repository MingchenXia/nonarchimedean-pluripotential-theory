\chapter*{Acknowledgements}
\label{front:acknowledgements}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Acknowledgements}

The mathematical sources of this book include the work of
Chambert--Loir and Ducros, Boucksom--Favre--Jonsson,
Boucksom--Jonsson, Gubler--K\"unnemann, Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff,
Gubler--Rabinoff, and the authors of the complex pluripotential and
ray theories cited throughout. The Stacks Project supplies many of the
scheme-theoretic inputs. Precise statements, versions and dependencies
are credited where they enter an argument and collected in
\Cref{app:provenance}. Author-supplied manuscripts are identified separately
from published sources. This source record is part of the exposition:
it allows the reader to trace each comparison to the hypotheses under
which its ingredients were established.
