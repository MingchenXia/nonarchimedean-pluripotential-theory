# Analytic realization of the weighted cross-fan obstruction

The verified abstract tropical example uses the cross fan
\[
 \Sigma=\mathbf R_{\geq0}e_1\cup\mathbf R_{\geq0}(-e_1)
       \cup\mathbf R_{\geq0}e_2\cup\mathbf R_{\geq0}(-e_2),
\]
the CLD-psh functions \(u=2|x_1|\), \(v=|x_1|+|x_2|\), and a positive closed
skeletal current with horizontal weights two and vertical weights one. The
fundamental unit-weighted Laplacians of \(u,v\) agree, but
\(\mathrm d'\mathrm d''((u-v)T)=2\delta_0\).

Treat this calibrated fan computation as input to the problem, not as a
theorem stated in the attached analytic-realization references.  Those
references provide harmonic tropicalization, balancing, calibration, and
delta-current machinery; every claimed realization below must still verify
the displayed weights and normalization directly.  For CLD, use the supplied
arXiv:1204.6277v2 (posted 25 July 2025; PDF dated 29 July 2025), not the older
draft.

Determine whether this exact configuration can be realized by:

1. a harmonic tropicalization of a good strict Berkovich analytic space;
2. a positive closed delta-current or cycle current carrying the displayed
   weights; and
3. analytic psh pullbacks of \(u,v\) for which the same current calculation
   holds.

Try nodal curves, products of annuli, semistable surface skeleta, toric
analytic spaces, and tropical modifications. Check balancing, calibration,
integral lattice indices, analytic multiplicities, and whether a map can have
the cross as image with the needed local branches.

If analytic multiplicity or harmonicity forces the current weights to be
proportional to the fundamental calibration, prove that obstruction. Do not
declare an abstract balanced fan to be analytically realizable without
constructing the space, chart, and current.
