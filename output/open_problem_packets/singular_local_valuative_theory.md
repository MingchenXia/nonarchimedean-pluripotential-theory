# Local valuative pluripotential theory on singular germs

Boucksom--Favre--Jonsson construct formal psh functions, nef exceptional
b-divisors, envelopes, and mixed local Monge--Ampère measures on the
normalized valuation space of a smooth complex germ, normalized by
\(\nu(\mathfrak m)=1\).

Develop the strongest functorial extension to an excellent reduced analytic
or algebraic germ \((Y,y)\), allowing reducible, nonnormal, and positive
characteristic cases only where the proof works.

Keep the source firewall explicit.  The BFJ construction starts with a
smooth complex germ and resolution of singularities.  Boucksom--de Fernex--
Favre develop the relevant birational intersection theory for normal
varieties over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, with
their local theory stated for normal isolated singularities; the later
BdFFU source treats normal complex algebraic varieties.  None of these
references supplies, without new arguments, a mixed local Monge--Ampère
theory for reducible or nonnormal analytic germs, or for positive
characteristic.  Treat every passage across those boundaries as an item to
prove rather than as inherited formalism.

Required questions:

1. Which ideal or boundary datum normalizes the valuation cone, and how does
   the theory transform when that datum changes?
2. Do exceptional Cartier/Weil b-divisors and a relative nef cone give a
   compact formal psh space on a resolution or alteration system?
3. Can primary-ideal approximation be proved without regularity?
4. How do normalization, conductor, and branch data descend psh functions and
   measures?
5. Is there a bounded mixed intersection measure independent of resolution,
   and what replaces it when resolution is unavailable?
6. Can a finite-energy completion be defined with the correct boundary term?

Test normal crossings, a cusp, a node with two branches, quotient
singularities, and purely inseparable universal homeomorphisms. Use the Stacks
Project or alterations where legitimate. Do not claim independence of the
normalization ideal without an explicit comparison map. Classify each layer
separately as VERIFIED, CONDITIONAL, DISPROVED, or OPEN.

Do not recycle two questions from Boucksom--de Fernex--Favre as open.
Yuchen Zhang, *On the Volume of Isolated Singularities*, Compositio Math.
150 (2014), 1413--1424, proves a positive dimension-dependent lower bound for
every nonzero BdFF volume of an isolated Gorenstein singularity, and gives a
non-\(\mathbf Q\)-Gorenstein isolated singularity of volume zero admitting no
boundary that makes the pair log canonical.  Thus the first question is
resolved positively and the proposed zero-volume implication is false.
