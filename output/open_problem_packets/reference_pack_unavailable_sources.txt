Citation precision discovered during audit
In Gubler--Rabinoff arXiv:2503.05513v1, Theorem 1.5 is the local uniform approximation of a semipositive R-PL function by locally Fubini--Study functions on a boundaryless strict analytic space. Theorem 5.3 is instead the curve-restriction characterization of semipositivity. Any prompt attributing the approximation statement to Theorem 5.3 should be corrected before verification.
