# Current positivity versus semipositive PL functions

Let \(k\) be a complete nontrivially valued non-Archimedean field, let \(X\)
be a good separated strictly \(k\)-analytic space, and let \(h\) be a
real-valued real PL function. Assume that the Chambert--Loir--Ducros current
\[
                    \mathrm d'\mathrm d''[h]
\]
is positive on the relative interior (globally when \(X\) is boundaryless).
Determine whether \(h\) is residue-nef/model semipositive.

Gubler--Rabinoff, arXiv:2503.05513v1, Theorem 5.3 proves that model
semipositivity is detected by restrictions to sufficiently small analytic
curves and Remark 1.8 records the current converse as open. Gubler--Rabinoff,
arXiv:2506.13548v2, Theorem 4.3 identifies model semipositivity with
classical curve-test psh for real PL functions. Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff,
Theorem 7.14, supplies the forward current implication (Corollary 7.15 is
the harmonic-current statement in the published numbering).

Try all natural routes:

1. construct a positivity-preserving pullback of the order-zero PL current to
   every analytic curve in the curve criterion;
2. replace pullback by tropical intersection with graph cycles or by a
   slicing theorem;
3. prove direct residue-nefness using test forms concentrated near lifted
   special-fibre curves;
4. search for a counterexample on singular or mixed-dimensional tropical
   charts, while respecting analytic multiplicities and balancing.

Separate an abstract tropical counterexample from an actual Berkovich
realization. Do not define current-psh to mean semipositive. If a proof fails,
state the exact missing theorem as a conditional equivalence. Classify the
outcome as VERIFIED, CONDITIONAL, DISPROVED, or OPEN, with every hypothesis
visible.
