# The finite-energy Monge--Ampère operator over an arbitrary field

This packet is **prepared but not run**.  The source is Pille--Schneider,
arXiv:2209.04879v2, Remark 4.4.

Let \(K\) be an arbitrary complete non-Archimedean field, \(X/K\) a projective
scheme of pure dimension \(n\), and \(L\) an ample line bundle.  The
finite-energy class \(\mathcal E^1(X,L)\) can be defined over such a field, but
the source states that it is unclear in general how to extend the
Monge--Ampère operator to this class.  The established endpoints cited there
are the discretely valued characteristic-zero theory of BFJ and the trivially
valued theory of BJ.

Construct, or prove an obstruction to, a symmetric mixed operator on the
largest justified finite-energy domain over arbitrary \(K\), satisfying:

- agreement with the continuous/model Monge--Ampère operator;
- positivity and the correct total intersection mass;
- monotone/truncation continuity and independence of approximants;
- integration by parts and the energy first-variation formula where finite;
- comparison/uniqueness and functorial scalar extension/descent.

Separate general valuations, residue characteristic, singularities of \(X\),
and nonalgebraizable analytic geometry.  Do not infer this extension from the
continuous theorem 4.3, and do not conflate Remark 4.4 with the distinct
Banach-base continuity Question 4.9.
