# Do generalized Lelong numbers determine the valuative transform?

Let \(u,v\) be plurisubharmonic germs at a smooth point of a complex
manifold. Boucksom--Favre--Jonsson, Valuations and plurisubharmonic
singularities (2008), prove equivalences among equality of all
infinitely-near Lelong numbers, equality of strong multiplier ideals, and
equality of relative types, and prove that these imply equality of generalized
Lelong numbers against tame psh weights.

Determine whether the converse holds:
\[
 \nu_\psi(u)=\nu_\psi(v)\quad\text{for every tame psh weight }\psi
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \widehat u=\widehat v
\]
on the normalized valuation space, with BFJ's normalization
\(\nu(\mathfrak m)=1\).

Translate the question exactly into the BFJ formal psh/nef b-divisor
language. Investigate whether the mixed local Monge--Ampère measures of tame
fundamental solutions separate bounded or finite formal psh functions. Try:

1. polarization and local intersection duality;
2. fundamental solutions representing quasimonomial valuations;
3. approximation by primary ideals and mixed multiplicities;
4. dimension-two valuative-tree potential theory;
5. toric/monomial convex duality;
6. a counterexample arising from a nontrivial kernel of the available test
   measures.

Track the sign and factor-two convention between \(u\sim\log|f|\) and the
squared-norm convention. Do not replace all generalized Lelong numbers by
relative types, and do not silently shrink "every tame psh weight" to the
tame maximal weights used to represent relative types; that is the
implication to be proved. Return the strongest
verified positive domain, a counterexample if one exists, and the exact
remaining obstruction.
