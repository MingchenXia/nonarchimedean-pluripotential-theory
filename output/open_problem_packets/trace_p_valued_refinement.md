# A P-valued refinement of analytic trace

This packet is **prepared but not run**. It isolates a book-formulated
lifting problem forced by the exact scope of Mingchen Xia, *Operations on
transcendental non-Archimedean metrics*, arXiv:2312.17150v3, Proposition
2.12. Do not present the question as a verbatim conjecture from the source.

Let \(X\) be a smooth compact Kähler manifold, let \(\theta\) be a smooth
closed real \((1,1)\)-form, let \(E\subset X\) be an irreducible reduced
closed subspace, and let \(\widetilde E\to E\) be its normalization. Set
\[
  D_E:=\{\varphi\in\operatorname{PSH}(X,\theta):
                 \nu(\varphi,E)=0\}.
\]

The source defines \(\operatorname{Tr}_E(\varphi)\) for
\(\varphi\in D_E\) as a quasi-psh function on \(\widetilde E\), well-defined
only up to \(\mathcal I\)-equivalence. Proposition 2.12 further proves that
this output \(\mathcal I\)-class depends only on the input
\(\mathcal I\)-class. Thus it supplies
\[
  D_E/{\sim_{\mathcal I}}\longrightarrow
  \operatorname{QPSH}(\widetilde E)/{\sim_{\mathcal I}},
\]
and, since \(P\)-equivalence implies \(\mathcal I\)-equivalence, a composite
from input \(P\)-classes to output \(\mathcal I\)-classes. It does **not**
supply a map with values in output \(P\)-classes.

Determine whether there is a canonical lift
\[
  D_E/{\sim_P}\longrightarrow
  \operatorname{QPSH}(\widetilde E)/{\sim_P}
\]
whose composition with the quotient to \(\mathcal I\)-classes is the sourced
trace map. Equivalently, determine natural additional hypotheses under
which \(P\)-equivalent inputs have traces with a well-defined common
\(P\)-class, independent of every quasi-equisingular approximation and
auxiliary choice.

Test analytic singularities, smooth hypersurfaces, split products, and
holomorphic retractions first. An \(\mathcal I\)-good prescribed datum being
\(P\)-equivalent to its canonical \(\mathcal I\)-model representative does
not by itself assign a \(P\)-class to the trace. A positive result must
control all mixed nonpluripolar masses or another exact characterization of
\(P\)-equivalence after restriction. A counterexample must give two
\(P\)-equivalent inputs in \(D_E\) whose trace outputs are not
\(P\)-equivalent, rather than merely choosing different representatives of
one output \(\mathcal I\)-class.

Return separately: the exact Proposition 2.12 statement; special positive
cases; a verified obstruction or counterexample if available; and the open
remainder. No conclusion may be promoted without a fresh production
Rethlas receipt for its exact statement and proof.
