# Exact open directions in Botero--Mazzon--Pille-Schneider

This packet is **prepared but not run**.  It isolates the source-stated open
directions in Botero--Mazzon--Pille-Schneider, arXiv:2603.08664v1, submitted
9 March 2026 and internally dated 10 March 2026.

Determine the strongest correct answers to:

1. Can one define higher-dimensional polyhedral smoothness hypotheses that
   ensure both regularity and orthogonality for \((X,\gamma)\), extending
   Definition 6.19 and Propositions 6.21--6.22 from dimension one?
2. Which polarized Calabi--Yau degenerations have tropicalizations satisfying
   regularity and orthogonality, and hence admit the polyhedral route to the
   non-Archimedean comparison property discussed in Sections 1.4--1.5 and 7?
3. For which matroids does the associated Bergman fan satisfy orthogonality
   (Remark 6.27), with the growth and strict-positivity conditions on
   \(\gamma\) stated explicitly?

Use the exact known boundary: Theorem 6.4 characterizes regularity;
Proposition 6.13 and Remark 6.6 give positive regularity loci; Proposition
6.21 and Theorem 6.22 give the one-dimensional polyhedrally smooth theorem;
Examples 6.23--6.24 and Remarks 6.26--6.27 give counterexamples and warnings;
Proposition 7.2 has a specific toric Calabi--Yau complete-intersection scope.

The book additionally asks for an intrinsic singular-fan contact theorem and
for comparison with CLD/delta/non-Archimedean operators under recursive
calibrated analytic realization.  Those are **book-formulated strengthenings**,
not questions attributed verbatim to BMPS.  Do not fold them into a claimed
answer to items 1--3 without proving the realization and every intermediate
intersection identity.
