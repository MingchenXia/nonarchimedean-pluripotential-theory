# Three questions on partial Okounkov bodies and Chebyshev transforms

This packet is **prepared but not run**.  It uses Mingchen Xia, *Partial
Okounkov bodies and Duistermaat--Heckman measures of non-Archimedean metrics*,
arXiv:2112.04290, Conjectures 5.29 and 8.8 and the final paragraph of Section
6.4 (PDF p. 34).

Treat the three source questions separately.

1. For a Hermitian pseudoeffective line bundle \((L,\phi)\), including the
   zero-mass case, prove or disprove
   \[
     \dim\Delta(L,\phi)=\operatorname{nd}(L,\phi),
   \]
   where the right side is Cao's analytic numerical dimension.  The limit
   body in Section 5.6 is already stated to be independent of the auxiliary
   ample effective divisor \(H\) and Kähler form \(\omega\); that independence
   is not the open question.
2. For Hermitian big line bundles \((L_i,\phi_i)\) of positive volume, prove or
   disprove the non-toric mixed-volume formula in Conjecture 8.8:
   \[
     \frac1{n!}\int_X \mathrm d\mathrm d^c\phi_1\wedge\cdots\wedge
     \mathrm d\mathrm d^c\phi_n
     =\sup_{\nu}\operatorname{vol}
       (\Delta_\nu(L_1,\phi_1),\ldots,\Delta_\nu(L_n,\phi_n)),
   \]
   with \(\nu\) ranging over rank-\(n\) valuations as in the source.
3. In Section 6.4, the Chebyshev transform for a positive-mass potential
   \(\varphi\) is defined by choosing an auxiliary \(\eta\preceq\varphi\) whose
   curvature is a Kähler current and passing through
   \(\varphi_\varepsilon=(1-\varepsilon)\varphi+\varepsilon\eta\).
   Is the resulting \(c_{[\varphi]}[v]\) independent of the choice of
   \(\eta\)?

For item 1 separate numerical dimension from affine dimension and prove all
continuity needed under Kähler perturbation.  For item 2 keep the supremum
over valuations and all normalizing factors.  For item 3 do not misstate the
auxiliary datum as the \(H,\omega\) used for the limit body.  Return a theorem
only if the exact source signature is proved.
