# Maximality of filtration test curves in a big class

This packet is **prepared but not run**.  It isolates the conjecture stated by
Darvas--Xia--Zhang, arXiv:2302.02541v3, immediately before Definition 4.4.

Let \(X\) be a connected projective manifold, \(L\) a big line bundle, and
\(\mathcal F\) a filtration of \(R(X,L)\) satisfying the source's multiplicative,
decreasing, left-continuous, and linear-boundedness hypotheses.  Let
\(\{\phi^{\mathcal F}_{\tau,m}\}_{\tau}\) be the Bergman test curves and let
\(\{\phi^{\mathcal F}_{\tau}\}_{\tau}\) be their limiting test curve as defined
in Section 4.1.

In the ample case the analogous filtration test curve is maximal.  In the big
case DXZ explicitly say that maximality of the test curve just constructed is
unclear and conjecture it.  Definition 4.4 therefore defines the associated
Phong--Sturm ray using the **maximization** of
\(\{\phi^{\mathcal F}_{\tau}\}_{\tau}\).

Prove or disprove that the unmaximized big-class filtration test curve is
already maximal, in the exact \(\mathcal I\)-maximal/model-envelope sense used
by DXZ.  Test first finitely generated filtrations, flag configurations,
analytic singularities, semiample big \(L\), and birational pullbacks of ample
data.  A proof must compare multiplier ideals at every level and endpoint; an
equality of Legendre transforms or energies alone is insufficient.  A
counterexample must satisfy all filtration hypotheses and exhibit a strict
gap from its maximization.

Do not claim that DXZ Definition 4.4 asserts maximality: it inserts
maximization precisely because the conjecture is open.
