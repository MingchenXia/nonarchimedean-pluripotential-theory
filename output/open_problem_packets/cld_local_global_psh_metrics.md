# Local CLD-psh metrics and global approximation

This packet is **prepared but not run**.  Both source questions come from
Chambert--Loir--Ducros, arXiv:1204.6277v2, Remark 17.8.10.  Keep them distinct.

## Source question A

Let \(X\) be the analytification of a projective variety over a complete
nontrivially valued field and let \(L\) be a line bundle on \(X\).  Suppose a
continuous metric on \(L\) is psh in the local CLD sense: the weight
\(\log\lVert s\rVert^{-1}\) is CLD psh for every local invertible frame \(s\).
Must it be **globally psh** in the sense of CLD 17.8.5, namely a uniform limit
of smooth psh metrics on \(L\)?

## Source question B

Let \(X\) be a proper strict analytic space possessing a formal model with
projective special fibre.  Does CLD Corollary 17.8.9 extend to this setting:
is every parlinear metric globally dpsh (a quotient of two globally psh
metrics)?

The second question is not literally the same as question A and must not be
rewritten as an already-defined Fubini--Study or nef-model statement.

## Required analysis

Identify all known common loci: semiample formal metrics, projective formal
models, Fubini--Study approximants, and differences of ample formal metrics.
Then either construct global approximants compatible with transition
functions and the numerical class, or isolate a genuine obstruction.  Do not
infer global approximation merely from positivity of local curvature
currents, and do not replace the CLD definition of globally psh by a different
modern global psh cone without proving equivalence.

Return separate verdicts for A and B, with the exact field, strictness,
properness, projectivity, formal-model, and continuity hypotheses visible.
