# Regularity and orthogonality on balanced polyhedral spaces

Use Botero--Mazzon--Pille-Schneider, arXiv:2603.08664v1 (submitted
9 March 2026; manuscript dated 10 March 2026), with the source cutoff
14 August 2026.  Let \(\mathbf X\) be a pure-dimensional balanced rational
polyhedral space and let \(\gamma\) be a piecewise-affine polyhedrally convex
reference function that is positive at infinity.

Characterize the pairs \((\mathbf X,\gamma)\) for which the source's
regularity and orthogonality properties hold.  Under an explicit analytic or
algebraic realizability hypothesis, compare its polyhedral Monge--Ampère
operator with CLD, delta-form, and non-Archimedean Monge--Ampère measures.

Do not assume the two properties on an arbitrary balanced space.  The exact
known boundaries in arXiv:2603.08664v1 are:

1. regularity is characterized in Theorem 6.4;
2. regularity holds in dimension one by Proposition 6.13, with complete and
   conical cases recorded in Remark 6.6;
3. orthogonality holds for one-dimensional polyhedrally smooth spaces by
   Proposition 6.21, yielding the one-dimensional existence theorem 6.22;
4. Section 6.5 gives counterexamples on balanced graphs and, without strict
   convexity of \(\gamma\), on Bergman fans; and
5. Proposition 7.2 compares solutions only for its specific toric
   degenerations of Calabi--Yau complete intersections.

Try to formulate an intrinsic higher-dimensional local smoothness condition
and a precise strict-positivity hypothesis implying regularity and
orthogonality, or construct a counterexample satisfying those hypotheses.
For comparison with analytic geometry, distinguish an abstract balanced
polyhedral space from a harmonic tropicalization with its integral lattice,
calibration, analytic multiplicities, and scalar-extension behavior.  Prove
the realization and the equality of operators; balancing alone is not a
realization theorem.

Return separately the results already proved in the 2026 source, any new
verified higher-dimensional theorem, analytic comparison statements,
counterexamples, conditional reductions, and the remaining open domain.

The three questions attributed directly to the source are isolated in
bmps_exact_source_questions.md: higher-dimensional polyhedral smoothness,
Calabi--Yau tropicalizations with regularity/orthogonality, and Bergman fans
of matroids with orthogonality.  The intrinsic singular-fan contact theorem
and recursive calibrated CLD/non-Archimedean comparison are stronger
book-formulated questions and must be labelled as such.
