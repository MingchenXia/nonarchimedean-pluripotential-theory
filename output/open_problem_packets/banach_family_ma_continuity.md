# Continuity of Monge--Ampère measures over Banach bases

Address Pille-Schneider, *Global pluripotential theory on hybrid spaces*,
Question 4.9 in its exact signature.  Let \(A\) be an integral Banach ring,
let \(X\) be a flat projective \(A\)-scheme of relative dimension \(n\), let
\(L_1,\ldots,L_n\) be semiample line bundles, and let \(\phi_i\) be continuous
psh metrics on \(L_i\).  For \(\psi\in C^0(X^{\mathrm{an}})\), determine
whether
\[
 x\longmapsto
 \int_{X_{\mathscr H(x)}^{\mathrm{an}}}\psi\,
 \operatorname{MA}\bigl((\phi_1)_x,\ldots,(\phi_n)_x\bigr)
\]
is continuous on \(\mathcal M(A)\).

Keep the known boundary exact.  Pille-Schneider imposes flatness in Question
4.9 to ensure that the total mixed intersection mass is independent of the
base point; do not turn this sufficient hypothesis in the source into an
if-and-only-if assertion.  Theorem 4.11 proves the hybrid result for a smooth
degeneration of complex manifolds, one relatively ample line bundle, and the
diagonal Monge--Ampère measure of one continuous hybrid psh metric.  Any mixed
or merely semiample extension must be obtained by a justified polarization
argument with all its hypotheses retained.  The source leaves a general
integral Banach ring open and singles out the arithmetic case of the ring of
integers of a number field as expected.  Lemma 4.10 gives the
Chern--Levine--Nirenberg estimate over a non-Archimedean field; it does not by
itself give estimates uniform as the base point varies.

First prove that reduced assertion by a precise intersection-theoretic
specialization formula, retaining every hypothesis needed to control
vertical cycles.  In parallel, search for a non-hybrid integral Banach base
where specialization of vertical intersection cycles jumps.  Check that any
purported counterexample satisfies flatness, semiampleness, continuity and
psh positivity, and does not merely change total mass.

Return the hybrid theorem as known, then separate arithmetic, affinoid,
model-metric, and fully general Banach-base verdicts.  State whether the
argument treats arbitrary nets in \(\mathcal M(A)\), and do not infer the
general result solely from density without a uniform estimate.
