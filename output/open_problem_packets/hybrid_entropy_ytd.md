# Hybrid entropy slopes and the residual compact-Kähler YTD bridge

Determine the strongest rigorous bridge, current as of 14 August 2026,
between non-Archimedean stability and existence of canonical Kähler metrics
on compact Kähler manifolds.  The source-scoped residual packet
post2025_ytd_residuals.md supersedes any broader wording below.

Focus on the unresolved analytic steps rather than restating algebraic
K-stability:

1. convergence of entropy and Mabuchi slopes along hybrid/model
   approximations;
2. passage from test configurations or model rays to arbitrary finite-energy
   weak geodesic rays;
3. coercivity modulo automorphisms;
4. compactness and attainment for destabilizing sequences;
5. compatibility of transcendental NA metrics with the hybrid topology.

Respect the published BHJ erratum and quarantine every corrected or withdrawn
statement. Compare the precise domains of Pille-Schneider, Boucksom--Jonsson,
Darvas--Xia--Zhang, Mesquita--Piccione--Witt Nyström, Trusiani, and any later
primary work available by the cutoff.

For chronology, use the supplied versions: Boucksom--Jonsson
arXiv:2509.15016v1 is dated 18 September 2025,
Boucksom--Jonsson arXiv:2602.10800v2 is dated 19 May 2026,
Darvas--Zhang arXiv:2509.15173v2 is dated 3 November 2025,
Mesquita--Piccione arXiv:2409.06221v2 is dated 18 September 2025, and
Mesquita--Piccione--Witt Nyström arXiv:2509.09442v1 is dated 11 September
2025.  Version-sensitive claims must remain labelled as such.

For the BHJ source, the erratum says that Theorem A, Corollary B, and the
results through Section 4 remain valid, whereas the gap in Theorem 5.6 also
affects Theorem 5.4, Theorem C, and Corollaries D--E.  Do not use the latter
statements, or descendants depending on them, without an independent repaired
proof.

Do not list the smooth polarized projective entropy-regularization problem as
wholly open at this cutoff.  Trusiani's arXiv:2605.30063v1 (28 May 2026),
Theorem B and Corollary A, reports special Fujita approximation for big line
bundles and a solution of the Boucksom--Jonsson entropy-regularization
conjecture; its Theorem A reports an automorphism-relative uniform YTD result
for smooth polarized projective varieties.  Treat these as dated preprint
claims.  Also keep separate the polarized weighted-cscK signature of
Boucksom--Jonsson arXiv:2509.15016v1, the \(K^\beta\)-stability and
log-discrepancy-model signatures of Darvas--Zhang arXiv:2509.15173v2, and the
two Mesquita--Piccione signatures: arXiv:2409.06221v2 assumes uniform
K-stability over \(\mathcal E^1\) and proves the implication to a unique cscK
metric, whereas Mesquita--Piccione--Witt Nyström arXiv:2509.09442v1 uses
uniform K-stability for models and also proves a transcendental
non-Archimedean Calabi--Yau theorem.  None is interchangeable merely by
terminology.

Trusiani's Corollary B lists equivalences among the standard smooth
polarized-projective stability formulations, including the model,
log-discrepancy-model, and \(b\)-\(K\)-polystability signatures (and
\(K^\beta\) when the connected automorphism group is trivial).  Therefore a
bare projective equivalence question is no longer open at this cutoff.
Residual targets are direct algebraic proofs of those equivalences, bounded
support for divisorial test measures, algebraic equivariance, regularization
of rays attached to log-discrepancy models, and transcendental, singular,
klt-pair, and genuinely hybrid extensions.  Label source-stated and
book-formulated residuals separately.

Try to prove a complete theorem under the weakest natural extra hypotheses,
such as uniform entropy integrability, model approximation with exact slope
convergence, or discrete automorphism group. If the general theorem remains
open, isolate a minimal list of independent conditional assumptions and show
formally that they imply the YTD conclusion. Do not present a conjectural
entropy-slope equality as established.
