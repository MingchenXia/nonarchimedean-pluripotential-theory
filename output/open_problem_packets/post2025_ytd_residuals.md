# Residual YTD questions after the 2025--2026 preprints

This packet is **prepared but not run** and has a literature cutoff of
15 August 2026.  It replaces the obsolete formulation in which smooth
polarized-projective entropy regularization and comparison of the standard
projective stability signatures were left wholly open.

## Dated positive claims that must not be reproved as open premises

- Boucksom--Jonsson, arXiv:2509.15016v1 (18 September 2025), prove their
  polarized projective weighted-cscK/b-\(K\)-polystability statements.
- Darvas--Zhang, arXiv:2509.15173v2 (3 November 2025), prove their
  \(K^\beta\) and log-discrepancy-model correspondences, including Theorems
  1.6/10.4.
- Trusiani, arXiv:2605.30063v1 (28 May 2026), claims special Fujita
  approximation, equivariant entropy regularization, the smooth polarized
  projective YTD theorem, and in Corollary B the listed equivalence of the
  standard smooth polarized-projective stability formulations.
- Mesquita--Piccione--Witt Nyström, arXiv:2509.09442v1, prove their
  transcendental model-stability implication and finite-energy
  non-Archimedean Calabi--Yau theorem.

Treat all preprint claims with their version and date.  Do not infer a
nonprojective or singular theorem from them.

## Exact source-stated residuals

From the “Questions and further remarks” paragraph of Boucksom--Jonsson:

1. give a direct algebraic proof that their \(b\)-\(K\)-polystability is
   equivalent to its uniform version;
2. decide whether uniform \(b\)-\(K\)-polystability can be tested on
   divisorial measures whose support cardinality is bounded in terms of
   \(\dim X\);
3. give algebraic proofs of the equivariance implications now obtained via
   cscK metrics;
4. extend the theory to polarized klt pairs and to the nonprojective Kähler
   setting.

From Darvas--Zhang, Remarks 1.7 and the following discussion:

5. regularize rays attached to log-discrepancy models by test configurations
   strongly enough to recover the strong uniform YTD formulation;
6. extend the log-discrepancy-model correspondence equivariantly to
   transcendental Kähler classes, especially with nontrivial automorphisms.

Trusiani explicitly notes after Corollary B that a direct algebraic bridge
between the equivalent projective signatures is still unavailable and lists
transcendental and singular developments.

## Book-formulated residuals

The following are comparison programmes formulated by the book, not quoted
primary-source questions: construct explicit normalization-preserving maps
between the various analytic/non-Archimedean signatures; extend the
equivariant theory to singular compact Kähler spaces; and prove hybrid
compactness and family Monge--Ampère continuity through degenerations.
