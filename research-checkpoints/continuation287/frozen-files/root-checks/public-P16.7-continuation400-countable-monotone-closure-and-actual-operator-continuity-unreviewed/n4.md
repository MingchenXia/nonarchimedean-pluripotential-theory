# All-test separation, classical recognition, and the unresolved uniqueness step

This note independently checks the entire current all-test argument and ROOT394/395. It uses the bounded and generated calculus proved in n1; the new countable closure and its consequences are n2 and n3. The published curve and classical-psh results are stated at their retained hypotheses below. No old positive verdict is a premise.

For continuous dpsh functions write $Q_T(f,h)=-\int fDD(hT)$, with the positive Hilbert pairing of n1 when $T$ is positive closed. Put $f=a-b$, $g=(a+b)/2$, $h=w-g$, and $I(a,b)=\int(a-b)[MA(b)-MA(a)]$. Bounded expansion and Green give
\[
 I(a,b)=Q_{\theta_a}(f,f)+Q_{\theta_b}(f,f)\geq0.
\]
The signed transgression is
\[
 Q_{\theta_w}(f,f)-Q_{\theta_g}(f,f)
 =Q_{\theta_a}(f,h)-Q_{\theta_b}(f,h).
\]
Both sides equal $-\int fDDf\wedge DDh$ by the symmetric continuous dpsh product and $DDf=\theta_a-\theta_b$. Thus Cauchy--Schwarz in the two positive current slots gives
\[
 Q_{\theta_w}(f,f)\leq I(a,b)/2+
 \sqrt{I(a,b)(Q_{\theta_a}(h,h)+Q_{\theta_b}(h,h))}.
\]

Normalize $a,b,w\leq0$ and assume $E(a),E(b),E(w)\geq-C$. The dual estimate in n1 gives $\int(-p)MA(q)\leq3C$ for any two of these weights. Since $B(q,r)\leq2MA((q+r)/2)$, concavity and the same estimate give $\int(-p)B(q,r)\leq6C$. From $|h|\leq-w+(-a-b)/2$ follow $\int|h|MA(q)\leq6C$ and $\int|h|B(q,r)\leq12C$. Expanding
\[
 Q_{\theta_a}(h,h)
 =-\int h\left[B(a,w)-(MA(a)+B(a,b))/2\right]
\]
bounds this slot by $12C+3C+6C=21C$; the other slot has the same bound. Consequently
\[
 Q_{\theta_w}(a-b,a-b)\leq I(a,b)/2+\sqrt{42C I(a,b)}.
\]
This estimate tests every bounded continuous $w\in\mathcal C$, rather than just the degenerate reference current.

If generated $u,v$ have equal MA, choose any continuous decreasing presentations and subtract fixed upper constants. Their energies have a common lower bound. The four moving moments from n1 show $I(u_j,v_j)\to0$, so $Q_{\theta_w}(u_j-v_j,u_j-v_j)\to0$ for every fixed bounded test. For $u,v\in\mathcal C$ themselves the same estimate gives zero pairing. Green and Cauchy--Schwarz against compact smooth scalar tests imply $DD((u-v)\theta_w)=0$.

Here is the exact bounded divisor consequence. In a lift torus chart put $\rho_a=|x-a|^2/2$. Let $P$ be a nonzero Laurent polynomial and $\chi$ a smooth convex function, zero on $(-\infty,0]$, with $0\leq\chi'\leq1$ and $\chi(t)=t-c$ for $t\geq1$. The capped function $\psi_N=\chi(\log|P|+N)$ is ordinary smooth and psh: it is locally zero at zeros of $P$, and elsewhere is a smooth convex unit-logarithm function. Choose $0<\delta<r^2/4$ on a chart $|x_i-a_i|<r$, and a positive $\varepsilon_N$ sufficiently small to make
\[
 w_N=\max(\rho_a,\delta+\varepsilon_N\psi_N)-\rho_a
\]
zero on a tropical boundary collar. Its zero extension is in $\mathcal C$. On the fixed inner neighbourhood $\rho_a<\delta/2$ its curvature equals $\varepsilon_N DD\psi_N$.

CLDv2 Theorem16.7.3 is the Poincare--Lelong identity $DD[\log|P|]=\delta_{\operatorname{div}P}$ for a regular meromorphic function on a boundaryless pure-dimensional analytic space. The whole applied statement and immediate proof were read in LF23458--23671, including its Stokes, boundary and calibration steps. The torus chart is smooth and boundaryless and nonzero holomorphic $P$ is regular. Compact smooth top-test supports avoid the lower-dimensional divisor, so $[\psi_N-N]$ is eventually $[\log|P|-c]$ there. Hence $DD\psi_N\to\delta_{\operatorname{div}P}$. Continuous multiplier convergence CLD8.5.1, used ONLY for bounded continuous $u-v$, gives
\[
 DD((u-v)\delta_{\operatorname{div}P})=0
\]
on that fixed inner neighbourhood. This is an ambient divisor-current identity. It is not an unbounded restriction or a proof of curve harmonicity for every analytic curve.

The newer classical bridge is valid, with the following exact source scope. Definition3.4 uses separated pure-dimensional analytic curves over nontrivially valued fields and includes the identically-minus-infinite alternative. One can therefore prove the ALL-stage continuous recognition without assuming a first-stage identity. On a compact neighbourhood take classical psh $h_j$ uniformly approaching continuous $h$, and pass to a subsequence with error at most $2^{-j}$. Then $g_j=h_j+3\cdot2^{-j}$ decreases: $h_{j+1}-h_j\leq 3\cdot2^{-j-1}$, precisely the decrease of the added constants. Its limit is $h$. The source's decreasing closure proves uniform closure of the finite continuous classical sheaf. Minimality places the complete continuous CLD sheaf P in it. Hence every generated local weight $\rho+u$ is classically psh, including at poles, by the same decreasing closure. The new limits and infinite sums from n2--n3 are thus actual classically psh analytic representatives.

The source's Theorem6.4 states the affinoid Shilov maximum principle for an USC classical psh function, with arbitrary complete fields and affinoid radii: $\max_A q=\max_{\Gamma_A}q$ for affinoid $A$. Its complete immediate proof uses Theorem6.1 (local maximum), Lemma5.4 (curve connectivity) and Proposition6.3 (boundary strata); all were read. On original PDF page33 the proof calls $q$ continuous, but the closed maximum set needed there is also closed for USC $q$, the stated hypothesis. Original pages3,33,34 were viewed. This repairs that wording locally without strengthening the theorem's hypothesis.

Here are independently checked consequences, not a fixed skeleton assertion. For every point and all arbitrarily small affinoid neighbourhoods contained in a local weight chart,
\[
 q(x)=\inf_{A\ni x\text{ neighbourhood}}\max_{\Gamma_A}q.
\]
The inequality from below follows from the maximum principle. If $q(x)$ is finite, USC supplies a neighbourhood where $q<q(x)+\eta$, and an affinoid neighbourhood therein gives the reverse inequality as $\eta\to0$. If $q(x)=-\infty$, use every finite bound instead. Thus values on the union of ALL these Shilov boundaries determine pointwise weights and their order.

There is also a Hartogs upper lemma. Suppose classical psh $q_j$ are defined near compact $Q$ and $h$ is finite continuous. If $\limsup_jq_j(\xi)\leq h(\xi)$ for every Shilov point of every sufficiently small affinoid neighbourhood, then eventually $q_j\leq h+\eta$ on $Q$. Choose a finite affinoid cover of $Q$ on each member of which the oscillation of $h$ is less than $\eta/3$. Each finite Shilov boundary has all $q_j(\xi)\leq h(\xi)+\eta/3$ eventually; one late index works for the finite cover. The maximum principle then gives the assertion (with room in the constants). The same proof works for a net using finite directed upper bounds. This is upper control only.

Wanner, arXiv:1801.04713v3, 8 November2018, Theorem4.7, states that a continuous function on an open subset of a smooth PROPER algebraic curve over a complete ALGEBRAICALLY CLOSED NONTRIVIALLY VALUED field is Thuillier-subharmonic if and only if its CLD Hessian current is positive. Properness is the standing hypothesis of Section4, explicitly checked at LF793--810. Its full immediate six-step proof was read, together with Definitions2.4,3.18 and the necessary smooth recognition/pullback results3.20--21 and approximation4.6. ROOT394's minimality bridge on those curve tests is valid at exactly these hypotheses. GR's all-extension definition avoids limiting classical recognition to these algebraic tests. Neither theorem transports the surface energy/current identity to a larger field or proves that ambient divisor tests recover all tests on an arbitrary curve.

The retained ROOT394 bridge itself has a short independent proof. Our local weight charts are open torus analytifications, so smooth ambient psh weights pull back to smooth psh weights on the stated algebraic curve tests by Wanner3.21, including after the stated field extension. Among finite continuous ambient functions, those whose pullbacks have positive curve Hessian currents form a sheaf closed under local uniform limits: uniform pullback convergence and continuous coefficient/current differentiation give weak Hessian convergence, and positivity is closed. This sheaf contains the smooth psh weights, so minimality contains every stage of P. Wanner4.7 recognizes these CONTINUOUS curve pullbacks. The decreasing extended-weight conclusion follows from the GR decreasing closure at those same curve tests, including the identically-minus-infinite case. This uses functorial pullback of functions and smooth tropicalizations, not a base-change formula for surface products or energy.

The separation and compactness gates therefore remain. We have not shown that $DD((u-v)\delta_{\operatorname{div}P})=0$ implies that every curve pullback of $u-v$ is harmonic, or that zero pairings determine an actual generated difference pointwise modulo constants. Such an argument must prove the divisor restriction, test-extension and any base-change steps; unbounded differences additionally need divisor integrability and null-set removability. Shilov reconstruction would identify weights if equality on ALL its boundary values were proved, but equal MA supplies no such equality yet. Upper boundary control supplies neither lower control nor uniform integrability of a general energy sublevel. These are precise unresolved steps, not claimed counterexamples to P16.7.
