The paper proves sufficiency by upper tails and exact integration, and proves necessity interface R independently using exact local smooth potentials, weighted L² methods, coherent generation, and finite relative kernel reduction. This is a functional-analytic characterization of arbitrary input ideals; it is not “admissible means already realizable,” a finite algorithm, or a purely algebraic axiom system.
