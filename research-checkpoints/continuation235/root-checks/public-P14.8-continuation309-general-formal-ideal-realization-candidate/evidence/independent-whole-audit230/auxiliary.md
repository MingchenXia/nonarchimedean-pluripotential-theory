Direct Cantor/raw-mass obstruction. Begin with [0,1], ell_0=1 and ell_k=exp(-4^k), k>=1. Inside each level-k interval place two next-level intervals at its endpoints. Their lengths have 2ell_(k+1)<ell_k, so this gives a nonempty compact endpoint Cantor set F. The Bernoulli probability mu gives each level-k interval mass2^(-k), is supported on F, and has no atoms because any point's masses are bounded by2^(-k)->0. F has planar area0 (already it lies on a real segment).

For x in F, its containing level-k interval gives at least2^(-k) measure within ell_k. For t in [4^(k-1),4^k], the ball radius exp(-t) is at least ell_k. Layer-cake for the nonnegative kernel -log|x-p| on [0,1] yields
integral -log|x-p| dmu(p) >= sum_(k>=1)(4^k-4^(k-1))2^(-k)
=sum_(k>=1)3*2^(k-2)=infinity.
Endpoints of the t-intervals do not affect the integral. The squared chordal kernel is
