Definition 2.5. A (real) semivaluation v on X is a map v : R → R ∪ {∞} such that
   (1) v(f + g) ≥ min{v(f ), v(g)}, ∀f, g ∈ R;
   (2) v(f g) = v(f ) + v(g), ∀f, g ∈ R;
