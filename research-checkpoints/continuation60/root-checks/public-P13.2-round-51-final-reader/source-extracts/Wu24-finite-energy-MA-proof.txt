Proposition 6.2. The space E 1 (ξ) satisfies the following:
   (1) If ϕ ∈ E 1 (ξ) and c ∈ R, then ϕ + c ∈ E 1 (ξ).
   (2) E 1 (ξ) is convex.
   (3) If ϕ ≤ ψ and ϕ ∈ E 1 (ξ), then ψ ∈ E 1 (ξ).
