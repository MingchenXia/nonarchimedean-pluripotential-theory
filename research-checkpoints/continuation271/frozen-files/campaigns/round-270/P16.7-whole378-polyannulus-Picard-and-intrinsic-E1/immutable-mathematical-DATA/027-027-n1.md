Retained hypotheses and construction. Let K=C((s)), |s|=exp(-1/8), t=s^8, and v=-log|·|, so v(t)=1. This is a complete nontrivially discretely valued field of characteristic and residue characteristic zero. Let T=(G_m^2)^an_K, with coordinates z_1,z_2. Set λ_1=(t,2), λ_2=(3,t), and λ(n)=(t^{n_1}3^{n_2},2^{n_1}t^{n_2}) for n∈Z². For x_j=-log|z_j|, multiplication by λ(n) changes x to x+n.

Existence and geometry of the analytic quotient. If B⊂R² is an ordinary open rectangle with side lengths less than one, U_B=trop^{-1}(B) is disjoint from every nonidentity lattice translate. Consequently its image in the set quotient is an ordinary open chart. Overlap identifications are analytic coordinate multiplications by λ(n), and satisfy the cocycle identity λ(n+m)=λ(n)λ(m). Gluing these ordinary open analytic charts constructs X=T/Λ and its analytic sheaf; the projection is a topological covering and local analytic isomorphism.

For compact C,D⊂T, an intersection λ(n)C∩D requires n∈trop(D)-trop(C), a bounded set containing only finitely many integer vectors. Relatively compact neighborhoods and separation of these finitely many translations separate distinct orbits, so X is Hausdorff. The analytic equivalence relation is a locally finite union of closed translation graphs; in the disjoint-sheet charts the diagonal is a closed immersion. Thus X is analytically separated. The compact strict polyannulus |t|≤|z_j|≤1 surjects onto X, establishing compactness. Local charts are ordinary opens of the analytic torus, establishing smoothness, pure dimension two and absence of relative boundary. Strict affinoid neighborhoods are obtained using tropical rectangles with grid endpoints in (1/8)Z, chosen with the point in their central half. Hence X is good and strict. Compactness, separatedness and empty relative boundary establish properness. Integral Laurent-series algebras make the exhausting polyannuli connected; the same argument survives complete field extension. The quotient is therefore geometrically connected.

There is a continuous tropical map τ:X→S=R²/Z² and a closed Gauss section i:S→X. On T, the Gauss norm at x is η_x(Σ_m a_m z^m)=max_m |a_m|exp(-m·x). Its associated graded Laurent algebra is integral, giving multiplicativity. Evaluation gives continuity. Translation by λ(n) sends η_x to η_{x+n}, so the section descends. Since τi is the identity and S is compact while X is Hausdorff, its image is closed.

Universal elementary period obstruction. Explicitly define a positive integral period form to mean a homomorphism c:Λ→Z² satisfying λ_j^{c(λ_i)}=λ_i^{c(λ_j)}, with B_ij=v(λ_j^{c(λ_i)}) positive definite. Write c(λ_1)=(a,b), c(λ_2)=(c,d). Symmetry is 3^a t^b=t^c2^d. Valuation gives b=c. The remaining equality 3^a=2^d forces a=d=0 by unique prime factorization, including negative exponents after clearing denominators. Thus c(λ_1)=(0,k), c(λ_2)=(k,0) and B=[[0,k],[k,0]]. For k≠0 the eigenvalues have opposite signs; for k=0 the matrix vanishes. Neither sign convention gives positive definiteness. Clearing denominators proves the corresponding rational obstruction. Field extension does not change these identities between elements of K.
