L23: 【8†Lemma 33.27.4.】 Let $k$ be a field. Let $X$ be an algebraic $k$-scheme. Then there exists a finite purely inseparable extension $k'/k$ such that the normalization $Y$ of $X_{k'}$ is geometrically normal over $k'$.
L24: Proof. Let $K = k^{perf}$ be the perfect closure. Let $Y_ K$ be the normalization of $X_ K$, see Lemma 【15†33.27.1】. By Limits, Lemma 【16†32.10.1】 there exists a finite sub extension $K/k'/k$ and a morphism $\nu : Y \to X_{k'}$ of finite presentation whose base change to $K$ is the normalization morphism $\nu _ K : Y_ K \to X_ K$. Observe that $Y$ is geometrically normal over $k'$ (Lemma 【17†33.10.3】). After increasing $k'$ we may assume $Y \to X_{k'}$ is finite (Limits, Lemma 【18†32.8.3】).
L25: Since $\nu _ K : Y_ K \to X_ K$ is the normalization morphism, it induces a birational morphism $Y_ K \to (X_ K)_{red}$. Hence there is a dense open $V_ K \subset X_ K$ such that $\nu _ K^{-1}(V_ K) \to V_ K$ is a closed immersion (inducing an isomorphism of $\nu _ K^{-1}(V_ K)$ with $V_{K, red}$, see for example Morphisms, Lemma 【19†29.52.6】).
L26: After increasing $k'$ we find $V_ K$ is the base change of a dense open $V \subset Y$ and the morphism $\nu ^{-1}(V) \to V$ is a closed immersion (Limits, Lemmas 【20†32.4.11】 and 【21†32.8.5】). It follows readily from this that $\nu $ is the normalization morphism and the proof is complete. $\square$
