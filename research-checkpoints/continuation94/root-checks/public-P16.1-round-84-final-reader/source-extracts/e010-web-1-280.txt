L3@P0: Abstract. We generalize theorems of Deligne-Mumford and de Jong on semistable modifications of families of proper curves. The main result states that
L4@P0: after a generically ´etale alteration of the base any (not necessarily proper)
L5@P0: family of multipointed curves with semi-stable generic fiber admits a minimal
L6@P0: semi-stable modification. The latter can also be characterized by the property
L7@P0: that its geometric fibers have no certain exceptional components. The main
L8@P0: step of our proof is uniformization of one-dimensional extensions of valued
L9@P0: fields. Riemann-Zariski spaces are then used to obtain the result over any
L10@P0: integral base.

L247@P3: 1.3. Overview. Our proof of the stable modification theorem originates in nonArchimedean analytic geometry. Namely, the stable reduction theorem of DeligneMumford (in the form of Bosch-L¨utkebohmert [BL1]) was used in [Ber1, §4.3] and
L248@P3: [Ber2, §3.6] to give a local description of smooth analytic curves. On the other
L249@P3: hand, it was clear to experts that the stable reduction theorem is a consequence of
L250@P3: such a description and that the latter is actually of a very elementary nature and
L251@P3: would follow from a description of certain extensions of analytic fields (i.e. complete
L252@P3: valued fields of height one).
L253@P3: In general outline, our proof goes as follows. The core is Theorem 6.3.1 which
L254@P3: provides a description of extensions of analytic fields K/k with k algebraically closed
L255@P3: (or, more generally, deeply ramified in the sense of Assumption 6.2.1) and K the
L256@P3: completion of a finitely generated field of transcendence degree one over k. Since
L257@P3: some readers may be interested in following the algebra-geometric arguments with
L258@P3: taking the non-archimedean analytic results as a black box, we build our exposition
L259@P3: accordingly. In §2 we formulate the only analytic black box we need. This is
L260@P3: Theorem 2.1.10, which is a light version of Theorem 6.3.1. Then we postpone until
L261@P3: §6 any work involving non-archimedean analytic geometry. As a next step in the
L262@P3: paper, we deduce Theorem 2.1.8 from (temporarily black boxed) Theorem 2.1.10.
L263@P3: Theorem 2.1.8 is the main result of §2 and it describes general extensions of valued
L264@P3: fields of transcendence degree one.

L267@P3: theorem. We prove uniformization directly, thus obtaining a new proof of the stable
