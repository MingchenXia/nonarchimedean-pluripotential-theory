L22: 【8†Lemma 29.55.12.】 Let $X$ be an irreducible, geometrically unibranch scheme. The normalization morphism $\nu : X^\nu \to X$ is a universal homeomorphism.
L23: Proof. We have to show that $\nu $ is integral, universally injective, and surjective, see Lemma 【14†29.46.5】. By Lemma 【15†29.55.5】 the morphism $\nu $ is integral. Let $x \in X$ and set $A = \mathcal{O}_{X, x}$. Since $X$ is irreducible we see that $A$ has a single minimal prime $\mathfrak p$ and $A_{red} = A/\mathfrak p$. By Lemma 【16†29.55.4】 the stalk $A' = (\nu _*\mathcal{O}_{X^\nu })_ x$ is the integral closure of $A$ in the fraction field of $A_{red}$.
L24: By More on Algebra, Definition 【17†15.108.1】 we see that $A'$ has a single prime $\mathfrak m'$ lying over $\mathfrak m_ x \subset A$ and $\kappa (\mathfrak m')/\kappa (x)$ is purely inseparable. Hence $\nu $ is bijective (hence surjective) and universally injective by Lemma 【18†29.10.2】. $\square$
