L23: 【8†Lemma 10.163.2.】 Suppose that $R \to S$ is a flat and local ring homomorphism of Noetherian local rings. Then
L24: 
L25: \[ \text{depth}(S) = \text{depth}(R) + \text{depth}(S/\mathfrak m_ RS). \]
L26: 
L27: Proof. This is a special case of Lemma 【10†10.163.1】. $\square$
