L24: 【8†Lemma 37.53.6.】 Let $f : X \to S$ be a morphism of schemes. Assume
L25:   1. $f$ is proper,
L26: 
L27:   2. $S$ is integral with generic point $\xi $,
L28: 
L29:   3. $S$ is normal,
L30: 
L31:   4. $X$ is reduced,
L32: 
L33:   5. every generic point of an irreducible component of $X$ maps to $\xi $,
L34: 
L35:   6. we have $H^0(X_\xi , \mathcal{O}) = \kappa (\xi )$.
L36: 
L37: Then $f_*\mathcal{O}_ X = \mathcal{O}_ S$ and $f$ has geometrically connected fibres.
L38: Proof. Apply Theorem 【10†37.53.5】 to get a factorization $X \to S' \to S$. It is enough to show that $S' = S$. This will follow from Morphisms, Lemma 【15†29.55.8】. Namely, $S'$ is reduced because $X$ is reduced (Morphisms, Lemma 【16†29.54.8】). The morphism $S' \to S$ is integral by the theorem cited above. Every generic point of $S'$ lies over $\xi $ by Morphisms, Lemma 【17†29.54.9】 and assumption (5).
L39: On the other hand, since $S'$ is the relative spectrum of $f_*\mathcal{O}_ X$ we see that the scheme theoretic fibre $S'_\xi $ is the spectrum of $H^0(X_\xi , \mathcal{O})$ which is equal to $\kappa (\xi )$ by assumption. Hence $S'$ is an integral scheme with function field equal to the function field of $S$. This finishes the proof. $\square$
