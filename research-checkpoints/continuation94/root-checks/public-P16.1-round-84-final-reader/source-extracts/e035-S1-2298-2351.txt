
4.2. Basic properties. The next few results provide some basic but important properties
of θ-psh functions.
Theorem 4.7. For all θ, θ0 ∈ N1 (X) and t ∈ R>0 , we have:
     (i) PSH(θ) + PSH(θ0 ) ⊂ PSH(θ + θ0 ) and PSH(tθ) = t PSH(θ);
    (ii) the set PSH(θ) is convex, and stable under uniform limits, finite maxima, the additive
         action of R, and the scaling action of R>0 ;
   (iii) for any morphism f : Y → X and ϕ ∈ PSH(X, θ), either f ? ϕ ∈ PSH(Y, f ? θ) or
         f ? ϕ ≡ −∞ on some irreducible component of Y an ;
   (iv) for any ϕ ∈ PSH(θ) and any α, we have ϕ|Xαan ∈ PSH(Xα , θ|Xα ).
