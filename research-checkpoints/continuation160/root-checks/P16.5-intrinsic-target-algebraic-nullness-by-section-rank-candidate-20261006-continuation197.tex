\begin{abstract}
Under explicit genuine-envelope and section-trace energy foundations,
we bound the cost of a capped section obstacle by a volume deficit.
This constructs a genuine relative finite-energy potential with a pole
on any specified effective divisor. Consequently every intrinsic target
integrating all genuine relative finite-energy tests gives zero mass
to every proper algebraic subset. This is a conditional supporting
result; it proves no general negligible-set nullness, integral
continuity, variational attainment or full inverse equation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Fixed domain and conditional foundations}
Let $X$ be smooth integral projective of dimension $n\geq1$ over an
algebraically closed trivially valued characteristic-zero field.
Let $L$ be an actual big rational line bundle, $\theta=c_1(L)$,
$K=X^{\mathrm{an}}$, $v_0$ the generic trivial valuation, and
$V=\vol(L)>0$. Supported semivaluations are retained.

Assume the following exact foundations of the unreviewed complete
partial original-scope paper. They are not independent theorem grants.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The genuine numerical cone is convex and closed under
decreasing all-point limits with a finite generic endpoint. It has
envelopes $P_\theta(f)$ for every bounded continuous obstacle with a
genuine minorant. Its normalized reference $R=P_\theta(0)$ has
$R(v_0)=0$, $R\leq0$, and every normalized genuine function lies below $R$.
The actual finite FS section potentials on $L$ are genuine.
\item The raw energy is concave, constant-equivariant,
decreasing-continuous, and $E(R)=0$. For continuous $f$ it satisfies
$E(P_\theta(f))=\mathscr E(f)$, where the actual full-section filtration is
\begin{align*}
 W_m(f;s)&=\inf_K\{mf-\log|s|\},\qquad0\neq s\in H^0(X,mL),\\
 \mathscr E(f)&=\lim_m n!m^{-(n+1)}
                  \sum_j\lfloor W_m(f;s_{mj})\rfloor.
\end{align*}
The basis is adapted to the intrinsic full filtration. The limit holds
in cofinal admissible degrees. At a section pole the bracket is
$+\infty$, and this convention is part of the definition.
\end{enumerate}
The standard section-volume asymptotics for an actual rational big
bundle and continuity of ordinary volume in a fixed rational direction
are ordinary algebraic background. They do not assert a nonample
analytic equation theorem.

An intrinsic target here is a positive Radon measure $\nu$ of raw mass
$V$ satisfying
\[
 \nu(\{R=-\infty\})=0,\qquad
 \int(R-u)\,d\nu<\infty
 \quad\text{for every normalized genuine }u\in\Eone(\theta).
                                                        \tag{1}
\]
No solution or Monge--Amp\`ere representation of $\nu$ is assumed.

\section{A capped section obstacle with small energy cost}
Let $D$ be an actual effective integral Cartier divisor with nonzero
canonical section $s_D$. In the trivial reference convention put
$a_D=-\log|s_D|\in[0,+\infty]$, which is finite continuous off
$D^{\mathrm{an}}$ and infinite on it. Its bounded caps are ordinary
continuous functions on $K$. Fix rational $t>0$ so that $L-tD$ is big,
and an integer $N\geq1$. Define
\[
 f_{t,N}=-\min(t a_D,N),\qquad u_{t,N}=P_\theta(f_{t,N}).
                                                        \tag{2}
\]
The obstacle is bounded continuous. Since $R-N\leq -N\leq f_{t,N}$,
it has a genuine minorant.

\begin{lemma}\label{cost}
The envelope in (2) is normalized, satisfies
$R-N\leq u_{t,N}\leq R$ and $u_{t,N}\leq-N$ on $D^{\mathrm{an}}$,
and belongs to the genuine $\Eone(\theta)$. Its raw cost obeys
\[
       0\leq -E(u_{t,N})
            \leq N\bigl(V-\vol(L-tD)\bigr).             \tag{3}
\]
For each fixed $N$ the upper bound tends to zero as rational $t\downarrow0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take degrees clearing $L,tD$ and containing nonzero sections of
$m(L-tD)$. For every nonzero $s\in H^0(X,mL)$, the trivial section
norm is at most one and equals one at $v_0$. Since $-N\leq f_{t,N}\leq0$
and $f_{t,N}(v_0)=0$, its filtration weight lies in $[-mN,0]$.

The actual injection
\[
 s_D^{mt}:H^0(X,m(L-tD))\hookrightarrow H^0(X,mL)          \tag{4}
\]
has every nonzero image section of weight zero. Indeed its logarithmic
norm is $\log|s'|-mt a_D\leq-mt a_D\leq m f_{t,N}$ at all points;
at $v_0$ both sides are zero. At a supported pole the first expression
is $-\infty$, so the obstacle inequality still holds with its literal
finite cap $-mN$. It follows that the image lies in the zero-weight
subspace of the full filtration, with its actual dimension unchanged.

Write $r_m=h^0(X,mL)$ and $r'_m=h^0(X,m(L-tD))$. An adapted basis
therefore has at least $r'_m$ zero weights and all its other weights
at least $-mN$. Since $mN$ is integral, flooring preserves this lower
bound. Consequently
\[
 n!m^{-(n+1)}\sum_j\lfloor W_m(f_{t,N};s_{mj})\rfloor
          \geq -N\frac{n!}{m^n}(r_m-r'_m).
\]
Full-section volume asymptotics and the exact envelope-energy identity
give (3). Nonnegative cost follows from $u_{t,N}\leq R$.

The same nonzero core sections in (4) define genuine FS minorants of
$f_{t,N}$ with value zero at $v_0$. Thus $u_{t,N}(v_0)=0$;
the envelope is not normalized by a shift that would alter its cap on
the divisor. The lower bound $R-N$ gives finite energy as well.
The pointwise envelope inequality yields $u_{t,N}\leq-N$ on
$D^{\mathrm{an}}$. Finally ordinary volume continuity yields the last
claim. These steps use actual line bundles and rank, not false
multilinearity of big positive products.
\end{proof}

\section{A finite-energy pole and target algebraic nullness}
\begin{theorem}\label{null}
There is a normalized genuine $w_D\in\Eone(\theta)$ with
$w_D=-\infty$ on $D^{\mathrm{an}}$. Every target in (1) therefore
satisfies $\nu(D^{\mathrm{an}})=0$, and in fact gives zero mass to
every proper algebraic closed subset of $X$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set $\lambda_j=2^{-j}$ and $N_j=4^j$. By Lemma~\ref{cost} choose
rational $t_j>0$ with $L-t_jD$ big and
\[
 N_j\bigl(V-\vol(L-t_jD)\bigr)\leq2^{-j}.
\]
Write $u_j=u_{t_j,N_j}$ and define actual finite convex combinations
\[
 w_q=\left(1-\sum_{j=1}^q\lambda_j\right)R
                                  +\sum_{j=1}^q\lambda_j u_j.
                                                        \tag{5}
\]
They are genuine, normalized and decrease all-point since $u_j\leq R$.
Concavity gives
\[
 E(w_q)\geq-\sum_{j=1}^q\lambda_j2^{-j}\geq-\frac13.
\]
The genuine decreasing-closure hypothesis applies with generic endpoint
zero. Its limit $w_D$ is genuine, and decreasing energy continuity
gives $E(w_D)\geq-1/3$. Thus it is an actual finite-energy function;
no abstract completion point is introduced.

On $D^{\mathrm{an}}$ each $u_j\leq-N_j$, and $R\leq0$, so
\[
        w_q\leq-\sum_{j=1}^q\lambda_jN_j
                     =-\sum_{j=1}^q2^j\longrightarrow-\infty.
\]
Where $R$ is finite, the relative cost $R-w_D$ is therefore infinite.
Its integral is finite by (1), which implies
$\nu(D^{\mathrm{an}}\setminus\{R=-\infty\})=0$. The first condition
in (1) handles the remaining set, proving divisor nullness without
assuming that $R$ is absolutely integrable.

For a proper algebraic closed subset $Y\subset X$, a sufficiently high
power of an actual ample bundle has a nonzero section vanishing on $Y$:
the ideal sheaf of $Y$ twisted by that power is globally generated,
and $X$ is integral. Its zero divisor contains $Y$. The proved divisor
nullness then implies $\nu(Y^{\mathrm{an}})=0$.
\end{proof}
