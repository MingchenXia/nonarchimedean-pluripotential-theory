# A corrected normalization route; not an accepted energy comparison theorem

This is a fresh root research deduction, unreviewed and not sent to either
active native task. It is not a paper or an original problem closure.

The actual source is Yueqiao Wu, arXiv:2106.13395v2. Its Lemma 3.16(b),
LF688–755, proves for a primitive integral Reeb field that the cumulative
filtration average is

    S(F,xi) = n/(n+1) * S_tilde(F),

where S_tilde is the degree-wise average for the projective quotient.
Its Corollary 5.14, LF1342–1354, identifies the analytic cone energy with
the projective energy with denominator n vol(L). These two displayed
formulas are incompatible with the chain `S_tilde(F)=S(F)` in the proof
of Theorem 5.15 at LF1366–1369. The constant-shift example is therefore
explained by a specific omitted factor, not merely a numerical anomaly.

For a nonnegative finite-type filtration for which the projective FS
metric has energy S_tilde(F), the two intermediate source statements
would give instead

    E_xi(phi_F) = (n+1)/n * S(F,xi)

for primitive integral xi. Scaling extends this equality to rational
Reeb fields. Extending it to irrational xi additionally requires genuine
continuity of the analytic energy for a fixed finite presentation, and
source Proposition 3.15 for S. The proof of Theorem 5.15 asserts the first
continuity, but its proof and its hypotheses have not been reconstructed
in this note. The quotient identification and the metric/norm identity
also remain application obligations. The formula is thus a research
route, not a proved replacement theorem in this record.

## Why the nonnegative comparison alone does not establish E1 transport

For a normalized potential phi<=rho_xi, the natural signed norm weights
can be nonpositive. Negating a multiplicative filtration weight need not
give another multiplicative filtration. Consequently it is invalid to
apply the source's nonnegative-filtration theorem simply to its negative.

A possible repair is to allow a real-indexed, linearly bounded signed
norm chi. For each Reeb field eta choose A so that

    chi_A(f_alpha)=chi(f_alpha)+A<eta,alpha> >= 0.

If this is a legitimate finite-type norm and all source applications are
checked, the associated FS function at eta is phi_eta+A. On the finite
dimensional cutoff R_eta(m), jumping sums are additive under this
homogeneous degree shift. The degree norm itself has cumulative average
n/(n+1), so the corrected formula and energy translation would cancel
the A and yield the signed identity

    E_eta(phi_eta) = (n+1)/n * S_signed(chi,eta).

This requires proving the signed cutoff limit, the shift applicability,
the exact FS/norm correspondence, and density for all FS presentations.
It is not established just by writing a formal difference of limits.

If every homogeneous weight of chi is nonpositive, minus its jumping sum
is a sum of nonnegative quantities on each cutoff. For degree ratios
l<=<zeta,alpha>/<xi,alpha><=u, restriction to homogeneous weight spaces
then gives

    B_xi(m/u) <= B_zeta(m) <= B_xi(m/l),

where B_eta(m) is minus the signed jumping sum. Combining this with
u^(-n)V_xi<=V_zeta<=l^(-n)V_xi would give the same two-sided factors as
the nonnegative cumulative comparison:

    l^n/u^(n+1) <= (-E_zeta(phi_zeta))/(-E_xi(phi_xi))
                  <= u^n/l^(n+1),

when the denominator is nonzero and all the preceding identities are
proved. This could survive decreasing approximation and control E1,
but neither the full signed representation nor that passage is proved
here. Uniform energy differentiation is a further obligation even if
these bounds are established.
