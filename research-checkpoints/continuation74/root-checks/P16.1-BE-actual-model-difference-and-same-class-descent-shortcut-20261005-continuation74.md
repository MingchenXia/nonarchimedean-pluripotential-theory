## 1. The exact primary proof already supplies a FS difference

In the authenticated Boucksom--Eriksson arXiv:1805.01016v3 extract, Theorem5.14 and its whole proof are LF1989--2007. Proposition5.16(vi), including its proof, is LF2017--2033. It states that pure PL metrics coincide with model metrics. For a rational model B on a projective model, choose any ample model line bundle A; for a sufficiently large integer r, B+rA and rA are ample. Their metrics are exact pure FS metrics, so metric(B)=metric(B+rA)-metric(rA).

The generic line bundle of A need not descend from k and need not be a power of a preselected ample L. No such assertion is used in this proof. This avoids the additional generic-triviality/ample-model domination check in the earlier proposed route. One still needs an actual projective algebraic model; GM proper algebraic/formal equivalence and projective domination provide that representation, with their hypotheses verified.

## 2. Same-class cancellation removes the descent issue

Apply this to a global rational model function F_K, which is a model metric on O_XK. The two pure FS metrics psi and psi' obtained above have the same generic rational line-bundle class M, although M itself may only be defined over K.

Now use actual BE Lemma5.17 with its whole proof, LF2036--2043. Choose an ample line bundle H on X over k and its base change H_K. The sums psi+tau and psi'+tau are pure FS metrics on the same line bundle aH_K, by the actual FS addition/product-section property. Their difference is exactly F_K because tau cancels.
