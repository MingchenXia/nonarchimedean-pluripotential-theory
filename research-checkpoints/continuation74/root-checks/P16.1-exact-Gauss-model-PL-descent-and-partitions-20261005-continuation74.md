# Exact model-PL Gauss descent, compact local extension and PL partitions

This is an unreviewed root proposal, not an accepted premise or a completed P16.1 proof. It targets the global PL extension and partition obligations in the local integration-by-parts and mass argument. The earlier strict rational representation and Gauss FS coefficient proofs must be reconstructed independently.

## 1. Extend an actual formal metric, not an unnamed local PL function

Fix a finite Laurent field K=k((t^(1/q))) with |t|=exp(-1). Suppose f is a rational finite PL function on an analytic open U of X^an over trivial k. Let W be a compact rational polydomain contained in U, preferably an analytic neighborhood of the compact set where agreement is required. Construct W using actual regular upper bounds after localization, so W_K is compact strictly analytic after clearing its finitely many rational thresholds. If a finite union is needed, it is still a compact strictly analytic domain. One must first prove f_K is actual Q-PL on a neighborhood of W_K, using the finite rational polyhedral branch cover; merely calling f relatively PL is insufficient.

Choose an integer n clearing the finitely many slopes and constants on this compact cover. Then n f_K is the function of a formal metric on the trivial bundle on W_K, by GM2.10. GM Proposition2.7 (literal LF277--287) extends this formal metric to the trivial bundle on the paracompact strictly analytic X_K. Its proof extends the local line bundle as a coherent sheaf and makes it invertible after an admissible formal blowup. Dividing the function by n gives a global rational formal model function F_K agreeing with f_K on W_K. Positivity is not required for this extension.

GM Remark2.6 (LF271--275), with proper algebraic X_K, identifies a formal metric with an algebraic model metric. The canonical reference for any ample line bundle L on X comes from the product model over K's valuation ring. Therefore F_K plus that reference is an actual rational algebraic model metric B of L on a common model. No G_m action on the K-model is assumed at this point.

## 2. Express any rational model metric as a difference of pure FS metrics

Use the actual ample-model domination statement GM Proposition4.11 (LF931--945): for projective X_K and ample L, a suitable projective model dominating the model carrying B admits an ample line bundle A whose generic restriction is L^m for some positive integer m. Pull B and the reference to that model. After clearing B's denominator, choose a positive integer r sufficiently large that r A+B is ample as a rational line bundle on the projective model; r A is ample too. Suitable integral multiples of each are relatively globally generated. Their finite integral section systems give exact pure FS model metrics on the corresponding generic rational line bundles by BE Theorem5.14, the same correspondence used in the proof of BE7.8.

Consequently the original model metric is exactly

    metric(B)=metric(r A+B)-metric(r A),

with generic line bundles (rm+1)L and rmL, after maintaining the cleared rational tensor factors. This is an exact equality, not uniform approximation, and B need not be nef. The finite section systems and their pure FS expressions must actually be exhibited or proved to exist with global generation on the model. The discrete Laurent valuation ring is noetherian, so ordinary relative ample twisting and coherent section finiteness apply.

Check the ample-model domination proof carefully. The printed GM Lemma4.12 argument introduces a relatively ample bundle E without explicitly saying why its generic restriction is trivial. Do not assert that an arbitrary choice of E has this property. For the needed domination result one may choose an additional projective model obtained by blowing up a coherent vertical ideal on a standard ample model; its relative exceptional bundle is trivial on the generic fiber. A sufficiently large positive multiple of the pullback ample bundle minus the exceptional divisor is then ample and has generic restriction a power of L. The actual vertical-blowup domination/indeterminacy step must be proved or used with its precise standard noetherian hypotheses. It is enough to construct this suitable dominating model; no claim about every preassigned model is needed.

## 3. The Gauss pullback is exact algebraic PL over k

Apply the continuation71 exact FS Gauss coefficient argument separately to the two pure FS metrics. A generic section over K is a finite linear combination of a k-basis with Laurent series coefficients, and hence has an expansion sum t^(r/q) s_r with s_r global sections over k. At the Gauss section the norm is the maximum of exp(-r/q)|s_r|. The canonical trivial-reference bound |s_r|<=1, compactness and a finite lower bound for the FS metric permit a finite exact cutoff. The retained sections globally generate; otherwise the pulled FS expression would be minus infinity at a point although the original metric is finite. Pure model constants are in the rational value group, so the retained weights are rational.

Subtract the appropriate multiples of the same canonical reference on both sides. The generic classes differ by L, and the reference terms cancel in their difference. Thus F_K composed with the Gauss section is a difference of two actual rational global FS potentials over k, and lies in BJ's PL(X). This produces an actual flag-ideal/test-configuration PL representation without assuming that the initial K-model was equivariant.

On W, the projection and section satisfy pi sigma=id, so this global algebraic PL function agrees exactly with f. This is the compact local extension statement required by the integration-by-parts argument. Agreement near a compact test support requires W to be a neighborhood of that support; the choice of W is part of the statement. The construction does not give agreement on the whole original arbitrary analytic open, and no such agreement is needed.

The same exact descent also shows that the continuous rational collar metrics constructed over K have actual algebraic PL potentials over k, in addition to the genuine CPSH membership obtained from curve-detected nefness. Positivity of the FS difference is a separate assertion; exact PL membership alone never supplies it.

## 4. Construct a subordinate PL partition from the actual BJ lattice

BJ Definition2.1 and Lemma2.3 (LF971--1003) give a rational vector lattice PL(X) containing constants and separating points. Theorem2.2 gives its uniform density in C^0(X^an). These statements retain reduced possibly nonnormal projective X and are statements about global algebraic PL functions.

For a finite analytic open cover U_1,...,U_N of compact X^an, choose compact sets K_i contained in U_i covering X^an, by the finite shrinking lemma for compact Hausdorff spaces. Choose continuous functions a_i with 0<=a_i<=1, a_i=1 on K_i, and support compactly contained in U_i. Approximate each a_i by p_i in PL(X) with uniform error less than 1/8. Set

    chi_i=min(1,max(0,2 p_i-1/2)).

The rational vector-lattice operations keep chi_i in actual PL(X). It equals one on K_i and zero outside U_i; moreover its support is contained in the compact set {p_i>=1/4}, which lies inside U_i because p_i<=1/8 outside U_i. Thus max_i chi_i=1 pointwise.

Define successively

    rho_1=chi_1,
    rho_i=min(chi_i,1-sum_(j<i) rho_j) for i>=2.

Inductively all rho_i are between zero and one, their supports are contained in U_i, and the partial sum is min(1,sum_(j<=i) chi_j). Since the total chi sum is at least one, sum_i rho_i=1. Every rho_i is global rational algebraic PL. This avoids normalizing by a variable denominator, which would leave the PL class, and avoids assuming an arbitrary analytic open cover is exactly the inverse image of a finite toric open cover.

The same density and clipping construction gives a global PL cutoff equal to one on any prescribed compact test support and supported inside its analytic neighborhood.

## 5. What follows for local IBP, and what remains

For local rational PL functions f_1,...,f_n and compactly supported rational PL test g on U, choose compact domain neighborhoods of supp(g) and use the exact extension above. Extend g by zero outside U, verifying its whole zero collar, or use a cutoff-supported extension. The actual global model/intersection integration-by-parts and locality, with signs and raw degrees checked, then give the compact local PL identity. This application does not use an already available singular Dirac equation or singular EP.

To pass from PL functions to bounded decreasing local functions, the genuine common global representation, weighted mixed decreasing continuity, intrinsic overlap/approximation compatibility and cutoff integrability still must be established. The partition then reduces the total-mass proof to summing compact IBP identities: the global potential difference is paired with ddc(sum rho_i)=ddc1=0. Locality must remove all terms supported away from the cutoffs before this sum is taken. This is a proposed reconstruction of Wu4.12--4.14, not permission to omit those continuity and compatibility proofs.

After these gates close, comparison with the correct winning-locus measure and domination using the already constructed positive model atoms can be reconstructed. Weak-input trace extension, singular Runge and envelope regularity remain subsequent obligations to the original P16.1 equation.
