## G1: finite sections and a primary correction

By Stacks30.16.1/3, T=H^0(Y,O_Y) and every coherent sheaf's global sections are finite R-modules. Localization of H^0 follows directly from a finite affine cover of the separated, quasi-compact scheme Y: global sections are the kernel of the difference map from the finite product of affine section modules to the finite product of section modules on pairwise intersections. These intersections are affine. Localization is exact and commutes with those finite products, giving H^0(Y,F)_r=H^0(Y_r,F_r) for every r in R.

Generic restriction embeds T into the total quotient ring of R. Indeed, each primary blowup chart is a subring of a localization R_f, where its tautological generator f is regular, and all generic components of R are retained by the whole blowup. A section vanishing at every generic point is zero on the reduced scheme Y. Thus T contains R and is a finite R-algebra. On every open of the punctured local spectrum it equals R, by the isomorphism there and the localization identity. The finite module T/R is supported at m, so m^h(T/R)=0 for some h. Set c0=m^(h+1). Then c0 T is contained in m AS ACTUAL ELEMENTS OF R. This primary correction concerns this blowup, not the full normalization's potentially nonprimary conductor.

## G3, integral and rational nef plus ample

## G3, real curve-nef perturbation without an assumed real Kleiman criterion

Fix a finite Cartier span on Y generated by integral divisors L_1,...,L_r and H=-E. For each i, G2 gives an integer N_i>=1 such that N_i H+L_i and N_i H-L_i are relatively ample. On every contracted integral curve C,

    |deg_C L_i| <= N_i deg_C H.

Let D=sum_i d_i L_i be any real curve-nef divisor in this span, and let delta>0 be rational. Choose rational d'_i with sum_i N_i |d_i-d'_i| < delta/4. Put D_r=sum_i d'_i L_i, e_i=d_i-d'_i and F=D_r+(delta/4)H. The displayed curve bound proves F rational curve-nef. Moreover

    D+delta H-F
      = (3delta/4-sum_i N_i|e_i|)H
        +sum_{e_i != 0}|e_i|(N_i H+sign(e_i)L_i).

The right-hand side is a positive real combination of actual integral relatively ample divisors; its H coefficient is greater than delta/2. Call it S. The preceding rational-nef-plus-real-ample result gives D+delta H=F+S real ample. For arbitrary real delta>0, choose rational 0<delta0<delta, apply the result at delta0 and add (delta-delta0)H. Thus real curve-nef plus EVERY positive ample H perturbation is ample on Y, without asserting integral approximation of the nef boundary.

Ampleness is open in each fixed finite Cartier coefficient span. For an integral ample class A choose N_i with N_i A +/- L_i ample as in G2. If sum_i N_i |e_i|<1, the explicit expression

    A+sum_i e_i L_i
      = (1-sum_i N_i|e_i|)A
        +sum_{e_i != 0}|e_i|(N_i A+sign(e_i)L_i)

is real ample. For a general real ample S=sum_j s_j A_j, reserve one positive term s_1 A_1 to absorb small changes by this formula and retain all other terms. This proves the precise finite-span openness needed for rational coefficient rounding. Linear relations among the chosen generators cause no problem; we assert openness in their finite coefficient space, which suffices.

A rational Cartier divisor in this real ample cone is genuinely rational ample. To check this instead of assuming it, take the finitely generated abelian subgroup of Pic(Y) generated by its integral constituents and the finitely many ample line bundles in a real ample presentation. Modulo its finite torsion this is a free abelian group of finite rank. The equation expressing the given rational class as a positive real combination of those ample classes is consequently a linear system with rational coefficients and rational right-hand side.
