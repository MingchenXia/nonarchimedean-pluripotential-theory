                                     Notation and conventions
   Throughout the paper, we work over a non-Archimedean field K, i.e. a field complete with
respect to a non-Archimedean absolute value | · |, which might be the trivial absolute value. The
corresponding valuation is denoted by vK := − log | · |. The valuation ring, maximal ideal and
residue field are respectively denoted by
              K ◦ := {a ∈ K | |a| ≤ 1},       K ◦◦ := {a ∈ K | |a| < 1},              K̃ := K ◦ /K ◦◦ .
If X is a scheme of finite type over K, we denote by X an its Berkovich analytification. The
space of continuous, real valued functions on X an is denoted by C 0 (X an ).
   We use additive notation for line bundles and metrics. If L, M are line bundles on X endowed
with metrics φ and ψ respectively, then L + M denotes the tensor product of the line bundles
and φ + ψ the induced metric. The norm on L associated to φ is denoted by | · |φ and k · kφ is
the associated supnorm on H 0 (X, L), which is a norm if X is reduced. See §1.2 for more details.
   For line bundles L1 , . . . , Ln on an n-dimensional projective scheme X over a field, we use
(L1 · . . . · Ln ) for the intersection number of the first Chern classes of L1 , . . . , Ln . Usually, we
will have L = L1 = · · · = Ln and we then simply write (Ln ) for this intersection number, which
agrees with the degree of X with respect to L.
