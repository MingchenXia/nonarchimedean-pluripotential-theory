1.1. The Berkovich analytification. Here we note some facts about the Berkovich ana-

1.1.1. The space of valuations. Assume first that X is irreducible, with function field k(X).
In this paper, a valuation on X means a real-valued valuation v : k(X)× → R, trivial on k.
The trivial valuation vtriv = vX,triv ∈ X val is defined by vtriv (f ) = 0 for all f ∈ k(X)× .

convergence on k(X)× . As soon as dim X ≥ 1, X val is not locally compact (see §1.1.6 below).

cX (v) ∈ X, characterized as the unique (scheme) point ξ ∈ X such that v ≥ 0 on the local
