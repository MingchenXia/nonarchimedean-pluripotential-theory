ample rational polarization H have the envelope property in c1(H).

The proof of this premise consists of the entire original-reference

The second premise is the precise realization theorem under QEP in

L1 continuity, and the fixed-Reeb image theorem for every rational or

general-variety primary-source and binding corrections stated there.

Write K=X_0^T, retain the original cone probability energy E_xi and operator
MA_xi, and normalize potentials by their prescribed supremum. For a

    L_xi(mu) = sup_{h in H(xi)} (E_xi(h) - integral_K h dmu).

Here H(xi) is the actual finite Fubini--Study class of the original cone

    mu lies in MA_xi(E1(xi)/R) if and only if L_xi(mu) is finite.

Equivalently, mu integrates every actual E1(xi) potential. The solution is

## 2. The actual quotient is an EP_U target

Choose finitely many nonconstant T-homogeneous generators of the coordinate
ring A. Strict positivity on their weights defines a nonempty open rational
cone: the given xi satisfies every inequality. Choose a rational eta in
that cone, clear its denominators, and divide the supported grading by its
gcd. This gives eta=s eta_e, where s is a positive rational number and
eta_e gives an effective positive integral grading

    A = direct_sum_{m>=0} A_m,    A_0=k.

Use the prescribed normal-Veronese quotient construction to form Y=Proj A
and choose a sufficiently divisible d such that the actual sheaf
L_d=O_Y(d) is invertible and ample. The variety Y is integral and projective
of dimension p=dim(X)-1. These are the original quotient and actual bundle,
with their original residual action and reference; no resolution or other
birational model is substituted.

For completeness, normality is checked on each chart D_+(f). The ring A_f
is normal. If z in Frac((A_f)_0) is integral over (A_f)_0, its integral
equation also makes it integral over A_f; hence z belongs to A_f. A ratio
of degree-zero elements is invariant under the grading action. The direct
graded decomposition of A_f therefore puts z in (A_f)_0. Thus every chart
ring (A_f)_0 is a normal domain.

At every point y of Y, the local ring is a normal domain. Its reduction and
its integral closure in its fraction field are that same ring, and the
residue-field extension is the identity. It is thus geometrically
unibranch, by the definition in Stacks Tag 0BPZ and the complete proof of

Consequently Y, k and L_d satisfy every hypothesis of EP_U on the original

## 4. Application to the original image theorem

Sections2--3 discharge QEP for the very quotient used in the conditional
realization proof. That proof can now be applied without an additional
envelope assumption on the cone. In particular its compactness limit is
an actual psh potential; energy upper semicontinuity makes the limit E1;
finite-target L1 convergence makes the variational supremum attained.
The fixed-xi outer derivative for both signs of dense continuous test
directions gives the genuine equality MA_xi(w)=mu.

The passage from eta to xi uses the universal root homeomorphism, energy
bounds, finite-energy orbit measures and the finite-grid/tail argument.
It does not transport Monge--Ampere measures between different Reeb
fields. There is no solution manufactured by completing an I-Cauchy
sequence. Necessity and uniqueness use the actual fixed-xi tangent and
embedding theorems of the same original theory.

For every normalized actual E1 maximizing sequence w_j with deficit d_j,
the established midpoint argument gives

    I_xi(w_j,w) <= d_j/(2a_p),    a_p=p/4^(p+1),    p>=1.

For p=1 this is I<=8d_j. For n=1, the normal projective quotient is the
one point Spec k and EP is immediate; the original cone theory gives the
calibrated one-point carrier and its unique probability. No a_0 is used.
