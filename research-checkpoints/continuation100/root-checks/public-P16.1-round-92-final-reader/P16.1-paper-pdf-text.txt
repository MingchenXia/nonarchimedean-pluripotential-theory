Abstract
We establish the retained domain of original P16.1: reduced irreducible geometrically unibranch projective varieties
over an arbitrary algebraically closed trivially valued field of characteristic zero, polarized by an actual ample
rational line bundle. The conclusion concerns the genuine intersection-theoretic finite-energy Monge–Ampère
operator and all positive Radon targets of its intersection mass and finite intrinsic dual energy, with arbitrary
support. The new deduction is original-target envelope regularity. We prove the application joining the specified
accepted all-point fiber theorem, actual-reference local conversion, geometric witness construction, and repaired
countable original-ring affine theorem. First genuine membership on the original variety precedes envelope
regularity and every use of onto birational descent. A separate countable field for each finite competitor removes
countability. The general-variety energy theory then gives an actually attained variational maximizer and its
equation, with both derivative signs. Normalization, mixed products and unbounded limits preserve the original
cycle and raw mass.
