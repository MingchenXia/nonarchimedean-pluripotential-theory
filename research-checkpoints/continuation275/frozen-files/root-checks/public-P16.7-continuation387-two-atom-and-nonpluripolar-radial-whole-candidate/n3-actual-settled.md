Let B contain every finite mixture from n2, with arbitrary positive real depths and additive constants. Fix the actual analytic point xi_(N0), 0<N0<infinity; it is not the rigid y. For T(t)=sum_(N_i>=t)c_i, 0<=T<=1, n2 and Tonelli give S=integral T^3 and -sum c_iD_(N_i)(xi_(N0))=integral_0^N0 T. Thus E(u)-2u(xi_(N0))=1/6+integral_0^N0(2T-T^3/3)-(1/3)integral_N0^infinity T^3. The first integrand is increasing on [0,1] with maximum 5/3; the second term is nonpositive. T=1 on (0,N0), zero after, attains the upper bound. Consequently sup_B(E(u)-2u(xi_(N0)))=1/6+5N0/3, attained by U_(N0).
