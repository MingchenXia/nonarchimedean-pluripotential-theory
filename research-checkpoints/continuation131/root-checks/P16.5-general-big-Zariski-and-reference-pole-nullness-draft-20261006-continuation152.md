# General big finite-energy operator: Zariski and reference pole nullness

This unreviewed supporting proof uses the proposed entire genuine operator of own150 and its coefficientwise deficient-measure tail bound. It proves that its outputs do not charge a proper Zariski closed subset, and in particular do not charge the minimal reference's poles. It does not prove nullness of every nonalgebraic psh pole set, absolute integrability of the reference, weighted relative limits, comparison or the full inverse. Original P16.5 remains open.

Let u be normalized genuine relative-E1 for the actual big rational L in own150, and put B=-E_theta(u)<infinity. Its defining cofinal model obstacles f_D>=u, f_D<=0 have E(D)>=-B. Use the full actual measures mu_D and deficient positive measures mu_D,c on the same original carrier, for each fixed c>B/V. Own150 gives

    0<=mu_D,c<=mu_D,
    ||mu_D-mu_D,c||_TV<=B/c,
    mu_D -> M_theta(u) against all original continuous functions. (1)

## 1. Uniform algebraic order moments for the deficient measures

Fix an actual nonzero section s of an actual line bundle on X, with effective zero divisor S. Use the fixed product model Z=X times P1, gamma=p_X^*L+cF, and a fixed ample Cartier T on Z. Choose a>0 with aT-p_X^*S nef, by taking a sufficiently ample multiple. On a refinement carrying D, the actual pulled divisor has the decomposition

    rho^*p_X^*S=S_hor+sum_i b_i x_i(s) E_i,

where S_hor is the effective horizontal strict transform with its actual multiplicities. For Q_D,c=<alpha_D(c)^n>, the C1 volume differential and monotonicity under adding S_hor give Q_D,c.S_hor>=0. Therefore

    integral x(s) dmu_D,c
       =Q_D,c.[rho^*p_X^*S-S_hor]
       <=Q_D,c.rho^*p_X^*S
       <=a Q_D,c.T
       <=a <gamma^n>.T=:C_c<infinity.                    (2)

The second upper bound pairs Q_D,c with the pulled nef aT-p_X^*S. The last uses Q_D,c<=<gamma^n> and the nef Cartier/positive Weil pairing from the one fixed model. There is no pairing of an arbitrary curve order with a nonnef S. The first bound uses positivity of the actual volume derivative along the effective horizontal divisor. The constant depends on c and the chosen original section but not on D or the refinement.

## 2. Passing to the full measure without an absolute integrability claim

The function b_k(x)=min(x(s),k)=-max(log|s|(x),-k) is continuous on the entire compact original X^an, including zero norms. From (1)--(2),

    integral b_k dmu_D<=C_c+kB/c.

Weak convergence against this actual continuous test gives the same bound for M_theta(u). The bounded functions b_k/k tend pointwise to the indicator of A_S={x:|s|(x)=0}. Dominated convergence for the finite Radon measure gives

    M_theta(u)(A_S)<=B/c.

Let c tend to infinity after k has tended to infinity. Thus M_theta(u)(A_S)=0. We have not claimed that C_c stays bounded as c increases or that integral x(s) dM_theta(u) is finite. In particular the latter may be infinite even though the pole set has mass zero.

Every proper Zariski closed Z in X is contained in the zero divisor of some nonzero section of a sufficiently ample actual bundle: twist its ideal by a sufficiently high ample power and choose a nonzero section. Its analytic points have support in Z and belong to A_S. Hence

    M_theta(u)(Z^an)=0 for every proper Zariski closed Z.  (3)

This retains every analytic support in the construction. It does not discard those supports in advance; the measure-nullness is a conclusion.

## 3. The canonical reference pole set

Use an actual rational big decomposition L=A+D_0 with A ample and D_0 effective, after clearing denominators. The canonical divisor section and the identically zero FS metric on an actual globally generated power of A give the same GF_L function sigma_D0=log|s_D0| on the original carrier. It is genuine, <=0, and thus a minorant in the definition of V_theta. Consequently

    V_theta>=sigma_D0,
    {V_theta=-infinity} subset D_0^an.

The actual Kodaira decomposition and ample global generation are explicit algebraic background premises. They do not make L nef or replace its generic class. Equation (3) proves

    M_theta(u)({V_theta=-infinity})=0.                    (4)

This is the reference-pole convention needed before defining relative integrals outside that set. It does not imply absolute V_theta integrability, nor prove integrability of V_theta-u for all E1 tests or vanishing on every nonalgebraic genuine psh pole set. Those weighted and full comparison gates remain unresolved. All dependencies and the unreviewed status of own150 are retained; no active reviewer has received this new proof.
