# General big relative energy: rooftops and countable finite-cost barriers

This unreviewed supporting note uses own143--145 and the exact genuine decreasing-limit argument of own150. It proves a rooftop energy bound directly from actual section flags. It neither proves a metric separation theorem nor weighted measure continuity or an all-target equation. Original P16.5 remains open.

Keep the actual big bundle and original genuine cone of those notes, with raw mass V=vol(L) and reference V_theta. For continuous model obstacles f,g and any degree m and real level lambda, write F_f(lambda) for the actual section-weight flag {0} union {s:W_m(f;s)>=lambda}. Then

    F_min(f,g)(lambda)=F_f(lambda) intersection F_g(lambda),
    F_max(f,g)(lambda) contains F_f(lambda)+F_g(lambda).

The first identity is the whole-carrier section inequality. For the second, the actual non-Archimedean triangle inequality gives, for two sections separately satisfying the f and g bounds, their sum's bound by max(f,g) at every original point. Consequently

    dim F_min+dim F_max>=dim F_f+dim F_g.

All four flags have a common finite lower and upper weight bound in this degree. Sum their dimensions over integer levels between those bounds, using the common r_m-dimensional baseline below the lower bound. This gives the same inequality for the sums of their floored intrinsic weights. Take the true actual section-energy limits of own144:

    E^obs(min(f,g))+E^obs(max(f,g))
         >=E^obs(f)+E^obs(g).                            (1)

Uniform model approximation extends (1) to continuous obstacles. This is an inequality for obstacle envelopes. It is not an assertion with the energy of max(Pf,Pg) substituted for the usually larger envelope P(max(f,g)).

Now let u,v be genuine relative-E1 functions with u,v<=V_theta. Direct their rational model majorants f>=u and g>=v, both <=0. The nonpositive max obstacle has E^obs(max(f,g))<=0. Equation (1) gives

    E^obs(min(f,g))>=E^obs(f)+E^obs(g)>=E_theta(u)+E_theta(v).

The genuine envelopes of these minimum obstacles form a decreasing product net. Their generic values are bounded above by zero. For every genuine w, E_theta(w)<=V w(v_triv); hence the displayed energy bound also bounds these generic values below by [E_theta(u)+E_theta(v)]/V. Their pointwise limit is genuine theta-psh by own150 Section5, lies below min(u,v), and dominates every genuine minorant of min(u,v). It is the actual rooftop P_theta(min(u,v)). Own145 energy continuity gives

    E_theta(P_theta(min(u,v)))>=E_theta(u)+E_theta(v)>-infinity. (2)

Thus this rooftop belongs to the entire genuine relative E1 on the original carrier. Its generic value need not be zero; normalizing it upwards would change the minorant relation, so no such normalization is made in (2).

In particular let u_j be a countable collection of genuine functions <=V_theta with finite energies and

    sum_j [-E_theta(u_j)]<infinity.

Define p_N=P_theta(min(u_1,...,u_N)). Induction using (2) gives E_theta(p_N)>=sum_{j<=N}E_theta(u_j). They decrease and their generic values have one finite lower bound. Their pointwise limit p is therefore genuine on the whole carrier, and energy continuity gives E_theta(p)>=sum_j E_theta(u_j)>-infinity. It is the actual largest genuine minorant of inf_j u_j and has p<=u_j for every j. This constructs countable finite-cost barriers; it does not replace the original arbitrary directed-net theory by sequences.
