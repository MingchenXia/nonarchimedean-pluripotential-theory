An independent affine bouquet is a finite union X=union_i(a+V_i) of complete rational d-planes with rational a, constant positive weights, saturated tangent lattices, and a direct sum of the V_i. In this geometric sector every actual positive-at-infinity finite rational-carrier real-PA reference has endpoint regularity for every bounded continuous obstacle. This includes arbitrary non-PA branch data and mixed coordinate dependence inside a branch. It does not include dependent planes or positive-dimensional branch overlaps. The compact/completion facts of n1 and interpolation theorem of n2 are used.

First, continuous PC is exactly ordinary convexity on each branch with a shared value at a. Necessity: on a rational segment in one branch use a finite rational subdivision. In each cell the PCC test gives convexity. At crossings, the two-sided PCC centered in the common face orders the left and right secant slopes; concatenation gives convexity of the whole segment, including a crossing at a. Continuity extends to real segments. Conversely, at any center away from a all incident cells belong to one plane, and ordinary convexity proves Jensen. At a, group PCC endpoints by branch. Independence forces the weighted spatial sum in each branch separately to be zero. For each nonempty group, convexity gives its weighted values at least its total weight times f(a); summing proves the entire PCC. These arguments cover every face, rather than only origin circuits.

The actual unrestricted envelope E for continuous gamma and u in C_b is between gamma-||u|| and gamma+u. It is finite PCC by taking the supremum. On every compact chart portion it is bounded, so n1's local star comparison gives a continuous completion F>=E. Rational comparisons with the continuous upper and lower bounds show F still satisfies them everywhere and differs boundedly from gamma. It is an eligible competitor, so F<=E and E=F. Hence E is continuous PC before regularization, without restricting the defining competitors to continuous ones. Translating competitors also proves ||P_gamma(u)-P_gamma(v)||<=||u-v||. No global C_b density of local obstacle approximations is asserted.

For any such continuous PC f, apply n2 on every plane with the same error 2^(-n) and prescribed rational set {a}. The majorants F_i,n have value f(a) at a, global error at most 2^(-n), and the exact recession of the actual gamma_i. Glue them. Refining each rational carrier by rational orthants makes a a common vertex, without any equality wall determined by irrational heights. The glued carrier is finite and rational, and n1's coefficient-space argument gives PA_R. The branch characterization gives every PCC inequality. Genuine divisor positivity can also be checked directly: at a choose an affine support on every branch with common constant f(a); direct-sum independence extends their linear parts simultaneously to one ambient affine support. At a nonorigin point a small neighborhood meets only its one plane and has the ordinary convex support. The tangent-corrected divisor test from n1 applies with saturated normals and all effective cycles. Add the same rational constant 4*2^(-n) on every branch. Values remain synchronized, and n2's estimate gives a decreasing global uniform sequence in the actual same-reference class. This puts every actual envelope in the genuine PA_R regularizable domain before any raw operator completion is invoked.

There is also an explicit synchronized convex-roof formula. For b_i=gamma_i+u_i let H_i be its greatest ordinary convex minorant and c=min_i H_i(a). Define Q_i as its greatest convex minorant with Q_i(a)<=c. Gamma_i-||u|| bounds H_i below; it is finite convex and continuous. H_i-(H_i(a)-c) is an admissible capped minorant, so Q_i(a)=c and Q_i has the same growth. The Q_i glue as PC. The actual continuous envelope has branch restrictions bounded by H_i and a common value at most c, hence is bounded by Q_i. The glued Q_i is itself a competitor, proving equality.

For finite rational-carrier PA_R b_i, take a pointed carrying refinement. Each epigraph piece is generated by lifted rational spatial vertices, lifted rational spatial recession rays with real heights, and a vertical ray. Bounded difference from gamma_i gives the exact gamma_i recession heights. Its closed convex hull is the convex hull of all lifted vertices plus epi gamma_i^infinity; adding (a,c) produces the capped roof. The shifted H_i bounds its lower boundary below. Every graph face projects to a rational polyhedron generated by subsets of rational positions and rays, as in n2. Thus this PA obstacle envelope itself is finite rational-carrier PA_R even when heights are irrational.

This is a geometric regularity theorem, not a classification by the defining envelope condition. A product of a cross with a line has positive-dimensional common traces and is not covered.
