# An intrinsic floor operator on the entire nef finite-energy cone

This is a new unreviewed own deduction, not a grant or an original completion. Neither active frozen native119 nor native121, nor prepared frozen review122, receives this later material. The construction addresses the larger floor-defined nef E1 class without assuming that its members belong to an auxiliary ample finite-energy space. Arbitrary decreasing-family continuity, a full variational inverse and general original P16.5 remain open.

## 1. Actual data and verified bounded input domain

Retain an algebraically closed trivially valued field k of characteristic zero and arbitrary cardinality, a smooth integral projective n-fold X with n>=1, an actual nef rational bundle L with class theta, and its canonical reference zero. Put V=theta^n>=0. Use the genuine numerical PSH(theta) of BJv4 Definition4.1. Its constants and finite maxima are genuine. No semiampleness is assumed.

For bounded u in PSH(theta), membership in PSH(theta+h) for an actual ample H follows by adding the genuine zero h-potential. Boundedness makes u finite-energy in that actual ample class, so u is in the published ambient E~1. Theorems7.14/7.18 and Proposition7.19 therefore construct its **actual** raw metric measure

$$\nu(u)=(\theta+dd^cu)^n$$

and raw energy E_theta(u)=(theta,u)^{n+1}/(n+1), with zero reference height. The measure is positive, of mass V, and its finite model coefficients include ord_F(X_0) times the actual intersection degree on F. Multilinearity identifies it with the epsilon-down-to-zero limit of actual positive ample measures for L+epsilon H, independently of H. This identification uses actual metric operators before any limit and defines no arbitrary inverse measure. These bounded facts are reconstructed in proposal132 and follow from the complete named published input units just specified.

Normalize u in PSH(theta) by sup u=0. Its value at the generic trivial valuation is then zero, as follows already by taking limits of the defining FS weights. Let u_j=max(u,-j), and define

$$E_\theta(u)=\inf_{j\geq1}E_\theta(u_j),\qquad
\mathcal E^1_\theta=\{u\in\operatorname{PSH}(\theta):E_\theta(u)>-\infty\}.$$

This is the **entire actual floor-defined cone**, including potentials outside E~1. Finite energy is the original theta-energy condition, with raw normalization. For u in that cone set C_u=-E_theta(u)<infinity.

## 2. Borel locality at the nef boundary

We need locality on the strict floor sets, not merely on ordinary open subsets. For bounded theta-psh a,b, both are finite-energy theta+epsilon h-psh. The complete actual ample Theorem7.40 yields equality of the two actual ample metric measures of max(a,b) and a on the Borel set {a>b}. Multiply by the raw positive ample volume to remove probability normalization.

Each of these raw measures is a finite polynomial in epsilon as a signed Radon measure, by the published product construction. Restriction to the fixed Borel set is a linear operation on these finite signed measures. Thus their restricted difference is a polynomial identically zero for every positive rational epsilon. Evaluating against any Borel set, or solving the finite Vandermonde system at distinct rational epsilon values, makes every measure coefficient zero. Its constant coefficient is the actual bounded theta-product. Hence

$$\mathbf1_{\{a>b\}}\nu(\max(a,b))=
\mathbf1_{\{a>b\}}\nu(a).$$

There is no passage of a merely weak limit through restriction to a discontinuous indicator. This exact coefficient argument retains the general Borel strict-wins statement of Theorem7.40, whose complete printed proof was read at literal LF4531--4533 and4565--4586, with Lemma7.43 LF4552--4564. The source's printed proof uses its model-component locality and then the actual weighted finite-energy passage. Its apparent reference to Proposition7.19(iii) at the end is a source cross-reference issue; the substantive weighted passage is Proposition7.21(ii), not the scaling assertion in (iii).

## 3. Construct the full operator from actual floors

Let A_j={u>-j}, nu_j=nu(u_j), and sigma_j=1_{A_j}nu_j. For m>=j, max(u_m,-j)=u_j and {u_m>-j}=A_j. Section2 gives nu_m|A_j=nu_j|A_j. Since A_j increases, the actual sigma_j form an increasing family of positive Radon measures. Borel restrictions of finite regular Radon measures on the compact original space are again regular Radon measures.

The bounded energy identity is the actual mixed formula

$$E_\theta(u_j)=\frac1{n+1}\sum_{r=0}^n
\int u_j\,(\theta+dd^cu_j)^r\wedge\theta^{n-r}.$$

Every mixed measure is positive. Since u_j<=0, this implies

$$\int(-u_j)\nu_j\leq(n+1)(-E_\theta(u_j))\leq(n+1)C_u.$$

On A_j^c, u_j=-j, so the mass of rho_j=1_{A_j^c}nu_j is at most (n+1)C_u/j. Consequently sigma_j has mass tending to V. Its increasing setwise limit is a positive Radon measure sigma of mass V: the difference sigma-sigma_j has mass tending to zero, giving regularity by finite-mass tail approximation. Moreover

$$\|\nu_j-\sigma\|_{\rm TV}\leq
\|\rho_j\|+\|\sigma-\sigma_j\|
\leq\frac{2(n+1)C_u}{j}.$$

Define M_theta(u)=sigma. This is a measure obtained from the original actual positive metric floor products and their strict-wins restrictions. It has the required raw top mass V, gives no mass to {u=-infinity}, and has total-variation convergence of the actual full floors. For bounded u the construction agrees exactly with the published bounded operator, since eventually A_j is all of X^an. All auxiliary ample choices have already disappeared at each bounded stage.

The same construction with arbitrary real floor levels is increasing and cofinal with the integers. Changing u by a constant merely shifts the real levels and leaves curvature products unchanged. Thus the operator and finite-energy membership are intrinsic under the stated constant gauge. If V=0, all bounded positive products vanish and all bounded energies vanish by their constant bounds. The resulting operator is zero on the entire genuine cone; this does not supply uniqueness modulo constants or a positive lower-degree replacement.

## 4. Every original E1(theta) potential is integrable

For bounded normalized theta-psh a,b, monotonicity of the published nef pairing makes every (theta,a)^r(theta,b)^{n+1-r} nonpositive. Its all-zero reference height is zero. The identity

$$\int b\,\nu(a)=(\theta,b)(\theta,a)^n-(\theta,0)(\theta,a)^n$$

therefore gives -integral b nu(a) <= -(theta,b)(theta,a)^n. Expanding (2theta,a+b)^{n+1}, all its terms are nonpositive and the displayed mixed term occurs n+1 times. Concavity of bounded energy gives

$$\int(-b)\nu(a)\leq-E_{2\theta}(a+b)
=-2^{n+1}E_\theta((a+b)/2)
\leq2^n(-E_\theta(a)-E_\theta(b)).$$

The concavity here follows from the actual ample concavity and polynomial passage on these bounded inputs; it is not applied to an unverified nonample ambient domain.

Apply the estimate to a=u_j and b=v_l, where u,v are any normalized original E1(theta) potentials. It bounds integral(-v_l)nu_j by 2^n(C_u+C_v), uniformly in j,l. First take j to infinity, using total-variation convergence with the bounded Borel test v_l. Then let l increase by ordinary countable monotone convergence. Thus

$$\int(-v)M_\theta(u)\leq2^n(C_u+C_v)<\infty.$$

In particular the constructed measure gives no mass to the pole set of **any** original E1(theta) test. This is an actual integrability statement on the larger intrinsic domain, not an assumed embedding into E~1.

## 5. Strong weighted limits along the actual floor sequence

Suppose V>0; the zero-mass case is immediate. At bounded theta-psh inputs, define the normalized I and J functionals by the actual raw energies divided by V and the actual metric measures divided by V. The fundamental energy identity, the bounds I/(n+1)<=J<=nI/(n+1), and Corollary7.31's Holder estimate transfer from theta+epsilon h by their finite polynomial and pairing formulas. The constants remain bounded: by the source's explicit convention at LF473 they depend only on n, while the positive volumes tend to V>0. Every input in this passage is bounded and thus lies in the actual ample finite-energy domain. No inequality on the larger unbounded cone is assumed.

Let v_l<=v_k be two floors of a normalized v in E1(theta), with l>=k, and set delta_kl=(E_theta(v_k)-E_theta(v_l))/V. The mixed energy difference formula makes

$$\int(v_k-v_l)\nu(v_l)/V\leq(n+1)\delta_{kl},\qquad
I_\theta(v_k,v_l)\leq n(n+1)\delta_{kl}.$$

All normalized J-values of v_k,v_l,u_j are bounded by B=max(C_v,C_u)/V. Corollary7.31, applied with reference u_j, gives

$$\int(v_k-v_l)\nu_j/V\leq\delta_{kl}
+C_n\big(n(n+1)\delta_{kl}\big)^{\alpha_n}B^{1-\alpha_n},
\qquad\alpha_n=2^{-n}.$$

Indeed J_{u_j}(v_k)-J_{u_j}(v_l) equals -delta_kl plus that integral. Put delta_k=(E_theta(v_k)-E_theta(v))/V. It tends to zero; delta_kl<=delta_k. Letting l increase and using integrability yields a bound integral(v_k-v)nu_j <=V r_k, uniformly in j, with r_k ->0 given by the right side. The same bound holds for M_theta(u), by first passing j to infinity with bounded v_k-v_l, and then l to infinity.

For fixed k, v_k nu_j tends to v_k M_theta(u) in total variation. The two tail bounds consequently give

$$v\,\nu_j\longrightarrow v\,M_\theta(u)
\quad\hbox{in total variation}.$$

The same argument gives v_j nu_j -> v M_theta(u) in total variation. Thus all fixed original E1(theta) weights, as well as their simultaneous original floors, have the claimed strong **floor-sequence** limit. This statement retains the original operator and weights before limits; it is stronger than merely checking numerical mass.

## 6. Precise remaining boundary

This construction supplies the actual entire floor-defined nef E1 operator, full raw mass, gauge and auxiliary-ample independence, integrability of every intrinsic original E1 test, and the stated weighted countable-floor limits. It does not establish arbitrary decreasing nets of E1(theta) operators against every original continuous test, arbitrary model approximations, or the required full variational equation and uniqueness. The source's Holder estimates on theta-psh test differences need not separate all continuous functions when theta is nonample; replacing the original test space by their span would be a new observable-carrier restriction. Those gaps remain explicit.

No general nonnef big reference theorem or positive lower-degree nonbig carrier is constructed. A zero top operator on a nonconstant cone cannot provide uniqueness modulo constants. Original P16.5 remains open, and the new deduction requires an independent review before any scoped acceptance count changes.
