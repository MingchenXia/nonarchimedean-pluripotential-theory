# General big classes: finite intrinsic dual energy of every operator output

This unreviewed candidate uses the moving-series construction of own161 and the original operator of own150. It proposes a uniform relative moment bound for every genuine E1 test against every genuine E1 output, and hence finite intrinsic output dual energy. It supplies no arbitrary-target inverse or full mixed nonnef operator. Original P16.5 remains open.

## 1. Exact proposed bound and source boundary

Retain the full general-big domain, raw volume V and original minimal reference V_theta. For normalized genuine u,v in E1(theta), put B(u)=-E_theta(u). The proposed bound is

    integral(V_theta-v) dM_theta(u)<=B(v)+n B(u).       (1.1)

The integral is relative, outside the reference poles; own158 already proves that every output gives those poles, and all pluripolar sets, zero mass. In particular (1.1) would imply

    D_theta(M_theta(u))<=n B(u)<infinity.               (1.2)

The algebraic positive-product definition and compact directed-order arguments used below are explicitly imported from Boucksom--Favre--Jonsson, arXiv:math/0608260v2, Section2.2: Definition2.5, Lemmas2.6/2.7 and their complete proofs. The pertinent full primary units were read at renderer L650--708, with the finite nef intersection positivity at L575--635, and compact order intervals in Proposition1.5 at L511--516. Their field domain is algebraically closed characteristic zero. This is ordinary algebraic intersection background, not an additional nonample equation grant. Original genuine energies and measures remain the unreviewed own143--150/156/158 constructions.

## 2. Complete moving parts converge also in the lower positive product

For a fixed rational big bundle alpha on a smooth projective variety Z of dimension d, let C_m be the complete moving part of |m alpha| divided by m, on a smooth resolution. Along a factorial sequence these are increasing as Cartier b-divisors: products of degree-m sections form a subseries in each divisible higher degree, so the normalized fixed divisor of the complete higher system is no larger. All C_m are nef and C_m<=alpha.

The classes C_m^(d-1) form an increasing net bounded above by <alpha^(d-1)>. Compact order intervals and the directed-order lemma give a weak limit. To identify it with the whole positive product, fix any rational nef Cartier beta=alpha-T allowed in Definition2.5, where T is effective on a determination. On that same smooth determination choose an actual ample rational A with alpha-A effective, possible since alpha is big. For rational 0<epsilon<1,

    beta_epsilon=(1-epsilon)beta+epsilon A

is actual ample and alpha-beta_epsilon is effective. For all sufficiently divisible m, m beta_epsilon is globally generated. The injected subseries in |m alpha| makes C_m-beta_epsilon effective on a common resolution. Positivity of an effective divisor paired with mixed nef factors gives

    C_m^(d-1)>=beta_epsilon^(d-1).

First pass m to its weak limit, then epsilon to zero on this fixed determination. The limit dominates every beta^(d-1) in the defining directed family. Conversely every C_m^(d-1) belongs to that family and is below its least upper bound. Therefore

    C_m^(d-1)-><alpha^(d-1)>                           (2.1)

in the weak Weil-class topology, in particular against EVERY fixed Cartier divisor, including a nonnef vertical model divisor. No positive-product order is directly paired with that nonnef divisor.

Apply this to alpha_D(c) of own161, with d=n+1. On the actual auxiliary nef generic A_m, let mu_m,D be the top central model measure of psi_m,D, pushed to the original carrier by the original function-field identification. Its atoms are original rational divisorial or generic points, its coefficients are nonnegative finite nef intersections, and its raw mass is V_m. For a fixed original model test h=f_H, the projection formula gives

    integral h dmu_m,D=C_m(D)^n.H -><alpha_D(c)^n>.H.

The right side is integral h dM_theta(P_theta f_D) by own143--150. Uniform model-function density, positivity and V_m->V give weak convergence against every original C0 test:

    mu_m,D -> M_theta(P_theta f_D).                    (2.2)

The generic modifications are used only for this finite intersection approximation. The measure on the right was constructed beforehand and is not defined by an inverse lifting procedure.

## 3. A finite nef support plane and its uniform moment bound

Take normalized nonpositive rational model obstacles f_D,f_G, with u=P_theta f_D and v=P_theta f_G. Carry them on a common model and choose one rational c sufficiently large for both complete lattice splittings. At every m their auxiliary generic bundle is the SAME A_m, defined solely by |mL|. Resolve both complete systems together and denote their bounded nonpositive semipositive model potentials by psi_m,D,psi_m,G. Their raw intersection energies converge respectively to E_theta(u),E_theta(v) by own161.

The auxiliary energy is concave along their segment. This can be checked without an unbounded pairing theorem. Write H for the vertical difference of the two compactifications, and C_t for their nef segment. The second derivative of C_t^(n+1)/(n+1) is n H^2.C_t^(n-1). For F=sum b_i E_i, put a_i=coeff_Ei H and m_ij=Ei.Ej.C_t^(n-1). Off-diagonal m_ij are nonnegative by nefness, and M b=0 since F restricts trivially to each central component. Direct expansion gives

    H^2.C_t^(n-1)
       =-sum_(i<j)b_i b_j m_ij(a_i/b_i-a_j/b_j)^2<=0.

The first derivative is the actual finite central integral. Consequently the support plane says

    e_m(psi_m,G)-e_m(psi_m,D)
        <=integral(psi_m,G-psi_m,D) dmu_m,D.

Let B_m,D=-e_m(psi_m,D), and similarly B_m,G. The finite positive mixed identity of own161 gives

    integral(-psi_m,D) dmu_m,D <=(n+1) B_m,D.

Combining these two inequalities yields the uniform cross moment

    integral(-psi_m,G) dmu_m,D <=B_m,G+n B_m,D.        (3.1)

All integrals are bounded finite central sums. Generic values of psi_m,D need not be exactly zero; the identity includes that finite generic term and therefore requires no normalization error. Their energies have the correct limiting normalization by own161.

## 4. Lower semicontinuous tests transfer the moment to the original carrier

For original degree ell let phi_ell,0 be the unweighted actual complete-section GF function of L, and Z_ell its proper base-ideal zero set. Choose ell along a factorial sequence with nonempty complete systems. These phi_ell,0 increase under divisibility and lie below V_theta. Own156's actual-section approximation shows that their divisorial supremum is V_theta. General theta-EP and exact auxiliary-ample all-point determination identify their usc supremum with V_theta.

The discrepancy from their pointwise supremum is negligible in one direct genuine auxiliary ample embedding. The published ample negligible/pluripolar implication used in own160 makes it pluripolar, hence null for every output by own158. Thus phi_ell,0 increases to V_theta almost everywhere for each fixed original output. This use of a countable sequence of ALL degree spaces imposes no countability on the field: each space has a finite basis over the given field, and every individual section degree divides all sufficiently large factorial degrees.

For a fixed genuine v<=V_theta define on the whole original carrier

    g_ell(v)=max(phi_ell,0-v,0) on X^an minus Z_ell,
    g_ell(v)=0 on Z_ell.

This is nonnegative lower semicontinuous. Off Z_ell the GF function is finite continuous and v is usc; at the boundary the assigned value zero cannot violate lower semicontinuity. It can have value +infinity where v has a pole off Z_ell. It is an original-carrier test, with no use of a difference of two undefined pole values.

For model v=P_theta f_G, fix ell and take m in the factorial sequence with ell dividing m. At every atom of mu_m,D all GF functions are finite, and

    phi_ell,0<=phi_m,0,
    phi_m,G<=v,
    phi_m,0-phi_m,G=-psi_m,G.

Therefore g_ell(v)<=-psi_m,G on that finite support. Equation (3.1), energy convergence and the nonnegative-lsc Portmanteau inequality for (2.2) give

    integral g_ell(v) dM_theta(u)<=B(v)+n B(u).        (4.1)

Let ell tend along its factorial sequence. The g_ell increase; their limit is V_theta-v almost everywhere outside the output-null reference and other polar sets. Ordinary countable monotone convergence proves (1.1) for these model-envelope u,v. Absolute integrability of V_theta is never used.

## 5. Entire genuine E1 endpoints and arbitrary defining nets

For fixed model u and arbitrary normalized genuine v in E1, take the cofinal nonpositive model envelopes v_i decreasing to v, with B(v_i)<=B(v) and converging energies from own145/150. For each FIXED ell, g_ell(v_i) increase to g_ell(v), including their assigned zero values on Z_ell. These tests are nonnegative lsc. Their Radon integrals have the directed monotone property: use the sets where a test exceeds a fixed level, compact inner regularity and finite subcovers, then the layer-cake formula with ordinary scalar monotone convergence. This is not a monotone theorem for arbitrary Borel nets.

Applying (4.1) with the uniform bound B(v)+nB(u), then this directed lsc passage, gives the same bound for g_ell(v). Countable ell monotone convergence then proves (1.1) for the full v.

Now let u be arbitrary normalized genuine E1. Its cofinal nonpositive model envelopes u_j decrease to it, B(u_j)<=B(u), and their original measures converge weakly to M_theta(u) by own150. For each fixed ell and the already fixed v, Portmanteau applies to the fixed nonnegative lsc original test g_ell(v). The established model-u bound gives

    integral g_ell(v) dM_theta(u)<=B(v)+nB(u).

The countable ell passage again gives (1.1) on the entire genuine cone. This does not interchange a moving discontinuous test with a weakly converging measure.

## 6. Consequences and the remaining variational step

Equation (1.1) proves relative integrability of every genuine E1 test against every original output. Taking the intrinsic dual supremum directly gives (1.2); the reference-pole condition was already established by own158. No target was chosen in constructing the operator.

Every fixed output is therefore an intrinsic finite-dual target. Own160 applies: relative integration against that fixed output is continuous under normalized rooftop-metric convergence. In particular normalized decreasing E1 nets converge in this metric by own145/156, so their relative integrals against a FIXED output converge. This is not joint weighted continuity when the output also varies, and does not construct mixed general-big products.

Combined with own161, every intrinsic target has a coercive objective and every output has finite intrinsic dual energy. Weak target-integral continuity on compact cost sublevels, actual arbitrary-target maximizer attainment, the unbounded exact supporting plane, all-E1 operator uniqueness and full comparison still require proof.
