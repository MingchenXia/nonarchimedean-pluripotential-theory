## 4. A finite nef intersection contraction with a vanishing generic error

All ingredients in this paragraph are finite nef model intersections. Let mu_m,l be the mixed central measure having l slots from psi_m and n-l from the zero A_m metric. Each central coefficient is a mixed intersection of nef restrictions, so it is nonnegative; its raw mass is V_m. Put

    J_m,0=-a_m V_m->0.

Telescoping the actual top intersection gives

    sum_l J_m,l=(n+1)(-e_m(psi_m)).

The convex metric r psi_m remains semipositive on A_m: its compactification is the convex combination of C_m(D) and p_Xm^*A_m+cF, both nef. Multilinearity gives

    -e_m(r psi_m)
      =1/(n+1) sum_(j=0)^n sum_(l=0)^j
         binom(j,l) r^(l+1)(1-r)^(j-l) J_m,l.

The l=0 part is at most r J_m,0. For l>=1, r^(l+1)<=r^2 and sum_(j=l)^n binom(j,l)=binom(n+1,l+1)<=2^(n+1). Therefore

    -e_m(r psi_m)<=r J_m,0+K_n r^2(-e_m(psi_m)).       (4.1)

This argument uses no unbounded nef pairing theorem and no nonnef mixed product. It is the finite-model algebra underlying the bounded part of own135, now applied to explicitly globally generated actual auxiliary bundles.

## 6. Intrinsic target characterization and coercivity

Let nu be a positive original Radon target of raw mass V with nu({V_theta=-infinity})=0. All integrals below are relative integrals off that null set. Put H_nu(u)=integral(V_theta-u)dnu for normalized u, and

    D_nu=sup_(u in E1)[E_theta(u)+H_nu(u)].

Constants cancel exactly by the raw mass. If D_nu is finite, every H_nu(u) is finite by this defining inequality.

Conversely, suppose every original genuine E1 relative test is integrable. H_nu is uniformly bounded on B(u)<=1. Otherwise choose normalized u_j of cost at most1 with H_nu(u_j)>=2^j. The genuine convex sums

    s_N=(1-sum_(j=1)^N 2^(-j))V_theta

    H_nu(u)<=B_nu sqrt(K_n B(u)),
