## 1. Exact proposed cross estimate

Retain the entire general-big genuine E1 cone, original raw operator and reference of own143--162. For normalized genuine u,v put B_u=-E_theta(u), B_v=-E_theta(v). There is a dimension-dependent finite C_n such that, with q=2^(-n),

    H_v(M_theta(u)):=integral(V_theta-v)dM_theta(u)
       <=C_n B_v^q max(B_u,B_v)^(1-q).                 (1.1)

If both energies vanish, both functions are the reference by own156, and the right side and integral are zero. All integrals are relative off the output-null reference poles. No mixed nonnef measure is defined in (1.1).

## 4. Uniform tails for a fixed unbounded relative test

Fix normalized genuine v in E1 and its intrinsic floors

    v_k=max(v,V_theta-k).

Set r_k=R(v+k,V_theta). Own159 gives r_k<=V_theta and

    b_k:=-E_theta(r_k)->0.

Its generic maximum a_k is nonpositive. Monotonicity against V_theta+a_k shows -V a_k<=b_k. Hence the normalized r_k'=r_k-a_k has nonnegative cost at most b_k. Apply (1.1) to this actual genuine normalized test, against any normalized output M_theta(u) with B_u<=B_0. Adding its constant back gives

    integral(V_theta-r_k)dM_theta(u)
       <=C_n b_k^q max(B_0,b_k)^(1-q)+b_k.             (4.1)

The right side tends to zero, uniformly in those outputs. Off the null reference poles,

    0<=v_k-v=max(V_theta-v-k,0)<=V_theta-r_k,

because r_k<=min(v+k,V_theta). Consequently

    sup_(B_u<=B_0) integral(v_k-v)dM_theta(u)->0.       (4.2)

This is uniform integrability of the FIXED test's relative truncation tail over a bounded output-cost family. It does not alone give continuity of its bounded relative floors against arbitrary weakly converging outputs, or joint weighted convergence for varying tests.

      >=E_theta(u_j)+s integral h dM_theta(u_j)-A_(h,B)|s|^(3/2).
