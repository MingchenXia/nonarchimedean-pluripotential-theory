ID: n1

SUMMARY:
Draft decreasing strict real-PL regularization for arbitrary classical psh on separated smooth boundaryless second-countable strict curves. Finite separable mesh realization, formal-model membership and Galois descent repair the invalid identification of Thuillier-lisse with strict real-PL. No sharp curve range is claimed.

DETAILED SUMMARY:
Over a complete nontrivially valued field, arbitrary classical psh functions, including permitted minus-infinity values, admit a local decreasing sequence under the stated curve and topology hypotheses. Thuillier supplies finite continuous lisse functions f_S on a compact strict domain Y, subharmonic on its ordinary interior W. Rational-sublevel Lindelof extraction supplies f_n without continuity of the target. Chord interpolation after finite separable mesh realization gives f_n <= g_n <= f_n+2^-n. Integral formal-model star functions prove actual strict real-PL membership; Galois averaging and GJR Corollary 5.12 descend it. The functions q_n=g_n+2^-n are semipositive, decrease because q_(n+1)<=f_(n+1)+2^-n<=q_n, and converge pointwise, including at minus-infinity points. No residue characteristic, perfection, genus or residue Picard-torsion restriction is added. This is an unverified draft proof of a sufficient curve range, not the original higher-dimensional characterization.

Draft theorem A. Let K be a complete nontrivially valued non-Archimedean field. Let C be a separated, smooth, strictly K-analytic space of pure dimension one. Smooth means rig-smooth and without relative boundary over K; such curves are good. Assume C is second countable. For every classical curve-test psh h:C→R∪{−∞} and every b∈C, there is one fixed ordinary open neighbourhood W of b and finite semipositive strict GJR real-PL functions q_n on W decreasing pointwise to h. Finite continuous targets additionally have uniform convergence on compact subsets. This is a draft mathematical proof, not an independent correctness certificate.

One-sided approximation lemma. Suppose Y⊂C is a compact strictly affinoid domain, W=Y\∂_K Y, and f∈A^0(Y) is finite continuous on Y and subharmonic on W. For every ε>0, there is a continuous strict real-PL g on all of Y, semipositive on W, satisfying f≤g≤f+ε on Y.

First obtain, from the compact-domain argument of Thuillier Proposition 3.2.4, a finite separable extension L/K, a simply semistable formal model of Y_L, a finite weighted skeleton Σ, a retraction τ, and a continuous piecewise affine real F with f_L=F∘τ. Replace L by its finite Galois closure and base change the model. Nodal charts xy=a retain their equations after extension. Retractions commute with extension: on a nodal tube they use −log|x|, and on a smooth tube they retract to the corresponding component vertex. Thuillier Proposition 2.2.21 supplies the weighted skeletal compatibility. Lifted edges retain their affine coordinates and slopes.

Let M_0 bound the absolute values of the finitely many slopes of F. Choose a mesh scale δ with M_0δ<ε; choosing δ<ε/(M_0+1) includes M_0=0. Write Γ_L=−log|L*|. Original edge lengths lie in Γ_L. If Γ_L is dense, choose such a positive δ in Γ_L. Cut each edge at successive multiples of δ, retaining its other endpoint. If Γ_L=γZ is discrete, choose an arbitrarily large integer N prime to char K when that characteristic is positive, and take δ=γ/N. These cut coordinates belong to (1/N)Γ_L. They become actual value-group coordinates after adjoining roots of finitely many scalars: X^N−a is separable because N is invertible in the field. Mixed characteristic requires no residue-characteristic restriction.

For strict star domains, choose an integer D≥5 prime to char K when necessary. Also include auxiliary coordinates at fractions 1/D, 2/D, (D−2)/D and (D−1)/D of every mesh segment. All the finitely many coordinates have denominators prime to the field characteristic. Adjoining appropriate roots realizes them in a single finite separable extension; take a finite Galois closure M/K containing L. In the dense case the additional D-th roots suffice for these auxiliary fractions. Base change the model to M. Every replacement and auxiliary coordinate now lies in the actual Γ_M. Original irrational breakpoints need not become legal vertices: they are replaced by the mesh, not asserted to be strict.

Write F_M for the lifted potential. Scalar subharmonicity, Thuillier Theorem 3.2.10 and Proposition 3.4.4 imply that F_M is convex on each original edge and has nonnegative weighted outgoing-slope sum at every original vertex outside the graph boundary. Define G to equal F_M at mesh and original vertices and to be affine between consecutive mesh vertices. At isolated vertices retain F_M. On a segment [s,t],
G(z)=((t−z)F_M(s)+(z−s)F_M(t))/(t−s).
Convexity gives G≥F_M. The Lipschitz bound gives 0≤G(z)−F_M(z)≤M_0(t−s)≤M_0δ<ε.

At an original vertex, the chord slope along every outgoing edge is at least the original outgoing derivative. Positive edge weights therefore preserve nonnegative weighted slope sum. At a replacement vertex in an edge, successive chord slopes are nondecreasing, and both halves have the same edge weight. Consequently all new Laplacian masses are nonnegative. No positivity condition is imposed at the actual boundary vertices. The skeletal lisse construction and graph Laplacian formula give g_M^0=G∘τ_M, continuous on Y_M and subharmonic on W_M, satisfying f_M≤g_M^0≤f_M+ε.

Actual strict membership is a separate argument. Cover each mesh vertex by its closed star running a fraction 2/D into each adjacent mesh segment, and cover the remaining edge portions by intervals with endpoints at fractions 1/D and (D−1)/D. This is a finite neighbourhood cover of the graph; parallel edges have distinct cut endpoints. Their retraction inverse images are strict analytic domains and form a G-cover of Y_M by GJR 15.3, 15.4 and 15.8. They include residual-disc tubes through the vertex stars. All subdivisions are admissible because their coordinates are actual Γ_M values, even for discrete value groups.

On an interval, G=c+at. The logarithmic annular coordinate t is integral PL up to sign and a value-group constant. Every real constant is real-PL: c=(c/log|α|)log|α| for any α∈M* with |α|≠1.

On a star centered at v, define H_e to be distance from v on the chosen arm e and zero on every other arm. Its slopes are 1 and 0; its intercepts belong to Γ_M because the center and endpoints have Γ_M coordinates. Thus it is piecewise (Z,Γ_M)-linear in GJR’s exact definition. Section 15.4 supplies a vertical Cartier divisor on an admissible formal model of the strict star domain with model function H_e∘τ_M. Sections 5.5–5.6 make this function strict integral PL. On the whole star,
G∘τ_M=G(v)+Σ_e a_e(H_e∘τ_M),
where a_e are its real outgoing slopes. Section 5.7 proves strict real-PL membership. No harmonicity or residue Picard-torsion condition is imposed on the basis functions. Their residue line bundles need not be trivial, so ordinary CLD smoothness is not asserted. Isolated vertices use the constant formula. All interval and star formulas agree on intersections because they are literally the same function G∘τ_M. This proves global strict membership on Y_M. The already proved subharmonicity then gives semipositivity on W_M by the classical/real-PL comparison.

The interpolation need not be Galois invariant. Average it:
ĝ_M=[M:K]^{-1}Σ_{σ∈Gal(M/K)}g_M^0∘σ.
Isometric semilinear automorphisms preserve strict domains, analytic units and subharmonicity. Finite positive real sums preserve subharmonicity and strict real-PL membership, using common refinements of strict G-covers. Since f_M is invariant, f_M≤ĝ_M≤f_M+ε.

This average has an actual continuous descent g on Y. Over y∈Y, the finite algebra H(y)⊗_K M is a product of finite field extensions. Normality of M/K makes the Galois action transitive on its factors, viewed through embeddings into an algebraic closure of H(y). Each factor has the unique absolute value extending that of the complete field H(y). The action is therefore transitive on the analytic fibre. The finite surjective map Y_M→Y is a closed quotient of compact Hausdorff spaces, so an invariant continuous function descends uniquely.

Apply GJR Corollary 5.12 to good strict Y, coefficient group R, and the finite complete algebraic extension M/K. It descends the proved strict M-real-PL membership to strict K-real-PL membership. Its finite case is Proposition 5.11: the projection is finite flat surjective, since on affinoid algebras tensoring with finite-dimensional M is free of rank [M:K]. This is strict descent across ground fields, not all-domain sheafification.

Classical curve scalar compatibility, GR Proposition 3.5(7), descends subharmonicity to W. Here W_M=Y_M\∂_M Y_M: both Y_M→Y and M(M)→M(K) are finite and boundaryless, and Berkovich’s good composition equality identifies the interiors first over K and then over M. GR Theorem 4.3 now proves semipositivity of g on W. Surjectivity passes the inequalities to Y. This proves the one-sided approximation lemma.

Countable extraction lemma. On a second-countable space Z, let h be upper semicontinuous and let f_i be a decreasing directed family of finite continuous functions with h=inf_i f_i. For every rational a, the open set {h<a} is the union of the open sets {f_i<a}. An open subspace of a second-countable space is Lindelof. Choose a countable subcover for each a and enumerate the union of the selected indices. Successive upper bounds in the directed set retain all previous choices and give a decreasing sequence. If h(z)<a, a selected index, and hence every sufficiently late chosen upper bound, has f_i(z)<a. All rational a>h(z) give infimum h(z); if h(z)=−∞, use all rational a. The reverse inequality follows from f_i≥h. No continuity or finite-valued hypothesis on h is used.

A countable dense subset of K supplies second countability for algebraic analytifications of finite type. A strict affinoid algebra has a countable norm-dense subset, obtained from polynomials over a countable dense subfield in a Tate presentation. Evaluation on that subset separates bounded multiplicative seminorms and induces the full topology, because | |a(x)|−|b(x)| |≤||a−b|| in the quotient Banach norm. Hence its compact spectrum embeds homeomorphically into R_{ ≥0}^N and is metrizable and second countable. More precisely the product here is indexed by the natural numbers, not a finite N. An affine algebraic chart, embedded in affine space, has an exhaustion by strict affinoid intersections with polydiscs of radii R^m, with R∈|K*| and R>1. The ordinary opens where the coordinate norms are strictly smaller than R^m cover the chart and are second countable subspaces of these affinoids. A finite affine covering gives a countable ordinary open covering and a countable base for the whole analytification. Ordinary open subspaces inherit it.

Proof of theorem A. Work in the connected component of b. If h≡−∞ there, q_n=−n gives the result; constants are strict real-PL and harmonic. Otherwise GR 3.4.1 identifies h with Thuillier subharmonicity. A connected smooth curve is irreducible, and GR 3.5(9) makes h finite at all non-rig-points. Choose a connected compact strictly affinoid neighbourhood Y of b with b in its ordinary interior. Goodness and strictness supply it; an affinoid connected component can be selected by idempotents. Its boundary is the finite Shilov boundary of non-rig-points, where h is finite. Because C has no boundary, W=Y\∂_K Y is precisely the ordinary interior of Y in C, by Berkovich 1.5.5. It is one relatively compact ordinary open neighbourhood of b.

Use Thuillier Theorem 3.4.2 on this particular Y. Its proof supplies finite continuous f_S∈A^0(Y), indexed by finite S⊂I(Y) containing ∂Y, with f_S=h on S, harmonic off S, subharmonic on W, decreasing under inclusions of S, and inf_S f_S=h on W. The source’s determination lemma 3.4.1 handles rigid and type-IV values in this infimum assertion; no continuity of h is inserted. The extraction lemma gives f_n decreasing to h on W. Upper bounds here are finite unions of S, so the chosen functions remain finite continuous lisse functions on all of Y and decrease there too.

Apply the one-sided approximation lemma to f_n with ε_n=2^{-n}, always on the same Y and W. Obtain f_n≤g_n≤f_n+2^{-n}. Set q_n=g_n+2^{-n}. The shift is a strict real-PL harmonic constant. Each q_n is finite, strict real-PL and semipositive on W, and
q_{n+1}≤f_{n+1}+2^{-n}≤f_n+2^{-n}≤g_n+2^{-n}=q_n.
Moreover h≤q_n and 0≤q_n−f_n≤2^{1−n}. Thus q_n decreases pointwise to h, also when h is −∞. Extensions and models may vary with n, but every descended function has domain Y and every semipositive approximant has the same ordinary domain W.

For a finite continuous target, the increasing opens {q_n−h<η} cover any compact subset of W. Compactness supplies one index covering it, proving uniform convergence there. For any analytic K'/K, GJR 5.4 and 7.6 preserve strict membership and semipositivity of q_n∘π, and evaluation at each point gives q_n∘π↓h∘π. This pulls back an existing sequence; no extraction for arbitrary new functions over nonmetrizable extension spaces is claimed.
