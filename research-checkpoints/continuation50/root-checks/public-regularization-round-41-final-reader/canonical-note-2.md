ID: n2

SUMMARY:
Draft higher-dimensional sector: every arbitrary classical psh on a full projective bundle over a second-countable smooth curve descends along compact projective fibres and has local decreasing strict semipositive real-PL pullbacks. The proof requires saturated domains.

DETAILED SUMMARY:
For complete nontrivially valued K with a countable dense subset, a smooth connected separated algebraic curve C0, an ordinary open Omega⊂C0^an, and an algebraic bundle E of rank r+1≥2, every classical psh u on X=P(E)^an restricted to Omega equals h∘p. Scalar pullback and the published local maximum principle prove constancy on compact connected boundaryless projective H(b)-fibres. Trivialization sections prove classical psh descent and exact overlap agreement. Note n1 supplies one fixed W and q_n, and v_n=q_n∘p is globally strict real-PL and semipositive on p^-1(W), decreasing pointwise to u. Every analytic scalar extension preserves the same sequence, even without metrizability of the extension space. These are smooth normal unibranch spaces of dimension r+1, including proper projective factors. No conclusion for every arbitrary psh germ on nonsaturated fibre charts, or for arbitrary normal/unibranch spaces, follows.

TEXT:
Draft theorem B. Let K be complete and nontrivially valued, with a countable dense subset. Let C0 be a smooth connected separated algebraic curve of finite type over K, Ω⊂C0^an an ordinary open subset, and E an algebraic vector bundle of rank r+1≥2. Let p:X=P(E)^an|_Ω→Ω. For every arbitrary classical psh u:X→R∪{−∞} and every x∈X there is one fixed ordinary open neighbourhood V=p^{-1}(W) and finite semipositive strict real-PL v_n on V decreasing pointwise to u. The construction is v_n=q_n∘p, using n1. The hypothesis on K can be replaced by second countability of the relevant base curve. No continuity, invariance, finiteness, residue-genus or residue-Picard-torsion restriction is placed on u. The product Ω×(P_K^r)^an is included.

Compact constancy lemma. A classical psh function w on a connected compact boundaryless analytic space F is constant, allowing −∞. If not identically −∞, upper semicontinuity and compactness give a finite attained maximum m: local finite upper bounds have a finite subcover, and compactness gives attainment. The set {w=m} is nonempty and closed. It is open by Gubler–Rabinoff arXiv:2506.13548v2, Theorem 6.1, since every point of F is an interior point. Connectedness makes it all of F. This applies to the full classical curve-test class.

For every complete nontrivially valued H, (P_H^r)^an is compact, connected and boundaryless. Its finite standard closed unit-polydisc covering proves compactness. It is good, strict and boundaryless as an algebraic analytification, as recalled on GJR page 8. Each polydisc is connected, and any two standard charts meet at the homogeneous point with all coordinates 1.

Here is a direct polydisc connectedness proof. For a point z of the closed unit polydisc, use its coordinate values z_i in H(z). Evaluate a polynomial P(z_1+U_1,…,z_r+U_r) with the Gauss norm of polyradius s∈[0,1]. This is a multiplicative seminorm bounded by the unit-polydisc norm. At s=0 it is z. At s=1 it is the unit Gauss point, because translation by coordinates of norm at most 1, and its inverse, preserve the unit Gauss norm. For each polynomial its value is a maximum of finitely many continuous expressions in s; norm density extends continuity to the Tate algebra. Every point has a path to the Gauss point. This works over H=H(b) without a countability assumption on that fibre field.

For b∈Ω, extend scalars to H(b), then include the analytic fibre p^{-1}(b)≅(P_{H(b)}^r)^an into X_{H(b)}. This uses the H(b)-point of Ω_{H(b)} over b. GR Proposition 4.2 makes the scalar pullback and its restriction classical psh. The compact constancy lemma therefore proves that u is constant on the entire fibre, including non-rigid points. Define h(b) to be this value. Then u=h∘p.

On an ordinary open base set U where the projective bundle trivializes, choose its analytic section s_U given by [1:0:…:0]. We have h|_U=u∘s_U, so h is upper semicontinuous and classical psh there by GR Proposition 4.2. Sections from different trivializations have the same u-value over every point, by fibre constancy. They agree exactly on overlaps. Classical psh sheafiness proves that h is classical psh on Ω, retaining its possible discontinuities and −∞ values. No quotient-topology assumption for this descent is needed because there are local sections.

The field hypothesis makes Ω second countable by the affinoid/algebraic argument in n1. Apply n1 to h at b=p(x), obtaining one W and one q_n. Let V=p^{-1}(W) and v_n=q_n∘p. This is an ordinary open neighbourhood of x. GJR Lemma 5.4(1) proves actual strict real-PL membership; Proposition 7.6(1) proves semipositivity. These results require good strict spaces but do not require p to be finite. Pointwise evaluation gives v_{n+1}(y)≤v_n(y) and lim_n v_n(y)=h(p(y))=u(y). The functions are defined globally on all of V. Every projective trivialization sees the same q_n∘p, so all chart overlaps agree term by term. There are no separately selected chart approximants requiring a gluing argument. If h is finite continuous, a compact subset of V has compact image in W, and the locally uniform conclusion of n1 pulls back.

These are genuine higher-dimensional normal unibranch examples. P(E) is smooth over K and has dimension r+1. Berkovich PMIHES 78 (1993), Corollary 2.2.7, printed pages 42–43, passes regularity and normality to the algebraic analytification and hence its ordinary open subspace X. Regular local rings are normal domains; a normal local ring is unibranch because its normalization is itself. No normalization descent occurs in this proof. Nontrivial projective-bundle transition functions are permitted. For example A_K^{1,an}×(P_K^r)^an has the result for all its globally defined classical psh functions, including pullbacks of log|t−a| with value −∞ at their poles. No torus invariance hypothesis is imposed.

Scalar compatibility. For any analytic extension K'/K, let π:V_{K'}→V be the projection. GJR 5.4(2) and 7.6(2) make v_n∘π strict real-PL and semipositive over K'. At each y', v_n(π(y')) decreases to u(π(y')). GR Proposition 4.2(5) also verifies that the target pullback remains classical psh. These formulas commute with p_{K'} and all projective coordinate transitions. Finite separable fields used in n1 only construct descended functions; they do not replace the assertion about every analytic extension by one about algebraic extensions. The extension space may be nonmetrizable. We pull back an existing sequence and do not claim countable extraction for arbitrary new starting functions over K'.

The overlap conclusion is local in the precise requested sense. A construction at one point supplies one sequence globally on W and one globally on p^{-1}(W), with exact compatibility throughout its charts. Independently constructed sequences near different base points need not agree on their common base open set. No global sequence on an arbitrary union of such neighbourhoods is asserted.

The domain must contain whole projective fibres. A psh function defined only on a nonsaturated ordinary open chart need not descend. Thus this is an unrestricted starting-function sufficient sector on the displayed X, not a characterization of every arbitrary higher-dimensional psh germ.
