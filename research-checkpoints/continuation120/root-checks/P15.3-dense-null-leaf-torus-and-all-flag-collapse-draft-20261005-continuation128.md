# A dense-null-leaf torus and collapse for every usual smooth flag

This is an unreviewed own proposed argument for P15.3. Its precise scope is the usual perturbative current-valuation body on smooth modifications. It is not an impossibility theorem for every richer proposed geometric construction and does not close the original problem.

## 1. Surface and numerical data

Choose a complex two-dimensional torus M=V/Lambda with no nonzero integral (1,1)-classes. Such tori exist by the Baire argument already proved in the reviewed round50 paper: for each nonzero integral alternating form, being of type (1,1) is a proper real-analytic condition on linear complex structures, and there are only countably many such forms. Every compatible positive constant Hermitian form gives a Kähler form Omega_M. In particular M has no curves. Indeed the class of a nonzero effective divisor would be an integral (1,1)-class with positive pairing against Omega_M, hence nonzero.

Choose a complex line W in V whose image is dense in M. A generic line has this property: the failure of density means W is contained in the kernel of some nonzero integral real character of the real torus, and these are countably many proper closed conditions on the space of complex lines. This uses the elementary character description of closed subgroups of a real torus. Let beta be a nonzero constant semipositive Hermitian (1,1)-form of rank1 with kernel W. Put alpha=[beta]. Then

$$\beta^2=0,\qquad\int_M\beta\wedge\Omega_M>0.$$

Thus alpha is nef, has numerical dimension1 in the smooth nef intersection definition, and has zero class volume. The last assertion follows directly: a hypothetical Kähler current T in alpha with T>=delta Omega_M would give

$$0=\int_MT\wedge\beta\geq\delta\int_M\Omega_M\wedge\beta>0.$$

The positive smooth current beta also has largest nonzero actual nonpluripolar product degree1. Its constant analytic approximation gives the same current-product degrees in Cao Definition3. This is a statement about that general current product, not a claim that the transcendental alpha is an actual line bundle in Cao Definition4. Since the divisor span is zero, alpha is genuinely transcendental.

## 2. Rigidity of the entire positive-current class

I propose that PSH(M,beta) consists exactly of constants. Let phi be such a quasi-psh potential; it is bounded above on compact M. For each affine complex leaf of W, its pullback to C is subharmonic or identically minus infinity, because beta restricts to zero there. Choose a point x where phi is finite. On its leaf the pullback is a bounded-above nontrivial subharmonic function on C, hence constant by the subharmonic Liouville theorem. The leaf is dense. Upper semicontinuity therefore gives phi>=phi(x) at every point of M. In particular phi is finite everywhere.

The same reasoning now applies to every leaf. Comparing the constants on any two dense leaves by upper semicontinuity in both directions shows that they agree. Thus phi is globally constant. Conversely constants are beta-psh. The dd^c lemma on the compact Kähler torus consequently gives a unique positive closed current in alpha, namely beta. The ordinary zero-obstacle P-envelope of the smooth reference0 is0. This is therefore the simplest possible prescribed P-type, rather than nonanalytic singularity data.

## 3. Rigidity survives every smooth Kähler modification

Let pi:X -> M be any proper bimeromorphic morphism with X a smooth compact Kähler surface. The exact potential descent proof from round50 applies without bigness: a pi*beta-psh potential descends off the exceptional image, stays bounded above, extends as a beta-psh potential across that analytic image, and agrees with its pullback almost everywhere and hence as the canonical quasi-psh representative. Thus PSH(X,pi*beta) is also the constants, and pi*beta is the unique positive current in pi*alpha. This repeats the actual proof of DRWNXZ Lemma2.1 with its otherwise retained nonbig restriction checked, rather than importing that statement outside its standing domain.

For any smooth connected flag X superset Y1 superset {q}, the image pi(Y1) is a point: a positive-dimensional image would be a curve on M, which was excluded. Consequently

$$\int_{Y_1}\pi^*\beta=0.$$

The argument quantifies over every such modification and flag. No selected bad flag is being substituted for a possible good flag.

## 4. The two valuation coordinates both tend uniformly to zero

Fix an arbitrary Kähler form Omega_X on X. For epsilon>0, alpha_X+epsilon[Omega_X], where alpha_X=pi*alpha, is Kähler. Take a positive analytic current T_epsilon in this class, of the exact sort whose valuations generate the usual DRWNXZ body. Write t_epsilon=nu(T_epsilon,Y1). Divisor subtraction gives S_epsilon=T_epsilon-t_epsilon[Y1]>=0. Its analytic residual has generic Lelong number0 on Y1, so its restriction there exists, is positive, and gives the second coordinate a_epsilon=nu(S_epsilon|Y1,q).

First, t_epsilon tends to0 uniformly over all such currents as epsilon tends to0. Otherwise choose epsilon_i -> 0 with t_i>=c>0. The total masses of T_i against a fixed Kähler form are bounded by their convergent cohomology classes. Divisor subtraction gives T_i>=t_i[Y1], which also bounds t_i above by that total mass divided by the positive area of Y1. Positive-current compactness gives a weakly convergent subsequence with t_i -> t>=c; closedness, type and cohomology are retained in the current limit. Section3 forces that limit to be the smooth pi*beta. Positivity of the residual passes to the limit, so pi*beta>=t[Y1]. Taking trace measures against a fixed Kähler form is impossible: the smooth left side gives mass0 to Y1, whereas the right side has positive mass there. This proves the uniform conclusion without a Lelong semicontinuity assertion.

The particular positive-current compactness input can be checked by writing the positive coefficient matrices as measures in finitely many coordinate charts. Their total variations are bounded by the fixed trace mass; weak compactness and a diagonal subsequence give a current limit. Distributional closedness and type survive. Pairing with smooth closed test forms preserves its cohomology. This is standard current background, whose full recursive foundations have not been independently audited in this draft.

Second, positivity on the compact curve gives

$$0\leq a_\epsilon\leq\int_{Y_1}S_\epsilon
=\epsilon\int_{Y_1}\Omega_X-t_\epsilon(Y_1^2).$$

The degree identity uses alpha_X.Y1=0. Its right side tends uniformly to0 by the first step. It holds with the actual fixed self-intersection Y1^2; no sign of that number needs to be assumed.

Thus both coordinates of every analytic valuation tend uniformly to0. The bounds pass to their Euclidean closure, or their convex hull if that formulation is used. The smooth current pi*beta+epsilon Omega_X has valuation(0,0), so all these bodies contain the origin. Their Hausdorff limit is therefore exactly{(0,0)}, and

$$\bigcap_{\epsilon>0}\Delta_{Y_\bullet}(\pi^*\alpha+\epsilon[\Omega_X])=\{(0,0)\}.$$

For the smooth prescribed reference0, the ordinary more-singular-up-to-a-constant constraint at each positive level is automatic: every quasi-psh potential on compact X is bounded above. The usual positive-level partial construction with that constraint therefore has the same limit. The result holds for every choice of Omega_X, every smooth complete flag, and every smooth Kähler modification of M admitting such a flag.

## 5. Exact obstruction and what it does not decide

Here the retained smooth current and nef class have numerical/product dimension1, while every usual perturbative current-valuation limit on every allowed smooth modification has affine dimension0. Consequently no extension agreeing with those usual positive-level bodies and taking their unrescaled perturbative intersection can also assert a universal equality of its intrinsic dimension with that numerical dimension, even for the smooth ordinary P-type0.

This would establish a geometric obstruction stronger than failure of a single inappropriate flag. It still does not disprove every different lower-dimensional realization, rescaling of valuation coordinates, nonstandard carrier or richer construction asked for by P15.3. Any such alternative must specify the extra scale and carrier and prove its geometry and independence; merely appending a label or a numerically chosen shape is insufficient.

The native author should independently test existence/density, potential restrictions, nonbig descent, all-current compactness and divisorial subtraction, analytic restriction, and uniformity of the two coordinate bounds. A fatal flaw is to be retained as a rejected attempt. Even if all steps hold, the original scope conclusion must be judged from the unchanged task, not promoted from this limited no-go statement.
