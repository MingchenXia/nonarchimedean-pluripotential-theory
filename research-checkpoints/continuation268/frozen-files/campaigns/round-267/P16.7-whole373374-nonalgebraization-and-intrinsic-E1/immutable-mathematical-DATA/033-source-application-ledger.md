## Missing algebraization bridge: precise nonapplication

CLD Appendix B.6.1–B.6.4, printed pp. 592–593, was read in full. It states that analytic line bundles in a split abeloid uniformization have descent data (M,u,r); symmetry of the descent pairing yields a symmetric integral character form. It states an ampleness criterion involving the reduction of M and positive definiteness of the induced quadratic form. Its essential classification/ampleness proofs are deferred to Lütkebohmert, *Rigid Geometry of Curves and Their Jacobians*, Section 6.2 and Theorem 6.4.4. Those full immediate proofs were not retrieved.

The public Springer Chapter 6 abstract of Fresnel–van der Put, *Rigid Analytic Geometry and Its Applications*, confirms that algebraic torus quotients are characterized by a Riemann-form condition. The abstract is not the proof. Full-body requests for both cited books failed. Conrad's *Higher-level canonical subgroups* Lemma 3.2.1 and its immediate proof were retrieved as geometric background; that lemma concerns polarization of an abelian scheme over a normal base and cites further foundations. It is **not applied** to our quotient and does not provide the required bridge.

To turn our period obstruction into a proof excluding **any** algebraization one still needs an applicable, audited argument that: (i) a proper scheme/algebraic-space algebraization of this analytic group admits descent of its analytic multiplication/inverse/identity by proper GAGA; (ii) the resulting connected smooth proper algebraic group is projective and has a polarization; (iii) its ample analytic line bundle produces precisely the symmetric positive period condition calculated in the paper. Neither failure of one presentation to be projective nor absence of one chosen equivariant ample bundle proves (i)–(iii). Consequently actual nonalgebraizability is not claimed certified, even though the calculated lattice is the expected nonpolarizable candidate.
