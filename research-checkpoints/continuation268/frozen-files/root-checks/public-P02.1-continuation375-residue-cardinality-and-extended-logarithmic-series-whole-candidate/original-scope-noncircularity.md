The two input classes used in the new proofs are defined before approximation: a specified indicator of the Gauss fibre on an actual strict affinoid polydisc, and a countable sum of logarithmic absolute values of globally given bounded analytic functions. Neither definition uses PL limits, generated psh stages, an envelope property, or a positivity current. Their classical psh status is proved using authenticated log/cone laws and a decreasing-limit argument that explicitly checks scalar curve tests.

Semipositivity of the approximants is geometric residue/model nefness. The proof constructs legal strict Laurent-domain PL expressions first, then uses the authenticated classical/real-PL comparison. It does not assume a continuous current-psh function is classical, or assume that any real-PL maximum remains strict real-PL. Every truncation radius is |a|^j for an actual field element; positive real coefficients are applied only after these integral truncations are constructed.

This gives partial progress beyond the supplied coefficient-Laurent, algebraically-closed-residue example and beyond finite continuous invariant toric potentials. It does not give a new all-functions geometric regime for arbitrary local germs, and no such regime is advertised. The positive logarithmic-sector theorem has an extra starting-function hypothesis. The negative example has relative boundary and refutes only sequential regularization. Neither is rebranded as the original characterization.

The series sequence is constructed from its explicitly countable input family. The boundary-jump sequence uses an enumerated countable family of irreducible residue polynomials and a proved cofinal chain. Neither argument extracts a sequence from an unrestricted net on a nonmetrizable space. For uncountable residue field the proof instead excludes every continuous sequence.

All local expressions in each construction are restrictions of one formula on one actual domain. Analytic/scalar pullbacks preserve these formulas and their limits. No termwise agreement is claimed between independently chosen sequences on different ordinary neighborhoods. General tropical overlap construction and arbitrary singular normalization descent remain open here.
