P02.1 remains unresolved. This directory is a completed partial-research implementation deliverable, not an accepted solution of the original atom.

The manuscript proves the following exact statements.

1. For every complete nontrivially valued K and n at least two, the function u=1_{p^{-1}(eta)} on the strict closed n-polydisc is finite bounded usc and classical psh in the full scalar-extension curve-test sense. The point x=(eta,0,...,0) lies in the actual relative boundary. This particular function has local continuous sequential approximation near x if and only if the residue field of K is countable. In the countable case there is a globally defined decreasing semipositive real-PL sequence. In the uncountable case no pointwise continuous sequence exists on any ordinary neighborhood of x, even without monotonicity. A decreasing semipositive real-PL net exists for every K.
2. On any good strictly K-analytic X over such K, every series u=sum_i lambda_i log|f_i| with analytic f_i bounded in absolute value by one and positive real lambda_i has one explicit decreasing semipositive real-PL sequence. Extended negative infinity is allowed, without summability assumptions. This is a sector of starting functions given by analytic input data, not a theorem about every classical psh function.
3. Under every analytic extension the constructed nets/sequences converge to the pullbacks of their original targets. A native boundary jump over the new field may be a different function. An explicit Gauss-valued extension of Q_p to an uncountable residue field demonstrates the distinction.

No conclusion is asserted for trivially valued fields, every psh germ in a boundaryless higher-dimensional space, arbitrary noninvariant functions without a logarithmic-series representation, general normalization descent, or a sharp characterization of all good strict spaces. No additional P02 atom is counted. Ordinary CLD smoothness, weak smoothness, locally Fubini--Study and semipositive real PL remain separate.

The negative result is only a sequence obstruction. It does not refute any interpretation of P02.1 allowing arbitrary decreasing nets, since its own net proves approximation for the same function. Countable residue field is sufficient only for the displayed jump, not for the original all-functions property.

The literal original quantifiers remain: a geometric classification for every arbitrary admissible classical psh input, near every point, with the requested real-PL positivity and precise sequence/net convention. Those quantifiers have not been established.
