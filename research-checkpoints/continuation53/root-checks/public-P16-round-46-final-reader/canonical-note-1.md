1. Boucksom–Favre–Jonsson, arXiv:1201.0187v2, Singular semipositive metrics in non-Archimedean geometry. The standing field is complete discretely valued with residue characteristic zero; X is a smooth projective analytification and L is ample. Theorems A and B establish normalized psh compactness and decreasing model regularization in that domain. These saved statements cannot be used as arbitrary-field replacements merely by deleting discreteness.

2. Boucksom–Favre–Jonsson, arXiv:1201.0188v1, Solution to a non-Archimedean Monge–Ampère equation. This cache is the actual available v1. Its Theorem A assumes a complete discrete valuation field of residue characteristic zero, a smooth projective variety defined over a one-variable function field admitting K as a completion, an ample L, and a positive Radon measure of raw mass (L^n) supported on one SNC dual complex. The introduction separately records curve background over any complete nontrivially valued field. Later statements removing algebraizability cannot be attributed to this v1. Its main theorem is not an arbitrary-support higher-dimensional application.

3. Boucksom–Eriksson, arXiv:1805.01016v3, Spaces of norms, determinant of cohomology and Fekete points in non-Archimedean geometry. This is the spaces-of-norms paper. Theorem A identifies relative volume with energy for continuous psh metrics on an ample line bundle over a geometrically reduced projective scheme and any complete valued field. Definition 7.2 and Lemma 7.4 concern decreasing nets of Fubini–Study metrics and closure under decreasing limits. Corollary 7.6 identifies continuous psh metrics with their uniform closure. Theorem 7.8 identifies these with uniform limits of nef model metrics when K is nontrivially valued and L is ample. Theorems 7.14 and 8.4 give the genuine mixed operator for continuous psh metrics, continuously under uniform convergence. Proposition 8.3 gives raw intersection mass and integration by parts; Definition 9.13 and Proposition 9.14 give continuous energy and its cocycle identity. These are valid background for X=P^1 and L=O(d). They do not themselves prove decreasing continuity for unbounded potentials. The smooth-curve continuity-of-envelopes endpoint is explicitly recorded in the introduction and after Conjecture 7.30.

4. Boucksom–Jonsson, arXiv:1801.08229v4, Global pluripotential theory over a trivially valued field. Theorem A constructs the finite-energy extension and an embedding with dense image; surjectivity is stated for smooth X when characteristic is zero or dimension is at most two. The field in this formulation is algebraically closed and trivially valued. No passage from this theorem to nontrivial valuations was proved here.

5. Boucksom–Jonsson, arXiv:2307.01697v1, Measures of finite energy in pluripotential theory: a synthetic approach. The source expressly says: “The emphasis in this paper is on measures of finite energy, as opposed to potentials of finite energy, that we do not seek to investigate here”. Theorem A and Theorems 2.23–2.24 establish strong density of test Monge–Ampère measures and a complete Dirichlet quasi-metric on finite-energy measures under orthogonality. Example 2.20 states orthogonality for the projective Berkovich formalism, using continuous-volume results and a uniform differentiation estimate. The proof of density compares the test-envelope energy at ψ_i+t f with the dual-energy upper bound, obtaining t∫f MA(ψ_i) ≤ t∫f μ + J(μ,ψ_i) + A t²; a maximizing sequence therefore gives convergence of measures. This argument does not extract an actual unbounded psh potential. Completeness of the target and density of the image do not alone imply surjectivity from the geometric potential space. Synthetic orthogonality is also distinct from continuity of every psh envelope. No arbitrary-field identification of numerical cohomology spaces was imported.

6. Pille–Schneider, arXiv:2209.04879v2, Global pluripotential theory on hybrid spaces. Theorem 4.3 gives the continuous operator over complete valued fields. Remark 4.4 states: “While the class E¹(X,L) of finite-energy psh metrics on L is defined over any non-archimedean field K, it is unclear in general how to extend the Monge-Ampère operator on the latter.” It then records the discrete characteristic-zero and trivial endpoints. The actual BFJ standing restriction was checked separately as residue characteristic zero; the abbreviated wording does not justify mixed-characteristic applications.

7. Gubler–Rabinoff, arXiv:2506.13548v2, dated 17 September 2025, Classically psh and pluriharmonic functions on Berkovich spaces. It allows arbitrary complete non-Archimedean fields, including trivial valuations, and proves properties of classically psh functions and maximum principles. Its introduction explicitly says that regularization or construction of a Monge–Ampère measure might require additional hypotheses and is not its subject. Appendix A supplies the disk-tree topology used as background below. Coordinate disks in Z and 1/Z are identified along their overlap; their intersection is not just the Gauss point. No higher-dimensional operator follows from the maximum principle alone.

8. Thuillier, thesis tel-00010990v1. Chapter 2 assumes a complete nontrivially valued field, without a discrete-valuation requirement. The curves are smooth and strictly analytic; Section 3.5 additionally assumes connected and proper. P^1 satisfies these hypotheses. Theorem 3.3.13 solves the mass-zero Laplace equation for currents; Lemma 3.3.12 identifies its kernel with harmonic functions; Theorem 3.4.19 regularizes subharmonic line-bundle metrics. Proposition 3.5.7 equates μ−μ(T)δ_x belonging to dd^c W¹, the Green potential belonging to W¹, and its μ-integrability. Original PDF page 117, printed page 103, was inspected and confirms the full raw-mass coefficient. Original PDF page 111 confirms that increasing norms correspond to decreasing additive weights. An isolated displayed curvature sign on printed page 97 is inconsistent with that local convention; the partial proof fixes its sign independently by integration by parts. No unproved field descent or identification of arbitrary smooth metrics with algebraic model metrics is used.

9. Berkovich, Etale cohomology for non-Archimedean analytic spaces, PMIHES 78 (1993). This is general analytic background, not a finite-energy Monge–Ampère theorem. No indispensable new application of its cohomological results is claimed.

10. Chambert-Loir–Ducros, arXiv:1204.6277v2, July 2025. The actual expanded v2 has transfinite psh definitions in Section 8.1. Original PDF page 207 was inspected: Lemma 8.5.5 explicitly states “Pour toute fonction continue psh u”. Proposition 8.7.2 requires continuous functions and local uniform convergence. The shorthand in 8.7.1 must be read with the product hypotheses it cites. The audited statements supply neither a global unbounded E¹ operator nor an all-support finite-energy equation. Earlier-version section numbers cannot be transferred to this v2.
