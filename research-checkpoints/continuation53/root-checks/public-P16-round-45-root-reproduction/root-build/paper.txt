3. Analytic normalization, conductor and PL descent
Normalization is nite for a variety of nite type over a eld. A normal curve over the perfect eld
k is smooth. Thus Y is a smooth integral projective curve. A unibranch curve has exactly one point
q above each closed point p of X ; since k is algebraically closed, both residue elds are k . For this
curve argument these are the precise consequences of unibranchness that are needed.
Every point of Y an is either the trivial valuation o, a point t ordq for q ∈ Y (k) and 0 < t < ∞, or
the endpoint q∞ with support q . Indeed a valuation of k(Y ) has a center by properness. If that
center is the generic point its valuation is trivial. Otherwise the local ring is a DVR, every unit
has value zero, and the valuation is a positive multiple of its order valuation. A semivaluation with
nongeneric support is the trivial valuation on the closed point's residue eld. The same function eld
is k(X). A nite birational normalization therefore bijects generic-support semivaluations, and the
unique-preimage condition bijects the closed-support semivaluations. Consequently ν an is bijective.
It is continuous, with compact source and Hausdor target, and is therefore a homeomorphism.
This proof does not claim that a normalization of an arbitrary singular variety is an analytic
homeomorphism.
