L24: 【8†Lemma 28.16.2.】 A normal scheme is geometrically unibranch.
L25: Proof. This follows from the definitions. Namely, a scheme is normal if the local rings are normal domains. It is immediate from the More on Algebra, Definition 【15†15.108.1】 that a local normal domain is geometrically unibranch. $\square$
