## 2. Lower and upper barriers for a strongly convergent sequence

Let normalized genuine u_j converge to normalized u in d_R. Equivalently by own156 they converge divisorially and in energy. To establish continuity it suffices to show every subsequence has a further subsequence with the desired relative L1 limit. Choose a subsequence, relabelled u_j, with summable consecutive d_R distances and tails tending to zero. Own155 constructs actual genuine E1 lower barriers

    p_j=R(inf_(k>=j)u_k),

where this notation means the actual decreasing limit of finite rooftops. They satisfy p_j<=u_k for all k>=j and

    0<=E(u_j)-E(p_j)<=sum_(k>=j)d_R(u_k,u_(k+1))->0.

Thus p_j converge to u in energy and in divisorial coordinates, using d_R(p_j,u_j)->0 and the triangle inequality plus own156's common-upper ordered separation. They increase and are <=0. General theta-EP makes their usc supremum genuine. At each divisorial point its value equals sup_j p_j by Theorem5.6, and that supremum is u there. The exact ample all-point determination then identifies this usc supremum with u. Its discrepancy from the pointwise supremum is negligible in Omega, hence pluripolar and nu-null. Therefore

    p_j increases pointwise to u nu-almost everywhere.

In particular p_j<=u on the whole carrier: the genuine usc supremum u dominates each p_j.

For upper barriers put q_j=sup^*_(k>=j)u_k, using the actual usc upper envelope. General theta-EP makes q_j genuine. They are normalized with generic value zero and <=V_theta. Theorem5.6 gives q_j(z)=sup_(k>=j)u_k(z) at every divisorial point; these values decrease to u(z). Their energies are bounded below by those of any tail endpoint, so they lie in E1. Their decreasing genuine limit exists by own150 and agrees divisorially with u, hence agrees everywhere by ample determination. Thus

    q_j decreases pointwise to u on the whole carrier.

The resulting inequalities are p_j<=u_j<=q_j and p_j<=u<=q_j.

## 3. Relative integration without absolute reference integrability

On the complement of the reference poles define each nonnegative gap V_theta-p_j, V_theta-q_j and V_theta-u. All potentials are finite nu-almost everywhere by the full polar-nullness of own158. Since p_1 is genuine E1, its relative gap is integrable even though its generic value need not be zero: subtract its finite generic value, apply the defining dual condition, then add the constant back in the integral.

For all j, both barrier gaps are bounded by the integrable V_theta-p_1. Their pointwise limits outside a nu-null set are the same gap V_theta-u. Ordinary dominated convergence gives

    integral(V_theta-p_j)dnu -> integral(V_theta-u)dnu,
    integral(V_theta-q_j)dnu -> integral(V_theta-u)dnu.

The pointwise squeeze yields, on that same full-measure set,

    |u_j-u|<=q_j-p_j.

The integral of the right side is the difference of the two finite relative gaps and tends to zero. Hence

    integral |u_j-u| dnu ->0.                           (3.1)

No assertion that V_theta itself is absolutely nu-integrable is made. Every difference is defined off its null pole set and compared by finite relative integrals.

The subsequence argument gives (3.1) for the entire original convergent sequence: otherwise a subsequence with errors bounded below by one positive number would admit the fast further subsequence just treated, a contradiction.

## 4. Arbitrary strongly convergent nets and the unresolved weak step

The domain carries the genuine metric d_R on normalized actual functions. If a d_R-convergent net failed the relative L1 conclusion, select for each positive integer j one of its sufficiently late bad indices with d_R distance to u below 2^(-j) and relative L1 error bounded below by one fixed positive number. This selected sequence contradicts Section3. It need not be cofinal in the original directed set; it is used only to test continuity of a map from a metric space. Thus (3.1) holds for arbitrary strongly convergent original nets, without a countability assumption on the field, the psh cone or the operator construction.

In particular, normalized E(u_j)->E(u) together with original divisorial convergence implies convergence of the target relative integration and of F_nu(u_j). This does not make F_nu upper semicontinuous for merely weakly convergent nets with a common energy bound, because their energies may have an upward jump at the limit. Nor does it prove normalized metric completeness, a quantitative coercivity estimate, strong Cauchy behavior of maximizing nets, finite dual energy of all operator outputs, comparison, domination or arbitrary-target existence/uniqueness.
