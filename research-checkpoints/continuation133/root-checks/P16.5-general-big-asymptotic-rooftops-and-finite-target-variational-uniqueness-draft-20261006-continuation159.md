## 1. Every E1 singularity recovers every E1 endpoint by shifted rooftops

For arbitrary genuine E1 w,v, put

    r_c=R(w+c,v),
    a_c=max(w,v-c),

for real c tending to infinity. The functions a_c decrease pointwise to w on the whole original carrier, including its poles, and have finite energies by genuine maximum closure. Their generic values eventually equal the finite generic value of w. Own145 decreasing energy continuity gives E(a_c)->E(w).

    E(r_c)+E(a_c)+cV>=E(w)+cV+E(v).

The rooftop lies below v, so

    0<=E(v)-E(r_c)<=E(a_c)-E(w)->0.                     (1.1)

Each r_c is actual genuine E1, increases with c, and is <=v. By own155's ordered-pair identity,

    d_R(r_c,v)=E(v)-E(r_c)->0.                          (1.2)

The common upper bound is that of the fixed endpoint v. Own156's uniform ordered energy separation at each rational divisor and generic point makes all its coordinates converge to those of v; convex-ray bounds extend this to all real divisorial coordinates. Thus the shift/rooftop construction has an actual original function as its strong limit. No abstract completed element or arbitrary modification lift is used.

In particular, taking v=V_theta gives

    R(w+c,V_theta)->V_theta in d_R.

Its divisorial values are those of v, either by the published nonnegligibility of divisorial points or by the genuine compactness/increasing-net construction. ## 2. Uniqueness of the finite-divisorial variational maximizer

Let nu=sum_i p_i delta_z_i with distinct original rational divisorial z_i, p_i>0 and sum p_i=V. By own146 its intrinsic relative dual supremum is finite and attained by an actual continuous-model obstacle envelope, with independently defined operator equal to nu.

For every genuine E1 w, its values a_i=w(z_i) are finite. Own146's finite-constraint construction gives a continuous-model obstacle envelope u_a with

    w<=u_a on X^an,
    u_a(z_i)=a_i=w(z_i) for every target vertex.          (2.1)

The extra model-vertex bounds in that construction impose no further constraint on genuine functions satisfying the target inequalities, by the fixed ample divisorial growth bound and generic maximum property.     F_nu(u_a)-F_nu(w)=E(u_a)-E(w)>=0.

Hence every genuine E1 maximizer is in the continuous-model envelope domain. The finite-parameter derivative argument of own146 gives M_theta(u_a)=nu for such a maximizer. Own157's model-envelope uniqueness then makes all these maximizers differ by one constant. In normalized generic value zero, the variational maximizer is unique on the entire genuine E1 cone.

This proves actual attainment and uniqueness of the variational maximizer for every finite rational-divisorial raw-mass target, and its genuine equation. It does not prove that an arbitrary genuine E1 w satisfying M_theta(w)=nu is a variational maximizer: the supporting inequality in an unbounded genuine direction is still missing. Thus uniqueness of all operator solutions is not silently substituted for uniqueness of maximizers.
