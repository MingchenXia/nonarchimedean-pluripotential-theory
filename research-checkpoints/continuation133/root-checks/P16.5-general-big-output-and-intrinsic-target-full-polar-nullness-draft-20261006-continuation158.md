# General big classes: full polar nullness of outputs and intrinsic targets

This unreviewed note extends own152 from algebraic/reference poles to every non-Archimedean pluripolar subset, using a uniform moment for sections of one fixed auxiliary ample bundle. It also makes explicit that own153's finite-energy barrier works for poles of any genuine ample psh function, not only theta-psh functions. It proves neither output dual finiteness nor weighted E1 integration. All own143--145/150 dependencies remain, and original P16.5 remains open.

## 1. Genuine auxiliary ample stages

Retain the original smooth projective characteristic-zero trivially valued domain, actual big rational L, reference V_theta and raw mass V=vol(L)>0. Let a be any genuine psh function in any fixed numerical class, normalized at the generic point to zero. Choose an actual ample rational Omega dominating that class by an ample difference. The direct zero-FS construction of own145 puts a tail of its original decreasing GF defining net into the same actual Omega without changing any functions. Denote the resulting original net by g_i. Its generic values c_i decrease to zero; take a tail with 0<=c_i<=1, so g_i<=1.

A section attaining the maximal generic weight of g_i, in degree M_i Omega, has canonical normalized metric q_i=log|s_i|/M_i and satisfies

    q_i+c_i<=g_i,
    q_i<=g_i.                                           (1.1)

No finite energy on either theta or Omega is assigned to g_i. The divisorial and proper-support cases remain in the entire original carrier. For each K>0 the function

    b_i,K=min(1-g_i,K)

is continuous on that carrier, including the zero norm locus, nonnegative, and <=K. It satisfies b_i,K<=1-q_i on rational divisorial points, where all quantities are finite.

## 2. Uniform moments of the deficient measures

Fix normalized u in the entire proposed E1(theta) and put B=-E_theta(u)<infinity. Its cofinal nonpositive model majorants f_D have energy >=-B. For fixed c>B/V use the actual full and deficient measures of own150:

    0<=mu_D,c<=mu_D,
    ||mu_D-mu_D,c||_TV<=B/c,
    mu_D -> M_theta(u) weakly against all original C0.

For the effective divisor S_i=div(s_i), on a common smooth model the pulled divisor decomposes into an effective horizontal part and vertical part sum_j b_j x_j(s_i)E_j. Positivity of the volume differential on the effective horizontal part gives

    integral(-q_i) dmu_D,c
       <=<alpha_D(c)^n>.rho^*Omega
       <=<gamma(c)^n>.rho^*Omega
       =c n <L^(n-1)>.Omega =:C_c.                      (2.1)

The second inequality pairs the positive-product order with the nef pulled Omega. The last equality is the actual product-volume differential of own154. The division by M_i in q_i exactly cancels the numerical class M_i Omega of S_i. Thus C_c is independent of i, of the chosen section system and of D and its refinement. This is the key difference from controlling only one fixed algebraic divisor.

Equations (1.1), (2.1), the deficient mass bound <=V, and the coefficientwise tail inequality give

    integral b_i,K dmu_D<=V+C_c+K B/c.                  (2.2)

No uniform bound on C_c as c tends to infinity is asserted. In fact its displayed linear growth is expected.

## 3. Passing to the entire operator and then to a pole set

For every fixed i,K, b_i,K is an actual original continuous test, so pass D through its genuine cofinal net in (2.2). Since the g_i decrease, b_i,K increase pointwise to b_K=min(1-a,K). The b_K are Borel and bounded by K. For a net, their integrals converge to the integral of b_K by the monotone theorem for directed increasing lsc functions on a compact space and a Radon measure; equivalently use the compact-superlevel argument with continuous b_i,K to obtain the same supremum of integrals. This passage does not replace the original i net by a cofinal sequence. Hence

    integral b_K dM_theta(u)<=V+C_c+K B/c.

Now let positive integers K tend to infinity after this passage. The bounded functions b_K/K converge pointwise to the indicator of {a=-infinity}. Ordinary dominated convergence for the finite Radon measure yields

    M_theta(u)({a=-infinity})<=B/c.

Finally let c tend to infinity. Thus M_theta(u) gives zero mass to the pole set of every genuine psh function in every numerical class. Any pluripolar set, being contained in such a pole set, is contained in a Borel null set. There is no use of an uncountable union of individual Zariski null sets. In particular the already needed reference-pole convention follows again.

This conclusion does not prove that integral(-a) or integral(V_theta-v) is finite: (2.2) keeps a term proportional to K, and the fixed-c moment bound grows with c. Polar nullness and unbounded weighted integrability remain distinct.

## 4. Intrinsic finite-dual targets exclude the same polar sets

The barrier proof in own153 uses only a normalized fixed-ample GF net g_i, the injective actual products

    s_i^(beta m/M_i) H^0(X,m(L-beta Omega)),

and the ordinary C1 volume loss V-vol(L-beta Omega)<=C beta. Its construction therefore applies without change to the g_i of Section1: it never used that a itself was theta-psh. For each 0<gamma<1/2 it gives an actual genuine theta-E1 b with

    b<=-(1-a)^gamma,
    {a=-infinity} subset {b=-infinity}.

Every stage of that proof remains the one in own153: continuous capped obstacles, an integrable dyadic rank tail in actual H0(mL), finite generic lower bounds, genuine decreasing closure, and decreasing energy continuity. It does not introduce an auxiliary ample finite-energy space for the original unknown.

Consequently a positive original Radon target nu of mass V with nu({V_theta=-infinity})=0 and finite intrinsic relative dual supremum integrates V_theta-b outside the reference poles. It gives zero mass to {a=-infinity} for every a of Section1, and therefore to every pluripolar subset. This is still only a necessary consequence of its dual condition. The argument does not show that polar nullness is sufficient, nor that all M_theta(u) have finite dual energy.
