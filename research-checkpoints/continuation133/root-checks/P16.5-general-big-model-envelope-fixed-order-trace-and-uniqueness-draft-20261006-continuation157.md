# General big model envelopes: fixed-order traces and uniqueness

This unreviewed note uses own143--146/150/156 and one explicit ordinary algebraic volume equality theorem to prove uniqueness modulo constants for the proposed operator on envelopes of real continuous model obstacles. It also identifies their central divisorial values with asymptotic fixed orders on the actual compactified test model. It does not extend uniqueness to all genuine E1 functions or solve the original arbitrary-target inverse problem. Original P16.5 remains open.

## 1. The actual section formula for the central fixed order

Retain the whole general-big domain of own143. On a smooth equivariant snc model Y->X times P1 write F=sum b_i E_i, a rational vertical divisor D=sum d_i E_i, the original obstacle f_D(E_i)=d_i/b_i, and

    alpha_D(c)=rho^*L+D+cF.

Choose rational c sufficiently large that every actual degree-m lattice weight w_m,j of D has mc+w_m,j>=0, and alpha_D(c) is big. A single c works in all divisible degrees because the weights lie between m min f_D and m max f_D. For a nonzero actual s in H^0(X,mL), let W_m(D;s) be its real intrinsic obstacle weight. The largest integer lambda for which t^(-lambda)s extends over A1 in m(rho^*L+D) is floor W_m(D;s), by the whole-carrier section criterion of own144. Thus the global section

    t^[-mc-floor W_m(D;s)]s

of m alpha_D(c) has central zero order along E_i equal to

    ord_Ei(s)+m d_i-b_i floor W_m(D;s).                  (1.1)

The c terms cancel between the t power and the divisor cF. More generally an adapted lattice basis splits the pushforward as direct summands O(mc+w_m,j); all summands have nonnegative degree. Its global sections generate each summand over a neighborhood of zero. Consequently the minimum central vanishing order of the global section space is exactly the minimum of (1.1). Arbitrary combinations cannot lower that minimum by the valuation inequality, and the displayed sections realize the necessary generators.

Let sigma_Ei(alpha) be the asymptotic minimal actual section order of a big rational bundle alpha on Y, equivalently the coefficient of its divisorial negative part. Divide (1.1) by m and use x_i=ord_Ei/b_i restricted to X. Every section metric

    q_s+floor W_m(D;s)/m

is genuine theta-psh and lies below f_D, hence below p_D=P_theta(f_D). This bounds all the divided orders below by b_i[f_D(x_i)-p_D(x_i)]. Conversely own156 Section2 supplies a section term q_s+lambda<=f_D whose value at x_i is arbitrarily close to p_D(x_i). Its arbitrarily high powers still satisfy the obstacle bound, and their integer weights differ from their allowed real weights by at most 1/m. Taking the infimum of asymptotic divided orders gives

    sigma_Ei(alpha_D(c))
       =b_i[f_D(x_i)-P_theta(f_D)(x_i)].                 (1.2)

In particular the central fixed-order trace is independent of all sufficiently large c. This proof does not assert fixed-system energy convergence; the selected individual section term can have energy -infinity.

## 2. The horizontal negative part is common to all vertical obstacles

For these same sufficiently large c, global sections of alpha_D(c) over a neighborhood of zero recover the full actual lattice and hence, over k(t), the full space H^0(X,mL). On a horizontal prime divisor, t is a unit. An adapted basis is a basis of the entire H^0(X,mL), so its minimal horizontal order is the minimal order of that full original space, independent of the adapted basis and D. Multiplication by the available powers of t cannot lower that order. Conversely the basis monomials give global sections attaining it. At a fiber away from zero, including infinity, nonnegative-degree O(mc+w) summands have sections not vanishing at that fiber; the minimum vertical order there is zero.

Thus the horizontal part of N_sigma(alpha_D(c)) is independent of D. More explicitly, its possible nonzero horizontal components are the strict transforms of fixed prime divisors of L; their orders are the corresponding actual asymptotic orders of L. A prime with dominant projection to X has zero order, since nonzero original sections do not vanish generically there. The same observation on any common refinement, still isomorphic away from the zero fiber, gives the same common horizontal negative part.

Equations (1.2) and this horizontal equality extend to real vertical D by rational coefficient approximation. The model obstacle changes uniformly, so its genuine envelope values change by at most that uniform error under constant comparison. For each fixed prime divisor, its asymptotic order is a finite convex homogeneous function of the big class and hence continuous there. Big classes remain big on the fixed approximation neighborhood. This establishes the real version without assigning literal integral powers to a real bundle.

## 3. A numerically trivial vertical divisor must vanish

We record a direct numerical rigidity fact. Let K=sum a_i E_i be a real divisor supported over zero on such a smooth connected model Y, and assume K is numerically trivial on Y. Choose an ample Cartier H on Y and put

    m_ij=E_i.E_j.H^(n-1).

For i!=j these numbers are nonnegative, and are strictly positive if E_i,E_j meet, since H is ample and their snc intersection is a nonzero effective codimension-two cycle. The vector b=(b_i) satisfies M b=0 because F.E_i.H^(n-1)=0: the fiber bundle restricts trivially to E_i. The graph of the central components is connected. Indeed the birational map to the normal product has connected fibers, and the product projection has connected fibers; their proper composite has connected fibers. For an snc fiber, connected support gives the connected component graph.

Using M b=0, direct expansion gives

    K^2.H^(n-1)
       =-sum_(i<j) b_i b_j m_ij (a_i/b_i-a_j/b_j)^2.

Numerical triviality makes the left side zero. Connectedness therefore forces a_i/b_i to be one constant a, so K=aF. Finally F.H^n>0 and numerical triviality imply a=0. Hence K=0 as a real divisor. This is stronger than identifying K only up to a fiber constant, and uses the actual compactified model.

## 4. The imported ordinary volume equality theorem

Use the following external algebraic result on a smooth projective variety Z of dimension d: for two big real divisor classes A,B, equality in

    vol(A+B)^(1/d)>=vol(A)^(1/d)+vol(B)^(1/d)

holds if and only if their numerical divisorial positive parts P_sigma(A),P_sigma(B) are proportional. This is the ordinary algebraic volume theorem of Lehmann--Xiao, Positivity functions for curves on algebraic varieties, Algebra & Number Theory13(2019), Theorem3.9 (Theorem1.6 in the introduction), DOI10.2140/ant.2019.13.1243. The primary source explicitly extends its Section3 statements to smooth varieties over algebraically closed fields in Remark3.25, with the algebraic-field inputs listed in Section2. We retain characteristic zero and smooth projective models.

## 5. Equality of model-envelope measures gives equality modulo constants

Let f_0,f_1 be real continuous model obstacles and u_i=P_theta(f_i). Assume their independently defined actual operator measures satisfy

    M_theta(u_0)=M_theta(u_1)=mu.

Own144/150 identify these with the actual obstacle derivatives. Concavity and the two support planes give

    E^obs(f_1)-E^obs(f_0)=integral(f_1-f_0)dmu.

Shift f_1 by a real constant so both energies are the same e. The measure does not change, and the integral of the shifted obstacle difference is zero because the raw mass is V. A support plane at f_0 and concavity now sandwich every segment energy:

    E^obs((1-t)f_0+t f_1)=e for all 0<=t<=1.             (5.1)

Carry both obstacles on one smooth common snc model, with vertical divisors D_0,D_1. For one sufficiently large real c, own143's eventual affine-volume formula and (5.1) give

    vol(alpha_((1-t)D0+tD1)(c))=d(cV+e)>0

for the entire segment. In particular the equal-volume endpoints and midpoint give equality in the ordinary volume Brunn--Minkowski inequality. Section4 makes their positive parts proportional. Their volumes are equal and positive, so homogeneity makes the proportionality factor one. Therefore

    P_sigma(alpha_D0(c))=P_sigma(alpha_D1(c))

as numerical divisor classes. Their negative parts have the same horizontal portion by Section2. Their positive-part difference is thus the numerically trivial vertical divisor

    K=D_0-D_1-[N_sigma(alpha_D0(c))-N_sigma(alpha_D1(c))].

Section3 makes this divisor identically zero. Its coefficient at E_i, by (1.2), is b_i[u_0(x_i)-u_1(x_i)]. Hence u_0(x_i)=u_1(x_i) at every central vertex on that common model.

Repeat the same argument after any smooth equivariant refinement. The volumes and energies are birationally invariant, so the same equality proof applies. Include each prescribed original rational divisorial point in a refinement by the finite Gauss model construction of own146. This proves equality at every rational divisorial point and at the generic point. Convex ray-coordinate continuity extends it to every real divisorial point, and the exact actual-ample G0 determination applied to their direct genuine Omega embeddings gives

    u_0=u_1 on the entire original X^an.

Undoing the constant shift proves uniqueness modulo constants in the domain of genuine envelopes of real continuous model obstacles. This includes the finite-divisorial solutions constructed in own146, whose obstacles may have real coefficients.

## 6. Exact equation scope

Own146 supplies actual existence for every positive raw-mass finite target on an arbitrary finite set of original rational divisorial points, including arbitrary common models. The present note shows that those constructed model-envelope solutions are unique modulo constants among model-envelope solutions. It does not show that an arbitrary genuine E1 solution of the same finite target must be a model envelope. In particular the new all-point separation of the rooftop metric in own156 cannot substitute for the unproved implication M(u)=M(v) =>u-v constant on the entire E1 cone.

Extending the present equality argument to unbounded endpoints requires the weighted support-plane or strict-concavity passages that remain open. Output dual energy, full comparison and domination, intrinsic coercivity, arbitrary-target attainment and the entire-E1 uniqueness theorem are not asserted.
