## 1. An explicit strict convex function, including infinite coordinates

For N≥1 choose an integer a≥log(N+1), and set, with exp(−a·∞)=0,

    χ_N(t) = a^{-1} log(1 + Σ_{i=1}^N exp(−a t_i)),    t∈(R∪{+∞})^N.

Then χ_N is continuous, convex on R^N, coordinatewise decreasing, and

    max(0,−t_1,…,−t_N) ≤ χ_N(t) ≤ max(0,−t_1,…,−t_N)+1.

At a given t, let I be its finite coordinates, let Z=1+Σ_{i∈I}exp(−a t_i), and put p_0=1/Z and p_i=exp(−a t_i)/Z for i∈I. The support

    l_t(s)=χ_N(t)−Σ_{i∈I}p_i(s_i−t_i)

has coefficient zero on infinite coordinates. Allow value −∞ when s_i=∞ for an i∈I. The log-sum-exp inequality gives χ_N≥l_t, with equality only at s=t. To see the strictness precisely, the function obtained by setting the other coordinates to ∞ is strictly convex in the finite coordinates (p_0>0). Adding a finite value in any formerly infinite coordinate strictly increases χ_N.

For every neighborhood V of t there is δ>0 with χ_N−l_t≥δ outside V. Indeed the difference is coercive in the finite coordinates: along a recession direction v≠0,

    max(0,−v_i:i∈I)+Σ_{i∈I}p_i v_i > 0,

since all p_i>0 and Σp_i<1. Thus a bounded-difference sublevel confines those coordinates to a bounded interval; the remaining coordinates have a finite lower bound. Its closure is compact in the extended product. The difference is continuous there, with value +∞ at a pole of l_t, and its zero set is {t}. Compactness gives the gap. This supplies the exact strict-support property without a Gaussian extension assertion.

### Actual affine FS approximation

Write z_0=1 and z_i=exp(−a t_i). For an integer m,

    (Σ_{i=0}^N z_i)^m = Σ_{|β|=m} binom(m;β) z^β.

If B_m=binom(m+N,N), the maximum summand is between (Σz_i)^m/B_m and (Σz_i)^m. Consequently the finite maximum

    q_m(t)=max_{|β|=m}{−Σ_{i=1}^N (β_i/m)t_i + log binom(m;β)/(a m)}

satisfies q_m≤χ_N≤q_m+log B_m/(a m), **uniformly on the whole extended product**, not just a bounded chart. Terms having β_i>0 at t_i=∞ contribute −∞, while the β_0=m term is the zero floor. Round the finite intercepts upward to rationals with a specified arbitrarily small error.

After tropicalizing regular functions f_i, each term is (1/m)log|∏f_i^{β_i}| plus an intercept. When the list includes generators of the coordinate ring, the terms β_i=1, β_0=m−1 contain each generator itself. Thus this finite maximum is actual affine FS under the retained embedding definition; generator powers alone have not been substituted for generators. Choose m_j so the uniform error is at most ε_j/4, rational intercept errors at most ε_j/4, and add ε_j with ε_{j+1}≤ε_j/4. The resulting genuine rational affine FS functions are decreasing and converge to χ_N∘Trop. All constants and inequalities can be checked from the displayed multinomial identity.

### Real support coefficients

The support composed with tropicalization is a real nonnegative linear combination Σp_i log|f_i|+C. It also has an explicit affine FS decreasing approximation. Choose a nonnegative rational affine FS exhaustion H and integers D_i≥1 with log|f_i|≤D_i H everywhere. First put F_{ij}=max(log|f_i|,2^{-j}H−j). These are genuine affine FS with the original generators retained through H, decrease to log|f_i| including −∞, and satisfy F_{ij}≤D_iH. Choose nested rational intervals q_{ij}≤p_i≤r_{ij}, with q_{ij} increasing and r_{ij} decreasing to p_i. Set

    ε_j=Σ_i D_i(r_{ij}−q_{ij}),
    L_j=Σ_i q_{ij}F_{ij}+(ε_j+2^{-j})H+C_j,

where C_j is rational and decreases to C. Then L_j≥Σp_i log|f_i|+C and L_j decreases: ε_j−ε_{j+1}≥ΣD_i(q_{i,j+1}−q_{ij}), while F_{i,j+1}≤D_iH and F_{ij}≥F_{i,j+1}. The L_j are finite maxima of rational logarithmic monomials. The positive 2^{-j}H term and the lower bounds F_{ij}≥2^{-j}H−j retain the original generators; low generator branches may be appended identically when products use powers. These are genuine affine FS and decrease to the support, including its −∞ locus: if p_i>0 and f_i vanishes, q_{ij}F_{ij} tends to −∞, while the other finitely many terms are bounded above at that fixed point. A constant floor applied only to the whole singular sum would lose proper generator growth along its zero set and is not used. Omit zero coefficients rather than multiplying zero by −∞. This real-coefficient step must not be replaced by a uniform approximation assertion at logarithmic poles.

## 2. The countable strict potential and its localized bump

Descend the finitely many defining equations and generators to a countable dense subfield K_0 and enumerate its coordinate algebra. Tropicalizations of the generators together with its first j functions give the topology: approximate arbitrary finite polynomial coefficients from K_0 on a fixed generator-bounded neighborhood to obtain the same evaluation neighborhoods. Identically zero functions can be omitted.

Let v≥1 be a rational affine FS exhaustion in the fixed generators. Polynomial degree bounds give max(0,log|f_i|,log|z_l|)≤D_j v+B_j for i≤j. Choose positive rational γ_j with

    0≤γ_j χ_{N_j}∘Trop_j ≤2^{-j}(v+1).

Then ρ=Σγ_j χ_{N_j}∘Trop_j is continuous by local uniform convergence and has upper logarithmic growth. It is a genuine affine FS decreasing limit: partial sums plus the tail majorant 2^{-r}(v+1) decrease, and each finite sum has a global uniform affine FS approximation by section 1. Summable shifts with separated upper and lower errors preserve monotonicity. Rational positive sums are finite maxima of logarithmic monomials, with dominated original generator branches retained. Hence this construction does not invoke singular EP.

For x and an open neighborhood V, choose a finite tropicalization Trop_j and a neighborhood V_j of Trop_j(x) whose inverse image lies in V. Replace its one χ term by the support l at Trop_j(x), leaving all other summands. Section 1 and the same tail construction give a genuine upper-growth affine FS decreasing limit ρ′, with

    ρ′≤ρ,    ρ′(x)=ρ(x),    ρ′≤ρ−δ on Y^an\V

for δ>0. The support may be −∞ on a divisor; its equality value at x is finite, which is all the later argument needs. No countable-cardinality assumption is imposed on a field outside this countable-density stage.

## 3. Correct localization comparison on a nonnormal affine variety

Let A be the integral affine coordinate ring, f∈A nonzero, h∈A_f, U a global rational affine FS function, and suppose

    b+a log|h| ≤ max(U,−c log|f|)+C  on (Spec A_f)^an,

with a,c>0. The denominator exponent of an arbitrary representation h=g/f^r is **not** the actual pole order. For example f=z², h=z^{-1}, a=2, c=1 disproves the printed c_1 r_1≤c argument in W5.11.

On the finite normalization Ā, every codimension-one divisor D satisfies

    a max(−ord_D h,0) ≤ c ord_D f.

For trivially valued fields this follows from the divisorial rays. For discretely valued fields use a smooth closed point of D where its leading units are nonzero, after a finite field extension, and a transverse smooth curve. On arbitrarily small analytic disks over a completed algebraic closure, f=t^e times a unit, h=t^r times a unit, while the regular polynomial branches of U are bounded above. Letting |t|→0 gives the same order inequality. The normalization is regular at generic codimension-one points and characteristic zero permits such a smooth transverse locus. Outside Z(f), h is already regular. The original growth inequality pulls back to these analytic points.

Choose positive integers p,q with q/p>c/a. Then z=h^p f^q has no height-one poles on Ā, so z∈Ā and is integral over A. The finite A-module A[z]/A vanishes after localizing at f, since z∈A_f. One fixed f^N therefore kills it, and

    f^N z^l∈A  for every l≥1.

Let x be in the original **A-polynomial hull** of a compact L⊂(Spec A_f)^an. If |log|f||≤M on L∪{x}, apply the actual A-hull inequality to f^{N+ql}h^{pl}. Divide by pl, let l→∞, and then let q/p decrease to c/a. It gives

    a log|h(x)|+b ≤ sup_L(a log|h|+b)+2cM.       (H)

No normalization hull has replaced the original hull; the fixed annihilator is what allows this conclusion. If h(x)=0 the inequality is immediate. If h vanishes throughout L, the same polynomial tests force h(x)=0. The positive f bound handles the remaining limits without subtracting infinities.

Now let v be already affine-FS-regularizable on Y_f and satisfy v≤U+C. For ε>0 rational, the function U_ε=max(U,−ε log|f|) is a genuine affine FS reference on Y_f: its arguments include the original generators and f^{-1}. The pre-EP compactification/floor construction G1–G2 gives a decreasing affine FS sequence w_i→v with |w_i−2U_ε| bounded separately for each i. Apply (H) to each branch of w_i, whose pole budget is 2ε. The floor branch is immediate, and finite maxima give

    w_i(x)≤sup_L w_i+4εM.

For continuous w_i decreasing to an usc v on a compact L, sup_L w_i decreases to sup_L v by nested compact superlevel sets. Uniform Dini convergence to v is **not** needed. Let i→∞ and then ε→0. Thus

    v(x)≤sup_L v.                              (LH)

This reconstructs W5.10 with actual pole orders. The claimed approximation with growth 2U_ε still requires its G1–G2 proof; it must not be imported via singular EP or an unrestricted local-to-global theorem.

## 5. Corrected split-piece Runge contradiction

The polynomial hull C of {π(x)} lies in π(Z). Its preimage splits into disjoint compact pieces in Z and Z′. Choose disjoint analytic neighborhoods A_1,A_2, with A_1 avoiding Z′ and A_2 avoiding Z. Properness shrinks the base neighborhood A of C so that π^{-1}A⊂A_1∪A_2.

Use finite polynomial inequalities with **strict margins** at C to choose a rational affine FS g≥0 on S such that Q={g≤1} is compact in A and g is identically zero on an open neighborhood U of C. Concretely start with a polynomial polytope defined by log|f_j|≤b_j contained in A, with C strictly satisfying every inequality, including generator bounds; choose a rational margin e>0 and set g=max(0,(log|f_j|−b_j+e)/e). Use W={g<1}, W_i=π^{-1}W∩A_i and U_i=π^{-1}U∩A_i. These are **open sets with compact closures**, not compact open sets. On W_1 let h=1, on W_2 let h=0, and glue

    h̃=max(3g∘π−1,h) on π^{-1}W;  h̃=3g∘π−1 elsewhere.

The outer reference wins on a whole collar before g=1, so G1 gives an actual continuous affine FS decreasing limit. Its G4 trace is continuous on the **whole** base: affine FS approximants converge uniformly on the compact preimage of each compact base set, and the trace difference is bounded by d times their sup norm, using positive weights summing to d. On U the trace is the sum of positive integer weights in U_1. It is consequently locally constant. Take γ to be its value at π(x), and shrink to a neighborhood of π(x) where this value is γ. Constancy on an arbitrary disconnected U is not claimed.

If γ>1, usc gives a neighborhood of x in U_1 on which u+cρ<δ/[2(γ−1)]. Since the U_1 fiber at π(x) consists only of x, properness shrinks the base again so that every U_1 fiber there is in this neighborhood. If γ=1 the division is omitted and no bound on other U_1 points is needed. Choose a relatively compact neighborhood B of x contained in this smaller U_1 preimage, disjoint from L. Let ρ′ be the strict bump at x in B and set u′=u+cρ′. Then u′(x)=0, u′≤u+cρ, and on K=∂B∩hull_Y(L), u′≤−e′ for some e′>0, with e′ including the factor c.

Set v=u′ on W_1 and 0 on W_2. Choose a rational C_0 larger than sup_{π^{-1}Q}max(u′,0)+δ+1. Define

    ṽ=max(C_0 g∘π−δ,v) on π^{-1}W;
    ṽ=C_0 g∘π−δ outside π^{-1}W.

The reference wins on a whole collar. Weak membership is established on the **single original proper witness** for u: pull back the globally genuine ρ′ and g, take the specified maxima on the two disjoint preimage pieces, and glue where they agree with the outer reference. No global sheaf gluing of unrelated proper witnesses is assumed. Its displayed bound gives global upper logarithmic growth. On U_1, ṽ=max(u′,−δ); on U_2 it is zero. In particular ṽ(x)=0 and ṽ≤−min(δ,e′) on K.

By G4, w=N_π(ṽ) is genuinely regularizable on S.     0=(w∘π)(x)≤sup_K(w∘π).

## 6. Hartogs radii with strict domains, not a false interior identity

In the countable-density setting an affine analytification is second countable: use the countable coordinate algebra and rational intervals, and approximate other coefficients on generator-bounded boxes. A Runge open D therefore has a compact exhaustion. Its compact polynomial hulls, separated from the complement in larger generator boxes, yield an increasing exhaustion by strict polynomial domains

    Q_i={|g_{ij}(y,w)|<exp(b_{ij}) for all j},

with compact closed bounds inside D and each previous closed bound strictly inside Q_{i+1}. Include an original w bound and base generator bounds. For y with (y,0)∈Q_i, write g_{ij}=Σ_l f_{ijl}(y)w^l. The exact radius is

    −log d_{Q_i}(y)=max_{j,l≥1}(log|f_{ijl}(y)|−b_{ij})/l.     (R)

The constant coefficients satisfy their strict inequalities. Every point in a fiber disk is bounded by the weighted Gauss maximum. Conversely its Gauss points at arbitrary positive radii test each coefficient, including over a trivially valued residue field; thus the supremal open disk radius is exactly (R). The w bound supplies a finite floor. Locally on a fixed generator-bounded compact box, dominated generator branches retain an actual affine FS expression for this maximum. The zero-section domains Ω_i increase to all Y if D contains Y×{0}. These local expressions decrease with i because Q_i increase.

For each y and each closed disk radius r<d_D(y), its fiber disk is compact and contained in D, hence contained in some Q_i. Thus d_{Q_i}(y) increases to d_D(y). If d_D=∞ the assertion holds for every finite r. Consequently −log d_D is a decreasing limit of the local tropical FS expressions, including its −∞ values.

For completeness the global upper-growth conversion is still pre-EP. Suppose u=−log d_D≤h for a nonnegative global rational affine FS exhaustion h. Use its projective ample-reference compactification, fix c=2 and a rational floor M, and let Φ_M=max(u−2h,M), extended by M at the boundary. The set where u−2h can exceed M is contained in a fixed compact generator box because u−2h≤−h. Choose a larger box whose boundary collar has u−2h<M−1. On its compact collar the decreasing local radius expressions u_i satisfy u_i−2h<M eventually: nested compact superlevel sets prove this eventual inequality without assuming continuity of u. Glue max(u_i−2h,M) inside and M outside. The whole winning collar and G2 give genuine continuous global PSH in the same ample reference class. Decreasing closure gives Φ_M, and then M decreases to −∞. The limit is generically finite when u is the original nontrivial weak input. Already-genuine global FS sequence regularization in the same ample class, followed by section trivialization on Y and identically dominated original generator branches, gives a genuine affine FS decreasing sequence for u. No singular upper-envelope assertion is used in this conversion.

Apply this to D={log|w|+u(y)<0}. Its fiber radius is exp(−u(y)), including ∞ at u=−∞. The product proper witness supplies weak membership.     Z_{j+1}=Z_j∩closure_Y(π^{-1}π(Z_j)\Z_j).
