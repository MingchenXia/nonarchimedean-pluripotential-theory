# Checking the polynomial Hölder estimate and its source application

We state the relevant consequence of D Theorem A.3, and check its proof mechanism so that the source use is not an analogy. For a fixed fiber, define

Thus no quasi-triangle inequality has been assumed before its proof.

$$\varepsilon_j-\varepsilon_{j+1}\ge\sum_iD_i(q_{i,j+1}-q_{ij}),\quad

At a zero of an $f_i$ with $p_i>0$, its term tends to $-\infty$ while all other terms are bounded above at that point. Zero coefficients are omitted. # A normal-ring localization estimate with actual divisor orders

Let $A$ be a normal integral finitely generated algebra over the original $k$, $f\ne0$, $h\in A_f$, and $U$ an actual global rational AFS. Suppose

For a prime divisor $D$ of $\operatorname{Spec}A$, use the rays $r\operatorname{ord}_D$, $r>0$. Every regular generator of $U$ has nonnegative divisor order, so $U$ is bounded above there. Sending $r\to\infty$ in (42) gives

All orders are orders of the actual rational function. A chosen denominator exponent is not a divisor order: $f=z^2$, $h=z^{-1}$, $a=2$, $c=1$ has equality in (43), although a denominator representation can suggest the wrong bound.

# Carrier envelopes: the retraction issue and raw roots

We isolate a topological lemma which avoids an openness assumption for torification. Let $q:Y\to Y$ be a continuous retraction on a compact Hausdorff space with image $B$. For a bounded-above $U$ satisfying $U=U\circ q$,
$$U_Y^*|_B=(U|_B)_B^*. \tag{45}$$
For any neighborhood $N$ of $b\in B$ in $B$, the set $q^{-1}(N)$ is a neighborhood of $b$ in $Y$, and the supremum of $U$ on it is the supremum on $N$. This gives one limsup inequality. Neighborhoods in the ambient space restrict to neighborhoods in $B$, giving the other. No openness of $q$ is required.

At a rational Reeb field the accepted quotient gives residual torification $q_V$ and its compact image $B$ identified with $K$ by the calibrated Gauss lift. Splitting projective FS sections into residual weight components with their original coefficients produces an actual globally generating FS metric $\psi\circ q_V\ge\psi$. Thus if unrestricted projective EP were independently proved on this actual normal quotient, apply it to the invariant family, then torify its actual envelope. Equation (45) identifies its carrier restriction with the carrier usc supremum. This gives the invariant carrier property without any claim about a full-link envelope equality away from the image.

The raw-root transport can be checked independently. On torified points off the vertex, the accepted maps give $\Omega\simeq K\times\mathbb R$, with $v\mapsto(s_\xi(v),\rho_\xi(v))$ and inverse $(x,r)\mapsto x-r\xi$. $$U(v)-Ct\le U(v+t\eta)\le U(v)-ct\quad(t\ge0).$$
Every orbit is finite with a unique root or identically $-\infty$. Bracketing the zero shows
$$\mathcal R(\sup_i u_i)=\sup_i\mathcal R u_i,\qquad
 \mathcal R(U^*)=(\mathcal R U)^*. \tag{46}$$
For the second identity, a value above the root has negative $U^*$ on a neighborhood and pulls back to an upper root bound. A value below the root has every neighborhood meeting $\{U>0\}$ and pulls back to a lower limsup root bound. If the root is $-\infty$, the upper argument works for every real parameter.
