# n4. Normal isolated algebraic germs in characteristic zero

Use only G331 and n1, together with the published applications specified here. R is the local ring at a CLOSED point of a normal algebraic variety over an ALGEBRAICALLY CLOSED field of characteristic ZERO. Assume the point is an ISOLATED singularity. All dimensions are allowed; the regular case is treated in n5 at the exact smooth BFJ hypotheses, a normal curve is a DVR, and dimension zero is empty. Localization to the closed germ is legitimate: the source ideal sheaves and projective models localize, and the relevant finite lists of primary generators spread to a neighbourhood on which their cosupport is only that point.

The source is the THREE-author Boucksom-de Fernex-Favre, The volume of an isolated singularity, Duke 2012, supplied complete official PDF/text. It is not the four-author singular-multiplier valuative paper. The complete immediate source units used were read: Section1 setting/sign and 1.8/1.12; Section2 definitions and 2.9-2.13 including the ENTIRE three-step proof of 2.12, plus 2.15-2.17; 3.12-3.17; and 4.1-4.11 with their entire immediate proofs. Supplied PDF pages22,41,46,47 were physically viewed to check the inequality directions. Recursive characteristic-zero resolution, asymptotic/singular multiplier ideals, compatible boundaries, Takagi subadditivity and numerical b-divisor foundations are ordinary published inputs. They are not claimed freshly proved from first principles. The source itself cites Takagi rather than proving its historical theorem; that boundary is retained.

Use the sign Z(I)_E=-ord_E(I). Source nefness is closure of numerical relatively nef Cartier classes on normal models. Every G331 core divisor gives a source-nef Cartier b-divisor: normalize its actual primary determination, and use the curve degree pullback rule (point or positive field degree). Normalization is finite, and the model is an isomorphism off the closed point. If h_j are core functions converging uniformly to h in C, their b-divisor coefficients converge at every divisor to H_E=ord_E(a)h(ord_E/ord_E(a)); their trace on the base is identically zero. COMPLETE source Lemma1.12(a) converts this coefficient convergence, with base traces in the fixed finite-dimensional space {0}, into numerical convergence on every fixed model. The source nef cone is closed, so H is source-nef. We need only this direction; no recognition of every source-nef b-divisor as a G331 D function is assumed.

Take a rational signed primary-profile obstacle f. A common ACTUAL normalized primary model pi:Y->Spec R principalizes all its finitely many ideals and a (normalize the product blowup). Thus f evaluates a rational exceptional Cartier b-divisor C determined on Y. Let E_i be the finitely many exceptional prime divisors on Y and b_i=ord_Ei(a)>0. Choose M with M Z(a)<=C, by actual Cartier order units, equivalently sufficiently large finite coefficient bounds on Y. Complete 2.13,2.15,2.17 give

    W=Env_X(C)=Env_pi(C_pi),   M Z(a)<=W<=C.

Both bounding traces on X are zero; thus W_X=0. Source 2.12 negativity gives W<=0. It is therefore bounded below and supported over the closed point. If W=0, C>=0 on all divisors centred there; divisorial density and continuity of f imply f>=0 on the entire boundary, and its envelope is zero. This zero/unit case is separate.

For W!=0 clear denominators BEFORE using the literal integral-Cartier Theorem4.11: N C is an integral Cartier b-divisor determined over the point, its envelope is N W by homogeneity. The source yields primary ideals b_k and positive rational q_k with

    B_k=q_k Z(b_k)>=W,    (B_k)_E -> W_E for every divisor E.

Their functions u_k=-q_k v(b_k) are actual geometric core functions. There are three necessary qualifications to the printed proof. Its Jacobian and nef-defect correction ideals have cosupport contained in the point; they may be UNIT, as the defect ideal is in the Gorenstein/smooth case. We use unit-or-primary, not a false assertion of strict primariness. Multiplier ideals also may initially be unit, but since some coefficient of W is strictly negative they are proper EVENTUALLY; discard a finite prefix. Finally if the positive correction c in the proof is real, round it UP to an integer: W<=0 makes (k+c)W weaker as c increases, and 1/(k+c) is rational. The source inequalities are Z(b_k)>=kW+Z(defect)+Z(Jac), correction>=c_0 Z(m), and W<=epsilon Z(m). These give Z(b_k)>=(k+c)W for a sufficiently large positive c and hence B_k>=W. They are coefficientwise assertions; they are not a uniform boundary estimate. Isolation is what makes a unit-or-primary correction bounded by the primary reference.

The missing uniform step follows from the FINITE determining trace. Set

    eta_k=max_i ((B_k)_Ei-C_Ei)^+/b_i,   eps_k=eta_k+1/k.

As W_Ei<=C_Ei and B_k converges coefficientwise, eta_k tends to zero. The source-nef divisor B_k+eps_k Z(a) has trace on Y at most C_pi. The largest-trace characterization 2.13 therefore puts it below W. Combined with B_k>=W, this proves on every centred divisor

    u_k-eps_k <= W_E/ord_E(a) <= u_k.

In particular |u_k-u_l|<=eps_k+eps_l on the dense divisorial set. BOTH u_k and u_l are continuous. G331 density therefore extends that inequality to ALL semivaluation supports. They are uniformly Cauchy, with a limit U in C. At divisors it has coefficient W. Since W<=C, continuity extends U<=f to the full boundary. This is the first point where an all-support function attached to W has been constructed; no unsupported evaluation of W at a nonminimal support was used beforehand.

Every D competitor is controlled through ONE actual C net, not through its USC values on a dense set. Let h=inf_alpha h_alpha<=f, with h_alpha in C. Given eps>0, its finitely many values at nu_i=ord_Ei/b_i decrease to h(nu_i)<=f(nu_i); even if a limit is bottom, one common index satisfies h_alpha(nu_i)<=f(nu_i)+eps for all i. The source-nef divisor H_alpha+eps Z(a) then has trace <=C_pi and is <=W by2.13. Thus h_alpha-eps<=U on divisors. These are continuous functions, so density extends the comparison everywhere. Since h<=h_alpha, h<=U+eps; letting eps decrease to zero gives h<=U. Hence U is the FULL greatest D minorant of f.

Rational signed primary profiles are dense in all finite continuous obstacles. N1 raw nonexpansivity and closedness of C pass the result to all such obstacles. N5 supplies the extended USC and decreasing-obstacle-net consequences. This proves actual positivity and continuous envelopes on normal closed isolated algebraic germs over algebraically closed characteristic zero in ANY dimension. It does not cover general nonisolated germs, positive/mixed characteristic, nonclosed points, arbitrary residue fields, or nonalgebraic excellent higher-dimensional germs. Only actual primary models were used for the geometric functions; no higher-dimensional nef pushforward or arbitrary-model cofinality was claimed.
