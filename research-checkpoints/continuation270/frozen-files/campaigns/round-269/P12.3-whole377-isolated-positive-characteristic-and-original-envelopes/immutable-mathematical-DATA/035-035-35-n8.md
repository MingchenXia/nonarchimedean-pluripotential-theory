# n8. An exact nonnormal obstruction in every characteristic

Use G331 and n1,n7. Over ANY field take R=k[x,y,z]_(x,y,z)/(xy), normalization S=A times B with A=k[x,z]_(x,z), B=k[y,z]_(y,z), maximal-ideal gauge downstairs and its extension upstairs. The ring is the fibre product over k[z]_(z). Polynomial pairs with common residue are represented by f(x,z)+g(y,z)-h(z); local unit denominators are cleared by multiplying their residue units. Its conductor is (x)A times(y)B. The boundary quotient identifies only q_A killing x and q_B killing y, each with z-order one. Their downstairs support (x,y) is nonminimal.

Define the finite continuous obstacle phi=-1-1/v(y), with reciprocal infinity zero. It restricts to -1 on A and -1-1/w(y) on B, in [-2,-1], and agrees at the identified pair. On B, w(f)>=ord_mB(f) for every normalized centred semivaluation, because m_B powers have value at least their exponent. Thus every negative ideal profile g satisfies g(w)<=g(o_B), o_B=ord_mB; uniform C limits and actual decreasing D nets preserve the inequality. Since phi(o_B)=-2, every B competitor is <=-2 everywhere, and constant -2 is admissible. The unrestricted source envelope is the continuous tuple (-1,-2), incompatible at its full conductor fibre.

For n>=1 use the A-primary ideal J_n=(x^n,xz^(n-1),z^(2n)), paired with constant -2 on B. The core profiles agree at q_A,q_B, hence positively descend by G331. Write a=w(x), b=w(z), min(a,b)=1. Their A value is -1 if a<=b; if a>b finite it is -min(1+(a-1)/n,2); it is -2 at q_A. At n=1 the middle generator is literally x, avoiding zero times infinity. These increase and prove that the exact downstairs raw envelope is -1 on A except q_A and -2 at the common point and throughout B. The obstacle on A and the universal B order bound give those same upper bounds for EVERY D competitor.

Monomial valuations w_L(x)=L,w_L(z)=1 converge to q_A on every localized polynomial: terms with a positive x exponent eventually exceed the surviving z-order, and unit denominators have value zero. Thus the raw envelope is not USC at the common point. It has no greatest or even maximal D minorant by n1 and finite max closure. Its upper regularization is still below phi, but not in D: it has value -1 at the common point, whereas its B restriction has value -2 at o_B, contradicting the same D_B order bound. N7's formula reproduces it as full-fibre maximum of the source tuple. This is a rigorous obstruction for a bounded strictly negative continuous obstacle with smooth normalization in every characteristic. It does not close or disprove the residual NORMAL higher-dimensional problem.

More generally, a fibre joining distinct positive-dimensional normal centres prevents universal CE. Pick on one centre a divisorial point with finite conductor value. Its singleton downstairs image is disjoint from the compact images of other centres. A continuous obstacle in [-1,0] can be -1 there and zero on the other images. On a second centre its raw envelope is zero. On the first the granted fixed-divisorial inequality g(nu)>=K g(w), K>0, forces every competitor at the selected fibre point to be <=-1/K. Thus the separated raw envelope is not fibre constant, and n7's general criterion rules out positive regularization downstairs. This statement does not classify internal fibres.
