\begin{abstract}
We give a necessary-and-sufficient infinite geometric certificate for simultaneous genuine regularized-domain membership and exact envelope contact, for every bounded continuous obstacle. The certificate quantifies finite rational spatial carriers, actual real matching coefficients, explicit common-support rows on entire incident original cells, finite legal printed-PCC trees at every real root, and finite primitive-recursion polynomials. Neither an unknown envelope nor a completed operator value occurs in the certificate. We prove its finite-PA equivalence, construct strict decreasing global majorants before applying measures, and pass to all bounded continuous obstacles using independently supplied endpoint regularity, compatible rational PA bump approximation, legal-tree tail bounds and fixed-relative-bound measure tightness. Literal positive-dimensional pairs fail the required domain antecedent; empty and dimension-zero cases use the zero-fold operator. This is an infinite classification, not a terminating algorithm or a claim of universal real orthogonality. Its proof uses the fully qualified R230, O323, S272 and S282 results only at their stated scopes. Independent whole-original review remains necessary.
\end{abstract}

\section{Finite geometric coefficient rows}
Every nonempty rational cell has a finite representation
\[
 c=\conv V_c+\cone R_c,                                               \tag{2.1}
\]
with rational point and ray lists, including both signs for lineality. Here and below lists may be redundant. For clarity the elementary foundations are as follows. A nonnegative representation in finitely many vectors can be reduced to independent active columns by subtracting a signed dependence until one coefficient vanishes. The finitely many independent inverse maps bound the coefficient sums. This proves closedness of a finite cone. For an inequality cone, remove its rational lineality; the sum of its nonnegative defining rows is strictly positive on the remaining cone away from zero. Its section at one is bounded, since otherwise normalization gives a nonzero joint-kernel vector. Move in both signs in the kernel of active rows at a nonvertex of this bounded section; each endpoint adds an independent active row. Induction expresses every point as a convex combination of the finitely many rational vertices. Homogenizing cell inequalities yields (2.1), including relative affine hulls. Eliminating representation variables by pairing lower and upper bounds proves that finite hull/cone descriptions and their projections are polyhedra. Finite rational wall arrangements give common refinements.

Fix any finite rational common refinement $\mathcal C$ of $\Pi$, the actual reference carrier and the obstacle carrier. On its cells, use intrinsic affine tuples $p_c$ and set $h=p-\gamma$. Matching on a common face is tested on a rational affine basis of that face. These are rational homogeneous equations in affine coefficients. Boundedness of $h$ is exactly its zero slope on every $r\in R_c$. The real kernel of these rational equations has a rational basis. Clearing denominators in each basis tuple makes its intrinsic slopes integral and leaves its constants rational. Thus every matching tuple is a finite real linear combination of literal PA tuples. In particular no irrational spatial wall is introduced. For bounded matching $h$,
\[
       \|h\|_\infty=\max_{c,v\in V_c}|h(v)|.                         \tag{2.2}
\]
This follows by the point/ray representation and vanishing recession slopes.

The following are the actual PC rows, not a PC oracle. For every original face $\tau\in\Pi$ and every carrier face $\rho\in\mathcal C$ contained in $\tau$, introduce \emph{one ambient affine} $s_{\tau\rho}(x)=a_{\tau\rho}(x)+b_{\tau\rho}$. Require:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $s_{\tau\rho}=p$ on $V_\rho$, and equality of slopes on $R_\rho$;
\item for \emph{every} original top cell $\sigma\succeq\tau$ and \emph{every} $c\in\mathcal C$ contained in $\sigma$,
\[
 s_{\tau\rho}(v)\leq p_c(v)\quad(v\in V_c),\qquad
 a_{\tau\rho}(r)\leq p_{c,\rm lin}(r)\quad(r\in R_c).                 \tag{2.3}
\]
\end{enumerate}
These rows control whole incident original cells, including unbounded parts and all inherited faces. Their single ambient covector retains all dependent directions and extra span intersections. Top cells also count as original faces. In coordinates $z$ for bounded matching $h$ and $b$ for all supports these are finite
\[
               A_\mathcal C z+N_\mathcal C b\leq d_\mathcal C,          \tag{2.4}
\]
where the left matrices are rational and the right side contains the actual real reference values/slopes.

\begin{lemma}[Exact fixed-carrier feasibility]\label{l:rows}
For bounded matching real tuples, feasibility of (2.3) is equivalent to printed PC, and hence to source finite PAPC in the qualified real convention. It is exact for all printed structures, not just tests on $\mathcal C$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The fixed-carrier theorem and local-cycle equivalence of qualified O323 apply: finite embedded rational geometry, all closed faces, strictly positive actual balanced weights, continuous real matching tuples boundedly relative to the actual admitted reference are exactly its hypotheses. Its necessity uses qualified S306 at the continuous finite PC tuple, and propagates initial supports by convexity along entire original-cell segments; it does not require a new S306 gluing theorem. All inherited faces and the actual reference refinement are present here.

We also give the sufficiency explicitly. The point/ray rows give a support equal to $p$ on all of $\rho$ and below it on every entire original cell incident to $\tau$. At any real point choose its minimal original face and a carrier face containing it and lying in that face. Finitely many closed nonincident original cells avoid a neighborhood of the point, so this gives a local ambient lower support there. The restriction of continuous $p$ to any segment contained in $X$ is convex: local supports give local midpoint inequalities. Subtract the endpoint chord; if the difference is positive somewhere, adding a sufficiently small $\eta t^2$ gives a positive interior maximum, contradicting the midpoint inequality by the extra $\eta s^2$ at neighboring symmetric parameters. At a printed PCC center, each segment to an endpoint lies in its witnessing incident convex cell. The local supporting covector bounds the initial segment secant, hence its whole secant by convexity. Summing cancels the covector by the barycenter equation. This proves every PCC, without coarsening a carrier or inserting an irrational point as a rational vertex.
\end{proof}

Support variables can be retained as finite witnesses or eliminated exactly: solve equalities or write them as paired inequalities, eliminate one real coordinate by pairing every lower bound with every upper bound, and retain rows not using that coordinate. A missing side adds no condition. The permissible interval is nonempty precisely when all these paired inequalities hold. Repeating yields a finite projected inequality system. Equivalently O323's finite multipliers characterize every valid linear bound on the projection by $A^T\lambda=a$, $N^T\lambda=0$, $d^T\lambda\leq t$, $\lambda\geq0$. Its nonempty-feasibility hypothesis is met by $p=\gamma$ in the bare PC system. No strict feasibility, independent normals or rational right-hand side is required.

For the certificate below $p\leq q$ is imposed on every point list of a common carrier and on recession slopes. Since $u$ is bounded, $q$ and $p$ have precisely the reference recession; the recession inequalities are equalities after the bounded-matching rows. The bound $|p-\gamma|\leq C+1$ is the finite point condition (2.2). Obstacles themselves may be quantified by their finite rational carriers, real matching tuples with zero recession slopes, and this same computable finite norm; thus the quantifier over $\PA_{\R,b}$ hides no envelope.

\section{Primitive recursion as explicit polynomials}
Choose all saturated intrinsic tangent bases and inward primitive lifts for the finite common carrier $\mathcal C$. For every facet $\tau\prec\sigma$ in every dimension choose a rational linear extension $\widetilde p_{\tau,\rm lin}$ to $V$ of the intrinsic derivative on $N_{\tau,\R}$. Such an extension is a fixed rational linear map of its coefficient tuple, found by a rational tangent basis and a rational complement. Set $c_0(\sigma)=w_\sigma$ on refined top cells and, for $j=1,\ldots,d$, $k=d-j+1$, set
\[
 c_j(\tau)=\sum_{\substack{\sigma\succ\tau\\\dim\sigma=k}}
 c_{j-1}(\sigma)
 \left[p_{\sigma,\rm lin}(n_{\sigma/\tau})-
       \widetilde p_{\tau,\rm lin}(n_{\sigma/\tau})\right],\quad
 \dim\tau=k-1.                                                       \tag{3.1}
\]
All signed and zero coefficients are retained. For balanced $c_{j-1}$ this is exactly
\[
 \sum_{\sigma\succ\tau}c_{j-1}(\sigma)p_{\sigma,\rm lin}(n_{\sigma/\tau})
 -p_{\tau,\rm lin}\!\left(\sum_{\sigma\succ\tau}
                          c_{j-1}(\sigma)n_{\sigma/\tau}\right).       \tag{3.2}
\]
Changing a lift adds a tangent vector to both terms and cancels by matching. Changing the extension cancels since the weighted sum lies in the face tangent space. For finite matching PA tuples the recursion remains balanced at every step, is refinement independent, and is symmetric in distinct factors; actual real/signed/zero coefficients are permitted.

Here is the exact source calibration for this new polynomial application. Map $x$ to $(x,1)$ to satisfy BBGS's affine-hyperplane quasi-embedding convention, with product Euclidean metric. If $a_c$ is the Euclidean covolume of $N_c$ ($a_v=1$), a saturated facet basis followed by a primitive lift is a basis of $N_\sigma$, so its perpendicular height is $a_\sigma/a_\tau$. Thus $c(\sigma)n_{\sigma/\tau}$ projected normal equals $(a_\sigma/a_\tau)c(\sigma)\nu_{\sigma/\tau}$, and balanced primitive cycles correspond to BBGS unit-normal weights $a_\sigma c(\sigma)$. Its product by $-p$ is precisely $a_\tau D_pc(\tau)$: balancing cancels the common tangent derivative and the minus sign in BBGS Definition 3.9 cancels $-p$. This conversion applies in \emph{every} recursive dimension with its own saturated lattice. BBGS's whole 3.12 proof gives subdivision compatibility, whole 3.15 gives commutation, 3.18 and 3.19 give balanced products for arbitrary real signed weights. In 3.19's product proof its printed reference to 3.12 must be 3.15 at the commutation step. These units use only the stated real affine functions and Euclidean normals; no positivity or integral coefficients are required for these identities. Their immediate linear algebra proofs were checked. This is also the finite raw package in qualified S272. BMPS delegated AR10 commutation is not used as a further grant.

Writing (3.1) as $c_j=D^{(k)}(z)c_{j-1}$ shows explicitly that each top atom
\[
                         m_{\mathcal C,v}(z)=c_d(v)                  \tag{3.3}
\]
is a degree-$d$ polynomial in affine slopes, linear in the \emph{actual real} top weights, with rational coefficients in intrinsic rational coordinates. Affine constants do not enter (3.1). For PC tuples, positivity follows by subtracting an ambient common support in (3.2): every residual inward derivative is nonnegative against a nonnegative balanced cycle. It therefore holds through all stages. On arbitrary tuples the polynomial is still computed with its full signed/zero stages; no clipping is permitted. On PA PC functions $\sum_v m_v\delta_v$ is the genuine finite raw measure. Artificial subdivision vertices have zero weight unless carrying a genuine atom, by the subdivision identity.

The normalization contains no extra factorial. For $p=A|x|+B|y|$ on the saturated plane of weight $w$, the first divisor has weights $2Bw$ on the two horizontal rays and $2Aw$ on the two vertical rays. The second gives $8ABw\delta_0=2^2\,2!\,ABw\delta_0$. The $2!$ is produced by the two surviving orders in the raw expansion, not appended. For a one-dimensional ray of primitive direction $n$ with slope $a(n)$ the vertex contribution is exactly its weight times that slope. These checks exclude an Alexandrov, inverse, Euclidean-unit or Minkowski-volume substitution for (3.1).

\section{Finite legal trees identify the unrestricted envelope first}
\begin{definition}[Geometrically legal finite tree]
A tree has finitely many nodes and a root labeled $x\in X$. Each internal node carries a printed legal PCC as defined in Section 1: the parent is its center, its children are its rational endpoints, with their real weights and a finite rational witnessing structure and incident faces. Different nodes may have different witnessing structures. A leaf may be any point, in particular a real unsplit root. Delete zero-weight children and their subtrees; repeated endpoints remain separate occurrences. Unary no-op splits are allowed but unnecessary. The node weight $\pi_a$ is the product of weights along its path; leaf weights sum to one. The $q$-cost is $\sum_{a\,\rm leaf}\pi_a q(x_a)$.
\end{definition}
Deleting zero terms preserves legality using the same faces. Conversely padding zero terms adds nothing, so the definition exactly represents the printed weak convention. We do not presume that a structure remains a legal witness after a common refinement: keeping the original witness at each node avoids that error. A global barycenter equation for the terminal distribution alone is not sufficient for legality.

\begin{theorem}[Tree envelope identity without density or an operator]\label{t:tree}
For every continuous bounded $u$, indeed whenever $|u|\leq C$ is finite-valued, the infimum of finite legal $q$-tree costs at $x$ is exactly the actual unrestricted envelope (1.2). Its bounds are $\gamma-C\leq e\leq q\leq\gamma+C$. For continuous $u$ this endpoint is continuous and satisfies real-endpoint PCC inequalities, by qualified R230. No intermediate tree or Bellman iterate is measured.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $F(x)$ be that infimum. Jensen for the actual PC reference, iterated down a finite tree, gives $\sum\pi_a\gamma(x_a)\geq\gamma(x)$. Since leaf weights sum to one, every cost is at least $\gamma(x)-C$. The single-leaf tree gives $F(x)\leq q(x)$.

Every finite-valued raw PC competitor $f$ in (1.2), including discontinuous ones with only upper growth, is at most every tree cost by finite repeated Jensen and $f\leq q$ at leaves. Hence $e\leq F$ without completing $f$ or assuming a lower bound for it.

For any fixed legal split at $x$, choose at each positive-weight child a finite tree with cost within $\eta$ of its finite infimum $F(x_i)$. Graft these finitely many trees. The new cost is at most $\sum_i\alpha_i F(x_i)+\eta$. Let $\eta\downarrow0$ to get $F(x)\leq\sum_i\alpha_i F(x_i)$. Thus $F$ is finite-valued printed PC on \emph{every} legal witnessing structure. Its sandwich supplies the upper-growth condition, so it is itself an original competitor. Therefore $F\leq e$ and equality holds.

Only now apply R230's actual all-$C_b$ envelope theorem. Its finite positive balanced embedded hypotheses, continuous PC actual $\gamma$ and bounded continuous $u$ all hold. It supplies continuity and real-endpoint inequalities, without PA density. The infimum over finite real-endpoint legal trees would give the same value: $e\leq q$ and its real Jensen inequalities bound it by each such tree, while printed trees are a subfamily. This comparison does not alter the printed raw competitor definition.
\end{proof}

The telescoping cost identity
\[
 \sum_{a\,\rm leaf}\pi_a\gamma(x_a)-\gamma(x)
 =\sum_{b\,\rm internal}\pi_b
       \left(\sum_i\alpha_{bi}\gamma(x_{bi})-\gamma(x_b)\right)         \tag{4.1}
\]
retains every nonzero reference cost. Each term is nonnegative. Thus actual reference geometry has not been replaced by an uncosted homogeneous relation. Finite-depth Bellman infima are an optional description of this proof, but are never assigned measures.

\section{The input-only certificate and finite-PA equivalence}
\begin{definition}[Carrier--tree--raw certificate $\mathfrak T$]\label{d:certificate}
The admitted real pair satisfies $\mathfrak T(X,w,\gamma)$ if for \emph{every} bounded matching genuine finite rational-carrier real-PA obstacle tuple $u$, putting $q=\gamma+u$ and $C=\|u\|_\infty$, and every real $0<\varepsilon<1$, there exist a finite rational common carrier $\mathcal C$ and actual real matching coefficients $p$ and support witnesses such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the zero-recession matching equations and all entire-incident-original-cell rows (2.3) hold;
\item the finite point/ray inequalities give $p\leq q$ globally and $|p-\gamma|\leq C+1$;
\item \emph{every} real $x\in X$ has a \emph{finite geometrically legal printed tree} of $q$-cost at most $p(x)+\varepsilon$;
\item with the full primitive polynomial recursion (3.1),
\[
      0\leq G_{\mathcal C}(q,p):=
          \sum_{v\in\mathcal C(0)}(q(v)-p(v))m_{\mathcal C,v}(p)
                         \leq\varepsilon.                            \tag{5.1}
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}
The certificate is entirely input-based: finite rational equations/inequalities and incident cells, actual reference and obstacle coefficients, geometric PCC equalities with finite rational witnesses, and explicit finite polynomials. Items 1,2,4 are finite algebra for each proposed carrier. Item 3 is universal over roots with individual finite witnesses; its depth, locations and weights need not be uniformly bounded. The criterion quantifies all carriers and all obstacle tuples, not just fans, separable obstacles, affine arrangements or a chosen gluing presentation. It does not assert an effective oracle for arbitrary real numbers.

\begin{theorem}[Exact finite-PA classification]\label{t:pa}
For every admitted real pair in Section 1, $\mathfrak T$ is equivalent to $\mathcal O_{\rm PA}$. All PA endpoints obtained from a successful certificate have explicit strict-decreasing genuine PA PC global majorants, one relative bound and the actual recession, before any completed measure is applied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Assume $\mathcal O_{\rm PA}$ and fix $q$, $C$, $\varepsilon$. Its actual endpoint $e\in\D$ has genuine relative PA PC approximants $a_n$ with global errors at most $\eta_n\downarrow0$. Choose positive rational error bounds $\eta_n$ and put $p_n=a_n-\eta_n$. Then
\[
    p_n\leq e\leq q,\quad 0\leq e-p_n\leq2\eta_n,\quad
    |p_n-\gamma|\leq C+2\eta_n.                                     \tag{5.2}
\]
Real constant shifts on rational geometry are genuine real PA; they do not introduce comparison walls. Choose finite common rational carriers. Lemma~\ref{l:rows} provides all exact PC rows. Once $2\eta_n<\min(1,\varepsilon)$, every $x$ has a finite tree of cost less than $e(x)+(\varepsilon-2\eta_n)$ by Theorem~\ref{t:tree}, hence at most $p_n(x)+\varepsilon$.

S272's actual-reference completed raw operator applies because $e\in\D$ by the assumed domain, and each $p_n$ is finite genuine relative PA PC. This sequence and its limit have one relative bound $C+1$ for large $n$. Its ordinary weak $C_b$ continuity gives $\MA(p_n)\to\MA(e)$, all of mass $M=\int\MA(\gamma)$. The fixed continuous test $q-e$ is bounded and nonnegative. The difference of the variable test $q-p_n$ from it has global norm at most $2\eta_n$, so
\[
 G(q,p_n)\longrightarrow\int(q-e)\MA(e)=0.                          \tag{5.3}
\]
The first equality is finite raw recursion, not a different measure. Taking sufficiently large $n$ meets item 4 as well. No attainment of a tree infimum is asserted.

Conversely assume $\mathfrak T$. Use $\varepsilon_n=2^{-n}$, $n\geq1$, and its witnesses $p_n$. Their finite rows make them genuine PA PC functions. Since they are original competitors, $p_n\leq e$. Their tree witnesses and Theorem~\ref{t:tree} give
\[
                         0\leq e-p_n\leq\varepsilon_n.              \tag{5.4}
\]
Before constructing any measure at $e$, set $g_n=p_n+4\varepsilon_n$. These are genuine finite rational-carrier real-PA PC functions, with
\[
 3\varepsilon_n\leq g_n-e\leq4\varepsilon_n,\qquad
 g_n-g_{n+1}\geq3\varepsilon_n-4\varepsilon_{n+1}
                       =\varepsilon_n>0.                            \tag{5.5}
\]
Their relative bounds are at most $C+3$ and they converge globally uniformly to $e$. This is an actual strict-decreasing regularization. Each $p_n,g_n$ has precisely $\gamma$'s recession on its common carrier because its bounded perturbation slopes vanish on every recession generator; no single finite carrier for the whole sequence is asserted. Thus $e\in\D$ independently of any measure limit.

Now, and only now, apply S272: $p_n\to e$ uniformly in its genuine completed domain with one relative bound. The nonnegative bounded gaps $q-p_n$ converge uniformly to $q-e$. As in (5.3), fixed-$C$ weak continuity and mass $M$ pass their finite polynomial integrals. Item 4 gives the limit zero, proving the exact sign-reversed identity (1.3). This proves both directions even when $e$ is not PA or $M=0$. Zero mass alone would not supply the domain proof (5.5).
\end{proof}

\section{Compatible bounded local PA approximation and tree tails}
\begin{lemma}[All-$C_b$ approximation with rational matching]\label{l:bumps}
Every $u\in C_b(X)$ has compactly supported literal PA functions $u_j$ on finite rational geometry such that $\|u_j\|_\infty\leq\|u\|_\infty+1/j$ and $u_j\to u$ locally uniformly. They are admitted genuine real-PA obstacles. They need not be PC. No global uniform approximation of arbitrary $C_b$ is asserted.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose an ambient lattice-coordinate integral polyhedral norm $\rho$, for example $\sum|x_i|$. The continuous compact cutoffs $\chi_j=\min(1,(j+1-\rho)_+)$ equal one on $\{\rho\leq j\}$ and lie in $[0,1]$. Use $f_j=\chi_j u$ only as continuous compact functions; the product is not claimed PA.

Here is a full compact-function approximation. A continuous compactly supported nonnegative $f$ on the closed support $X$ is uniformly continuous in ambient distance: on a sufficiently large compact ball use continuity and compactness; outside it $f=0$, and a small distance cannot connect that outer region to its support. Put $B=\|f\|_\infty$. Given $a>0$, choose $\delta>0$ so $|f(x)-f(y)|<a$ whenever $\rho(x-y)<\delta$. Choose a positive integer $L$ with $L\delta>B+a$. A sufficiently fine finite net of the compact support can be chosen at rational locations $v_i\in X_\Q$, within distance less than $\min(\delta,a/L)$ of every support point. Rational points are dense on every rational cell, including all shared faces. Choose nonnegative rational heights $b_i$ with $|b_i-f(v_i)|<a$ and $b_i\leq B+a$. Then
\[
             F(x)=\max_i(b_i-L\rho(x-v_i))_+                         \tag{7.1}
\]
has compact support. A positive bump has $\rho(x-v_i)<\delta$, so $F(x)\leq f(x)+2a$. At every support point use a net point to get $F(x)\geq f(x)-3a$. Outside the support the upper estimate and nonnegativity suffice. The zero function needs no samples. Each bump has integral ambient slopes, rational constants and rational walls; finite maxima and positive parts preserve this rational carrier. Its restriction to $X$ is continuous and matches on all shared faces automatically, since it is one ambient function.

Apply this separately to the positive and negative parts of any continuous compact $f$. Their difference has uniform error at most $6a$, remains literal PA on a common finite rational arrangement, and has compact support. For $f_j$ choose $6a\leq1/j$. Thus the output $u_j$ has the stated common norm and local limit. Disconnected components and proper cells require no separate trace prescription, and no coefficient of $\gamma$ was perturbed.

\begin{lemma}[Legal-tree tail estimate]\label{l:tails}

\end{lemma}

\end{proof}
