L714: Recall the definition of $\mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(\theta)$ from Definition 【66†3.3】. When $\{\theta\}=c_{1}(L)$ for some pseudoeffective $\mathbb{Q}$-line bundle $L$ we now define the map:
L715: 
L716:  | $$\textup{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(\theta)\ni\phi\mapsto\phi^{\mathrm{an}}\in\textup{PSH}(L^{\mathrm{an}}).$$  |  | (39)
L717: Let $\phi=\{\{\phi^{\omega}_{\tau}\}_{\tau}\}_{\omega\in\mathcal{K}}\in\textup{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(\theta)$. Let $\omega\in\mathcal{K}$ be such that $\{\omega\}=c_{1}(T)$, for a $\mathbb{Q}$-ample line bundle $T$. We get that $\{\phi^{\omega}_{\tau}\}_{\tau}\in\textup{MTC}_{\mathcal{I}}(\theta+\omega)$, hence $(\phi^{\omega})^{\mathrm{an}}\in\textup{PSH}((L+T)^{\mathrm{an}})$ by Theorem 【153†3.12】(i). We make the following preliminary definition:
L718:  | $$\phi^{\mathrm{an}}:=(\phi^{\omega})^{\mathrm{an}}.$$  |  | (40)
L719: 
L720: Among other things, we need to show that this definition is independent of the choice of $\omega.$ Let $\omega^{\prime}\in\mathcal{K}^{\prime}$ such that $\{\omega^{\prime}\}=c_{1}(T^{\prime})$, for some $\mathbb{Q}$-ample line bundle $T^{\prime}$ and $\omega\leq\omega^{\prime}$. Then we have that
L721: 
L722:  | $$P_{\theta+\omega^{\prime}}[\phi^{\omega}_{\tau}]_{\mathcal{I}}=\phi^{\omega^{\prime}}_{\tau},\ \ \ \ \tau<\tau^{+}_{\phi}.$$  |
L723: As a result, using [【77†DX22】, Proposition 2.18(ii)], we get that $(\phi^{\omega}_{\tau})^{\mathrm{an}}=(\phi^{\omega^{\prime}}_{\tau})^{\mathrm{an}},\ \tau<\tau^{+}_{\phi}$. Comparing with (【123†24】), we arrive at $(\phi^{\omega})^{\mathrm{an}}=(\phi^{\omega^{\prime}})^{\mathrm{an}}$, as desired.
L724: Moreover, our above analysis also shows that $\phi^{\mathrm{an}}\in\bigcap_{L^{\prime}}\mathrm{PSH}((L+L^{\prime})^{\mathrm{an}})$, where $L^{\prime}$ runs over all ample $\mathbb{Q}$-line bundles on $X$. The latter space is equal to $\mathrm{PSH}(L^{\mathrm{an}})$ by [【42†BJ21】, Theorem 4.5], so our map $\phi\mapsto\phi^{\mathrm{an}}$ from (【160†40】) is indeed well-defined.

L728:  | $$\phi^{\mathrm{an}}(v_{\mathrm{triv}})=\tau^{+}_{\phi},$$  |  | (41)
L729: where $v_{\mathrm{triv}}$ denotes the trivial valuation.
L730: 
L731: We show that the map of (【69†39】) is actually a bijection, giving a transcendental interpretation of the non-Archimedean metrics of Boucksom–Jonsson:
L732: ###### Theorem 3.14.
L733: 
L734: Assume that $\{\theta\}=c_{1}(L)$ for some pseudoeffective $\mathbb{Q}$-line bundle $L$. The map of (【69†39】) is a bijection. In addition, let $\phi,\chi\in\mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(\theta)$. If $\phi^{\mathrm{an}}\leq\chi^{\mathrm{an}}$ then $\phi\leq\chi$.
L735: ###### Proof.
L736: 
L737: By scaling, we may assume that $L$ is a line bundle. We first address the last statement. Let $\phi=\{\{\phi^{\omega}_{\tau}\}_{\tau}\}_{\omega}$ and $\chi=\{\{\chi^{\omega}_{\tau}\}_{\tau}\}_{\omega}$. Given that $\phi^{\mathrm{an}}\leq\chi^{\mathrm{an}}$, Theorem 【153†3.12】(i) gives that $\phi^{\omega}_{\tau}\leq\chi^{\omega}_{\tau},\ \tau\in\mathbb{R},\omega\in\mathcal{K}$, implying that $\phi\leq\chi$. This immediately gives that $\phi\mapsto\phi^{\mathrm{an}}$ is injective.
L738: To address surjectivity, let $\chi\in\textup{PSH}(L^{\mathrm{an}})$. Let $\omega\in\mathcal{K}$ such that $\{\omega\}=c_{1}(T)$ for a $\mathbb{Q}$-ample line bundle $T$. Then $\chi\in\textup{PSH}((L+T)^{\mathrm{an}})$, and by Theorem 【153†3.12】(ii) there exists $\{\phi^{\omega}_{\tau}\}\tau\in\textup{MTC}_{\mathcal{I}}(\theta+\omega)$ such that $(\phi^{\omega})^{\mathrm{an}}=\chi$.
L739: Let $\omega^{\prime}\in\mathcal{K}$ such that $\{\omega^{\prime}\}=c_{1}(T^{\prime})$, for some $\mathbb{Q}$-ample line bundle $T^{\prime}$ and $\omega\leq\omega^{\prime}$. Due to injectivity of $\textup{MTC}_{\mathcal{I}}(\theta+\omega^{\prime})\mapsto\mathrm{PSH}((L+T^{\prime})^{\mathrm{an}})$ we get that $P_{\theta+\omega^{\prime}}[\phi^{\omega}_{\tau}]_{\mathcal{I}}=\phi^{\omega^{\prime}}_{\tau},\ \tau<\tau^{+}_{\phi}$.
L740: For a non-rational form $\omega^{\prime\prime}\in\mathcal{K}$, let $\omega\in\mathcal{K}$ such that $\{\omega\}=c_{1}(T)$ for a $\mathbb{Q}$-ample line bundle $T$ and $\omega\leq\omega^{\prime\prime}$. Then we define
L741: 
L742:  | $$\phi^{\omega^{\prime\prime}}_{\tau}=P_{\theta+\omega^{\prime\prime}}[\phi^{\omega}_{\tau}]_{\mathcal{I}},\ \ \ \tau<\tau^{+}_{\phi}.$$  |
L743: It is immediate to see that the above definition is independent of the choice of $\omega$. We see that $\{\{\phi^{\omega}_{\tau}\}_{\tau}\}_{\omega}\in\textup{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(\theta)$, and $\phi^{\mathrm{an}}=\chi$, proving surjectivity. ∎
