L774: ### 3.4 The non-Archimedean finite energy space
L775: 
L776: Let $X$ be a compact Kähler manifold of dimension $n$ and $\{\theta\}$ be a big cohomology class on $X$. Let $V=\int_{X}\theta_{V_{\theta}}^{n}$ denote the volume of $\{\theta\}$.
L777: Recall that $\textup{MTC}_{\mathcal{I}}(\theta)$ naturally embeds into $\mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(\theta)$ (【164†29】). For $\{\eta_{\tau}\}_{\tau}\in\textup{MTC}_{\mathcal{I}}(\theta)$ we define the Monge–Ampère energy $I^{\mathrm{NA}}_{\theta}:\textup{MTC}_{\mathcal{I}}(\theta)\rightarrow[-\infty,\infty)$ as
L778:  | $$I_{\theta}^{\mathrm{NA}}(\eta):=I_{\theta}\{\eta_{\tau}\}=\tau^{+}_{\eta}+\frac{1}{V}\int_{-\infty}^{\tau^{+}_{\eta}}\left(\int_{X}\theta_{\eta_{\tau}}^{n}-V\right)\,\mathrm{d}\tau.$$  |  | (43)
L779: 
L780: We extend $I^{\mathrm{NA}}_{\theta}$ to a function on $\mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(\theta)$ by setting it to be $-\infty$ on $\mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(\theta)\setminus\textup{MTC}_{\mathcal{I}}(\theta)$.
L781: 
L782: We then define the non-Archimedean finite energy space as
L783:  | $$\mathcal{E}^{1,\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta):=\left\{\phi\in\mathrm{PSH}^{\mathrm{NA}}(\theta):I_{\theta}^{\mathrm{NA}}(\phi)>-\infty\right\}\,.$$  |
L784: Since $V\geq\int_{X}\theta_{\eta_{\tau}}^{n}$, for (【165†43】) to be finite $\int_{X}\theta_{\eta_{\tau}}^{n}$ must converge to $V$ as $\tau\searrow-\infty$. By [【108†DDNL18a】, Theorem 2.3] this implies that $\int_{X}\theta_{\eta_{-\infty}}^{n}=V$. Since $\eta_{-\infty}$ is maximal, we obtain that $\eta_{-\infty}=V_{\theta}$, i.e., $\{\eta_{\tau}\}_{\tau}$ is a test curve relative to $V_{\theta}$, whenever $\{\eta_{\tau}\}_{\tau}\in\mathcal{E}^{1,\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta)$.
L785: Similarly, the non-Archimedean $\mathcal{J}$ functional is introduced as
L786: 
L787:  | $$\mathcal{J}_{\theta}^{\mathrm{NA}}(\eta)=\tau^{+}_{\eta}-I_{\theta}^{\mathrm{NA}}(\eta)=\frac{1}{V}\int_{-\infty}^{\tau^{+}_{\eta}}\left(V-\int_{X}\theta_{\eta_{\tau}}^{n}\right)\,\mathrm{d}\tau.$$  |  | (44)
L788: When $\{\theta\}=c_{1}(L)$ for some ample $\mathbb{Q}$-line bundle $L$, it has been pointed out in [【77†DX22】, Theorem 1.1, Theorem 1.2] that our definitions of $\mathcal{E}^{1,\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta)$ and $\mathcal{J}_{\theta}^{\mathrm{NA}},I_{\theta}^{\mathrm{NA}}$ coincide with the ones given in [【42†BJ21】].
L789: In the literature, the space $\mathcal{E}^{1}(L^{\mathrm{an}})$ has not yet been defined for a general big $\mathbb{Q}$-line bundle $L$. Above we gave an analytic definition, but it is desirable to have a purely non-Archimedean/algebraic definition as well.
L790: 
L791: After defining finite energy non-Archimedean spaces, we can finally relate the $\mathcal{I}$-maximal finite energy rays to non-Archimedean potentials, as a direct consequence of Theorem 【74†2.6】:
L792: ###### Theorem 3.17.
L793: 
L794: There is a bijective function $\{\psi_{\tau}\}_{\tau}\mapsto\{\check{\psi}_{t}\}_{t}$, between $\mathcal{R}^{1}_{\mathcal{I}}(X,\theta)$ and $\mathcal{E}^{1,\mathrm{NA}}(X,\theta)$. Moreover, under this correspondence, the radial Monge–Ampère energy corresponds to the non-Archimedean Monge–Ampère energy defined in (【165†43】).
