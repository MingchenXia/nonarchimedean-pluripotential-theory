L111: ## 2. K-stability for big line bundles
L112: 
L113: We will be interested throughout in a smooth complex projective variety $X$ of dimension $n$, with canonical class $K_{X}$. For a big line bundle $L$ on $X$, our aim is to define the pair of conditions that $(X,L)$ be uniformly K-stable and divisorially stable. In subsequent sections, we will prove the equivalence of the two conditions.
L114: ### 2.1. Preliminaries on positivity and volume
L115: 
L116: We begin by recalling the basics of positivity theory in algebraic geometry, referring to Lazarsfeld and Boucksom–Favre–Jonsson for introductions [【69†Laz17】, 【70†Laz04】, 【49†BFJ09】]. In the present section, we assume that $X$ is merely normal.
L117: #### 2.1.1. Positivity of line bundles.
L118: 
L119: We recall that a line bundle $L$ on $X$ is big if its volume
L120: 
L121:  | $$\mathrm{vol}(L):=\lim_{k\to\infty}\frac{h^{0}(X,kL)}{k^{n}/n!}$$  |
L122: is strictly positive; it is a classical result that the limit involved in the definition exists. Equivalently, $L$ is big if and only if $L=A+E$ with $A$ ample and $E$ effective on $X$. Bigness can be generalised in the natural way to $\mathbb{R}$-divisors and is a numerical property. In particular, the big cone in $N^{1}(X)$ is open [【69†Laz17】, Corollary 2.2.24]. A line bundle $L$ is pseudoeffective if its numerical class lies in the closure of the big cone.
L123: We say that $L\geq L^{\prime}$ if $L-L^{\prime}$ is pseudoeffective.
