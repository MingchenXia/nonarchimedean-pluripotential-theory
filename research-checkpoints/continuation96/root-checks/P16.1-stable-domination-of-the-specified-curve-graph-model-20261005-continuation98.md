To check the multipointed-curve definition, choose a relatively very ample bundle H on G. For each integer m, proper flat finite-presentation Euler-characteristic constancy (Stacks0B9T) for H^m gives the same Hilbert polynomial on the generic and special fibers. The generic polynomial has degree one with positive leading coefficient. Hence the special fiber's maximum dimension is one. A zero-dimensional irreducible component of a finite-type scheme over the algebraically closed residue field would be a point not contained in any other component. The finitely many other components form a closed complement, making this point open and closed. Connectedness excludes it. All special components therefore have dimension one. The generic components do also. Flat finite presentation and this pure relative dimension, with the empty flat marking D, make (G,empty) a multipointed R-curve in the exact definition at rendered61–95. Its generic fiber is smooth, hence semistable.
