ID: n1

SUMMARY:
Arbitrary classical psh targets admit decreasing ordinary CLD-smooth psh sequences on specified curves over countably dense fields, including finite unibranch algebraic normalization. This is a sufficient curve sector, not a characterization.

DETAILED SUMMARY:
Let K be complete, algebraically closed, nontrivially valued, and have a countable dense subset. For smooth proper C, assume its residue field is algebraic over a finite field, or C is P1, or C is Mumford. Every arbitrary classical psh target on an ordinary open subset of C admits ordinary smooth psh approximants decreasing on one fixed ordinary neighborhood, including discontinuities and permitted minus-infinity values. The proof extracts a sequence from Thuillier’s lisse net on a second-countable strictly affinoid domain, applies Wanner Lemma 5.2 separately to its terms, and enforces a quantitative decreasing gap. A finite unibranch normalization permits ordinary smooth descent because each smooth term is constant near the rational preimage of the singularity. Scalar compatibility concerns pullbacks of constructed sequences; general scalar descent, multibranch synchronization, and necessity of the hypotheses remain unresolved.

TEXT:
Claim and published premises. Let K be complete, algebraically closed, nontrivially valued, with a countable dense subset. Let C be smooth proper algebraic, and suppose either the residue field is algebraic over a finite field, C=P1, or C is Mumford. Every arbitrary classical psh u:W→R∪{−∞} on an ordinary open W⊂C^an has, near each point, a decreasing sequence of finite ordinary CLD-smooth psh functions on one fixed ordinary neighborhood. GR arXiv:2506.13548v2, 3.4.2 and 4.1.2, identifies the starting class on connected smooth strictly analytic curves with Thuillier subharmonic functions or the identically −∞ case. Thuillier Theorem 3.4.2 supplies a decreasing net of finite continuous lisse subharmonic functions. Wanner’s standing field assumptions are exactly the field assumptions above except countable density. Her Lemma 5.2, under H_C⊂C∞, uniformly smooths each lisse subharmonic function on a strictly affinoid Y by continuous functions on Y which are ordinary smooth and psh on Y\∂Y. Her Corollary 5.4 verifies H_C⊂C∞ under the three displayed conditions; its own target is finite continuous, so it is not invoked directly for arbitrary u.

Countable extraction. On a second-countable space Z, let continuous f_i decrease as a net to u. For rational q, {u<q}=⋃_i{f_i<q}. Each such open subspace is Lindelöf, so select countably many indices covering it. Enumerate all selected indices over all rational q and recursively choose indices dominating the preceding choice and the next selected index. The resulting terms decrease, remain above u, and eventually lie below every rational q>u(z). This proves pointwise convergence, including u(z)=−∞. The chosen indices need not be cofinal in the original directed set.

Why the countability hypothesis holds here. A strictly affinoid algebra A is a quotient of K〈T1,…,TN〉. Polynomials over a countable dense subring give a countable dense subset A0. For every bounded multiplicative seminorm x, |a(x)|≤||a||, by applying boundedness to powers. Therefore ||a(x)|−|b(x)||≤||a−b|| uniformly in x. All evaluation functions are uniform limits of evaluations from A0. Their rational interval inverse images and finite intersections form a countable base for M(A).

Construction on a fixed neighborhood. Take a relatively compact ordinary open W′ around x inside W and one strictly affinoid neighborhood Y⊂W′ of x. Restrict Thuillier’s net to Y and apply the extraction argument to obtain h_j↓u on Y. Put V=Y\∂Y, an ordinary open neighborhood of x. Apply Wanner Lemma 5.2 to each h_j on this same Y, obtaining a continuous s_j on Y, ordinary smooth and psh on V, with ||s_j−h_j||_Y≤ε_j=2^(−j). Define v_j=s_j+4ε_j. Then h_j+3ε_j≤v_j≤h_j+5ε_j and
v_j−v_(j+1)≥h_j−h_(j+1)+4(ε_j−ε_(j+1))−ε_j−ε_(j+1)≥ε_j/2>0.
Thus v_j↓u on the single fixed V. At a minus-infinity value the upper bound tends to minus infinity. For an identically minus-infinity neighborhood use v_j=−j. Local uniform convergence is asserted only for finite continuous targets, by Dini’s theorem.

Ordinary regularity, positivity, and compatibility. Wanner’s lemma uses ordinary smooth harmonic pieces under H_C⊂C∞ and convex regularized maxima on annuli, with agreement near the gluing boundaries. The positivity conclusion is supplied by that lemma. Adding constants preserves it. The actual saved CD v2 uses ordinary open charts in Proposition 15.1.1(b); its smooth stage is Psh_0, its first decreasing-sequence stage is Psh_1 in Lemma 8.1.4, and smooth pullback is covered by Proposition 8.1.5. These functions have nonnegative d′d″ forms and, on the boundaryless neighborhoods here, positive d′d″[v_j] currents by CD §8.3 and Proposition 8.3.1. Ordinary chart representatives agree because each v_j is one globally defined function on V.

For every analytic extension L/K, the same smooth real chart functions applied to the pulled-back analytic units represent v_j∘π on ordinary opens of V_L. CD Lemma 6.5.1 gives local positive tropical representatives of d′d″v_j. Their pulled-back images remain in the same paralinéaire support, and CD Lemma 1.2.11 preserves their positivity under linear pullback. Thus the scalar pullbacks are ordinary smooth psh. Their gaps remain ≥2^(−j−1), and their pointwise limit is u∘π. No countability of L is required, and no independently constructed sequences must be synchronized.

Unibranch extension. Let D be an integral proper algebraic curve with finite normalization ν:C→D bijective on geometric points, where C satisfies the conditions above. Near a singular point x remove the other finitely many singularities. There is one rational rigid point x′ over x, and ν is an isomorphism elsewhere. The finite closed bijection is a homeomorphism. Pull u back to C and construct the preceding sequence on V′ around x′. Closedness supplies one ordinary N around x with ν^(−1)(N)⊂V′. Descend the sequence by the homeomorphism. Every ordinary smooth function is constant near a rational rigid point: in an ordinary chart, finitely many invertible a_i satisfy |a_i−a_i(x′)|<|a_i(x′)| on a sufficiently small neighborhood, so all |a_i| are constant. Each descended term is consequently constant near x and smooth by the isomorphism elsewhere. Its d′d″ form is zero on the constant neighborhood and nonnegative elsewhere. Global descent preserves its gaps and limit. Scalar pullback then follows from the preceding chart argument. This includes cuspidal proper curves with normalization P1.

Limitations. The field and harmonic-smoothness conditions are sufficient, not proved necessary. Multibranch normalization can give different constant values on different preimages at every stage; limit agreement does not justify descent. No general finite smooth descent or strict scalar descent is claimed. All-domain lisse functions were not identified with strict real-PL functions, and weak smoothness was not substituted for ordinary smoothness.
