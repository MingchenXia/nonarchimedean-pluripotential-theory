An arbitrary generator of a primary ideal in a reduced nonnormal ring need not be regular. For example, in R=k[x,y]/(xy) localized at (x,y), the primary ideal (x,y) has zero-divisor generators. The chart with tautological generator x misses the generic component on which x=0. Thus the ROOT326 sentence describing every chart as R_f with regular tautological f needs a chosen regular generating cover or a different proof. Global generic restriction gives the required conclusion without assuming regularity of an arbitrary generator.

Let R be a positive-dimensional reduced Noetherian local ring, with minimal primes p_1,...,p_s, and let J be proper m-primary. The Rees algebra A=R[Jt] is an actual subring of R[t], so it is reduced. Define

P_i = A intersect p_i[t].

Each P_i is prime because the image of A in (R/p_i)[t] is the domain (R/p_i)[J_i t]. Their intersection is zero, and their contractions to R are the distinct incomparable p_i. They are therefore incomparable. Every prime containing zero contains some P_i: the product of the finitely many P_i is contained in their zero intersection, and primeness applies. Hence these P_i are EXACTLY the minimal primes of A. This proves that there are no additional irreducible components of the Rees algebra unsupported over the generic components of R.

No p_i equals m: if m were minimal in a reduced local ring, it would be the sole minimal prime and would equal zero, making R a field. Since J is m-primary, J is not contained in any p_i. Consequently A_+ is not contained in P_i and every component survives in Y=Proj(A). Y is reduced, its irreducible components correspond precisely to the generic components of Spec(R), and over each generic point J is the unit ideal, so Y is isomorphic to Spec(R) there. The blowup is likewise the identity on the entire punctured local spectrum.

For any reduced scheme, a section of O_Y vanishing at every generic point of every irreducible component is zero. On an affine open this follows because a reduced ring injects into the product of the fraction fields of its minimal-prime quotients. Apply this on the finitely many affine blowup charts.
