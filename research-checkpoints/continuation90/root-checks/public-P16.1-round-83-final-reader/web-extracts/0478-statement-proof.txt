L23: 【8†Lemma 33.14.1.】 Let $X$ be a scheme which is locally of finite type over $k$. Then
L24:   1. for any closed point $x \in X$ the extension $\kappa (x)/k$ is algebraic, and
L25: 
L26:   2. $X$ is a Jacobson scheme (Properties, Definition 【14†28.6.1】).
L27: Proof. A scheme is Jacobson if and only if it has an affine open covering by Jacobson schemes, see Properties, Lemma 【15†28.6.3】. The property on residue fields at closed points is also local on $X$. Hence we may assume that $X$ is affine. In this case the result is a consequence of the Hilbert Nullstellensatz, see Algebra, Theorem 【16†10.34.1】. It also follows from a combination of Morphisms, Lemmas 【17†29.16.8】, 【18†29.16.9】, and 【19†29.16.10】. $\square$
