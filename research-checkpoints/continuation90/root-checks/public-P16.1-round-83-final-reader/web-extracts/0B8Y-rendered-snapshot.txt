L23: 【8†Lemma 33.43.8.】 Let $k$ be a field. Let $X$ be a curve over $k$. Let $x \in X$ be a closed point. We think of $x$ as a (reduced) closed subscheme of $X$ with sheaf of ideals $\mathcal{I}$. The following are equivalent
L24: 
L25:   1. $\mathcal{O}_{X, x}$ is regular,
L26: 
L27:   2. $\mathcal{O}_{X, x}$ is normal,
L28: 
L29:   3. $\mathcal{O}_{X, x}$ is a discrete valuation ring,
L30: 
L31:   4. $\mathcal{I}$ is an invertible $\mathcal{O}_ X$-module,
L32: 
L33:   5. $x$ is an effective Cartier divisor on $X$.
L34: If $k$ is perfect or if $\kappa (x)$ is separable over $k$, these are also equivalent to
L35: 
L36:   1. $X \to \mathop{\mathrm{Spec}}(k)$ is smooth at $x$.
L37: Proof. Since $X$ is a curve, the local ring $\mathcal{O}_{X, x}$ is a Noetherian local domain of dimension $1$ (Lemma 【15†33.20.3】). Parts (4) and (5) are equivalent by definition and are equivalent to $\mathcal{I}_ x = \mathfrak m_ x \subset \mathcal{O}_{X, x}$ having one generator (Divisors, Lemma 【16†31.16.2】). The equivalence of (1), (2), (3), (4), and (5) therefore follows from Algebra, Lemma 【17†10.119.7】. The final statement follows from Lemma 【18†33.25.8】 in case $k$ is perfect.
L38: If $\kappa (x)/k$ is separable, then the equivalence follows from Algebra, Lemma 【19†10.140.5】. $\square$
