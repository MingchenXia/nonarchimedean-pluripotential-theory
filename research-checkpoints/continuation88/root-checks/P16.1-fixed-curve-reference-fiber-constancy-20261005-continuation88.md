# Fixed curve reference measures and fiber constancy

This is an unreviewed reconstruction for original P16.1 and the shared singular-quotient obstruction in P13.2. It does not assert target-side genuine psh membership, singular EP, or a solved original. The independent native Runge continuation83 has frozen input and receives no feedback from this draft.

## 1. The precise fiber claim

Let \(k\) be algebraically closed, trivially valued, of characteristic zero and of any cardinality. Let \(\pi:Y\to X\) be projective birational, with \(Y\) smooth projective integral, \(X\) projective integral geometrically unibranch, and \(L\) an actual ample rational line bundle on \(X\). Put \(\theta=\pi^*c_1(L)\). The fibers are geometrically connected: factor through the finite universal-homeomorphism normalization and apply normal-target Stein factorization to the resolution, as reconstructed with exact Stacks hypotheses in continuation81.

For an actual bounded \(\varphi\in\operatorname{PSH}(Y,\theta)\), BJ Definition4.1 gives a decreasing NET \(\varphi_i\in H_{\mathbb R}(L_i)\) with \(c_1(L_i)\to\theta\). Boundedness of the limit makes each finite-stage \(L_i\) semiample after passage to a tail, since its generating sections have no common zero. Retain the actual canonical reference metric on each \(L_i\). Do not assume that this is a sequence of sections of \(\pi^*L\): same-bundle regularization in the semiample class is exactly part of the unresolved descent issue.

The proposal is to show \(\varphi\) constant on each analytic fiber. Then apply the assertion to \(\max(\varphi,-m)\), which belongs to the same nef class, to obtain constancy for every unbounded actual \(\varphi\), including fibers with value \(-\infty\).

## 2. Fixed model background on a single proper curve

Fix an analytic point \(s\in X^{an}\), \(F=\mathscr H(s)\), and a complete algebraically closed nontrivially valued extension \(K/F\). Let \(f:C\to Y_K\) be a morphism from a smooth proper connected \(K\)-curve whose composite to \(X_K\) is the \(K\)-point defined by \(s\). Write \(\varphi_i^C=\varphi_i\circ f^{an}\).

The following geometric/reference applications M1–M3 must be established in full; they are explicit antecedents of the deduction below, rather than silently inferred from a numerical class:

* M1: choose ONE projective admissible formal model \(\mathfrak C\) of \(C\), dominating the graph closure of \(f\) into the constant formal model of \(Y_K\), and carrying the pullbacks of ALL the canonical references \(L_i\). Its map to the constant model of \(X_K\) factors through the section \(\operatorname{Spec}K^\circ\to X_{K^\circ}\) extending the constant point. This section exists by properness. There are finitely many reduced special-fiber curve components \(E\), and their associated analytic points form one fixed finite set \(\Sigma\subset I(C)\).
* M2: the curvature \(\nu_i\) of the pulled canonical reference on \(L_i|_C\) has the raw model formula \(\nu_i=\sum_{E}a_{E,i}\delta_{\zeta_E}\), where \(a_{E,i}\ge0\) and each coefficient is the fixed multiplicity/residue factor times the degree of \(L_i\) on the image of \(E\). The pulled FS metric has positive curvature \(\mu_i\), with \(dd^c\varphi_i^C=\mu_i-\nu_i\). Fundamental-cycle and residue factors remain in this formula.
* M3: for each of these fixed proper curves over the residue-field extension of \(k\), degree is a continuous linear functional on \(N^1(Y)\). Numerical triviality is preserved under extension of algebraically closed fields. Since the map of \(E\) to \(X\) is constant, \(\deg_E\theta=0\). Consequently \(a_{E,i}\to0\), and \(\|\nu_i\|\to0\) on the FIXED finite support.

The graph model is selected from the fixed morphism \(f\), not separately from each \(i\). Even though the line bundles \(L_i\) vary, they are all pulled back from the same constant model of \(Y\). This is the proposed reason that a common reference model can work. A complete proof must verify admissible-model domination, reference base-change identity and the numerical degree functional; no normalization-finiteness assertion over an arbitrary nondiscrete valuation ring is inserted.

## 3. Local Green correction with a uniform limit

Choose a connected strict affinoid neighborhood \(V\) in \(C^{an}\), with nonempty boundary, containing the tested point in its interior, and with \(\partial V\cap\Sigma=\varnothing\). For \(\zeta\in\Sigma\cap V\), Thuillier3.3.7(ii) gives a finite continuous lisse \(g_\zeta^V\), zero on \(\partial V\), with
\[
dd^c g_\zeta^V=-\delta_\zeta\quad\text{on the interior of }V.
\]
The actual primary sign is retained. Put
\[
p_i=-\sum_{\zeta\in\Sigma\cap V}a_{\zeta,i}g_\zeta^V.
\]
Then \(dd^cp_i=\nu_i\) in the interior and \(\|p_i\|_{C^0(V)}\to0\), because the support is fixed and finite and every coefficient tends to zero. Thus \(h_i=\varphi_i^C+p_i\) is continuous with positive local curvature \(\mu_i\). Wanner4.7, on the analytification of this smooth proper curve over the complete algebraically closed nontrivially valued \(K\), makes \(h_i\) Thuillier-subharmonic. No unrestricted higher-dimensional positive-current converse is invoked.

The \(h_i\) need not decrease. It is the genuine \(\varphi_i^C\) that decreases, while \(p_i\) tends to zero UNIFORMLY. Let \(D\subset V^\circ\) be any strict affinoid comparison domain, and let \(q\) be harmonic near \(D\) with \(\varphi^C\le q\) on \(\partial D\). For every \(\epsilon>0\), compactness and the decreasing continuous majorants imply that eventually \(\varphi_i^C<q+\epsilon\) on the ENTIRE boundary. This is the decreasing-superlevel finite-intersection argument, not uniform convergence to a discontinuous limit. Also eventually \(|p_i|<\epsilon\) on \(D\). Subharmonic comparison gives \(h_i\le q+2\epsilon\) on \(D\), hence \(\varphi_i^C\le q+3\epsilon\) there. Let the net decrease, then \(\epsilon\downarrow0\).

This proves \(\varphi^C\) subharmonic. It is bounded and usc on the proper connected curve, so it attains a finite maximum. Thuillier3.1.11 makes the maximum set open; usc makes it closed. Therefore \(\varphi^C\) is constant on \(C^{an}\).

Small total reference mass with MOVING unbounded Green potentials would not suffice. Fixed finite support and its finite continuous local Green functions are the indispensable extra facts.

## 4. Every original analytic point can be compared

The geometrically connected reduced projective fiber \(Y_s\) has a closed point \(z\) with residue field finite over \(F\). That extension has a unique absolute value because \(F\) is complete; it defines a fixed analytic fiber point. Fix any other analytic point \(y\) in the original fiber. Extend its completed residue field \(\mathscr H(y)\) to a complete algebraically closed nontrivially valued \(K\); if needed first adjoin a nontrivial Gauss variable. Embed the finite residue field of \(z\) into \(K\) over \(F\). Its norm is unchanged by uniqueness.

Both \(y\) and \(z\) now lift to \(K\)-rational points of the scheme fiber. The latter is geometrically connected and projective. Resolve its finitely many reduced irreducible components and use ordinary Bertini on a blowup of two smooth points to join preimages within each component. Its finite component-intersection graph gives a finite chain of morphisms from smooth proper connected curves joining the two points, as in continuation81. These are curve MORPHISMS, including resolution/blowup maps, not merely smooth embedded curves.

Apply the preceding zero-background curve assertion to every curve in this chain. Actual base-change pullback preserves the original values of the scalar functions \(\varphi_i\) and \(\varphi\). Thus \(\varphi(y)=\varphi(z)\). One comparison point \(z\) works for every \(y\); no common valued overfield for all points, density-plus-usc inference, or simultaneous descent of all potentials is required.

For unbounded \(\varphi\), the genuine floors decrease in the same nef class. Fiber constancy of every floor implies constancy of \(\varphi\) with its actual finite or \(-\infty\) value.

## 5. The exact descent limit

If M1–M3 and the indicated curve/reference identities hold, define \(\psi(s)\) to be the common fiber value. Properness makes this descended function usc: the closed superlevel set downstairs is the proper image of the corresponding closed superlevel set upstairs. It is finite at the generic trivial point if \(\varphi\) is.

This provides an actual scalar function \(\psi\) with \(\psi\circ\pi^{an}=\varphi\). It does NOT yet prove \(\psi\in\operatorname{PSH}(X,c_1(L))\). ## 6. A concrete model application and its remaining limit

BE Proposition5.13 (LF1973–1990) supplies a useful part of M1 without imposing discrete valuation. Given a projective model of the curve and the constant projective model \(Y_{K^\circ}\), take the schematic flat closure of the graph of \(f\) in their product. It dominates the chosen curve model and extends \(f\). Pullback of EVERY line bundle on the constant target is now available on this ONE graph model. The proposition identifies the induced model metric with the actual pullback, not only its numerical class. The reference is the trivial metric over \(k\), whose constant model after base extension induces exactly that metric (BE5.9,5.10 and the local-frame definition).

The composite graph-model morphism to \(X_{K^\circ}\) agrees on the generic fiber with the extension of the constant \(K\)-point. Since the model is flat over \(K^\circ\), its generic fiber is schematically dense; separatedness of \(X_{K^\circ}\) extends the equality to the whole model. Thus every special component maps to one point of \(X_{\widetilde K}\).

BE4.20 (LF1750–1775) permits integral closure in the reduced generic fiber and proves its finiteness specifically when the complete nontrivially valued \(K\) is ALGEBRAICALLY CLOSED. This is the actual auxiliary field chosen in section4. It also gives reduced special fiber. This precise theorem does not permit arbitrary densely valued fields, as BE4.21 explicitly explains. No such general finiteness was used.

These primary statements establish the graph domination and reference identity, but the remaining application still must relate that one admissible graph model to a suitable semistable refinement and identify its curve curvature formula. Wanner3.26–3.28 give the formal metric and slope formulas, citing semistable reduction and the pertinent primary slope theorem. Every hypothesis and the domination of the chosen graph model must be verified; existence of SOME semistable model alone does not prove this refinement statement. The numerical base-extension statement in M3 also remains to be justified.

## 7. The exact conditional route from descent to EP

Suppose M1–M3, the actual curve/reference restrictions and geometric fiber chain are established. Suppose additionally that the unresolved countable-field Runge/global theorem C1 proves the following precise conclusion, with its original reference compatibility: every scalar function descended in section5 belongs to \(\operatorname{PSH}(X,L)\), for every countable algebraically closed trivially valued characteristic-zero field and actual ample rational \(L\). C1 is an explicit hypothesis here, not an accepted result of continuation83 or this draft.

Then \(\pi^*:\operatorname{PSH}(X,L)\to\operatorname{PSH}(Y,\pi^*L)\) is onto. Actual pullback gives the forward inclusion by BJ4.7. Birationality identifies the divisorial valuations of the two common function fields; BJ4.20 defines both topologies by their values on precisely this set. Pullback is therefore a homeomorphism, and surjectivity of the analytic map identifies their suprema. The normalized spaces are homeomorphic.

The upstairs class \(\pi^*c_1(L)\) is nef on smooth projective \(Y\). BJ5.20 and5.21 give compactness of its normalized genuine psh space. The homeomorphism makes the downstairs normalized space compact. BJ5.11, valid for ANY numerical class on the target, now gives its envelope property. This uses that theorem's equivalence after actual onto membership has been proved. BJ5.13 is not used to obtain that membership, because its hypotheses already require approaching target EP.

This conditional argument has no premise involving a closed invariant subset or a uniform-continuity statement on divisorial points. It uses actual all-point fiber descent and exactly the divisorial topology already defining the genuine spaces.

To remove the countable-field restriction, continuation80 sections5–6 provide a separate conditional finite-obstacle/finite-competitor route. Keep its C2–C4 as explicit operator/descent hypotheses until proved. BJ4.15 supplies decreasing continuous finite \(H^{dom}(L)\) majorants without assuming EP on a singular target. Given any competitor \(q\le f\) for a continuous PL obstacle, compactness implies that these majorants eventually satisfy \(h_i\le f+\epsilon\), by the decreasing closed-set finite-intersection argument. Only each individual finite \(h_i-\epsilon\), its model, sections and nef data must descend to an enlarged countable algebraically closed subfield. Real finite weights remain real constants. One must not assert that an arbitrary net or all competitors descend to one field.

The countable envelope pulled back from the original obstacle field has actual orthogonality on every such enlarged countable field if continuous MA base-change pushforward and the fundamental-cycle formula are proved. Countable finite-energy domination then bounds each descended finite competitor. Passing through the majorants gives the exact envelope downstairs over the arbitrary-cardinality \(k\). This is an actual candidate route, with those operator and descent gates retained. Equality of inverse images of measure supports is neither required nor asserted.
