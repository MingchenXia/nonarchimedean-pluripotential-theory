2     Upper-semicontinuous local obstacle envelopes
Active items: P03.1, P03.2, P03.3. Status: Open in the residual scope.

2.1   Atomic targets
P03.1 (T2): Classical positivity of regularized local envelopes
Find sharp normal or geometrically unibranch higher-dimensional hypotheses under which the
upper-semicontinuous regularization of the local-Fubini–Study/semipositive-real-PL envelope
is classically psh for every upper-semicontinuous obstacle. The perfected-unibranch reduced
strict curve range is already proved. Necessity must account for the branched, nonsplit-node,
and codimension-two pinching obstructions.

P03.2 (T3): CLD-v2 positivity of regularized local envelopes
For the same obstacle envelope, determine the additional hypotheses ensuring that its
upper-semicontinuous regularization belongs to the second CLD psh stage for every upper-
semicontinuous obstacle. Classical positivity alone is not the requested conclusion.

P03.3 (T3): Continuity of local obstacle envelopes
For every ﬁnite continuous obstacle, determine sharp additional hypotheses ensuring
continuity of the regularized local-Fubini–Study/semipositive-real-PL envelope. A modulus-
controlled subfamily or an invariant toric envelope does not establish continuity of the full
competitor family.

2.2   What constitutes a solution
Prove the asserted conclusion for the full competitor family and every obstacle in the stated
class. Account for branches, curve pullback, scalar extension and relative boundary, and
distinguish positivity from continuity. A suﬃcient property of one selected equicontinuous
subfamily is not a classiﬁcation of all envelopes.
