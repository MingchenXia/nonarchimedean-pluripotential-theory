# Divisor-reference envelope and a direct finite-energy polar test

Throughout k is algebraically closed, trivially valued, characteristic zero;
X is smooth integral projective of dimension n>=1. Retain actual rational
bundle data L=A+D, A ample and D effective rational Cartier. Let sigma be
the canonical section logarithm of D, and Z its analytic support. Use the
full reference-dominated cone proved in continuation106, subject to its
independent audit:

    P_D={u in PSH(L): u<=sigma+C for some real C}=sigma+PSH(A).

Q denotes the unique actual A-psh extension of u-sigma off Z. The following
arguments do not assume a minimal reference of class-volume mass.

## 1. The chosen reference is its own original-carrier envelope

Define the singularity-constrained envelope directly by

    R_D(0)=sup{u in PSH(L): u<=0 and u<=sigma+C for some real C}.

There is no implicit change of carrier and no requirement that all
candidates stay a bounded distance from sigma. Canonical section evaluation
gives sigma<=0 and sigma(v_triv)=0. Thus sigma itself is a candidate.

For any other candidate u, the full cone identity gives u=sigma+psi with
psi in PSH(A). The generic trivial valuation is outside Z, so

    sup psi=psi(v_triv)=u(v_triv)<=0.

Here sup psi=psi(v_triv) is the actual projective maximum principle, not
density of divisorial points in an arbitrary space of continuous functions.
Consequently psi<=0 everywhere and u<=sigma everywhere, also on Z where
both are minus infinity. It follows exactly, before regularization, that

    R_D(0)=sigma.

Since sigma is already genuine and upper semicontinuous, its usc
regularization changes nothing. The reference is therefore geometrically
specified by an actual constrained-envelope property. This does not prove
that the unrestricted minimal reference V_L equals sigma. It does show
that the prescribed singularity datum is fixed before solving the equation.

## 2. A finite-energy A-test with a pole along the whole fixed divisor

If Z is empty there is nothing to prove. Otherwise choose an integral
clearing power A_0=rA. By Stacks01Q3 Proposition28.27.13(7), the coherent
ideal sheaf I_{|D|} twisted by A_0^m is globally generated for all large m.
Its generic fiber is nonzero, because X is integral and |D| is proper.
There is consequently a nonzero section

    s in H^0(X,I_{|D|} tensor A_0^m) subset H^0(X,A_0^m).

The genuine one-section potential

    a=(rm)^(-1) log|s|

belongs to PSH(A), is at most zero, has supremum zero at v_triv, and is
minus infinity on Z. Put a_-=a-1, which is at most -1. For
alpha=1/(n+2), choose the convex nondecreasing function

    chi(t)=-(-t)^alpha                for t<=-1,
    chi(t)=-1+alpha*(t+1)             for -1<=t<=0.

Its one-sided derivatives agree at -1 and lie in [0,1]; its derivative on
(-infinity,-1] is alpha*(-t)^(alpha-1), increasing to alpha. BJ4.10 gives
w=chi(a_-)+1 in PSH(A), using the genuine zero reference in the ample
class. Its supremum is zero, w<=0, and w=-infinity on Z.

For t>=1 its sublevel set is exactly

    {w<=-t}={a<=1-(t+1)^(1/alpha)}.

BJ7.47(i), applied to the normalized genuine a, therefore gives

    Cap_A{w<=-t} <= n/((t+1)^(1/alpha)-1)=O(t^(-1/alpha)).

The sufficient estimate BJ7.47(ii) is

    J_A(w)<=1+integral_1^infinity t^n Cap_A{w<=-t}dt.

The displayed integral is finite, since n-1/alpha=-2. Hence w is an ACTUAL
member of E1(X,A), with a pole along every point of Z. No assumption about
a Dirac equation on Z is used.

The printed source Corollary7.48 states a larger exponent range. This
argument only uses the conservative alpha above and the stated sufficient
capacity estimate. It does not depend on that larger range. To see the
factor t^n in the estimate, bounded floors a_t=max(a,-t) satisfy
MA_A(a_t/t)>=t^(-n)MA_A(a_t) by the positive mixed expansion; compare tails
by strict-winning locality and equal mass, then use layer-cake integration.
The proof thus keeps the factor even where extracted source typography
omits it from the last displayed line.

## 3. Polar mass is excluded by the intrinsic target condition

Let V=A^n. Define the raw relative energy and intrinsic dual, with extended
integrals interpreted as usual for upper-bounded psh functions, by

    E_D(u)=E_raw,A(Q(u)),
    E_D^dual(mu)=sup_{u in sigma+E1(A)}(E_D(u)-integral Q(u)dmu),

for positive Radon mu of mass V. The quotient is an actual original-carrier
function and is determined by u and the retained datum. Under the genuine
bijection its dual is exactly E_raw,A^dual(mu).

If mu(Z)>0, the actual test u=sigma+w has Q(u)=w, finite raw energy and
integral w dmu=-infinity. Its dual objective is +infinity. Therefore

    E_D^dual(mu)<infinity => mu(Z)=0.

This is a consequence, not an extra arbitrary-support hypothesis. More
generally the granted ample intrinsic-dual theorem gives equivalence of
finite dual energy with integrability of EVERY actual quotient Q(u).
All support outside Z is allowed subject to that intrinsic condition.

Likewise any genuine ample E1 output integrates w by the ample weighted
pairing theorem, so its measure is zero on Z. The same is true of each
mixed E1 product: either use the granted mixed-test integrability, or
dominate it by n^n/n! times the top product of the finite-energy average.
Thus the relative local metric product's zero extension is justified by
one explicit actual finite-energy test, without needing to infer a
nonpluripolar property from an arbitrary inverse construction.
