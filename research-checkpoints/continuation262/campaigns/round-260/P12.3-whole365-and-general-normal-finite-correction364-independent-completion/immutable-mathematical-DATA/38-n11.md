# n11. Attempt at the remaining normal higher-dimensional theorem

Use n1-n10 with their stated qualifications. The isolated characteristic-zero gap is removed by n4, and n6 also covers concrete nonisolated targets with good finite covers. What remains is CE on arbitrary excellent NORMAL local domains of dimension at least three outside those sectors: in particular general nonisolated closed algebraic germs even over algebraically closed characteristic zero, higher-dimensional positive/mixed characteristic, and nonalgebraic or other-residue-field germs. We attempted three routes and obtain a precise sufficient criterion and a concrete failed estimate, but no complete theorem or exact normal counterexample.

The finite-trace argument of n4 is a useful conditional reduction. In the characteristic-zero normal algebraic source framework let every rational signed primary Cartier obstacle C have W=Env_pi(C_pi), and suppose W has source-nef ACTUAL primary-profile upper approximants B_k>=W converging coefficientwise. Also retain the proved embedding C_geom into source nef via1.12 and fixed base trace. Then n4's eta_k=max_i((B_k)_Ei-C_Ei)^+/b_i tends to zero; largest-trace2.13 puts B_k+eps_k Z(a)<=W. It gives uniform Cauchy profiles on every support by continuity and density, and EVERY D competitor is handled through its ONE actual C net at those finite vertices. Consequently CE follows by density. This criterion requires upper approximants, not merely coefficientwise lower primary approximations or numerical nef closure. It isolates exactly the missing input for the general source route.

The source Jacobian/defect correction does not supply that input when its cosupport is positive-dimensional. A concrete normal nonisolated characteristic-zero example is

    R=k[U,V,W,z]_(U,V,W,z)/(UW-V^2),   a=m.

Its Jacobian ideal is (U,V,W), and the singular locus is the z-axis. The centred semivaluation with support (U,V,W) and z-order one has v(a)=1 and v(Jac)=infinity. Thus no finite constant c can bound v(Jac)<=c v(a) on the full section. Equivalently the inequality Z(Jac)>=c Z(a), which is used in4.11's primary correction, cannot be assumed. Fraction-field divisorial monomial valuations also witness unboundedness: give U,V,W value L and z value one, for positive integers L. On the rank-three parity-lattice toric ring their rational weight functional has a rank-two kernel, giving residue transcendence degree TWO (the dimension minus one); the value group is discrete rank one, so they are divisorial. Their normalized m-value is one and Jacobian value is L. Defect unit does not repair this Jacobian obstruction. Nevertheless this R has a finite dominating smooth cover k[x,y,z] via U=x^2,V=xy,W=y^2, so n6 PROVES CE there. The example refutes that estimate, not the normal-envelope theorem.

The surface projection route stops at dimension two. Current0BGP is a surface theorem. A negativity lemma for the exceptional difference cannot supply the missing pushforward nefness. We therefore do not transfer the surface polytope proof automatically.

The energy route concerns existing D/E1 functions. The finite-cover route needs an ACTUAL dominating cover with source CE; no theorem in this reconstruction gives one for every excellent normal germ.
