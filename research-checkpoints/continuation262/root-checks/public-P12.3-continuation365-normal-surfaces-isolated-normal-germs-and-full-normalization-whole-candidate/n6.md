# n6. Normal targets with finite dominating covers

Use G331 and n1, with CE sectors established in n3-n5. Let A be an excellent normal local domain and A inject into a finite reduced algebra B whose EVERY minimal component dominates A. Keep all marked semilocal centres and every support. Pullback preserves C,D: this is granted by G331, and at the core can be checked without false blowup base change. The map Rees_A(I) tensor B -> Rees_B(IB) is surjective in every degree, so the actual source blowup maps to the finite base change. An exceptional Cartier equation stays regular on dominating reduced source components because it is nonzero at each generic point. Effectivity pulls back, contracted curve degrees obey the point/positive-field-degree rule, and support-quotient evaluation composes correctly. Uniform and actual decreasing limits then pull back.

G331's normal coefficient-norm trace has a finite positive weight at EVERY point of the full fibre, independent of choices, preserving C,D. For a domain component with fields K subset L, choose a finite normal extension M/K containing all embedding images and normalize A in M to E. Excellence makes E finite. Put G=Aut_K(M) and q=[M:K]/|G|, allowing inseparability. Norm factorization, with generic inseparable length q, and quotient-support Gauss coefficient minima identify its kernel measure at any base semivaluation as q sum_{sigma in G} delta_{w composed with sigma}, for ANY lift w. The choice independence and full support proved in the ENTIRE G331 force this orbit to be the ENTIRE E/A fibre. Every B/A fibre point lifts to E by its finite support-quotient lifting theorem. Thus all these fibres are finite, and G is transitive on the full E/A fibre, not merely on generic primes. No defectless ramification formula is invoked.

For H in C_B restrict to a dominating domain component, pull to E, and take the finite maximum of its G-translates. It is in C_E and full-fibre constant, so G331 positive C descent gives its descended C_A function. Its value is the maximum of H on that component's full fibre. Finite maximum over all dominating components gives

    MH(v)=max_{rho(w)=v} H(w) in C_A.

Every source semivaluation lies on a minimal component, including those with nonminimal support. For H=inf_alpha H_alpha in D_B, finite fibres and directedness give max_w inf_alpha H_alpha(w)=inf_alpha max_w H_alpha(w), also at bottom: any finite strict threshold is met simultaneously at the finitely many points by one index. Thus MH is in D_A through ONE decreasing C representation. The dominating-component hypothesis is essential; no generic degree is assigned to vertical components.

For either K=C or D and a finite bounded obstacle f, H<=f rho implies MH<=f and H<=rho*(MH). Base competitors pull back in the other direction. Supremizing proves the unrestricted RAW identity P_B^K(f rho)=rho*P_A^K(f), without assuming either supremum positive. If all source centres have CE, the left side for continuous f is a C_B function, fibre constant by the identity, and positive descent proves CE on A. An independent averaging check is useful: let U be the source continuous envelope and p its normalized positive full-fibre average. Then p belongs to C_A, is below f and dominates every target competitor. Its pullback is a source minorant, hence p(v)<=U(w) for every lift w. Since p(v) is a positive average of the finite U(w), all values equal p(v). This check does not attempt to force constancy by averaging extended bottoms. For USC obstacles use n5's ONE continuous-majorant net and pull it back; it dominates every source competitor termwise, hence gives pullback commutation there too. The one-factor gauge and decreasing-net rules follow.

There are actual NONFLAT and nonisolated examples. Over any field, A=k[U,V,W]_(U,V,W)/(UW-V^2) embeds in B=k[x,y]_(x,y) via U=x^2,V=xy,W=y^2. Normality follows from k[x^2,xy,y^2]=k[M] intersect k[x,y], M={(i,j) in Z^2:i+j even}, in k(x,y); both larger rings are normal, so their intersection is normal in its own fraction field. The extension is finite of degree two, with one marked centre and no vertical component. Its closed fibre has basis 1,x,y since m_A B=(x,y)^2, so dimension three; a finite flat rank-two algebra would have fibre dimension two. Surface CE holds on its source in every characteristic by n3. Adjoining any number of smooth variables over algebraically closed characteristic zero gives higher-dimensional normal NONISOLATED targets with smooth source at a closed point, hence CE by n5. More generally the transfer uses ANY actually provided finite dominating cover with proved source CE; no universal good-cover theorem is assumed.
