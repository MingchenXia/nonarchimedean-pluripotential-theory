Determinant kernels and positive fibre maxima

Let R->B be reduced finite FLAT of rank d>0. Over the local target B is a finite free module. Choose finite R-MODULE generators g_j of an ideal J (possible because B is finite over R). Let C_i(J) be the coefficient ideal of the degree-i characteristic coefficient of multiplication by sum T_j g_j, and N(J)=C_d(J). Changing basis conjugates the multiplication matrix; changing R-module generators gives R-linear substitutions in both directions. Hence coefficient ideals are independent. Determinants are top-exterior-power scalars and commute with arbitrary base change. The actual complete 0BD2 CURRENT 31.18.6 proves the matrix/conjugation and polynomial-base-change formulation; the exterior-power interpretation is the same elementary determinant. If J contains a^r B, redundant generators x in a^r give pure norm coefficients x^d, so N(J) contains a boundary power and is unit-or-primary.

Fix ANY base semivaluation with support P. First pass to K=Frac(R/P). The d-dimensional algebra B tensor K can be nonreduced. Over a finite splitting extension it decomposes into Artinian local factors of lengths l_j with sum l_j=d. On a composition series, multiplication by an element acts by its residue scalar h_j on every quotient; therefore its characteristic polynomial is product(t-h_j)^l_j. The l_j include inseparability and nilpotence, even over a reduced source. Lift the quotient valuation to a finite integral splitting algebra. Gauss coefficient minimum is multiplicative: divide a polynomial by a least-valued coefficient and reduce to a nonzero polynomial over the valuation residue field; products stay nonzero. Polynomials vanishing modulo support have infinite minimum. Applied to the norm family this gives
  v N(J)=sum_j l_j w_j(J).
Boundary-power ideals make these values finite.

The rational ideal-difference span separates ALL support points: (g)+a^N gives min(w(g),N), and centre-separating ideals handle the semilocal pieces. It contains constants and is a lattice, since

Its real uniform closure is all continuous functions by the compact lattice approximation argument in G331. Thus the mass-d measure sum l_j delta_wj is independent of all splitting/lifting choices: its integrals on this dense span are fixed. Any source fibre point can itself be extended to a splitting field of its support quotient and then the same Artinian factorization. It occurs with positive length. Choice independence therefore proves FULL fibre support, at most d atoms, and no omitted supported lift. The coefficient identity proves continuity on ideal tests; density and the fixed mass bound prove it on every continuous test. The trace tau H is positive, continuous-valued, and sends -wJ/q to -vN(J)/q, hence preserves C. The FULL fibre maximum also preserves these classes. Put m=min_{rho w=v} w(J). Then
  m=min_{1<=i<=d} v C_i(J)/i.
Every root residue has value at least m, so all degree-i coefficients have value at least i m. Choose a generator attaining m at a least-valued fibre point. Its monic characteristic equation cannot have its leading term uniquely least-valued, so some specialized coefficient has value <=i m. Specialization is an R-linear combination of family coefficients, giving the other inequality. The C_i may NOT be primary. Let L=sup_X v C_d(J)/d, finite, and choose an integer N>dL. Replacing EVERY C_i by C_i+a^N changes its value to min(vC_i,N). Since N/i>L>=m, it preserves the displayed minimum EXACTLY. Therefore MAX_fibre(-wJ/q) is a maximum of genuine unit-or-primary rational profiles and belongs to C. The maximum operator is 1-Lipschitz, hence preserves uniform C closure.
