All-field two-plane conductor counterexample with exact primary masks

Over ANY field k let R=k[x,y,z]_(x,y,z)/(xy), a=m, A1=k[x,z]_(x,z), A2=k[y,z]_(y,z), S=A1 times A2. R=A1 fibre-product over T=k[z]_(z) with A2; its conductor is (x,y), R/c=T and S/c=T times T. Each plane is normal. The normalization quotient identifies only w1 killing x and w2 killing y, both giving z value one; all other points remain separate. Let v0 be their common restriction. Thus the example retains nonminimal supports, schemes, and the conductor-infinite stratum.

Take phi=-1-1/v(y), with 1/infinity=0. It is finite continuous. On the first plane it is -1. On the second it is <=-2 at the ordinary m-order o2, with value -2 there. Every negative primary profile u satisfies u(w)<=u(o_i), since w(m)=1 implies w(J)>=ord_m J. This inequality survives uniform and actual decreasing limits. Therefore every second-plane D minorant is <=-2 everywhere. Upstairs the constant tuple (-1,-2) is a C minorant and equals the selected envelope.

For N>=2 put alpha=1/(N-1). On the first plane the ACTUAL PRIMARY mask
 h_N=-(1-alpha)-alpha w((x,z^N))
is <=-1, equals -2 at w1, and has h_N->-1 at each fixed off-shared point. At w1, w(z)=1 and w(x)=infinity, so the mask has value -(1-alpha)-alpha N=-2 exactly. The tuple (h_N,-2) is full-fibre constant, hence descends into C_R by G331, and is <=phi. Consequently the raw base envelope is -1 at all first-plane off-shared points and -2 at all second-plane points, including v0.

Monomial valuations w_M(x)=M,w_M(z)=1 converge on EVERY polynomial and local unit to w1: for large M the least x-degree decides whether the value stabilizes or tends to infinity. The analogous sequence on the other plane converges to w2. The first sequence raises the base USC value at v0 to -1. At second-plane off-shared points a conductor-finite neighbourhood excludes the first branch, so the regularized value remains -2. Thus
 E_R=-1 on the entire first plane INCLUDING v0; E_R=-2 on second-plane off-shared points.
Its pullback is not D: at w2 it is -1 while at o2 it is -2, violating the order inequality. The constrained raw upstairs regularizes to the continuous C tuple (-1,-2), which is NOT fibre constant. At the second shared lift E_R rho=-1>E_S=-2, a reverse-direction gap of exactly ONE. These computations prove failure of positive selected envelopes and failure of universal pointwise pullback<=source-envelope without openness.

The same two sequences show that a continuous full-support generic degree-one trace cannot exist on all continuous tests: for the clopen component test its integral is 1 along the first singleton fibres and 0 along the second singleton fibres, but both approach v0. No assigned value at v0 can make it continuous. Normalization self-base-change retains vertical components:
  (S tensor_R S)_red=A1 times T times T times A2.
The off-diagonal T components map into the shared line. Discarding them would change the full conductor fibres.
