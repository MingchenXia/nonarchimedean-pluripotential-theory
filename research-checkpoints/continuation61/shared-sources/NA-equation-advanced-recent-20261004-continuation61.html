The measure vanishes on the toric boundary and is supported on the tropical skeleton. Our main result explicitly describes their local structure in terms of the tropical germ of the subvariety and the tropical Green function of the metric. More precisely, the tropicalized Monge-Ampère measure is the corresponding polyhedral Monge-Ampère measure, which extends the tropical intersection product, while the original measure is recovered locally by tropical pullback from explicit tropical and convex data. This provides a toric counterpart to analogous results for subvarieties of abelian varieties, with new local phenomena arising from tropicalization. △ Less
