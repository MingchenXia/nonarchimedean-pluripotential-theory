                       4. Twisted energy and differentiability
   As in §3, we assume that the orthogonality and submean properties hold, and recall that
this is satisfied when X is a compact connected Kähler manifold or any irreducible projective
Berkovich space. We fix ω ∈ Z+ with [ω] ∈ Pos(X). In this section we introduce and study
the twisted energy of a measure, and show that it computes certain directional derivatives
of the energy.
