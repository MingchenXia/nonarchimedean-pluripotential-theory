# General big classes: model weighted limits and I-Cauchy maximizing sequences

This unreviewed candidate transfers finite nef weighted estimates to genuine envelopes of continuous MODEL obstacles. It proves exact weighted convergence in the moving-system approximation for this domain, uniform strict energy concavity and four-point Holder estimates there, and divisorial/energy Cauchy behavior of model maximizing sequences. It does not identify the energy of their weak endpoint, prove metric completeness or attain the arbitrary-target supremum. Original P16.5 remains open.

## 1. Fixed model endpoints have section approximants continuous near each divisor

    phi_m,D(z) increases to u_D(z).                    (1.1)

This includes the generic point. Each phi_m,D is finite continuous in an open neighborhood of z, since a nonzero section has nonzero finite norm at a full-support valuation. The envelope u_D is usc. Fix epsilon>0; choose a degree ell with phi_ell,D(z)>u_D(z)-epsilon. Continuity of phi_ell,D and upper semicontinuity of u_D give a neighborhood U of z on which, for all sufficiently divisible m>=ell,

## 2. Exact weighted moving-series limits for model tests

To state that last elementary passage precisely, choose disjoint neighborhoods of the finitely many vertices, refine them so all the finite tests have the stated oscillation bounds, and choose continuous functions equal to one near each vertex and supported within its neighborhood. Bound the test error by its small oscillation on each neighborhood and its uniform bound on the complement, then let epsilon tend to zero. ## 7. A useful model regularization with finite original energy

For a fixed normalized model endpoint v=P_theta f, choose k>=-min f. Let phi_m,f be its actual weighted GF approximants and define

    w_m=max(phi_m,f,V_theta-k),
    z_m=P_theta(max(phi_m,f,-k)).

The first is genuine E1 by own145. The obstacle in the second is bounded continuous PL: the pole-valued finite GF maximum is capped by the constant branch. Since phi_m,f<=f and -k<=f,

    w_m<=z_m<=v.

Every canonical actual L-section metric q_s is a normalized genuine theta-psh function, hence q_s<=V_theta. Consequently all original flag weights of ANY continuous majorant of w_m are >=-k times the degree.     E_theta(w_m)>=e_m(psi_m,f)-k(V-V_m)->E_theta(v).

    I(z_m,v)<=(n+1)[E_theta(v)-E_theta(z_m)]->0.

It still does not replace those relative model tests by original continuous tests, establish energy completeness of the full cone, or pass a bounded Borel test through arbitrary weak measure convergence.
