# General big bundles: minimal divisorial orders and zero-energy separation

This unreviewed continuation of own143--147 proves an exact numerical formula for the canonical reference on original divisorial points and shows that normalized zero energy has only that reference. It proves uniqueness for the generic Dirac equation in the existing obstacle-operator domain, not uniqueness for arbitrary targets or an entire-E1 operator. Original P16.5 remains open.

## 1. Actual asymptotic section orders

Keep smooth integral projective X over algebraically closed trivially valued characteristic-zero k, actual big rational L, theta=c1(L), and V=vol(L)>0. For an original nontrivial rational divisorial valuation v and any actual big rational bundle B define

    sigma_v(B)=inf {v(s)/m: 0!=s in H^0(X,mB)}.           (1.1)

Degrees are positive and divisible by a denominator. The infimum is finite and nonnegative: big bundles have sections in all sufficiently divisible large degrees, and nonzero sections have finite value at a divisorial valuation. Minimum section orders are subadditive in degrees, by multiplication of nonzero sections on integral X. Fekete's elementary argument, after discarding finitely many empty degrees, gives the corresponding asymptotic minimum as the infimum. Passing to a fixed further divisible subsequence gives the same value: one can raise any particular section to powers in that subsequence. Thus sigma_v(qB)=q sigma_v(B) for positive rational q.

Actual section multiplication gives

    sigma_v(B+C)<=sigma_v(B)+sigma_v(C)

whenever the terms have sections asymptotically. A common degree can be chosen by taking powers of any two selected sections; then improve the two selected orders to their infima. For any actual ample rational A, sigma_v(A)=0: a sufficiently divisible globally generated power has a section nonvanishing at the center of v. Its pullback is a local unit there.

For a fixed ample H the function t -> sigma_v(L+tH), on the rational interval where L+tH is big, is finite and convex by these multiplication identities. A finite convex function on an open rational interval is continuous there in its unique real extension: convex interpolation between rational endpoints bounds it locally above, nonnegativity bounds it below, and the usual secant inequalities bound its slopes on compact smaller intervals. In particular sigma_v(L +/- epsilon H)->sigma_v(L) as rational epsilon decreases to zero.

## 2. Numerical independence and varying actual bundles

If B and L have the same numerical class, epsilon H+(B-L) and epsilon H-(B-L) are actual ample rational bundles. Section multiplication then sandwiches

    sigma_v(L+epsilon H)<=sigma_v(B)
                            <=sigma_v(L-epsilon H),       (2.1)

for small positive rational epsilon. Let epsilon decrease to zero. Hence sigma_v(B)=sigma_v(L). This proves numerical independence of this actual section invariant; it does not throw away Picard data in any actual tensor construction.

More generally if actual rational L_i have numerical classes tending to theta, choose rational epsilon_i>0 tending to zero such that epsilon_i H +/- (L_i-L) are ample. For sufficiently late indices all L_i and L-epsilon_i H are big. The same sandwich gives

    sigma_v(L_i)->sigma_v(L).                             (2.2)

Only openness of the ample and big numerical cones, actual ample global generation, and the elementary section identities have been used here. No nonample equation or fixed-L finite-energy regularization is assumed.

    phi_i(v)<=phi_i(v_triv)-sigma_v(L_i).

## 4. A divisorial drop forces strictly negative energy

    u(v)<V_theta(v)=-sigma_v(L).

    alpha=r_0/(m_0+r_0)>0, alpha T<=delta/4.

    v(s)/m <= (1-alpha)(sigma_v(L)+delta/4)+alpha T
