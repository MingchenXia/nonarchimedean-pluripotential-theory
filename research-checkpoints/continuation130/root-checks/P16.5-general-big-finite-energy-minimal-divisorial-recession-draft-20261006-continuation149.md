# General big finite energy forces minimal divisorial recession

This unreviewed supporting note uses own148, with the actual energy of own143--145 and its imported background retained. It gives a necessary singularity property on the original carrier, not an entire-E1 operator, full-mass characterization or inverse theorem. Original P16.5 remains open.

Let u be normalized genuine theta-psh for the same actual big rational L, and suppose E_theta(u)>-infinity. Fix an original rational divisorial valuation v. Put sigma=sigma_v(L). Then

    lim_{t -> infinity} u(tv)/t=-sigma
      =lim_{t -> infinity} V_theta(tv)/t.                 (1)

The conclusion keeps the original valuations and all numerical/Picard conventions of own148. No formal horizontal b-divisor or alternate finite-energy completion is substituted for u.

## 1. Convexity and the reference slope

Each original GF stage restricts to t -> max_j[-t v(s_j)/m+c_j], a finite convex function. The direct actual-ample embedding of own145 bounds the limiting function on every finite interval by u(v_triv)-tT_Omega(v)<=u(tv)<=u(v_triv). Therefore the original decreasing-net limit is finite and convex on this ray, with value zero at t=0. It is continuous for t>0 by finite convexity. At t=0 the same linear lower bound gives continuity.

Own148 proves V_theta(tv)=-t sigma for every positive rational t. The same finite convexity and continuity extends this identity to positive real t, which are actual analytic valuations even though only rational divisorial scales are counted in X^div. Since normalized u<=V_theta, the finite convex function

    h(t)=u(tv)+t sigma

is nonpositive and has h(0)=0. Convexity implies h(t)/t is nondecreasing for t>0. Its limit exists and is at most zero. It remains to exclude a strictly negative limit.

## 2. A negative excess recession gives unbounded energy loss

Suppose that limit is negative. Choose a positive rational epsilon smaller than its absolute value. Monotonicity of h(t)/t implies

    u(tv)<=-t sigma-epsilon t

for every t>0. Choose one actual nonzero section s_0 of m_0 L with v(s_0)/m_0<=sigma+epsilon/16, and a finite linear-growth bound T for sections of this actual L as in own148. By taking a high power of s_0, arrange with a fixed divisible r_0>0 that

    alpha=r_0/(m_0+r_0)>0, alpha T<=epsilon/16.

These choices are independent of t. For each positive rational t, the cofinal model-majorant construction gives f_t>=u, f_t<=0, and

    f_t(tv)<=-t sigma-(epsilon/2)t.

In degree m=q(m_0+r_0), the same injective section subspace s_0^q H^0(X,q r_0 L) has all its nonzero section orders at v bounded by m(sigma+epsilon/8). Evaluation at the original point tv consequently gives

    W_m(f_t;s)<=m f_t(tv)+t v(s)
                <=-(3 epsilon/8)tm.

This subspace is disjoint from the high-weight flag {W_m>=-(epsilon/4)tm}. All other weights are <=0, and its dimension asymptotically has proportion alpha^n of the full volume V. Thus the actual section-energy limit gives

    E_theta(u)<=E_theta^obs(f_t)<=-(epsilon/4)t V alpha^n.

Let rational t tend to infinity. This forces E_theta(u)=-infinity, a contradiction. Hence the excess recession limit is zero and (1) follows.

## 3. Exact implication and remaining gates

Every original relative-E1 function has the minimal reference's asymptotic slope on every original divisorial scaling ray. Any genuine normalized function with a strictly more negative recession on even one such ray has energy -infinity. Conversely this condition alone has not been proved sufficient for finite energy; sublinear relative singularities can still have infinite energy.

The estimate concerns actual section weights, their positive-dimensional subspaces and the already defined scalar energy. It does not assign a measure to u, identify weighted limits, prove that singular horizontal data can be subtracted globally, or establish comparison and uniqueness for general targets. Those obligations remain. Frozen native124--127 inputs and the existing P16.4 editor have not been changed.
