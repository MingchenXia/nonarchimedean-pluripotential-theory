## Smooth upper-growth weak affine input

Let Y be smooth affine and let u be weak in the corrected sense: a proper
birational algebraic witness pi:Z0->Y makes pi*u locally a decreasing limit of
corrected rational contact functions. Suppose u<=b+C for actual AFS b on Y.
Properness gives usc of u. Resolve the witness, retaining the pullback local
class, and construct a smooth projective compactification X of Y with an
effective ample boundary divisor D having support X\Y. The intended standard
algebraic construction is: projective closure with ample boundary, resolution
which is an isomorphism on smooth Y, and an effective boundary exceptional
correction making the pulled boundary ample (Wu3.32 is diagnostic; supply the
precise blowup divisor calculation). Choose a sufficiently high very ample
B=O(rD), a boundary section s, and a rational FS reference metric h on B.
On Y let g=-log||s||_h. Add a rational constant so g>=1. Its section ratios
include affine generators after taking a complete very ample system; pole
clearing gives a rational A>0 and rational constant with u<=Ag. This bound
must be uniform. It is also proper growth: g tends uniformly to +infinity
at the boundary, since h is continuous and s vanishes exactly there.

For the boundary calculation, for a blowup centered on the boundary take
I_Z O=O(-E). Then E is effective and -E is relatively ample, contrary to
the two signs printed in Wu LF1193--1196. Since the center is contained in
Supp(D), some O(-mD) lies in I_Z; pulling back gives m pi*D-E effective.
For m sufficiently large it is also ample because -E is relatively ample
and D ample. Its support stays within pi^-1 Supp(D). Choose m still larger
than the vanishing order ratios along the finitely many components so that
every such component has positive coefficient, retaining exactly that
support. Repeat through the finite sequence of boundary blowups. Resolution
centers must be kept outside the already smooth Y.

Compactify the witness graph and dominate by a smooth projective Z, obtaining
a proper birational map bar_pi:Z->X which still dominates Z0 over Y. No
quasiprojectivity of an arbitrary given witness may be silently assumed: use
a projective domination/Chow lemma and resolution. Pullback preserves the
specified local contact class on bar_pi^-1(Y).

For rational c>1 and a rational floor M<0, put on Z

    Psi_M = max(bar_pi*u - cA bar_pi*g, M) on bar_pi^-1(Y),
    Psi_M = M on its complement.

These are potentials relative to the actual nef reference cA bar_pi*h.
On the open the metric weight is max(bar_pi*u, M+cA bar_pi*g), hence is locally
in the corrected decreasing class. The upper bound

    bar_pi*u - cA bar_pi*g <= -(c-1)A bar_pi*g

gives a whole constant collar for each fixed M at every boundary point, so
the extension belongs locally to that same nef class on all Z. The floor
also bounds the potential globally. G0's smooth nef local/global theorem now
makes each Psi_M genuine global PSH on Z. Let M decrease to minus infinity;
the limit Psi is genuine global PSH by decreasing closure, nontrivial because
u is generically finite. Its boundary value is minus infinity. Do not omit
the actual FS reference potential: if h=h0+p in additive weight notation,
the BJ potential relative to the canonical reference is Psi+cA bar_pi*p.

Now apply actual BJ Lemma5.13 LF2859--2873 to bar_pi with target class cA c1(B).
The target is smooth, and approaching larger ample classes have known smooth
EP. This meets the target-side antecedent. It is not an application to the
singular original target. The genuine descended metric has on Y exactly

Use actual Corollary12.18(iii) on X to obtain decreasing rational FS metrics

## Smooth principal-open removal for an already regularizable input

Let Y be smooth affine, U=Y_f, v an actual decreasing AFS limit on U, and
v<=b+C on U for an actual AFS exhaustion b on Y. This is exactly the
antecedent of the controlled damping construction of round68; it is not a
weak-input identification. For each rational c>0 that construction gives

    v_c = v+c log|f| on U; v_c=-infinity on Z(f),

as an actual AFS decreasing limit on Y. Reconstruct its uniform control and
the moving-collar monotonicity; do not reuse a rejected proof. Choose c_j
decreasing to zero and one actual rational AFS h which bounds every v_cj
above, using c_j log|f| <= max(c_1 log|f|,0) and the polynomial degree bound.
The functions v_cj are not monotone globally when log|f| changes sign.

On a smooth ample-boundary compactification X of Y as above, dominate h by
Ag with g>=1. The already-regularizable globalization construction gives
genuine potentials

    Phi_j=v_cj-2Ag on Y; Phi_j=-infinity on X\Y,

in the one fixed class 2A c1(B), with the same actual reference correction
2Ap, and a common global upper bound. Known smooth EP gives

    H_m=(sup_{j>=m} Phi_j)^* in genuine PSH;

H_m decreases, so its limit H is genuine PSH provided it is nontrivial.
On U, where a=log|f| is continuous finite, the unregularized tail supremum is
v-2Ag plus sup_{j>=m} c_j a, a continuous function of a. For a>=0 the latter
is c_m a, and for a<0 it is 0. Thus it is usc on U, so H_m there equals that
tail supremum. Consequently H=v-2Ag on U. This proves nontriviality and
avoids assuming globally monotone damped functions or interchanging usc and
a pointwise limit without justification.

Restrict H+2Ag to Y and apply the fixed-class global FS sequence and exact
generator conversion. It extends v and is AFS-regularizable. Its uniqueness
is a divisorial-determination assertion. To match an existing weak u on Y,
pull the extension to a resolved proper witness. The two local functions
agree on all divisorial points, whose algebraic support is the generic point
and which therefore lie in the preimage of U. On a compact neighborhood
replace both by max(function,-M), use the common bounded collar and genuine
divisorial order in both directions, and then let M tend to infinity.
This supplies equality at exceptional and Z(f) points; plain usc or ordinary
density of U alone would not do so.
