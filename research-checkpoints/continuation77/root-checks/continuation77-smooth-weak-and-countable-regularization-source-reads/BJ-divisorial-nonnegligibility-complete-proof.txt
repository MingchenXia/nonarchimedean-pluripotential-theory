Theorem 5.6. Divisorial points are nonnegligible.

Proof. By Lemma 2.12 we may find a flag ideal a for which v is a Rees valuation, that is,
v ∈ Σa . Pick L ∈ Pic(X)Q ample such that c1 (L) ≥ θ. Then PSH(θ) ⊂ PSH(L), so we may
