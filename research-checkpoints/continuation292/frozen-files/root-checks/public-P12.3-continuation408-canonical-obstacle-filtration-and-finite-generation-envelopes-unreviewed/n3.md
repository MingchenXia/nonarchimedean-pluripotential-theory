# n3. Uniform criterion, exact transports, decreasing obstacles, and a necessary limitation

Support: entire qualified G331 and earlier own n1,n2. The geometric classes and all qualifications remain those of G331.

For every finite continuous obstacle f, n2 gives an unconditional canonical pointwise limit p(f)=lim h_m, not yet an attained positive envelope. Its continuity is equivalent to uniform convergence of h_m, and equivalent to uniform convergence of H_N=max_{r<=N}h_r. Indeed, if p is continuous, the increasing continuous H_N converge to it; their strict error-test open sets cover compact X, so one N works uniformly. Given epsilon and such N, n2's residue estimate bounds p-h_m by epsilon+NM_1/m. Conversely uniform convergence puts p in the uniformly closed C by G331, so p is continuous and the greatest geometric envelope. This criterion states the missing regularity plainly; it does not prove it for arbitrary f.

Raw envelopes obey a one-sided obstacle shift estimate without any regularity hypothesis. If f<=g+epsilon, every u<=f in D has u-epsilon<=g in D, because a negative constant is an actual primary profile. Therefore p(f)<=p(g)+epsilon. If ||f-g||<=epsilon, then ||p(f)-p(g)||<=epsilon, since both raw envelopes are finite. This transfers any proved uniform approximation by obstacles having continuous geometric envelopes: their envelopes are uniformly Cauchy and converge in C to p(f). It does not establish density of finitely generated obstacles.

There is also an actual decreasing construction that permits infinitely determined and unbounded obstacles. Let f_i be a nonempty decreasing directed family of continuous obstacles, each satisfying the finite-generation condition of n2, and set f=inf_i f_i. Their geometric envelopes H_i lie in C and decrease by monotonicity. The ACTUAL decreasing C-net has infimum H in D by G331's definition; no net flattening is used. Since H<=f and every D competitor u<=f satisfies u<=H_i at every index, H is the greatest geometric envelope of f. It is upper semicontinuous and nonpositive, with bottom and partial poles retained. The assertion is conditional on the individual finite-generation hypotheses and the specified decreasing family; it does not approximate an arbitrary obstacle by such a family. If f_i converge uniformly to a finite continuous f, the shift estimate additionally gives uniform convergence of H_i and a continuous C envelope.

Primary changes preserve the canonical construction exactly, not just after comparison. For another primary b, d(v)=v(b)>0 is bounded continuous on X_a, T(v)=v/d(v), and f_b(Tv)=f_a(v)/d(v). Then

\[
(Tv)(g)+m f_b(Tv)=(v(g)+m f_a(v))/d(v).
\]

Hence A_m(f_a)=A_m(f_b) as ideals and their graded algebras are identical. The raw envelopes, the h_m, and any constructed H transport by division by d. Substituting twice gives the exact three-gauge cocycle. The uniform error in n2 becomes at most D_0 M_1/(m min d); equivalently use the value norm of A_1 in the new gauge. No gauge-dependent redefinition of positivity is made.

For finite normalization S of R and a downstairs obstacle f, define the source ideals using f composed with restriction on the entire semilocal normalization boundary. Boundary surjectivity and equality of restricted values give the exact identity A_m^R=A_m^S intersect R. At finite conductor value the fibre is singleton; at infinite conductor value it is the full quotient relation through S/c -> R/c. Both are in this identity. It does not imply finite generation of one algebra from that of the other by mere contraction.

If geometric source envelopes exist and their tuple is constant on EVERY normalization fibre, G331's positive D descent gives a downstairs h. It is below f by surjectivity. Pullback of any downstairs competitor is dominated by the source envelopes, so u<=h. Thus h is the downstairs envelope. This is the exact conditional normalization rule. Neither source positivity nor full-fibre compatibility is inferred just from the fact that the obstacle descends. For a decreasing family whose source envelopes are each fibre-compatible, the infimum remains fibre-compatible and the same argument applies to the actual decreasing limits.

On an excellent reduced curve the normalization is a finite set of DVR local centres, and a regular conductor element has finite order at every centre. Thus the boundary fibres are singleton. A DVR has one ray, and the closed point is an actual effective Cartier divisor with no contracted curves to test. All finite nonpositive branch coordinates are independently geometric; semilocal gluing and G331 positive descent give C equal to the full finite nonpositive orthant. For an extended vector u, the vectors max(u,-j) decrease in C to u, so D is the full extended nonpositive orthant. Its geometric obstacle envelope is exactly min(f,0), matching the ideal envelope through the independent geometric comparison. Irrational coordinates cause no obstruction.

For n1's explicit conifold obstacles, n2's converse computes the entire canonical filtration, not just its limit: A_m(f_delta)=integral-closure(J^m), independently of delta>=0. Its algebra is the finite normalized Rees algebra. Integral dependence makes v(A_1)=v(J) on every support; in the m-gauge its norm is four, since J contains powers with exponents at most four of every maximal generator, and the singular-supported z-order realizes four. Thus any homogeneous generator degree bound D_0 for that particular normalized Rees algebra yields the all-support error 4D_0/m. No numerical generator-degree bound for this ring was computed in the finite arithmetic diagnostic.

For n1's mixed-characteristic obstacles there is an even sharper exact computation. The order valuation nu has F_delta(nu)=-1, so g in A_m must have ord_n(g)>=m and hence belong to n^m. Conversely membership in n^m gives v(g)>=m on every support, and F_delta>=-1 makes the defining obstacle inequality hold. Therefore A_m(F_delta)=n^m at EVERY degree, independently of delta>=0, and h_m=-1 exactly with zero error. These are actual fixed-obstacle canonical-algebra applications on the nonisolated cancellation-sensitive examples, not computations of general arbitrary model-obstacle algebras.

In fact finite generation in n2 is NOT necessary for continuous-envelope regularity. On the smooth DVR R=k[[x]], normalized by (x), take the singleton obstacle f=-alpha for a positive irrational alpha. Its geometric envelope is -alpha. Its canonical ideals are (x^{ceil(m alpha)}). If their algebra were generated by finitely many homogeneous elements of degrees d_i, the minimum generator ratios ord_x(g_i)/d_i would be rational; n2's elementary generator argument would make the asymptotic order slope that rational number. But ceil(m alpha)/m tends to alpha, a contradiction. Hence this algebra is not finitely generated although the envelope is continuous and already in the geometric core as a real Cartier divisor. Non-finite-generation is therefore not a CE counterexample, even in dimension one. Likewise the historical failure of a universal primary correction is not an obstruction to this fixed-obstacle construction.

At the exact published smooth affine CLOSED germ over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, G331 identifies this same C/D class with BFJ's all-semivaluation class, with bottom adjoined. A greatest minorant proved here is consequently the BFJ envelope in that same class; the published domain is not enlarged. Nilpotents reduce away, and zero-dimensional local reduction has the unique empty boundary function. Literal classical/CLD radial classes keep their multibranch mixed-zero exclusion; this construction does not identify them with the valuative class. Products, energy and full finite-map operators remain separate.
