# n2. Normal excellent surfaces: actual models and all competitors

Use qualified G331 and n1. Let R be excellent normal local of dimension two, with arbitrary residue field and positive, zero or mixed characteristic. Given finitely many primary ideals including a, normalize their product blowup. This is an integral projective surface, identical on the puncture, with finitely many singular points: normality excludes codimension one and excellence makes the regular locus open. The completions of its singular local rings are normal by current Stacks 0C23. These are exactly condition (4) of current 54.14.5, 0BGJ/0BGP, including finiteness of normalization, finiteness of the singular locus and normality of each completion. Its (4)->(3) proof gives a regular projective T by normalized blowups, still identical on the puncture and principalizing the prescribed ideals. The complete immediate surface proof was checked; complete-ring, rational-singularity and gluing foundations are published dependencies, not a new higher-dimensional resolution claim.

Full Stacks 0AY8 applies to this integral birational model over normal R: Gamma(T,O)=R and the full closed fibre is geometrically connected. Write a O_T=O_T(-sum b_i E_i), with positive integral b_i and E_i the fibre curves. Regular local factoriality makes them actual Cartier divisors. The support is the entire fibre; the Cartier cut on a regular surface has pure codimension one, so no isolated points are dropped.

Set M_ij=deg_k O(E_j)|E_i, degrees taken over the actual residue field k. For i!=j the intersections are local lengths times [kappa(p):k], so M is symmetric with nonnegative off-diagonal entries. Its graph is connected. Pullback of the ample tautological bundle on Bl_a gives Mb<=0, with a strict coordinate. Indeed Bl_a has a fibre curve: otherwise its proper birational map is finite and an isomorphism over normal R, making a principal, impossible for a primary ideal in dimension two by the height theorem. A curve on T dominates such a curve and has positive pulled-back degree.

Let A=-M, t_i=b_i z_i. Direct expansion gives

t^T A t = sum_i (Ab)_i b_i z_i^2 + sum_(i<j) (-A_ij)b_i b_j(z_i-z_j)^2.

Every term is nonnegative; equality forces z constant on the connected graph and zero at a strict coordinate. Hence A is positive definite. Every principal A_J is positive definite with nonpositive off-diagonal entries. Its inverse is entrywise nonnegative: if A_J x=y>=0 and x=x_+-x_- has disjoint nonnegative parts, a nonzero negative part gives x_-^T y=x_-^T A_J x_+-x_-^T A_J x_-<0, contradiction. A^-1 1 is strictly positive, since a zero coordinate would give a nonpositive row product. It is rational. Clearing denominators gives integers d_i>0 with Md<0.

The actual exceptional Cartier divisor D0=sum d_i E_i has L=O(-D0) of positive degree on every fibre curve. Full Stacks 0B5Y applies to the whole possibly nonreduced fibre of dimension <=1, over any field, and proves ampleness there. Relative ampleness equals absolute ampleness over this affine base. Serre generation and the complete 0B5S coherence/vanishing statements yield a high power L^q with finitely many sections giving a closed immersion.

I=Gamma(T,L^q) is an actual finite ideal in Gamma(T,O)=R, proper because its sections vanish along the nonempty boundary and unit off m by localization, hence primary. Generation gives I O_T=L^q. Include the immersion sections in a generating set of I. Adding sections preserves the closed immersion: on an old section chart the extra coordinates are regular ratios, and properness closes the image. T and the actual Bl_I are integral closed subschemes of the same projective space with the identical dense punctured graph of these generators. Their affine prime ideals are exactly the functions vanishing on that graph, so T=Bl_I. The construction also applies to every regular common refinement below. It is stronger than having an abstract regular modification or a numerical ample boundary.

We next prove the surface pushdown before interpreting envelopes by ideals. On a regular common primary refinement mu:T'->T let D' be a negative effective real curve-nef core divisor and D=mu_*D'. On regular T it is actual negative Cartier. For every E_i the actual effective pullback mu^*E_i is a nonnegative combination of contracted curves, and cycle projection gives D.E_i=D'.mu^*E_i>=0. This uses the supported Cartier pairing and actual residue degrees; complete Stacks 0AYA gives divisor-cycle projection before Chow. F=D'-mu^*D is mu-exceptional. On the mu-exceptional curves F.C=D'.C>=0, so its coefficients satisfy M_J f>=0. The inverse sign just proved forces f<=0. Thus F is actually negative effective, and h_(D')<=h_D on every support.

For this evaluation, first use Frac(R/support(v)). Its generic point is on the puncture, where the model is the identity; properness supplies a centre on T. Exceptional effective equations are nonzero on this generic point and have finite nonnegative values. There is no map from Frac(R) to this quotient fraction field presumed. Let nu_i=ord_Ei/b_i. A uniform core approximation to h in C gives c_i<=0, Mc>=0 and h<=sum c_i v(E_i); finite coefficient errors are bounded by max |delta c_i|/b_i. An arbitrary D competitor under f>=-B can first be clamped to max(h,-B), which remains an admissible bounded D competitor.

For a signed rational primary-profile obstacle determined on T,

f(v)=sum b_i f(nu_i) v(E_i).
