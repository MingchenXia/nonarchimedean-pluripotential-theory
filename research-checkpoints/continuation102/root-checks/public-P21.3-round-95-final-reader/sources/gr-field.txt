2.1. General notation and conventions. In this paper, k will always denote a nonArchimedean field, that is, a field k equipped with a non-Archimedean complete absolute
value | |: k → R, which may be trivial.
The set of natural numbers N includes 0. We allow equality in a set-theoretic inclusion
S ⊂ T. For an abelian group P, let PΛ
:= P ⊗Z Λ. For a ring A, let A
× be the group of
invertible elements.
A topological space is called compact if it is quasi-compact and Hausdorff.
