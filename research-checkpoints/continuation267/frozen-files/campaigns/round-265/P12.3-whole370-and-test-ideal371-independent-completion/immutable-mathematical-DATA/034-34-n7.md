# n7. Full normalization formula and exact positive domain

Use G331 and n1, n3-n6. Let S be the finite normalization of an excellent reduced local germ R, separated into ALL marked normal centres, rho:X_S->X_R the compact surjective quotient, and c its conductor. Fibres are singleton at finite v(c); at infinite value they are exactly the restrictions over R/c subset S/c. This includes internal fibres in one centre, nonpure centres, and nonminimal supports. Positive C,D descent is exactly full-fibre constancy. No fibre is deleted because of energy degree or capacity.

For finite continuous phi write p_R for its raw envelope and p_S for the unrestricted raw envelope of phi rho. Downstairs pullback is exactly the supremum of upstairs minorants constant on every full fibre. Choose B>0 with -B<=phi. Clamp any upstairs C minorant g to [-B,0]. The ACTUAL unit-or-primary ideal K_N=c+a^N gives the downstairs C mask

    b_N(v)=-(B/N) min(v(c),N),   -B<=b_N<=0.

It equals -B at infinity AND at finite conductor values >=N; no converse involving infinity is asserted. The source C function max(g+b_N rho,-B) remains below phi rho and is full-fibre constant: every nontrivial fibre has infinite conductor and the function there is -B. It positively descends. At finite conductor value b_N tends to zero, proving p_R rho=p_S there. This finite-conductor locus is dense upstairs: the conductor contains a regular element and G331 divisorial density uses centred fraction-field divisors, where that element has finite value.

Assume explicitly that EVERY marked normal centre has CE, now PROVED for normal dimension two by n3, and in the isolated/regular closed algebraic characteristic-zero and finite-cover sectors by n4-n6. In dimension one it follows directly: a normal excellent local domain is a DVR, its boundary is one point, and the closed-point Cartier divisor with arbitrary nonnegative real multiples gives every nonpositive constant and clipping. Dimension zero is empty. Thus every excellent reduced germ of dimension at most two meets this hypothesis. Then U=P_S(phi rho) is continuous. Everywhere p_R rho<=U, with equality on the dense finite-conductor locus. Therefore (p_R rho)*=U on ALL supports: every neighbourhood meets the dense set, and continuity of U gives the reverse local-sup inequality.

For any bounded-above function F on a compact quotient, the exact topological rule is

    F*(v)=max_{rho(w)=v} (F rho)*(w).

Proof: continuity of rho gives (F rho)*(w)<=F*(rho(w)). The upper semicontinuous right-side function on a compact fibre attains its maximum. If t exceeds that maximum, choose at each fibre point a neighbourhood where F rho has supremum <t, then take finitely many. The complement of their union is compact; its image is closed and misses v. The complementary base neighbourhood has entire inverse image inside that union, giving sup F<t there. Let t decrease to the maximum. This requires neither openness of rho nor singleton generic fibres. Apply it to F=p_R to obtain the FULL formula

    E_R(phi):=p_R*(v)=max_{rho(w)=v} U(w).

It remains valid when E_R is not positive. The following are equivalent: (i) E_R belongs to D_R; (ii) p_R is continuous, belongs to C_R, and equals E_R; (iii) U is constant on EVERY full fibre; (iv) the displayed fibre maximum is continuous. N1 gives (i)<->(ii). In (ii), the continuous pullback equals U on the dense finite-conductor locus, hence everywhere, proving (iii). Positive C descent proves (iii)->(ii). If the fibre maximum is continuous, its pullback agrees with U on the same dense locus and hence everywhere; this proves (iv)->(iii), and (iii)->(iv) is compact continuous descent. Outside this domain no maximal D minorant exists: finite max with every competitor would make a maximal one dominate them all and equal the non-USC raw supremum.

Even without CE, the exact finite-continuous criterion is p_R* in D_R iff the unrestricted raw p_S is continuous AND full-fibre constant. For necessity n1 makes p_R continuous. Its continuous pullback lies below the LSC p_S and equals it on the dense locus. Approaching any point along that locus, lower semicontinuity gives p_S<=p_R rho there too. The converse is positive continuous descent. Thus unknown normal CE remains an explicit condition outside proved sectors.

E_R is uniformly nonexpansive on finite continuous obstacles, even outside its positive domain: source P is 1-Lipschitz and a fixed-fibre maximum is 1-Lipschitz. The positive domain is uniformly closed, since full-fibre constancy passes through uniform limits of U. Under a primary change, all lifts divide by the SAME positive base factor d(v)=v(b). Its full-fibre maximum commutes with that factor, giving precisely the n1 cocycle. These assertions retain all nilpotent/reduction and empty/nonpure conventions.

For a bounded-above EXTENDED USC obstacle, source envelopes supplied by CE are in D and may have poles or discontinuity. The dense-continuity step above is then unavailable. We make NO unconditional fibre-maximum formula at that scope. What always holds is the exact constrained identity: the base raw D envelope pulls back to the supremum of full-fibre-constant source D minorants. If the source greatest D minorant is fibre constant, it descends and commutes; if both greatest envelopes exist, commutation is equivalent to constancy of that source envelope. Existence of a base envelope alone is not promoted to an unrestricted USC formula. This is the precise limit needed for future theorem use.
