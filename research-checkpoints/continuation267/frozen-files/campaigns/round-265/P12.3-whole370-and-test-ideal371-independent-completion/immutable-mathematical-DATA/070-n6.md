# Finite reduced dominating covers transfer normal continuous envelopes.

Let A be excellent normal local and A->B a finite injection with B reduced and EVERY minimal component dominating A. All source closed centres above m are retained and source gauge is aB. G331 gives continuous surjective restriction rho, positive C/D pullback and full-fibre descent. Pullback on cores can be checked with the actual Rees algebra map onto the extended Rees algebra and the induced blowup morphism; a false isomorphism with a tensor-product Rees algebra is unnecessary. Actual effectivity and contracted-curve degree positivity pull back.

To prove that the full fibre MAX M H(v)=max_{rho w=v} H(w) stays positive, first take a dominating domain component with field L/K. Choose a finite normal extension M/K containing all embedding images, allowing inseparability, and let E be the finite integral closure of A in M. Put G=Aut_K(M) and q=[M:K]/|G|. G331's normal coefficient-norm construction supplies the positive trace kernel q sum_{gamma in G} delta_{gamma w}, independent of the lift w and supported on the ENTIRE restriction fibre, including nonzero supports. Thus that fibre is a finite G-orbit, not just a list of generic field valuations. Pull H to E and take the finite maximum of its G translates. It belongs to C_E, is invariant and full-fibre constant, and hence descends positively to C_A. Its value is the fibre MAX for that component. Finite MAX over all dominating minimal components gives the full B fibre MAX. The no-vertical-component hypothesis is essential to this argument.

For H=inf_alpha H_alpha in D_B, the fibres are finite and MAX commutes with this specified decreasing net. To prove this even with bottom, for a threshold above the maximum of the finite limits choose an index below it at each fibre point, then one common index by directedness. Hence M H=inf_alpha M H_alpha is ONE actual decreasing C_A-net. Thus M preserves D without an increasing completion step. Positive full-fibre trace/normalized averages give another route for finite bounded inputs; an average at bottom is not used.

If H<=f composed with rho, then M H<=f and H<=rho^*M H. Taking all source and base competitors shows p_B(f composed with rho)=rho^*p_A(f), for C or D raw envelopes of finite continuous f. This identity alone does not establish continuity. If B has CE, its continuous source envelope is full-fibre constant by this identity; G331 positive C descent puts p_A(f) in C_A. Therefore CE transfers. On these CE spaces extended USC envelopes also commute by the same D MAX argument and n5's existence. Nothing asserts that every normal germ has such a finite cover.
