## 5. What this rules out, and what remains

The following lower-degree recipe cannot supply the desired unrestricted original-carrier variational equation: retain the entire L-cone, choose the named mixed positive operator with canonical H, impose only its mass1 and its intrinsic dual-energy finiteness, and assert both continuous outer derivatives plus attainment for every such target. Sections3--4 separately disprove the target and differentiability assertions. They do not show that every alternative zero-volume theory is impossible.

A valid alternative must retain and justify additional fiber/carrier data, restrict its exact target image, alter its variation class, or construct a different genuine equation with proved compatibility. For this product example the exact mixed image is the Gauss-graph lift of ample finite-energy measures on Y, and that limited characterization follows from the actual product formula. A general nonbig class need not admit this product map or named Gauss section. Neither a general P16.5 lower-degree construction nor general reference independence has been supplied.
