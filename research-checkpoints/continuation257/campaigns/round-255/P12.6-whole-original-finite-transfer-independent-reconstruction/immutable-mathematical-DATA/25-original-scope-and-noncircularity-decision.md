# Original scope and noncircularity decision

The original P12.6 asks for geometric cone, envelope and Monge--Ampere compatibility with finite maps, normalization and conductor descent. It does not literally state unrestricted normalization-envelope commutation as its sole assertion. The augmented assignment specifically asks to test that equality. Our complete counterexample gives a negative answer to that unconditional strengthened interpretation, and constrains every correct original formulation.

It would be unjustified to call that counterexample a complete solution of the exact original solely by choosing the strengthened interpretation. We therefore retain candidate=false and do not declare P12.6 or the entire original family closed.

The positive theorems go beyond valuative/ideal history: actual Cartier cone pullback is checked on actual source primary models; general-degree products are identified with G345's independently positive geometric operator; normal and flat one-slot traces are identified with that operator; raw envelope identities are based on the positively descended geometric class and, in the flat case, a new characteristic-coefficient argument. These are fully proved scoped results.

No envelope positivity/regularity is proved by renaming an ideal completion or by an arbitrary supremum assumption. The two grants are used only at their full mandatory scopes. The normal-domain maximum proof explicitly excludes vertical components; the flat proof has a separate determinant argument. New product projection uses prime filtrations to compute an already geometric operator, and not to define it.

Unresolved original obligations are general admissible or regularized geometric envelope existence and the intended full finite-map operator rules outside the proven raw/fibre-admissible identities, together with general incompatible upstairs operator descent over nonnormal nonflat targets if requested. Exact-image extension is also not automatic. P12.3 regularity and P12.5 energy/private ACTIVE252 work supply no premises.

No accepted manuscript, catalogue, count, editor, authentication or runtime state was changed. Candidate=false is a scope verdict, not an automatic mathematical verdict on the proved drafts. Independent review remains necessary.
