# General big classes: a quantitative outer error and measure stability

This unreviewed continuation strengthens own150's qualitative derivative estimate. It proves a uniform error of order |t|^(3/2) for each fixed original model direction on an upper-bounded, energy-bounded part of the entire genuine E1 cone. It consequently gives a cubic-root bound for the corresponding continuous measure observable along ordered energy gaps. It does not prove weighted E1 integration, comparison, output dual finiteness, arbitrary-target attainment or uniqueness. All dependencies of own143--145 and own150 remain explicit, and original P16.5 remains open.

## 1. Retained domain and the fixed direction

Keep smooth integral projective X of dimension n>=1 over any algebraically closed trivially valued characteristic-zero field, the actual big rational bundle L, theta=c1(L), raw mass V=vol_X(L)>0, and d=n+1. The reference, genuine cone, relative energy and proposed original-carrier operator are exactly those of own145/150. Nothing is renormalized to probability mass.

Fix a rational model function h=f_H carried on a fixed smooth equivariant model p:Z->X times P1. Fix B>=0. We first consider rational model obstacles f_D<=0, carried on arbitrary higher common models, with E^obs(f_D)>=-B. The direction H and all auxiliary nef divisors below are pulled from the one fixed Z. The constants will depend on that fixed direction, X,L and B, but not on D or its model.

Write gamma(c)=p^*L+cF on Z. Its pullback dominates alpha_D(c)=p^*L+D+cF in pseudoeffective order. Choose an ample Cartier divisor T on Z and nef Cartier T_1,T_2 on Z with H=T_1-T_2. Also choose a>0 so aT+H and aT-H are nef. Such choices are made on the fixed Z, and remain nef after pullback. We will estimate all the necessary pairings of Q_gamma(c)=<gamma(c)^n> by a constant times c+1.

## 2. Affine growth of fixed nef pairings on the product class

For any fixed Cartier divisor A on Z, push its actual divisor to the smooth product X times P1. Numerically that pushforward has the form p_X^*A_X+a_A F. Its pullback differs from A by a real linear combination of p-exceptional divisors. For every such prime exceptional G,

    Q_gamma(c).G=0.                                      (2.1)

Indeed for rational r>=0, sections of p^*B+rG for a big product bundle B are exactly sections of B: normality and the codimension-at-least-two image imply p_*O(kG)=O for all k>=0. Their volumes agree. The volume differential at the big class p^*B therefore has zero right derivative in G. By differentiability it is zero as a linear differential; the same pairing vanishes for either sign and for every exceptional linear combination. This is the same actual-section exceptional argument used in own143, now for the product class.

On the product, actual section tensor products and the true section-volume limit give

    vol_(X times P1)(p_X^*C+bF)=d b vol_X(C)

for every big rational C and rational b>0. Continuity extends it to real classes and b>0. Differentiate at C=L,b=c in the direction p_X^*A_X+a_A F. The algebraic C1 volume theorem yields

    Q_gamma(c).A = a_A V + c n <L^(n-1)>.A_X.             (2.2)

Here the last term is the ordinary positive-product differential of the volume on X. A_X need not be nef; equation (2.2) is a linear differential identity, not an order comparison with A_X. If A is nef on Z, the left side is nonnegative. In particular, for the finite set T,T_1,T_2 there is one C_Z<infinity such that

    0<=Q_gamma(c).T,
    0<=Q_gamma(c).(T_1+T_2)<=C_Z(c+1),
    Q_gamma(c).T<=C_Z(c+1)                              (2.3)

for all c>=1. This bound uses the absolute values of the finitely many coefficients in (2.2). It is uniform under further pullback. It does not assert that the trace of a positive curve product is nef, and does not pair a curve-order difference with a nonnef divisor.

## 3. A big margin and direction interval independent of c

Own150 gives vol(alpha_D(c))>=d(cV-B). For c>=max(1,2B/V), this is at least d cV/2. While alpha_D(c)-rT remains big, the volume derivative and positive-product order paired with the nef pulled T give

    0<=d< (alpha_D(c)-rT)^n>.T
       <=d Q_gamma(c).T<=2d C_Z c.

Choose once for all

    r_* = V/[8(C_Z+1)]>0.

If the segment exited the big cone before r_*, integration of the displayed volume bound up to that endpoint would leave volume at least

    d cV/2 - 2d C_Z c r_* > 0,

contradicting continuity and zero volume on the boundary. Thus alpha_D(c)-r_*T is big for every c in this range, every allowed D and every higher model. In pseudoeffective order alpha_D(c)>=r_*T. Put lambda=a/r_*; then lambda alpha_D(c)>=+/-H.

For |t|<=t_* with lambda |t_*|<=1/2,

    (1-lambda|t|)alpha_D(c)
       <=alpha_D(c)+tH
       <=(1+lambda|t|)alpha_D(c).

Positive-product homogeneity and order, paired separately with nef T_1,T_2, show

    |[<(alpha_D(c)+tH)^n>-<alpha_D(c)^n>].H|
       <=C_h(c+1)|t|.                                  (3.1)

For example use the elementary bounds |(1+e)^n-1| and |1-(1-e)^n| <=C_n e for 0<=e<=1/2, and (2.3). Both C_h and t_*>0 are independent of c,D and the refinement. Integrating the actual volume differential gives

    |g_(D+tH)(c)-g_D(c)-t integral h dmu_D,c|
       <=C_h(c+1)t^2/2.                                (3.2)

This supplies the additional c-dependence absent from own150's qualitative estimate.

## 4. Balancing the truncation and Taylor errors

Let H_0=||h||_infinity and B'=B+2V H_0. For |r|<=min(t_*,1), own150's constant correction D'=D+rH-|r|H_0 F is nonpositive and has energy >=-B'. The coefficientwise deficient-measure estimate gives

    ||mu_(D+rH)-mu_(D+rH),c||_TV<=B'/c

whenever c>B'/V. The initial D has the sharper B/c bound. Integrating the full energy differential and using (3.1) gives

    |E(D+tH)-E(D)-t integral h dmu_D|
       <=C_h(c+1)t^2/2+|t|H_0(B+B')/c.                 (4.1)

Crucially (4.1) now holds on one fixed t interval for every sufficiently large c. For nonzero |t| sufficiently small set c=|t|^(-1/2). Real c is allowed by volume continuity and differentiability. Make the fixed interval smaller so c>max(1,2B/V,B'/V). Since |t|<=1, equation (4.1) yields

    |E(D+tH)-E(D)-t integral h dmu_D|
       <=A_(h,B)|t|^(3/2).                             (4.2)

This is uniform over all nonpositive model obstacles with energy >=-B. It is an error estimate for the outer obstacle energy, and is not an asserted mixed-product formula.

## 5. Passing to actual genuine E1 functions and ordered gaps

Own150's genuine majorant construction and energy continuity pass (4.2) to every actual genuine u<=0 with E_theta(u)>=-B, including all unbounded relative-E1 functions:

    |E_theta(P_theta(u+th))-E_theta(u)
       -t integral h dM_theta(u)|
       <=A_(h,B)|t|^(3/2).                             (5.1)

All envelopes in (5.1) are actual genuine functions by own150 Section6. Their operator is the target-independent operator constructed there. The cofinal model nets need not be sequences. Subtracting a common upper constant gives the same estimate on any upper-bounded, lower-energy-bounded family with the correspondingly shifted B.

Let u<=v be two such genuine E1 functions and Delta=E_theta(v)-E_theta(u)>=0. Monotonicity gives E(P(u+th))<=E(P(v+th)) for either sign of t. Applying (5.1) at both endpoints yields

    |integral h d[M_theta(u)-M_theta(v)]|
       <=Delta/|t|+2A_(h,B)|t|^(1/2).                  (5.2)

For Delta>0 small enough, choose |t|=Delta^(2/3) within the permitted interval. Hence

    |integral h d[M_theta(u)-M_theta(v)]|
       <=(1+2A_(h,B))Delta^(1/3).                      (5.3)

For Delta=0, let t tend to zero in (5.2). For larger Delta, the fixed bound 2V||h||_infinity supplies (5.3) after increasing its constant. Thus one finite constant works for every ordered pair in this fixed family. Uniform model density and raw mass V extend the resulting qualitative convergence to every original continuous observable, although (5.3)'s explicit rate is only claimed for the fixed model direction.

In particular, relative floors u_k=max(u,V_theta-k) are genuine, decrease pointwise to u, and have energy gaps tending to zero by own145. Equation (5.3) gives their model-observable convergence rate in terms of that actual energy gap. No total-variation rate, weighted-integral convergence, absolute-reference integrability or metric separation is inferred.

## 6. Exact remaining scope

The proof uses actual product section-volume factorization, exceptional section descent, algebraic C1 positive-product differentials, pseudoeffective order with nef pairings, and the entire genuine construction of own150. Its own new point is that the fixed product upper class has affine, rather than arbitrary, growth in c. This permits a fixed big margin and a balanced |t|^(3/2) error. The whole chain remains unreviewed. Active native124/125 and frozen126/128 packets are unchanged and have received no hot feedback.

An error estimate for fixed continuous model observables does not supply the unbounded relative observables V_theta-v. Weighted E1 integrability, finite dual energy of outputs, comparison and domination, intrinsic coercivity, all-target attainment and all-point uniqueness remain to be proved. This note closes none of those gates and changes neither the seven solved originals nor the 47 independently accepted scoped papers.
