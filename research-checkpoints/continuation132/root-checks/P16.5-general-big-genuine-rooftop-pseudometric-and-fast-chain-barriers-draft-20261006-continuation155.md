# General big genuine rooftops: an energy pseudometric and fast-chain barriers

This unreviewed note strengthens own151 by taking its flag inequality through cofinal genuine majorants at both endpoints. It proves the rooftop/max energy inequality on the entire genuine relative E1 cone, the triangle inequality for the resulting rooftop energy pseudometric, nonexpansion of a fixed rooftop map, and actual finite-energy lower barriers for fast Cauchy chains. Separation of functions and completeness are not proved. All own143--145/150 dependencies are retained; original P16.5 remains open.

## 1. Passing the flag inequality to genuine endpoints

Retain the actual big rational L and original characteristic-zero trivially valued domain of own150. Write E=E_theta, M=M_theta, V=vol(L), and V_theta for the genuine minimal reference. For genuine E1 endpoints u,v put

    R(u,v)=P_theta(min(u,v)).

Own151 proves that this is an actual genuine E1 function on the whole original carrier. Its largest-minorant definition gives R(u,v)<=u,v, symmetry, and associativity of iterated rooftops. Associativity follows because either iterated rooftop has exactly the same genuine minorants as min(u,v,w).

First assume u,v<=0. Take cofinal decreasing rational model majorants f_i>=u and g_j>=v with f_i,g_j<=0. The actual flag-dimension inequality of own151 gives

    E^obs(min(f_i,g_j))+E^obs(max(f_i,g_j))
       >=E^obs(f_i)+E^obs(g_j).                         (1.1)

The minimum envelopes decrease pointwise to R(u,v), by own151's proof. The maximum envelopes decrease pointwise to max(u,v). Indeed max(u,v) is genuine by own145 and lies below every max obstacle, whereas each envelope lies below its obstacle. Those maximum obstacles have pointwise limit max(u,v), which forces the envelope limit to be exactly that genuine function at every original point. Own145 decreasing energy continuity therefore applies to both envelope nets. Passing (1.1) through the product directed net yields

    E(R(u,v))+E(max(u,v))>=E(u)+E(v).                    (1.2)

This does not replace E(P(max(f,g))) by E(max(Pf,Pg)) for a fixed pair of obstacles. That finite-stage substitution would be unjustified. It uses the actual pointwise maximum-envelope limit of cofinal majorants of the genuine endpoints. For arbitrary endpoints, subtract a common upper constant first; the four energy shifts in (1.2) cancel. Thus (1.2) holds on the entire original genuine E1 cone.

## 2. A pseudometric on actual functions

Define

    d_R(u,v)=E(u)+E(v)-2E(R(u,v)).                      (2.1)

This is finite, symmetric and nonnegative; d_R(u,u)=0. Monotonicity gives

    d_R(u,v)>=|E(u)-E(v)|,
    d_R(u,v)=E(v)-E(u) when u<=v.                       (2.2)

To prove its triangle inequality take p=R(u,v), q=R(v,w), and r=R(u,v,w). Both p,q lie below v; their rooftop is r. Equation (1.2) gives

    E(p)+E(q)<=E(r)+E(max(p,q))
              <=E(R(u,w))+E(v).

Rearrangement is exactly

    d_R(u,w)<=d_R(u,v)+d_R(v,w).                         (2.3)

The objects here remain actual genuine functions. No completion is being declared to be the finite-energy space.

For a constant c, R(u+c,v+c)=R(u,v)+c and the common energy shifts show simultaneous translation invariance. For two different constants on the same genuine u, (2.2) gives d_R(u,u+c)=V|c|. Consequently constant differences have their correct raw-mass cost; this pseudometric is not invariant under independent constant shifts of its endpoints.

## 3. A fixed rooftop map is nonexpanding

Fix any genuine E1 a, and set p=R(u,a), q=R(v,a), s=R(u,v), r=R(u,v,a). The rooftop R(s,q) is r and max(s,q)<=v. Equation (1.2) gives

    E(q)-E(r)<=E(v)-E(s).

The same argument with p,u gives E(p)-E(r)<=E(u)-E(s). Add the two inequalities:

    d_R(R(u,a),R(v,a))<=d_R(u,v).                       (3.1)

This proof requires only the genuine lattice operations and their energy inequality. It does not assume a mixed Monge--Ampere product or an integration-by-parts identity.

## 4. Lower barriers for a fast chain

Let u_j be genuine normalized E1 functions, u_j(v_triv)=0, with

    sum_j d_R(u_j,u_(j+1))<infinity.

Their energies have a common finite lower bound by (2.2). For k>=j form the actual finite rooftop p_(j,k)=R(u_j,...,u_k). Put delta_l=d_R(u_l,u_(l+1)). Since p_(j,k)<=u_k, let s=R(u_k,u_(k+1)). The rooftop R(p_(j,k),s) is p_(j,k+1), and max(p_(j,k),s)<=u_k. Equation (1.2) gives

    E(p_(j,k))-E(p_(j,k+1))
       <=E(u_k)-E(s)<=delta_k.

Induction yields

    0<=E(u_j)-E(p_(j,k))<=sum_(l=j)^(k-1) delta_l.       (4.1)

As k increases, these rooftops decrease and have a uniform finite energy lower bound. Their generic values are bounded below by E(p_(j,k))/V and above by zero. Own150's genuine decreasing closure and own145 energy continuity give an actual genuine E1 pointwise endpoint p_j. It is the largest genuine minorant of inf_(k>=j) u_k and satisfies

    p_j<=u_k for all k>=j,
    0<=E(u_j)-E(p_j)<=sum_(l>=j) delta_l.                 (4.2)

Thus d_R(p_j,u_j) tends to zero. The p_j increase as j increases because a constraint is dropped. We do not assert that their increasing limit or usc regularization preserves their energy, nor that it is a d_R limit; proving those facts would require additional arguments.

For a d_R Cauchy sequence, a subsequence with summable successive distances exists by the ordinary numerical Cauchy condition. The displayed construction supplies the corresponding actual lower barriers for that subsequence. It does not replace arbitrary original directed nets by sequences, and does not by itself prove completeness.

## 5. Measure control, with the exact separation limitation

If d_R(u,v)=0, monotonicity implies

    E(u)=E(v)=E(R(u,v)).

The ordered zero-energy-gap consequence of own150 shows M(u)=M(v)=M(R(u,v)) against all original continuous observables. Equality of these measures has not yet been proved to imply equality of u,v modulo constants on the entire general-big cone. Accordingly d_R is presently only a pseudometric on actual functions; no separation theorem is asserted.

On an upper-bounded, energy-bounded family, own154 and the two ordered pairs R(u,v)<=u,v give, for every fixed model direction h,

    |integral h d[M(u)-M(v)]|<=C_(h,B) d_R(u,v)^(1/3).   (5.1)

The rooftop energy bound of own151 supplies the common energy bound required for its lower endpoint. More precisely, after a common shift all endpoints are nonpositive; (1.2) and E(max(u,v))<=0 give E(R(u,v))>=E(u)+E(v). Thus an endpoint cost bound B gives a rooftop cost bound 2B. Apply own154 with that bound and add the two ordered estimates. Uniform model density extends the qualitative consequence of (5.1) to every original continuous observable.

These results strengthen genuine-energy control and the stability of the proposed operator. They do not prove weighted integrability of V_theta-v against M(u), finite dual energy of outputs, comparison, domination, metric separation, completeness, coercivity or arbitrary-target variational attainment. All new mathematics is unreviewed, active and frozen native packets are unchanged, and the original/scoped completion counts are unchanged.
