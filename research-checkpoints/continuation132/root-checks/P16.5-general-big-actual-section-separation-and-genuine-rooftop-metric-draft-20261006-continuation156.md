# General big classes: actual-section separation and a genuine rooftop metric

This unreviewed continuation proves divisorial approximation of continuous-model obstacle envelopes by actual fixed-L section maxima, then uses high-weight section spaces to prove strict monotonicity of the genuine energy. Together with own155 it makes the rooftop pseudometric a metric on actual original functions and identifies its convergence with divisorial-plus-energy convergence on the normalized cone. It assigns no finite energy to its individual fixed-system GF functions: own147's moving-volume warning is retained. Weighted measure integration, comparison, output dual finiteness, completeness and arbitrary-target inverse equations remain open. Original P16.5 remains open.

## 1. Retained genuine domain and finite divisorial constraints

Keep all hypotheses of own143--150: arbitrary-cardinality algebraically closed trivially valued characteristic-zero k, smooth integral projective X, actual big rational L, theta=c1(L), and raw mass V=vol(L)>0. Write E=E_theta. All functions below are actual genuine functions on the entire original X^an. Choose the fixed actual ample Omega with Omega-theta ample from own145. Direct zero-FS tensoring places any genuine theta-psh function, without changing its values, in genuine PSH(Omega).

For every original rational divisorial point z, including the generic point, own145's exact ample section bound gives a finite T_z>=0 such that

    q_s(z)>=-T_z                                      (1.1)

for every actual nonzero section s of mL and q_s=log|s|/m. Indeed q_s is normalized GF_L and its direct fixed-Omega embedding has generic value zero. The same bound gives a(z)>=a(v_triv)-T_z for every genuine theta-psh a. For the generic point take T_z=0.

We use own146's already supplied finite-constraint proof: if f_D is carried on a smooth snc test model with central vertices x_1,...,x_N, then a genuine theta-psh a satisfies a<=f_D on the whole original carrier if and only if a(x_i)<=f_D(x_i) for all i. That proof evaluates actual section zero orders in the local equation of t and passes the finite-vertex inequality through the original defining GF net. It includes semivaluations with proper support. A finite rational divisorial set can be included in one such model by the Gauss-extension/invariant-blowup construction recorded with the primary BHJ Theorem4.6 in own146.

## 2. Actual fixed-L sections approximate an obstacle envelope at a divisor

Let f be a rational model obstacle, let p=P_theta(f), and fix an original rational divisorial z. Then, for every epsilon>0, there is an actual nonzero section s of mL and a real lambda with

    q_s+lambda<=f on all X^an,
    q_s(z)+lambda>=p(z)-epsilon.                        (2.1)

To prove this start with the original genuine decreasing GF defining net phi_i on actual rational L_i with c1(L_i)->theta and phi_i->p pointwise. Use an actual big decomposition L=A+D_0 with A ample and D_0 effective. Choose positive rational eta_i tending to zero along a suitable tail such that

    B_i=(1+eta_i)L-L_i-eta_i D_0
       =L-L_i+eta_i A

is an actual ample rational bundle. This is possible because c1(L_i)->theta and the ample cone is open; the condition on an actual bundle is numerical, so no Pic^0 identification is assumed. Tensor the actual systems defining phi_i, the actual canonical section of eta_i D_0, and a generating system giving zero FS on B_i. After division by 1+eta_i, the resulting identical function

    chi_i=(phi_i+eta_i sigma_D0)/(1+eta_i)

is GF on the actual L, with a finite section system in a divisible power of L. At every fixed divisorial point it tends to p, since sigma_D0 is finite there and the original phi_i coordinate tends to the finite coordinate of p. Only existence of approximants for a finite set is used; no new decreasing net of the chi_i is asserted.

Refine the model of f to include z. At its finitely many central vertices, p<=f. Choose i and eta_i so chi_i(x_j)<=f(x_j)+epsilon/3 at every vertex and chi_i(z)>=p(z)-epsilon/3. The genuine fixed-L function chi_i-epsilon/3 then satisfies the finite constraints and hence lies below f everywhere. Its value at z is at least p(z)-2epsilon/3. Choose an actual section attaining this finite weighted maximum at z. Each term of the maximum lies below f, which gives (2.1).

The chi_i and the selected section term need not have finite E_theta energy. They are used only to furnish actual sections satisfying a bounded obstacle filtration. No energy convergence of fixed-L GF systems, and no false assertion that their moving volumes equal vol(L), enters the proof.

## 3. A uniform positive rank of high-weight sections

Let f<=0 be a rational model obstacle with E^obs(f)>=-B for some fixed B>=0. Write W_m(f;s) for its actual intrinsic section weight, and F_f(l) for the corresponding high-weight linear subspace. All weights are <=0. Choose

    T=2B/V+1>0.

The true actual weight-sum limit and the ordinary section-volume limit give

    liminf_m (n!/m^n)dim F_f(-mT)>=V-B/T>=V/2.           (3.1)

For precision, the number of weights strictly below -mT is at most the sum of their negatives divided by mT. Floors change the normalized sum by at most n!h^0(mL)/m^(n+1), which tends to zero. Thus (3.1) follows from -E^obs(f)<=B. It holds along every sufficiently divisible actual degree subsequence. Its constant is uniform over all such f, although the degree at which any fixed f approaches its asymptotic bound need not be uniform.

## 4. Ordered divisorial gaps force positive genuine energy gaps

Let w<=v<=0 be genuine E1 functions with E(v)>=-B. Suppose at a fixed original rational divisorial z that

    v(z)-w(z)>=delta>0.

Choose once for all a positive rational alpha<1/2 with

    alpha(T_z+T)<=delta/8.                              (4.1)

For instance choose any rational alpha smaller than min(1/2,delta/[8(T_z+T+1)]). This choice depends only on B,delta,z and the fixed original data.

Take any cofinal rational model majorant f>=v with f<=0. Its energy is >=E(v)>=-B. Section2 gives an actual section s_0 of degree m_0 and a real lambda_0 such that q_s0+lambda_0<=f everywhere and

    q_s0(z)+lambda_0>=P_theta(f)(z)-delta/8
                         >=v(z)-delta/8.                (4.2)

Replace s_0 by a power, preserving its normalized metric and weight, so there is a positive divisible actual degree r_0 with r_0/(m_0+r_0)=alpha. The rational choice of alpha permits this while clearing all actual L denominators. For each sufficiently divisible q form the injective multiplication subspace

    U_q=s_0^q F_f(-q r_0 T)
        subset H^0(X,q(m_0+r_0)L).

Every nonzero element is a product with a nonzero section r in the indicated high-weight subspace. The normalized weighted product, with

    Lambda=(1-alpha)lambda_0-alpha T,

lies below f on the whole carrier: the normalized metric is the convex combination of q_s0+lambda_0<=f and q_r-T<=f. At z, (1.1), (4.1) and (4.2) give

    q_product(z)+Lambda
       >=(1-alpha)(v(z)-delta/8)+alpha(-T_z-T)
       >=v(z)-delta/4,                                 (4.3)

where v(z)<=0 was used in the last bound.

Now take model majorants h_j>=w decreasing to w and set g_j=min(f,h_j). Then w<=g_j<=f and eventually g_j(z)<=w(z)+delta/8. Put m=q(m_0+r_0). Evaluating the intrinsic weight at z and using (4.3) shows, for every nonzero element of U_q,

    W_m(f;s)>=m Lambda,
    W_m(g_j;s)<=m(Lambda-5delta/8).

Thus U_q is contained in the f high-weight flag at m Lambda and has zero intersection with the g_j flag at m(Lambda-delta/2). On the whole interval between those two levels the dimension difference of the flags is at least dim U_q: g_j<=f includes its flag inside the f flag, and U_q provides the disjoint additional subspace. Summing integer levels, allowing the vanishing floor error, and taking the true degree limits gives

    E^obs(f)-E^obs(g_j)>=delta V alpha^n/4.              (4.4)

Indeed (3.1) in the degrees q r_0 implies liminf (n!/m^n)dim U_q>=alpha^n V/2, and the level interval has length m delta/2. No finite set of degree asymptotics is required to be uniform in f; the fixed alpha and rank coefficient are uniform and the limit is taken separately for each fixed f,g_j.

The genuine envelopes P_theta(g_j) decrease pointwise to w, since w is a minorant and g_j decreases pointwise to w. Own145 energy continuity gives E^obs(g_j)->E(w). Then let the cofinal f decrease to v. The same continuity and the uniform right side of (4.4) yield

    E(v)-E(w)>=delta V alpha^n/4>0.                     (4.5)

This is a quantitative ordered energy separation theorem. It uses only actual sections, bounded-obstacle weight sums, the genuine energy limits and finite divisorial constraints. It does not assume that M(v) puts positive mass at z or that it integrates the unbounded function v-w.

## 5. All-point strict monotonicity and the rooftop metric

If two ordered genuine E1 functions differ at an original point, their actual ample embeddings cannot have identical divisorial coordinates, by the exact all-point divisorial determination in the sole allowed actual-ample G0. Their difference is already detected at a rational divisorial point or at the generic point: along each divisorial ray the genuine functions have finite convex coordinates, by their original GF nets and the ample bound, hence are continuous at every positive real parameter. Rational parameters therefore detect any strict gap on that ray. Subtracting a common upper constant allows Section4 to apply. Consequently

    w<=v and E(w)=E(v) imply w=v on all X^an.            (5.1)

Apply this to the actual genuine rooftop R(u,v) of own155. If d_R(u,v)=0, then E(R)=E(u)=E(v), so (5.1) gives R=u=v everywhere. Own155's symmetry, triangle inequality and raw constant identity now make d_R a genuine metric on actual original E1 functions. This does not prove that equal operator measures imply equal functions.

## 6. Convergence on the normalized genuine cone

Restrict to u_i(v_triv)=u(v_triv)=0. If d_R(u_i,u)->0, their energies converge by own155 (2.2) and have a common lower bound. Their rooftops lie below both endpoints; the two nonnegative ordered energy gaps sum to d_R(u_i,u). If a fixed rational divisorial coordinate difference stayed >=delta on a subnet, its gap from at least one endpoint to that rooftop would also be >=delta. The uniform lower bound (4.5), applied with the common endpoint energy bound, would contradict d_R->0. Thus u_i(z)->u(z) at every rational divisorial z and at the generic point.

This also gives convergence at every real divisorial parameter. On a fixed positive compact interval of a divisorial ray, the normalized fixed-Omega bound gives uniform finite upper and lower bounds for the convex ray-coordinate functions. Elementary secant inequalities then give a uniform Lipschitz bound on any smaller interior interval. Approximation of any real parameter by rational parameters and the convergence just proved yield convergence at that real parameter. Hence d_R convergence implies original divisorial convergence plus energy convergence.

Conversely suppose normalized u_i->u in original divisorial topology and E(u_i)->E(u)>-infinity. The genuine maxima a_i=max(u_i,u) converge divisorially to u. Weak energy upper semicontinuity of own145, together with E(a_i)>=E(u), implies E(a_i)->E(u). The genuine rooftop/max inequality of own155 gives

    0<=d_R(u_i,u)<=2E(a_i)-E(u_i)-E(u)->0.              (6.1)

Both arguments apply to arbitrary directed nets; no countability is imposed on the original cone or field. Thus on the normalized genuine E1 cone the metric topology is exactly divisorial-plus-energy convergence. Own155's model-observable estimate consequently gives continuity of the proposed original-carrier M in this topology.

This is not a completeness theorem. A metric Cauchy sequence has actual weak cluster functions by compactness and a numerical limit of its energies, but weak energy upper semicontinuity alone permits an upward energy jump at that cluster function. Own155's fast-chain lower barriers do not yet rule it out. The full weighted measure estimates, comparison, output dual condition, arbitrary-target coercivity/attainment and all-point equation uniqueness are still required.
