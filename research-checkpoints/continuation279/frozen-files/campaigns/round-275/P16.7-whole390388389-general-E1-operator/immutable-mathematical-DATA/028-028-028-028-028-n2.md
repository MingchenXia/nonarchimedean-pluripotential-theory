Use the analytic quotient, tropical map and Gauss section proved in n1. Put q(x)=(x_1²+x_2²)/2 and θ=d'd''q. A deck translation changes q by n·x+|n|²/2, a smooth real affine logarithmic function with zero Hessian. Hence θ is a globally defined closed positive ordinary CLD (1,1)-form. It is a real differential datum; no ample algebraic/formal line bundle, strict positivity in every analytic direction, or equivalence with classical, weak-smooth or BFJ model psh is asserted.

Normalization. With positive interleaved volume dV=d'x_1∧d''x_1∧d'x_2∧d''x_2, exterior algebra gives (d'd''F)²=2 det(D²F)dV. The identity coordinate moment has analytic degree one and saturated tangent lattice Z². Its canonical calibration is therefore one. Bounded domain and genuine operator. Define C_θ to consist of real continuous u on X such that q+u is locally a uniform limit of ordinary CLD-smooth functions with positive Hessian. This is intrinsic relative to θ because changes of lift have smooth zero Hessian. It is not declared to be the full analytic psh cone or E¹. Let α_u=θ+d'd''[u]. Locally define its mixed measure with α_v by d'd''((q+u)d'd''(q+v)). Positive currents have order zero, so continuous multiplication is legitimate. If positive T_j→T and continuous f_j→f locally uniformly, choose a smooth g uniformly close to f on a compact test neighborhood and write f_jT_j−fT=(f_j−g)T_j+g(T_j−T)+(g−f)T. Positive-test domination bounds the first and last terms by the approximation error, while the middle tends to zero. This proves f_jT_j→fT. Recursion then proves positivity, closedness and uniform continuity of Hessian products; simultaneous smooth approximation proves symmetry. Smooth zero-Hessian changes of local potentials leave the products unchanged, so they glue globally.

The global identity is α_u∧α_v=θ∧α_v+d'd''(uα_v), and θ∧α_v=θ²+d'd''(vθ). Pairing exact currents with the smooth constant one gives zero. Thus every mixed measure has mass two. Positive top currents are Radon measures, and smooth-test density turns current convergence into weak measure convergence. Decreasing continuity follows by Dini when the limit is continuous and belongs to C_θ; no continuity toward unbounded limits is proved.

Comparison proof. Smooth convex regularized maxima show that C_θ is stable under maximum. Set w=max(u,v−ε). Locality and total mass yield MA_θ(v){u<v−ε}≤2−MA_θ(u){u>v−ε}=MA_θ(u){u≤v−ε}. Let ε decrease to zero and use continuity from below on both sets. Consequently MA_θ(v){u<v}≤MA_θ(u){u<v} for all u,v∈C_θ.
