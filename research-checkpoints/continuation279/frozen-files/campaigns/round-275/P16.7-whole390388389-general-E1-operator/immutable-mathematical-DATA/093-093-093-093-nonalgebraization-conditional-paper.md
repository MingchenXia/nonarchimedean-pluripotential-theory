Here is a complete proof of the remaining implication F_L⇒Γ(X_L,M)=L and exclusion of every separated finite-type algebraic-space algebraization. Write a=-log|s| and P_m=P_m,L. Pull a line bundle on X_L to T_L and choose its global frame. A section becomes θ(λz)=c_λ z^(h_λ)θ(z). The multipliers are units, hence monomials. The section is a scalar monomial and has no zeros. It trivializes its line bundle downstairs; in particular X_L has no nonzero effective analytic Cartier divisor.

For arbitrary nonzero meromorphic F on X_L, its pullback to the smooth torus has an effective Cartier pole divisor P. Smooth affinoid rings are regular; locally their height-one divisors are Cartier. The pair has no common height-one divisor: at a pole the adjusted numerator has order zero, and elsewhere θ0 has order zero. Invariance of F implies P is invariant, so θ0(λz)/θ0(z) has divisor zero; normality of it and its inverse makes it an analytic unit. Both θi have that common multiplier. The preceding recurrence forces them to be scalar multiples of the same monomial. Hence F∈L. This treats every meromorphic function, not selected characters.
