Definition 7.1. Let V be a set of non-Archimedean metrics on L that is closed under trans-
lation and scaling. Then a functional F : V → R is homogeneous if F (φd ) = dF (φ) for φ ∈ V
and d ∈ N∗ , and translation invariant if F (φ + c) = F (φ) for φ ∈ V and c ∈ Q.
   For example, Lemma 6.9 shows that when L is ample, the Lp -norm is a homogeneous and
translation invariant functional on HNA (L).
