## A direct local extension, without Lemma3.25

Let Y be smooth affine over the retained trivially valued characteristic-zero
field. Take its smooth projective compactification X, an ample semipositive
model form theta and a section s with Y=X\Z(s). Let g=-log|s|_theta be the
genuine affine FS potential of theta on Y. Suppose u is locally psh on Y
and satisfies the upper logarithmic bound u<=A g+B, with A>0 rational
chosen large enough. Choose rational c>1 and put theta'=c A theta.

Set phi=u-c A g on Y and phi=-infinity on X\Y. For rational C define

    phi_C=max(u-c A g,C) on Y,
    phi_C=C on X\Y.

The upper bound gives phi<=B-(c-1)A g. Since |s|_theta is continuous and
vanishes exactly on the boundary divisor, g tends to +infinity near that
boundary. Thus phi_C is identically C on a neighborhood of X\Y. It has
no unresolved boundary extension or regularizability assumption.

On Y, c A g is an actual model potential of theta'. By the local definition,

    phi_C+c A g=max(u,C+c A g)

is locally psh. Finite maxima of local psh functions are local psh: this is
the elementary decreasing-PL-approximation maximum argument in Wu3.13.
Near the boundary, phi_C is constant and theta' is semipositive, so phi_C
is locally theta'-psh there too. Hence phi_C belongs to LPSH_theta'(X).

The smooth projective X has the envelope property by the independently
established smooth theorem. The local-to-global identification on this
smooth X (Wu4.17, with its smooth-use comparison and domination antecedents)
then gives phi_C in the genuine global PSH_theta'. Let C decrease to
-infinity. The global decreasing-limit theorem gives phi in PSH_theta'
when phi is generically finite. The identically -infinity endpoint can be
handled separately by an explicit decreasing FS sequence.

Finally the genuine global FS regularization and the exact affine/model
correspondence produce a decreasing affine FS sequence for
u=phi+c A g. This is the conclusion of smooth affine regularization;
regularizability was not assumed in the extension step.
