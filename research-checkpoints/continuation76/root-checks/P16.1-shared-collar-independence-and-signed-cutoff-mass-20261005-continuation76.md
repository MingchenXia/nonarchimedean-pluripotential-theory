# Shared collars, weighted signed cutoffs, and the bounded mass step

This is an unreviewed reconstruction proposal for the next P16.1 continuation.
It is not an accepted theorem or an original solution. The active round68 input
is immutable and this note has not been sent as hot feedback. Work first in the
normal integral projective, rational ample-class domain. Reduced/nonnormal
spaces, arbitrary real classes and the singular envelope theorem remain further
obligations.

## 1. Exact hypotheses of the proposed bounded-local construction

Let X be normal integral projective over the original algebraically closed
trivially valued characteristic-zero field. The following antecedents must be
proved by the collar/PL bridge; they are not consequences of this note:

* A relatively compact shared chart admits nested compact neighborhoods P in Q
  with interior collars and an actual ample FS exhaustion v. Its positive
  curvature is the restriction of one fixed global ample reference theta.
* For a bounded local tuple u_0,...,u_n, there are supplied decreasing continuous
  corrected local PL approximants on a neighborhood of Q, with a common bound
  on one tail and one fixed winning boundary collar.
* With one rational A and h=A v+M+1, the continuous functions
  Psi_(j,i)=max(u_(j,i)-h,0) inside Q and zero outside are actual global
  A theta-psh metrics. Their bounded limits Psi_j are actual global E1 metrics.
  On P, dd^c u_(j,i)=A theta+dd^c Psi_(j,i), and the same identity is the
  intended meaning of the limit's local positive curvature.
* Every continuous local rational PL function extends exactly to an actual
  global BJ PL function on a neighborhood of a specified compact set. This is
  the compact formal-extension plus BE5.16/5.17 and Gauss-descent gate, not a
  conclusion obtained by the word “PL”. Global genuine mixed measures and CLD
  continuous products agree, with their actual unnormalized intersection mass.

The existence of such common data for the stated local class is load-bearing.
It must not be supplied by the bounded local operator being constructed.

## 2. Independence uses one shared collar before comparing limits

For fixed Q,h, the global pointwise limit is the explicit function

    Psi_j=max(u_j-h,0) on Q, and zero outside.

It therefore does not depend on a choice of approximating sequence. Genuine
global decreasing closure and BJ7.19 give one global mixed limit. Positivity
and finite local mass follow from its positive global measures, restricted
against continuous tests compactly supported in the interior of P.

To compare two original charts or two different approximation sequences, do not
equate two arbitrary approximant measures. Choose a smaller shared Q inside the
intersection, and a single new h,A that works for the finitely many restricted
sequences. This gives a three-step comparison:

1. Use the first original sequence in both its original collar and the new
   shared collar. For every index their continuous local products agree by
   actual CLD/model compatibility. Genuine decreasing convergence passes this
   equality against every continuous test supported in the new interior.
2. Use the second original sequence similarly to compare its original collar
   with the shared collar.
3. On the shared collar, both sequences have exactly the same explicit global
   Psi_j limit above. Their genuine global mixed limits are consequently equal.

This proves overlap and approximation independence under the antecedents. It
uses no assertion that an increasing family has decreasing continuity, no Dini
argument for a noncontinuous limit, and no unproved order inequality for MA
measures. It also works for directed nets when the antecedent supplies them.

For a supplied bounded psh weight u_0, on the shared interior write
u_0=Psi_0+h. BJ7.21 applies to Psi_0 and all the genuine E1 curvature slots;
h is fixed and continuous on the compact support. Extend its product with a
test continuously by zero. Thus the weighted local measures converge as well.
The weighted statement is needed explicitly, not just unweighted weak
convergence.

## 3. Signed PL cutoffs can be handled through genuine global products

Let rho be an actual global rational PL function supported compactly in a
chart. The BJ PL algebra represents rho as a difference of two actual
continuous psh model potentials after adding a sufficiently ample common
reference. Thus dd^c rho is the difference of two positive curvature pairs in
one common ample class. Multilinearity defines dd^c rho wedge T for the n-1
remaining bounded local slots.

Use the common collar for those slots, and use the two fixed positive global
pairs representing dd^c rho. BJ7.19 and BJ7.21 pass respectively the signed
measure and every supplied bounded local psh weight to the limit. A continuous
background local weight is handled separately as a continuous test. The sign
of the difference does not permit dropping either term; each positive term has
to be passed independently before subtraction.

For continuous local PL inputs, take their exact global PL extensions near the
support. The actual global model integration-by-parts identity (BJ (3.9)) gives

    integral rho dd^c u_1 wedge T
      = integral u_1 dd^c rho wedge T.

Both sides have compact support where the extensions agree. Genuine
continuous-product locality makes the result independent of the extensions.
Pass this identity along the common decreasing approximants using the weighted
statement above. This gives bounded-local IBP without invoking a general
decreasing CLD theorem: CLD8.7.2 supplies only locally uniform continuous
compatibility, and the noncontinuous limit is supplied by the genuine global
weighted theorem.

The support of the signed cutoff measure stays inside Supp(rho). For the
continuous products it vanishes on every open set where rho is constant, by
locality. Test on those open sets and pass the two positive measures' difference
to the limit. No support conclusion is inferred from weak convergence on a
noncompact chart without compact test supports.

## 4. The finite partition mass argument

Fix an actual continuous global model reference omega in a rational ample
class. Its local weights g_alpha are finite continuous PL functions. Let phi
be bounded and locally omega-psh, so u_alpha=phi+g_alpha is in the supplied local
class. Assume the preceding local construction and all its overlap/weighted
identities have been established. Let

    T=(omega_2+dd^c phi_2) wedge ... wedge (omega_n+dd^c phi_n).

Use the actual global rational PL partition from the continuation74 lattice
construction: rho_alpha has compact support in U_alpha and their finite sum is
exactly one. Refinement to intersections of the relevant charts makes each
signed measure below intrinsic on its support. Apply bounded-local IBP to
u_alpha, and ordinary continuous PL IBP to g_alpha, to get

    integral dd^c phi wedge T
      = sum_alpha integral rho_alpha (dd^c u_alpha-dd^c g_alpha) wedge T
      = sum_alpha integral (u_alpha-g_alpha) dd^c rho_alpha wedge T
      = integral phi dd^c(sum_alpha rho_alpha) wedge T
      = 0.

The equality of the third and fourth lines requires overlap independence for
the signed cutoff measures and weighted integrals, which Section3 addresses.
It is not merely a formal cancellation of undefined local products. Iterate
this identity one slot at a time. The reference-only global measure already has
the actual intersection mass, so the resulting mixed bounded local measure has
mass (omega_1 ... omega_n)_X. For a single rational ample omega this is
(omega^n)_X, before probability normalization.

The constants A and changes of line-bundle tensor power must be retained in
each collar. With Phi=Psi/A in class theta, multiply a raw r-factor product
by A^r. With Psi in class A theta as in Section1, those factors are already in
the curvature and no additional A^r is inserted. Merely asserting that a
global representative is a probability measure would give the wrong local mass.

## 5. Locality and the next implications

BJ7.40 supplies the strict winning-branch identity on genuine E1 metrics.
BJ7.41 proves ordinary-open locality by using max(Phi+epsilon,Phi') and
letting epsilon decrease to zero; exchanging the two metrics completes the
argument. Its mixed version is by polarization. This is independent of EP.
For the constructed local operator, use a shared collar so maxima commute with
the construction:

    max(max(u-h,0),max(v-h,0))=max(max(u,v)-h,0).

On the smaller interior both u,v beat h, so their strict winning sets agree
with those of the genuine representatives. Transfer the genuine locality
there. The finite partition mass identity and max stability then give the
bounded global comparison inequality by the epsilon argument, provided the
maxima remain in the stated local class. These hypotheses must be checked.

There is also an explicit local divisorial-order argument under the collar
antecedents. Suppose two bounded global local potentials phi,psi satisfy
phi<=psi on Xdiv. For any given point choose a shared Q,h and the same local
reference weight g. Apply the collar to phi+g and psi+g to form genuine global
Psi_phi,Psi_psi in class A theta. On divisorial points in Q the
inequality survives the maximum with zero; outside Q both functions are zero.
Thus Psi_phi<=Psi_psi on all Xdiv. Actual BJ4.22, with the usc Psi_psi as upper
function, gives this inequality on all Xan. On the smaller P both functions
are phi+g-h and psi+g-h, respectively. Therefore phi<=psi at the original point.
This proves the needed order conclusion; injectivity of divisorial evaluations
alone would not prove it.

Under the same rational ample-class antecedents, the continuation70 positive
model atom at any x in Xdiv supplies a bounded genuine b with MA(b)({x})>0.
No Dirac equation is asserted. Translate b so b<phi with a uniform margin.
For positive rational t<1 put phi_t=(1-t)phi+t b. Positive affine-combination
closure in the local class and mixed positivity give

    MA(phi_t)>=t^n MA(b), and phi_t<phi.

If phi<=psi MA(psi)-almost everywhere, comparison and equal raw mass V imply

    V=MA(psi)({phi_t<psi})
      <=MA(phi_t)({phi_t<psi})<=V.

Since x has positive MA(phi_t) mass, x belongs to {phi_t<psi}. Let rational t
decrease to zero. This proves phi(x)<=psi(x) on Xdiv, and the explicit collar
order argument gives the global inequality. Restriction of the genuine model
b to the constructed local class, positive-combination closure, positivity of
all mixed terms, and the positive-atom model construction must be verified at
this application. They are not licensed by the desired existence theorem.

Arbitrary weak affine regularization, finite flat trace regularization,
Rossi/Runge induction, singular EP, real classes and the original
nonnormal-unibranch existence theorem are not concluded here.
