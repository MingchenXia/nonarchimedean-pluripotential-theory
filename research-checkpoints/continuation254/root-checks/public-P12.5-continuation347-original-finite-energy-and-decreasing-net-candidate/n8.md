# n8. Exact smooth specialization via the primary local intersection definition
Supports: G331, B341, n1, n4, n5, n6, n7.

The primary source is Boucksom--Favre--Jonsson, *Valuations and plurisubharmonic singularities*, published RIMS44 (2008), particularly its complete Setting, Proposition1.14, the relevant2.4--2.8 units, and **Definition4.3** within the entire Section4. The source has been read at its exact hypotheses: a smooth **closed** point of an affine variety over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, dimension $n\ge2$, with maximal-ideal section. No analytic arbitrary germ, positive characteristic, singular resolution, or broader multiplier theorem is asserted. G331 already supplies full cone comparison on all semivaluations with bottom adjoined; B341 supplies bounded operator comparison. These grants are not unbounded energy citations.

Here is an explicit local smooth extension convention, selected by a published **local** primary definition rather than a global projective energy theorem. For a BFJ formal psh function $g$, let $Z(g)$ be its nef Weil divisor. Define its local intersection energy and finite-energy class by
$$E_{\mathrm{BFJ,int}}(g)=\frac1n\langle Z(g)^{[n]}\rangle,\qquad E^1_{\mathrm{BFJ,int}}=\{g:\langle Z(g)^{[n]}\rangle> -\infty\},\tag{35}$$
where the bracket is **exactly BFJ Definition4.3**: the infimum of the intersections of nef Cartier majorants of its entries. The zero anchor, sign, raw multiplicity and degree-$n$ normalization are explicit; this is a local degree-$n$ energy, not a degree-$n+1$ projective Aubin--Mabuchi formula or a probability normalization. Equation (35) specifies the canonical finite-self-intersection convention; we do not claim the source uses this $E^1$ notation or proves an independently named energy theorem.

We prove the full comparison, rather than assume that convention agrees with truncation. Let $u_i\in D$ be nonbottom BFJ functions with associated $Z_i$. For a nef Cartier $W_i$, its function $w_i$ is continuous and belongs to $C$, by BFJ1.14 and G331's exact class comparison. Moreover $W_i\ge Z_i$ coefficientwise if and only if $w_i\ge u_i$ on the entire section. One direction is immediate at divisorial points. Conversely coefficient order gives function order there; on each closed simplex both formal functions are continuous by the precise G331/BFJ comparison, and rational divisorial points are dense, so order holds at every quasi-monomial. Taking their canonical infima over all retractions, as BFJ2.8 and G331 prescribe, preserves order at every semivaluation. B341's bounded operator comparison and the actual bounded test pairing of n4 identify $F(\boldsymbol w)=-\langle\boldsymbol W\rangle$.

Definition4.3's negative intersection is therefore the supremum over these Cartier majorants, which is at most the full $C$-majorant supremum (19). To prove the other bound, fix any $C$ majorants $h_i\ge u_i$. If one is zero, its form value is zero and contributes no positive supremum. Otherwise G331's normal zero dichotomy, or BFJ2.4 in this smooth range, gives $h_i\le-c_i<0$ uniformly. For $0<\eta<1$, approximate $(1-\eta)h_i$ uniformly by a rational primary profile $r_{i,\eta}$ with error less than $\eta c_i/2$ and tending to zero as $\eta\to0$. Then
$$r_{i,\eta}\ge h_i+\eta c_i/2\ge u_i.$$
Each is a nef Cartier majorant, and $r_{i,\eta}\to h_i$ uniformly. N4's uniform form convergence implies $F(\boldsymbol h)$ is at most the Cartier-majorant supremum. Thus
$$F_\infty(u_1,\ldots,u_n)=-\langle Z_1,\ldots,Z_n\rangle\quad\text{in }[0,\infty].\tag{36}$$
This argument does not invoke an unbounded continuity or energy grant from BFJ. It compares directly with its primary local definition. Zero target entries give zero on both sides; bottom has infinite self energy and is excluded from (35). G331 says every other $D$ function is a full BFJ function, so (36) proves equality of the **entire** finite-energy spaces under convention (35), not merely smooth core or bounded agreement.

For finite-energy tuples, Holder guarantees that the mixed moment with $Z(\mathfrak m)$ is finite. BFJ4.6's moment characterization at this mass condition and (22),(36) identify our finite-energy measure with its local intersection operator. Its integrability against every $E^1$ test is independently proved by (24), not inferred from an incomplete weak-test argument. Formula (21) shows uniqueness of the bounded decreasing extension under this convention: every individually bounded decreasing approximation of a specified finite-energy function gives the same energy and mixed operator. General primary gauges are transported by n7 with its one-factor law; no unpublished arbitrary-gauge theorem is attributed to BFJ. Smooth DVR and empty cases are n7.

This is a precise smooth local specialization, with published primary intersection definition and a proved canonical extension convention. It does **not** force equality with Cegrell classes of classical psh germs or with global projective BJ finite energy. Such spaces have different input objects and reference conventions. G331's geometric cone may contain the nodal vector $(-1,0)$; the qualified classical/CLD literal logarithmic radial obstruction remains unchanged. No literal radial comparison is claimed for the energy or measure.
