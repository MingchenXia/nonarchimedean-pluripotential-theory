# n1. Domain, imports, and the theorem's conventions

This is a mathematical draft, not an acceptance or a formal certificate. Only the ENTIRE qualified G331 and B341 are imported results. Their unchanged full bodies, mandatory annotations, ledgers, exact scopes and the SH17.4.7 colon qualification accompany this paper verbatim. The supplied ROOT342 and ROOT344 are unreviewed data: the new arguments below are not conditional on accepting either draft. No private active campaign was accessed.

Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be excellent local. **Throughout replace $R$ by $R_{\mathrm{red}}$, explicitly**, and use the image of each gauge there. This is a theory of its reduced semivaluation boundary, not a reconstruction of unreduced generic lengths. Put $n=\dim R$. When $n>0$, fix a proper $\mathfrak m$-primary ideal $\mathfrak a$ and the compact Hausdorff section
$$X_{\mathfrak a}=\{v:v(\mathfrak a)=1,\ 0<v(\mathfrak m)<\infty\}.$$
All prime supports strictly below $\mathfrak m$ are included. Functions are homogeneous of degree one on the centred rays. G331 supplies actual evaluation of exceptional Cartier divisors using the valued field of the support quotient, and supplies the independent geometric cone: actual negative effective finite real Cartier presentations, curve-nef on actual primary blowups. It proves its continuous uniform class $C$ equals the ideal uniform class, and its actual decreasing-net completion $D$ equals the geometric completion. We use its addition, nonnegative scaling (with $0u=0$), finite maxima, upper semicontinuity, primary-gauge cocycle, rational primary-or-unit profile density, full normalization-fibre descent, and density of the rational difference lattice of ideal gauges in $C(X_{\mathfrak a},\mathbb R)$. No arbitrary infimum or increasing-limit closure is imported.

B341 supplies **only** the bounded algebraic moment measures with $p=n-1$ potential slots, full all-field mixed kernel, symmetry/product/power/integral-closure laws, scalar ideal-moment order, real uniform continuity, bounded specified-limit decreasing-net continuity, one-factor gauge and top-degree residue-weighted normalization. It supplies actual curve zero-arity Cartier geometry and the bounded BFJ comparison in its precise published range. It supplies no higher-dimensional geometric comparison, locality, energy, or arbitrary finite-map theorem; n2--n3 provide the new geometric work.

Use raw degree-$n$ length coefficients, without dividing by $e_R(\mathfrak a)$. In a nonpure ring all smaller-dimensional components and all their supports remain in $X$ and $D$, but have zero contribution to this top-degree convention. Their own lower-arity energies would define additional conditions; they are not silently added. Put $m_{\mathfrak a}=e_R(\mathfrak a)>0$ and $\delta_{\mathfrak a}=m_{\mathfrak a}^{1/n}$. The additive energy anchor is the zero homogeneous potential. The constant $-1$ on this section is the homogeneous profile $-v(\mathfrak a)$; its reference mixed measure has raw mass $m_{\mathfrak a}$. A different gauge changes that homogeneous reference profile; it does not change the anchor zero.

The theorem constructed below is: a canonical energy $E:D\to[-\infty,0]$, its cone $E^1=\{E> -\infty\}$, finite positive Radon mixed measures on $(E^1)^{n-1}$, actual Cartier comparison on geometric cores, ordinary open locality, mixed integrability and polynomial variation, and continuity for every decreasing net with a specified $D$ and $E^1$ limit. Energy is invariant under primary changes, measures have exactly one gauge factor, and normalization/conductor descent uses every fibre and actual residue degrees. The smooth specialization is precisely the **finite-self-intersection extension of BFJ Definition 4.3**, proved in n8, rather than an assertion about a differently defined analytic or projective energy space. For $n=1$ this is actual reduced-curve linear Cartier energy. For $n=0$ set $E=0$ on the unique empty function and use the zero measure; no negative arity or division by $n$ is made.
