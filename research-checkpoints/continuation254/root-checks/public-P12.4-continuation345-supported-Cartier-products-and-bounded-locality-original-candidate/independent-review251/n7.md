## n7. Gauge, normalization, curves and degeneracies

For another proper primary b let d(v)=v_a(b)>0 and T(v)=v/d(v). G331 supplies a homeomorphism and continuous bounded d,d^-1. Transport inputs by u_b(Tv)=u_a(v)/d(v). Rational profiles retain the same ideals and denominators, and v_b(J)(Tv)=v_a(J)/d. Thus testing T_*[d mu_a] against v_b(J) gives exactly the gauge-b mixed moment. Density proves

    mu_b=T_*[d mu_a].                                    (GAUGE)

There is ONE d factor independent of n, since it cancels the test's homogeneous denominator, rather than a count of input factors. The transported C and bounded decreasing D representations preserve uniform or pointwise decreasing convergence, so both proved continuities extend (GAUGE). Multiplication by fixed d and compact pushforward are weakly continuous. The new mass is the old moment against v_a(b), rationally e(I_1,...,I_p,b)/prod q_i. Along a third gauge, d_ab(v) d_bc(T_ab v)=v_a(c), proving the exact cocycle. On a geometric atom, b_Z changes from ord_Z(a) to ord_Z(b)=d(nu_Z)b_Z and its normalized point changes by T, giving the same rule directly.

Let S be the finite normalization of R and q its local normal centers over m. A regular conductor element kills S/R. For any prime in that module's support, a chain to m extends strictly below it to a minimal prime avoiding that regular element. Thus dim(S/R)<n. The top-form exact-sequence additivity of n2 gives H_R=H_S. For each primary product P, Artinian decomposition and composition series give

    l_R(S/PS)=sum_q f_q l_(S_q)(S_q/PS_q),
    f_q=[kappa(q):kappa(m)].

These are field-vector-space dimensions, with no separability assumption. Comparing degree-n forms yields

    e_R(I_1,...,I_n)
      =sum_(dim S_q=n) f_q e_(S_q)(I_1 S_q,...,I_n S_q).

When dim S_q<n its degree-n form is zero; it is not replaced by its own lower-dimensional intrinsic operator. Test on the lattice, then use uniform C and prescribed bounded decreasing D continuity, and obtain

    mu_R,a(u)=sum_(dim S_q=n) f_q rho_(q,*)
                  mu_(S_q,aS_q)(rho_q^*u).              (NORMAL)

All lower-dimensional centers, their boundary points, and every support remain in the space and potential domain. This is zero top-degree contribution, not point deletion.

For full-fiber-constant compatible upstairs C or D tuples, G331 grants actual positive descent. Its normalization quotient is unchanged: finite conductor value gives singleton fibres, and infinite conductor value gives exactly the equality of restrictions through S/c -> R/c. Apply (NORMAL) to their descended inputs, using the FULL maps rho_q. Identified fibres have their masses added under pushforward; no choice of representative is made. The same formula is now geometric by (COMPARE). This does not construct the entire arbitrary-finite-map operator calculus of P12.6.

For an excellent reduced curve n=1, normal local centers are DVRs. Their centered real semivaluations have zero support and are multiples of order, since every nonzero element is a parameter power times a unit. The regular conductor element has finite value on every branch. G331's singleton fibres therefore give the finite branch set nu_q=ord_q/b_q, b_q=ord_q(a)>0. The independent zero-input reference measure is

    mu_R,a()=sum_q f_q b_q delta_nu_q,
    mu_R,a(X)=e_R(a).                                   (CURVE)

Branches mean normalization LOCAL centers, including two branches on one irreducible nodal component. To verify actual Cartier degree on EVERY curve primary model Y, IS is invertible, so Spec S -> Y exists. Its graph in Spec S x_R Y proves it finite; the finite image contains all component generic points of reduced Y and hence is surjective. It is birational and normal, thus is Y's finite normalization. At a closed point y take B=O_Y,y, its finite semilocal normalization Btilde, and finite-length C=Btilde/B. For a regular Cartier equation g the snake exact sequence is

    0 -> C[g] -> B/gB -> Btilde/gBtilde -> C/gC -> 0.

The two end modules have equal lengths. Hence the local supported Cartier length equals the sum of the DVR orders with closed residue degrees, first over kappa(y) and then multiplied by [kappa(y):k]. Summing y and extending by effective differences and REAL coefficients gives Q_Y(F)=sum_q f_q ord_q(F)=integral H_F dmu(). This preserves local degree even when the global Chow class vanishes. DVR lengths also give the ideal moment e_R(J). Zero-arity continuity and locality are constant identities.

Residue degrees must not be replaced by generic degrees. For any finite k subset l of degree d>1, including purely inseparable extensions, set R=k+t l[[t]], S=l[[t]]. With k-basis 1,alpha_2,...,alpha_d, R=k[[t]] plus direct sum_(i>1) t k[[t]] alpha_i is a finite algebra over the excellent DVR k[[t]], hence excellent Noetherian. It is local with m=tS, residue k, fraction field l((t)), normalization S, conductor tS, generic degree one, residue degree d. For J=t^r S,

    l_R(R/J^N)=1+d(rN-1), e_R(J)=dr.

Its m-reference measure is d delta_ord_t, with moment dr. The calculation uses vector dimensions only and is valid for inseparable l/k.

If n=0, R is a field, X is empty and its measure is zero; there is no negative arity and no degree-zero multiplicity mass on an empty boundary. For n>=2 a zero input, represented by a unit, annihilates the product; a unit test has zero moment. At p=0 there is no input zero to annihilate the reference measure. Inputs -t_i v(a) have mass (prod t_i)e_R(a), with empty product one. The zero ideal has infinite value, its negative profile is unbounded bottom, and it is excluded from bounded inputs and primary gauges. Unit and zero ideals have no level-one section. Zero scaling is the actual zero function, never the undefined expression zero times infinity.

For nonreduced germs this is the reduction convention, not a hidden nilpotent intersection assertion. k[[t]][epsilon]/epsilon^2 and k[[t]] have identical semivaluation boundaries but parameter-power lengths 2s and s. Generic nilpotent lengths require extra reference data. In contrast, nilpotent thicknesses of exceptional Cartier divisors on reduced models are intrinsic to our supported construction and were fully retained in (C).
