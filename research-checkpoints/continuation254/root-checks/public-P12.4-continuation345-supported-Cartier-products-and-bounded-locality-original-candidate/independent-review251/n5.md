## n5. Independent atomic geometry, invariance and real comparison

Normalize a common primary model Y. Excellence makes N->Y finite. Retain the top normal components for degree n; every other component and boundary point remains in the domain. Let Z range over the finitely many dimension-p exceptional prime divisors. Their generic local rings are DVRs. Put b_Z=ord_Z(a)>0 and nu_Z=ord_Z/b_Z, pulled back to R from its minimal component fraction field. For actual negative-effective real curve-nef D_1,...,D_p define, BEFORE identifying algebraic measures,

    mu_geom,Y=sum_Z b_Z (D_1|Z ... D_p|Z . Z) delta_nu_Z.  (A)

For p=0 the numerical coefficient is deg_k Z=[kappa(Z):k]. There is no generic residue factor omitted from this coefficient.

Positivity uses precisely qualified G331. On Y, subtract a small positive multiple of E_I from D_i. This is real ample by its proved real nef-plus-ample argument. Round all nonnegative effective-presentation coefficients upward to rational numbers, sufficiently little that finite-span openness preserves ampleness. G331's finite-Picard-subgroup rationalization makes the result genuinely Q-ample. These negative rational ample divisors L_ij converge in the finite coefficient spans to D_i. Finite normalization pulls their ample bundles to ample bundles, and restriction to each proper Z is ample. Clear denominators and use 33.45.9 for positive mixed intersections. Intersections are finite multilinear coefficient polynomials, so their limits are nonnegative. This establishes positivity of (A) independently of ideal positivity.

At nu_Z evaluation gives H_F(nu_Z)=ord_Z(F)/b_Z for every exceptional real Cartier F. Formula (C) on the normal model has DVR multiplicities ord_Z(F) for effective F; extend by differences. With (PI) this gives the independent geometric identity

    integral H_F dmu_geom,Y=Q_Y(D_1,...,D_p,F).             (G)

Independence of refinement is proved now, BEFORE algebraic moment comparison. If Y'->Y is a primary refinement, its normalization N' maps to N on each component: an integral function over the target chart is in every normal source local ring in the same fraction field. These local lifts agree generically and hence glue. The graph in N' x_Y N is closed since N is separated over Y; projection to N is proper because N'->Y is proper. The lift is birational on top components.

If an exceptional Z' maps dominantly to a dimension-p Z in N, the source DVR dominates the target DVR in the SAME fraction field. They are equal. For every field element write x=t^m u in the target DVR, with u a unit. Both u and u^-1 remain units upstairs and t is a nonunit there. Thus its sign of order is precisely the sign of m, proving equality of valuation rings, normalized orders and residue fields. Apply the ACTUAL cycle equality (P) to E_I, whose coefficient at every target exceptional Z is positive. Each dominant Z' then has the same positive coefficient and degree one, so (P) forces EXACTLY ONE such Z' over Z. Numerical projection gives its mixed intersection equal to the old mixed weight. Any remaining Z' has image dimension <p; its p pulled-back line bundles have zero intersection by 33.45.7. Consequently (A) is unchanged as a measure on X. This works componentwise, for common refinements and auxiliary normal lifts. In particular (G) can be tested on a common refinement for a test not originally determined on Y.

The bridge needs an actual sheaf identity. Fix Y=Bl_I R. From G331, T=H^0(Y,O_Y) is finite birational and T/R has closed-point support. Fix h with m^h(T/R)=0 and c_0=m^(h+1); then c_0 T subset m AS ELEMENTS OF R. For a negative rational ample exceptional L and large divisible q, O(qL) is globally generated. Its finite module of sections F_q lies in T. Put J_q=c_0 F_q. It is an ideal of R and is proper by c_0 T subset m. Away from m, section localization, the identity model and the divisor trivialization give J_q R_P=R_P. Hence J_q is proper primary, and contains a power of m.

Use ONE refinement Y'=Bl_(I c_0) R. Its product-ideal universal property makes the images of I and c_0 invertible; it maps to Y and Bl_c0 R. Let D_0 be the pulled-back negative divisor of c_0. Global generation gives a surjection F_q tensor O_Y -> O_Y(qL) whose IMAGE in O_Y is exactly that invertible ideal. Pullback preserves the surjection. The pulled-back ideal inclusion stays injective: its Cartier generator is nonzero on every reduced dominating component, hence regular. Multiply by the invertible regular image of c_0. Thus as actual image ideals in O_Y',

    J_q O_Y'=O_Y'(q f^*L+D_0).                            (S)

Its regular invertibility gives Y'->Bl_Jq R by 31.33.5. This is not merely equality of valuations, and the same c_0,Y' works for every divisor and approximation exponent.

For simultaneous negative rational ample L_1,...,L_n, with generated divisible q_i, use (I), (PI), and (S):

    e(J_(1,q_1),...,J_(n,q_n))/prod q_i
       =-Q_Y'(f^*L_1+D_0/q_1,...,f^*L_n+D_0/q_n).        (S2)

On EVERY support the equality of image ideals at the valued center also gives

    -v(J_q)/q=H_L-v(c_0)/q.                              (S3)

G331's all-support evaluation avoids using the total fraction ring at a nonminimal support: pull the invertible ideal and its generic trivialization to the support quotient valuation ring. A generating section has a unit coefficient there. Because c_0 is PRIMARY, v(c_0) has a uniform finite bound. Hence (S3) is uniform convergence.

Now approximate real core D_i by the negative rational ample L_ij above on the FIXED model; choose generated divisible q_ij tending to infinity. The same Y',D_0 applies. The right side of (S2) converges by its finite coefficient polynomial, independently of function convergence. The profiles in (S3) converge uniformly. The moment continuity of n3 and its common mass bound also allow the last test to vary uniformly: the error integral is at most the test's sup error times that mass. Since the test -v(J_(n,q_n))/q_n has negative moment -e/prod q_i, this proves

    integral H_Dn dmu_alg(H_D1,...,H_Dp)
       =Q_Y(D_1,...,D_p,D_n).                            (B)

No implication from uniform functions to intersection convergence was assumed.

For an arbitrary integral exceptional F choose N large enough that F-NE_I and -NE_I are both actual negative and ample, using G331's order unit and ample twisting. They are admissible core tests and their difference is F. Test linearity extends (B) to F, then to every real exceptional test. To test a primary ideal not determined on Y, go to a common primary refinement. The INDEPENDENT atomic invariance above, (G), and (B) identify all primary moments there. The dense ideal-difference lattice and finite mass give

    mu_geom,Y(D_1,...,D_p)
       =mu_alg(H_D1,...,H_Dp).                           (COMPARE)

Thus the geometric measure depends only on the functions, is independent of their presentations and primary models, and has the exact supported Cartier pairing on any common refinement. N3 extends this independently constructed geometric core to the full bounded geometric D. This supplies the required extra comparison theorem rather than merely renaming an ideal completion.
