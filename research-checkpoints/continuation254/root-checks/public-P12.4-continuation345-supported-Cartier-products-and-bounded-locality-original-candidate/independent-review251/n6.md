## n6. Strict maximum and bounded open locality

Suppose p>=1. For rational selected profiles synchronize denominators by powers: u=-v(A)/q, w=-v(B)/q; their maximum is -v(A+B)/q. Choose a common primary product model making A,B,A+B, every other input and a reference primary ideal invertible, then normalize. The reference factor handles all-unit cases. Unit divisors are zero and cause no change in the argument.

Set S=(A+B)O_N. Define the ACTUAL invertible subideals L=AO_N/S and M=BO_N/S in O_N. They satisfy L+M=O_N. If u(nu_Z)>w(nu_Z), then ord_Z(A)<ord_Z(B), hence ord_Z(M)>0. The map M|Z -> O_Z is zero: on any trivializing open its regular generator is zero at the generic point of integral reduced Z, and a regular section on this integral scheme that vanishes generically vanishes identically. Restrict L+M=O_N to Z. With M's map zero, L|Z surjects onto O_Z, so it is isomorphic to O_Z as a line bundle. Therefore the difference D_A-D_(A+B) restricts trivially to Z. Every mixed weight of (A) at an atom in the strict set is identical for u and max(u,w), with all other slots fixed. Both measures are on the same finite divisorial set, giving

    mu(max(u,w),others)|{u>w}=mu(u,others)|{u>w}.          (MAX)

This is an equality of geometric atomic weights, not an inference from moment order.

For C selected and other slots, take uniform rational approximants. A continuous test h with compact support K inside the open strict set has a positive minimum of u-w on K. Accurate approximants keep that strict gap throughout K. Their (MAX) identities and uniform weak continuity give equality of integrals against the FIXED h. Such tests determine Radon restrictions. Next hold selected u,w in C fixed and represent all other bounded D slots by actual decreasing C nets. The open set and h remain fixed; n3's prescribed-limit decreasing continuity extends (MAX) to those other slots.

For arbitrary bounded u,w in D agreeing on open U, fix the other bounded D slots and a continuous h with compact support K subset U. Take actual decreasing C representations u_alpha down to u and w_beta down to w; all terms have a common bound. Fix alpha and epsilon>0. The continuous open sets {w_beta-epsilon<u_alpha} form a directed cover of K, because their pointwise limits there satisfy w-epsilon=u-epsilon<u_alpha. Compactness selects beta_0; the strict open neighborhood of K at that index is fixed for this alpha,epsilon and remains valid for every later beta. The already proved selected-C identity yields

    integral h dmu(max(u_alpha,w_beta-epsilon),others)
       =integral h dmu(u_alpha,others).

First decrease beta. The known limit max(u_alpha,w-epsilon) belongs to bounded D by G331's finite-max stability (also by this actual representation). Apply n3's decreasing-net continuity. Then decrease alpha; the prescribed limit max(u,w-epsilon) is again in bounded D. A second application yields

    integral h dmu(max(u,w-epsilon),others)
       =integral h dmu(u,others).                        (L1)

These are two successive limits with fixed h, not flattening of nested D representations. Interchange u,w for a second identity. Both shifted maxima are uniformly within epsilon of the ALREADY KNOWN bounded D function max(u,w). Their possible increase as epsilon tends to zero is immaterial: use (U) inside this known domain and then weak convergence. The two right sides of (L1) have the same limiting integral, so mu(u,others) and mu(w,others) agree on all continuous tests supported compactly in U.

For completeness these tests determine the restrictions on U. For compact K subset U, compact Hausdorff normality gives a continuous cutoff equal to one on K whose support is contained in U (first shrink to an open set with closure in U). For positive Radon measures, inner regularity makes mass of an open subset of U the supremum of integrals of such cutoffs bounded by one. Hence equality of the test functionals gives equality on open subsets of U, and then on its Borel sets by Radon regularity. Replace input slots one at a time to prove full mixed locality. At p=0 locality is automatic. This is ordinary open locality on the entire compact normalized boundary; no plurifine or arbitrary Borel cutoff claim is made.
