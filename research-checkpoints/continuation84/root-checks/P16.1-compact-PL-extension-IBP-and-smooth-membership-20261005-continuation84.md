# Compact PL extension, signed IBP, and smooth membership

This is a new unreviewed draft. Original P16.1 remains open. Work over algebraically closed trivially valued characteristic-zero \(k\), on reduced irreducible projective \(X\), and fix very ample \(H\). The final membership result additionally assumes \(X\) smooth.

The continuous contact representation and bounded shared-collar theory of actual round75, Theorem4.1 and Proposition8.1, are explicit unreviewed antecedents: common genuine global collars, actual local weights/references, positive mixed products, continuous compatibility, and bounded contact-test weighted decreasing convergence independent of sequences. Their author labels and finite diagnostics do not establish these assertions. The new argument derives further steps from their exact statements.

## 1. Rational PL bumps and a partition without products

BJ2.1–2.3 define the rational vector lattice \(\mathrm{PL}(X)\subset C^0(X^{an})\), containing rational constants, separating points and uniformly dense. Max/min closure concerns this space; the real linear span is expressly not max closed in Remark2.4. Compact Hausdorff \(X^{an}\) is normal, without metrizability.

For \(K\Subset U\), choose \(K\subset W\subset\overline W\subset U\) and continuous \(\beta\in[0,1]\) equal to1 near \(K\), zero outside \(W\). Approximate it by global PL \(f\) with uniform error \(<1/4\). Then \(b=\min(1,\max(0,2f-1/2))\) is global rational PL, equals1 near \(K\), and has support in \(\overline W\Subset U\). Strict error gives a zero collar outside \(W\).

For finite cover \((U_i)_{1\le i\le r}\), take compact \(K_i\subset U_i\) whose interiors cover, and corresponding \(b_i\). Set
\[
 R_0=1,\qquad \rho_i=\min(b_i,R_{i-1}),\qquad
 R_i=R_{i-1}-\rho_i=\max(0,R_{i-1}-b_i).
\]
All are global rational PL, \(0\le\rho_i\le1\), and \(\mathrm{supp}\rho_i\Subset U_i\). At each point some \(b_j=1\), so \(R_j=0\) and \(\sum_i\rho_i=1\). Neither division by a PL sum nor multiplication of PL functions occurs.

## 2. Exact finite local PL extension near a compact set

Local rational PL here means a continuous finite selection from rational affine log expressions on specified algebraic tropical charts with the finite extended-coordinate convention. At a zero coordinate, every finite affine branch on its neighborhood has zero coefficient on that infinite logarithm; shrink and omit it. Near a compact set there are finitely many compact rational neighborhoods in algebraic principal charts, with positive lower bounds on all used units. This statement is restricted to the specified finite local PL class.

On \(D(a)\), a regular unit is \(g=F/a^m\), with sections of powers of the original \(H\), by section localization on the original nonnormal ring. Canonical trivial metrics give \(\log|g|=\log\|F\|-m\log\|a\|\). For rational \(M\) beyond the lower bounds, \(L_F=\max(\log\|F\|,-M)\) and \(L_a=\max(\log\|a\|,-M)\) are global rational PL, by the coherent vanishing ideals and BJ2.1. Their difference equals \(\log|g|\) on the entire neighborhood. Powers and the original bundle are retained; infinities are not subtracted.

The selected function on the compact unit-log image extends to finite rational PL on \(\mathbb R^q\). A strict Laurent extension \(k((t))\), clearing rational thresholds, lets published Ducros3.2 supply a finite rational polytope complex. Its image equals the original image: projection preserves unit values and the independent-variable Gauss section lifts every original point with the same constraints. Refine by branch-equality hyperplanes; the selected affine class is constant on each relative open cell, and continuity extends it to the closed cell. Put the complex inside a larger rational box, triangulate a rational subdivision respecting its faces, retain the prescribed affine vertex values, assign zero to other vertices including the outer boundary, interpolate, and extend by zero outside the box.

A finite rational PL \(F\) on \(\mathbb R^q\) is a difference of finite maxima of rational affine functions. Include all affine subdivision facet hyperplanes \(\ell_j=0\). For large rational \(C\), \(F+C\sum_j|\ell_j|\) has nonnegative normal slope jumps across every wall: continuity makes each gradient jump normal, finitely many jumps are bounded, and each absolute value adds \(2C\) to that normal jump. Generic lines have nondecreasing slopes; continuity gives convexity on all lines. A finite convex PL function is the maximum of its affine supporting pieces; the sum of absolute values is also a finite maximum. Substitute the global PL unit-log extensions: this yields global \(q_i\in\mathrm{PL}(X)\) equal to the local function on each selected neighborhood \(U_i\).

For \(K\Subset U\), choose finitely many \(U_i\Subset U\), with \(q_i=f\) there, and bumps \(b_i\) supported there whose1-plateaus cover a neighborhood of \(K\). Choose rational \(M>\max_K|f|\) and \(C>\max_i\|q_i+M\|_\infty\). Then
\[
 r=\max\bigl(0,\max_i(q_i+M-C(1-b_i))\bigr)
\]
is compactly supported global PL. Near \(K\), a plateau supplies \(f+M>0\); every active candidate is at most that and every inactive candidate is negative. Hence \(r=f+M\) there. Subtract \(Mb\), with another compact bump equal to1 near \(K\), to obtain a compact signed extension of \(f\).

If \(f\) already has compact support in \(U\), extend \(f_+\) and \(f_-\) by this construction with \(M=0\), and plateaus covering their support. Off the support, active candidates are at most \(f_\pm=0\), while inactive candidates are negative. Their difference is exactly the zero extension with the original support. Only rational scalar multiplication is used.

## 3. Signed local IBP from global intersections

BJ3.6–3.9 define signed model mixed measures and give
\[
 \int_X f_0\,dd^cf_1\wedge T=\int_X f_1\,dd^cf_0\wedge T
\]
by symmetric intersection pairings. Raw mass is the intersection number against the original central fundamental cycle. This signed identity requires neither positivity nor weak/global membership.

For local PL \(f_1,\ldots,f_n\) and compactly supported PL \(g\) on \(U\), extend \(f_i\) exactly near \(\mathrm{supp}g\) by Section2 and extend \(g\) by zero. Continuous compatibility and locality identify the signed product restrictions. Outside the support, \(g=0\) on the left and \(dd^cg=0\) on the right. Global BJ3.9 proves compact local signed IBP with support retained.

Every fixed global rational PL \(q\) is a difference of two genuine rational FS potentials in one common ample class \(c\,c_1(H)\). BJ2.25–2.26 give \(\mathrm{PL}^+=\mathbb Q_+H(H)\): twist the finitely many flag-ideal coefficient ideals by a common sufficiently large power of \(H\) so all are generated. Split a rational linear combination into positive and negative sums; pad the smaller total class by the zero FS potential of very ample \(H\). Positive rational sums are FS after common powers/tensor products. This does not claim that \(q\) itself becomes psh by increasing its numerical class; negative exceptional curvature cannot be repaired that way.

For bounded local decreasing contact limits \(u_1,\ldots,u_n\), apply compact IBP at each finite stage. The left side passes by collar weak convergence with fixed continuous test \(g\). On the right decompose \(dd^cg\) into the difference of local weights of these two common-class FS metrics. They are continuous contact weights, so bounded contact-test weighted convergence applies with test \(u_{1,j}\) and factors \(u_{i,j}\). Insert one compact continuous cutoff equal to1 near \(\mathrm{supp}g\): the positive pieces need not separately have compact support, though their difference does. Thus
\[
 \int_U g\,dd^cu_1\wedge T=\int_U u_1\,dd^cg\wedge T.
\]
The argument includes fixed reference weights and their differences. No difference of decreasing sequences is assumed decreasing.

## 4. Raw mass and bounded local comparison

Let bounded locally \(\omega_i\)-psh \(\phi_i\) have actual algebraic frame transitions and finite contact model reference weights \(g_{i,\alpha}\). Write \(u_{i,\alpha}=\phi_i+g_{i,\alpha}\). The collar theory gives positive local products; frame changes add harmonic unit logs. Continuous zero curvature and bounded decreasing convergence make the products agree on overlaps.

Fix \(T\) made from the last \(n-1\) curvature factors and a global PL partition subordinate to common local approximation domains. Compact IBP gives
\[
 \int_X dd^c\phi_1\wedge T
 =\sum_\alpha\int_{U_\alpha}\rho_\alpha(dd^cu_{1,\alpha}-dd^cg_{1,\alpha})\wedge T
 =\sum_\alpha\int_{U_\alpha}\phi_1\,dd^c\rho_\alpha\wedge T=0.
\]
The last equality uses \(\sum_\alpha dd^c\rho_\alpha=dd^c1=0\). Signed products and bounded test integrals are defined by fixed FS decompositions; finite-stage multilinearity passes to their limits. Replace one factor at a time by its reference. Total RAW mixed mass is \((\omega_1\cdots\omega_n)\), including the original fundamental cycle. No probability normalization occurs here.

For bounded local \(\omega\)-psh \(\phi,\psi\), take one shared collar at each point for both local weights and their maximum. The genuine glued maximum is exactly their glued maximum, including the whole reference-winning collar. BJ7.40 supplies Borel strong locality after restoring \(A^n\), even if \(\{\phi>\psi\}\) is not open. Split raw mass \(V\) of \(\max(\phi,\psi-\epsilon)\) into its strict regions:
\[
 \int_{\{\phi<\psi-\epsilon\}}M^{raw}(\psi)
 \le \int_{\{\phi\le\psi-\epsilon\}}M^{raw}(\phi).
\]
Let \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) to obtain bounded local comparison. Wu4.11(1) prints \(MA(v)\) on its winning-\(u\) side; BJ7.40 correctly gives \(MA(u)\).

## 5. Smooth weak domination and genuine membership using nets

Assume \(X\) smooth and irreducible, characteristic zero, \(\omega=c_1(L)\) ample. For every divisorial \(v\), genuine background theory supplies bounded continuous \(G_v\) with \(M_\omega^{raw}(G_v)=V\delta_v\): BJ1.28/11.1 give finite energy, smooth EP/12.9 give existence, and12.12–12.14 give continuity. This is the established smooth theorem in its own domain, not singular EP. Decreasing FS approximations of continuous genuine potentials give actual local contact weights with the original reference.

Bounded weak order is determined on \(X^{div}\). If \(\phi\le\psi\) there, take a common genuine collar near a tested point. Its glued potentials are ordered at all global divisorial points: inside by the given inequality, outside because both equal the reference. BJ4.22 orders them everywhere; their difference on the inner region is the scaled local difference. This proves weak order without first assuming global genuineness.

If bounded weak \(\phi\le\psi\) almost everywhere for \(M^{raw}(\psi)\), shift \(G_v\) strictly below \(\phi\). For rational \(0<\epsilon<1\), put \(\phi_\epsilon=(1-\epsilon)\phi+\epsilon G_v\). Positive mixed multilinearity gives \(M^{raw}(\phi_\epsilon)\ge\epsilon^nV\delta_v\), while \(\phi_\epsilon<\phi\le\psi\) almost everywhere for \(M^{raw}(\psi)\). Comparison and total mass force the Borel strict set \(\{\phi_\epsilon<\psi\}\) to have full \(M^{raw}(\phi_\epsilon)\)-mass, so its atom at \(v\) lies inside. Let \(\epsilon\downarrow0\), then use weak divisorial order to obtain \(\phi\le\psi\) everywhere.

For bounded usc weak \(\phi\), use the decreasing NET of continuous majorants directed by finite minima and converging pointwise to \(\phi\). Compact Hausdorff normality provides this for arbitrary-cardinality \(k\); no decreasing sequence is assumed. Smooth EP and BJ5.17 give continuous genuine \(P_\omega(f_\alpha)\). Orthogonality BJ13.1 gives \(f_\alpha=P_\omega(f_\alpha)\) almost everywhere for its genuine MA; continuous compatibility identifies it with the local measure. Hence \(\phi\le P_\omega(f_\alpha)\) almost everywhere there. Weak domination gives \(\phi\le P_\omega(f_\alpha)\le f_\alpha\) everywhere. The envelopes decrease and squeeze to \(\phi\); genuine decreasing-net closure BJ4.5 gives membership. For an unbounded generically finite weak \(\phi\), first treat \(\max(\phi,-m)\), with actual finite reference weights, then use decreasing closure.

A specified nef FS model reference on a smooth projective resolution can be treated at this bounded first-membership step by adding \(\epsilon c_1(A)\), for very ample \(A\) with canonical zero FS potential. The bundle becomes ample; its actual local reference is explicitly \(g_\omega+\epsilon g_A\). Apply ample membership to the unchanged reference-relative potential after adding its fixed model potential. Let \(\epsilon\downarrow0\); BJ4.5 varying-class closure gives original nef-class membership. Numerical convergence alone is not used to identify a reference. This may address initial membership of bounded resolved-witness growth truncations, but not their removal values or singular EP.

### 5.1. Upper-growth regularization with an actual smooth compactification

Retain explicitly an actual smooth projective compactification \(X\), very ample \(L\), section \(a\) with \(Y=D(a)\), and an FS reference on \(L\) whose local exhaustion is \(g=-\log\|a\|_{FS}\). Let \(u\) be usc, generically finite and locally bounded above on the original smooth affine \(Y\), with upper bound \(u\le Ag+C\), for rational \(A>0\). Retain ONE proper birational witness on which its pullback has the stipulated local decreasing contact approximations. The compactification, original-ring/generator compatibility and projective resolution retaining that witness are actual antecedents here; their existence for every input is not silently assumed.

Choose a smooth projective modification \(\pi:Z\to X\) carrying the original witness over \(Y\). Pull back each finite contact approximant before decreasing limits; this preserves the actual witness data. For rational \(c>1\), use the reference-relative truncations
\[
 \Phi_M=\max(u\circ\pi-cA\,g\circ\pi,M)\quad\hbox{over }Y,\qquad
 \Phi_M=M\quad\hbox{over }X\setminus Y.
\]
The upper bound and properness give a WHOLE boundary collar where the second branch wins: \(u-cAg\le-(c-1)Ag+C\), and \(g\to+\infty\) toward the boundary. The local metric weight is exactly \(\max(u\circ\pi,M+cA\,g\circ\pi)\), so it is a bounded local contact limit in the actual nef class \(cA\pi^*c_1(L)\). Section5 supplies its first genuine membership via specified ample perturbations. Let \(M\downarrow-\infty\); genuine closure gives the generically finite extended metric, including value \(-\infty\) at every boundary point.

Write the original FS reference potential as \(p_L\) relative to the canonical trivial metric and set \(g_0=-\log\|a\|_0\), so \(g=g_0+p_L\). The canonical-reference potential on \(Z\) is thus \(u\circ\pi-cA\,g_0\circ\pi\). BJ5.13 applies to the SMOOTH target \(X\): every approaching class \(cA\,c_1(L)+\epsilon c_1(B)\), for ample \(B\), has smooth EP. Its onto pullback identifies this genuine potential with \(\pi^*\psi\), for \(\psi\in PSH(X,cAL)\). Its proof uses the birational error theorem and divisorial order, retaining target-side EP; it is not an assertion of onto pullback to a singular target without EP.

For a projective birational modification one can realize the morphism as a blowup of a coherent fractional ideal after an invertible twist, as used in that proof. Choose a sufficiently positive relatively very ample line bundle \(M_Z\), with surjective evaluation from \(\pi_*M_Z\), and identify its generic fiber with the common function field. The pushforward is a coherent rank-one fractional module. The evaluation embeds \(Z\) in its projectivization; its integral image is precisely the closure of the generic rank-one section, namely the Proj of the Rees algebra. Twisting to clear denominators does not change that Proj. Relative integral closure retains the original generic fiber. General proper witnesses still require the specified projective dominating modification.

Analytic surjectivity of \(\pi\) now proves \(\psi=u-cAg_0\) at EVERY original point of \(Y^{an}\), since that identity already holds at every witness point above it. This is not an inference from dense-open equality and usc. At the boundary it similarly gives \(\psi=-\infty\). Only after this genuine membership invoke BJ12.18's decreasing same-class FS sequence \(\psi_j\downarrow\psi\).

Rational intercepts can be retained explicitly. For \(\delta_j=4^{-j}\), round the finitely many real FS weights of \(\psi_j\) upward so that the resulting same-bundle rational FS \(F_j\) satisfies \(\psi_j\le F_j\le\psi_j+\delta_j\). Set \(B_j=\sum_{i>j}\delta_i\in\mathbb Q\). Then \(F_j-F_{j+1}\ge-\delta_{j+1}\) and \(B_j-B_{j+1}=\delta_{j+1}\), so \(\widetilde\psi_j=F_j+B_j\) decreases exactly to \(\psi\). No uniform convergence to the possibly discontinuous \(\psi\) is asserted.

On \(Y\), \(u_j=\widetilde\psi_j+cAg_0\) is a finite maximum of logarithms of actual regular ratios \(\sigma_i/a^{mcA}\), with rational slopes and intercepts. Add a finite dominated rational floor: \(g_0\ge0\) and continuous \(\widetilde\psi_j\) has a finite lower bound on compact \(X^{an}\). Every ORIGINAL generator \(z_\ell\) is \(F_\ell/a^{q_\ell}\), so \(\log|z_\ell|\le q_\ell g_0\) in the canonical trivial metric. Since \(u_j\ge cAg_0-\|\widetilde\psi_j\|_\infty\), append a small positive rational \(z_\ell\)-branch with a low rational intercept without changing the function. These are rational AFS on the original ring, decreasing exactly to \(u\).

This is an upper-growth regularization implication with concrete geometric antecedents and the unreviewed bounded-collar antecedent. It does not construct the extension of an input given only on a principal open, identify unspecified removed-set values, or prove the singular Runge/envelope chain.
