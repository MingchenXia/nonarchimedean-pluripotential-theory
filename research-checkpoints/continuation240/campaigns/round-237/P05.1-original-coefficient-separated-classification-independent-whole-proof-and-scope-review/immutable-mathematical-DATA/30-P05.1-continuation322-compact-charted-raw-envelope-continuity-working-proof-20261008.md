## 1. Exact category and proposed conclusion

Let X be a compact Hausdorff space with a finite compatible rational polyhedral atlas. Locally every point has a neighborhood represented by a neighborhood of a face in a finite pure rational polyhedral complex in a real vector space. There are strictly positive real top weights, balanced at every facet in the neighborhood; artificial truncation facets outside that neighborhood require no balancing. Saturated tangent lattices and inward primitive normals use the original convention. Atlas changes preserve the rational points and the legal rational-endpoint PCC inequalities. This last compatibility is an EXPLICIT assumption: a merely real affine translation need not preserve the printed rational-endpoint convention. A finite compatible bounded rational cell decomposition may additionally be supplied for the arithmetic exact-minimum assertion below.

A raw PC function is a function satisfying every local legal printed PCC Jensen inequality on every rational refinement, with rational endpoints and nonnegative real barycentric coefficients. Its continuity is NOT presumed. Competitors may obey only an upper growth bound. Let gamma be a finite continuous raw-PC reference. On compact X positivity at infinity is vacuous. For H=gamma+u with arbitrary u in C(X,R), define the actual pointwise raw envelope

    E(x)=sup{f(x): f raw PC, f<=H, and the admitted upper growth convention}.

Constants are admitted. On this compact space the growth bound f-gamma<=C follows automatically from f<=H. The proposed result is that E is continuous and PC, and on every connected component X_j

    E|X_j = min_X_j H.                                      (1)

The finite atlas makes X locally connected and gives only finitely many connected components (a finite cover by connected relative neighborhoods suffices). Continuous gamma itself is constant on each component. In the literal category all constants in (1) are uniformly approximated by rational constants with zero integral slopes; in the genuine real scalar category they are already REAL-PA. ## 3. A bounded raw PC function has a canonical continuous majorant

Let h be raw PC on one such neighborhood with finite bounds m<=h<=M. Restrict h to rational points of each face tau. Ordinary rational Jensen inequalities imply a convex continuous extension F_tau on relint(tau). Here no automatic continuity of raw h is used. The elementary justification is bounded convexity on a rational ball in the affine span: secant inequalities to a fixed larger rational ball bound differences on a smaller ball by a constant times (M-m)|p-q| for rational p,q. A uniform Cauchy extension exists; rational Jensen passes to arbitrary endpoints by continuity. In dimension zero F_tau is its single rational value.

We must prove these face extensions fit continuously at LOWER faces; ordinary convexity on a closed cell alone would permit an upward jump at its boundary. Fix p in relint(tau). Use a rational inner product in the lattice coordinates. For a rational point x in an incident cell sufficiently near p, its orthogonal projection q onto aff(tau) is rational and lies near p in relint(tau). Write t=x-q. The local polyhedral inequalities active at tau put t in the appropriate star cone. Fixed opposing generator representations give

    -(x-q)=sum b_i v_i,    b_i>=0,    sum b_i<=C|x-q|.

With z_i=q+r_i v_i put beta_i=b_i/r_i, and B=sum beta_i<=C_0|x-q|. Then

    q=(x+sum beta_i z_i)/(1+B).

This is a legal rational-endpoint PCC centered on tau: x and every z_i belong to cells incident to tau. Jensen and m<=h<=M give

    h(q)-h(x) <= B(M-m).                                   (2)

For the reverse estimate, choose a rational z on the same ray q->x, still in the incident cell and chart, at a distance between a fixed small r/2 and r from q. The local cone description makes this possible uniformly for small t; a rational scaling factor supplies rational z. Writing x=(1-a)q+a z with a<=2|x-q|/r and using ordinary cellwise Jensen yields

    h(x)-h(q) <= (2/r)(M-m)|x-q|.                           (3)

Consequently rational h-values near p differ from h(q) by at most C_1(M-m)|x-q|. Meanwhile h(q)=F_tau(q) converges to F_tau(p). There are only finitely many incident cells, so the bound is uniform over them. Rational points near p therefore have the UNIQUE limit F_tau(p). This proves that all F_tau glue to a continuous F on the neighborhood; their values on rational points equal h. The construction uses continuity on each relative interior plus (2)/(3), not an assumption of a global Lipschitz extension of h.

At an arbitrary possibly irrational x in relint(tau), choose rational endpoints in an arbitrarily small simplex of tau whose convex hull contains x. Real barycentric coefficients give a permitted PCC with center x. Its endpoint h-values are F-values tending to F(x). The raw inequality implies

    h(x)<=F(x).                                            (4)

In particular a raw downward spike is removed, whereas a forbidden upward spike is not introduced. If h<=H for a continuous H, then F<=H by taking rational limits. The bounds m<=F<=M also persist.

Finally F is PC. A legal real configuration whose center belongs to tau and endpoints to incident cells can be approximated by legal rational configurations retaining these incidence conditions. One precise way to see this is to use homogenized endpoint variables u_i=alpha_i x_i. The conditions u_i in alpha_i sigma_i, alpha_i>=0, sum alpha_i=1 and sum u_i=y in tau form a rational polyhedron when endpoints and center are restricted to small bounded rational neighborhoods. Rational points are dense in every face of this polyhedron; approximate the configuration inside its minimal face. Zero coefficients may be discarded first, so division by the remaining alpha_i is continuous. The resulting endpoints and centers are rational and converge to the originals. The raw Jensen inequality at those rational centers passes to F by its established continuity. Thus F satisfies the local PC inequalities, including every rational subdivision. The same argument applies directly if the definition tests only rational endpoints and arbitrary centers.

On overlapping atlas charts the continuous extensions agree because they equal h at the common rational points, which are dense by the explicit atlas compatibility. Hence a globally bounded raw PC h on X has a canonical continuous PC majorant F, equal on rational points; if h<=a continuous H then F<=H.
