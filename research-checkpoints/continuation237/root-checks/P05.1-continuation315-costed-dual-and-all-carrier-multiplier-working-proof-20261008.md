# A costed dual criterion for simultaneous real polyhedral regularization

ROOT independent working mathematics, unreviewed. No accepted paper or original closure is asserted. This note is not fed to active frozen native233 or232. The criterion below is an exact infinite dual characterization in the finite globally embedded continuous REAL category. It does not prove that every positive balanced pair satisfies it, furnish a finite geometric classification, or settle the separate literal/compact-charted residuals.

Fix a finite pure rational complex Pi in one ambient real vector space, all closed faces present, with strictly positive actual real top weights balanced at every facet. Let X be its support. Fix the actual admitted continuous genuine REAL-PA PC reference gamma, carried affinely by a finite rational refinement and positive at infinity in the stated original sense. REAL-PA means the genuine finite real scalar extension of integral-slope/rational-constant PA on rational spatial carriers. It does not mean arbitrary irrational switching walls. Every target considered here is continuous and bounded relative to this gamma.

Write B=C_b(X,R), with its uniform Banach norm. This retains all bounded continuous functions, without a separability assumption. A legal printed PCC P has rational endpoints x_i, actual nonnegative real coefficients alpha_i summing to one, barycenter y, and the required incident face/cell data in some rational structure. Define the continuous linear functional and its nonnegative geometric cost

    J_P(h)=h(y)-sum_i alpha_i h(x_i),
    b_P=sum_i alpha_i gamma(x_i)-gamma(y)>=0.

Let K={h in B: J_P(h)<=b_P for every legal P}. Thus gamma+h is continuous printed-PC exactly when h is in K. This is a closed convex set containing zero; it is not presumed a vector space or cone. Gamma remains fixed throughout.

**Costed infinite Farkas lemma, with no Slater assumption.** In B* times R set

    D=closure cone({(J_P,b_P): P legal} union {(0,1)}),          (1)

where closure uses sigma(B*,B) on the dual coordinate and the ordinary topology on R. Then

    (ell,t) in D  <=>  ell(h)<=t for every h in K.               (2)

The forward implication holds on generators, finite nonnegative combinations and weak-star limits, since h is a fixed test element. For the converse, separate a point (ell,t) outside the closed convex cone D by a continuous linear functional of the product locally convex topology. Every such functional is a pairing with some (h,s) in B times R; use pairing ell(h)-t s. Cone separation gives

    ell(h)-t s>0,
    J_P(h)-b_P s<=0 for every P,
    -s<=0.

If s>0, h/s belongs to K and violates ell(h/s)<=t. If s=0, J_P(h)<=0 for all P. Because b_P>=0, n h belongs to K for every n>=0. A valid bound ell(n h)<=t for every n forces ell(h)<=0, contradicting the separation inequality. Thus any inequality valid on K must belong to D. This proves(2), including possible unbounded support functions. Only the usual locally convex separating form of Hahn--Banach is used. It explains why replacing weak-star closure by unclosed finite combinations, or nets by sequences on an arbitrary nonseparable B, is not justified.

**A purely finite matrix on each carrier.** Let C range over all finite rational refinements of the original complex and the gamma carrier. Let W_C be the finite-dimensional space of globally continuous functions affine on C and bounded on X. Matching affine formulas are tested on finite rational point/ray generators, including both signs for lineality. Boundedness is exactly zero slope on every recession generator. These are rational homogeneous equations. Choose a rational basis phi_1,...,phi_m for W_C by Gaussian elimination; clear denominators to obtain literal bounded PA basis functions if desired. Any h=sum z_j phi_j is genuine REAL-PA, irrespective of whether z_j is rational. The uniform norm of h is the maximum of its absolute values at the finite chosen point generators, since its recession slopes vanish.

For each original face tau and each nonempty carrier region rho contained in tau, add an unknown ambient affine support ell_(tau,rho)=p x+d. Impose equality with gamma+h on point/ray generators of rho, and impose gamma+h>=ell_(tau,rho) on every point/ray generator of every carrier cell contained in each ENTIRE original top cell incident to tau. Include original top cells as faces, to cover their interiors. Convert equalities to paired inequalities. The complete finite matrix has form

    A_C z + N_C u <= d_C,                                     (3)

where u contains all support coefficients, spatial coefficients are rational, and d_C contains the actual real gamma values/slopes. The original geometry, the gamma formulas and generator arithmetic completely specify the matrix. No target function or PC oracle is an input to(3).

For clarity, the exact support equivalence is reconstructed here rather than importing the unregistered native231 draft as a grant. A feasible matrix tuple gives continuous matching formulas and one touching affine minorant on all entire incident original cells at every real point of each rho. Thus every point has a common support. A segment in X has a finite partition into original-cell intervals; these supports prove convexity on each interval and prevent a downward derivative jump at a transition. For any printed PCC in any carrying structure, its center-to-endpoint segments lie in X. A center support bounds each initial directional slope, convex secants extend its inequality to the endpoint, and the barycenter equation cancels the covector upon summing. Hence all printed PCC inequalities hold, including irrational centers.

Conversely suppose gamma+h is continuous PC and h belongs to W_C. Consequently the projection of(3) onto z is exactly K_C=K intersect W_C. It is a nonempty finite polyhedron; zero is feasible because gamma itself is PC. Refinement sends K_C into K_C' without changing the represented function. Every bounded-relative continuous REAL-PA PC function belongs to some K_C, since common refinement of its finite rational carrier, the original complex and the gamma carrier is finite rational. Thus the union of the K_C is directed and convex.

**Exact finite multipliers.** For ell in B*, write a_C(ell)=(ell(phi_j))_j. For any real t, the inequality ell(h)<=t on K_C is equivalent to the existence of a finite multiplier lambda_C satisfying

    lambda_C>=0,
    A_C^T lambda_C = a_C(ell),
    N_C^T lambda_C = 0,
    d_C^T lambda_C <= t.                                     (4)

This is ordinary finite-dimensional linear-programming duality, applied to the feasible program maximizing a_C(ell).z with constraints(3), where z,u are unrestricted. If the maximum is unbounded, there is no finite t satisfying either side. If bounded, the primal and dual attain their finite optimum and have equal values, giving(4). The usual proof uses finite elimination/closed polyhedral projection and separation; its hypotheses allow the actual irrational d_C. There is no claim to an effective oracle for black-box real numbers. The equality involving N_C is essential: it removes the auxiliary support variables. Merely testing nodal heights ignores that equality and is insufficient.

**All-carrier criterion.** The following two statements are equivalent:

(A) Every h in K is a uniform limit of bounded-relative continuous REAL-PA PC perturbations.

(B) Whenever ell in B* and t in R admit a multiplier(4) on EVERY finite rational carrier C, the pair (ell,t) lies in the explicit costed PCC cone D of(1).

To prove this, let L be the uniform closure of the directed union of K_C. It is a closed convex subset of K containing zero. By(4), the hypothesis in(B) says exactly that ell(h)<=t on every K_C, hence on L. By(2), membership in D says that the same inequality holds on K. If L is a proper subset of K, norm-topological separation of a point h_0 in K outside L supplies ell and t with ell(h)<=t on L but ell(h_0)>t. The separation margin is positive because L is closed and convex. This violates(B). Conversely L=K makes every bound on L a bound on K, so(2) proves(B). This establishes the equivalence, rather than claiming finite-dimensional feasibility for a non-PA target.

The criterion quantifies only geometry/reference-based matrices, bounded linear functionals and costed legal PCC generators. It avoids quantifying over an unknown target in its statement(B). Nevertheless its weak-star closure and ALL-carrier quantifiers are infinite; no finite combinatorial classification or automatic satisfaction of(B) follows. A measure-type separator, if used, must represent the full dual of C_b(X), not only compactly supported countably additive measures on X. The Banach-dual formulation above avoids that unproved replacement.

**Relation to the actual all-C_b envelopes.** Qualified accepted R230 identifies the original upper-only-competitor envelope for every u in C_b with a continuous PC function e=gamma+h_e satisfying ||h_e||<=||u||. It supplies continuity, not(A). If(B) holds, apply(A) to h_e. From |q_n-e|<=delta_n, with q_n continuous REAL-PA PC and delta_n=2^-n, the function q_n-delta_n is a legal PA competitor below e<=gamma+u, and its error is at most2delta_n. Thus the PA competitors have the same supremum e. The functions r_n=q_n+delta_n lie above e with errors in[0,2delta_n]; r_n+4delta_n have errors in[4delta_n,6delta_n] and decrease by at least delta_n. These are uniform decreasing REAL-PA approximants, with exact actual recession since their differences from e are bounded. The shifts need not retain contacts.

Conversely, if all these actual envelopes admit uniform REAL-PA PC approximants, every h in K is itself an envelope endpoint: with obstacle u=h, gamma+h is an admissible competitor and the obstacle bounds all competitors by exactly gamma+h. Approximation of those endpoints proves(A). This includes arbitrary bounded continuous, possibly nonseparable, obstacles and respects the original upper-only competitor convention. The argument does not identify raw discontinuous printed-PC with the continuous class or assign MA to intermediate functions.
