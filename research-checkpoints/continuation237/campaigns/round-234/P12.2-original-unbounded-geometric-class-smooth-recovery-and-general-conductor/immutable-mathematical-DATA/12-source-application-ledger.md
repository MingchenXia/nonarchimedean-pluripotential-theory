## Immediate BdFFU units inspected

The introduction and source scope, valuation definitions and normalized spaces, LF 1--273, were checked. Proposition 2.4 and its complete proof are LF 274--299, on PDF page 6. Definitions 2.1/2.2 and Theorem 2.3 specify normalizing subschemes, good resolutions and finite-valued function-field valuations. Proposition 2.5 and proof, LF 304--317, was read as its immediate follow-up. No theorem from this source is applied positively to the new construction.

Literal LF 279--280 of Proposition 2.4(b) prints `r_pi^N(nu)(a) >= nu(a)`. LF 295--298 of the proof instead gives `nu(f) >= nu_w(f)`. PDF page 6 visibly prints the same reversed statement sign; its proof also calls the ideal minimum convex after calling the functions concave (LF 291--292). `evidence/BdFFU-06.png` retains that page. The independent counterexample takes `z=x+y`, weights `(nu(x),nu(z))=(1,2)`, so `nu(y)=1`, while retraction to the first blowup vertex is ordinary order: the ideal `(x+y)` has actual value 2 and retracted value 1. This refutes the printed sign in exactly the source's allowed smooth complex domain. It does not refute its correctly signed elementary monomial argument or certify other versions.
