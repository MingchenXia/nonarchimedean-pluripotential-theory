Abstract and status
This paper gives a partial reconstruction of original P16.2. The ground field is any algebraically closed trivially
valued field of characteristic zero, with no cardinality assumption. The original carrier is any smooth integral
proper variety of positive dimension. We prove existence of an actual big line bundle on every such carrier and
of a retained polar presentation for every specified actual big rational line bundle. We construct actual original
metrics, prove their section approximation and all-point descent, and repair the fixed-divisor cone argument
without the false general extended-valued flattening lemma. We then construct a positive raw measure-valued
pairing before choosing any target, prove its mass and finite-energy transports, and prove the corresponding
conditional variational equation theory.
The completed local argument has a specific scope: decreasing finite section maxima in a fixed actual ample
bundle on the retained modification, divided on original frame neighborhoods. It covers bounded discontinuous
endpoints and passes frame-unit identities through those limits. What remains unproved here is the agreement
of this construction with the local contact/collar intersection product in the complete approximation category
required for an intrinsic original-carrier local product. This is a compatibility and independence statement for
the actual weights constructed below, not a request for a theorem about arbitrary unrelated local psh functions.
Consequently this paper does not claim a complete solution of P16.2. Its status is candidate=false.
All results below are mathematical proof drafts. The supplied actual-ample theorem G0 is the sole task grant,
in exactly its actual ample domain. Published background theorems are recorded separately. Supplied histori-
cal reports and continuations supply no additional grants. Executions, hashes, and PDF compilation have no
mathematical force.
