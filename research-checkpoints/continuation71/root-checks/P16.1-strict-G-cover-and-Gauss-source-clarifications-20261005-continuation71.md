# Strict model gluing and the actual Gauss-section source

This is an unreviewed root clarification of the Gauss-descent proposal. It is not an accepted dependency or a solution of P16.1. The previously frozen author input of round61 is unchanged and receives no feedback from this note.

## 1. What the actual Gauss source supplies

BJ1801.08229v4, inclusive LF724--757 of the saved literal text, defines Gauss extension by

    sigma(v)(sum_d f_d t^d) = min_d(v(f_d)+d).

The actual Remark1.9 explicitly states that sigma:X^an -> X_K^an is a continuous section of the natural projection, for K=k((t)). This is the topological fact needed in the companion descent proposal. No identification of the entire base-extended analytification with sigma(X^an) is used. In particular, the printed identification language at LF749--753 must not be interpreted as surjectivity of the section. The Gauss construction, its compatibility with algebraic local frames and its extension to the Laurent expansions of global sections still need the independent justification given in the companion proposal.

In that proposal, "generically finite function" means a potential finite at the generic point of each component, rather than a generically finite morphism. For the FS-defined metric conventions, one should state the admissible singular-metric/non-identically-minus-infinity condition exactly as required by the chosen cone. The exact finite descent of each FS metric itself has no unbounded-potential antecedent. Its cutoff uses the compactness of X^an and the lower bound of that particular finite-valued FS metric along sigma.

## 2. A sufficient cover-level hypothesis for strict PL gluing

Let K be a complete nontrivially discretely valued field, with a parameter t of valuation 1/q if a finite ramified extension has been made. Let V be a separated paracompact strictly K-analytic space. Let L be a line bundle. Suppose a continuous metric is described on an actual admissible G-cover by compact strictly affinoid domains W_i, analytic frames e_i of L, and finitely many branches

    -log ||e_i|| = c_ia + sum_j b_iaj log |u_ij|,

on subdomains forming an admissible G-cover of W_i. Here the u_ij are analytic units, all c_ia and b_iaj are rational, and each branch-selection subdomain is specified by finitely many comparisons between these expressions. The description may arise from finite max/min operations, but the actual cover and the branch formulas are hypotheses, not inferred merely from a continuous tropical picture.

Choose one common integer N clearing the denominators of the slopes and the constants relative to the value group, after finite ramification if necessary. On each branch there is an analytic unit

    h_ia = t^r_ia product_j u_ij^(z_iaj)

whose valuation is the displayed expression multiplied by N. The signs r_ia and z_iaj must be chosen according to the actual metric formula. The rescaled frame h_ia^(-1) e_i^N, or its inverse rescaling if the formula uses the opposite sign, has metric norm exactly one. Branch comparisons become inequalities |h_ia/h_ib| <= 1 between analytic units. Such cuts are strictly Laurent domains, and their finite covers are the usual admissible Laurent covers. This verifies the strict piecewise-linear metric definition, after N-th tensor power, under the stated actual-cover hypothesis.

For a compact proper space a finite subcover makes N common to the whole metric. On noncompact spaces a locally finite cover and locally varying denominators give GM's piecewise-Q-linear notion; a globally uniform denominator must not be presumed. Pulling a metric from a trivially valued variety to a Laurent field is not by itself proof that an arbitrary previously chosen analytic domain becomes strict. The existence of the required strict compact neighborhoods and the admissible refinement has to be checked at every use of this sufficient criterion.

## 3. Formal construction and local-to-global nefness

The actual GM1608.08030v3 Definition2.8 and Proposition2.10, LF288--378, identify formal metrics with this G-cover/frame definition. In the proof, one refines the cover to a finite-type strictly affinoid cover, chooses a common formal model with those domains as formal opens, then performs admissible blowups making every frame transition an invertible formal function. The resulting transition cocycle is a formal line bundle. The formula is therefore a construction of the same metric, not a numerical equivalence of unrelated bundles. Actual Remark2.6, LF271--275, supplies the proper algebraic/formal bridge.

Nefness is a separate requirement. Suppose the curvature/residual argument proves that this formal metric is semipositive on a compact strictly analytic neighborhood of every point, with the exact degree and multiplicity conventions. Actual GM Proposition3.11, LF754--782, then proves global nefness: for a proper residual curve C, choose a point reducing to its generic point; properness makes that point interior. A locally nef model near that point can be placed as a formal open in a common dominating model. Its residual closure is C and has nonnegative degree. This is a check on every proper residual curve of the global model.

The CLD strict-integer criterion17.8.4 can supply the local residual step only after its stated hypotheses are checked. The differently printed18.8.8 must not silently replace it. In particular, positive curvature of a distributional metric over the original field is not assumed to pull back automatically as a positive current. A use after Laurent extension must independently verify the positive calibrated facet formula there, or justify the exact available base-extension theorem with its hypotheses. This remains a gate for the companion application.

With an ample generic-fiber bundle on a proper algebraic space, actual BE1805.01016v3 Theorem7.8 then gives genuine continuous FS-defined psh from the resulting nef model. Its proof chooses an ample model A of the same generic-fiber L on a dominating projective model and uses (1-epsilon)L+epsilon A for rational epsilon decreasing to zero. The printed H/A mismatch and epsilon direction at LF2348--2351 are typographical; the reconstructed bundle identity and uniform convergence must use A consistently. No smoothness hypothesis is inserted.

## 4. Scope after these checks

Combined with exact Gauss FS descent, this is a potential continuous rational-PL-to-CPSH bridge on singular projective varieties. It does not establish that every relatively convex local support has the asserted admissible branch cover, that real coefficients admit semipositive rational approximation, or that every bounded local psh function admits an algebraic collar. It also does not prove arbitrary analytic Rossi pieces, localization, Runge induction, singular envelope regularity or variational attainment.
