Abstract
We reconstruct a relative equation theory on every actual big rational line bundle on a smooth integral projective
variety over every algebraically closed trivially valued field of characteristic zero. The retained datum is an actual
Kodaira decomposition L = A + D, with A ample and D effective rational Cartier. The reference is the actual
divisor potential σ = log |sD |. We prove that its entire genuine reference-dominated cone is σ + PSH(A), including
all points of the divisor. Division is repaired by compactness rather than an incorrect monotonicity assertion about
divided approximants. The inverse quotient is a uniquely determined actual psh extension on the original variety.
The relative energy is an integral of this quotient against genuine relative metric products. We identify these
products first locally, with the correct sign in the frame identity, and then prove their nonpluripolar extension, raw
mass An , unbounded and weighted decreasing limits, comparison, intrinsic target criterion, actual attainment and
uniqueness modulo constants. Every continuous direction on the original space is available with both derivative
signs. The ample equation is used solely in its exact task-granted actual ample domain.
The additional zero-volume equation proved here is exactly the actual trivial-bundle equation in positive dimension.
We distinguish a big blowup with nonpositive self-intersection from a genuinely zero-volume example with
noninjective top operator. This is a theorem about prescribed retained Kodaira references, not a class-volume or
minimal-singularity theorem, and not a general zero-volume equation. All original big rational bundles occur; no
finite-generation, cardinality, invariance or measure-support hypothesis replaces that domain. The conclusion is
a mathematical proof draft relative to an explicit task grant and published premises. Evidence processing and
compilation do not certify it.
