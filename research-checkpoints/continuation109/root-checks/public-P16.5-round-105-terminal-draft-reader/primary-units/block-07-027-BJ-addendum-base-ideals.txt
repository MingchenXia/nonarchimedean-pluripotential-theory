L159: ###### Lemma 1.5.
L160: 
L161: Suppose $\varphi\colon X^{\mathrm{an}}\to\mathbb{R}$ is bounded, with lsc regularization $\varphi_{\star}\colon X^{\mathrm{an}}\to\mathbb{R}$. Then $\qq_{L}(\varphi)=\qq_{L}(\varphi_{\star})\leq\env_{L}(\varphi_{\star})$, and equality holds outside $\mathbb{B}_{+}(L)$.
L162: 
L163: In particular, $\qq_{L}(\varphi)=\env_{L}(\varphi_{\star})$ when $L$ is ample. In this case, $\qq_{L}$ coincides with the envelope $\qq_{c_{1}(L)}$ in [【17†BJ22a】, §5.3].
L164: ###### Proof.
L165: Since any function $\psi\in\mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{gf}}(L)$ is continuous, it satisfies $\psi\leq\varphi$ iff $\psi\leq\varphi_{\star}$. Thus $\qq_{L}(\varphi)=\qq_{L}(\varphi_{\star})$, and we may therefore assume wlog that $\varphi$ is lsc. Since $\mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{gf}}(L)\subset\PSH(L)$, we trivially have $\qq_{L}(\varphi)\leq\env_{L}(\varphi)$. Conversely, pick $\psi\in\PSH(L)$ such that $\psi\leq\varphi$. Let $E$ be an effective $\mathbb{Q}$-Cartier divisor such that $A:=L-E$ is ample.
L166: By [【17†BJ22a】, Theorem 4.15], we can write $\psi$ as the pointwise limit of a decreasing net $(\psi_{j})$ in $\mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{gf}}(L+\varepsilon_{j}A)$ with $\varepsilon_{j}\to 0$. Pick $\varepsilon>0$, so that $\psi<\varphi+\varepsilon$. As in the proof of Lemma 【24†1.2】, since $\psi_{j}$ is usc and $\varphi$ is lsc, a simple variant of Dini’s lemma shows that $\psi_{j}<\varphi+\varepsilon$ for all $j$ large enough.
L167: Set $\log|s_{E}|:=m^{-1}\log|s_{mE}|$, where $s_{mE}$ is the canonical global section of $\mathcal{O}_{X}(mE)$ for any $m\geq 1$ such that $mE$ is integral. Then $\log|s_{E}|\leq 0$ lies in $\mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{gf}}(E)$, so it follows that $\tau_{j}:=(1+\varepsilon_{j})^{-1}(\psi_{j}+\varepsilon_{j}\log|s_{E}|)$ lies in $\mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{gf}}(L)$. Further,
L168: 
L169:  | $$\tau_{j}\leq(1+\varepsilon_{j})^{-1}(\varphi+\varepsilon)\leq\varphi+\varepsilon+C\varepsilon_{j}$$  |
L170: for some uniform $C>0$, since $\varphi$ is bounded, and hence
L171: 
L172:  | $$\tau_{j}\leq\qq_{L}(\varphi+\varepsilon+C\varepsilon_{j})=\qq_{L}(\varphi)+\varepsilon+C\varepsilon_{j}.$$  |
L173: 
L174: We have thus proved $\psi_{j}+\varepsilon_{j}\log|s_{E}|\leq(1+\varepsilon_{j})(\qq_{L}(\varphi)+\varepsilon+C\varepsilon_{j})$; at any point of
L175: 
L176:  | $$(X-E)^{\mathrm{an}}=\{\log|s_{E}|>-\infty\},$$  |
L177: 
L178: this yields $\psi\leq\qq_{L}(\varphi)$, and hence $\env_{L}(\varphi)\leq\qq_{L}(\varphi)$, which proves the result. ∎
L179: ### 1.3. Envelopes from test configurations
L180: 
L181: Let $L$ be a big line bundle. Any test configuration $(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{L})$ for $(X,L)$ defines a function $\varphi_{\mathcal{L}}\in\PL$, and we seek to compute the Fubini–Study envelope $\qq_{L}(\varphi_{\mathcal{L}})$.
L182: To this end, we introduce a slight generalization of the definitions in [【17†BJ22a】, §2.1]. To any $\mathbb{G}_{\mathrm{m}}$-invariant ideal $\mathfrak{a}\subset\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{X}}$, we attach a function $\varphi_{\mathfrak{a}}\colon X^{\mathrm{an}}\to[-\infty,0]$ by setting $\varphi_{\mathfrak{a}}(v):=-\sigma(v)(\mathfrak{a})$, where $\sigma=\sigma_{\mathcal{X}}$ denotes Gauss extension (see [【17†BJ22a】, Remark 1.9]).
L183: In terms of the weight decomposition $\mathfrak{a}=\sum_{\lambda\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}}\mathfrak{a}_{\lambda}\varpi^{-\lambda}$ with $\mathfrak{a}_{\lambda}\subset\mathcal{O}_{X}$, we have $\varphi_{\mathfrak{a}}=\max_{\lambda}\{\log|\mathfrak{a}_{\lambda}|+\lambda\}$.
L184: If $\mathcal{L}$ is an honest line bundle such that $\mathcal{L}\otimes\mathfrak{a}$ is globally generated, one easily checks as in [【17†BJ22a】, Proposition 2.25] that $\varphi_{\mathcal{L}}+\varphi_{\mathfrak{a}}$ lies in $\mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{gf}}_{\mathbb{Q}}(L)$.
L185: ###### Lemma 1.6.
L186: Let $L$ be a big line bundle on $X$, and $(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{L})$ an integrally closed test configuration for $(X,L)$. For each sufficiently divisible $m\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$, denote by $\mathfrak{a}_{m}\subset\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{X}}$ the base ideal of $m\mathcal{L}$, and set $\varphi_{m}:=\varphi_{\mathcal{L}}+m^{-1}\varphi_{\mathfrak{a}_{m}}$.
L187: Then $\varphi_{m}\in\mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{gf}}_{\mathbb{Q}}(L)$ and $(\varphi_{m})_{m}$ forms an increasing net of functions on $X^{\mathrm{an}}$ converging pointwise to $\qq_{L}(\varphi_{\mathcal{L}})$.
L188: Here we consider $(\varphi_{m})_{m}$ as a net indexed by the set $m_{0}\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$ for some sufficiently divisible $m_{0}$, and partially ordered by divisibility.
L189: 
L190: To prove the lemma, recall [【17†BJ22a】, §1.2] that if $\mathcal{L}$ (and hence $L$) is an honest line bundle, then $\Hnot(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{L})$ lies as a $k[\varpi]$-submodule of $\Hnot(X,L)_{k[\varpi^{\pm 1}]}$. The next result provides a valuative characterization of this submodule in terms of $\varphi_{\mathcal{L}}$.
L191: ###### Lemma 1.7.
L192: 
L193: Assume $\mathcal{L}$ is an honest line bundle, pick $s\in\Hnot(X,L)_{k[\varpi^{-\pm 1}]}$, and write $s=\sum_{\lambda\in\mathbb{Z}}s_{\lambda}\varpi^{-\lambda}$ with $s_{\lambda}\in\Hnot(X,L)$. Then $s\in\Hnot(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{L})$ iff $\max_{\lambda}\{\log|s_{\lambda}|+\lambda\}\leq\varphi_{\mathcal{L}}$ on $X^{\mathrm{an}}$.
L194: ###### Proof.
L195: 
L196: By $\mathbb{G}_{\mathrm{m}}$-invariance, we have $s\in\Hnot(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{L})\Leftrightarrow s_{\lambda}\varpi^{-\lambda}\in\Hnot(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{L})$ for all $\lambda\in\mathbb{Z}$, and we may thus assume $s=s_{\lambda}\varpi^{-\lambda}$ for some $\lambda\in\mathbb{Z}$.
L197: Since $\mathcal{X}$ is integrally closed, we have $\rho_{\star}\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{X}^{\prime}}=\mathcal{O}_{\mathcal{X}}$, and hence $\Hnot(\mathcal{X}^{\prime},\rho^{\star}\mathcal{L})=\Hnot(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{L})$, for any higher test configuration $\rho\colon\mathcal{X}^{\prime}\to\mathcal{X}$ (see the proof of [【17†BJ22a】, Proposition 2.30]).
L198: After pulling back $\mathcal{L}$ to a higher test configuration, we may thus assume that $\mathcal{X}$ dominates the trivial test configuration via $\mu\colon\mathcal{X}\to\mathcal{X}_{\mathrm{triv}}$. Set $D:=\mathcal{L}-\mu^{\star}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{triv}}$, so that $\varphi_{\mathcal{L}}=\varphi_{D}$. Viewed as a rational section of $\mathcal{L}$, $s$ is regular outside $\mathcal{X}_{0}$. For any $v\in X^{\mathrm{an}}$ with Gauss extension $w=\sigma(v)$, we further have
L199:  | $$w(s)=v(s_{\lambda})-\lambda+w(D)=-\log|s_{\lambda}|(v)-\lambda+\varphi_{D}(v).$$  |
L200: If $s$ is a regular section, then $w(s)\geq 0$, and hence $\log|s_{\lambda}|(v)+\lambda\leq\varphi_{D}(v)$ for any $v\in X^{\mathrm{an}}$. Conversely, the latter condition implies $b_{E}^{-1}\ord_{E}(s)=-\log|s_{\lambda}|(v_{E})-\lambda+\varphi_{D}(v_{E})\geq 0$ for each irreducible component $E$ of $\mathcal{X}_{0}$, since $\sigma(v_{E})=b_{E}^{-1}\ord_{E}$; this yields, as desired, $s\in\Hnot(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{L})$ (compare [【17†BJ22a】, Lemma 1.23]). ∎
L201: ###### Proof of Lemma 【25†1.6】.
L202: 
L203: Replacing $L$ and $\mathcal{L}$ by sufficiently divisible multiples, we may assume that $L$ and $\mathcal{L}$ are honest line bundles.
L204: 
L205: We have $\mathfrak{a}_{m}\cdot\mathfrak{a}_{m^{\prime}}\subset\mathfrak{a}_{m+m^{\prime}}$ for all $m,m^{\prime}\in\mathbb{N}$. This implies that the net $(\varphi_{m})_{m}$ is increasing.
L206: By definition of $\mathfrak{a}_{m}$, $m\mathcal{L}\otimes\mathfrak{a}_{m}$ is globally generated. As noted above, this implies $\varphi_{m\mathcal{L}}+\varphi_{\mathfrak{a}_{m}}\in\mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{gf}}_{\mathbb{Q}}(mL)$, and hence $\varphi_{m}\in\mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{gf}}_{\mathbb{Q}}(L)$. Since $\varphi_{\mathfrak{a}_{m}}\leq 0$, we further have $\varphi_{m}\leq\varphi_{\mathcal{L}}$, and hence $\varphi_{m}\leq\qq_{L}(\varphi_{\mathcal{L}})$, see (【26†1.1】).
L207: Conversely, pick $\psi\in\mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{gf}}_{\mathbb{Q}}(L)$ such that $\psi\leq\varphi_{\mathcal{L}}$, and write $\psi=\frac{1}{m}\max_{i}\{\log|s_{i}|+\lambda_{i}\}$ for a finite set of nonzero sections $s_{i}\in\Hnot(X,mL)$ and $\lambda_{i}\in\mathbb{Z}$. For each $i$, we then have $\log|s_{i}|+\lambda_{i}\leq m\varphi_{\mathcal{L}}=\varphi_{m\mathcal{L}}$, and hence $s_{i}\varpi^{-\lambda_{i}}\in\Hnot(\mathcal{X},m\mathcal{L})$, see Lemma 【27†1.7】.
L208: Since $\mathfrak{a}_{m}$ is locally generated by $\Hnot(\mathcal{X},m\mathcal{L})$, this implies in turn $\log|s_{i}|+\lambda_{i}\leq\varphi_{m\mathcal{L}}+\varphi_{\mathfrak{a}_{m}}$, and hence $\psi\leq\varphi_{m}$. Taking the supremum over $\psi$, we conclude, as desired, $\qq_{L}(\varphi_{\mathcal{L}})\leq\sup_{m}\varphi_{m}$. ∎
