Theorem 6.21. Assume that X is normal. Pick θ ∈ N1 (X) and E an effective Q-Cartier
divisor. For any ψ ∈ PSHhom (θ), we then have
                       ψ ≤ log |sE | ⇐⇒ ψ − log |sE | ∈ PSHhom (θ − E).
  See Example 6.10 for the notation.

Proof. By Corollary 6.17, we can write ψ as the limit of a decreasing net ψi ∈ Hhom (Li )
with Li ∈ Pic(X)Q and c1 (Li ) → θ. Since K := {log |sE | = −1} is compact in X an and
supK ψ ≤ −1, we can find ti ∈ Q>0 such that limi ti = 1 and supK ψi ≤ −ti , by Dini’s
lemma. By homogeneity, this implies ψi ≤ ti log |sE | on R>0 K = {log |sE | > −∞}, and
hence on X an , since {log |sE | > −∞} ⊃ X div is dense and ψi and log |sE | are continuous (or
by Theorem 4.22).
   By Lemma 2.23, ψi0 := ψi − ti log |sE | = ψi − log |sti E | lies in Hhom (Li − ti E). Since (ψi )
is a decreasing net, (ψi0 ) is decreasing on X div ⊂ {log |sE | > −∞}, and hence on X an , by
Theorem 4.22. Further, ψi (vtriv,α ) = 0 for all α and all i. Since c1 (Li ) − ti E → θ − E,
Theorem 4.5 shows that ψ 0 := limi ψi0 is (θ − E)-psh, with ψ = ψ 0 + log |sE | on X div , and
hence on X an , by Corollary 4.23.                                                               
