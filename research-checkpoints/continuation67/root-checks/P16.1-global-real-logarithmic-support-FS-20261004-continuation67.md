# Direct global affine FS regularization of real logarithmic supports

Unreviewed root proof proposal, 2026-10-04. This resolves, as an elementary
construction to be independently checked, the real-slope obligation retained
in continuation66's global entropy approximation. It does not use the
unproved identification of arbitrary singular analytic PL functions with
equivariant model PL functions. No original closure is asserted and no
feedback is sent to an active worker.

Let R=k[z_1,...,z_r] be any reduced finitely generated affine coordinate
algebra over trivially valued k. These z_i are an algebra-generating list;
they need not be algebraically independent. Set

    v=max(0,log|z_1|,...,log|z_r|)>=0.

This is actual affine FS, with generators and rational floor. For finitely
many nonzero f_i in R, polynomial degree bounds give positive integers d_i
such that log|f_i|<=d_i v everywhere. The bound follows from the
non-Archimedean triangle inequality and |c|=1 for nonzero coefficients.
It remains valid at zeros, where log|f_i|=-infinity.

Fix real alpha_i>0 and a real C, and put

    s=C+sum_i alpha_i log|f_i|.

Choose positive rational q_(i,m) increasing to alpha_i, rational B_m
decreasing to A=sum_i d_i alpha_i from strictly above, and rational C_m
decreasing to C. Put

    b_m=B_m-sum_i d_i q_(i,m)>0,
    s_m=C_m+sum_i q_(i,m)log|f_i|+b_m v.

All displayed coefficients of s_m are rational. At points where every f_i
is nonzero,

    s_m-s=(C_m-C)+sum_i(alpha_i-q_(i,m))(d_i v-log|f_i|)
                      +(B_m-A)v>=0.

At their zeros both s_m and s are -infinity. Furthermore

    s_m-s_(m+1)=(C_m-C_(m+1))+(B_m-B_(m+1))v
       +sum_i(q_(i,m+1)-q_(i,m))(d_i v-log|f_i|)>=0

where the finite difference is meaningful; at zeros monotonicity follows
from the common -infinity value instead of subtracting infinities. Thus
s_m decreases globally to s. This deals with both positive and negative
log|f_i| using a vanishing positive multiple of one fixed global exhaustion.
One-sided coefficient approximation alone would not do so.

To make the approximants finite and ensure the exact generator condition,
take positive rational eps_m decreasing to zero and set

    H_m=max(s_m, eps_m v-m).

Both arguments decrease, so H_m decreases to s, including logarithmic
poles. The second argument expands into the branches -m and
eps_m log|z_j|-m. Hence the original coordinate generators z_j explicitly
occur with positive rational coefficients, and -m is a rational floor.
The first argument expands using the branches of v. Clear a common
denominator in each finite sum to write each branch as a positive rational
multiple of the logarithm of a regular monomial in the f_i,z_j plus a
rational constant. Constants and zero branches cause no obstruction.
Consequently every H_m belongs to the **actual affine FS class**, not
merely the local rational tropical PL class. All terms are finite after
the maximum with the generator floor, on the entire affine analytification.

This proves direct decreasing global FS regularizability of every finite
positive real linear combination of log-norms of regular functions plus
a real constant. If all terms are omitted, the same generator-floor
construction approximates the constant C using C_m decreasing to C.

Now take the strict supporting expression in Wu6.7's countable barrier.
Its selected finite entropy support at the contact point is

    ell=C+sum_(i in I)alpha_i log|f_i|, alpha_i>0.

The support coefficients vanish for coordinates whose norm vanishes at the
contact point; those terms are omitted. Thus ell is finite at contact,
and is generically finite even though it may be -infinity elsewhere.
Let rho_j be the finite entropy barriers, gamma_j positive rational, and
gamma_j rho_j<=2^(-j)(v+1). Continuation66 proves **global uniform** rational
FS approximants for every rho_j and a global decreasing FS approximation
of the series. Omitting one fixed j has the same proof: include the tail
2^(-N)(v+1), and the difference of successive upper partial sums remains
nonnegative. Therefore the remainder sum_(i!=j)gamma_i rho_i has a global
decreasing actual FS sequence K_m.

For the selected support, H_m decreases to ell by the construction above.
Then gamma_j H_m+K_m decreases globally to

    rho'=gamma_j ell+sum_(i!=j)gamma_i rho_i.

A positive rational combination of affine FS functions is affine FS:
distribute their finite maxima and clear denominators for mixed branches.
Branches obtained by choosing the constant floor in every other factor
retain the original coordinate generators of each factor with positive
rational coefficients. These single-generator branches may be kept in
their original form rather than unnecessarily replacing z_j by a power.
The union of these lists still generates R. Thus the displayed sequence
has rational constants, a rational floor and a genuine algebra-generating
list. The continuous remainder is finite everywhere, so no infinity-minus-
infinity operation is involved.

The contact equality and strict separation outside the prescribed open
neighborhood were proved by the entropy gap argument of continuation64/66.
This direct construction now supplies the additional **global affine FS**
membership without a singular PL-exhaustion gluing lemma or singular EP.
It applies even to the nonnormal affine algebras in the original unibranch
problem, with the source's corrected upper-growth convention. An independent
paper must still verify the exact use in the localization/Runge induction,
including the pole-order hull repair from continuation65 and the curve
trace boundary replacement from continuation66. Those separate obligations
are not proved merely by the present membership construction.
