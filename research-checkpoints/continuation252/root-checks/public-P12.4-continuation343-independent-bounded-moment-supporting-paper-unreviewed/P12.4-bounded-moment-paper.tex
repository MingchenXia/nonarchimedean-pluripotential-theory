\begin{theorem}[The bounded algebraic supporting theorem]
Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be excellent reduced local of dimension $n\ge1$,
let $\mathfrak a$ be proper $\mathfrak m$-primary, and let $X_{\mathfrak a}$
be the entire normalized centered semivaluation section. Let $C,D$ be the
exact geometric classes of qualified G331, with $D$ defined by actual
decreasing $C$-nets. Set $p=n-1$. There is a unique supporting moment
construction on bounded $p$-tuples in $D$, specified on rational profiles by
\[
 \int_{X_{\mathfrak a}}v(J)\,d\mu_{-v(I_1)/k_1,\ldots,-v(I_p)/k_p}
 =\frac{e_R(I_1,\ldots,I_p,J)}{k_1\cdots k_p}.
\]
It depends on input functions, is symmetric and additive with nonnegative
real homogeneity, is weakly continuous under uniform $C$ convergence and
under actual decreasing bounded $D$-nets whose specified limit belongs to
$D$, and has mass bounded by $M^p e_R(\mathfrak a)$ when
$-M\le u_i\le0$. For $d(v)=v_{\mathfrak a}(\mathfrak b)$ and
$T(v)=v/d(v)$,
\[
 u_{i,\mathfrak b}(Tv)=u_{i,\mathfrak a}(v)/d(v),\qquad
 \mu_{\mathfrak b}=T_*\bigl(d\mu_{\mathfrak a}\bigr).
\]
For the finite normalization $S$ and its local centers $q$,
\[
 \mu_{R,\mathfrak a}(u)=
 \sum_{\dim S_q=n}[\kappa(q):\kappa(\mathfrak m)]\,
 (\rho_q)_*\mu_{S_q,\mathfrak aS_q}(\rho_q^*u).
\]
All support primes and conductor fibres are retained. In dimension one
this is the zero-potential reference measure
\[
 \sum_q[\kappa(q):\kappa(\mathfrak m)]\operatorname{ord}_q(\mathfrak a)
 \delta_{\operatorname{ord}_q/\operatorname{ord}_q(\mathfrak a)}.
\]
Dimension zero has only the empty boundary and zero measure, with no
negative-arity or multiplicity-moment assertion. At a smooth affine closed
germ over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, this operator
is the bounded published BFJ operator, on all semivaluations.
\end{theorem}
