# Global entropy approximation and decreasing affine FS series

Unreviewed root argument, 2026-10-04. This continues the continuation64
strict-support barrier and continuation65 local-source diagnostics. It is
not an accepted premise, is not supplied to the active native workers, and
does not close an original problem. The new point below is uniformity on
the entire tropical space, rather than only compact subsets.

Let R be a reduced affine coordinate algebra over a trivially valued field,
and let f_1,...,f_r be nonzero elements containing an algebra-generating
list. Put x_i=log|f_i|, allowing -infinity, and fix positive rational a.
For finite x define

    chi(x)=a^(-1) log(1+sum_i exp(a x_i)).

On the compact simplex Delta={p_i>=0, sum_(i=0)^r p_i=1}, set
F(p)=a^(-1)sum_(i=0)^r p_i log p_i, with 0 log 0=0 and x_0=0.
The finite-dimensional Gibbs variational formula is

    chi(x)=max_(p in Delta)(sum_(i=1)^r p_i x_i-F(p)).

Choose a rational triangulation of Delta of mesh tending to zero, including
all boundary faces and vertices, and let p_j be its vertices. Define

    F_eps(p)=inf{sum_j lambda_j F(p_j):
                lambda_j>=0, sum_j lambda_j=1, sum_j lambda_j p_j=p}.

Convexity of F gives F<=F_eps. Barycentric coordinates in a small simplex
and uniform continuity of F on Delta give F_eps<=F+eps, uniformly on Delta.
Its dual is the finite maximum

    h_eps(x)=max_j(sum_i p_(j,i) x_i-F(p_j)).

Consequently h_eps<=chi<=h_eps+eps **for every finite x**, with no bound
on x. Round each intercept -F(p_j) downward to a rational within eps.
The resulting h_Q satisfies h_Q<=chi<=h_Q+2eps on the entire space.
The vertex intercepts are exactly zero and are left unchanged. The same
inequalities extend when some x_i=-infinity: simplex faces with those
coordinates zero are included, and every branch with positive coefficient
on such a coordinate is -infinity. Equivalently take finite x_i downward
to -infinity in the finite-max formula and in chi.

Each branch has nonnegative rational coefficients. Clearing a common
denominator D expresses it as

    D^(-1)log|prod_i f_i^(D p_(j,i))|+lambda_j, lambda_j rational.

The branches from simplex vertices include zero and each log|f_i|. Thus
this is an actual affine FS function with a rational floor and an
algebra-generating list; neither a local-model representation theorem nor
a projective envelope is used. Normalize the errors so that a function
h_j obeys chi-e_j<=h_j<=chi globally, e_j=4^(-j). Then

    g_j=h_j+2 e_j

decreases to chi globally: g_j>=chi+e_j, whereas
g_(j+1)<=chi+2e_(j+1)<=chi+e_j/2<=g_j. Additive rational shifts preserve
the floor and generators. This proves direct decreasing global affine FS
regularizability of each finite entropy barrier even on singular R.

For the countable construction, let rho_j>=0 be these finite barriers,
and let v>=0 be a fixed actual affine FS exhaustion. Polynomial degree
bounds give rho_j<=C_j(v+1). Choose positive rational gamma_j so that

    gamma_j rho_j<=2^(-j)(v+1).

The series rho=sum_j gamma_j rho_j converges locally uniformly. Set

    U_N=sum_(j<=N)gamma_j rho_j+2^(-N)(v+1).

The geometric-series tail implies U_N>=rho and U_N decreases to rho,
because U_N-U_(N+1)=2^(-N-1)(v+1)-gamma_(N+1)rho_(N+1)>=0.
For each N the finitely many summands admit global uniform FS
approximants. A positive rational linear combination of affine FS functions
is affine FS, by distributing the finite maxima and clearing denominators;
the zero floor and algebra generators remain available. Choose W_N actual
FS with U_N-e_N<=W_N<=U_N, e_N=4^(-N), by summing appropriately accurate
approximants. Then V_N=W_N+2e_N decreases to rho. Indeed

    V_(N+1)<=U_(N+1)+2e_(N+1)<=U_N+e_N/2<=V_N.

This gives a **global decreasing** FS sequence for the infinite barrier.
It does not infer regularizability from an increasing psh limit, a property
which would risk using the still-unproved singular envelope theorem.

For the strict supporting function in Wu6.7, only one finite barrier is
replaced by its entropy support; the rest of the series is retained. The
support has real nonnegative coefficients in log|f_i|. The argument above
does not automatically make this real-slope support globally rational-FS
regularizable: log|f_i| can have either sign and arbitrary magnitude, so
one-sided rational coefficient approximation on the entire affine space
does not work. On bounded-above coordinate neighborhoods one can use
coefficients increasing to the real coefficients in log|f_i|-B_i and
decreasing rational intercepts, with a decreasing floor. Globalizing those
local approximants needs a separate valid model/PSH argument. This is an
explicit remaining obligation, not a premise supplied by the finite barrier.

This independent argument should be reconstructed and checked together
with the exact affine FS definition before publication. The arbitrary
completed-residue-field curve step identified by the completed round56
author is a separate issue, unaffected by this elementary approximation.
