# n5. Canonical flattening, an actual primary model and the raw model envelope

Use qualified G331 and n2. Let k be perfect of characteristic p>0, B a normal integral affine finite-type k-algebra, and x a CLOSED point of codimension d>=2. Shrink so B minus x is regular. Fix an x-primary gauge a and ANY finite collection of primary ideals including a. Blow up their product and normalize finitely. All factors become invertible: if I_1...I_s=(f) in a local domain, multiply I_i by f^{-1} times the other factors. Their product is the unit ideal. A summand of an expression for 1 is a unit in the local ring, and division by it shows I_i is principal with a nonzero generator. The universal property gives all prescribed principalizations. The pulled product defines a full-fibre effective Cartier F_0 with O(-F_0) ample.

On this normal, possibly non-Cohen--Macaulay model the canonical sheaf is coherent torsionfree rank one, invertible on the regular puncture. Take G=Fitt_1(omega). It is unit off the full fibre. Wherever G is unit the module is cyclic; torsionfree rank one over a local domain makes its nonzero cyclic generator free. Thus the outside-V(G) hypothesis of15.27.4 holds, including any such points on the fibre. On each chart R'=R[G/a_0] subset R_{a_0}, current Stacks15.27.3 makes the strict transform M'=(M tensor R')/(a_0-power torsion) have Fitt_1=R'. Its Fitt_0 vanishes after inverting a_0, where the module is free of rank one, and hence vanishes in R', which injects into R_{a_0}. The Fitting local-freeness criterion makes M' invertible. Finite normalization preserves that line bundle Lscr.

Choose ONE rational identification of Lscr with the canonical sheaf on the puncture, and use the SAME rational section to define Cartier L_0 and canonical Weil K_Y. Only then is K_Y-L_0 supported on the fibre. The pullback of F_0 has positive coefficients at every fibre prime. Choose integer r with rF_0>=K_Y-L_0, put Delta=rF_0-(K_Y-L_0)>=0. Then L=K_Y+Delta=L_0+rF_0 is Cartier. No resolution or smooth Y has been constructed.

Let E_G be the effective Fitting-blowup Cartier divisor (possibly zero). O(-E_G) tensor O(-bF_0) is ample over B for large b, by composition of relative ampleness; finite normalization preserves it. A sufficiently high power is generated and very ample. Write it O(-F), with F effective and supported on the ENTIRE fibre, and J=Gamma(Y,O(-F)). Normal codimension-two Hartogs puts these sections in B. Coherence makes J finite. Localization makes it unit off x. It is proper, since its generated pullback vanishes on the nonempty full fibre. Its radical is m_x, so it contains a primary power. Generation gives J O_Y=O(-F)=A exactly.

This is an ACTUAL primary blowup, not just a projective modification. Choose finitely many generators of J containing the immersion sections. The evaluation map gives a closed immersion into projective space over B. On the puncture it is the graph of these generators. The image is integral and equals its scheme-theoretic dense graph closure. The Rees blowup Bl_J has the same closure, since its Rees algebra is the domain subalgebra B[Jt] and its punctured graph is dense. Thus Y=Bl_J B scheme-theoretically. All previous principalizations remain. This does not assert cofinality for arbitrary proper birational models.

Two corrections are now uniformly bounded. Delta<=bF as Weil divisors for some b. The Jacobian ideal Jac(Y/k) is unit on the puncture because regular finite-type schemes over a PERFECT field are smooth. Its coherent quotient has support inside the full fibre, so J^a O_Y subset Jac(Y/k) for some a. Also a^{N_J} subset J. At every normalized semivaluation, including nonzero supports, 0<=v(Jac)<=a v(J)<=a N_J and v(bF)=b v(J). These bounds are primary, not nonprimary conductor estimates.

Fix ANY integral Cartier C supported on this fibre, with either sign. Evaluation first passes to Frac(B/support(v)). Its generic point is on the puncture; properness and a finite-valued primary blowup denominator give a centre. Pull O(C) with its generic trivialization to the valuation ring and put f_C=-v(its generator). Actual Cartier order bounds by the principalized gauge make it finite. G331 gives its continuity on all supports; no homomorphism Frac(B)->Frac(B/support(v)) is assumed.

For every m>=1 set I_m=Gamma(Y,O(mC)) and b_m=I_m O_Y tensor O(-mC). Hartogs makes I_m an actual ideal of B. It is unit off x and contains a^{Mm} for one fixed integer M with C>=-M E_a. Hence it is unit or primary, nonzero for EVERY m. Coherence does not imply finite generation of the graded algebra. The b_m are actual nonzero graded ideals, generated after twisting by O(mC) by the global generators of I_m; they are unit off the fibre. At all supports u_m=-v(I_m)/m=f_C-v(b_m)/m<=f_C. Ideal multiplication makes v(I_m) subadditive. The all-index Fekete lemma yields u=lim_m u_m=sup_m u_m, unchanged along multiples of any fixed index. The sequence need not be increasing.

The raw C/D envelope of f_C is u BEFORE any recognition of positivity. Each u_m is a competitor. If -v(I)/q<=f_C, comparison on every fibre prime divisor of normal Y gives I^r O_Y subset O(rqC), for every r>=1; at other height-one primes orders are automatically nonnegative because I is in B and C is zero. Normal height-one intersection then gives I^r subset I_{rq}, and -v(I)/q<=u_{rq}<=u. Approximate a C competitor rationally and subtract a small rational gauge constant to preserve its constraint; let the error vanish. For a D competitor use its decreasing continuous representation and the decreasing compact closed sets {h_alpha>=f_C+epsilon}: empty intersection implies one is empty. The selected h_alpha-epsilon is a C competitor and h<=u+epsilon. Therefore p(f_C)=u on the full section. Fekete alone supplies neither continuity nor geometric positivity.
