# n3. Relative regularity reconstructed over a singular affine base

Let Y be projective over a Noetherian affine scheme, A an ample globally generated line bundle, and M coherent. Put d=dim Y. Suppose H^i(Y,M tensor A^{-i})=0 for 1<=i<=d. Then M is generated by its GLOBAL sections. This statement requires no smoothness, Cohen--Macaulayness, flatness over the base or field base.

Choose finitely many global sections giving O_Y^r onto A. Their Koszul complex is locally contractible because they generate the unit ideal after trivializing A. It is therefore exact even after tensoring by an arbitrary coherent M. Let Reg(t) denote H^i(M tensor A^{t-i})=0 for all i>0. Reg(0) is the hypothesis plus the usual dimension bound for coherent cohomology on a Noetherian scheme. Twist the Koszul complex by M tensor A^{t+1-i}. The terms preceding the target are copies of M tensor A^{t-i-j}, j>=0. Their H^{i+j} vanish by Reg(t). Successive short exact sequences of kernels embed H^i of the target into the next higher cohomology of each kernel, ending in zero. This proves Reg(t+1), by induction for every t>=0.

Twist the same complex by M tensor A^{t+1}. The obstruction to surjectivity of H^0(M tensor A^t)^r onto H^0(M tensor A^{t+1}) lies in H^1 of the first kernel. Repeated kernel sequences kill it using H^j(M tensor A^{t-j})=0 for j>=1, which is Reg(t). Thus multiplication by the chosen sections surjects in every degree t>=0. Iteration expresses every section of M tensor A^t as products of those sections with sections of M. Ordinary Serre generation makes M tensor A^t globally generated for large t. At any stalk the generated image is then contained in the image of H^0(M) tensor A^t; hence H^0(M) generates M after untwisting. This proves the full relative theorem, including its immediate proof.

In particular, if T_1,...,T_s are finitely many coherent sheaves and B_i finitely many ample line bundles, Serre vanishing gives the stated hypotheses for sufficiently large powers in each application. The thresholds can depend on T_j, while the exponent used to choose the ample B_i can be fixed. This distinction is essential in n6.

GJKM4.6 explicitly assumes Y smooth over a perfect field and projective over an affine finite-type base; its immediate proof refers to relative regularity and corrected Keeler. I use it only to identify the issue, not to apply its theorem to singular Y. The argument above replaces the relative-regularity dependency; no unread whole BS book proof is claimed. Ordinary coherent cohomology, its dimension bound, Serre vanishing/generation and locally split Koszul exactness are explicit background at these hypotheses.
