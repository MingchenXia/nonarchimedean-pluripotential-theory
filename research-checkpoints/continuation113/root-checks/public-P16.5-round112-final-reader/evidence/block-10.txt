# Obtaining the retained modification on every original proper carrier

Root continuation112, 5 October2026. This is an unreviewed geometric
application audit for proposal109. It does not certify its analytic
theorem or close original P16.2. Active native109 was not inspected or
given this note as feedback.

Let k be any algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, with its
trivial absolute value, and let X be a smooth integral proper k-variety.
There is no countability or projectivity hypothesis on X or k. Here a
variety is finite type, so the use of noetherian resolution below does
not introduce an extra restriction on the original carrier.

## 1. A projective integral Chow modification

Stacks0200 and0201, in the exact primary units already read and bound for
109, give Chow's lemma for the proper original X. The resulting proper
modification Z->X is an isomorphism over a dense open and has an immersion
into projective space over k. Since Z is proper over k and projective
space is separated over k, this immersion is proper, hence closed. Thus
Z is projective over k. If necessary take the reduced irreducible component
dominating X. It is a closed projective integral subscheme and still
agrees with that original dense open. Denote it again by Z. The morphism
Z->X is actual, proper and birational, rather than an abstract
identification of two function spaces.

## 2. Exact resolution hypotheses

Temkin0904.1592v2, Theorem1.2.1, is stated for noetherian quasi-excellent
generically reduced schemes over Spec(Q). It supplies a finite blowup
sequence, regular centers away from the preimage of the regular locus,
and regular final scheme. The printed main proof and the finite
stabilization Lemma4.1.3 were read in the supplied literal dictionary,
and checked again in the actual downloaded v2 text. This does not assert
that the whole35-page paper or every recursively cited lemma was read.
In particular its variety-resolution algorithm is an external input to
the paper; that background is not a new theorem supplied by this note.

The actual Z satisfies every relevant hypothesis. A field is excellent,
and any finite-type algebra over a field is excellent (Stacks07QW).
Finite-type affine patches of Z are therefore noetherian and
quasi-excellent. Z is integral, hence generically reduced. Characteristic
zero gives its structural map to Spec(Q). There is no need for Z to be
finite type over Q: the theorem asks for quasi-excellence and a map to Q,
and those have just been checked. The field k may have arbitrarily large
transcendence degree and cardinality.

The output Y is regular, integral and finite type over k. Integrality
of each blowup follows by writing the Rees algebra of a nonzero ideal
of an integral ring as a subring of its polynomial ring. The centers
avoid a dense regular open, so each blowup is birational and does not
remove the generic point. A characteristic-zero field is perfect.
Stacks0B8X therefore makes regularity of this finite-type reduced Y
equivalent to smoothness over k. Hence Y is smooth.

Stacks07QU also states preservation of quasi-excellence under localization,
but its printed proof explicitly omits the localization part. That
unprinted proof is not represented as read or needed here: a cover by
finite-type affine k-algebras already suffices for quasi-excellence of Z.
The inspected proof of07QW invokes earlier G-ring and J-2 results; a
reading of their full proofs is not asserted either.

## 3. Why the final scheme is actually projective

The finite sequence in Temkin's definition2.2.1--2.2.2 consists of ordinary
blowups along fixed coherent ideal sheaves. Projectivity can be checked
on the actual projective carrier at every step without assuming that
every proper original X has an ample bundle.

Suppose the current scheme Z_i is projective over k and I is its coherent
center ideal. Choose an ample integral H_i. For a large m, I tensor H_i^m
is globally generated (Stacks01Q3, the previously bound exact global
generation unit). Thus H_i^(-m) direct-sum r surjects onto I for a finite r.
Taking symmetric powers gives a surjection of graded algebras onto the
Rees algebra direct-sum I^j. Relative Proj realizes the blowup as a closed
subscheme of P_{Z_i}(H_i^(-m) direct-sum r), which is isomorphic to
Z_i times P^{r-1}_k. The blowup is therefore projective over k. Empty
centers give the identity and cause no difficulty. Induction through the
finite blowup sequence proves that Y is projective over k.

The composite pi:Y->Z->X is proper and birational. This proves existence
of the smooth projective modification retained by proposal109 for EVERY
smooth integral proper original X under the stated field assumptions.

## 4. The replacement positivity datum is nonempty

For completeness, the subsequent algebraic construction of109 can now
be applied without a hidden projectivity assumption on X. Choose an
effective very ample Cartier divisor H on Y avoiding all exceptional
prime divisors of pi. There are finitely many such primes. Each imposes
a proper linear condition on the finite-dimensional global section space;
their finite union cannot fill it over the infinite field k. Let B=pi_*H.
It is effective Cartier on smooth X. Since a proper birational map onto
normal X is an isomorphism at its codimension-one points (Stacks0BFP),
the strict-transform coefficients of pi^*B equal those of H. The pullback
pi^*B is effective and H contains no exceptional prime, so

    pi^*B=H+E, with E effective and exceptional.

The actual pushforward identity pi_*O_Y=O_X (Stacks0AY8) and the
projection formula identify H^0(X,mB) with H^0(Y,m pi^*B). Multiplication
by the section of mE embeds H^0(Y,mH) in that space. The positive leading
coefficient of the ample Hilbert polynomial proves growth at least c m^n,
so B is a big Cartier line bundle on the actual nonprojective X.

For ANY retained big rational L on X, clearing denominators and applying
the same pushforward identity gives the equality of section spaces for
L and pi^*L, hence pi^*L is big on projective Y. The no-Bertini
hyperplane-restriction proof107 then gives a decomposition pi^*L=A+D
with A ample rational and D effective rational Cartier. That proof uses
finite associated primes and a regular section on a possibly nonreduced
hyperplane, so no unmentioned smooth-hyperplane choice is required.

Consequently the retained data (L,pi:Y->X,A,D) proposed for the
nonprojective axis exist on every carrier and for every actual big L in
the declared domain. Different data remain different equation theories;
no independence of the chosen modification or decomposition is inferred
from this existence proof. Original-carrier psh descent, genuine metric
products, all energy limits, all Radon targets and the actual equation
are separate proof obligations.
