# Direct nonample FS foundations without extended-valued flattening

Retain a smooth integral projective X over an algebraically closed,
trivially valued characteristic-zero field of arbitrary cardinality.
Only the exact actual-ample theorem accepted99 is a task grant. For any
numerical class theta use the original BJ definition: genuine functions
are generically finite decreasing-net limits of actual generically finite

No varying-class decreasing closure theorem is used below.

## 1. Rational coefficients directly within the defining net

Let phi_i=m_i^(-1)max_j(log||s_ij||+lambda_ij), where lambda_ij are real.
For every delta>0 round each of this finite family's coefficients upward
to a rational coefficient with error at most m_i delta. Denote the
result by f_i,delta. It is an actual rational-coefficient FS function
in the SAME actual L_i and satisfies, at every point,

    phi_i <= f_i,delta <= phi_i+delta.

At a common zero both sides are minus infinity and these non-strict
inequalities remain valid. Now put g_i,delta=f_i,delta+delta, taking
positive rational delta so g_i,delta still has rational coefficients.
Order pairs by i>=i' and g_i,delta<=g_i',delta' everywhere. This is
directed: choose i3 above two given i's and rational delta3 with
2delta3<=min(delta1,delta2). Then

    g_i3,delta3 <= phi_i3+2delta3
               <= min(phi_i1,phi_i2)+min(delta1,delta2)
               <= min(g_i1,delta1,g_i2,delta2).

All inequalities also hold at common poles. The resulting g-net decreases
pointwise to phi: first choose i approximating its desired point value,
then delta small; at a minus-infinity value use an arbitrary finite
threshold. Its line-bundle classes still tend to theta because the
pair order refines the original i order. This gives genuine rational
FS approximation directly. No compact strict-shift argument or nested
decreasing closure is needed.

## 2. Every such numerical function lies in a fixed actual ample cone

Choose an actual rational ample H with c1(H)-theta ample. Eventually
the ACTUAL rational bundle H-L_i is ample. A generating family of a
sufficiently divisible power of H-L_i has logarithmic section maximum0

phi_i by these generators after clearing degrees. The resulting actual
H-section maximum is identically phi_i. Hence the defining decreasing
tail is a fixed actual-H net, and phi belongs to the genuine ample

Consequently the ample grant supplies finite divisorial values, generic

divisorial inequalities to any usc obstacle for every numerical phi in
this definition. This application does not need a nonample
regularization or closure theorem. Different theta's may require
different H's. Finite groups of functions can be placed in one H by
taking it sufficiently positive.

## 3. Direct homogeneous approximation

For a function phi with nonnegative generic maximum define

    hat(phi)(v)=inf_{t>0} phi(t v)/t,

where scaling t v scales the valuation and keeps its support. This
operator preserves pointwise order. If phi_i decreases to phi, then

    inf_i hat(phi_i)(v)
      =inf_i inf_t phi_i(t v)/t
      =inf_t inf_i phi_i(t v)/t
      =hat(phi)(v).

The two infima commute for arbitrary directed nets and extended values.
Each approximant can be chosen rational by Section1, still with
nonnegative generic maximum. For

    phi=m^(-1)max_j(log||s_j||+lambda_j), lambda_j rational,

hat(phi) is an ACTUAL homogeneous FS section maximum in the same
rational bundle. Here is the finite-dimensional proof. For finite real
x_j and max lambda_j>=0,

    inf_{r>=0} max_j(x_j+r lambda_j)
      =max_{w in W} sum_j w_j x_j,

where W is the finite set of vertices of

    {w_j>=0, sum w_j=1, sum w_j lambda_j>=0}.

One can verify the equality without a general duality black box. Each
weighted expression is at most the displayed max for every r. A
minimum of that convex piecewise-linear function occurs at r=0, at a
breakpoint with active slopes of opposite signs, or is its limit as
r tends to infinity. At r=0 choose an active nonnegative slope. At an
interior minimum take weights on two active slopes with weighted slope0.
In the final case max lambda=0 and choose an active zero slope. These
are vertices of the displayed polytope and give equality. Its vertices
are unit vectors of nonnegative slope, and the two-slope zero averages;
all weights are rational for rational lambda.

Clear a common denominator b of these finitely many w's. The monomials
product_j s_j^(b w_j) are sections of mbL. Their logarithmic maximum
divided by mb is exactly hat(phi). Coefficients zero in a monomial
mean that its corresponding section is absent from the product. The
same formula holds if some x_j=-infinity: replace them by max(x_j,-N),
commute the two infima over N and r, and pass through the fixed finite
maximum of products. Thus it includes all base-locus points.

The generic value of hat(phi_i) is0. The homogeneous functions decrease
to hat(phi); their actual bundle classes converge to theta. Therefore
hat(phi) is genuine by the original definition. If h is already
homogeneous, hat(h)=h. This proves exactly the nonample homogeneous
approximation required in draft105, without BJ4.15 or BJ4.6.

## 4. Direct decomposition by homogeneous components

For an arbitrary genuine phi with C=phi(v_triv), put

    h_lambda=hat(phi-lambda), lambda<C.

Section3 makes h_lambda a genuine homogeneous theta-psh function with
generic value0. Taking t=1 gives h_lambda+lambda<=phi. At every actual
point v we claim

    phi(v)=sup_{lambda<C}(h_lambda(v)+lambda).

The proof is one-dimensional. Along its scaling ray put g(t)=phi(t v).
Each defining FS approximant is convex and decreasing in t>0 and is
bounded above by its generic value. Its decreasing limit g is either
finite convex on (0,infinity), or identically minus infinity there.
Indeed a minus-infinity value forces minus infinity at all smaller t
by convexity with a finite upper-bound point, and then at all larger t
by monotonicity. The identically-minus-infinity case gives the asserted
formula immediately.

In the finite case, choose a supporting line a t+lambda0 at t=1.
The slope is nonpositive and its intercept lambda0 is at most C:
evaluate at t approaching0 and use g(t)<=C. If lambda0<C, then
h_lambda0(v)>=a and the formula follows from equality at t=1. If
lambda0=C, lower its intercept by epsilon>0. Then
h_(C-epsilon)(v)>=a and the resulting expression is at least
g(1)-epsilon. Let epsilon decrease to0. Together with the opposite
inequality h_lambda+lambda<=phi, this proves the formula at every
point, including singular-support points.

Only elementary convexity and the definition enter this proof. It
does not import the unproved varying-class closure behind BJ6.24.

## 5. The actual fixed-divisor cone follows from ample compactness

Let L=A+D with actual A ample rational and D effective rational Cartier,
and write sigma=log||s_D||. For homogeneous h<=sigma, Section3 gives
decreasing actual homogeneous FS h_i in L_i with c1(L_i)->c1(L).
On compact K={sigma=-1}, these continuous extended functions decrease
to values at most-1. For each rational 0<t<1 compactness gives eventual
h_i<-t on K. Use the cofinal directed pairs (i,t), t tending to1.
Homogeneity implies h_i<=t sigma at all divisorial valuations: if
sigma(v)<0, scale v to K; if sigma(v)=0, use h_i(v)<=0. Section2 and
the ample usc-obstacle principle extend this inequality everywhere.

Literal section division now needs no closure argument. At each
prime divisor E of smooth X the inequality says that every displayed
section has vanishing order at least t times its degree times the
coefficient of E in D. Clear denominators. Normality makes the quotient
by s_(p tD) a global section of p(L_i-tD). Hence

    r_i=h_i-t sigma

is the corresponding genuine actual FS quotient, with generic value0.
Refining the index by an additional positive rational parameter if needed,
choose rational c_i>1 tending to1 such that the ACTUAL bundle
c_i A-(L_i-tD) is ample; openness of the ample cone permits this
because L_i-tD tends numerically to A. Multiplication by a generating
family of a sufficiently divisible power of that ample difference,
whose norm maximum is0, realizes r_i/c_i as an actual A-section maximum.
These quotients need not decrease.

Normalized ample compactness supplies a convergent subnet with genuine

regularization, multiplies by s_D to give a decreasing actual L-section

For general u<=sigma+C0, apply Section4. For its homogeneous component

## 6. A direct constants-only zero-class cone

Let phi be genuine numerical0-psh and let its defining real FS net
phi_i in L_i have c1(L_i)->0. Put C_i=phi_i(v_triv), decreasing to
C=phi(v_triv). Refine the defining index by positive rational epsilon
decreasing to0: for every epsilon require that epsilon A-L_i is ample,
which holds eventually in i. The admissible pairs are directed by
increasing i and decreasing epsilon; for a smaller epsilon first increase
i far enough for its ample-difference condition. Set c_i,epsilon=epsilon
on this refined net, suppressing the second index below. Thus c_i>0
tends to0 and c_i A-L_i is ample for one fixed actual ample A, even if
the original defining net was stationary. The same generator multiplication as
above makes (phi_i-C_i)/c_i an actual normalized A-section maximum.

For each divisorial v, normalized ample compactness and continuous
evaluation give a finite lower bound M_v for all normalized A-psh
functions. Therefore

    c_i M_v <= phi_i(v)-C_i <=0.

Passing to the limit gives phi(v)=C at every divisorial point.
Section2 places phi in a fixed actual ample H cone; the constant C
also belongs to that cone. Ample divisorial determination implies
phi=C on all of X^an. This proves PSH(0)=R without applying a
varying-class decreasing closure theorem to the sequence m phi.
It supplies the missing foundation for the actual trivial-bundle
zero-mass calculations of draft105.
