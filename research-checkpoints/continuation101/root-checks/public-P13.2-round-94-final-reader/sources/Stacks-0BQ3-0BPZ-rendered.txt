L24: 【8†Lemma 28.16.2.】 A normal scheme is geometrically unibranch.
L25: Proof. This follows from the definitions. Namely, a scheme is normal if the local rings are normal domains. It is immediate from the More on Algebra, Definition 【15†15.108.1】 that a local normal domain is geometrically unibranch. $\square$

L24: 【8†Definition 15.108.1.】 Let $A$ be a local ring. We say $A$ is unibranch if the reduction $A_{red}$ is a domain and if the integral closure $A'$ of $A_{red}$ in its field of fractions is local. We say $A$ is geometrically unibranch if $A$ is unibranch and moreover the residue field of $A'$ is purely inseparable over the residue field of $A$.
