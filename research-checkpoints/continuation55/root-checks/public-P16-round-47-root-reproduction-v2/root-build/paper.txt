1. The target and the valuation distinction
The residual P16.3 target retains a complete nontrivially discretely valued non-Archimedean
field K, a smooth projective variety X/K, dimension n ≥ 1, and an ample line bundle L. Write
V = (Ln ) > 0. The restriction to remove is char(K)
                                                  f = 0, where K  f is the residue field. Thus both
equal characteristic (p, p) and mixed characteristic (0, p) must be addressed. Field characteristic
zero alone does not meet the original residue-characteristic hypothesis. For uniqueness by one
constant one works on a connected variety; disconnected components instead permit independent
constants. The unresolved case already contains geometrically integral smooth projective varieties,
so introducing that restriction would not close the gap.
The required equation is


                              MAraw
                                ρ (u) = µ,         µ ≥ 0,   µ(X an ) = V,

for an actual psh potential u of finite energy relative to a specified continuous semipositive reference
metric ρ on L. The positivity datum remains the ample L, and its raw mass remains V . Measures
must satisfy the dual-energy condition specified below. No support restriction to a skeleton,
toric invariance, or replacement inverse measure is authorized. There must be an approximation-
independent extension of the genuine model Monge–Ampere measure, followed by existence and
uniqueness modulo constants on the stated finite-energy space.
If k has the trivial absolute value, then k ◦ = k, k ◦◦ = 0, and ke = k. Thus a trivially valued field
of characteristic p has residue characteristic p, but it is not a nontrivially valued field. Neither
Fp with trivial valuation nor its test configurations give a proof for Fp ((t)), much less Qp . Test
configurations are auxiliary models over k[t], not an identification of an arbitrary nontrivially valued
variety with a trivially valued one.

Theorem (published endpoint in raw normalization). Let k be algebraically closed of
characteristic p > 0, equipped with the trivial absolute value. Let X/k be smooth, connected and
projective, of dimension n ≥ 1, and let L be ample. Fix the canonical trivial reference metric ρ, and
put ω = c1 (L), V = (Ln ). There is a genuine, approximation-independent operator


                     MAraw
                       ρ   : E 1 (L) −→ {positive Radon measures of mass V }.

It extends the intersection-theoretic model MA measure, is continuous along decreasing nets, and its
normalized version is continuous for the strong topologies. Its image consists of finite-dual-energy
measures; it is injective modulo constants, with dense image in that measure space. If 1 ≤ n ≤ 2,
its image is the entire finite-dual-energy measure space. Equivalently, for every positive Radon µ
with mass V and Eρ∗,raw (µ) < ∞, there is a solution u ∈ E 1 (L), unique modulo addition of a real
constant. There is no restriction on measure support. No assertion that u is continuous is made.
For n ≥ 3, the same surjectivity follows if L has the envelope property, but that extra hypothesis is
not proved here.
This is a source theorem application, not a newly proved removal of the nontrivial residue-
characteristic restriction.

5. Why the recent local theories do not close this argument
Gubler–Rabinoff, arXiv:2506.13548v2, banner 17 September 2025, introduces classically psh functions
through curves and base change. Its introduction expressly states that defining MA may require
additional hypotheses and is not the subject of the paper. Its identification of classically psh R-PL
functions with semipositivity is useful local positivity information, but is not an approximation-
independent global E 1 equation theorem.
Chambert-Loir–Ducros, arXiv:1204.6277v2, banner 25 July 2025 and interior date 29 July 2025,
states in Section 0.2.15 that its forms/currents theory works in every characteristic without resolution
or alterations. It develops local Bedford–Taylor and metric-curvature products. Those characteristic-
independent statements replace inputs for that local theory. They do not by themselves identify
every singular metric in the target global psh cone with its regularizable local class, prove global
normalized compactness, or supply the required variational existence and injectivity. No such
identification is proved in this draft.
Thuillier’s thesis is potential theory on analytic curves; Berkovich’s 1993 paper is Etale cohomology
for non-Archimedean analytic spaces. Their analytic and curve-theoretic results are compatible with
positive residue characteristic. Neither is used as a replacement for higher-dimensional envelope
regularity or as an equation theorem in higher dimension. Their PDFs were checked as sources, not
invoked to collapse the assignment to P1 .


6. Proof boundaries and failed inference chains
The following passages would be invalid in the present proof and were not made:
  • Smooth generic fiber ⇒ cofinal regular SNC models in arbitrary positive residue characteristic.
    The relevant total spaces are one dimension larger; equal and mixed characteristic require
    distinct geometric inputs.
  • Resolution alone ⇒ the multiplier-ideal subadditivity and vanishing used by BFJ. Positive-
    characteristic test ideals require the hypotheses under which their generation estimates hold;
    the reported BE characteristic-p theorem imposes function-field descent and resolution. Mixed
    characteristic is not covered by that report.
  • Alterations or a universal homeomorphism ⇒ a descended finite-energy operator. Even a
    topological statement does not prove preservation of the ample class, psh cone, masses, energy,
    derivative, unbounded products or comparison.
  • Strong convergence of genuine model MA measures ⇒ a psh solution of their limit equation.
    A realization/completeness theorem for actual potentials is missing.
  • Local current products ⇒ global variational existence for every finite-dual-energy measure.
    Compactness, envelope compatibility and unbounded injectivity remain separate.
These are logical gaps in attempted extensions, not mathematical counterexamples to the target.
No nonexistence assertion is proved.
