\section{A finite four-point estimate}
On one actual generated nef generic bundle $A$, all quantities
here are finite bounded model intersections with raw mass $V_A=A^n$.

\[
 I_A(\xi,\eta)=\int(\xi-\eta)(d\mu_\eta-d\mu_\xi),

                                                        \tag{2}
\]
If $T_\xi=(A,\xi)$, finite telescoping gives
\[

   \|\xi-\eta\|_{T_\xi^jT_\eta^{n-1-j}}^2\geq0.          \tag{3}
\]
The graph quadratic form is $-D^2\Lambda$, with all component
multiplicities. Constants have zero quadratic form.
Write $J_A(\zeta)=V_A\zeta(v_0)-e_A(\zeta)$,
$d_A(\zeta,0)$ for the maximum of its own-slot quadratic forms,
$\alpha=2^{-n}$ and $\beta=2^{1-n}=2\alpha$.

\[
 \|\zeta\|_{T_\xi^jT_\eta^{n-1-j}}^2
 \leq C_n J_A(\zeta)^\beta
     \max\{J_A(\zeta),J_A(\xi),J_A(\eta)\}^{1-\beta}.
                                                        \tag{4}
\]
Here its correct finite $n-j$ tangent identity gives
$d_A(\zeta,0)\leq(n+1)J_A(\zeta)$.

\begin{lemma}[Finite four-point bound]\label{finite}
For every such triple,
\[
 \left|\int\zeta(d\mu_\xi-d\mu_\eta)\right|
 \leq C_n I_A(\xi,\eta)^{1/2}J_A(\zeta)^\alpha
       \max\{J_A(\zeta),J_A(\xi),J_A(\eta)\}^{1/2-\alpha}.
                                                        \tag{5}
\]
For $n=1$ the last factor is omitted.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Expand the difference of top powers. The integral on the left is,
up to sign, the sum of the $n$ quadratic polarizations of $\zeta$
and $\xi-\eta$ in the slots of (3).
Cauchy--Schwarz in each nonnegative graph form, and then for the
finite sum, bound it by the square root of (3) times the square
root of the sum in (4). This proves (5).
Generic constants contribute zero because the two raw masses agree.
No division by volume, ample equation in a nef class, or unbounded
auxiliary operator occurs.
\end{proof}
