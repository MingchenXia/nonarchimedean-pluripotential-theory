# Curve detection as a substitute for the higher-dimensional corner calculation

This is a new, unreviewed root proposal. It is not an accepted premise, a singular envelope theorem, or a completed solution of P16.1. The round61 curve proof and the checkpoint71 Gauss descent argument must be reconstructed, not assumed. The original projective, geometrically unibranch, arbitrary-support finite-energy target remains unchanged.

## 1. Actual primary statements

Gubler--Rabinoff, arXiv:2503.05513v1, Proposition 5.1 (inclusive LF818--843 in the authenticated literal extract), proves that an R-PL function on a smooth strict analytic curve is subharmonic at a point if and only if its residue line bundle is nef. Its proof uses local algebraicity of smooth analytic curves and a formal curve model; it does not assume that the curve map to the ambient variety is algebraic.

Their Corollary 5.4 (LF890--923, including the full proof) proves the following for a good strictly analytic space over a complete nontrivially valued field. An actual R-PL function is semipositive if and only if, over one algebraically closed analytic field extension, its pullback by every smooth analytic curve map is semipositive. The proof reduces a closed analytic curve to its normalization, uses proper surjective descent of residue nefness, and invokes Theorem 5.3. Consequently the ambient space need not be smooth or normal. This is a theorem about actual R-PL functions in their strict G-topology, not about an arbitrary continuous function called piecewise linear on a relative subset.

Ducros, published DOI10.1142/S1793744212500077, Theorem 3.2 (LF2077--2150), gives a finite c-polytope image for finitely many analytic units on a compact Gamma-strict analytic space, where c=(Q,(|K*|Gamma)^Q), and dimension is at most the analytic dimension. Over a discretely valued Laurent field and on a strict domain, the logarithmic affine constants are rational multiples of the value-group generator. Its proof invokes the actual valued-field image Theorem 1.2; assertion A and its closedness and dimension proofs were read at LF1276--1402. The printed stronger dimension equality in CLD12.2.4 is unnecessary here and fails for a constant moment map.

Gubler--Jell--Rabinoff, arXiv:2111.05741v3, Proposition 7.1 (LF2298--2330) identifies residue nefness with a nef formal model on a sufficiently small strict neighborhood. Lemma 7.4 (LF2356--2363) relates pointwise semipositivity to nefness of a represented paracompact formal model. Proposition 7.6(2') (LF2385--2425) detects semipositivity after any analytic field extension. These statements apply to represented model functions; the local line-bundle trivializations and their transition norms must be verified for a metric.

## 2. Construct actual Q-PL metrics before applying the criterion

Consider a continuous, globally glued metric on an ample rational line bundle on X. Suppose its local weights are finite continuous selections of rational affine expressions in finitely many regular log-norm coordinates, with rational constants, and satisfy the corrected relative contact-support condition. The displayed condition always includes the additive contact constant in the lower inequality. Infinite coordinates are handled by the finite affine convention: on a smaller chart containing such a point, only finite coordinates used with nonzero coefficient remain, and those coordinates are analytic units.

Pass to K=k((t^(1/q))) with a common denominator for the finitely many rational constants on a compact cover. The explicit localized rational neighborhoods in the companion continuation72 proposal pull back to strict affinoid domains. On each such domain apply Ducros3.2 to the retained units. Refine its finite rational polyhedral image by every equality hyperplane between candidate affine expressions. On the connected relative interior of each refined cell, a continuous finite selection has a fixed value expression: two distinct values cannot be exchanged without equality. It extends with that same value to the cell closure by continuity. Clear denominators in the finitely many rational inequalities. Each polyhedral preimage is then a strict Laurent domain, since the expressions are monomials in analytic units and the constants lie in |K*| after a further finite Laurent extension if necessary. The finite weak-inequality domain covers must be checked to be admissible G-covers.

On every resulting domain an integer tensor power of the local weight is log|a| for an analytic unit a, including its value-group constant. The transition functions are the actual line-bundle frames, not newly chosen unrelated functions. This proves the strict Q-PL metric representation needed for GM2.10 and GR5.4. The construction requires a globally glued metric and a finite rational presentation; it does not manufacture either from arbitrary local data. Rational approximation of arbitrary real coefficients while preserving semipositivity is a separate problem.

## 3. Curve pullbacks detect the sign without a high-dimensional current

Let Omega be the completion of an algebraic closure of K, so it is complete, algebraically closed, nontrivially valued and of residue characteristic zero. For any smooth analytic Omega-curve map into a weight neighborhood, pull back the finitely many unit coordinates. The tropical image of the curve lies in the original relative image under the projection. A contact affine minorant on the original image is still a contact minorant at every curve image point. Coordinates vanishing identically are discarded by the same finite affine convention.

Reconstruct the round61 slope argument with analytic units. On a sufficiently small strict semistable curve neighborhood all the unit log-norms factor through one finite skeleton and are harmonic, by the actual Thuillier unit theorems. Their edge restrictions are affine after subdivision. A finite selection of affine expressions is lisse, including real breakpoints. At an interior skeleton point subtract the pulled contact harmonic function. The remainder has minimum zero there, so every outgoing derivative is nonnegative; the positive weighted outgoing derivatives of the contact harmonic function sum to zero. Therefore the lisse Laplacian is nonnegative. Type I and IV unit germs give no additional negative coefficients. Boundary coefficients are not tested on the curve open. Thuillier's positive-Laplacian criterion gives subharmonicity.

The extension from algebraic to smooth analytic curves must be written explicitly. GR5.1 and the van der Put result used there give local algebraicity, while the skeleton and harmonicity statements are already statements about analytic units. The target coordinate pullbacks need not become algebraic functions under this local identification. The proof only uses their analytic-unit property. This is the key scope check, rather than replacing arbitrary analytic maps by algebraic maps.

Apply GR5.1 and GR5.4 to these actual PL local weights. They give residue nefness over K, with detection after Omega allowed. GJR7.1 supplies local nef formal models; the actual globally represented model and GM3.11 identify their local metric semipositivity with global model nefness. Check the hypotheses of GM3.3 if field descent of the global model is used. A local frame change adds a harmonic unit logarithm and preserves the curve slope test.

This route uses neither a general pullback theorem for positive currents nor an ambient convex extension. It can therefore replace the unresolved all-dimensional calibrated-facet step for this precisely represented rational subclass.

## 4. From the nef model to genuine CPSH over the trivial field

Use the actual BE Theorem 7.8 on the ample line bundle over the complete nontrivially valued Laurent field K, with its general-variety scope and the proved nef model. This yields genuine FS approximation there. Apply the checkpoint71 exact Gauss-section coefficient argument over K to descend the approximants to k. For s_j=sum_r t^(r/q) f_jr, the Gauss value is max_r(e^(-r/q)|f_jr|); compact lower bounds and the global trivial-reference bound |f_jr|<=1 cut off the tail uniformly. The retained coefficient sections form an actual global generating family because the metric stays finite at every point. The resulting expression is a genuine FS metric over k. Decreasing and uniform limits are passed through the continuous section.

Do not apply this coefficient formula directly to Omega: arbitrary elements of the completed algebraic closure need not have the Laurent expansion used in the proof. Detect or descend model nefness to K first, apply BE over K, and only then use the Laurent coefficients. This avoids a new field-expansion gap.

## 5. Citation corrections and remaining work

CLD17.8.4 proves nef to psh for strict integer PL; it cannot establish positive current to nef. CLD18.8.4 supplies a positive-current/residual-degree equivalence in its actual integer-PL setting, and 18.8.6 explicitly permits complete field extension for an already represented integer-PL positive metric. The checkpoint71 caution about unsupported positive-current pullback concerns unrepresented local functions; it is not a denial of 18.8.6. The curve route above avoids needing that implication entirely.

If fully reconstructed, the route proves genuine global membership of the continuous rational PL collar metrics on singular X, removing the smooth-only BJ4.19 application and the corresponding high-dimensional corner gate. It does not by itself construct the arbitrary analytic collar, prove bounded discontinuous local weighted products, establish their total mass and integration by parts, prove Rossi/Runge or removable extension, descend all psh functions through normalization, or establish singular envelope regularity. Those are still separate gates to the original P16.1 equation and must not disappear from the conclusion.
