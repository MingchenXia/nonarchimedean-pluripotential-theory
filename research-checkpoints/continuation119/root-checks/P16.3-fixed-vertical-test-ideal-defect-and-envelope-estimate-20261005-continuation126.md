# A fixed vertical ideal and the mixed-characteristic envelope estimate

This is a new, unreviewed own proof unit for original P16.3. It makes explicit the ideal-to-uniform-estimate step in the regular-model theorem of Fang--Gubler--Kunnemann, arXiv2203.12282v2. It is not inserted into the active frozen author116 or review114. It does not establish regular-model cofinality, an unbounded operator or original closure.

## 1. Precise data and a bounded vertical correction

Let R be a complete discrete valuation ring, K its complete fraction field, pi a uniformizer, and lambda=-log|pi|>0. Let X be a geometrically integral projective K-variety with ample actual line bundle L. Retain a normal integral projective flat R-model M, and actual model line bundles scriptL and scriptA with specified generic-fiber identifications with L. Let theta be the curvature of the actual scriptL model metric. For an ideal I on M write log||I||(x) for the logarithm of the maximum of its local generator norms at the reduction center of x. This is independent of those generators. For a vertical coherent ideal it is a finite continuous model function: its admissible blowup principalizes the ideal, giving its actual model metric.

Assume q is a coherent ideal with q|X=O_X. Since M is Noetherian and quasi-compact, there is one integer a>=0 with pi^a O_M contained in q: on finitely many affine charts the finitely generated quotient O_M/q is annihilated by a power of pi, and one maximum exponent works. At every analytic point the R-model center makes regular coefficient norms at most one. Thus

$$-a\lambda\leq\log\|q\|\leq0.$$

In particular q contributes a uniform O(1/m) correction; it is not necessary or justified to assume q=O_M. For any ideal a_m,

$$\frac1m\log\|a_mq\|=\frac1m\log\|a_m\|+
\frac1m\log\|q\|.$$

The equality uses multiplicative seminorms and finite local generators. It holds at every analytic point. No positivity assertion for the correction or the modified ideal function follows merely from it.

## 2. An ideal-to-envelope lemma that keeps the actual generic bundle

Let a_m be the complete base ideal of scriptL^m, and put phi_m=m^-1 log||a_m|| for all sufficiently large m. As L is ample, its sufficiently high powers are generated on X. Every such generic section extends after multiplication by a power of pi: apply the quasi-coherent principal-localization isomorphism, Stacks01P7, to the quasi-compact separated model M and its uniformizer open X. Thus a_m is vertical. The blowup base-ideal construction gives a semipositive actual model metric on L, hence phi_m is theta-psh. Since a_m is an ideal, phi_m<=0.

Let P be a bounded function on X^an, with P<=0, dominating every phi_m. In the application P=P_theta(0). Suppose ideals b_m, for sufficiently large integers m, have the following three specified properties:

1. a_(ml) q is contained in b_(ml) along an unbounded set of admissible positive integers l, with the one fixed q of Section1;
2. b_(ml) is contained in b_m^l;
3. b_m tensor scriptA^m0 tensor scriptL^m is globally generated on M, with one fixed integer m0>=0 independent of m.

Assume moreover phi_(ml) tends pointwise to P as l tends to infinity for each fixed sufficiently large m. This pointwise model-envelope input must be established separately; the algebra below does not prove it.

Property1 and property2 imply at every point

$$\beta_m:=\frac1m\log\|b_m\|
\ \geq\ \frac1{ml}\log\|a_{ml}q\|
=\phi_{ml}+\frac1{ml}\log\|q\|.$$

The sign comes from ideal inclusion: norms of a smaller ideal are at most those of a larger ideal. Let l increase. The bounded correction tends uniformly to zero, so beta_m>=P on the entire analytic space. This also excludes a generic or analytic base zero of b_m.

To use property3, retain the actual generically trivial model bundle

$$\mathcal K=\mathcal A^{m_0}\otimes\mathcal L^{-m_0}.$$

Choose a_J>=0 so multiplication by pi^a_J followed by the specified generic identification with O_X extends to an injective sheaf map K -> O_M. Indeed K differs from O_M by an actual vertical Cartier divisor. Its finitely many coefficients along special-fiber primes are bounded by a sufficiently large multiple of the uniformizer divisor. Normality extends the resulting codimension-one regular rational section over M: in each affine bundle frame this is exactly the height-one intersection criterion, Stacks031T(2). The image J is an invertible vertical ideal. Its model logarithm eta=log||J|| is finite continuous, at most zero, and bounded below by -C for a fixed finite C. Alternatively pi^c O_M is contained in J for some c, by Section1.

Choose finitely many actual global generators s_j of the coherent sheaf b_m tensor scriptA^m0 tensor scriptL^m. Apply the fixed sheaf injection to them. They become actual global sections of scriptL^(m+m0). In local model frames their coefficients generate J b_m: the original coefficients generated b_m, and the injection multiplies them by a generator of J. Therefore

$$a_{m+m_0}\ \supset\ Jb_m,\qquad
(m+m_0)\phi_{m+m_0}\ \geq\ m\beta_m+\eta\ \geq\ mP+\eta.$$

Together with phi_(m+m0)<=P, this gives the all-point uniform estimate

$$0\leq P-\phi_{m+m_0}
\leq\frac{m_0\|P\|_\infty+C}{m+m_0}.$$

Consequently the actual theta-psh model metrics phi_k converge uniformly to P along every sufficiently large k=m+m0. This is an actual same-generic-bundle statement; a numerical class limit or an assumed ample pullback was not substituted. The model bundle scriptA may differ from scriptL vertically, which is precisely why the fixed ideal J was retained.

## 3. Application check and the remaining field-axis obligation

For the regular mixed-characteristic model in FGK Theorem5.1, take q=the perturbation-friendly ideal tau(O_M,0), which is vertical by Remark4.2(v). Lemma4.4 inserts this q in the base-ideal inclusion; omitting it would be unwarranted. Theorem4.7 gives asymptotic subadditivity and fixed global generation. The latter application chooses E=mD+H+K_M, so E-K_M-mD=H is actually big and nef. Its integer generation threshold uses the special-fiber dimension. The regular ample model A is a hypothesis of Theorem5.1. The separately supplied pointwise model-envelope identity finishes the preceding lemma.

This proves the explicit uniform estimate once those exact inputs hold. It supplies none of the missing arbitrary-dimensional regular-model or embedded-resolution assertions.
