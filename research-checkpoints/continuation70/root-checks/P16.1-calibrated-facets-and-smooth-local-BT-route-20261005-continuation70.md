# Relative supports, calibrated corners, and a smooth bounded-local route

This is a new unreviewed reconstruction proposal. It addresses the smooth
weak-affine and bounded-local gates left in P16.1 round58; it is not an
accepted dependency or a solution of the original singular envelope
problem. In particular Rossi gluing and the full singular Runge induction
are still independent obligations.

## A. Current positivity for the actual relative support class

Let Y be an algebraic analytification, boundaryless and separated. Take a
finite continuous rational PL weight u=f(-log|g_i|), retaining the finite
affine convention and corrected contact inequality from the accompanying
curve note. Near a point with infinite coordinates discard the coordinates
which finite affine expressions cannot use. On the remaining neighborhood
the g_i are units, so their log norms are harmonic. The proof uses a
calibrated harmonic tropical chart, not an ambient convex extension of f.

To evaluate d'd''[u] against a compactly supported strongly positive
(n-1,n-1) test, refine the harmonic chart by the units defining that test.
The original support affine function pulls back by the projection from
the refined chart. On a strongly supporting skeleton subdivide into
finitely many cells on which u is affine. The common refinement must be
chosen on a compact domain inside the open under consideration, so its
interior facets are separated from the analytic boundary. CLD7.8.4's
Green formula and slope calculation are the local input.

Fix an interior (n-1)-facet tau and a point of its relative interior.
Let ell be a contact support. Since u and ell are affine on tau and
u-ell has a minimum zero at an interior point, their tangential
derivatives on tau agree. For every adjacent n-cell sigma, let e_sigma
be its inward transverse vector with the positive canonical calibration
used in CLD7.8.4. The contact inequality gives

    derivative_(e_sigma)(u_sigma-ell) >= 0.

Subtracting ell changes no corner coefficient: the harmonic-chart
balancing identity cancels the affine contribution. In a common
facet-volume normalization the coefficient is therefore

    sum_sigma weight_sigma derivative_(e_sigma)(u_sigma-ell) >= 0.

The signs of the Green boundary orientation must be checked so that the
one-dimensional formula agrees with the outgoing-slope Laplacian in the
curve note. A strongly positive test restricts to a nonnegative
facet-volume density. Summing these corner contributions gives
d'd''[u] >= 0. Refinement by test coordinates handles charts whose
original tropical map has rank less than n. No unsupported cell with a
negative calibration is introduced. This needs an explicit reconstruction
of the calibrated corner formula, including collapsed cells, during the
native author/reviewer pass; the preceding inequalities alone are not a
standalone proof of that formula.

After clearing rational slopes, exp(-u) is an integer PL metric on the
trivial line bundle on this open: finite affine selections are locally
log norms on their rational domains, with real constants as permitted
by CLD12.5. The required finite rational-domain representation must also
be checked, rather than inferred just from the word PL. Under that
verified representation CLD18.8.8 applies and makes u a CLD psh
continuous weight. The exact source proof uses residual nefness and
17.8.4, not a claim that every positive continuous current is
smooth-approachable. Over a nontrivially valued extension rational
constants can be cleared into powers of t when a strict metric is needed.

Strictness is a real source-application check:17.8.4 itself states
strict integer PL, whereas18.8.8 is printed for integer PL. Do not
silently identify those hypotheses. For the global smooth metric route
one can first use positive PL current pullback18.8.6, clear the rational
constants over k((t)), and apply the strict theorem there. Any separate
claim that all original trivially valued local weights are in CLD's
generated continuous psh cone needs the exact descent or nonstrict
extension argument. The later bounded-local construction should rely
on genuine global weighted continuity rather than assume this claim.

For curves the separate slope proof proves subharmonicity directly and
does not need the higher-dimensional corner formula.

## B. The smooth collar can use an analytic PL metric

Let X be smooth, connected and projective over algebraically closed
trivially valued k of characteristic zero. Let theta be a rational actual
semipositive FS reference in an ample class and let g be its local affine
exhaustion. Suppose a_i is a supplied common-neighborhood decreasing
sequence of corrected rational local-support PL functions, with a bounded
limit a on a compact neighborhood under consideration. A metric obtained
by max(a_i-h,0) inside that neighborhood and zero outside is continuous
analytic rational PL when the maximum is inactive on an open boundary
collar. Its local reference weight plus this potential is corrected
locally psh, so A supplies positive curvature.

Pull the complete metric to K=k((t)), |t|=e^-1. It remains positive by
CLD18.8.6, with boundaryless separated analytifications in scope. On the
proper K-scheme, GM1608.08030v3 Theorem1.1 identifies a strict integral
PL metric, after a tensor power, with an algebraic/formal metric. The
exact complete-field and strictness conditions must be retained; GM is
not applied directly over k. CLD18.8.4, via the actual residual pre-model
of each central curve, gives nonnegative degree on every such curve.
The model is nef and hence the pulled metric is BFJ semipositive. BJ
Remark4.19, expressly smooth and connected in characteristic zero,
returns the original potential to the genuine global PSH cone.

This route need not assert that the analytic glued PL potential is
already a BJ equivariant model-PL function over k. It is sufficient that
it is continuous and genuine global PSH. Sequential decreasing genuine
model approximation and Dini then give uniform approximation by genuine
models. For this continuous limit, CLD8.7.2 applies legitimately, and
CLD18.4.7 identifies the actual global mixed operator with the local
curvature product. The uniform convergence here is justified by the
continuity of the limit; it is not assumed for a later singular limit.

## C. A common bounded-local representation with the correct reference

For x in an analytic open U of this smooth X choose a compact rational
polytope Q contained in U and a smaller P with x in its interior. Use
an affine embedding and rational thresholds to obtain

    v=max(lambda, log|f_1|-C_1,...,log|f_r|-C_r),

with a rational *negative* floor lambda, v(x)<-epsilon,
P={v<=-epsilon}, Q={v<=0}, and theta the actual complete-system FS
metric. Include the distinguished section s_0 with its negative weight:
including it with weight zero would force v>=0 and invalidate P.
The denominators, section powers and complete generator system must be
constructed from the finite rational-domain data. Take lambda below the
chosen negative threshold. The exhaustion v is -log||s_0||_theta on its
affine chart, with the line-bundle tensor/scaling convention retained.

Let omega be the requested reference and write

    theta=omega+ddc p,  g_omega=v-p.

Assume |u|<=M on Q and a common decreasing continuous corrected PL
sequence u_i->u on a neighborhood of Q. Fix rational A>0 with
A epsilon>2M+1 and set h=A v+M+1. On P, h<-M, and on a neighborhood
of the boundary {v=0}, h>M. A compact nested-superlevel argument gives
one whole tail of u_i for which u_i<h on a fixed boundary collar.
No Dini convergence to a noncontinuous limit is used. Define

    Psi_i=max(u_i-h,0) on Q, zero outside Q.

The common boundary collar gives a continuous rational analytic PL
metric in the class A theta. Apply B to make each Psi_i genuinely
global psh. They decrease globally to bounded Psi, so global decreasing
closure gives Psi in genuine E1 without local MA or comparison having
been used. On P, Psi=u-h. Put

    Phi_omega=Psi/A+p,
    G_omega=v-p+(M+1)/A.

Then Phi_omega is genuine global omega-psh and

    Phi_omega+G_omega=u/A on P.

Exactly the same formulas hold on P for a tail of the approximants.
The same A, p and G_omega work for any finite tuple of bounded inputs
and common supplied sequences after taking a common bound M and a
common tail. A rational bound larger than an arbitrary real M can be
used throughout to keep the PL constants rational.

This establishes the legitimate common bounded-local representation
in the smooth domain *provided A's actual current/PL representation
checks are completed*. It invokes neither the local bounded product
being constructed nor arbitrary weak global regularization.

## D. Local mixed measures from the genuine global operator

For a bounded tuple u_1,...,u_n on a common neighborhood use C with one
common scaling and reference. On P define

    M_local(u_1,...,u_n)
      = A^n M_global((omega,Phi_1),...,(omega,Phi_n))|P.

For continuous PL approximants, B and actual CLD/model compatibility
identify this with dd u_1 wedge ... wedge dd u_n. Thus two choices of
extensions give the same approximant measures on overlaps. Genuine
global decreasing continuity then makes their limits equal. This proves
overlap and approximation independence, rather than assuming it.

For a bounded test u_0 represented in the same chart, write
u_0=A(Phi_0+G_omega). The fixed G_omega is continuous and bounded on
compact chart subsets. The actual BJ mixed weighted decreasing
continuity, applied to Phi_(0,i) and the remaining global tuple, gives
the weighted convergence u_(0,i) M_local(u_(1,i),...,u_(n,i)). This is
where the noncontinuous bounded limit is handled. CLD8.7.2 itself
requires locally uniform convergence and is not claimed to prove this
step. The original round58 source index records BJ7.19/7.21 for the
actual global continuity and mixed integrability statements.

Restriction follows from the overlap construction; changing a local
frame adds a harmonic log norm and changes no product. A positive
operator, mixed mass control on relatively compact charts, and weighted
integrability are inherited from the genuine global representation.
For a bounded locally omega-psh metric on all X, the local mixed
products glue. Their total mass and integration by parts still need
an explicit argument from the global ddc-exact telescoping and local
weighted continuity, with a partition of unity and harmonic frame
transitions; no mere naming of a current proves these identities.

Locality on {u>v} uses the winning potential u. It can be transported
from genuine global locality by the common local representation, and
local comparison from a common representation with compactly supported
differences. Completing that transported comparison, the total-mass
argument and global domination is the remaining step needed to
globalize an arbitrary bounded locally psh potential as in Wu4.17.
These applications must be proved before using them to establish
arbitrary smooth weak affine regularization.
