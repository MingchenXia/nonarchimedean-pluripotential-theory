# Model atoms replace a Dirac equation in local domination

This is a new unreviewed proof proposal following the two continuation70
local-support notes. It does not establish singular local globalization or
singular EP. Its first model-atom construction is unconditional in the
genuine global theory; subsequent local domination requires the actual
bounded local measures, their exact total mass, locality and comparison.

## 1. A bounded genuine test with positive mass at each divisorial point

Let X be reduced irreducible projective over algebraically closed
trivially valued k, and let L be ample rational. For any x in Xdiv,
BJ Lemma2.12 gives a flag ideal a whose integrally closed blowup
pi:mathcalX->Xtriv has a central component E with v_E=x. The construction
uses relative integral closure in the prescribed generic fiber, so it
does not replace a nonnormal X by its normalization.

BJ Lemma2.33 gives an ample test configuration mathcalL for L on this
same mathcalX. Concretely choose a vertical rational divisor D relatively
ample over Xtriv and take mathcalL=L_mathcalX+epsilon D for positive
rational epsilon small enough. The actual model potential b is bounded
and genuine global L-psh. Every central component F has

    (mathcalL|F)^n > 0,

because the restriction is ample; b_F>0 is the multiplicity of F. The
actual raw model measure is sum_F b_F (mathcalL|F)^n delta_(v_F),
so MA(b)({x})>0. Normalizing by V=(L^n) changes no positivity. A
coinciding valuation from another component adds another positive
term. The root/trivial valuation is handled either by the trivial
ample configuration or the same construction. Rational scaled divisorial
valuations are exactly the source's Xdiv convention.

This construction uses neither equation surjectivity nor a continuous
Dirac solution. It supplies exactly the positive atom used in the
perturbation argument, with a concrete bounded model test.

## 2. Domination from the stated local measure antecedents

Assume bounded locally omega-psh potentials have genuine compatible
mixed measures, mixed positivity, exact raw mass V, locality on strict
max loci, and the comparison inequality

    MA(psi)({phi<psi}) <= MA(phi)({phi<psi}).

Also assume the corrected local class is determined by its divisorial
values; in the smooth case the common bounded-local representation in
the previous note obtains this from BJ's genuine divisorial determination.
The finite locally psh cone is stable under positive affine combinations.

Suppose phi<=psi MA(psi)-almost everywhere. Fix x in Xdiv and choose
the bounded genuine b from1. Translate b so that b<phi globally, with
a fixed strict margin; both functions are bounded. For 0<t<1 put

    phi_t=(1-t)phi+t b.

Then phi_t<phi everywhere and phi_t<psi MA(psi)-almost everywhere.
Comparison and exact equal masses give

    V=MA(psi)({phi_t<psi})
      <=MA(phi_t)({phi_t<psi})<=V.

Mixed positivity gives MA(phi_t)>=t^n MA(b), hence
MA(phi_t)({x})>0. Since the strict set has full MA(phi_t) mass, x belongs
to {phi_t<psi}. Letting t decrease to zero yields phi(x)<=psi(x).
Divisorial determination then gives phi<=psi globally.

This repairs Wu4.16's use of a continuous Dirac solution with a weaker
explicit ample-model test. It is not an independent proof that its
local measure antecedents hold on singular spaces. Dimension zero is
the separate one-point constant case, not a slope or divisorial test.

## 3. Smooth bounded local/global equality uses continuous majorant nets

Let X be smooth connected projective, characteristic zero, and omega
ample rational. Known smooth EP is the BJ primary theorem; it is not
being inferred from the singular desired conclusion. Suppose the
preceding bounded-local construction and its mass/comparison completion
have been reconstructed on X. Then2 applies.

Take a bounded locally omega-psh phi. On an arbitrary compact Hausdorff
Xan there is a directed decreasing *net* of continuous majorants g_i
with limit phi. A sequence is not asserted for an uncountable k or a
nonmetrizable Xan. This net is obtained from continuous majorants using
finite minima and the usual compact Hausdorff separation of the closed
superlevel sets of the usc phi. The majorants can be truncated to common
finite bounds without changing their limit.

Under smooth EP the genuine envelope P_omega(g_i) is continuous global
psh, and genuine orthogonality gives

    supp MA(P_omega(g_i)) subset {P_omega(g_i)=g_i}.

On this support, phi<=g_i=P_omega(g_i). Domination2 gives
phi<=P_omega(g_i) on Xan. The envelopes decrease, are bounded below by
a common constant, and are at most g_i. Thus their pointwise limit is
phi. Genuine global decreasing closure proves phi is global omega-psh.
The actual envelope-continuity and orthogonality statements, with their
exact ample and smooth hypotheses, must be cited/read in the native
reconstruction; this proof does not extend smooth EP to singular X.

For an unbounded locally psh generically finite phi, truncate with
max(phi,-M). Global usc on compact Xan bounds it above; each truncation
is bounded locally psh and hence global by the preceding argument.
Their decreasing nontrivial limit is global psh. Nef rational classes
can be approached from above by omega+epsilon theta, theta ample;
the exact genuine class-closure statement and known smooth nef EP are
required at this passage.

## 4. Noncircular smooth affine regularization

Let Y be smooth affine over the retained k and choose a smooth
projective compactification X with very ample boundary section s_0.
Let g=-log||s_0||_theta be a proper actual affine FS exhaustion, theta
the actual semipositive reference. For a locally psh generically finite
u with u<=A g+B, take rational A'>A and c>1. For each finite rational
floor C define on Y

    Phi_C=max(u-c A' g,C),

and extend constantly as C across the boundary. A single outer collar
has u-c A'g<C because g is proper and the upper growth is strict. On
Y the local reference weight gives max(u,C+c A'g), so Phi_C is bounded
locally c A'theta-psh. Near the entire boundary it is constant relative
to the actual semipositive reference. Step3 makes Phi_C genuine global
psh. Let C decrease to minus infinity. The limit is not identically
minus infinity, because u is finite somewhere; it is genuine global
psh, equals u-c A'g on Y and minus infinity on the boundary.

Use actual sequential model/FS approximation and complete-system
generator/floor branches to restrict a decreasing sequence back to Y.
Adding c A'g returns u in the actual global affine FS-regularizable
class, as in round58's already-global argument. This does not invoke
Wu3.25 with a regularizability antecedent in order to prove that same
regularizability. Constants, tensor powers and rational clearing still
need to be checked in this restriction step.

For a *weakly* locally psh upper-growth u on this smooth Y, resolve its
proper birational witness and compactify it to a smooth projective
birational pi:Z->X. On pi^-1(Y), its pullback is locally psh and is
bounded above by A pi*g+B. The same floor construction gives bounded
locally psh metrics in the nef big class c A' pi*theta. Smooth
nef-class local/global equality makes them genuine. Decrease the floors.
BJ5.13 applied to the smooth downstairs ample class, whose slightly
larger classes have known EP, makes PSH pullback onto. The upstairs
potential descends, and analytic surjectivity plus its prescribed
pullback values identifies the descended function with u-c A'g on Y.
It follows that this weak u has the same actual affine FS
regularization. Properness, compactification, witness resolution,
birational onto hypotheses, and the nef approximation step must all
be verified; smoothness of the downstairs X is essential here.

The principal-open removable extension needed for traces still requires
a separate proof with the original exhaustion's growth bound across
the removed divisor. Regularizability on Y_f alone does not give
controlled pole-free approximants on Y. No such implication is asserted
without that proof. Singular Rossi analytic-piece gluing, integer trace
localization, gamma=1, and the complete Runge induction remain further
obligations before singular EP or P16.1 existence can be claimed.
