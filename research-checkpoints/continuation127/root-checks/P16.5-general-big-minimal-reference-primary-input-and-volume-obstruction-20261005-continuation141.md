## 2. Minimality and the reference actually retained

For an actual big rational L and theta=c1(L), choose any genuine theta-potential u. A common larger actual ample cone gives u(v_triv)=sup u. Normalize that supremum to zero. Then u is a competitor for P_theta(0), so
$$
 u\le V_\theta\le0.
$$
Consequently V_theta is a genuine potential with minimal singularities. It is intrinsic to the original numerical cone and canonical metric convention; an arbitrary effective divisor in an ample-plus-effective decomposition is not substituted for it.

For any usc obstacle h with a genuine minorant, the external envelope property ensures the regularized supremum of genuine competitors is genuine; because h is usc, it remains below h and is itself a competitor. Thus P_theta(h) is the genuine obstacle envelope. In particular max(u,V_theta-k) belongs to the original cone. These reference floors are genuine functions. That fact does not alone construct a positive relative operator, prove a finite-energy definition, or identify the operator's mass with volume.

## 3. Why the nef raw-intersection recipe fails for a big nonnef bundle

Let X be the blowup of P2_k at one point, H the pullback of O(1), and E the exceptional divisor. Take the actual integral bundle L=2H+E. The intersection numbers H^2=1, H.E=0 and E^2=-1 give
$$
 L.E=-1,\qquad L^2=3.
$$
Thus L is nonnef. For each m>=1 and j=1,...,m,
$$
 (2mH+jE)|_E\simeq O_{P^1}(-j).
$$
The Cartier restriction sequence and the absence of sections of O(-j) show that multiplication by the canonical E-section identifies
$$
 H^0(X,2mH+(j-1)E)\simeq H^0(X,2mH+jE).
$$
Iterating gives H^0(X,mL)=s_E^m H^0(X,2mH). Proper birational normal descent identifies the latter section space with H^0(P2,O(2m)). Hence
$$
 h^0(X,mL)=\binom{2m+2}{2},\qquad \operatorname{vol}(L)=4>0.
$$
This is a big nonnef example on the retained smooth projective original carrier for which volume differs from the ordinary top intersection. Replacing the mass4 by L^2=3 would produce the wrong equation.

The nef proof's canonical zero is also unavailable here: a bounded endpoint of the genuine numerical FS definition would force every defining stage to have no common zeros. Those sections generate their bundle, making each stage's numerical class nef. Its numerical limit would then be nef, contradicting L.E=-1. In particular zero is not a genuine L-potential.
