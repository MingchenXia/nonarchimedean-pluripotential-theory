# Nef section volume and the canonical minimal reference

**Status: unreviewed supplementary proof draft.** This note is outside the frozen native125 packet. It does not change that packet, certify its variational equation, or close original P16.5. It identifies the ordinary section volume with the raw top intersection for actual nef rational bundles, including zero volume, over the retained arbitrary-cardinality characteristic-zero field.

## 1. Domain and statement

Let k be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, with trivial valuation and no cardinality restriction. Let X be smooth integral projective of dimension n>=1. Let L be an actual nef rational line bundle, theta=c1(L), retaining actual tensor identifications. Define its section volume after clearing denominators: for an integer d>0 with dL integral,
$$
 \operatorname{vol}(L)=d^{-n}\limsup_{m\to\infty}
             \frac{n!\,h^0(X,mdL)}{m^n}.
$$
We prove
$$
 \operatorname{vol}(L)=L^n\ge0.                                      \tag{1}
$$
The genuine numerical single-net cone of native121 and own132 has canonical scalar reference zero. Zero has minimal singularities: every genuine theta-psh scalar u satisfies u<=u(v_triv), hence is at most zero after generic normalization. This assertion concerns the originally defined cone, rather than a cone declared by a change of reference.

## 2. Ordinary algebraic proof

First take L integral. Choose a sufficiently large very ample effective divisor B such that B-K_X is ample. If n>=2, choose B smooth integral by Bertini. If n=1, choose B a reduced finite divisor. These choices are possible over the infinite algebraically closed field k.

For every m>=0, mL+B-K_X is ample, since L is nef and B-K_X is ample. Kodaira vanishing in smooth projective characteristic zero gives H^i(X,mL+B)=0 for i>0. Consequently
$$
 h^0(X,mL+B)=\chi(X,mL+B)
            =\frac{L^n}{n!}m^n+O(m^{n-1}).                          \tag{2}
$$
The last equality uses the intersection Euler polynomial, not an assertion that L itself is ample. Indeed, the polynomial chi(X,O_X(B) tensor L^m) has degree at most n. Its degree-n coefficient is unchanged on replacing the rank-one sheaf O_X(B) by O_X, by the leading-coefficient lemma for coherent sheaves. If P(m)=chi(X,L^m), then the coefficient of m_1...m_n in P(m_1+...+m_n) is L^n by the intersection definition, and equals n! times the coefficient of m^n. This proves the coefficient in (2).

We claim
$$
 h^0(B,(mL+B)|_B)=O(m^{n-1}).                                      \tag{3}
$$
For n=1 this follows because a line bundle on a finite reduced scheme has a fixed number of sections. For n>=2 put N=L|_B and F=O_X(B)|_B. Choose a very ample line bundle C on B such that C-N is ample. For all sufficiently large m, m(C-N) has a nonzero section. Since B is integral, multiplication by such a section gives an injection
$$
 H^0(B,mN+F)\hookrightarrow H^0(B,mC+F).
$$
Serre vanishing and the Hilbert polynomial on the projective (n-1)-dimensional B bound the latter dimension by O(m^{n-1}). This proves (3).

The actual Cartier restriction sequence
$$
 0\longrightarrow O_X(mL)\longrightarrow O_X(mL+B)
       \longrightarrow O_B((mL+B)|_B)\longrightarrow0
$$
therefore gives
$$
 0\le h^0(X,mL+B)-h^0(X,mL)\le O(m^{n-1}).
$$
Combining this with (2) proves the stronger asymptotic
$$
 h^0(X,mL)=\frac{L^n}{n!}m^n+O(m^{n-1}),                            \tag{4}
$$
and thus (1). Replacing L by dL proves the rational statement and independence of d. Nonnegativity follows from nonnegative section counts, or from nef intersection continuity.

## 3. Why the characteristic-zero proof has no cardinality restriction

The complex vanishing theorem suffices here without an embedding of the original k into C. For each fixed m, the finite presentation data X, L, B, the canonical bundle and their tensor identifications descend to a finitely generated subfield k0 of k over Q. Smoothness, projectivity and ampleness of mL+B-K_X descend along the faithfully flat field extension. An algebraic closure of k0 embeds in C. Apply complex Kodaira vanishing after that embedding. Cohomology of the descended quasi-coherent sheaf commutes with each flat field extension, so vanishing returns first to k0 and then to k. This is done for each m; it does not require countability of k, a common analytic embedding of k, or a cardinality restriction on the original variety.

Flat cohomology base change in this application has a direct finite-affine-cover proof: X is separated and quasi-compact, so its finite affine-cover Cech complex computes coherent cohomology; extension of scalars tensors that complex, and flatness commutes with its cohomology. The cited Stacks proof makes precisely this argument. The Hodge-theoretic foundations of complex Kodaira vanishing remain an explicitly imported classical theorem, rather than a newly reconstructed foundation.

## 4. Actual big decomposition and minimal reference

If L is nef and section-big, (4) implies L^n>0. Conversely L^n>0 implies section-bigness by (4). One can obtain an actual ample-plus-effective decomposition without finite generation: take a sufficiently very ample smooth integral divisor H when n>=2, and a finite effective H when n=1. The argument for (3), now with fixed twist zero on H, gives h^0(H,mL|_H)=O(m^{n-1}). Positive order-m^n section growth then implies H^0(X,mL-H) is nonzero for some sufficiently large divisible m. For a nonzero section s, set D=div(s)/m and A=H/m. The actual rational tensor identity is L=A+D; A is ample and D is effective. No contraction or semiampleness is used.

For the canonical reference, for every positive rational epsilon and actual ample H the class L+epsilon H is ample. Generators of a sufficiently divisible power have canonical zero-weight scalar exactly zero at every original analytic point. Taking epsilon down to zero supplies a genuine defining numerical net for zero. The maximum principle in a fixed larger ample cone gives u<=u(v_triv) for every genuine theta-potential, including unbounded ones. Thus zero has minimal singularities, and P_theta(0)=0 for the genuine obstacle envelope.

Under the bounded-nef products and intrinsic floor construction of own132--134, the raw top operator has mass L^n, now equal to the ordinary section volume. When that number is zero, positivity makes all bounded top measures zero; the top floor energy and top operator are zero as well. There is no division by this mass and no uniqueness assertion for a nonconstant cone. This last operator statement depends on the separately identified intrinsic construction; the algebraic equality (1) does not depend on it.
