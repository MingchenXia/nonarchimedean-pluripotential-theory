# Fixed section systems: an explicit energy-loss obstruction

This is an unreviewed supporting calculation for original P16.5, using the actual section energy and entire genuine energy of own143--145. It explains a failure of a proposed regularization route; it neither refutes the original big-class equation nor closes an original problem.

## 1. A genuine fixed-system function on the original carrier

Let X,k,L,n,V have the general-big hypotheses of own143. Fix a positive divisible integer M and finitely many nonzero actual sections s_1,...,s_r of ML. Let I be their base ideal, defined using local trivializations of ML, and

    phi=(1/M) max_j log|s_j|.

This is a genuine GF_L function, with phi<=0 and phi(v_triv)=0. Resolve and principalize I on a projective birational p:X' -> X with X' smooth. Write I O_X'=O_X'(-B), B=sum beta_l B_l effective, and put

    A=p^*L-B/M.

The pulled back chosen sections generate MA everywhere; thus A is nef and semiample. They give phi(v)=-(1/M)v(I), including value -infinity when the ideal vanishes on the semivaluation support. Suppose that the explicit moving-volume gap is positive:

    delta=V-vol_X'(A)>0.                                 (1.1)

This is a conditional hypothesis on this section system, not an assertion that every non-finitely-generated big bundle has such a gap in every degree.

## 2. Continuous model caps and the true relative energy

For real k>0 let f_k=max(phi,-k). This is an original continuous real function: each section norm is continuous with values in [0,1], and the logarithm capped at -k is continuous, even at its zeros. Rational k makes this a rational model function by the original model-function calculus. The f_k decrease pointwise to phi on the entire original carrier, including the ideal pole set.

The genuine envelopes w_k=P_theta(f_k) satisfy phi<=w_k<=f_k and therefore decrease pointwise to the actual phi. Own145 decreasing-net energy continuity and agreement on obstacle envelopes give

    E_theta(phi)=inf_k E_theta^obs(f_k).                  (2.1)

Each w_k lies between V_theta-k and V_theta, so each has finite energy; this does not imply that their decreasing limit does.

## 3. A positive fraction of actual section weights is lost

Choose a small rational epsilon in (0,1) such that

    V_epsilon=vol_X'(p^*L-(1-epsilon)B/M)<V.

It exists by volume continuity and (1.1). This class is big: it is (1-epsilon)A+epsilon p^*L, the sum of a pseudoeffective class and a big class. In divisible degree m let

    U_m=H^0(X',m[p^*L-(1-epsilon)B/M])

inside R_m=H^0(X,mL), using p_*O_X'=O_X and the actual line-bundle pullback. It consists precisely of sections whose pulled back order along every B_l is at least m(1-epsilon)beta_l/M.

If s is not in U_m, some component with beta_l>0 satisfies

    ord_B_l(s)/m < (1-epsilon)beta_l/M.

Use the original valuation x=(k M/beta_l)ord_B_l. For rational k it is a rational divisorial point of the original carrier. At this point phi(x)=-k and f_k(x)=-k. Consequently its actual filtration weight from own144 satisfies

    W_m(f_k;s)=inf_x [mf_k(x)-log|s|(x)]
              <=-mk+(kM/beta_l)ord_B_l(s)
              <-m epsilon k.                           (3.1)

Every section's weight is <=0, by evaluation at v_triv. Moreover, the high-weight flag {0} union {s:W_m(f_k;s)>=-m epsilon k} is a vector subspace contained in U_m by (3.1). Hence at most dim U_m of its r_m adapted-basis weights can be at least -m epsilon k. The remaining at least r_m-dim U_m weights are strictly less than this negative threshold. Taking floors cannot increase the sum. Thus

    e_m(f_k)<=-epsilon k (n!/m^n)(r_m-dim U_m).            (3.2)

The two true section-asymptotic limits apply to the actual big L and to the actual big rational bundle in U_m. Taking sufficiently divisible m -> infinity in (3.2), and using the original section-energy limit of own144, gives

    E_theta^obs(f_k)<=-epsilon k [V-V_epsilon].            (3.3)

This estimate is uniform in k along positive rational k. Since V-V_epsilon>0, (2.1)--(3.3) prove

    E_theta(phi)=-infinity.                              (3.4)

This detects the horizontal base-ideal volume loss through actual original-carrier divisorial evaluations. It does not identify that volume with a different ambient equation or normalize a deficient mass.

The conclusion also covers any finite real weights in the same chosen section system. The weighted maximum lies between phi plus the least weight and phi plus the greatest weight, as literal inequalities including its poles. Energy monotonicity and the constant-shift identity therefore still give energy -infinity. No difference of infinite pointwise values is taken.

## 4. Why weak fixed-L FS approximation is insufficient

The actual big decomposition L=A_0+D_0, with A_0 ample and D_0 effective, permits a useful weak approximation construction. If phi_i is a GF function on actual L_i with numerical class close to theta, choose epsilon_i>0 tending to zero such that

    (1+epsilon_i)L-L_i-epsilon_i D_0

is ample. Tensoring the original sections with the canonical D_0 section and with a finite generating system of this ample difference places

    eta_i=(phi_i+epsilon_i log|s_D0|)/(1+epsilon_i)

in GF_L as the same all-point function. On every fixed original divisorial valuation, the added divisor value is finite, so eta_i tends to the original u there when phi_i tends to u and epsilon_i tends to zero. Rational denominators are absorbed in actual tensor powers. This is a fixed-L weak approximation argument, not a monotone or finite-energy approximation argument.

Whenever such an eta_i has the moving-volume gap of (1.1), the preceding calculation gives E_theta(eta_i)=-infinity, however small its gap is. Thus weak density of actual GF_L functions cannot by itself establish strong finite-energy density or justify operator limits. This warning is conditional on the proved volume gap for each particular stage. It does not assert that every GF stage has that gap, or that no stages of full moving volume exist.

For example, even with ample L on a positive-dimensional X, a one-section system has A=0 after removal of its divisor and has this gap. Conversely the fixed negative-part surface example L=2H+E on Bl_p P2 has complete-system moving part 2H and volume 4=vol(L); this argument gives no energy loss for that full system. Ordinary top intersection 3 is irrelevant to both examples.

## 5. The route that still needs proof

After subtracting eta_i(v_triv), relative floors max(eta_i,V_theta-k) are genuine by own145 and have finite energy bounded below by -kV. They preserve the mandatory minimal-reference singularities instead of forcing a bounded original psh metric. To use them for the full equation, one must prove their energy convergence, a target-independent measure limit on every genuine relative-E1 function, mixed and weighted estimates, comparison, and uniqueness. Weak fixed-L approximation alone proves none of these statements. The independent reviews and original research inputs remain frozen; this new diagnostic is not hot feedback to an active worker.
