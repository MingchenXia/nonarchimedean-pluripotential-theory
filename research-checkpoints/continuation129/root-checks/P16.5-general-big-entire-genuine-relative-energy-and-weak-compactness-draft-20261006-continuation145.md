# General big bundles: entire genuine relative energy, monotone nets and weak compactness

This is an unreviewed continuation of own143--144, with all of their external algebraic and general-envelope dependencies retained. It defines energy on actual functions of the entire original psh cone, proves exact relative-floor characterization, decreasing-net energy continuity, weak upper semicontinuity and normalized compact energy sublevels. It does not yet extend the Monge--Ampere operator to that entire space, prove its mixed/weighted continuity, or solve the original equation. Original P16.5 remains open.

## 1. Genuine operations without an abstract net-flattening lemma

Keep smooth integral projective X over algebraically closed trivially valued characteristic-zero k, of any cardinality, and any actual big rational L, theta=c1(L), V=vol(L)>0. Genuine PSH(theta) has exactly the original decreasing generically finite FS-net definition, and V_theta=P_theta(0) is the genuine canonical minimal reference established by the published envelope input of own141. The weak topology is the original pointwise topology on X^div, including its generic valuation.

Here are direct constructions of the operations needed below. An actual ample rational bundle B has a finite generating set in a divisible power. Its weight-zero FS function is identically zero: at each analytic point at least one generator is nonzero at its center and has trivial norm one. Tensoring by these generators therefore embeds a generically finite FS function in the class of an actual bundle A into that of A+B with exactly the same function.

Let u,v in PSH(theta) be given by decreasing genuine FS nets phi_i on actual L_i and psi_j on actual M_j, with numerical classes tending to theta. Choose an ample H. For each sufficiently late pair i,j choose rational epsilon_i,j>0 tending to zero such that L_i+epsilon_i,j H-M_j is ample. Set B_i,j=L_i+epsilon_i,j H. Both B_i,j-L_i and B_i,j-M_j are ample. The zero-FS tensor construction places phi_i and psi_j in the same actual B_i,j without changing their values. Their maximum is a GF FS function on B_i,j; its product-index net is decreasing to max(u,v), and c1(B_i,j)->theta. This is one actual defining net, so max(u,v) is genuine. No monotonicity of the numerical epsilon values is required, since the function values are independent of them.

Real convex combinations are genuine by another single-net construction. Take a tail on which phi_i and psi_j are <=C for a fixed finite C. For 0<a<1 choose increasing positive rational a_l->a and b_l->1-a. The actual GF FS functions

    a_l(phi_i-C)+b_l(psi_j-C)+C

are defined by rational tensor powers on actual bundles a_l L_i+b_l M_j. They decrease in the three product indices because the shifted functions are nonpositive. Their numerical classes tend to theta, and their pointwise limit is au+(1-a)v, including the -infinity convention. Addition of a real constant is immediate at each GF stage. The cases a=0,1 are immediate. This proves the needed cone operations directly, including real coefficients.

Choose an actual ample Omega with Omega-theta ample numerically. On a tail, Omega-L_i is ample, and the same zero-FS tensor construction makes the entire defining net for u a GF FS net on Omega. Thus every genuine u belongs to PSH(Omega) as the same all-point function. This does not identify its intrinsic finite energy with ambient ample finite energy. It does give the standard ample finite-coordinate bound on every divisorial valuation: for normalized u, -T_Omega(v)<=u(v)<=0. The finite growth of divisorial valuations follows by the intersection estimate of the complete local primary Proposition1.28, literal LF922--931. Alternatively the granted actual-AMPLE G0 supplies this supporting bound within its exact ample domain. The full actual G0 body remains the sole equation grant; no nonample equation is granted.

In fact the finite-coordinate bound has a direct stage proof here: every GF FS function phi_i on the same actual Omega satisfies phi_i(v)>=phi_i(v_triv)-T_Omega(v), since each nonzero section in its maximum has m^(-1)v(s)<=T_Omega(v). The finite maximum inherits this bound. Let the original decreasing net tend to u; its generic values tend to u(v_triv), and the bound follows without an abstract flattening or a new ample regularization step.

## 2. Actual continuous-obstacle envelopes decrease to every genuine function

For any genuine u, let C_u be the directed set of original continuous real functions f>=u, ordered so that functions decrease. It is nonempty because a genuine u is bounded above, and is directed by minimum. On the compact Hausdorff X^an an upper semicontinuous function is the pointwise infimum of its continuous majorants, including value -infinity. For completeness, at a point x and any real a>u(x), choose a neighborhood where u<a, a global upper bound B, and a continuous cutoff that is zero at x and one outside that neighborhood. The function interpolating from a to max(a,B) is a majorant with value a at x. Normality of compact Hausdorff spaces supplies this cutoff.

Since u is a genuine psh minorant of each f,

    u <= P_theta(f) <= f.

Both outer bounds have pointwise infimum u. Therefore the genuine continuous-obstacle envelopes P_theta(f) decrease on every original analytic point to the actual original u. This constructs an approximation on the original carrier for all genuine u; it does not replace them by formal completion elements.

Rational model majorants also suffice. Given a continuous majorant f and epsilon>0, uniform model density yields a rational model g with ||g-f||<epsilon/2; add a rational constant in [epsilon/2,epsilon] to obtain g'>=f. Rational model majorants are directed by their model-function minimum and have the same pointwise infimum u. Arbitrary cardinality and the original net are retained.

## 3. Energy of the entire genuine cone

Use E_theta^obs on continuous obstacles from own144, with raw mass V and E_theta^obs(0)=0. Define on actual genuine functions

    E_theta(u)=inf {E_theta^obs(f): f continuous and f>=u}.    (3.1)

The value lies in R union {-infinity}; a constant upper majorant gives a finite upper bound. It is unchanged if the infimum is restricted to rational model majorants, by the V-Lipschitz obstacle energy. It is monotone in u, and shifting the majorants gives

    E_theta(u+c)=E_theta(u)+cV.

It agrees exactly with own144 on every genuine continuous-obstacle envelope w=P_theta(g). Indeed any f>=w has P_theta(f)>=w, and envelope-order monotonicity of own144 gives E_theta^obs(f)>=E_theta^obs(g). Conversely g is a continuous majorant of w. Taking the infimum gives E_theta(w)=E_theta^obs(g).

In particular E_theta(V_theta)=0 and E_theta(V_theta-k)=-kV. For normalized genuine u, sup u=u(v_triv)=0 and u<=V_theta, so E_theta(u)<=0. The normalization of V_theta is also zero: it is a minorant of zero and has generic value zero by the published reference construction.

There is no subtraction of -infinity values in (3.1), and no arbitrary target measure occurs in the definition.

## 4. Exact energy continuity for decreasing nets

Suppose u_i is any decreasing net of genuine functions with pointwise genuine limit u. Write ell=inf_i E_theta(u_i), so ell>=E_theta(u) by monotonicity. Fix a continuous f>=u and epsilon>0. The closed compact sets

    A_i={x: u_i(x)>=f(x)+epsilon}

are decreasing and have empty intersection, because u<=f. Compactness and directedness imply A_i is empty for one index and hence every later index. Thus u_i<=f+epsilon eventually, so

    ell <= E_theta^obs(f+epsilon)=E_theta^obs(f)+epsilon V.

Take the infimum over f and then epsilon down to zero. This proves

    E_theta(u_i) decreases to E_theta(u).                   (4.1)

If E_theta(u)=-infinity, apply the same argument to majorants with arbitrarily negative finite obstacle energy. This is a topological compact-set argument, not Borel monotone convergence applied to a possibly uncountable net.

For normalized u set

    u_k=max(u,V_theta-k).

The direct maximum construction makes u_k genuine, and u_k decreases pointwise to u. Where V_theta=-infinity, u also equals -infinity and each floor has that same value; no extended subtraction is taken. Monotonicity gives

    -kV <= E_theta(u_k) <=0,
    E_theta(u)=inf_k E_theta(u_k).                           (4.2)

Consequently the actual intrinsic relative finite-energy class

    E_theta^1={u in PSH(theta): E_theta(u)>-infinity}

is exactly the class with uniformly bounded relative-floor energy cost after generic normalization. The reference singularity type is built into these genuine floors. This is the entire original cone, not an ample ambient E1 or a fixed set of continuous envelopes.

## 5. Finite-divisorial supporting planes and weak upper semicontinuity

For a rational model f_D let mu_D be the actual finite divisorial measure of own143--144, with mass V. Concavity and the derivative of the continuous obstacle energy yield, for every continuous g,

    E_theta^obs(g) <= E_theta^obs(f_D)+ integral(g-f_D) dmu_D.

Take the full decreasing net of continuous majorants g of a genuine u. On the finitely many atoms of mu_D the integrals tend to the finite sum of u values. The left side tends to (3.1). Hence

    E_theta(u) <= A_D(u),
    A_D(u)=E_theta^obs(f_D)+ integral(u-f_D) dmu_D.            (5.1)

Every A_D(u) is finite: a genuine u is finite on each divisorial or generic atom, as proved in Section1. If f_D itself majorizes u, the integral in A_D(u) is nonpositive and A_D(u)<=E_theta^obs(f_D). Model majorants suffice in (3.1), so

    E_theta(u)=inf over all rational models D of A_D(u).      (5.2)

The index set in (5.2) is independent of u. Each A_D is continuous for the original pointwise-divisorial topology, since it is a fixed finite coordinate sum. Thus E_theta is weakly upper semicontinuous on the entire genuine cone. The same formula makes it concave on this convex cone: an infimum of fixed affine functionals is concave. No continuity of evaluations at nondivisorial poles is assumed.

The general envelope primary input also gives compactness of normalized PSH(theta) in this exact weak topology, as recorded in own141. Therefore for every finite B the normalized cost sublevel

    {u in PSH(theta): u(v_triv)=0, E_theta(u)>=-B}

is weakly closed in that compact space, hence compact, and all its members are intrinsic E1. No metrizability, countability, support reduction, or ambient finite-energy assumption enters this proof.

## 6. Further conclusions not established here

Sections2--5 solve several energy/compactness gates left by own144 on actual genuine functions. They do not prove that the derivative measures of different continuous-obstacle majorant nets have the same limit at every intrinsic E1 endpoint. Uniform derivative control in own144 depends on a uniform bound on the continuous obstacles, which has not been proved for these majorant nets; bounded relative floors need not have uniformly bounded absolute continuous obstacles when V_theta is singular.

The general-big operator at every E1 function, model-compatible mixed/weighted products, comparison, finite-dual target characterization, coercivity and target integral continuity, actual attainment, and all-point uniqueness remain necessary original obligations. Concavity alone does not imply the quadratic energy scaling or the quantitative I estimates used in the nef proofs. They cannot be imported by replacing zero with V_theta without proof. The raw volume-zero cases also remain separately unresolved beyond the earlier scoped results. Every supporting proposal here remains independently unreviewed.
