## Precise background and own proofs

Standard commutative algebra used with explicit hypotheses: finite normalization of reduced excellent rings and their domain quotients (Nagata property); a normal one-dimensional Noetherian local domain is a DVR; lying over for integral extensions; Nakayama; Noetherian finite generation; m-adic quotient identification. Valuation extension to finite fields is reconstructed in n1 using a dominating valuation ring, a Zorn argument, and polynomial least-term cancellation. No separability or defectless assertion is needed.

Standard projective geometry used in n2: proper coherence for a proper Noetherian morphism; ample twisting and relative global generation over an affine base; ample openness in a finite real Cartier span and nef-plus-ample positivity on a projective scheme over a field; invariance of line-bundle ampleness under Noetherian nilpotent thickening; relative ampleness detected locally from fiber ampleness. Here the base is Spec of a Noetherian LOCAL ring, each primary blowup is projective and an isomorphism away from its closed point, so all contracted curves lie in its projective closed fiber. The fiber need not be reduced: its integral curves are those of its reduction, and nilpotent invariance transfers ampleness back. The positivity facts apply to reduced nonnormal fibers and arbitrary characteristic. We use actual Cartier effectivity, not Weil coefficient positivity. Rational approximation uses an effective integral presentation with coefficients rounded UP, so divisible multiples are actual negative integral Cartier divisors. Curve degree pullback is checked in the proof. These are classical background applications, not new source claims from BFJ or BdFFU.

Real/functional background: finite real coefficient spaces are complete; uniform limits of continuous functions; finite convex functions are continuous on relative interiors; compact Dini proof; infima over a product commute, including extended values. The compact Dini argument and infimum exchange are explicitly proved in n3/n4. No metric/sequential compactness hypothesis is used.
