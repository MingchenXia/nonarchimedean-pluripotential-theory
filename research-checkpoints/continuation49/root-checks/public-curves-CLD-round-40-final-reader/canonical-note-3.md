On X=M(K<x,y>/(xy)), write D_x and D_y for the two closed branch discs and o for their common rigid origin. Define f=0 on D_x and f=-a|y| on D_y. Both restrictions are continuous and agree at o, so f is a finite continuous obstacle. Its value at the y-Gauss point eta_y is -a.

Every full competitor restricts to a semipositive real-PL, hence classically psh, function on D_y. Since the constant -a is a global minorant, P_X(f)=-a on all of D_y, including o.

Define q_n=log max(|alpha|^n,|x|) and h_n=(a/(nA))q_n. The global rational-domain cover {|x|<=|alpha|^n}, {|x|>=|alpha|^n} verifies actual PL membership: q_n is respectively the constant n log|alpha| and log|x|, with x an analytic unit on the second domain. Thus h_n is GLOBAL real PL. GRa Proposition 4.2 makes q_n classically psh as max of a constant and log|x|, with the latter allowed to be -infinity where x vanishes; Theorem 4.3 makes h_n semipositive. It equals -a on D_y, is <=0 on D_x, and hence belongs to the FULL exact competitor union below f.
