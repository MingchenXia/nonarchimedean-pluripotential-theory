# Fresh self-review; not an independent verification

The argument is an unverified mathematical draft. This review records the obligations
checked during construction, and does not convert successful execution into proof.

- Global real-PL membership is supplied before positivity: local vertical Cartier
  star metrics and annulus charts give the G-local analytic-unit expressions on the
  whole compact curve. Positivity is checked at ordinary germs by proper-component
  degrees, not glued from a G-cover.
- Genus does not require a principal residue divisor. The star metric uses O(p_i);
  arbitrary real coefficients are allowed locally. Cycles impose no global integral
  commensurability assumption.
- Formal cuts use Gamma coordinates. In a discrete group, a finite separable root
  extension supplies a fine grid before formal subdivision. Arbitrary real breakpoints
  are approximated, not declared admissible subdivisions.
- Graph coordinatewise maxima are only operations on feasible finite vertex vectors.
  No closure of real-PL functions under pointwise maximum is assumed.
- The full-family upper bound uses harmonic comparison on the entire analytic curve.
  Compact maximum loci, actual relative boundary and all connected components are
  treated; omitted directions and type-4 points are not excluded.
- Arbitrary scalar extensions are handled before passage to a limit using the same
  graph comparison, boundary inclusion and a common extension with a finite Galois
  field. No scalar openness or scalar usc-regularization identity is assumed.
- Projective fibers are actual fibers over H(x). Their minimum obstacle is continuous
  by properness and relative smooth openness. Local sections, or a finite extension
  supplying a closed point of a proper factor, establish real-PL membership downstairs.
- The nodal example uses explicit global rational-domain charts for its competitors.
  It does not cite any book proposition or prior generated note.
- The extra homeomorphic-normalization sector claims ground-field envelope equality
  and continuity; its upstairs full-family scalar equality is deliberately not claimed.
- Higher-dimensional sharpness, general branched necessity, imperfect non-rig-smooth
  normal curves and noncompact relative-boundary classifications remain open.
