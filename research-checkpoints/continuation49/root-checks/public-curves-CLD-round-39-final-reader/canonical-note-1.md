These are checked published foundations and fresh stage bookkeeping. CD denotes the supplied arXiv:1204.6277v2 PDF, dated 25 July 2025. Definition 8.1.1 (p.195) defines the smallest subsheaf containing ordinary smooth q with d'd''q≥0 and closed under decreasing sequences. Lemma 8.1.4 (p.196) defines Psh_0 as those smooth functions, Psh_{α+1} by local decreasing sequential limits of Psh_α, and limit stages by local membership in earlier stages. Thus paper version v2 and ordinal stage 2 are distinct. Current positivity or classical pshness does not alone establish membership in a specified ordinal stage.

CD 12.4.1/12.4.6 supplies the canonical refined analytic G-tropical structure. CD 15.1.1(b)/15.1.2 describes ordinary smooth functions through ordinary open local expressions φ(-log|g_1|,…,-log|g_r|), with analytic units and smooth φ. G-local real PL and harmonic tropicalizations cannot replace this ordinary smoothness. CD 15.7.1 associates the tropical structure to a Hausdorff pure-dimensional analytic space with its actual analytic boundary; 15.7.2 records the boundary formula for domains. CD 12.4.5 and 8.1.5 preserve each ordinal stage under analytic morphisms, including scalar projections between different complete fields. On boundaryless tropical spaces, CD 8.3.1(b) converts current positivity into positive d'd'' forms for ordinary smooth functions.

GJR denotes the supplied arXiv:2111.05741. Definition 5.1 defines real PL using G-local real combinations of unit log norms. Definition 7.8 and 7.9 make LFS a semipositive real-PL class and supply ordinary local expressions as nonnegative combinations of finite maxima of negative log norms of analytic units. For each maximum, on its fixed ordinary neighborhood, use M_ε=ε log Σ exp(ℓ_i/ε). It satisfies 0≤M_ε−max ℓ_i≤ε log N, has positive Hessian, and ∂M_ε/∂ε=−Σp_i log p_i≥0. Consequently ε↓0 gives a decreasing ordinary smooth positive sequence. Finite nonnegative combinations preserve this construction. Thus LFS functions belong locally to Psh_1.

GJR Proposition 7.10 (pp.47–48) requires an interior point. Its proof produces a common ordinary open tube U=red^{-1}(V') and LFS h_i converging uniformly to a semipositive real-PL h. Choose ||h_{i_j}−h||_U≤e_j=4^{-j} and set b_j=h_{i_j}+2e_j. Then h+e_j≤b_j≤h+3e_j and b_{j+1}≤h+3e_{j+1}<h+e_j≤b_j. Each b_j belongs to stage 1, so b_j↓h proves stage 2. Each inner smooth sequence has a fixed ordinary neighborhood; the outer sequence is defined on U. A single smooth sequence on U approximating h is not proved by this argument.

GJR Proposition 15.11 (pp.80–81) explicitly assumes a smooth curve, meaning rig-smooth without boundary. It produces a decreasing net of weakly smooth psh functions on any relatively compact ordinary open subset. Remark 15.10 identifies weak and ordinary smooth sheaves when the residue field is algebraic over a finite field, or the curve is potentially locally P1. Remark 10.10 identifies its ordinary smooth sheaf with the CLD ordinary sheaf. After this identification, positive weak currents restrict to positive ordinary currents, and CD 8.3.1(b) gives stage-0 form positivity. Outside these cases harmonic germs can be non-smooth; this is not proof that stage 1 or 2 fails.
