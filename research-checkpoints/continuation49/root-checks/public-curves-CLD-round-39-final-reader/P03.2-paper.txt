Countable local topology and extraction of an actual sequence
Let K have a countable dense subset. A strict K-affinoid algebra A has a countable norm-dense
subset: polynomials with coefficients in a countable dense subset of K are dense in a Tate algebra,
and their images are dense in a quotient Banach algebra. For a bounded multiplicative seminorm x,
|a(x)| ≤ ∥a∥, and
|a(x)| − |b(x)| ≤ ∥a − b∥.
Thus all evaluation functions on M(A) are uniform limits of evaluations on this countable set. The
evaluations on that set separate points and generate the topology. The compact Hausdorff space
M(A) therefore embeds in a countable product of real compact intervals, so is metrizable and
second countable. A relatively compact ordinary open subset W of a good Hausdorff analytic space
has compact closure covered by finitely many interiors of affinoid neighborhoods. Consequently W
is second countable. This proof needs no global paracompactness of X.

What remains unresolved for the original atom
The sufficient residue-field and countability assumptions are not shown necessary. A non-smooth
harmonic germ does not establish absence from a decreasing CLD stage. The general curve argument
only reaches stage 3; reducing it to stage 2 or 1 without the GJR ordinary/weak sheaf coincidence
needs an additional construction. Countability was used to extract genuine sequences from nets,
and no replacement for that step over general nonseparable fields is proved.
Theorem A includes the bijective-normalization curve range over the specified perfect ground fields.
It does not prove ordinary approximation descent for arbitrary imperfect fields merely satisfying a
perfected-normalization condition. Neither Theorem A nor C includes curve relative-boundary points;
GRc’s classical boundary extension proposition does not furnish a CLD ordinary approximation
sequence there. GJR 7.10’s interior hypothesis and GJR 15.11’s boundaryless hypothesis are
respected throughout the positive proofs.
The projective-bundle sector is geometric and unrestricted in its obstacle and competitor family,
but the higher-dimensional argument depends on proper connected fibers that force all competitors to descend. It does not extend to open-disc products, arbitrary normal or geometrically
unibranch domains, general proper fibrations without ordinary local sections, or the supplied
nonsplit-node/product and codimension-two pinching claims. Those dossier claims were not imported and their proofs have not been established here. Exact scalar pullback of the proved envelope
is handled, while exact identification of all competitor families after arbitrary scalar extension is
neither needed for positivity nor asserted.
There is no sharp necessary-and-sufficient classification for the original full range in this argument.
P03.2 remains open, and candidate=false is required. No P03.1 or P03.3 classification is claimed;
finite usc envelope positivity does not imply continuity.
