L24: 【8†Lemma 29.43.1 (Valuative criterion for properness).】 Let $S$ be a scheme. Let $f : X \to Y$ be a morphism of schemes over $S$. Assume $f$ is of finite type and quasi-separated. Then the following are equivalent
L25: 
L26:   1. $f$ is proper,
L27: 
L28:   2. $f$ satisfies the valuative criterion (Schemes, Definition 【15†26.20.3】),
L29: 
L30:   3. given any commutative solid diagram
L31: 
L32: \[ \xymatrix{ \mathop{\mathrm{Spec}}(K) \ar[r] \ar[d] & X \ar[d] \\ \mathop{\mathrm{Spec}}(A) \ar[r] \ar@{-->}[ru] & Y } \]
L33: where $A$ is a valuation ring with field of fractions $K$, there exists a unique dotted arrow making the diagram commute.
L34: 
L35: Proof. Part (3) is a reformulation of (2). Thus the lemma is a formal consequence of Schemes, Proposition 【16†26.20.6】 and Lemma 【17†26.22.1】, Lemma 【18†26.22.2】, and the definitions. $\square$
