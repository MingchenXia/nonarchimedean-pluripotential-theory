L23: 【8†Proposition 26.20.6 (Valuative criterion of universal closedness).】 Let $f$ be a quasi-compact morphism of schemes. Then $f$ is universally closed if and only if $f$ satisfies the existence part of the valuative criterion.

L25: Proof. This is a formal consequence of Lemmas 【15†26.20.2】 and 【10†26.20.5】 above. $\square$
