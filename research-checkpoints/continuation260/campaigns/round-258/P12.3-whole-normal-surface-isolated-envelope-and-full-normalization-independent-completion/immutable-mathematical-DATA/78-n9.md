# n9. Geometric curve envelopes and conventions

Use the entire qualified G331 and E350, and n1. For every excellent reduced local curve, its finite normalization has finitely many local DVR centres q. Away from the closed point it is the same generic field on each component. Thus S/R is supported only at the closed point, and its conductor contains a maximal-ideal power (or is the unit ideal). Every boundary point has finite conductor value, so every normalization fibre is singleton. The full link is the finite discrete set nu_q=ord_q/a_q, a_q=ord_q(a)>0; centres, not only minimal components, index its points.

On a DVR the actual closed-point Cartier divisor is effective and there are no contracted curve degree tests. Its arbitrary nonnegative real multiples give all finite nonpositive boundary constants; zero is the unit model. Semilocal finite products and G331's positive C-descent therefore prove C_R is EVERY finite nonpositive branch vector with genuine completed Cartier positivity. The finite vectors max(h,-N) decrease to every extended nonpositive vector h, proving D_R=[-infinity,0]^q by an actual representation. Only after this geometric membership proof does clipping give
\[
P_{geom}(phi)(q)=\min(phi(q),0)=P_{ideal}(phi)(q).
\]
This holds for every extended branch obstacle, including positive infinity, zero, mixed zero and partial bottom. Finite outputs are continuous; extended outputs are USC on this finite set. It is the complete ideal-envelope comparison through independently defined Cartier positivity, not an ideal-order completion renamed geometric.

Primary changes multiply branch coordinates by ord_q(a)/ord_q(b), exactly the one-factor cocycle, and commute with clipping. Qualified E350's curve reference is sum_q f_q a_q delta_nu_q with actual residue degrees f_q, including inseparability. Energy is the weighted sum of branch coordinates and is finite exactly for finite vectors; a bottom branch has positive reference mass. The smooth DVR is the one-point case, with maximal-ideal reference mass one. Dimension zero is empty and has zero measure/energy without negative arity. Nonpure lower-dimensional centres remain function and full-fibre data although ambient top-degree products can charge them zero. Nilpotents are reduced away and generic nilpotent intersection lengths are not reconstructed. Unsurjective arbitrary finite branch maps are not part of the normal dominating-cover theorem; bottom must remain available for their empty-fibre phenomena.

These valuative envelopes are distinct from literal logarithmic radial analytic extensions. At a complex two-disc node, the boundary vector (-1,0) is geometric here. A literal zero radial slope forces a common nodal value >=0 by USC; a negative slope on the other disc then conflicts with its maximum principle and outer value zero. The dossier's CLD radial assertion is retained as a scoped distinction, not used as a new general CLD theorem or as a reason to remove this geometric branch vector. No identification of general boundary traces with literal radial extensions is claimed.
