# n4. Surface nef projection and the full finite obstacle envelope

Use G331, n1 and n3. Fix a regular ACTUAL primary surface model T and E_a=sum b_i E_i as constructed in n3. Let a geometric core competitor be presented by a negative effective real curve-nef Cartier divisor D' on another actual primary model. A regular common refinement T' is itself an actual primary blowup by n3. Pullback preserves actual effectivity and curve-nefness. Write mu:T'->T, and D=mu_*D'. This is a negative exceptional real Cartier divisor on regular T. For each E_i, the effective Cartier pullback mu*E_i is a nonnegative sum of proper curves contracted to R. The actual supported degree projection formula gives D.E_i=D'.mu*E_i>=0. Explicitly, on each curve component C' of D', a point image gives degree zero for the pulled-back bundle, and a curve image gives its degree multiplied by the positive function-field degree; these are precisely the coefficients of mu_*C'. Expanding D' proves the projection identity, and symmetry of actual surface Cartier intersections exchanges its two slots. This is componentwise divisor-cycle projection and the ordinary degree rule already used in G331 (complete Stacks 0AYA); it is applied to actual supported cycles before a Chow quotient. Thus D is nef over R.

F=D'-mu*D is supported only on mu-exceptional curves, since a proper birational map of normal surfaces is an isomorphism at generic codimension-one points. For such a curve C, mu*D.C=0 and F.C=D'.C>=0. The corresponding principal submatrix of the intersection matrix of T' has negative inverse by n3. If f is the coefficient vector of F, M_J f>=0 implies f<=0. Hence F is actually negative effective, not just numerically bounded. Evaluation gives h_D'<=h_D on EVERY semivaluation support. For this statement first pass to Frac(R/p), where p is the support, lift its valuation-ring morphism to the proper model from its generic point in the punctured spectrum, and evaluate Cartier local equations. Effective exceptional equations have nonnegative finite value. No map from Frac(R) into Frac(R/p) is presumed. Coefficients on E_i are exact: c_i=b_i h_D'(nu_i), where nu_i=ord_Ei/b_i.

The same projection works for any h in C. Choose actual core approximants converging uniformly to h. Their projected coefficients b_i h_j(nu_i) converge to c_i=b_i h(nu_i); coefficient order and M c>=0 survive. At a valued centre define v(E_i) from a local Cartier equation, zero if the centre is off E_i. All are finite, nonnegative, and sum b_i v(E_i)=v(a)=1, so the projected evaluations converge uniformly and h<=sum c_i v(E_i). This limit is a genuine negative effective nef real Cartier divisor on the actual primary T.

For bounded h in D use its SPECIFIED actual C-net. The same finitely many coefficients decrease to b_i h(nu_i), remain finite, and the finite evaluation sum commutes with the limit. Thus its projected nef model function again dominates h. For an unbounded competitor below an obstacle f>=-B, the actual representation max(g_alpha,-B) proves that max(h,-B) is in bounded D, still below f and at least h. This avoids any unproved closure operation.

Now let f be a finite SIGNED rational or real linear combination of finitely many primary ideal profiles and constants. Construct T by n3 for those ideals and a. Its actual exceptional Cartier representation makes
\[
f(v)=\sum_i b_i f(nu_i)v(E_i)
\]
on ALL supports. Choose B>0 with f>=-B. Consider the finite compact polytope
\[
-B b_i\le c_i\le\min(0,b_i f(nu_i)),\qquad M c\ge0.
\]
It is nonempty because c=-B b is feasible. It is stable under coordinatewise maximum: in row i choose the vector attaining the maximum ith coordinate; all other coordinates only increase, and off-diagonal entries are nonnegative. Choose a maximizer of each coordinate and take their finite maximum. This yields a greatest vector c*. Its actual negative effective nef Cartier divisor sum c*_i E_i on T evaluates to H* in the geometric core. Since every v(E_i)>=0, H*<=f everywhere. Every D-minorant, after the clamp and projection above, has a feasible vector and is at most H*. Thus H* is the FULL geometric envelope, not an envelope only among competitors already determined on T.

This proof requires neither SNC intersections nor a singular retraction. Its decisive all-support dominance is actual Cartier negativity of F on a common actual primary regular model.
