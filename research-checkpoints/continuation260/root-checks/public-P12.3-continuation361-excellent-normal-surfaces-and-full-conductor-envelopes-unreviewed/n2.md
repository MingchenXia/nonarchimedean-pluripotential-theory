# n2. Actual closure under decreasing geometric nets

Use G331 and n1. Suppose u_i in D decreases, and u=inf_i u_i. On an excellent normal local domain, G331's normal zero dichotomy says that a D-function vanishing anywhere is identically zero. If u vanishes at a point, all u_i vanish there, hence u=0. Otherwise choose a nonzero u_i0. Upper semicontinuity and compactness give u_i0<=-c for a finite c>0; this includes bottom. Thus u<=-c.

For every finite continuous F>u there is q in C with u<q<F. Indeed the directed open sets {u_i<F} cover X. Choose a common tail index i>=i0. An actual C-representation g_alpha decreasing to u_i has one term g with g<F and g<-c/2 throughout X, by two compact directed covers. Let m=min(F-g)>0 and choose 0<b<1 with b||g||<m/2. Then q=(1-b)g is in C, below F, and exceeds u at every finite point by at least bc/2. At a bottom point q is finite and also exceeds u. No positive constant has been added to C.

All strict C-majorants of u contain zero and are downward directed: apply insertion to the continuous minimum of two such majorants, which need not itself be psh. Their infimum is u. For x and finite t>u(x), choose u(x)<ell<t and an open neighbourhood where u<ell. A compact-Hausdorff Urysohn cutoff supported there interpolates between ell at x and a global strict upper bound outside, giving continuous F>u with F(x)<t. The bottom case uses any finite ell<t. Insertion supplies a C-majorant whose value at x is below t. Reverse pointwise order therefore gives an ACTUAL decreasing C-net with infimum u.

For finitely many semilocal normal centres take finite products of these index sets. For arbitrary excellent reduced R, pull u_i back to its finite normalization S. Centrewise infima belong to D_S by the preceding proof. The tuple is constant on EVERY full restriction fibre, since each pulled-back term is. G331's positive D-descent puts its descent, exactly u, in D_R. This includes conductor-infinite and internal fibres; the empty case is immediate. Thus D is closed under nonempty decreasing D-nets on every excellent reduced germ. No closure under nondirected infima, finite minima, increasing limits or arbitrary nested-net flattening is asserted.

Only after proving membership use the entire qualified E350: for a decreasing E1-net, u=inf u_i is in D, and u is in E1 iff sup_i F_infinity(u_i^[n]) is finite. Its weak mixed-measure and capacity convergence then apply at their exact stated conditions. E=-F_infinity/n, with ambient top-degree n, and lower-dimensional centres retain their class/fibre constraints. The criterion is not used to manufacture membership of an increasing obstacle supremum.
