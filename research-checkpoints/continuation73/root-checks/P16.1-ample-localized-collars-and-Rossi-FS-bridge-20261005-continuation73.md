# Ample localized collars and a direct FS bridge for the Rossi construction

This is an unreviewed root proposal. It supplies concrete algebraic and analytic steps for reconstructing the continuation72 curve-to-nef route, not an accepted singular envelope theorem. None of the following is a premise until independently proved with the actual primary hypotheses. The original P16.1 completion criteria remain unchanged.

## 1. The localized affine open can be an actual ample principal open

Let X be projective over algebraically closed trivially valued k and L ample. For an analytic point x, first choose a sufficiently high very ample power of L and a global section s not vanishing at x's algebraic support. Its standard open Y=D(s) is affine. A basic analytic neighborhood inside Y^an has finitely many upper and lower evaluation constraints. For each positive lower bound, the constrained regular function f has f(x) nonzero; localize at f to replace that constraint by an upper bound on f^-1. No zero-valued function is inverted.

Every f in k[Y] is a quotient tau/s^d for a global section tau of a suitable power of L, after increasing d. This is the standard section-localization identity for an ample principal open; write or verify it even on reduced nonnormal X, rather than using normalization. With finitely many such f_i, the resulting affine open is

    Y'=D(s) intersect D(tau_1) intersect ... intersect D(tau_r).

The product s times the tau_i is one global section a of a positive ample power of L. Thus Y'=D(a), exactly on the original X. The extra factor s prevents unintended points on the previous boundary from entering D(a). A power of a is still a distinguished section with exactly this nonvanishing locus.

Apply the same section-localization identity to every regular constraint g_j on Y'. Choose one common power m and global numerator sections sigma_j with g_j=sigma_j/a^m. Include a global generating section system for the very ample bundle carrying a^m and all sigma_j. It may be a redundant generating system; the given numerator sections need not be a linearly independent basis. Ratios of the complete very ample system to a^m generate k[Y'], by its standard closed affine embedding. Assign large rational bounds to the additional ratios so x remains strictly inside them. Retain the original rational upper constraints with smaller margins than the original analytic neighborhood.

The resulting weighted section maximum is a genuine global FS reference, and its local weight relative to a^m is an explicit finite rational maximum of the constraint log-norms, the extra generator log-norms, and a rational negative constant. Tensor and scalar conventions must be kept consistent: if v=(1/m)max(log|sigma_j/a^m|-B_j,m lambda), then a rational constraint C_j on log|g_j| is encoded by B_j=C_j, not mC_j unless the whole expression is rescaled accordingly. One may instead work in the larger ample power with no 1/m and rescale the entire later collar formula. Since the original target allows every ample line bundle, changing the auxiliary reference power does not change the target.

All generator bounds make Q={v<=0} compact in Y'^an. Strict margins at x and the negative floor give a rational epsilon>0 and P={v<=-epsilon} with x in interior(P) and P contained in interior(Q), while Q remains inside the required analytic neighborhood. The inclusion in interior(Q) follows from the uniform negative margin in every inequality on P, not merely from compactness. The same finite rational data become strictly affinoid after a finite Laurent extension. This completes the algebraic compactification step left as a gate in the previous neighborhood proposal, subject to verifying section localization and the exact FS conventions.

## 2. A continuous compact perturbation of an affine FS function

Let Y be any reduced affine variety and let b be a rational affine FS function in the actual Wu3.15(1) sense: a finite maximum of positive rational multiples of log|f_i|, rational constants, and a constant floor, with the f_i generating k[Y]. Its elementary compactification is explicit. Clear slopes by an integer multiple, add the original generators with sufficiently low weights if necessary, use the resulting closed affine embedding Y -> A^N, and take its projective closure Xbar in P^N. The hyperplane section a cuts out exactly Xbar\Y. The section maximum on O(1)|Xbar, divided by the cleared scalar, is a genuine rational FS metric whose local weight along a is b. It does not require Xbar to be normal or geometrically unibranch.

Suppose v is continuous and finite on Y^an, agrees with b outside a compact set contained in Y^an, and is locally a corrected relative-contact rational PL selection. Interpret v-b as the potential difference from this fixed FS reference. It is zero near the hyperplane boundary in Xbar^an, since that compact set is closed in the proper Hausdorff analytification and disjoint from the boundary. Hence v defines a continuous metric on the same ample rational line bundle on Xbar. Near the boundary it is exactly the reference metric; on the interior it has the prescribed finite rational local presentation. Its local weights remain relative-contact functions after a frame change by a unit logarithm.

Reconstruct the continuation72 strict rational-domain representation, curve tests, residue nef detection, global formal model, BE ample nef-to-FS theorem and exact Laurent Gauss descent for this particular metric. Those steps should imply genuine CPSH on Xbar without singular EP. This is an application to an actual global continuous metric, so it does not assume arbitrary local/global equality or manufacture a metric from incompatible pieces.

Uniform global approximation by genuine FS metrics now gives uniform approximation of v on the entire noncompact Y^an: subtracting the same local reference weight leaves exactly the same bounded potential difference as on compact Xbar^an. Verify conversion to the affine FS class, including its requirement that the functions generate k[Y]. A global generating section set is not automatically a closed embedding. One can use sufficiently high very ample powers and a complete global section system, appending missing sections at low weights so that they do not change the metric, or prove the corresponding genuine-FS density with this condition. Low weights exist because each additional section has bounded norm for the continuous metric on proper Xbar. Include a low constant branch and the original affine coordinate generators. Choose rational weights with arbitrarily small error if a source FS approximation initially has real weights.

If a decreasing affine FS sequence is needed rather than one approximation, select rational FS approximants w_j with |w_j-v|<=epsilon_j uniformly on Y, then add a rational shift 2epsilon_j. Choose positive rational epsilon_j with 3epsilon_(j+1)<=epsilon_j. The shifted functions lie between v+epsilon_j and v+3epsilon_j, so they decrease to v. Adding a rational constant preserves the actual affine FS class. This builds a sequence from uniform density without assuming metrizability, a countable source field, or a previously available decreasing global sequence.

## 3. Apply this only to the actual Rossi glued function

In Wu Theorem5.6, let L0 be the compact set, z in its polynomial hull outside L0, and V a relatively compact analytic neighborhood of z with V intersect L0 empty. If a polynomial f and rational t violate the proposed boundary maximum principle, choose a polynomial-defined rational polytope P containing the hull and avoiding the part of the boundary where log|f|>=t. A separating exhaustion u in the actual affine FS class vanishes on P and is positive outside it. Choose a positive integer C large enough that

    C u > log|f|-t on the entire boundary of V.

The compactness and strict inequality give a whole boundary neighborhood on which C u remains the winning branch. Therefore the function

    v=C u outside V,
    v=max(C u,log|f|-t) on V

is a continuous rational PL compact perturbation of the affine FS function C u. At zeros of f the finite branch C u wins on a smaller neighborhood; no infinite affine coordinate is assigned a nonzero coefficient there. Away from zeros it is a maximum of corrected contact functions and hence has a corrected contact minorant at each point. Arbitrary geometry of V creates no extra transition locus, because the branch is already fixed on a whole boundary collar. Finite compact covers supply the rational unit-coordinate presentations needed by the new route.

The preceding compactification lemma then supplies a uniform affine FS approximant separating z from L0. Indeed v|L0=0, while v(z)=log|f(z)|-t>0. An error below one third of this gap preserves strict separation. The elementary polynomial-hull/affine-FS-hull identity contradicts z's hull membership. Check the actual construction of P and u from polynomial inequalities independently; no general Runge theorem is an antecedent of that finite hull construction.

This would prove the arbitrary analytic-neighborhood Rossi application directly once the precise compact-perturbation FS bridge is established. It would remove the circular appeal to arbitrary affine psh regularization in Wu3.24 at this one step. It still does not prove controlled principal-open removable extension, the weak-input finite trace extension, singular-stratum Runge induction, weak/global identification, or singular EP. Those remain explicit obligations for the original equation.
