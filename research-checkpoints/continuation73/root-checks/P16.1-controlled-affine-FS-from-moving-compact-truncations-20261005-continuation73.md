# Controlled affine FS approximants from nested compact truncations

This is an unreviewed root reconstruction proposal. It depends on independently establishing the precise continuation72 curve-to-nef-to-FS bridge for continuous rational PL metrics. It does not assume singular EP. It addresses the controlled regularization antecedent in Wu3.30, and keeps the subsequent smooth envelope application and singular Runge induction separate.

## 1. Start with the actual already-regularizable input

Let Y be reduced affine over algebraically closed trivially valued k. Suppose u_j are rational affine FS functions decreasing pointwise to a generically finite usc u on all of Y^an. Let g be a rational affine FS exhaustion with u<=g. Shift its rational constant so g>=1. A fixed compactification determined by g realizes it as the local weight of an ample rational FS reference along a hyperplane section, with Y exactly its nonvanishing chart. The sublevels Q_R={g<=R} are compact and exhaust Y^an. This is the already-regularizable hypothesis; it is not inferred for an arbitrary local psh function.

Choose negative integers C_r decreasing to minus infinity and rational R_r increasing to infinity, with R_r>2-C_r. Let Q_r={g<=R_r}. For each r choose j_r>j_(r-1) so that

    u_(j_r) < g+1 on Q_r.

Existence follows from compactness and decreasing upper semicontinuity: the closed sets {u_j>=g+1} intersect Q_r decrease to the empty set because u<=g. No continuity of the limit or Dini theorem is needed. In fact the strict inequality holds on a neighborhood of Q_r because u_(j_r) and g are continuous.

On the compactification define the potential relative to the reference of twice the class of g by

    phi_r=max(u_(j_r)-2g,C_r) on Q_r,
    phi_r=C_r outside Q_r.

Near the boundary g=R_r, the first term is less than 1-R_r<C_r. Thus the constant branch wins on a whole analytic collar, and phi_r is a globally continuous metric potential. On the interior chart its weight is max(u_(j_r),C_r+2g), a finite rational PL maximum of actual affine FS functions. Outside Q_r it is the positive reference metric shifted by C_r. Its frame transitions are actual unit logarithms. Apply the reconstructed strict-G-cover and smooth-analytic-curve nef criterion, then the proper model/BE theorem and exact Laurent Gauss descent, to prove genuine CPSH for each phi_r. This step does not invoke arbitrary bounded local/global equality or an envelope theorem.

## 2. Check monotonicity across the changing collars

On Q_(r-1), u_(j_r)<=u_(j_(r-1)) and C_r<=C_(r-1), so phi_r<=phi_(r-1). On Q_r\Q_(r-1), the previous potential equals C_(r-1), whereas

    u_(j_r)-2g < 1-g <= 1-R_(r-1) < C_(r-1)

and C_r<=C_(r-1). Thus the same inequality holds in the new annulus. Outside Q_r, both are their respective constants. Consequently the whole sequence phi_r is decreasing on the compactification. At any fixed point of Y^an it tends to u-2g; on the projective boundary it tends to minus infinity. Generic finiteness of u prevents the limit from being identically minus infinity. Genuine global PSH is closed under this decreasing limit by its actual generated-cone definition, independently of EP.

The explicit moving-collar argument matters: choosing a diagonal among unrelated compact approximations without proving the annular inequality would not establish global monotonicity.

## 3. Obtain a controlled decreasing FS sequence without countability assumptions

Each phi_r is continuous genuine CPSH on the fixed compactification. Establish the actual uniform FS density there using the applicable source theorem. Choose positive rational errors epsilon_r, decreasing geometrically with summable rational tails, and a rational FS metric F_r with |F_r-phi_r|<=epsilon_r uniformly on the compactification. Set

    B_r=2 sum_(i>=r) epsilon_i,
    H_r=F_r+B_r.

Then H_r>=phi_r and

    H_r-H_(r-1)
       <= (phi_r-phi_(r-1))+epsilon_r+epsilon_(r-1)-2epsilon_(r-1)
       <= 0.

Thus H_r decreases to the same limit phi. Rational shifts preserve genuine FS membership. Appending a complete high-degree section system at sufficiently low weights, as in the companion Rossi proposal, ensures that on Y the weights

    w_r=H_r+2g

belong to the actual affine FS class with affine algebra generators and a floor. They decrease to u. Compactness of the global metric gives the controlled bound

    w_r <= 2g+sup_X H_r.

Since H_r decreases, the constants can even be bounded by sup_X H_1. There is no dependence on field countability or metrizability, and no inference that a general unbounded PSH net automatically contains an approximating sequence. The explicitly constructed continuous phi_r and summable uniform errors supply the sequence.

All tensor/scalar conventions must be checked against the actual compactification of g: twice its ample rational class is fixed throughout this construction. Uniform errors are errors of metric differences, so subtracting the same reference preserves them on all of the noncompact affine chart.

## 4. The principal-open pole damping application

Suppose Y_f is the principal open f nonzero, v is already regularizable there, and v<=g on Y_f for an affine FS function g on Y. For positive rational c set epsilon=c/3 and

    g_epsilon=max(g,-epsilon log|f|)

on Y_f. After shifting g to be at least one, g_epsilon is an actual affine FS exhaustion of Y_f: the original generators of k[Y] together with f^-1 generate k[Y_f]. Apply the preceding controlled sequence theorem on Y_f, with its own explicit projective compactification, to obtain w_i decreasing to v and w_i<=2g_epsilon+B uniformly in i for a fixed finite B.

Then

    w_i+c log|f|
       <= max(2g+c log|f|,(c/3)log|f|)+B.

The right side tends to minus infinity along f=0 on every bounded affine neighborhood, because g is finite and continuous there. For rational constants D_i decreasing to minus infinity, define

    v_(c,i)=max(w_i+c log|f|,D_i) on Y_f,
    v_(c,i)=D_i on Z(f).

Each is continuous and locally rational PL with corrected contact supports. The constant branch wins on an actual neighborhood of Z(f) for each i. They decrease to v+c log|f| on Y_f and minus infinity on Z(f). This establishes membership in the corrected local decreasing class on Y. It does not yet show affine FS regularizability on Y merely from that membership.

For this last conversion, note that each v_(c,i) has upper growth bounded by one positive rational affine FS exhaustion h on Y, up to a common constant: choose h to dominate 2g+c max(log|f|,0)+B and all D_i. Positive rational sums and maxima of affine FS functions can be converted to the actual generator-based affine FS class by explicit product branches and low-weight original generators. Verify these finite algebraic operations.

For a continuous corrected rational PL function b on Y with b<=h, repeat the single-function moving-compact truncation above using max(b-2h,C_r) on Q_r={h<=R_r}, constant C_r outside. There is no need for an already regularizing sequence in this continuous PL case: b itself supplies the contact PL charts, and b<=h gives the boundary and annular inequalities directly. The curve-to-nef bridge makes each continuous global truncation genuine CPSH. Their decreasing limit is a genuine global PSH potential b-2h, with the same actual hyperplane boundary. Apply this to each v_(c,i), all on the fixed compactification of h, and call the resulting potentials psi_i. They decrease with i, including boundary value minus infinity.

The final sequence passage can be explicit as well. On Y, psi_i is continuous; at the hyperplane boundary psi_i<=-h tends uniformly to minus infinity in the reference chart, since h=-log||a|| for the fixed continuous FS reference. Therefore

    eta_i=max(psi_i,-i)

is continuous on the whole compactification, taking the constant value -i on a whole boundary collar. It is genuine CPSH by the actual stability under the maximum with a constant metric in the same ample class. The sequence eta_i decreases to the nontrivial limit psi=lim psi_i. Approximate each continuous eta_i uniformly by rational genuine FS metrics, and add the summable tail shifts from Section3. This yields an explicitly decreasing global FS sequence tending to psi. Converting its section systems to affine generator systems gives a decreasing affine FS sequence tending to v_c=psi+2h on Y. This reconstructs regularizability directly; it does not declare it equivalent to local membership or invoke an unavailable unbounded-net sequence theorem.

## 5. What this repairs and what it does not

The first three sections give a concrete controlled-approximation replacement for the circular invocation of Wu3.25(2), conditional only on the new continuous rational model bridge. Section4 gives an exact pole damping construction and an explicit final sequence passage on Y. After those steps are independently reconstructed, Wu3.31 can use a smooth ample compactification and known smooth EP to remove the damping parameter. Since log|f| may have either sign, do not call that parameter family globally monotone; use the actual upper-envelope/usc closure consequence of smooth EP and verify its stated hypotheses.

No singular EP is assumed in obtaining controlled approximants. No general weak-input trace extension, Hartogs/Runge induction, normalization onto theorem or arbitrary-support singular equation follows automatically. Those are subsequent gates to reconstruct before the original task can be declared solved.
